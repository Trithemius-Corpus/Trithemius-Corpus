The midday meal, however, shall be roast hen, or a young chicken, a turtledove, a partridge, or something similar; the drink shall be wine one year old, moderately diluted. Every night, near the hour of sleep, a lozenge shall be inserted into the right nostril, and it shall be stopped with cotton. You shall keep it so until you have recited no more than twenty verses of Virgil. After some days have elapsed, insert a lozenge moistened with rose-water into the left nostril, and you will retain everything you are going to read, or will quickly attain understanding.

But one who wholly desires to preserve his memory in this way must abstain from drunkenness, sex, and especially an overloaded stomach, from all satiety, green fruits, vegetables, salted meats, and everything that produces flatulence. At the end of the meal prepared coriander seed is to be taken. Above all, care must also be taken that it is diligently and properly prepared. It will also not be harmful, at intervals, to hold one lozenge in the mouth and swallow it. It will also help to throw a lozenge onto hot coals, so that the smoke may be well received into the nostrils and, once received, retained, the nostrils being stopped with cotton so that it does not escape; or also to dissolve several lozenges in lavender-water, or rose-water, or anise-water, and frequently, morning and evening, to moisten the forehead, temples, nostrils, and the back of the head around the seat of memory, and to put into the nostrils clean little linen cloths soaked in it. And yet this remedy has been disclosed to few.

But to return to celestial things: in precisely the same way and form as for Mercury, the powers and influences of the remaining planets will be able to be conciliated. Thus, for obtaining honors and dignities, and the favor and goodwill of princes and magnates, there is the image of the Sun when it is in Leo and in the tenth house, in trine with Jupiter or the Moon. Nevertheless, in this year 1608 the solar influence is impeded because of Mars opposite from Aquarius, as even the remarkable and unusual changes of the air itself clearly demonstrate. Hence, in forming these figures, this alone must be observed: that the planet whose power and influence someone desires to conciliate for himself be as free as possible from every impediment.

DEMONSTRATION
OF THE REASONS WHY THERE IS SUCH GREAT POWER AND EFFICACY IN THESE MAGICAL IMAGES

TREATISE VII

But why and whence rings, images, and figures engraved on a certain metal or stone should have the powers credited to them is not to be assigned to temperament, nor to manifest qualities, nor to the whole substance, but to a divine and magical cause, that is, to a certain higher and more sublime cause, which some not inappropriately call a spiritual vehicle and bond. Through this mediation the body and soul of substances, though mutually distant, are joined together with one another; by it the magnet draws iron, and chicory follows the motion of the Sun.

Through this vehicle and bond, coming between or intervening, Mercury is effective in quicksilver; in sharpening or weakening the memory and the cogitative power; in the alectory stone; in mixed color and taste; in Egypt, Greece, Flanders, and Silesia.

Through the intervention of this vehicle, the Moon is effective in silver and glass; in the brain; in the left eye of a man,
