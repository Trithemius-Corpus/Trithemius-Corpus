Thus the image of Leo or Cancer should be engraved on a ring or stone when the Sun is traversing Leo and the Moon Cancer, yet in such a way that each is free from every impediment, especially from the rays of Saturn or Mars.

It must also be held that any sign, so long as the Sun is in it, becomes alive and rules over the rest, and that its effect is felt before the others. Therefore the planet must always be directed to the place, that is, to the sign, face, and degree which the Sun illuminates.

So far concerning the magical images of the ancient sages. I have chosen to list them here chiefly for this reason: to make plain that they were constructed from the principles of magical philosophy, that is, of true and natural magic, not of goetia and necromancy, although it is certain that superstition has crept into many of them through abuse. Use has praise; abuse has blame. For if anyone wishes, with superstition, to refer them to evil action, he will be opposed to the divine order and to the principles of the art itself from which they flowed; and he must beware lest, surrounded by mad superstitions, he become wholly mad and superstitious.

SEALS. 44

Moreover, from almost the same foundations and causes, there have now also begun to be made gilded and silvered plates, round, square, or triangular, on which certain signatures and characters of the planets are engraved according to certain positions and configurations of the heavens, or constellations, in order to obtain the favor and influence of each planet. For it is consistent with reason and experience that the influence of the planets is chiefly received and retained in figures and likenesses of this sort, marked with a definite form and with images.

Meanwhile this must be observed with the greatest exactness: that the planet whose influence you seek be strong and fortunate, that is, in its own house, exaltation, or triplicity, in the angle of the east or in midheaven, free from the harmful rays of Saturn or Mars, supported by the trine or sextile of Venus or Jupiter, or joined to them, direct, swift in course, not combust, and ascending in the circle of its apogee.

MAGIC. 45

ERECTION OF THE SIGNATURE
of Mercury, for sharpening the memory.

Let Mercury serve as an example. If anyone desires to obtain its influence for sharpening the intellect, talent, and memory, let him observe at what time of year Mercury, in Gemini or Virgo, the signs in which it has its house, is strong and fortunate in the east or in midheaven. When the hour and the minute of the hour have been found, let a celestial figure be made, together with a careful and skillful engraving of the aspects, in pure silver. Afterwards it is enclosed in white silk and kept, and so it is sewn in, on that part where it precisely touches the cerebellum, with a few grains of the best musk added. It has been observed to complete its operation especially at night, and more evidently at that hour and time at which the engraving was both begun and completed.

In this year 1608, Mercury was found strong and fortunate around 11 May, at the second morning hour. Again it will be strongest and most fortunate around the 14th and 15th day of the month of September, a little before and after the fifth morning hour, at which time it precedes the Sun at the eastern angle, in its own house, exaltation, and the fortunate trine of Jupiter.

And this is the method of sharpening intellectual power and memory from the powers of the heavens. From plants and animals also, I hope I shall win no small favor among those whose memory is fragile and fleeting, though they are quick in understanding, if I bring forth certain remedies for strengthening memory. I know that the most illustrious and learned men have used them successfully; until now they have lain hidden among secrets. I do not begrudge them, since they can be of public benefit. There are many things in them which you would not know, and which cling neither to temperament, nor to matter, nor to any manifest quality, but must be attributed only to some hidden property.
