The figure of a tree, vine, or ear of grain, carved on some stone, makes the wearer abound in food and clothing, and renders princes favorable.

Mars armed, or a robed maiden with a flowing garment, holding laurel in her hand, if found on a stone, makes the wearer powerful for accomplishing deeds of arms, and frees him from violent death, from submersion in water, and from adverse accidents.

The figure of an armed man on a horse with a lance, carved in amethyst, makes the wearer victorious in open battle.

If you find carved on a stone the figure of Hercules, holding a club in his right hand and killing a lion or another monster, know that it grants victory in open combat.

The figure of Jupiter, which is in the form of a man with a ram’s head, makes the wearer lovable, and makes him obtain whatever he wishes.

A virgin or girl with a lamp, carved in crystal, has the virtue of preserving the light of the eyes.

The figure of the fiery triplicity, that is, of Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius, carved on a precious stone, is said to be effective against all cold illnesses

It ought to be carved so that, in the triangle, Leo holds the apex, Aries the right side of the base, and Sagittarius the left.

The image of the earthy triplicity, that is, of Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn, engraved on a stone, will provide help against all hot and moist illnesses, and will incline the wearer toward rural works, such as plowing, planting, sowing, cultivating vineyards, and the like. Capricorn ought to be at the apex, Taurus on the right side of the base, and Virgo on the left.

The image of the airy triplicity, that is, of Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius, carved on a stone, frees one from all cold and dry illnesses arising from melancholic humor, especially quartan fever. Libra will be at the point of the triangle, Aquarius on the right side of the base, and Gemini on the left.

The figure of the watery triplicity, that is, of Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces, engraved on a stone, frees one from hot and dry diseases, such as hectic fever, and from all choleric diseases; but it renders the wearer inconstant, unjust, and a hater of truth. Cancer ought to be at the crown of the triangle,

The figure of Saturn engraved on stones is of this kind: an old man holding a sickle in his hand, having a beard that is not very hairy. This figure, carved on a stone of the nature of Saturn, renders the wearer powerful, with a continual increase of power.

The image of Jupiter is of a man seated on a leaf, or on a four-footed chair, holding a rod in one hand and a ball in the other; or holding an image of Cancer or a fish, and having an eagle before his feet. But among the doctors of magic it is represented otherwise: for they carve the figure of a man with a ram’s head, wrinkled heels, and a slender chest. If this is engraved on a stone, especially a krabatus, it renders the wearer fortunate, lovable, and pleasing to religious people, and raises him to honors and dignities.

The image of Mars is represented on stone in many ways: sometimes with a banner in his hand, sometimes with a lance or another instrument of war, yet always armed, and sometimes mounted. The virtue of this carved stone renders the wearer victorious, bold, and warlike, especially if it is carved on a stone of a similar virtue.
