Andromeda is the image of a girl, having loose hair and hands let down; she is contained by Taurus in the northern region, and is of the nature of Venus. Its virtue is that, if it is carved in a stone, it reconciles quarrels and disputes that have arisen between husband and wife, confirms stable love, and protects from very many illnesses.

Perseus is an image holding a sword in the right hand and the head of the Gorgon in the left; it is located in Taurus and in the northern part, and has the power of Saturn and Venus. If it has been carved in a stone, it turns misfortunes away from the bearer, and preserves him from lightning and tempest; not only the bearer, but also the places where it is kept. It also dissolves bewitchments.

Serpentarius is the figure of a man holding a serpent, holding the head in the right hand and the tail in the left. It is in the sign of Scorpio and in the north, and has the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its virtue, when carved in a stone, is to prevail against poisons, to cure the bites of venomous animals, and, if its washing-water is drunk, it makes one vomit up poison without injury.

The Eagle, or falling Vulture, is the image of an eagle flying with an arrow beneath its feet. It is placed in Cancer and in the northern part; it is of the nature of Jupiter and Mars, but the arrow is of the nature of Mars and Venus. These constellations, when they have been carved in a stone,

are said to preserve the bearer's old ranks of honors, to acquire new ones, and to contribute to victory.

The Fish, or Dolphin, is the image of a humped fish. It is in the sign of Aquarius and in the north, having the nature of Saturn and Mars; when this has been carved in a stone and bound to the limbs, it makes the bearer fortunate in fishing.

Pegasus, or the winged horse, is, according to some, half a horse with wings; according to others, an entire horse with wings and without a bridle. In the sign of Aries and in the northern part, having the nature of Mars and Jupiter, when it has been carved in a stone it gives the bearer victory in battle on open ground, and makes him swift, cautious, and bold. But when it is hung from a horse's neck, or placed in the water where the horse drinks, it will free him from many illnesses.

Cetus is the figure of a great fish with a curved tail and a large mouth. It is contained by Taurus and in the southern part, and obtains the nature of Saturn. This figure, carved in a stone, when it has a crested serpent above its back, makes the bearer fortunate at sea, renders him prudent and lovable, and restores things that have been taken away.

The image of Orion is that of an armed man with a sword or

sickle in his hand. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part, and has the nature of Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars. This figure, carved in a stone, makes the bearer victorious and causes him to carry off victory over his enemies.

The Ship is an image with a curved prow and a raised sail, sometimes with oars and sometimes without them. It is contained by Leo and in the southern part; it is of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Carved in a stone, it renders the bearer secure in conducting affairs on sea and water, and does not allow him to take harm from water.

The Dog is the figure of a hunting dog with a curved tail. It exists in the sign of Cancer and in the southern part, having the nature of Venus. If it has been carved in a stone, they say that it has the particular power of completely freeing lunatics, maniacs, and demoniacs.

The image of the Hare is in the likeness of a hare, with ears and feet extended, as if it were running. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part. Its nature is Saturnian and Mercurial. Its virtue is to cure the frenzied and to prevail against the wiles of demons; nor will the bearer be able to be harmed by a malignant spirit.
