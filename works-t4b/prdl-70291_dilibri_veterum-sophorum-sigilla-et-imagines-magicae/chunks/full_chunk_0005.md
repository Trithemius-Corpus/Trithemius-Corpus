The figure of a bearded man with a long face and curved eyebrows, sitting upon a plough between two bulls and holding a vulture in his hand, carved in stone, you should know to have power in the planting of trees and in the discovery of treasures, and to make the bearer victorious in battle. Serpents are driven away from the face of the bearer; it heals epileptics and removes every affliction of evil spirits. It ought to be set and carried in an iron ring.

The figure of a man holding a sickle in his hand above his head, with a crocodile beneath his feet: when it has been carved in stone and set in a leaden ring, and under the stone there is a little root of squill, whoever carries this ring with him will be safe from enemies and, on a journey, from robbers.

The figure of a man holding a sword in his hand and sitting upon a dragon, when it has been carved in amethyst and set in a leaden or iron ring and worn on the finger, spirits will obey him and will reveal to him whatever treasures there are.

The figure of a standing eagle, when it has been carved in eagle-stone and set in a leaden ring, has the power, for whoever carries it with him, of catching many fish; no wild beast will harm him, and he will be loved by men.

*TREATISE IV.*

*THE IMAGES, OR SEALS, OF THETEL.*

Thetel, a very ancient Doctor, treating of carvings on stones, says that these carvings, found on suitable stones, have very great power.

The image of a man carved in jasper, having a shield in his left hand and in his right an idol, or something warlike, and in place of feet there are vipers, and in place of a man’s head there is the head of a cock or of a lion, will give power against enemies and renders the bearer victorious. It is also effective against poisons, and checks blood from whatever part it flows.

The figure of a man having a bundle of herbs at his neck, when it has been carved in jasper, has the power of discerning or recognizing illnesses. It restrains blood from whatever place it flows; and they say that Galen possessed this stone.

A woman holding a goose in one hand and a fish in the other: when she has been carved in chrysolite, it is very useful for conducting business.

The figure of a wolf carved in jasper is effective against spells and prevents mad words from being uttered.

The figure of a stag carved in jasper has the power of healing and freeing lunatics and the frenzied.

The figure of an emperor carved in jasper, with head raised, renders the bearer lovable to every creature; and whatever he seeks, he will obtain.

The figure of a virgin with flowing garment, holding laurel in her hand, when it has been carved in jasper, makes the bearer safe from drowning in water, nor will he be troubled by a demon; and it makes him powerful and one who obtains all things.

The figure of a man having in his hand an inscribed palm, when it is found in jasper, makes the bearer powerful and pleasing to princes. Thus far Thetel.
