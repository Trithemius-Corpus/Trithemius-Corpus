SEALS

ON THE METHOD OF MAKING MAGICAL SEALS AND IMAGES.

TRACTATE VI.

Having set forth magical seals and images, and the method of making them, at least insofar as it has been handed down by the most ancient writers themselves, it remains for a more exact method of making them also to be added here, and for the preceding matters to be explained more accurately.

And indeed, before all else in their composition, the time must be considered; I mean the time of election, which must be diligently observed in inscribing or carving figures on stones. For they then receive greater virtue if they are carved at fixed and opportune times; in this way, indeed, they find the stars more favorable. Thus one who wishes to stir up love uses benevolent aspects, but one who wishes to kindle hatred uses unfavorable aspects. They therefore wish the image of Saturn to be fashioned when he himself is in Capricorn.
