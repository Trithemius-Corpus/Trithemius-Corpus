SEALS

The image of a man having a shield at his neck and a helmet on his head, holding a sword in his hand and trampling a serpent under his feet, carved in a red stone: if it is hung from the neck, he will overcome everyone in battle, especially if the engagement has taken place on the day of Mars.

The image of a horse having a crocodile above it, carved in hyacinth, has the power to make the wearer victorious in civil cases; it makes him loved, compliant, and amiable. It should be set in gold, because its power is increased by gold.

The figure of a seated man, and of a woman standing before him with her hair spread toward her loins, and of a man looking upward, carved in carnelian: its power will be to acquire goodwill from all who are touched with this stone. A little amber and terebinth resin should be placed beneath the stone.

The figure of a foaming and fiery horse, upon which a black man sits with a scepter in his hand, carved in hematite, grants the power of ruling and restores lost favor. It should be set in an equal weight of gold and silver.

The image of a seated man with a lit candle in his hand, if carved in chrysolite, makes the wearer rich. It should be set in the purest gold.

MAGIC. 9

A carved figure of a stag, or a hunter, or a dog, or a hare has the power of restraining demons, lunatics, and frenzied persons.

Know that the carved image of a woman having a bird in one hand and a fish in the other has power in catching fish and birds. It should be set in silver.

An image whose front part is a horse, but whose rear part is a goat, if carved in some stone, gives fortune in feeding and equipping animals or beasts of every kind. It should be set in lead.

The figure of a woman seated with a trumpet on a horse, or of a soldier running with a horn at his neck and with a tree before him, carved in some stone, grants hunters success in hunting if they carry it with them.

The figure of a man with bent knees looking upward and holding a cloth, if it is carved in stone and someone carries it with him, gives favor and riches in buying and selling goods.

The figure of a scorpion and Sagittarius fighting each other, if carved in some stone, will make the malicious and discordant harmonious, and enemies friends. It should be set in silver.

The half-figure of a ram and a lion, when carved in some precious stone: when you touch discordant people with it, they will become amiable and harmonious. It should be set in silver.

The figure of a woman in the upper part, but of a fish in the lower part, holding a mirror in one hand and a branch in the other, carved in hyacinth and worn on the finger in a gold ring, makes one invisible. The ring with the stone must be turned toward the palm, and the palm must be closed.

The figure of a basilisk, one part of which is female and the lower part serpent, when carved in some precious stone, will have the power of driving away all poisonous animals.
