The figure of a winged man, having a serpent under his feet and holding its head in his hands, makes the bearer rich, prudent, and lovable to all.

The image of an ant holding an ear of grain or a grain, carved in some stone, is effective for acquiring riches.

The figure of a cock holding a crown or a belt in its beak will give victory in a duel, especially if it is in cock-stone.

When you find carved in some gem the figure of a standing man whose head is that of an ox and whose feet are those of an eagle, and impress it in wax, no man will speak ill of you.

When you find a basilisk and a dragon carved in cornelian, fighting with each other, and there is also the head of an ox there, apply it to your neck when you are about to fight, and you will conquer all beasts, both marine and terrestrial.

The figure of a naked and crowned man, holding in one hand a cup and in the other a branch of an herb, carved in jet and bound in a ring made of any metal, when a feverish person wears it, immediately frees him from fever.

and placed on someone’s cloth, if the person dining eats with his right hand, will make him not be satisfied.

When you find carved in hyacinth, garnet, or crystal the figure of a woman with her hair loose, or her breasts bare, and in her sight there is a man looking at her face, and bind it in a gold ring of the same weight as the stone, with amber, aloes, and polypody beneath the stone, it will make the bearer lovable to all; and when it is placed beneath his head, he will see in sleep whatever he wishes.

When you find carved in pyrite the figure of a man riding and holding a bridle in one hand and in the other a bow, with a sword belted on, and bind it in a gold ring, it makes the bearer victorious in battle, and no one will be able to resist him. And if someone submerges this ring in musked oil, all will fear him, if he anoints his face with that oil, nor will they be able to oppose him.

When you find in lodestone the figure of a standing man who is naked, with a naked girl standing at his right, her hair bound around her head, and the man holds his right hand upon the girl’s neck and his left above her breast, and the man looks at the girl’s face while she looks at the ground, set it in an iron ring

of the same weight as the stone, and enclose beneath the ring the tongue of a hoopoe, myrrh, alum, and human blood in the same weight as the tongue. For the one bearing this ring, no enemy will be able to resist him in his sight, whether in war or elsewhere; nor will any thief or harmful wild beast be able to enter the house in which this named stone has been. And when an epileptic drinks water in which it has been washed, he will be healed. Also, if red wax is sealed with it and hung from a dog’s neck, it will prevent the dog from barking.

A soldier running on a horse, with a horn at his neck and with a tree before him, carved in some precious stone: know that it is effective for all hunts.

A woman holding a bird in one hand and a fish in the other, carved in crystal: know that it is effective for catching birds, and it ought to be bound in silver.

The figure of a man with a goat’s head in place of his own: know that it is effective for acquiring riches.

The figure of an eagle carved in crystal makes the bearer rich, victorious, and eloquent.

When you find the figure of a turtle-dove carved in pangonus and wear it in a lead ring, you will never be wounded, nor will you suffer harm, but you will be honored by all, especially by elders.

When you find carved in gold the figure of a bird holding a leaf in its mouth, and in its mouth and sight there is the head of a man or a vulture, the one who bears it will acquire profit from everything that he buys and sells, and he will be sought out by buyers.

When you find carved in crystal the figure of the face of a lion or dragon having two heads with a delicate tail, or of a man in whose right hand there is a staff and who strikes the lion’s head, and place it in orichalcum, with musk and amber beneath the stone, and carry it with you, all will obey you, and you will increase your property. Wax sealed with it will have the same virtue.

When you find carved in some stone the figure of a winged horse, which is called Pegasus, it is very good for soldiers, and in open-field war it gives boldness and vigor.
