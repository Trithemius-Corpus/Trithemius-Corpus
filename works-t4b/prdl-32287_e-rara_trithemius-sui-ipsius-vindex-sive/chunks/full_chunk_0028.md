The fifth kind is called subterranean, because it dwells in caves and caverns and in the remote hollows of mountains. These demons are of the worst disposition, and they especially attack those who dig wells and mines, and those who seek treasures hidden in the earth, being most ready for the destruction of the human race. They make openings in the earth, stir up flame-spewing winds, and shake the foundations of buildings. Sometimes at night they go out from the mountains in bands and lead strange and astonishing dances in the fields; and, as if fearing the command of a single leader, they suddenly vanish at a sign and return to their own hiding places. At times the sound of bells is heard among them, and they sometimes falsely claim that they are the spirits of people who have died. They seek nothing more than human fear and amazement. Hence we have found that they have sometimes led some of the simpler sort of people into their hiding places in the mountains, showing astonishing spectacles to those who marvel at them, and, as if there were dwellings of the blessed there,

they falsely pretend to be friends of the living. Among these demons, too, some are guardians of treasures, which the avarice of mortals has hidden in the earth; and, lest they should again come into human use, they withdraw them, steal them, guard them, and sometimes move them from place to place. This kind of demon is not accustomed to have any commerce with women’s acts of witchcraft.

The sixth kind of demons is called light-fleeing, because they greatly abhor and detest the light: for they never appear by day, nor can they assume a body in any way except at night. This is the business that walks about in darkness, an inscrutable and utterly dark kind of demon, stirred by cold passions, malicious, restless, and disturbed, which violently oppresses people it meets during the hours of night, and quite often, with God’s permission, kills them either by a certain breath or by touch. This kind of demon does not associate with acts of witchcraft, nor can it be constrained by charms,

because it flees both light and human voices, and likewise every tumult. The aerial kind is especially accustomed to attend the witchcraft of women, since it is subtler than all the others and of the boldest rashness. Some of the terrestrial demons also, as has been said, serve certain acts of witchcraft; yet, being of a lower rank, they cannot at all bring about those greater acts of witchcraft. It is therefore manifestly clear that the whole power of acts of witchcraft, in which they do such astonishing things, is granted to demons in limited measure by the Lord God; and when he does not permit it, they accomplish nothing.

ON DIVINE PERMISSION.

Question Seven.

The seventh question of your Most Serene Majesty was this: Why does God, since he is just and the avenger of evils, permit such great acts of witchcraft to be done for the destruction of the human race, by which not only sinners, but also many innocent people perish after being gravely

tormented?

Why God permits so many evils and acts of witchcraft.

I remember that, when I was once placed before your Majesty, I answered this question in the following way. The best God, just and upright, gracious and merciful, permits justly whatever he permits, because he is just and there is no iniquity in him; rather, all things that occur, he either does or permits by true judgment. Therefore, when it is asked why God permits evils to be done, we answer: he permits them because he is just. Does the answer we have given seem absurd to your Majesty, O Caesar? For it in no way prejudices the divine goodness that he permits evils to be done by the most just reasoning; indeed it would prejudice it instead if he did not permit evils to be done in the present world. For if he did not permit evils to be done, then man could not sin; he would certainly be incapable of sin, which does not befit his nature, to which the Creator of all things gave free will from the beginning.
