In accordance with this comparison, wicked and reprobate people are admitted by demons into their familiarity, and through a more prolonged confidence they become very bold. They receive a simulated, not a true familiar, command over them, which they have merited by the likeness of a most depraved will. Hence whatever they request from demons who cooperate with them by an implicit or explicit pact, they receive, except when, being prevented by divine ordinance, the demons are unable to provide what is asked. Yet they do not cooperate in this way indiscriminately with all the reprobate and wicked, but only with those with whom, by the intervention of some pact, they have entered into covenants, such as sorcerous women, who have paid homage to the devil, and those invokers of demons whom the common people call by the usual term necromancers.

of those people who, beloved by God through the merit of holiness, have received from him the power to trample on the head of the ancient serpent. Such were the apostles in the primitive Church, and their followers and disciples, of whom the Lord says in the holy Gospel: In my name they will cast out demons, and they will take up serpents. The second kind is that of those people to whom, by reason of their order in the Church of God, it is granted to drive demons from human bodies by the received power of their ministry. And this ministry is entrusted to exorcists in the Church of God, when, that is, they are ordained by the bishop.

Again, there are two kinds of people who desire to command demons, and the most wicked conditions show how unlawfully they presume to do this. The first and most destructive kind is that of sorceresses, who enter into pacts with demons and, after a solemn profession has been made, hand themselves over to some demon as a perpetual right of property and obedience. How great are the evils this most destructive kind of people commits in your empire,

For first denying God, the faith of Christ, and holy baptism, they offer themselves in mind and body to demons; by their sorceries they sicken, injure, and kill human beings and beasts. There is no disease that, with God permitting it, they cannot bring on, because when they present themselves to demons as servants in observance of the pact they have made, in comparison with them they dare whatever they wish. Hence they are not to be tolerated anywhere, but rather to be utterly exterminated in every place, as God the Creator of all commands: You shall not suffer sorcerers to live.

Some sorceresses call demons by spells, others entice them by prayers and sacrifices, and the rest, provoking them with unspeakable things and ceremonies, draw them into the aid of their impiety. Therefore it is no wonder that a sorceress seems to command demons, since through the piety of a perverse will they have been made like them, and by mutual association they are united for the evil of the human race. These most destructive people, full of falsehood and superstitions, commit evils so great that no good person could imagine them.
