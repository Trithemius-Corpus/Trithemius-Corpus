The sixth reason is that a person harmed by the assaults of demons may be called back to repentance and may resolve in his heart upon the necessary amendment of life. For almighty God does not immediately send vengeance when we sin, but awaits our amendment until the appointed time. But if we have neglected to do penance, the avenger of evils comes to us when we do not expect it; and we who, as sinners, disdained to acknowledge him by repenting in tranquillity, will at last, though late, acknowledge him in tribulation. Hence the holy prophet speaks in the person of God, saying: “If his sons forsake my law, and do not walk in my judgments, and do not keep my commandments, I will visit their iniquities with a rod, and their sins with scourges; but my mercy I will not scatter from them, nor will I do harm in my truth.”

The seventh reason why almighty God permits the sorceries of demons to prevail against the good and innocent is so that an excessively licentious occasion for sinning may be cut off from them. For there are many poor and infirm people pleasing to God, good, devout, and humble, whom want and bodily sickness preserve in the love of God. If they were rich and raised up with honors, they would quickly flow down from their purpose of holiness into the depth of vices. It is not expedient for everyone’s salvation to have honors and riches together with health; nor does a longer life always make a person better. Therefore almighty God, wishing to show his mercy toward those whom he has chosen, permits the good, the just, and the innocent to be subject to various calamities; and sometimes through demons, sometimes through evil people, sometimes by various other ways and means, he takes away from such people riches and honors, which he foreknew they would use badly, so that they may preserve humility in poverty and low estate, which, if placed in wealth

Finally, he permits certain people to be driven to death by sorcery, so that the license for sinning may be cut off from them, which they would have abused if allowed to live longer. Hence Scripture says of the just man: “He was snatched away lest wickedness alter his understanding, and lest deceit beguile his soul.”

The eighth reason for divine permission is that people may venerate the sacraments of the Church, which have great power against all the sorceries of malignant spirits. For a person is rarely harmed by the sorceries of demons if he venerates the sacraments and rites of the Catholic Church with due honor. And we have very often heard, from the witches’ own confession, that hearing Masses and using things blessed by the Church greatly weakens the powers of demons. For they are not blessed in the Church of God in vain, but so that they may be fearsome signs which show demons the protection of divine protection.

Concerning boys and infants, however, who, having been reborn in Christ, are sometimes killed by the sorceries of demons or of human beings, we do not doubt that divine providence permits this to happen for their salvation, for it knows how to order all things for the better for mortals. Yet it often happens that infants perish by sorceries because of the sins of their parents.
