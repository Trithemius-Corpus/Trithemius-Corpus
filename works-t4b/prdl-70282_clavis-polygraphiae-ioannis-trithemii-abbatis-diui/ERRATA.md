## 2026-11-18 · key-messengers

Tenebroso agrees with ministerio, the service that Augustus wishes to employ covertly. Nunciorum identifies whose service he uses. AI scan collation. No human verification of this correction is documented.

Previous wording: Wishing in difficult affairs to use the service of obscure messengers

## 2026-11-23 · key-numeral-cipher

The type on C ii verso gives i&kahk, &kif and icei&kaib. C ii recto assigns the cipher group i& to q and the single ampersand to f. The open-foot h can be distinguished from the closed b in the same lines. The inter-group punctuation is regularized and the line breaks are represented by spaces. The unusual i&kahk is transcribed as printed. The source footer identifies HAB images 535 (key) and 536 (example), corpus images 529 and 530. The independently digitized BSB copy of this setting agrees. AI visual collation. Human review is undocumented.
