With the aid of our Lord Jesus Christ, savior of faithful souls, who lives in the sacred Trinity in unity with the Father and the Holy Spirit, threefold in persons and one God in substance, incomprehensible, almighty, and everlasting; from whom, in whom, and through whom every good, beautiful, and true thing proceeds, we have brought this second book of the Polygraphia to an end. To him be praise, honor, power, and glory: he who by the innocence of his death freed our race from the ruin of eternal perdition and, by perpetual mercy, opened the entrance to eternal life for all who believe in him with a pure conscience. Line by line we record three hundred and eight paraphrases, deliberately avoiding a more extended digression. For at the beginning of this little work it seemed enough to us if we had given those who wished it a reason to seek out fuller things, since both time and method failed us for them. Farewell, friendly reader. From Peapoli, on the penultimate day of the month of February, in the year of the Lord 1508.

END OF THE SECOND BOOK
of the Polygraphia.
