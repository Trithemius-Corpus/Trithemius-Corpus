Whoever rightly understands the table of the orchema will compose for himself from it as many alphabets as he wishes. Writers will need diligence, lest the multitude of numbers confuse memory or understanding.

We have not expanded the orchemata, for a reason that moves us: because, as each one ranges farther abroad in its own progression, the immense labor has terrified the mind. We have also deliberately passed over unity without assigning it a place, since we judge it rash to put it before our own inventions in order. For from the things that we have already shown by way of example, anyone fit in understanding will easily attain greater things. Everything serves secrets.
