We have thus far, by way of example, set out the method used by the ancient Franks of Germany for writing numbers with Latin letters, so that we might also furnish knowledge of it to those who do not know it. But the fact that, in setting beside them the figures of algorism, we have departed from the common rule of calculation was done not by error but for a reasonable cause, namely so that we should not be overburdened in an excessive progression. There were also some who wrote larger numbers in this way.

The sixth book of the Polygraphy of Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Saint James the Greater, Apostle, at Würzburg, begins.

Before every invention and use of letters, at the beginning of the world, when human beings were still rude in intellect and uncultivated in habits, under the administration of a threefold alternation, as some think, of intelligences succeeding one another in order through 1,063 years, the influences of the planets in the sphere held sway in their own manner over bodies on earth. The ancient investigators of this matter say that Saturn, from the beginning of the world, presided by his influence over things germinating and growing on earth, chiefly through his governing intelligence, whom they called Orisiel, that is, the life-giving one of God, for 354 lunar years, likewise four months, and as many days and hours. These things we report as poetic, but we do not affirm them.

After Saturn, Venus is said to have assumed rule; after Venus, Jupiter. After Jove, Mercury, the fourth ruler in order, adorned human minds with various arts. Under the three intelligences just mentioned, that is, while Saturn, Venus, and Jove were ruling, they assert that human beings had no use or knowledge of letters. Therefore, whenever one person wished to announce some secret matter to another, he did this not in writing, since the service of letters was lacking, but symbolically, by certain likenesses of animals, trees and plants, or of other sensible things, according as each had agreed among themselves.
