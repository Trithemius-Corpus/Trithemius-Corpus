You are young, and flexible in either direction. Therefore you must watch over yourself with the greatest diligence, lest you be corrupted by the companionship of the undisciplined. For nothing harms young men so much as the corrupt examples of their elders. Indeed, the younger you are, the more carefully you must see to it that you guard your life in sincerity of the fear of the Lord. For it is written: “A young man according to his way, even when he has grown old, will not depart from it.” What you learn as a youth, you will practice in old age.

On avoiding the companionship of women. Chapter 2.

But what shall I say about familiarity with women, which is most destructive to the priests of God, since the decrees of the holy fathers forbid it to them with a sufficiently strict prohibition? If I set before you the warnings of the most blessed father Jerome, you will at once cry out: “I cannot manage a household without the service of a maidservant; I am not able to prepare food for myself; I do not wish to clean dirty clothes. Why,” you say, “do you cite to me the precepts of Jerome, which bind only the willing? Who appointed Jerome as my lawgiver?” I answer: the universal holy church approves his sayings. Among the canons it places his precepts issued on this matter, and do you presume to mutter against so great an authority? But you will say: “Contrary custom has abrogated this law, and by long time it must be held as law.” But wickedness lies to itself and by foolish consideration is deceived within itself. A custom contrary to good morals can never either remove a law or establish one. And what is so contrary to priestly honorableness as to delight in the companionship of young women? Chastity is put in danger where the society of priests passes among little women. For the first and strongest temptations of clerics come from frequent access to women. Foolish presumption has overthrown many who, so to speak, handed over into the enemy’s hands the instruments of victory. Danger is avoided only with the greatest difficulty if the occasion is not avoided. Indeed, while a woman stays with a cleric, and she sits alone with him alone, prepares food for him as he eats, arranges his little bed as he sleeps, speaks to him, smiles at him, she supplies fuel for lust. However holy a woman may be, she is always to be feared by a cleric. For just as sparks spring from coals, so the force of desire is diffused from constant familiarity with women. [unclear]
