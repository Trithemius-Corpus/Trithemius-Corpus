*De Republica Ecclesiae et Monachorum Ordinis* — "On the Commonwealth of the Church and the Order of Monks" — is one of Trithemius's monastic-reform tracts on the right ordering of religious life within the Church. It belongs with the reform writings of his Sponheim abbacy in the 1490s — *De Statu et Ruina Monastici Ordinis*, *De Triplici Regione Claustralium*, the *Sermones ad Monachos* — arguing for the restoration of strict observance of the Benedictine Rule. Where the narrower reform tracts address the interior discipline of the cloister, this one situates the monastery within the wider commonwealth of the Church.
