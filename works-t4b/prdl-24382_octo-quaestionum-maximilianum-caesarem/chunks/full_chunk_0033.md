Nor would the devil gain strength against us unless he received his strength from our sins. Hence Scripture admonishes us, saying, “Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” And again, “Do not give place to the devil.”

Therefore, to bring the discussion to a conclusion for understanding the question proposed, why God permits so many acts of harmful magic to be done, I believe the reasons have been sufficiently made clear. For even if the will of demons is always wicked, their power can never be unjust, since what it wickedly wills it has from its own choice, but what it can wickedly do it has from God alone. And these things have been said insofar as they concern those who do evil.

But why does almighty God permit demons, through the agency of sorcerers, to harm people who have been baptized in the name of the holy Trinity and faithfully believe in his only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ? For if they could harm only the evil, the perverse, and unbelievers in body and goods, but were restrained from approaching and harming the good and faithful, the permission would rightly seem to be a just judgment. But now the good and innocent are more often made sick by the harmful magic of demons, and sometimes are even deprived of life, to pass over in silence the loss of temporal goods that those bewitched often suffer. Many are troubled as to why the Almighty permits these things, he who permits nothing unjustly.

For this proposed question, I shall set out in order, as briefly as I can, the necessary reasons, received from no others than the ancient and Catholic doctors. The first reason is the testing of Christians’ faith and the proving of their integrity: so that, when scourged by the harmful magic of demons, they may learn in the sufferings of the flesh what faith and love toward God they have hidden in the mind. In prosperity it is not difficult to be long-suffering; but in adversity, with humble patience, to await help from the Savior of the faithful

is not the mark of the soft, but of the strong and of truly faithful Christians. In his tribulation most holy Job learned how great a constancy of faith toward God he possessed, he who before, in tranquility, had preserved his piety without the dart of temptation.

Here at the same time it must be noted that those whom the harmful magic of demons or men has in any way made sick, and who, in order to recover health, either approach or consult any sorcerers and enchanters, whether by themselves or through others, act against the precepts of God and the Church; and they have neither true faith in God nor love. For they prefer the health of mortal flesh to God, and seek salvation not from Christ but from the devil and his minister. They prefer to live healthy, but unhappily, in the name of the devil, rather than to suffer a little here for the love of Christ and afterward rejoice with him forever.

But one who does not approach sorcerers because God has forbidden it, preferring to live with observance of God’s commandments in poverty and sickness rather than against God in riches and bodily health, truly loves God and rightly preserves the integrity of the Christian faith.

Most sacred Caesar, people corrupt in mind and reprobate with respect to the faith must not be tolerated among Christians: those who cure the bewitched by harmful magic; those who reveal thefts and adulteries through the ministry of demons; those who foretell future things by inspecting stones or mirrors. I beg you, do not listen to those who, ignorant of the divine laws, think that ministers of demons of this kind are useful to your rule because they know how to dissolve acts of harmful magic by harmful magic. For that destructive cure of bodies brings about the death of souls, and makes men withdraw from God, the creator of all things, and adhere to impious apostate demons, with whom after death they will be damned forever.
