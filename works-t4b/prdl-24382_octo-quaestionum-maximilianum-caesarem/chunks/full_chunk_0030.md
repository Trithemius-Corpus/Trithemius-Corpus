For nowhere do we read it written that a good spirit was seen in the form of a woman or of any beast, but always in male appearance. Demons, however, have rather often been seen around rivers and springs in the appearance of women: sometimes dancing, sometimes letting down their hair in the manner of a woman, and sometimes speaking with human beings and carrying out various mockeries. And this kind of demons has not customarily had commerce with witches.

The fifth kind is called subterranean, because it dwells in caves and caverns and in the remote hollows of mountains. And these demons are very evil in disposition. They especially attack those who dig wells and mines, and those who seek treasures hidden in the earth, being most ready for the destruction of the human race. They make openings in the earth, stir up flame-vomiting winds, and shake the foundations of buildings. Sometimes at night, coming out of the mountains in troops, they lead marvelous and astonishing dances in the fields, as though fearing the command of a single leader. At a sign they suddenly vanish and return to their hiding places. At times the sound of bells is heard among them, and sometimes they falsely claim that they are the spirits of men departed from life. They seek nothing more than the fear and wonder of human beings. Hence we have found that they have sometimes led certain simpler people into their hiding places in the mountains, showing astonishing spectacles to those who marveled, and they falsely claim that dwellings of the blessed are there, and that they are friends of the living.

Among these demons, too, some are brooding guardians of treasures, which the avarice of mortals has hidden in the earth; and lest they come again into human use, they remove them, steal them, guard them, and sometimes transfer them from place to place. And this kind of demons has not customarily had any commerce with witch-women.

The sixth kind of demons is called light-fleeing, because they especially abhor and detest light: for they never appear by day, nor can they assume a body in any other way than at night. This is the business that walks about in darkness: a kind of demons that is inscrutable and utterly dark, agitated by cold passions, malicious, restless, and disturbed, which violently oppresses people it encounters in the night hours. Rather often, God permitting, it kills certain people either by its breath or by its touch. This kind of demons does not associate with witches, nor can it be constrained by charms, because it flees both light and human voices, and every disturbance as well.

The aerial kind is especially accustomed to attend the sorceries of women, since it is more subtle than all the others and of the most audacious rashness. Some of the terrestrial demons also, as has been said, serve witches; yet because they are held in a lower rank, they are by no means able to bring about those greater sorceries. It is therefore plainly established that the whole power of witches, by which they do such wondrous things, is granted to demons by the Lord God within limits; and when he does not permit it, they accomplish nothing.

ON DIVINE PERMISSION. Seventh Question.

The seventh question of your most serene majesty was this: Why does almighty God, since he is just and the avenger of evils, permit such great sorceries to be done for the destruction of the human race, by which not only sinners but also many innocent people perish, grievously tormented?

To this question, I remember that I answered in this way some time ago, when I had been placed before your majesty. Almighty God is just and upright, kind and merciful. Whatever he permits, he permits justly, because he is just, and there is no iniquity with him; rather, he permits all things that happen in true judgment.
