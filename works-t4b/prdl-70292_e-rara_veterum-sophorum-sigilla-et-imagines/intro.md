*Veterum Sophorum Sigilla et Imagines Magicae* — "The Seals and Magical Images of the Ancient Sages" — is a short astral-magical text: directions for cutting planetary seals and images into stones and gems, tied to the name Tetragrammaton and the planetary courses, "for the production of wonderful effects." It belongs to the later esoteric corpus attributed to Trithemius.

> **Attribution note.** This text is **not by Johannes Trithemius**. It is a 17th-century astral-magic grimoire published under Trithemius's name in 1612 — a *pseudo-Trithemian* work of the Hermetic tradition, riding his posthumous occult reputation — and it should not be cited as Trithemius's own writing. It is retained here because it circulated under his name and forms part of the Trithemius *legend*.
