MAGICA. 19

The figure of a bearded man, having a long face and curved eyebrows, sitting upon a plough between two bulls and holding a vulture in his hand, carved in a stone, you should know to have power for the planting of trees and the discovery of treasures, and to make the wearer victorious in battle. Serpents are driven away from the presence of the wearer; it heals epileptics and removes every vexation of evil spirits. It must, however, be set and worn in an iron ring.

The figure of a man holding a sickle in his hand above his head, and beneath his feet a crocodile, when it has been carved in a stone and set in a lead ring, and beneath the stone there is a little root of squill: whoever carries this ring with him will be safe from enemies and, on a journey, from robbers.

The figure of a man holding a sword in his hand and sitting upon a dragon, when it has been carved in amethyst and set in a lead or iron ring and worn on the finger: spirits will obey him and will reveal to him any treasures whatsoever.

The figure of a standing eagle, when it has been carved in an aetites stone and set in a lead ring, has the power, for whoever carries it with him, of catching many fish; no wild beast will harm him, and he will be loved by men.

20 SEALS

The figure of a man holding a palm branch in his hand, if it has been carved in some stone, renders the wearer pleasing and lovable to the powerful and to princes. These things are from Hermes.

TRACTATE IV.

IMAGES, OR SEALS, of Thetel.

Thetel, a most ancient doctor, treating of carvings in stones, says that these carvings, when found in suitable stones, have very great power.

The image of a man carved in jasper, having a shield in his left hand and in his right an idol or something warlike, and in place of feet there are vipers, and in place of the man’s head there is the head of a cock or a lion, will give power against enemies and renders the wearer victorious. It is effective against poisons, and it also staunches blood from whatever part it flows.

The figure of a man having a little bundle of herb at his neck, when it has been carved in jasper, has the power of discerning or recognizing illnesses; it restrains blood from whatever place it flows. They say that Galen possessed this stone.

TRACTATE V.

IMAGES, OR SEALS, of Solomon.

A very ancient little book was found in the desert among the children of Israel, which, because many works of Solomon were found in it, was ascribed to Solomon. The title of the book was: On the Seals of Stones According to Your Name, O Lord, and According to the Course of the Stars.

The figure of a man sitting upon a plough, having a small neck and a long beard, and four men lying upon his neck, and holding a fox in one hand and a vulture in the other: you should know that this seal, hung from the neck, is effective for all plantings and for every discovery of treasures. You will establish the proof of it in this way: take pure black wool without dye, wrap the stone in it, place it among wheat chaff, and put it under your head; then in sleep you will see all the treasures of the region in which it is. At the same time it has the power of healing all the illnesses of beasts, if they drink the water in which it has been washed.
