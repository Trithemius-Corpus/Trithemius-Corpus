MAGICA. 9

The figure of a stag, or of a hunter, or of a dog, or of a hare, carved in stone, has the power of restraining demons, lunatics, and the frenzied.

You should know that the carved image of a woman holding a bird in one hand and a fish in the other has power in catching fish and birds; it must be set in silver.

An image whose front part is a horse, but whose rear part is a goat, if it is carved in some stone, gives good fortune in feeding and equipping animals or beasts of every kind, and it must be set in lead.

The figure of a woman seated with a trumpet on a horse, or of a soldier running with a horn at his neck and with a tree before him, carved in some stone, grants hunters favor in hunting, if they carry it with them.

The figure of a man with knees bent, looking upward and holding a cloth, if it is carved in stone and someone carries it with him, gives favor and wealth in buying and selling goods.

The figure of a Scorpion and a Sagittarius fighting one another, if it is carved in some stone, will make the malicious and discordant concordant, and enemies friends; it should be set in silver.

The image of a vulture with an olive branch in its mouth, carved in pyrite, if you carry it with you in a silver ring,

The figure of a half ram and half lion, when it has been carved in some precious stone, if you touch discordant people with it, they are made amiable and concordant; and it must be set in silver.

The figure of a woman in the upper part, but a fish in the lower part, holding a mirror in one hand and a branch in the other, carved in hyacinth and worn on the finger in a gold ring, renders people invisible. The ring with the stone must be turned toward the palm, with the palm closed.

The figure of a basilisk, of which one part is a woman and the lower part a serpent, when it has been carved in some precious stone, will have the power of putting all poisonous animals to flight.

The figure of a basilisk fighting with a dragon, with a human head above it, carved in carnelian, when hung from the neck, will give the wearer the power of overcoming every beast, both terrestrial and marine.

The figure of a naked swollen man, together with another figure of a well-dressed man holding a cup in one hand and a branch of an herb in the other, when it has been carved in jet, you should know has the power of freeing one from all

The statue of a man having an ox’s head and an eagle’s feet, carved in some stone, when you carry it with you, no one will speak ill of you.

You should know that a man bending his knees and looking upward, holding a single cloth, carved in turquoise, is effective for buying and selling goods.

The figure of Scorpions and Sagittarii warring among themselves, carved in some stone, set in an iron ring, and impressed in wax, at once brings it about among discordant people, whomever you touch with it.

You should know that the figure of a ram with the half of an ox, carved in some gem, has the power, when it has been set in a silver ring, that whomever you touch with it, such persons immediately become concordant.

The figure of a woman in the upper part and of a fish in the lower part, holding a mirror in one hand and a branch in the other, when it has been carved in hyacinth and set in a gold ring, renders you invisible whenever you wish. The seal, however, must be covered with wax and worn on the finger toward the palm.
