Moreover, on almost the same foundations and for the same causes, gilded and silver plates, round, square, or triangular, have now also begun to be fashioned; on them certain signatures and characters of the planets are engraved according to certain positions and configurations of the heavens, or constellations, in order to obtain the favor and influence of each planet. For it is especially consonant with reason and experience that the influence of the planets is received and retained in figures and images of this kind, marked with a definite form and with images.

Meanwhile this must be observed with the greatest care: that the planet whose influence you seek be strong and fortunate, that is, in its own house, exaltation, or triplicity, in the eastern angle or midheaven, free from the harmful rays of Saturn or Mars, supported by the trine or sextile of Venus or Jupiter, or joined with them, direct, swift in motion, not combust, and ascending in the circle of its auge.

THE ERECTION OF THE SIGNATURE
of Mercury, for sharpening the memory.

Let Mercury serve as an example. If anyone wishes to obtain its influence for sharpening understanding, wit, and memory, he should observe at what time of the year Mercury is in Gemini or Virgo, the signs in which it has its own house, and is strong and fortunate in the east or in the midheaven. Once the hour and minute of the hour have been found, a careful and skillful engraving of the celestial figure, together with a delineation of the aspects, should be made in pure silver. This is afterward enclosed in white silk and kept; and, with a few grains of the best musk added, it is sewn into a cap at the part where it precisely touches the cerebellum. It has been observed to perform its operation chiefly at night, and more evidently at that hour and time at which the engraving was both begun and completed.

In this year 1608, Mercury was found strong and fortunate around 11 May, at the second hour of the morning. Again it will be strongest and most fortunate around the 14th and 15th day of the month of September, a little before and after the fifth hour of the morning, at which time it precedes the Sun in the eastern angle, in its own house, exaltation, and the favorable trine of Jupiter.

And this is the method of sharpening the intellectual power and memory from the virtues of the heavens. From plants and animals too, I hope I shall gain no small favor among those whose memory is fragile and fluid, although they are quick in understanding, if I bring forth certain remedies for strengthening the memory. I know that very distinguished men have used them successfully; until now they have lain hidden among concealed secrets. I do not begrudge what can be of public use. You should know, however, that there are many things in them that adhere neither in temperament, nor in matter, nor in any manifest quality, but must be ascribed solely to some occult property.

Take seven hearts of swallows, still beating; two of turtledoves; dried mint, pennyroyal, vervain, eyebright, prepared coriander, galitrichum, each one ounce; flowers of rosemary, half an ounce; aromatic calamus and cinnamon, each two ounces; cloves, one and a half ounces; musk and musky Gallia, each six grains; anacardium honey and storax calamita, each half an ounce. Of these, let the ingredients that admit of grinding be reduced to a fine powder, and with fennel water let them be made into pastilles in the shape of a date-stone.

The person who will use this shall observe the following order. After the body has been purged, for fifty nights he shall eat nothing for supper except raisins with a few almonds and a little bread. His lunch

will be either a roasted hen or a young chicken, a turtledove, a partridge, or something similar; his drink will be one-year-old wine, moderately diluted. Each night, near the hour of sleep, a pastille shall be inserted into the right nostril and stopped with cotton, and he shall keep it so no longer than it takes to recite twenty verses of Virgil. After several days have passed, put into the left nostril a pastille moistened with rosewater, and you will retain everything you are about to read, or will quickly grasp it with understanding.

But anyone who wishes in this way to preserve his memory completely must abstain from drunkenness, sex, and especially from an overloaded stomach, from all satiety, green fruits, vegetables, salted meats, and everything that generates wind. At the end of the meal, prepared coriander seed is to be used. Above all, it will also be harmless if it is carefully and properly prepared. It will also not be harmless to hold a pastille in the mouth from time to time and swallow it. It will also help to throw a pastille upon coals, so that the smoke may be properly received into the nostrils and, once received, retained, the nostrils being stopped with cotton so that it does not escape; or also to dissolve some pastilles in lavender water, rosewater, or anise water, and frequently, morning and evening, to moisten the forehead, temples, nostrils, and the back of the head around the capsule of memory, and to put clean soaked little linen cloths into the nostrils. And yet this remedy has been disclosed to few.
