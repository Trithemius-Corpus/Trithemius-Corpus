But one must carefully observe, as was also warned above, that the planet whose image you wish to have carved in a suitable ring ought to be very well disposed, that is, not combust, retrograde, oppressed or touched by the rays of malefics, but in a good place of the heaven, at the Ascendant or Midheaven, with Jupiter or Venus, and in its essential dignities; otherwise the image will be empty and of no importance.

Moreover, the ancients formed the image of the Bear in this way.

They engrave two bears encircled by a serpent: the Greater Bear is contained at the head of the coil, the Lesser at the tail. This image, carved in some stone, makes the wearer learned, crafty, and powerful.

The image of the Crown has the likeness of a royal crown with many stars; sometimes it is figured in the likeness of a king with a crowned head. It is located in the northern part and in the sign of Sagittarius, and is of the nature of Venus and Mercury. When this image has been carved in stone, it gives honors and knowledge to those who wear it, and royal favor.

A man fallen to his knees, holding a club in his hand and killing a lion; sometimes a man with a lion skin in his hand. Such figures, carved in a suitable stone, contribute to victory in war, especially if it is worn in agate.

The image of the Swan or Hen, with wings spread and neck bent, makes the wearer pleasing to the people, learned, and wealthy, and drives away gout, paralysis, and quartan fever.

The figure of Cepheus, having a sword girded on, with arms and hands spread out, carved in stone, makes one cautious and learned; and when it is placed beneath the pillow of a sleeper, it makes delightful things appear in sleep.

Cassiopeia is the image of a woman sitting in a chair, having her hands stretched out in the manner of a cross, sometimes with a triangle on her head. It is placed in the sign of Taurus and in the northern part. Its nature is of Saturn and Venus. When this has been carved in a suitable stone, its virtue will be to provide health to people, to restore weak and weary bodies, to give rest and tranquility to those who wear it after labor they have undertaken, and to provide pleasant and sweet sleep.

Andromeda is the image of a girl with disheveled hair and lowered hands. She is contained by Taurus in the north, and is of the nature of Venus. Its virtue, if it has been carved in stone, is to reconcile quarrels and disputes that have arisen between husband and wife, to strengthen stable love, and to guard against very many illnesses.

Perseus is an image holding a sword in the right hand and the head of the Gorgon in the left; it is located in Taurus and in the northern part. It has the force of Saturn and Venus. If it has been carved in stone, it turns misfortunes away from the wearer and preserves him from lightning and storm; not only the wearer, but also the places where it is kept. It also dissolves enchantments.

Serpentarius is the figure of a man holding a serpent, grasping its head in his right hand and its tail in his left. It is in the sign of Scorpio and in the north, and has the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its virtue, when carved in stone, is to be effective against poisons, to cure the bites of poisonous animals, and, if its washing is drunk, to make one vomit up poison without injury.
