*Magic.*

If the figure of a man sitting upon a fish has been carved in red jasper and placed on someone’s cloth, it will make a diner who eats with his right hand unable to be satisfied.

When you find carved in jacinth, garnet, or crystal the figure of a woman with loose hair or bare breasts, and in her sight a man looking upon her face, and you bind it in a gold ring of the same weight as the stone, with amber, aloe, and polypody beneath the stone, it will make the wearer lovable to everyone; and when it is placed under his head, in sleep he will see whatever he wishes.

When you find carved in pyrite the figure of a man riding and holding a bridle in one hand and a bow in the other, with a sword girt on, and it is bound in a gold ring, it makes the wearer victorious in battle, so that no one will be able to resist him. And if someone submerges this ring in musk-scented oil, all will greatly fear him, if he anoints his face with that oil, nor will they be able to oppose him.

When you find carved in magnet the figure of a standing man who is naked, and at his right a naked girl standing, her hair bound around her head, and the man holds his right hand on the girl’s neck and his left hand on her breast, and the man looks at the girl’s face while she looks at the ground, set it in an iron ring

*Seals.*

of the same weight as the stone, and enclose beneath the ring the tongue of a hoopoe, myrrh, alum, and as much human blood as the tongue weighs. No enemy will be able to resist the one carrying this ring in his presence, whether in war or elsewhere; nor will a robber or harmful wild beast be able to enter the house in which this named stone has been. And when an epileptic drinks water in which it has been washed, he will be healed. Red wax also, when sealed with it and hung from a dog’s neck, will prevent it from barking.

Know that a soldier running on a horse, with a horn at his neck and with a tree before him, carved in some precious stone, has power for all hunts.

Know that a woman holding a bird in one hand and a fish in the other, carved in crystal, has power for catching birds; and it must be bound in silver.

Know that the figure of a man with the head of a goat in place of his own has power for acquiring riches.

The figure of an eagle carved in crystal makes the wearer rich, victorious, and eloquent.

Know that a horned animal that carries a horse beneath it, while the horse draws behind itself the half of a goat, carved in some more precious stone,

*Magic.*

has very great power for taming any beast whatsoever; and it must be bound in a leaden ring.

When you find the figure of a turtle-dove carved in pangonius, the one who carries it in a leaden ring will never be wounded, nor will he receive harm, but he will be honored by everyone, especially by elders.

When you find carved in gold the figure of a bird holding a leaf in its mouth, and in its mouth and sight there is the head of a man or of a vulture, the one who carries it will gain profit from everything he is about to buy and sell, and he will be sought out by buyers.

When you find carved in crystal the figure of the face of a lion or dragon having two heads with a slender tail, or of a man in whose right hand there is a staff and who strikes the head of a lion, and you set it in orichalcum, with musk and amber beneath the stone, and carry it with you, all will obey you and you will increase your wealth. Wax sealed with it will have the same power.

When you find carved in some stone the figure of the winged horse called Pegasus, it is excellent for soldiers, and in field battle it gives boldness and eagerness.
