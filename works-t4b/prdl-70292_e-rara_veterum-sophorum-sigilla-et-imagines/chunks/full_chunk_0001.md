Treatise I.
The Seals of Raphael.

The image of a handsome dragon, if it has been carved in ruby or in another stone of similar nature: know that its power lies in increasing the goods of this world, and in making the person who carries it cheerful and healthy.

The image of a falcon, if it is in topaz, serves for acquiring the favor of kings, princes, and magnates.

if carved, its power will be to make the person who carries it healthy and free from all sicknesses and adverse mishaps on a journey.

The image of an ass, if it is in chrysolite, will have the power to foretell and predict future things, insofar, that is, as this can fall to human weakness; for truly to predict future things belongs to God alone.

The image of a ram, or of the head of a bearded man, carved in sapphire, has the power of healing and freeing from many infirmities, from prison, and from every trouble. It is a royal image, for it grants dignities and honors, and raises one on high.

The figure of a frog, if carved in beryl, has the power of reconciling enemies and producing friendship among people, if you touch with it those who are at odds.
