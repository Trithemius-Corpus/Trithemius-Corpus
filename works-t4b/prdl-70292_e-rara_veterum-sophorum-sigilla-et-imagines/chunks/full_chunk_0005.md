*Seals*

The image of a sea-turtle, if it is carved on a sea-stone and is carried set in a lead ring, will not be able to be harmed by anyone, and will be loved by elders and by the lords of lands.

The figure of Aquarius, if it has been carved on a green jasper and someone carries it with him, provides profit in selling and buying goods; merchants will seek counsel from him, and will bring merchandise to his dwelling.

A bird holding a leaf in its mouth, and in its sight the head of a man looking at that bird: if it is carved on Pangon stone, and you set it in gold and carry it with you, you will be rich and wealthy and venerable to all.

The figure of Jupiter, which is that of a man sitting upon a four-footed chair, with four men standing there before Jupiter, and with Jupiter's hands raised toward heaven, and with a diadem on his head: if it has been carved on hyacinth and set in gold, and someone carries such a ring with him, or hangs from his neck the figure of this ring impressed in wax, he will obtain from kings and wise men whatever he wishes.

The image of a man having the face of a lion and the feet of an eagle, with a dragon beneath his feet and its tail extended, and in the hand of this man a staff with which he strikes the dragon's head:

if it has been carved on crystal, or on some precious stone, and is set in orichalcum, and beneath the stone there is musk and amber, then if someone carries such a stone with him, he will have obedient spirits and will accumulate great resources.

The image of a man sitting upon an eagle and holding a rod in his hand, if it has been carved on crystal and set in a bronze or copper ring: if someone looks upon this ring on the day of the Sun before its rising, he will conquer and overcome all his enemies; if he looks upon it on the day of Jupiter, he will be victorious in war, and all men will willingly obey him. But the wearer must be dressed in white garments and abstain from dove's flesh.

The figure of a man riding a horse, holding a bridle in one hand and a drawn bow in the other, and having a sword girded on: if it has been carved on pyrite stone and set in a gold ring, and someone carries it with him, it will make him victorious in battles, nor will anyone be able to resist him.

The image of a woman having her hair spread over her breasts, and in her sight a man as if approaching and making some sign of love to her: if it has been carved on hyacinth or crystal and set in gold, and beneath the stone there is amber, aloes, and artemisia, then for the person carrying this stone

The image of a man sitting upon a fish, and above the man's head a peacock: if it is carved on a red stone and you place it beneath the table at a banquet, no one eating with his right hand will be satisfied.

The figure of a naked man standing, and at his right a girl standing, whose hair is bound around her head, with the man holding his right hand above the girl's neck and his left above her breast, and looking at her face, while she looks at the ground: when it has been carved on some stone and set in an iron ring, and beneath the stone there is a little tongue of sparrow, hoopoe, myrrh, alum, and human blood, whoever carries this ring will be unconquered, nor will anyone be able to resist him, nor will any wild beast harm him. It is also effective for epileptics if you mark red wax with it; and if it is placed at a dog's neck, it does not permit the dog to bark.

The image of a man holding flowers in his hand, if it has been carved on carnelian and placed in a tin ring, and the ring has been made on the day of the Moon or of Venus, at the fourth, eighth, or twelfth hour: touch whomever you wish with this ring, and he will at once obey you.
