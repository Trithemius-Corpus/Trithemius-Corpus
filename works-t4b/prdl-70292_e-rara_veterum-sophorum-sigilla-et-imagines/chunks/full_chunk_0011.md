Magic.

When these constellations have been engraved on a stone, they are said to preserve the bearer’s former ranks of honors, to acquire new ones, and to confer victory.

The Fish, or Dolphin, is the image of a humped fish. It is in the sign of Aquarius and in the north, having the nature of Saturn and Mars. When it has been engraved on a stone and bound to the limbs, it makes the bearer fortunate in fishing.

Pegasus, or the winged horse, according to some is half a horse with wings, according to others a whole horse with wings, and without a bridle. It is in the sign of Aries and in the northern part, having the nature of Mars and Jupiter. When it has been engraved on a stone, it gives the bearer victory in battle on open ground, and renders him swift, cautious, and bold. But when it is hung from a horse’s neck, or placed in the water where the horse drinks, it will free him from many illnesses.

Cetus is the figure of a great fish with a curved tail and a large mouth. It is contained by Taurus and in the southern part, and possesses the nature of Saturn. When this figure has been engraved on a stone, with a crested serpent above its back, it makes the bearer fortunate at sea, renders him prudent and lovable, and restores what has been taken away.

Seals.

The image of Orion is that of an armed man with a sword or sickle in his hand. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part, and has the nature of Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars. This figure, engraved on a stone, renders the bearer victorious and makes him carry off victory over his enemies.

The image of the Ship has a curved prow and a raised sail, and sometimes is without oars and sometimes with them. It is contained by Leo and in the southern part, and is of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Engraved on a stone, it renders the bearer secure in affairs conducted on the sea and water, and does not permit him to receive harm from water.

The image of the Dog is the figure of a hunting dog with a curved tail. It is in the sign of Cancer and in the southern part, having the nature of Venus. If it is engraved on a stone, they say that it has the very power of completely freeing lunatics, maniacs, and those possessed by demons.

The image of the Hare is in the likeness of a hare, with ears and feet extended, as if it were running. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part. Its nature is Saturnian and Mercurial. Its power is to cure the frenzied and to be effective against the wiles of demons; nor will the bearer be able to be harmed by a malignant spirit.

It is established in Libra and in the southern part, and is of the nature of Jupiter and Mars. The power of this engraving is to render the bearer constant and healthy with lasting health.

The image of a seated Dog in Cancer is of the nature of Venus. When it has been engraved on a stone and you have carried it with you, you will not fear dropsy, and you will be freed from plague and from a dog’s bite.

The figure of an altar or censer is of the nature of Venus and Mercury, with a kindled fire. When it has been engraved on a stone, it makes the bearer fit for summoning spirits, for being in converse with them, and renders them obedient. They also say that it renders the bearer adorned with perpetual virginity, since it induces chastity.

The image of Hydra, or the Serpent, is the figure of a serpent having above it an urn near the head, and a raven near the tail. It is of the nature of Saturn and Venus. Its power is to make the bearer very rich, astute, cautious, and free from harmful things, and it resists every harmful color.
