We see certain things spring forth from the lap and bosom of nature whose causes, although they are wholly inexplicable to us, nevertheless meanwhile it is pleasing and delightful to the lover of medicine, and to the diligent and keen investigator of natural things, to devise probable reasons for them. If he does not fully attain these reasons, still he does not take away trust from manifest facts, nor does he disparage the effects, but follows nature and its maker with admiration.

From this it is clear, and is elicited, that those things are not altogether vain which concern characteristic healing, which through engraved images hung from the neck accomplishes natural operations by the celestial properties of the stars, agreeing with our bodies by a wondrous influence. I say that those things are not plainly vain which the most ancient investigators of nature committed to letters and monuments concerning the written word, or characters and seals, fashioned at fixed times according to the power and influx of heaven, apart from every superstition, which ignorance alone of this mystery has invented, apart from profanation of the divine name and scandal, and apart from any violation of faith and religion. Although these things have hitherto, and especially in these last times, lain hidden, concealed and covered over among certain persons,

who abused them rather than used them lawfully, since, having slipped into superstitions and not understanding the beginnings, they dared to attribute more to them than God, the creator of all things, granted and willed to be made manifest through them.

Yet I am so far from attributing any power in itself to these images, or affirming that a character or a named constellation has power by reason of its figure, since figure, number, magnitude, position, and the division of the continuous are the principles of no action, that I flatly deny that there is anything else acting and moving by whose power characters are effective. God heals human beings through the Prophets by the spoken word alone. For God is living; and the name of the living God is also living; and the letters of the living name are also living. God lives through himself; his name lives through him; and the letters live through the name. And just as God has life in himself, so also he gave to his name to have life in itself, and further gave the name to the letters.

Images or characters are therefore effective by reason of the power or office ordained by God or by nature for such a name or character. For there is no power either in heaven or on earth that does not descend from God. Thus medicines are visible bodies, words invisible bodies. Whether herb or word heals, God is in the cause, that is, the Spirit of God assisting nature
