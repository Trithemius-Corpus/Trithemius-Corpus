TRACTATE II.

THE IMAGES, OR SEALS, OF CHAEL.

Chael, one of the sons of the sons of Israel, a very ancient teacher, when he was in the desert, observed and had many figures carved according to the course of the signs and planets, and recognized a very great effect in them. And so that these powers of the images might be known to posterity, he composed a book, from which the following have been excerpted. And blessed be God, who gave such powers to the earth for the salvation of the human race. (These words are found in the very book written by Chael, immediately at the beginning.)

The figure of a man sitting behind a plow, with a long beard, a long face, and curved eyebrows, on whose neck there are four men lying, and who holds a fox and a vulture in his hands: if such a stone, on which these things have been carved, is hung from the neck, it is effective for all planting and for finding treasures. For if, when going to bed, you place it beneath your neck, in sleep you will see treasures and the manner of extracting them. It also has another power, namely to help the illnesses of brute animals when they have drunk water in which this stone has been washed. It must be carved on the day and hour of Venus.
