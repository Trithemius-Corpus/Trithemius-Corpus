To hand down: by means of it any devout cenobite may be able to exercise himself in the Lord’s Passion against all temptations. If he remains in it for a few months, exercising himself as he ought, he will gradually fly sweetly from Christ’s humanity to his divinity by the ray of love. For continuous meditation on the Lord’s Passion offers an easy approach to those who desire to come to the love of God; for when the mind, through compassion, is pierced by the memory of the benefits of so great a lover, then, growing warm with tears in desire, it is raised up to love. Therefore you cloistered men dedicated to God, who have no leisure to attend to many things, receive these few things. Although in them there may not be a continuous fire of love, nevertheless for those who persevere it will be able to be renewed.

Here ends the compendium of daily spiritual exercise, by Johannes Trithemius, abbot.
