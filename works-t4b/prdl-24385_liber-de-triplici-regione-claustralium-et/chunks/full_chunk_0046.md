Fourth, consider the negligence of your ungrateful heart, since you, who were created in the image of God and ought to have loved your maker above all things, dishonor him in many ways by your sins. You ought to devote continual service of love to Christ, the Son of God, by whose blood you have been redeemed; but you do not fear to love the flesh more than Jesus your Savior. Finally, with a compassionate mind you ought daily to turn over in your mind his most holy Passion, and you are accustomed to think of nothing less than it. Adopted among the sons of God by mercy alone, you ought to have shown yourself worthy of the heavenly calling by becoming an imitator, and still you are not afraid to lie in the lowest things through sloth. And why do I say more? Behold, you who have been marked with the Holy Spirit, who are counted with the angels, are called to the inheritance of the sons of God, are invited by all the saints to the feasts of paradise, and daily partake of the body and blood of our Savior Jesus Christ, nevertheless do not observe the commandments of God and the rule which you promised to keep so that you might be blessed. Therefore examine your conscience diligently, and you will find that you ought to do many good things which you omit, and to omit many evil things which you commit against God’s good pleasure.

Fifth, consider whom you have offended by sinning: not a man, but God; not a creature, but the Creator. Great is he against whom you have sinned, immeasurable, terrible, and glorious; at his majesty the angels tremble, and at his power all the heavenly spirits marvel. To the highest and unchangeable good, God, the beginning and end of every creature, the rational spirit ought to offer love and the desire of adhesion beyond human understanding, and should truly raise its whole self to that one as to its own center, despising all phantasms, the more easily able to rise to him insofar as

it has striven, leaving all other things behind, to unite itself to itself. For by the power of that one there is union where there is unity; nor for any other reason are things more alike more easily united with one another than because they are one in some likeness. Therefore the soul is then most rightly said to be united with that one principle of things, when it, having been made most perfectly one in itself, is separated from all multiplicity and, above all imagination, reason, and understanding, its unity alone escapes, enjoying by loving affection the unity of God, and joining, so to speak, its own center with the center of the universe. But when the human soul itself, through the evil fellow-feeling of the flesh as if with a spouse, is turned toward any animal passions, then that very unity is badly changed into a multiplicity contrary to the one alone, and it is made so much the more distant from its principle as it has been divided from itself. The soul itself contracts a great difficulty in returning to that one ineffable divine thing whenever, confounded in itself, it falls into the sin of apostasy. Now think how great and ineffable is he against whose honor sin is committed; and, since you are wholly unable to understand the greatness of his majesty, learn humbly to fear his power.

Sixth, consider what you have lost by sinning, so that, once the greatness of the loss is known, penitence may hasten and restore through tears what has been lost. What, then, wretched one, have you lost by sinning? Hear what you have lost. Made like God through grace, a companion of the angels, a member of Christ, and an heir of eternal happiness, you had been chosen; you ought to have lived and rejoiced without end in the goods of the Lord with all the saints. But what happened when you sinned? You were compared to senseless beasts and made like them. Since you refused to understand when you were in honor, you abandoned the fountain of life; by sinning you were made a companion and member of the devil, a harlot of Antichrist, an heir of hell. You abandoned life; you chose everlasting death. If you fear the sentence, change your will, and you will find remission.
