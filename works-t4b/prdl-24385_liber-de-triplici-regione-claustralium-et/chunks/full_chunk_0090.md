Another Prayer.

I beseech you, holy Father, almighty and eternal God, through the power of the most holy passion of your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ: enlighten the eyes of my heart, so that, pierced in spirit, I may see how often I have offended against your commandments; and, cleansed by your mercy from every stain of sin, may never offend you hereafter.

After Matins.

When Matins is finished, while you are still sitting in the church, or even when you are in your cell, take up for yourself the disposition of true resignation, praying thus.

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, who at the hour of Matins, placed in the greatest anguish of your most holy heart, after praying three times resigned yourself most fully to the will of God your Father: grant me, in virtue of that charity of yours, perfectly to despise my own will in all things for love of you, and truly at all times to resign myself to the disposition of your will and of those whom you have set over me. Who lives and reigns.

After Prime.

After Prime, when you leave the choir and enter the chapter room, in remembrance of your most innocent Savior accused before Pilate, you will form your disposition toward humble confession, praying thus to the Lord.

Prayer.

Most holy and most sweet Lord Jesus, who at the hour of Prime, falsely accused before Pilate, truly confessed yourself to be the Son of God: have mercy on me and grant me, through your innocence, the virtue of true confession, so that I may humbly endure the words of any who accuse me, and may not withdraw from inward tranquility of mind because of any reproaches. Who lives and reigns with God the Father.

After Terce.

After Terce you will call back to mind how many insults, how many mockeries and derisions your most innocent Lord Jesus endured for your redemption. In remembrance of this, take up for yourself the desire for the sixth degree of humility, praying thus.

Most sweet Lord Jesus, who for us were miserably scourged, mocked, derided, crowned with thorns, and spat upon at the hour of Terce: grant me, in virtue of all your sufferings and of the outpouring of your most holy blood, always to be content for love of you with every abasement and extremity; so that, aware of my sins, I may humbly regard myself as an unworthy worker of yours, and yet may in no way grow slack in your holy service.
