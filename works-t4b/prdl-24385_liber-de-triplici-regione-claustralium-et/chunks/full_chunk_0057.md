For the Lord says in the holy Gospel: “Where your treasure is, there your heart also will be.” This is the treasure hidden in the field of the Scriptures: when a man has found it, he hides it, and goes and sells everything, so that he may buy the field with the treasure. The field is this supreme region of the spirit; the treasure is divine love. When a man finds it, he ought to count all other things as nothing, so that he may joyfully possess the field with the treasure. There is no possession in the world more delightful than divine love; there is no greater beauty in this life, since whoever has this treasure of love seeks nothing further. And just as fire is naturally borne upward, so love is lifted up in this region of the spirit as though to its own proper dwelling. For the mind, set aflame with divine love, is always raised up to God by fiery affections, and, beyond every thought, is moved wondrously in the sweetness of its own beginning.

For when it has been purified in the exercise of the second region, it prepares itself for the ascent of this third region through loving affection. It pants constantly to come to him whom it loves; it thinks of nothing but Jesus, the treasure of its heart; it adores him with burning sighs because of the greatness of its love. But when the most sweet office of contemplation has been completed, suddenly the beloved slips away, and the mind, languishing with love, is called back to the exercise of the second region. In that exercise the true lover of God sees that he dwells bodily among human beings, yet in affection and desire he converses with his most beloved Jesus.

And although this anagogical action of the created spirit takes place by the impulse of the Spirit of God, nevertheless the mind, purified through the exercise of the second region, must move itself forward to the ascent and, disposed by a most fervent affection, must concur with the divine Spirit. For almighty God, who has granted us freedom of choice, will lift no one unwilling to this supreme region of his contemplation. Therefore our will is required for our own salvation; love demands the ascent. If you wish to ascend to this supreme summit of divine darkness,

you must love very greatly. The will, when purified, receives love; love, when greatly kindled, produces ecstasy. Therefore, if you desire to fly up to the mountain of contemplation, you must utterly go beyond yourself by loving God most fervently. But if you truly love God, you despise both yourself and all things for his sake alone. For he truly is the treasure of the soul; if anyone has obtained him through love, he will always think on Jesus. This treasure is acquired by loving; no one understands its usefulness unless he has received it through love. Whoever desires to have this inestimable treasure should fix his heart continually in the love of sweetest Jesus. For indeed, the one who loves has; the one who has loves. The mind that has merited to have this treasure of the love of Jesus dwells continually with him in affection in this supreme region.

That this third and supreme region of the soul enjoys the greatest freedom of spirit.

Seventh, this most holy region of the soul overflows with great freedom of spirit. In it the soul, free from the passions of the flesh, reigns through love with God alone. For the holy Apostle says: “Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.” And again: “Whoever are led by the Spirit of God, they are sons of God.” For the spirit that dwells in this region is made free from every defilement of flesh and blood; stripped of every phantom of thought, naked and alone, it is lifted into the darkness of divine contemplation. This is the true freedom of the soul: to rest in the fervor of divine love and to love none of the consolations of this world. Therefore let a person consider himself, and diligently examine his love; and if he finds himself devoutly affected, frequently raised to divine love by fiery desires even without prior thought, let him trust that he can rightly be enrolled among the citizens of this region. For the Spirit of God bears witness to our spirit, and when he raises the mind into the ray of divine love, he also makes it know the condition of its own state.
