Morning Recollection

Then enter for a little while into the examination of your conscience, and see whether you still have in yourself the marks of a good cloistered monk described in the seven articles of the second region. If you recognize that anything is lacking, do not neglect to reform it as quickly as possible, lest little by little you waste away through negligence. For it is written: He who neglects the smallest things gradually falls away. And Augustine, the holy lover of God, says: No sin is so small that it does not grow through neglect. Therefore, when your conscience has been examined and reformed in holy fervor, say to the Lord:

I Pray for Perseverance

Grant, we ask, almighty God, through the merits of the passion of our Lord Jesus Christ, and through the intercession of blessed Mary ever virgin, of all the holy heavenly powers, and of the blessed patriarchs, prophets, apostles, martyrs, confessors, virgins, and all your elect, perseverance for us in your will, so that we may continually be able to offer you service pleasing to you. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

As he enters the bed, let him fortify it with the sign of the holy cross, and with attentive spirit say: In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, I intend to lay myself down, so that, strengthened by his divine majesty, I may be able to praise him more fully by day.

Sitting in bed:

May the soul of our Lord Jesus Christ, the integrity of blessed Mary ever virgin, the sign of the holy cross, the virtue of the Lord’s passion, and the intercession of all the saints guard my mind and spirit. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Let him sign himself and lie down.

The Exercise of the Monk at Prime

Consideration II

When our devout monk has been awakened in the usual way for Prime, let him observe the order and manner of gathering himself and praying set down above before Matins.

And, fortifying himself with the sign of the life-giving cross, let him say: In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Then, getting down from his bed, fervent in holy desire, let him again fall to his knees and, with hands joined, say:

Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of your faithful, and kindle in them the fire of your love; you who, through the diversity of all tongues, gathered the nations together in the unity of faith. Send forth your Spirit, and they shall be created. And you will renew the face of the earth.

O God, to whom every heart lies open, every will speaks, and no secret is hidden: purify, by the infusion of the Holy Spirit, the thoughts of our heart, so that we may deserve to love you perfectly and praise you worthily. Through Christ our Lord. Let him add “O flowering rose,” and, if he wishes anything else out of devotion, let him add it.

At the First Signal

Then, at the first signal for any hour of the day, or also at the striking of the clock, if he has not been occupied with higher meditations, let him say: Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and forevermore. Let him add some brief little prayer, formed from the fervor of the mind, in this or a similar manner.

Little Prayer

Receive me, most sweet Lord Jesus, into perpetual service, so that I may always have you as my one good.

Another

I beseech you, most merciful God: grant me to live so innocently in holy religion that I may be able to come to you, the beginning and end of all good things.

Another

May my ways be directed to keeping your statutes at all times.

Another

Most loving Lord Jesus, I ask you through the merits of your most holy passion: teach me to despise all things, so that I may continually deserve to find you, the highest good of the soul.

Then let the general preparation for prayer be observed.
