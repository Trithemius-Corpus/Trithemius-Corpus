# Errata — prdl-24385_liber-de-triplici-regione-claustralium-et

## 2026-11-18 · region-human-condition

The Mainz print abbreviates hominum, meaning “of people”. The threefold classification includes all monks. The added qualification “good” narrows it. AI scan collation. No human verification of this correction is documented.

Previous wording: From this threefold state of good persons we take a fitting title for this work

Revised wording: From this threefold human condition we take a fitting title for this work

*Ex hoc triplici hominum statu huic operi titulum convenientem assumimus … In his tribus regionibus omnis turba monachorum concluditur.*. [Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, 4r (0013)](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00036941_00013/full/full/0/default.jpg).
