The notary should subscribe himself to this citation in the usual way, and have the execution made at vespers or at compline.

The one who is to deliver the sermon on the following day should, on Saturday, present it in writing to the lord presiders, to be examined and corrected if necessary, lest scandal arise.

The past presiders, before the day of the chapter, should give the future presiders a full account of the receipts and expenditures, which the latter should read beforehand in writing if there is need. And therefore both groups are ordered to come to the chapter on Wednesday, as was established in chapter 17 at Nuremberg in the year of the Lord 1459.

On Sunday let the signal for terce be given rather early, according to the arrangement of the presiders; and the abbot appointed to sing the Mass should vest with the ministers and, during the introit, enter the choir, as is the custom. Before the presbytery he should recite the Confiteor with the ministers; and let the Mass of the Holy Spirit be sung solemnly, at which all the fathers must be present. When the Mass has ended, let a lectern be prepared at the head of the choir. Let there be a conference for the fathers and any others who wish to be present, on religion, discipline, humility, the virtues, and perseverance. The preacher, however, before he begins the sermon,

should ask a blessing from one of the presiders, who is the principal presider, if he is present. When the sermon is finished, let them enter the chapter place and let each take his seat: first the abbots, then the provosts and priors, and the proctors of the absent, according to the placement made by the promoter.

XXII

Then the promoter, standing while all listen, should say:

In the name of God, amen. By command of the venerable lords, the presiders, I warn and require all and each of those present here to whom it does not belong to attend this our sacred provincial chapter, under penalty of excommunication and suspension, to go out and withdraw from this chapter place. The notary should remain with the witnesses; nevertheless even they may be ordered to leave while secret matters are being discussed. If those matters must afterwards be written down after their withdrawal, they shall also be recited to the notary.

Then, in the customary way, let readings be made from the martyrology, and let the Precious be added by the prior of the convent, after he has first asked a blessing from the presider, saying: Lord, bid a blessing. And the presider who makes the response should answer: May the almighty and merciful Lord direct us in the way of his commandments. And all should answer: Amen.

And in place of the regular reading, let him read from the second chapter of the Rule, namely what sort of person the abbot ought to be. Chapter 2 of the Rule.

The abbot who is worthy to preside over a monastery must always remember what he is called and fulfill the name of superior by his deeds. He is believed to act in the place of Christ in the monastery, since he is called by his own name, as the Apostle says: You have received the spirit of adoption as sons, in which we cry, Abba, Father. Therefore the abbot must neither teach, establish, nor order anything outside the Lord’s command, which God forbid; rather, his command or teaching should be sprinkled into the minds of the disciples as the leaven of divine justice. This section is often customarily read as an introduction.

For an examination must be made in the dread judgment of God concerning both matters: his own teaching and the obedience of his disciples. And the abbot should know that whatever lack of profit the father of the household finds among the sheep will be charged as the shepherd’s fault. [unclear]
