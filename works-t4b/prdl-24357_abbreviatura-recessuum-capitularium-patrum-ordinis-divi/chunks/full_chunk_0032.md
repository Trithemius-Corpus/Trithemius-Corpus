Bull of the Council of Basel on the reform of the Order of Saint Benedict throughout the province of Mainz.

The sacrosanct general synod of Basel, lawfully assembled in the Holy Spirit and representing the universal Church, to the beloved sons of the Church, all and singular presidents and visitors deputed everywhere, and to be deputed in the future, for future provincial chapters of the black monks: greeting and the blessing of almighty God.

Among the many cares by which we are distracted amid the varied affairs and business of the universal Church, that care especially stirs and moves our minds by which regular orders and places dedicated to the yoke of holiness may flourish again in spiritual matters. Therefore we willingly devote effective solicitude to this end, so that those orders and places which have incurred the reproach of deformity may be brought back to the paths of due reform, and, with the Lord continually directing them, may receive increase in these same spiritual matters and also in temporal ones.

Indeed, a petition lately presented to us by the beloved sons of the Church, the religious men of the Order of the black monks of this nation, contained that, although previously the fathers had included certain salutary articles concerning the reform of that order, nevertheless some of the subjects of this order and nation, when the visitors or provincial reformers come to them, do not allow themselves to be reformed according to the form of those articles,

taking as their pretext that such articles did not proceed from us, and that, if we should ordain some reform, they would receive it.

Therefore, on behalf of the religious men of this nation, humble supplication was made to us that we would deign to command that reform be carried out in all monasteries of the nation and order in which stricter regular observance does not prevail, according to the content and form of the aforesaid articles.

We, therefore, whose principal concern this is, and who have been gathered into one by divine providence for this purpose, that the saving reform of the Church of God may steadfastly take place and persevere in head and members, considering that articles of this kind are in conformity with written law and the Rule of blessed Benedict, and that they ought to have place and to be observed not only in the aforesaid nation but in all and singular other nations, monasteries, and places of the said order to which such reform applies, and wishing also to provide opportunely concerning the premises, command your discretion by these writings that you and each of you, as pertains and for the time will pertain to the office of each, as often as it shall seem expedient to your consciences, which we burden in this regard, faithfully and diligently exercise the office of visitation and reform in all monasteries and places of the often-mentioned order in which stricter regular observance does not prevail, according to the content and form of the said articles, the tenor of which we have caused to be noted below word for word, by the authority of the universal Church.

For we, so that the desired fruits may follow from the zeal of such visitation and reform, grant and bestow upon you full and free faculty and authority to correct and reform, by salutary remedies, all and singular abbots and other presidents, and persons of whatever state, rank, order, and condition they may be, of the monasteries and places which it shall fall to you to visit, according to the quality and gravity of the offenses perpetrated by them from time to time, and according to the canonical sanctions and the regular statutes of the said order, as you shall see to be fitting for the salvation of souls and for the condition and indemnities of the monasteries and places themselves.
