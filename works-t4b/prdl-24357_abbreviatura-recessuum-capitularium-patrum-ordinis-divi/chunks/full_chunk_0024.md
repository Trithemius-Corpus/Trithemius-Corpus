But what is set forth above concerning a provincial chapter to be held every three years, in order to remove every scruple of hesitation, we wish to be referred to a sound understanding: namely, that when a provincial chapter has been held in one year, it may be omitted in the next following year, and in every case shall be held in the third year following.

Indeed, since it would be useless to issue sanctions if their execution were neglected and there were no one to uphold them, we ordain and will that the said common or provincial chapter ought to be celebrated at one of the monasteries of the same order or religion suitable for this purpose, if such a monastery exists in the same province; otherwise, let it be celebrated in another fitting and secure place. Concerning this, and concerning the day for holding the first chapter of this kind, and the other matters to be done opportunely about it, those who have been appointed for this by the apostolic see shall make provision on the first occasion.

Let them also provide which abbots, how many, and how often, until the next provincial chapter itself, ought to visit the monastic cathedral churches, monasteries, and other places of the same order or religion. If it seems necessary to the presiding lords, the following chapter from the same constitutions of Benedict XII, concerning subventions and collections, can also be read. Likewise from the Benedictine constitution, chapter 5.

Since in the often-mentioned constitution of the general council, among other things, it is expressed that those who have assembled in the triennial chapter are to lead a common life and proportionally meet common expenses as sons in common, we, considering that besides expenses of this kind other expenses are required as necessary not only for the aforesaid provincial chapter but also for the annual chapters, decree and ordain that in the same chapters, notwithstanding anyone’s contradiction or absence, for liberties, necessities, utilities, common expenses, and other customary matters, since a burden divided into parts is more lightly borne, collections, subventions, or contributions are to be made, imposed, and assessed by apostolic authority, according to the judgment of those presiding or of the definitors with respect to such a provincial chapter, or of the abbot or other president of the annual chapter itself and of the definitors with respect to the aforesaid annual chapter.
