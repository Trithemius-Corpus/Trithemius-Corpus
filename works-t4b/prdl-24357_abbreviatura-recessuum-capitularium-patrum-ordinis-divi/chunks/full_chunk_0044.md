...the celebration of the provincial chapters of your order: if at any time it should happen that one or more of the presidents in those same chapters are unwilling to accept their office, or cannot conveniently be present at them, the other presidents, with the consent of the greater part of those then constituting the chapter, or, if none of the presidents is present, the others assembling for the celebration of the chapter, may appoint other suitable persons as presidents for these occasions only. By apostolic authority, by the tenor of these presents, we grant this.

So also that the aforesaid presidents may change the place chosen for celebrating a chapter of this kind and afterward found inconvenient for certain reasons, and may choose another place nearer to the former one, with postponement, as shall be expedient, of the fixed time for celebrating the chapter: by the same authority we grant this.

Benedict XII, in his constitutions.

Concerning subsidies and collections to be imposed in chapters.

The same, in the same constitutions. Those coming to the chapters themselves shall have respectable servants with them, and, under penalty of excommunication latae sententiae, they shall beware of stirring up or causing, by themselves or through others, disturbances or impediments to chapters of this kind.

Again, we decree that each and every person coming to the same chapters shall not bring, cause to be brought, or permit mounts or attendants superfluous in number or excessive in any way; rather, we will and command them to be content with a number of mounts and attendants suitable to the status of each of them.

Moreover, those attendants themselves, and others whom they bring, cause to be brought, or keep, shall be humble and respectable, such that it can in no way be presumed that, by themselves or through other secular or regular persons, they would inflict harm upon any abbot, prior, administrator, official, monk, or anyone else coming with them or otherwise to the chapters themselves, or staying at them, or returning from them, or upon churches, monasteries, priories, or their other places...
