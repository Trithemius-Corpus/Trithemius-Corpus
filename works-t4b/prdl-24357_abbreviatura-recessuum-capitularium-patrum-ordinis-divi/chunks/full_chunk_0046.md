In the same bull of the aforesaid council all the abbots and prelates of the whole province of Mainz and of the diocese of Bamberg of this order were summoned, so that on the day before the Kalends of March, in the year of the Lord 1417, they should appear in person in the city of Constance, in the monastery of Petershausen, to hold such a provincial chapter. This was in fact done in this way. And from that time up to the present, in the aforesaid province, a chapter has been held every three years according to the constitutions of the supreme pontiffs.

The first provincial chapter was held at Constance in the year of the Lord 1417.

In the year of the Lord 1417, on the last day of February, in the tenth indiction, while the apostolic see was vacant, in the third year of the Council of Constance, the first provincial chapter of our order, of the province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg, was held at Constance in the monastery of Petershausen. Presiding in it were the venerable fathers Ludovicus, abbot of the monastery of Tournus in the diocese of Chalon; Thomas, abbot of Ebrach; Sifridus, abbot of Ellwangen in the diocese of Augsburg; and Johannes, abbot of the monastery of St. George in the diocese of Constance.

With what reverence and discipline the work of God is to be performed.

Directing the eyes of our mind toward the reform of the order, we ordain that the day office and likewise the night office be celebrated devoutly, slowly, with pauses, not hurried through, not syncopated, and at suitable hours, as is contained in the Rule of our most holy father Benedict. To this, as soon as the signal has been heard, they shall leave everything that is in their hands and hasten together with all speed, yet with gravity, lest frivolity find fuel; and thus nothing is to be preferred to the work of God. And lest they be able to excuse themselves under the pretext of ignorance, saying that the signal was not heard, we will that the course of Matins, which we wish to begin in the middle of the night, and that of Prime and Vespers, last at least more than a quarter of an hour. Whoever does not come to the aforesaid divine office is to be punished without remission, as is contained in the Rule.

Concerning the chapter of faults.

We further will and ordain that each day, in places where there are six monks, and in other places where this is customary, after Prime, if only one Mass is said, but if there are two, after the first of them or at another customary hour, the daily chapter is to be held. In it, concerning regular discipline, after the martyrology and the reading of the Rule, before they proceed to other matters, the faults and excesses of the negligent or delinquent are to be punished.

The ornaments pertaining to divine worship are to be clean.

We will that each abbot or presiding superior, when he comes to his monastery, provide within a month for clean and suitable vessels and for the place where the Eucharist is kept there. And the books, together with the other ornaments concerning the divine office of the monastery, are to be preserved with careful custody.
