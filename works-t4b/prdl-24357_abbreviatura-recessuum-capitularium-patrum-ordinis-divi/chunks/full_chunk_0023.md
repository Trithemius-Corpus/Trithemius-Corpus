The discretion of the presidents of such provincial chapters is reserved, and their power shall continue from one provincial chapter to another, so that they may decide whether a cause of excuse, or causes of excuse when several have been alleged, are sufficient or insufficient; and no one shall be permitted frivolously to appeal from their decree on this matter, or to protest in any other way.

Those, however, who do not come when no lawful impediment exists, or who do not send to it, as stated above, their fellow monk or another suitable person as excuser and procurator with sufficient mandate, shall be bound, within a term set by those presidents, to pay double what they would have spent in coming to the said chapter, remaining in it, and returning from it, as estimated by the judgment of those presidents or the greater part of them, the sum to be turned to the benefit of that provincial chapter. The presidents themselves may compel them to pay such double amount by ecclesiastical censure, by apostolic authority, and may publish, or have published, in places of the same order or religion, the proceedings to be carried out against them in this matter and the sentences to be issued. No favor may be granted to anyone concerning that double amount, whether in whole or in part, nor may any remission be allowed. Nevertheless proceedings shall be taken against the aforesaid non-attendees, especially if they maliciously withdraw themselves either to avoid correction or so that the condition of their church or monastery may not be known, or otherwise as shall seem good to the said presidents or to the greater part of them, notwithstanding their absence.

And because this is a salutary institution and observance for the prelates and monks of that order or religion, we decree that the priors of cathedral churches of the same order or religion, or the chief persons in those churches after their bishops, who after those bishops have regular care of the monks, by whatever name they are called, are to assemble in those provincial chapters under the modes and penalties designated above. We also decree that those same priors or chief persons, the chapters, and all monks of those churches or monasteries, even if they have priories, offices, or administrations, or have their residence, in the churches or monasteries themselves or outside them wherever they may be, both exempt and non-exempt, even if the non-exempt are subject to certain exempt prelates, are included and comprehended under the coercive authority and ordinances of the said provincial chapters. They are to be compelled to those ordinances, under the penalties that the said presidents shall think should be imposed on them, by the presidents themselves; and any of the aforesaid monks of the said bodies who hold monastic benefices belonging to those churches are also to be compelled by the aforesaid priors or chief persons, by apostolic authority, through ecclesiastical censure and other suitable remedies, with the obstacle of appeal removed, to contribute to the moderate and reasonable expenses that the said priors or chief persons incur in coming to those chapters, staying in them, and returning from them.

Wherefore all local ordinaries, exempt and non-exempt, shall place no impediment against the aforesaid matters, either collectively or individually, nor permit one to be placed; but, when requested at the lawful times, they shall cause each and all of them to be ordered into execution and inviolably observed.
