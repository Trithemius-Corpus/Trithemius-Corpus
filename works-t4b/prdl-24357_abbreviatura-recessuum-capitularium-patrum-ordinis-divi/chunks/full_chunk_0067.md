It pleased us that, when any one of the presidents or visitors dies, the one who succeeds him in the abbacy should also succeed him in the presidency or visitation, without any new summons from anyone, both in the office and in the dignity of the presidency.

Concerning the feast of the commemoration of Saint Benedict.

We further will that the feast of the translation of our most holy father Benedict, in accordance with the apostolic prohibitions, should no longer be observed; but in place of the translation, the feast of his commemoration should be celebrated in the hours and divine offices, with all the customary ceremonies.

It was also decreed: A new penalty for negligent visitors.

All visitors who do not fulfill the office of visitation enjoined upon them shall be bound, for each monastery not visited, to pay as a penalty three Rhenish florins to the chapter, unless a legitimate cause intervenes.

Concerning the form of a proxy document.

And because negligence often occurs in the proxy documents of absent fathers, when they are not sent in proper form, with ignorance, as we believe, being the cause of this, therefore, to guard against those errors, we unanimously receive and approve this form of a sufficient proxy document inserted here.

Form of the proxy document.

To the reverend fathers in Christ, the lord presidents of the sacred provincial chapter of the province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg, of the black monks of the Order of Saint Benedict, to be celebrated in the monastery N., in the diocese N., on the coming Sunday Jubilate: N., called abbot in N., of the same order, in the diocese N., obedience, reverence, and honor.

Venerable fathers and lords: Because, according to the praiseworthy statutes of the fathers, the provincial chapter is coming to be celebrated, to which, as I ought and am bound, I cannot come in person, being prevented by such a cause: let the cause be expressed. Hence it is that I appoint the venerable father, lord N., abbot in N., or the religious brother, a professed monk of our monastery N., as my undoubted proxy, in the best form, with full mandate, to appear in my place in the aforesaid chapter, to treat, vote,

In faith and testimony of all these things, my abbatial seal has been impressed below upon these presents. Given in my aforesaid monastery, in the year, etc.

The twentieth provincial chapter, held for the fourth time at Erfurt, in the year of the Lord 1470.

In the year of the Lord 1470, in the third indiction, on the fifteenth day of the month of May, which was Sunday Jubilate, the twentieth provincial chapter was held at Saint Peter’s in Erfurt, for the fourth time, with the customary protests: that the fathers do not intend to admit excommunicated persons, and that neither they themselves nor the proxies are to depart before the end of the chapter, under penalty of an excommunication to be imposed by sentence.

Presiding at it were the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Gunther, abbot there; Melchior of Saints Afra and Ulrich at Augsburg; Sebald of Saint Giles at Nuremberg; and Paul of Saint Mary at Elchingen. In it it was ordained that the individual acts of each of the preceding chapters should be brought together in one compendium, as has been attempted here in the present compilation.

Concerning the old statutes that were here again renewed.

In the present chapter many statutes of the old chapters were renewed and inserted again into the recess as though anew. Among them are these. First, the form of the proxy document is inserted and confirmed word for word. Likewise, that the presidents of both chapters, together with the visitors, should arrive on the fourth weekday before the appointed day for celebrating the chapter, as above, chapter 17.
