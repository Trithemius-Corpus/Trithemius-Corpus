Nor may that division, nor any other custom, avail in opposition for the abbot himself or for any officials. We wish the visitors of the chapter to attend especially to this matter.

*On Places Not to Be Leased Out. Article 19.*

Let them not lease out places of the order except with observance of Benedict’s constitution concerning places not to be leased out, which we wish to be firmly observed, both with respect to the persons to whom they are leased and also with respect to the other circumstances.

*On Loans. Article 20.*

We also will that, when loans have to be made, likewise the constitution of the aforesaid Pope Benedict XII concerning loans and sales be provided for; and we most strictly command that it likewise be observed, under the penalties added there and others to be imposed by the aforesaid visitors of the chapter.

*On Inventories of Monasteries. Article 21.*

So that it may more clearly be seen and known who are dissipators of the monastery’s goods or restorers of them, we will that, at the assumption of office of any abbot or other president, inventories be made, as is contained in the constitutions of the aforesaid Pope Benedict XII concerning inventories.

*On Monks Who Own Property. Article 22.*

Against the vice of ownership, let what is contained in chapter 33 of the Rule be observed, together with what is contained on this matter under the same title in the constitution of the lord Pope Benedict XII, and according to the form of common law.

*On the Resignation of Officials. Article 23.*
