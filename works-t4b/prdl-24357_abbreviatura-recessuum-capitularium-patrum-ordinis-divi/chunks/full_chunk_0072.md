LXXV

We further establish and ordain that in every monastery of our province, immediately after the chapter has been celebrated, each prelate shall have two offices celebrated conventually on the appropriate days: one for the living, and the other for the dead, with the proper vigils, for the brethren and benefactors of the provincial chapter and of the Order.

On the Initiation of Excommunication.

We also will and decree that those excommunicated, and those hereafter to be excommunicated in provincial chapters for any cause whatever, even for a contribution imposed by the presidents and not paid, shall be announced in their dioceses and monasteries; and that executors shall be appointed for this by the chapter, so that they may not excuse themselves in any way from sentences and censures of this kind, or claim ignorance, until they learn to obey the commands of the presidents.

Visitors shall make charters for the monasteries visited by them, and shall also send these to the presidents at the time of the chapter to be celebrated.

We further command the visitors to leave the charters of their visitations in the monasteries visited by them, according to the ancient statutes and ordinances, and to send copies of them, either personally or through their procurators, to the chapter itself in due time. In these they shall clearly inform the lord presidents concerning the condition of those monasteries in temporal and spiritual matters and with regard to persons, so that the presidents themselves, once the defects have been known, may be able to come as quickly as possible to the aid of such monasteries. But if the condition and necessity of the monasteries visited shall require it, once the visitation has been made the visitors shall immediately transmit copies of the charters to the presidents with suitable notice.

On the Expenses of Monasteries Attempting to Separate Themselves from the Order.

Furthermore, we will and ordain that expenses which the chapter may have had to undergo in any way for monasteries attempting to separate themselves from the Order, or even for recovering those already transferred, once such monasteries have been brought back, shall be paid to the chapter, with due consideration of their respective means.

On the Briefs of the Dead.

We will and ordain that in all future chapters the names of deceased brethren shall be sent by the abbots or

The Twenty-Sixth Provincial Chapter Was Celebrated at Saint James, Mainz, in the Year of the Lord 1487.

In the year of the Lord 1487, in the sixth indiction, on Jubilate Sunday, the twenty-sixth provincial chapter was celebrated in the monastery of Saint James near Mainz. In it presided the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Henry in Wissembourg, Conrad in Wiblingen, Berthold of Saint Gotthard in Hildesheim, and Johannes in Seligenstadt.

On Diffinitors to Be Chosen by the Presidents.

It pleased all of us gathered capitularly that henceforth, in each chapter to be celebrated, the diffinitors shall be chosen by the presidents from among the fathers.

On the Age Required in a Person to Be Received to Profession in the Order.

We further forbid all abbots and prelates of our chapter to receive anyone henceforth to profession in the Order unless that person has reached the required age: a male, namely, having completed fourteen years, and a female twelve years.

On the Age of Brethren to Be Ordained.

We also forbid all the same prelates henceforth to promote their subjects, while under age, to major orders, contrary to the provision of common law and the ordinance of the supreme pontiffs.

On Novices Obliged to Perform the Canonical Hours.

We decree that all professed members and novices of our Order, even those established in no sacred order, are obliged to say the canonical hours every day according to the ordinance of the Rule.
