For almighty God, by the right of the imperial majesty of the universe, uses even the ministry of demons, both for the testing of the faithful and for the punishment of the impious. Since they are perverted in affection, obstinate and proud in their rebellion, they greatly desire divine esteem and honors; and therefore they gladly answer the invocations of unbelievers, which they are accustomed to pour out to their gods, as though they themselves were gods. They also seem among unbelievers to raise the dead to life, and they show wonders to curious people, so that, confirmed by miracles, they may render them altogether obstinate in their former error. but deceive human senses in various ways. For either they themselves enter the corpses of the dead and carry them about for a time as though alive, or, after these have been taken away to a more remote place, they present to the onlookers for viewing some likeness in the appearance of the life of the deceased, as the experience of certain people has informed us.

We believe it is clear that demons can show, in a certain false likeness, everything whatever that the saints do as miracles in truth. All the miracles of demons are either phantasms or things done by a certain natural skill. For if human beings experienced in the more hidden mysteries of nature, by a certain application of matter to form that is concealed from others, produce unusual and wondrous effects, such as forming natural eels from the dew of heaven, bees from calves’ flesh, serpents from certain roots of herbs, and many things like these, not to mention greater things, as experiment has made us tested in many cases, who will deny that demons, of subtler nature and greater experience, can perform more wonders of natural things which are evident to no human being?

In this way the holy apostles and their imitators, by the power of God together with the merit of holiness in the unity of faith, performed miracles. But unbelievers cannot perform miracles in this way, since they do not walk the necessary path of faith toward salvation. For even though very many of them believe in God, nevertheless, because they refuse to believe in the mediator of God and human beings, they have gone very far away from God’s mercy.

But all things, as the holy Gospel promises, are possible for those who believe in God and our Lord Jesus Christ, that is, for those who adorn right faith also with holy works. Indeed, just as without faith it is impossible to please God, so also faith without works is dead. For almighty God would grant us everything that we ask in the faith of Christ, if we did everything according to the faith that we owe. Believe firmly, and act faithfully, and you will always obtain everything.

Second, a human being performs miracles through likeness to angelic purity. For the more our mind, confirmed in the faith of Jesus Christ, becomes purer through love, the more, having been made like the holy angels, it will attain greater familiarity with them.
