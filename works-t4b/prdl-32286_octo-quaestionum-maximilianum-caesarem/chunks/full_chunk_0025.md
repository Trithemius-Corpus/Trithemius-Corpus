But far be it from every Christian to wish to command demons through a depraved likeness of will; for if anyone begins in this life to have fellowship with malignant spirits, in the future he will burn with them without end.

But why can good and just men not command the holy angels of God, into whose likeness of good will they have passed? I think that from what has already been said the solution of this question would easily be given, if the difference of understandings is recalled to mind. For although many men are established in the Church of God whose righteousness benefits many unto salvation, nevertheless very few in our times advance so far that, by the merit of holiness, they are either feared by demons or received by angels into the fellowship of familiarity. Hence it happens that men of our age neither command malignant spirits by merit of holiness, nor deserve to be familiar with the holy angels. But if any have advanced to that degree of holiness by which, performing miracles in the Church, the saints once advanced, then they are already able powerfully both to command demons and to have familiar fellowship with the blessed spirits.

On the Power of Witches.

QUESTION SIX.

The sixth question of your unconquered Majesty was: Whence comes this power so great in witches, by which they can accomplish so many various and wondrous things, even in one hour, such and so great things as none of the good could do in his whole life? To this question, if we wish to answer briefly, we must necessarily say that all power is from the Lord God, without whom no creature can do anything.

QUESTION SIX.

For he says in the holy Gospel of Christ: Without me you can do nothing. Hence it is manifest that every power of doing anything is granted to human beings by the Lord God and from nowhere else, whether human beings do good or do evil. Hence the most holy Father Augustine says: The will of God is the first and highest cause of all bodily forms and motions, and nothing happens visibly in this whole, most ample and immense commonwealth of creation that is not either commanded or permitted from the inner, invisible, and intelligible court of the supreme Emperor, according to the ineffable justice of rewards and punishments.

Therefore there is no doubt, Caesar, that that power of witches, by which, with the cooperation of demons, they produce wondrous and horrifying effects, is granted by the Lord God, whose will no one can resist, and without whose influence a human being cannot even move his hand. For the holy doctors conclude that neither demons nor witches can do anything, whether to harm a human being or a beast, unless almighty God wills it, consents to it, and grants the power, so that he may do what he has determined beforehand by his will. Unless God consents and wills, no creature has any power at all.
