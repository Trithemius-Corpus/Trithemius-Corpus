Christ did not say this, but that whoever believes in the only-begotten Son of God will be saved, while whoever does not believe will be condemned. He excepted no one; he admitted no condition, for he had come to suffer for all, and by faith alone had provided for the salvation of all. If faith in the Savior is lacking, then no confidence will save the unbeliever. For the holy bishop Augustine said in On Faith to Peter: Hold most firmly, and do not doubt at all, that not only all pagans, but also all Jews, heretics, and schismatics who end this present life outside the Catholic Church will go into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.

Now you see where foolish compassion drags you: measuring divine things by human laws, so that you may make God merciful in all things, you do not fear to judge his only-begotten Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, a liar. But Christ is truthful, who says that those who do not believe in him are to be damned; you, however, are rash and false, who hold that anyone can be saved outside his faith. Whoever truly believes in Christ does not permit anyone at all outside the unity

of the Catholic Church of Christians to be called saved. Whoever says the contrary shows that he is not a true and good Christian.

For this is the inviolable judgment of Catholic tradition, which the Lord Jesus himself, the Son of God, defined in the holy Gospel: that no one is to be saved outside his faith, which he himself established, taught, and confirmed with his own blood. Therefore whoever affirms, or believes in his heart, that a man is to be saved in any sect whatever outside the faith of Jesus Christ, is not a good Christian, but rather an apostate and a disciple of Antichrist; with whom, unless he repents, he should know that he will be tormented forever in hell. For he is Antichrist, not a Christian, who says that one who does not believe in the Lord Jesus is to be saved without the Savior.

Therefore, most wise Caesar, let no one appear in the sight of your Majesty, let no one feel that you adhere to him, who thinks that men are to be saved outside the faith of Jesus Christ. For everyone who believes that someone is to be saved outside the faith of Christ has denied both Christ and his Gospel. But when we say that all who are not Christians are damned, this is not a mark of cruelty but of true piety. For it is better to believe the truth firmly than to agree falsely with a man. pagans, gentiles, Saracens, schismatics, heretics, and unbelievers are without doubt to be damned with the demons, the Church of the saints testifies; and it has taken up and confirmed this judgment from no other source than Christ's words. For thus he said: He who believes in the Son has eternal life; by this, good Christians are designated in faith and deed. But he who is unbelieving toward the Son will not see life, but the wrath of God remains upon him. Here all Jews, pagans, Saracens, gentiles, and whoever do not believe in our Lord Jesus Christ are denoted.

Concerning schismatics and heretics, moreover, Christ's judgment is manifest in the Gospel when he says: Whoever refuses to hear the Church, let him be to you as a gentile and a publican. Gentiles are pagans, and publicans are manifest and public sinners; of their damnation the most certain Church of the saints has no doubt. For who among Christians would doubt that gentiles and notorious sinners, manifest as such, are to be damned? Since, therefore, all schismatics and heretics despise the holy Church, they are most rightly said to be like gentiles and publicans, who are to be damned for their offenses and demerits; and with them they will justly undergo equal punishments of everlasting damnation.
