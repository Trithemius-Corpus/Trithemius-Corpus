IOHAN. TRIT. ABBA. SPAN.

If you believe, you will necessarily say that the divine Scriptures were inspired by the Holy Spirit. But if, having fallen with apostates into a reprobate mind, you say that they were composed by human beings, you impiously show how far you are from true wisdom, wandering in darkness.

You openly show yourselves to be ignorant and rash judges, since you condemn a Scripture that you do not understand. Truly, because it is more familiar to you to despise wisdom than to learn it, it is not the purpose of this work to weary the mind any longer against the rashness of the unskilled. This one thing I want you to know: no one understands unless he receives; but because you place the filth of your affections in the way, so that you may not receive, you therefore do not have the true understanding of wisdom.

Almighty and eternal God deigns, as enlightener, to be present by grace to those who, stripped of carnal desires, burn with the fire of inward love, who desire nothing in this world except God. To them the Spirit of God brings help by his presence, those whom he has found rooted in the love of truth; and, once they have been purified from every foreign thing, he leads them into the knowledge of all things.

For truly, to those who love God, all things are open, all things bare, all things manifest; for whoever, through love, beholds in the faith of Jesus Christ the one who creates and governs all things, knows all things that are necessary for his salvation.

QUESTION FOUR.

But on the contrary, the enlightenment of God dwells very far from the slothful, the wicked, gluttons, the envious, the greedy, those given to concubinage, the filthy, the lustful, murderers, the impious, the proud, and those alien to piety, leaving them to the judgment of the avenging demon, who, driving in the sharp point of savage fire, afflicts the senses and the mind, and arms and drives the impious still more toward committing crimes, so that, guilty of a more shameful fault, they may also be liable to a harsher punishment.

Hence those entangled in vices, desires, and worldly things not only cannot penetrate the divine Scriptures by understanding, but, neglecting even themselves, they do not know themselves. Since, therefore, sacred Scripture is the perfect work of God, Caesar, we must so strengthen the mind that we believe it to be true, right, whole, solid, divinely inspired, and most abundantly sufficient for all who understand it for the confirmation of the Christian faith.

Indeed, unless divine Scripture is believed to be true, holy, perfect in all things, and whole, the Christian faith would be deprived of its foundation. Therefore every Christian must take the greatest care that, by examining the divine Scriptures more than is fitting, he not overstep the boundaries of piety, because the author of Scripture is not a human being, but the Holy Spirit; and whoever struggles against the integrity of the Scriptures of God is convicted of resisting his ordinance.

Now Scripture, by the ordinance of God, is usefully woven together with parables, riddles, likenesses, and comparisons of figures, so that readers may be exercised toward understanding, and, once its inner sweetness has been tasted, the mind may be strengthened toward piety. And so, if with purified minds we attend to the sense of the Scriptures and not merely to the words, we shall understand that all things have been ordered rightly and properly.

For it was not fitting that the sacred Scriptures, in which the knowledge of the divine majesty is handed down, should be so common and manifest as to be understood everywhere by all, since it is set forth to be understood only by the good and holy, not by evil, filthy, and polluted minds. For hence it is written: “The beast that touches the mountain shall be overwhelmed with stones.” What is designated here by the beast, unless human beings who live in the manner of brute animals, without the love of God, in the pleasures of the flesh? And what is expressed by the mountain, unless the height of the divine Scriptures?
