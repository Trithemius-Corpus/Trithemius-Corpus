On the contrary, concerning the wicked and the reprobate we read it written thus: “Why do the impious live, raised up and strengthened with riches? Their houses are secure and at peace, and the rod of God is not upon them. They spend their days in good things, and in a moment they go down to hell.” For it is a manifest sign of damnation if you live wickedly here in sins and vices, opposed to God, and yet prosper in the goods of fortune and in bodily health.

The fifth reason why the Almighty permits good and righteous people to be troubled by the wicked and reprobate is for the complete purification of the mind. For, as Scripture says: “There is no righteous man on earth who does good and does not sin.” But since God lets no sin pass unpunished, by mercy he ordains temporal punishments for righteous people for their offenses, but by justice eternal punishments for the impious and reprobate. Therefore by mercy he changes eternal punishment for the penitent into temporal punishment, which they must suffer either in this world or after death in purgatory. But because the torments of purgatory are dreadful and very grave, the merciful God, loving the penitent, has been accustomed to purify them in this life in various ways, through diverse hardships of tribulations in body and in possessions. The sixth reason is so that a man bewitched through assaults of demons may be recalled to repentance and may resolve in his heart upon the necessary amendment of life. For Almighty God does not immediately send vengeance when we sin, but waits for our amendment until the appointed time. But if we neglect to do penance, the avenger of evils comes to us when we do not expect him; and we who, while sinners, scorned to acknowledge ourselves by repenting in tranquility will at last, though late, acknowledge ourselves in tribulation. Hence the holy prophet speaks in the person of God, saying: “If his sons forsake my law, and do not walk in my judgments, and do not keep my commandments, I will visit their iniquities with the rod, and their sins with stripes; but my mercy I will not scatter from them, nor will I harm them in my truth.”

The seventh reason why almighty God permits the sorceries of demons to prevail against the good and innocent is so that he may cut away from them an excessively unrestrained occasion for sinning. For there are many poor and sick people, pleasing to God, good, devout, and humble, whom poverty and bodily illness preserve in the love of God;

if they were rich and raised up with honors, they would quickly flow down from the purpose of holiness into the depth of vices. It is not expedient for everyone’s salvation to have honors and riches together with health, nor does a longer life always make a man better. Therefore Almighty God, wishing to show his mercy toward those whom he has chosen, permits the good, the righteous, and the innocent to be subject to various calamities. And sometimes through demons, sometimes through wicked people, and sometimes in diverse other ways and by other means, he turns riches and honors away from people of this sort, knowing beforehand that they would make bad use of them, so that they may keep humility in poverty and abasement, which they certainly would not have preserved if placed in opulence and high rank. He also quite often permits them to fall into many and great illnesses, when joyous bodily health would have brought very great sickness upon the mind. Finally, he permits certain people to be driven to death by sorcery, so that the license for sinning may be cut away from them, a license they would have abused if allowed to remain longer in life. Hence Scripture says of the righteous man: “He was taken away lest malice change his understanding, and lest deceit beguile his soul.”
