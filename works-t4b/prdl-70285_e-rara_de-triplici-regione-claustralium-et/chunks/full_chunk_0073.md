I ask by that wonderful and ineffable charity with which you so loved us, wretched and unworthy as we are, that you washed us from our sins in your blood: have mercy on me and forgive me all my sins. Teach me, your unworthy servant, whom, among your other gifts, you have also deigned to call to the priestly office, not by any merits of mine but by the sole graciousness of your mercy, through your Holy Spirit, to handle this mystery with that reverence and honor, devotion and fear, with which it ought and is fitting to be handled. Grant me, I beseech you, always to believe and understand, feel and firmly hold, say and think about so great a mystery what pleases you and what is expedient for the salvation of my soul. Let your good Spirit enter into my heart, and there sound without sound; and without the noise of words let him speak in me the truth of such great mysteries. For they are exceedingly deep and covered with sacred veils. Because of your great mercy, most sweet Lord Jesus, grant me to celebrate the solemnities of the Masses with a pure heart and a clean conscience. I pray you, by the sweetness of your most holy heart, most loving Savior, free my heart from all vain, unclean, harmful, and illicit thoughts, so that it may become a worthy dwelling place of your love, now and without end forever.

I pray you, lover of humankind, most sweet Lord Jesus, by the whole power of your most holy passion: drive away from me the spirit of elation and pride, vainglory and envy, lust and blasphemy; and fill my soul with the virtue of charity and innocence. King of virtues, Jesus Christ, lover of holy chastity and integrity, I pray you, by the most holy virginity of the most blessed Mother of God and undefiled Virgin Mary, and by the purity of your most reverend body: extinguish in me, with the dew of heavenly blessing, the tinder of burning lust, so that the brightness of all purity may be and remain in me. Mortify in me, I beseech you, the goads of the flesh, and all lustful stirrings and disordered affections; and give me true and perpetual chastity, together with the other

gifts of yours that truly please you, so that at all times, with an undefiled body and a clean heart, I may be able to offer to you the sacrifice of your body and blood.

I know, Lord, and truly know, and I confess this very thing to your goodness, that I am not worthy to approach so great a mystery because of my very many sins and my many negligences. But I also know, and with my whole heart believe and with my mouth confess, that since you are almighty, you can make me worthy, for you alone justify the ungodly, and from what is unclean you can make clean.

Therefore, by your omnipotence, I pray you: grant to me, your unworthy servant, true contrition for my sins, pure devotion, and most fervent fear and reverence; with purity of heart and a fountain of sweet tears, with spiritual joy and heavenly gladness, with humility and the fervor of charity, may I be able to offer to you. O most sweet Lord Jesus, grant me your mercy in this hour, so that my mind may feel the sweetness of your most blessed presence, the devotion of the holy angels around me, and a peaceful lifting up of myself into you.

Take away from me, Lord Jesus, all my iniquities, and mercifully kindle in me the fire of your love. Do not be angry with me, O Jesus, ineffable good, nor restrain the multitude of your supreme compassion. For it is not in my justifications that I pour out prayers before your face, Lord, but in your many mercies. Take away from me, Lord Jesus, a stony and hard heart, and give me a heart of flesh and compunction, one that fears you, loves you, cherishes you above all things, delights in you alone, follows you, and enjoys you forever.
