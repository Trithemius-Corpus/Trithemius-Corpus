You were given gall and vinegar to drink and were mocked in many ways: I beseech you, by the pain of your wounds, grant me forgiveness of all my sins, the grace of holy compunction, a fountain of tears, the profound virtue of humility, and continual remembrance of your most bitter Passion, so that all that I am and can do may always serve you, and my mind may continually burn with your love.

After None.

After None, be mindful of most sweet Jesus dying on the cross; and, falling in spirit at his knees, with tears and with as much devotion as you can, give thanks and pray.

Prayer.

I adore and hold you, most sweet Lord Jesus Christ, king of heaven and earth, dying on the cross for me, wretched as I am; and I humbly give you thanks for your holy Passion and most innocent death. O most loving Savior, what shall I repay you for all the things you have repaid to me? Behold, I offer myself to you; I long continually to dwell in your most holy wounds. I beseech you, by the virtue of your most innocent death: have mercy on me as you know how; pierce my flesh with fear of you; restrain in me all disordered movements, so that, inflamed with the fervor of your most holy love, I may love you with a pure heart, confess you worthily with my mouth, and follow you in deed, as far as I am able, with a humble manner of life. I beseech you, most merciful Lord Jesus, by that anguish of your most holy heart which you endured on the cross when your most holy soul departed from your body: have mercy on my soul at its departure.

The first hour after dinner.

At the first hour after dinner, unless you are occupied with higher exercises, you shall pray to the Lord in this or a similar way, to obtain knowledge of your own frailty.

Prayer.

Lord God, whose great kindness created me, in my first parent, from the mud of the earth, so that, mindful of my humble condition, I might glorify you, my creator, in true humility: grant me, I beseech you, to know my frailty truthfully at all times, so that, when I consider how vile I am and almost nothing by my own merit, I may deserve to please you by your gift. Through Christ.

The second hour.

At the second hour after midday, let our devout cenobite briefly recall to memory the gravity of his sins by which, especially while living in holy religion, he has sinned against the Rule; and, turned to the Lord, let him pray thus.

Prayer.

Almighty, everlasting God, who by the incarnation and death of the only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, out of your mercy alone, deigned to bring us wretches, dead in sins, to life: grant me, by the virtue of his most innocent Passion, pardon for all my sins, so that henceforth I may deserve to serve you with a pure conscience. Through the same our Lord.

The third hour.

At the third hour after dinner, let the faithful cenobite remember his vows which he promised to the Lord, and especially the renunciation of all ownership, namely in possessions, judgments, and will; and, moved to compunction, let him pray to the Lord in fervor of spirit thus.

Prayer.

Almighty and merciful God, who with gracious favor sanctify and receive the vows of your servants offered to you from faithful devotion: grant me to render all the things promised to you through the virtue of humility, chastity, obedience, and poverty

with such purity of mind that, by your mercy, I may deserve to come to your kingdom promised to the poor in spirit. Through our Lord.

After Vespers.

After Vespers, let our good cenobite consider how vain all visible things are; and, with his mind lifted up to love of invisible goods, let him cry to the Lord with as much devotion as he can.

Prayer.

Almighty, everlasting God, who wished to lead us through the visible things which you created to knowledge and love of invisible things: grant me so to know the vanity of present things that I may deserve always to think with a pure mind on the eternity of future things. Another.

O God, protector of those who hope in you, without whom nothing is strong, nothing holy: multiply your mercy upon us, so that, with you as ruler and you as guide, we may so pass through temporal goods that we do not lose eternal ones.

Another prayer.

God, who have prepared invisible goods for those who love you, pour into our hearts the affection of your love, so that, loving you in all things and above all things, we may obtain your promises, which surpass every desire. Through our Lord.

After supper and collation.

After supper or collation, let the faithful cenobite put on a desire for good zeal, and especially for souls, with all the fervor of charity he can; and, praying to the Lord, let him say.

Prayer.

Lord God, greatest lover of faithful souls, who for the salvation of human beings willed your Son to die on the gibbet of the cross: grant me, through the example of the same your Son, to grow warm in good zeal, and to fear no labors, no adversities for the salvation of souls, so that, strengthened by the fire of your love, I may dare to resist, at the opportune time, those who oppose you:
