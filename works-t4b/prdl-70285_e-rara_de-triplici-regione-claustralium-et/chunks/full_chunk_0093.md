If, in this exercise, he remains for even a few months, practicing as he ought, little by little he will sweetly fly from the humanity of Christ to his divinity through the ray of love. For the continual meditation of the Lord's passion opens an easy approach for those who desire to come to the love of God, because, while the mind is pierced with compassion and with the memory of the benefits of so great a lover, it is lifted up to love, burning with desire and tears. Therefore you cloistered persons dedicated to God, who have no leisure to attend to many things, receive these few things. Although there is not in them the continual fire of love, nevertheless for those who persevere it will be able to be renewed.

Here ends the compendium of daily spiritual exercise, by Johannes Trithemius, abbot.
