Another Prayer.

I beseech you, holy Father, almighty, eternal God, by the power of the most holy passion of your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ: enlighten the eyes of my heart, so that, pierced in spirit, I may see how often I have offended in your commandments; and, cleansed by your mercy from every stain of sin, may never offend you hereafter.

After Matins.

When Matins has been completed, while still remaining in the church, or also when placed in your cell, take up for yourself the disposition of true resignation, praying thus.

Prayer.

O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, who at the hour of Matins, placed in the greatest anguish of your most holy heart, after your threefold prayer resigned yourself most fully to the will of God your Father: grant me, by the power of that charity of yours, perfectly to despise my own will in all things for love of you, and truly to resign myself at every time to the ordering of your will and of those whom you have set over me. Who lives and reigns.

After Prime.

After Prime, when, leaving the choir, you enter the chapter place, in memory of your most innocent Savior accused before Pilate, you shall form your disposition toward humble confession, praying thus to the Lord.

Prayer.

Most loving and sweetest Lord Jesus, who at the hour of Prime, falsely accused before Pilate, truly confessed yourself to be the Son of God: have mercy on me and grant me, through your innocence, the virtue of true confession, so that I may humbly endure any words of accusation against me, and may not withdraw from the inward tranquillity of mind on account of any reproaches. You who live and reign with God the Father.

After Terce.

After Terce you shall call to mind how many reproaches, how many mockeries and derisions the most innocent Lord Jesus endured for your redemption. In memory of this, take up for yourself the desire for the sixth degree of humility, praying thus.

Prayer.

Sweetest Lord Jesus, who for us were pitiably scourged, mocked, derided, crowned with thorns, and spat upon at the hour of Terce: grant me, by the power of all your passions, through the outpouring of your most holy blood, always to be content for love of you with every baseness and extremity, so that, in view of my sins, I may humbly regard myself as your unworthy servant, and yet may in no way grow lukewarm in your holy service.

After Sext.

After Sext, consider the Lord Jesus carrying his cross before all and then hanging miserably upon it, together with the other articles of his sufferings; and, inflamed with desire for the seventh degree of humility, pray to him with fervor of spirit.
