He is not a strong lover of God who is afraid to undergo death for him. For love is strong as death. One’s neighbor is loved wisely if it is not the human being but the vice that is hated. He is loved strongly, however, if every injury is forgiven him from a disposition to do good to that same person.

Here ends the second region.
