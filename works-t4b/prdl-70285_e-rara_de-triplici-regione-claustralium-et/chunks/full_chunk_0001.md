Letter of Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim, concerning the little work On the Threefold Region of Cloistered Persons.

To the reverend fathers in Christ, the lord presidents, definitors, and the other abbots of the annual chapter of the Order of Saint Benedict, of the Bursfeld Union and Observance: Brother Johannes Trithemius, called abbot in Sponheim, of the same order and congregation, in the diocese of Mainz, sends reverence and charity.

I have regarded your commands, most honored fathers, as small, and I undertook to correct the little work On the Threefold Region of Cloistered Persons, together with the spiritual exercise of monks, lately published by the venerable father lord Johannes, abbot of Bursfelde, as you instructed me. And indeed, unless your authority had compelled me, I would never have dared to set my hand to the distinguished work of so great a father, who is known to have written excellently and to be far more learned and holier than I. For what should I correct, where everything is found most approved? A good and devout man wrote no otherwise than as he lived and as was fitting.

Nevertheless, to satisfy both your command and the prayers of the author himself, I rendered the whole little work in briefer, though not fewer, sentences, and, while serving its emphases, reduced it as far as I could into my own manner and style of writing. I kept the order and matter of the earlier volume, but completely changed its form and customary mode of expression. I added many things that seemed useful to me in almost every place; I removed a few; in individual passages I altered the words, not the sense. Your command gave me the courage to do this, and the insistent request of the aforesaid father persuaded me. For you wished me to make a new work from old material: to add whatever seemed suitable to me, to cut away superfluities, and to alter what needed changing. I did what I could; I obeyed your mandates; by rewriting, I completed the work enjoined upon me according to the powers of my intellect, which are slight.

Now therefore receive, most beloved fathers, a work not mine but yours, not composed by me, but rather rewritten. If the labor of the aforesaid father pleases you—
