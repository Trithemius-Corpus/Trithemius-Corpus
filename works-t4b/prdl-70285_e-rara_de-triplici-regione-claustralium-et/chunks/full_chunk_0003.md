On the first region of the soul, in which carnal monks dwell with beasts: disordered by many passions, living sensually in the lowest region.

The first region of the soul, then, is called sensual, lowest, and animal; in it the roots of all evils are contained. Persons placed in this dark region share with beasts in their own kind, since they observe, so to speak, beastly habits, while they defer either not at all or only a little to reason, but yield everything to sensuality like beasts. For in this lowest region three sensual powers of the soul preside. The first, the sensitive power, is poured out upon the objects of each of the exterior senses; the second, the concupiscible power, takes delight in carnal things; the third, the irascible power, pursues by fleeing the pleasures contrary to the flesh.

And this lowest region is compared to the airy heaven, because, just as hailstorms, snows, lightning, and various tempests are generated in the air, so also in the minds of carnal persons inhabiting this dark region, various storms of vices rage. Woe to carnal monks who inhabit this dark region, in which there is frequent movement of crimes and a continual tempest of pleasures. These are monks given over to animal passions, who subject reason to sensuality, who live according to the flesh in beastly desires. The works of the flesh dwelling in this lowest region are manifest: fornication, uncleanness, immodesty, luxury, service of idols, sorceries, enmities, rivalries, angers, quarrels, dissensions, sects, envies, murders, drunkenness, revelries, and things like these; concerning them the holy Apostle proclaims that those who do such things will not obtain the kingdom of God. These are the marks of the works of the lowest region; these are the deadly tempests of the minds of carnal monks, who live irreligiously in religion, who despise the traditions of the holy fathers, who with ruinous presumption scorn the precepts of their elders.

These are the unclean cultivators of the lowest region, given over to vices and carnal desires, who do not care to have God in their knowledge, who serve the pleasures of the flesh, who contumaciously break the vow of holy religion that they have professed, who, despising the salvation of their souls, refuse to follow the grace of God when it calls. Therefore he delivered them over to a reprobate sense and to false judgment, so that they judge evil to be good, and falsely suppose that things which are in truth the worst are the best. These are the carnal monks about whom the holy Apostle prophesied: whisperers, detractors, hateful to God, abusive, proud, haughty, inventors of evils, disobedient to superiors, foolish, disorderly, without affection, without covenant, without mercy, filled with every iniquity, malice, wickedness, contention, deceit, and malignity. For those who do such things are worthy of eternal death, and not only those who do them, but also those who consent to those who do them, and those who, by duty of office, do not in the least oppose the pursuits of the wicked.
