*Liber de Triplici Regione Claustralium et Spirituali Exercitio Monachorum* — "On the Threefold Region of Cloister-dwellers and the Spiritual Exercise of Monks" — is Trithemius's ascetic guide for monks, charting interior progress through three "regions" or stages of discipline: purification, illumination, and union with God — a Pseudo-Dionysian scheme adapted to Benedictine observance. He finished it in manuscript in 1497; it was first printed at Mainz by Peter von Friedberg on 8 August 1498, and it is one of the central texts of his Sponheim reform programme.
