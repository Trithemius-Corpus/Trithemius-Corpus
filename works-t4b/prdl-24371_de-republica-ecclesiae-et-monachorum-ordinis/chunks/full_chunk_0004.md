For almighty God once, because of the holiness of priests, magnificently endowed the Church with temporal goods through good princes; now, because of the crimes of his ministers, he strips her again through evil men.

What more shall I say? You see plainly what the Church suffers in these times, since those who would confer anything further upon her are lacking; only those who would seize what was once conferred by others present themselves. No one now builds churches; no one now erects monasteries, because where greed is seen to abound among priests, the accustomed honor and worship is denied by laymen.

To this end the commonwealth of the Church has fallen: those who plunder her think that they are rendering service to God. For they say: Behold, priests and monks, sinners, have obtained riches; behold, with the worship of God despised, they consume them with prostitutes; they have no care for honorableness, no concern for holiness of life. Why then do we delay? Why do we not invade their temporal goods, so that such criminal pride may be humbled? Come, let us oppose the bishops and priests of the Lord; let us plunder the tithes, take away revenues and pensions, strip houses, drive off livestock, and let there be no one who can escape our hands. Thus speak the attendants of the world; thus they act, and they omit no kind of plunder that may harm priests. The cruelty of the devil’s attendants rages everywhere against the priests of the Lord; and those whom they ought to have treated with honor and reverence, they pursue, because of the offenses of a few, with hatred and robbery.

Where now is the privilege of clerical immunity preserved, when, because of innumerable declarations of hostility, no one is secure, and no one goes out anywhere in safety? Truly, fathers, it is not to be wondered at if we are abandoned by the Lord in necessity, since by evil habits we resist his commandments. Sins alone have made us contemptible before God and men. And so the state of the ecclesiastical commonwealth descends from Jerusalem into the Jericho of defection, because, falling away from its holy institution, it fell among robbers, while at the devil’s suggestion it rushed as if into a certain spring of vices. This the Lord long ago announced in the Gospel:

“Because,” he says, “iniquity will abound, the charity of many will grow cold.”

Iniquity abounds no less among the people than among the clergy, while anger rages, lust runs wild, pride goes mad, and discord breaks the covenants of peace. All things are full of crimes, and crimes are not kept even from monasteries. Where now is the one heart and one soul of the multitude of believers? Where is the fervor of the Christian faith, where integrity of morals, where purity of actions? Where is zeal for discipline, where the keeping of God’s commandments, where love for the heavenly mansions? O Christians, Christians, where now among you is the harmony of brotherly love? Where is faith, where piety, where sincerity of hearts?

O holy commonwealth, where now is the beauty of your adornment, with which you were once adorned and shone like a most brilliant star above all the kingdoms of the earth? Where is your honor, where the awe, where the reverence for your power? It has gone down; it has abandoned its former adornment; it has grown cold from the love of heavenly charity; it has withdrawn from discipline; it has scorned virtues; it has despised observance of the law. Behold, you have fallen among the robbers of crimes, among the swords of wickedness, among the arrows of vices. Behold, you have been stripped and left almost naked and half-dead, while inwardly you are stripped of your former virtues and outwardly of temporal goods.

O you priests, you Levites, you prelates of churches and guardians of the Lord’s flock, who see the desolation and ruin of your commonwealth and pretend not to see it: how will you stand before the tribunal of the just judge Jesus Christ, who will require a most exact account of the souls of all those entrusted to you? The great arbiter entrusted to you the care of his sheep; do not doubt that he will require their blood from your hands. Woe to you. Set in place to rebuke vices, you commit vices, and by your unjust morals you destroy the souls of those subject to you. O what will you answer to the supreme shepherd at the last judgment, when he begins to require his sheep from your hands? Behold, because of your sins the people collapses, the commonwealth is destroyed, the treasury of the Church is dissipated, preaching is scorned, the granting of indulgences is despised. But why should I use many words? From the sole of the foot to the crown of the head there is no health in our commonwealth.
