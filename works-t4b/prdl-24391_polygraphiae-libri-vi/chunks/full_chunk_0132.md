8 ON THE SEVEN SECONDARY INTELLIGENCES

...in a wonderful manner increased, and whatever subtlety and ingenuity is customarily attributed to Mercury was at that time increased. Moses, the most wise leader of the Hebrews, skilled in many matters and arts, a worshiper of the true and one God, delivered the people of the Hebrews from servitude to the Egyptians into freedom. At this time also Janus first reigned in Italy, after whom came Saturn, who taught that fields were enriched with dung, and was held to be a god. Around this time Cadmus invented Greek letters, and Carmentis, daughter of Evander, Latin letters. Under the rule of this angel Raphael of Mercury, almighty God gave his people the law in writing through Moses, which bears manifest testimony to the future birth of Christ in the flesh. A remarkable diversity of religions also arose in the world. Many Sibyls, prophets, soothsayers, haruspices, magi, and seers flourished in these times, such as the Erythraean, Delphic, and Phrygian Sibyl, together with the others.

In the twelfth order Samael, the angel of Mars, began again to rule the world on the second day of the month of October, in the year of the world 3892, in the eighth month; and his rule lasted 354 years and 4 months, down to the year 4252. Under his rule occurred that great and most famous destruction of Troy in Asia Minor, and there took place a remarkable transformation of monarchy and of many kingdoms, and the foundation anew of many cities, such as Paris, Mainz, Carthage, Naples, and many others. In these times there were very great wars and battles of kings and peoples throughout the whole world, and various changes of empires. From this time the Venetians reckon the beginning both of their people and of their city from the Trojans. And it should be noted that very many other nations, both in Europe and in Asia, claim that they took their origin from the Trojans; to these I have decided to grant only as much credence as they can persuade me of by sufficient testimony to the truth. Frivolous are the things they bring forward about their nobility and antiquity, wishing openly to boast, as though there had been no peoples in Europe before the destruction of Troy, and as though no one among the Trojans themselves had been ignoble.

Likewise, under the dominion of this planet Saul was made the first king of the Jews, and afterward David, whose son, King Solomon, built in Jerusalem the temple of the true God, the most famous in the whole world. From this point the Spirit of God, illuminating his prophets with a fuller light of his grace, caused them to foretell not only the future incarnation of the Lord Savior, but many other things as well, as the sacred histories testify. Among them were Nathan, the prophet of King David, Gad, Asaph, Ahijah, Shemaiah, Azariah, Anan, with many others. Homer, the Greek poet and writer of the destruction of Troy, Dares the Phrygian, and Dictys the Cretan, who were present at that very destruction and likewise described it, are read to have lived in these times.

10 ON THE SEVEN SECONDARY INTELLIGENCES

In the thirteenth order Gabriel, the spirit of the Moon, again assumed the rule of the world on the thirtieth day of January, in the year from the origin of the universe 4252; and he presided over his dominion for 354 years and 4 months, down to the year of the world 4606 and the fourth month. At that time several prophets flourished among the Hebrews, namely Elijah, Elisha, Micah, Obadiah, and many others. There were various changes in the kingdom of the Hebrews. Lycurgus gave statutes and laws to the Lacedaemonians. Capetus Silvius, Tiberinus Silvius, Romulus Silvius, and Numitor, kings of Italy, flourished under this spirit. Many new kingdoms also took their beginnings under him, such as those of the Lydians, Medes, Spartans, and others. The monarchy of the Assyrians came to an end in Sardanapalus. Likewise the kingdom of the Macedonians was consumed. Various laws were imposed upon human beings, and the worship of the true God was neglected; the religion of false gods was propagated exceedingly.

The city of Rome was built under the dominion of this spirit, in the year 1484, which was the 239th year of the rule of Gabriel himself in the order; and the kingdom of the Silvii of Italy came to an end, and that of the Romans began. In these times also Thales, Solon, Periander, Cleobulus, Bias, and Pittacus, the seven sages, flourished in Greece, and from this point philosophers and poets began to be held in esteem. Romulus, the founder of the city, first ruled at Rome for 37 years, a fratricide and promoter of sedition. After him Numa Pompilius ruled for 42 years; he enlarged the worship of the gods, in the time of Hezekiah, king of Judah. [unclear]
