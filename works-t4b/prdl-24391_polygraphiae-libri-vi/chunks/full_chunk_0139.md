28 ON THE SEVEN SECONDARIES, BOOKLET.

1508. There remain 17 years until the end of the rule of Samael. In these years there will be signs indicating the beginning of the figure of evils. For in the year of the Christians 1525, the crosses seen on men’s garments a decade earlier, which has now passed, will show their effect. But in the thirteenth year from now, lawfully summoned, you will yield place to one who does not know; greater after death you will rise again for me in the third, unless perhaps a mist conceals you.

In the twentieth order Gabriel, angel of the Moon, will receive the government of the world in the year of the world 6732, in the fourth month, on the fourth day of the month of June, in the year of the Christians 1525; and he will govern the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 7086 and the eighth month, and, from the Lord’s nativity, likewise to 1879 years and 11 months. The future sequence of this revolution requires prophecy.

I have not written these things assertively, most sacred Caesar, as matters to which, to the injury of the Orthodox faith, credence should in any way be given. There are, however, some who have reckoned lunar months in these matters; if you think they should be agreed with, those things which I have written otherwise must be changed. With my own hand also I protest, and with my mouth I confess, that in all these matters I believe nothing and admit nothing except what the Catholic Church believes; the rest I reject and despise as vain, fabricated, and superstitious.

Praise be to almighty God.
