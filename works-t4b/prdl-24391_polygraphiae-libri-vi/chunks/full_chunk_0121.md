If anyone wishes to fashion a new alphabet for himself according to his own judgment, he should know that two things in particular must be observed, though I do not always find them preserved in the preceding examples. The first is that he form characters easy to handle; the simpler they are, the better they will be. The second is that he so arrange the individual letters that, when joined into words, they present a beautiful and graceful appearance. I add a third, not previously promised: if you wish the writing to remain secret, make some letters of the alphabet double, after the manner of the Hebrews.
