Therefore we must warn, as necessary, that anyone who wishes to copy this work of our Polygraphia, either in whole or in part, should first take diligent care not to depart in any respect from our archetype, nor skip, neglect, or transpose any word whatever. For truly, if he omits a single letter or word in essential matters, if he changes a word contrary to its nature, he has made the whole procedure of the work useless. Therefore let him either copy it properly, or not copy it.
