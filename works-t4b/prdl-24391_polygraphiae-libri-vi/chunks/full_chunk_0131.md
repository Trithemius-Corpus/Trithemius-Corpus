In the eighth order thereafter, Orifiel, the angel of Saturn, again began to govern the world on the twenty-sixth day of the month of June, in the year from the beginning of the world 2480 and four months; and for 354 years and four months he governed the world a second time, up to the year of the world 2834 and the eighth month. Under the governance of this angel, peoples were multiplied, the earth was divided into regions, many kingdoms were established, the Tower of Babel was built, the confusion of languages occurred, and human beings were scattered over the whole earth. People also began to cultivate the earth more carefully, to establish fields, sow grain, plant vineyards, set trees in the ground, and acquire more diligently whatever pertains to food and clothing. From that time distinction of nobility first began to arise among human beings, when men surpassing the rest in character and ability received marks of honor from the great ones of the earth according to their merits. From then on the whole world first began to come into human knowledge, since, as peoples multiplied everywhere, many kingdoms were arising and various differences of languages followed.

In the ninth order, Anael, the spirit of Venus, again began to govern the world on the twenty-ninth day of October, in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 2834, in the eighth month; and he presided for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the world 3189. In these times human beings, delivering the true God to oblivion, began to worship and honor the dead and their statues as God; this error occupied the world for more than two thousand years. Incantations and evil arts were devised in those times by Zoroaster, first king of the Bactrians, and by various others; Ninus, king of the Assyrians, defeated him in war.

In the tenth order, Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, again began to govern on the last day of the month of February, in the year of the founding of heaven and earth 3189; and he governed, as usual, for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the world itself 3543 and the fourth month. These were joyful times, which could truly be called golden, in which the abundance of all things both brought increase to the human race and gave the world its greatest adornment. Likewise at this time God gave Abraham the law of circumcision and first promised the restoration of human salvation through the incarnation of his only-begotten Son. Under the rule of this spirit, the patriarchs, founders of justice, shone forth, and the just were divided from the impious by their own will and work. In these times Jupiter also flourished in Arcadia, called Lisanias of Heaven and Son of God, a king who first gave laws to the Arcadians, made them much more civilized in their customs, taught the worship of God, built temples, instituted priests, and procured many useful things for the human race; for these reasons he was called Jupiter by them, and after death was regarded as a god. his origin from the sons of Heber, namely from Jerah, as the histories relate. Prometheus also, son of Atlas, under the rule of this angel, was said to have made human beings, because from rude people he made them learned, humane, gentle, and polished in manners and knowledge. By art he made images move of themselves. He first devised the use of the ring; likewise he invented the scepter, the diadem, and royal insignia. Other Jovian persons also flourished in these times, very wise men and women, who by their ability handed down many useful inventions to the human race; and because of the greatness of their wisdom, when dead they were thought to have been numbered among the gods. These were, namely, Phoroneus, who first established laws and judgments among the Greeks; Sol, Minerva, Ceres, Serapis among the Egyptians, and many others.

In the eleventh order, Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, again took up the government of the world on the first day of the month of July, in the year of the world 3543 and the fourth month; and he presided for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the creation of heaven and earth 3897 and the eighth month. In these times, as is clearly evident from the histories of the ancients, human beings devoted themselves more to wisdom. Among them were the last very famous figures: Mercury, Bacchus, Omogyrus, Isis, Inachus, Argus, Apollo, Cecrops, and many others, who by their inventions benefited both the world then and the later world.
