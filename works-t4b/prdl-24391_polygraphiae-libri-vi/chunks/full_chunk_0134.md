14 ON THE SECONDARY POWERS

The civil wars, which lasted forty years, greatly wore down the Romans. Three suns appeared at Rome, and not for long, but soon they were reduced into one. A few years later, Gaius Julius Caesar seized the rule of the Romans; after him Octavian Augustus enlarged it and united Asia, Africa, and Europe into one monarchy. He ruled for thirty-six years, and through him God bestowed peace on the whole world.

In the year 751 from the founding of the city, the forty-second year of Octavian Caesar Augustus, in the 245th year and eighth month of the prescribed rule of Orifiel, spirit of Saturn, on the twenty-fifth day of the month of December, JESUS CHRIST, the Son of God, was born of the Virgin Mary in Bethlehem of Judea.

Notice how beautiful is the ordering of divine providence. For the world was first created under the rule of Orifiel, and under his third rule it was also mercifully redeemed, restored, and renewed, so that such harmony of events seems to lend no small credibility to this account of the governance of the world by the seven planetary spirits. For in the first rule of Orifiel there was one monarchy over the whole world; under the second, as we said above, it was divided into many. Again under the third, as is seen, it was called back into unity, although, if we measure rightly, it is also clear that in the second rule of Orifiel there was one monarchy of the whole world, when the tower was being built at Babylon. From this point the kingdom of the Jews was taken away; the victims and the continual sacrifice ceased; nor will freedom be restored to the Jews before the third revolution

In those times many of the Jews and Gentiles took up the Christian religion, while the very simple and rustic preached it, men whom no human instruction, but the Spirit of God, had enlightened. Then the world began to be called back to the first innocence of simplicity, with Orifiel, the spirit of Saturn, governing it in each case. Heavenly things were mingled with earthly things. Many Christians also were cruelly killed by the rulers of the world for the faith that they preached. Around the end of Orifiel’s rule, Jerusalem was destroyed by the Romans, and the Jews were scattered into every land; 1,100,000 were killed and 80,000 sold. The rest fled, and thus the Romans utterly destroyed Judea.

Sixteenth in order, after Orifiel, Anael, the spirit of Venus, resumed the rule of the whole world for the third time, on the last day of January, in the year 5315 from the creation of heaven and earth, but in the year 109 from the birth of Christ. He governed the world for 354 years and four months, until the year of the world 5669 and four months, but until the end of the 463rd year of the Lord’s Nativity. And it should be noted that through almost the whole period of this rule of the angel of Venus, the Church of the Christians flourished and prevailed amid persecutions, countless thousands of human beings being killed for the faith of Christ. Finally, in these times several heresies began to spring up in the Church, and not without the labor and blood of good men.
