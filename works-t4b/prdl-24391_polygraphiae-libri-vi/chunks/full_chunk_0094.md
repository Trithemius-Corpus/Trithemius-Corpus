With the help of our Lord Jesus Christ, Savior of faithful souls, who lives in the sacred triune unity with the Father and the Holy Spirit, threefold in persons and one God in substance, incomprehensible, almighty, and everlasting, from whom, in whom, and through whom every good, beautiful, and true thing proceeds, we have brought the second book of this Polygraphia to its end. To him be praise, honor, power, and glory, who through the innocence of his death freed our race from the ruin of eternal perdition, and by perpetual mercy reopened the entrance to eternal life for all who believe in him with a pure conscience. Line by line we have set down three hundred and eight paraphrases, deliberately avoiding a more prolix rambling digression. For at the beginning of this night-work it seemed enough to us if we had given those who desire greater things a reason for seeking them out, since both time and method were lacking to us for them. Farewell, friendly reader. From Peapoli, on the next-to-last day of the month of February, in the year of the Lord 1508.
