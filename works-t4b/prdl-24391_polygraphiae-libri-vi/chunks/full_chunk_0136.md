18. On the Seven Secondary Intelligences

Comets, earthquakes, rains of blood. Merlin, born in Tumbe, foretold marvelous things at the beginning of this rule. Arcturus, whom people commonly call Arcus, the most famous king of Britain, conquered the barbarians, restored peace to the Church, fought many battles victoriously, enlarged the faith of Christ, and subjected all Gaul, Norway, Dacia, and many provinces to his empire. He was the most glorious of all the kings of his time; after he had accomplished many outstanding deeds, he appeared nowhere, though the Britons waited for many years for him to return. Concerning him, minstrels once produced marvelous songs. In his reign England was in flower, and thirty kingdoms served him.

In these times the orders of monks in the Church of God began to multiply. Theodoric, the Arian king of the Goths, possessed all Italy and killed the consul Boethius. Everything was full of disturbance, both the empire and the Church. In the East the emperors Zeno and Anastasius were Arians; in Italy Theodoric and his successors, and in Africa Honorius, king of the Vandals, exercised no small tyranny. At last Clovis, king of the Franks in Gaul, became a Christian, overcame the Goths, and imposed peace on affairs, though not everywhere in the world, in the time of St. Benedict, in the five-hundredth year of the Christians, or thereabouts, at the beginning of the rule of this angel Zachariel of Jupiter. It is proper to his spirit to change empires and kingdoms; histories show in many ways that this happened in this revolution, and that Raphael the angel could not do it himself.

It was almost submerged by the waves of the sea, and more than 100,000 people perished. The Tartars laid waste Hungary and Poland, after first overcoming Greater Armenia and many regions. In the year of the Christians 1244, a certain Jew at Toledo, while digging, found a book in which it was written: “In the third world Christ will be born of the Virgin Mary, and will suffer for the salvation of human beings.” He soon believed and was baptized. The “third world” was the third revolution of the spirit of Saturn, of which it was spoken above; at its beginning Christ was born of the Virgin.

The Roman pontiffs, depriving Frederick of the empire, said that it had been vacant for twenty-eight years. [unclear] William, count of Holland; Conrad, son of Frederick; Alfonso, king of Castile; and Richard, count of Cornwall, brother of the king of England; and evils were multiplied on earth.

At this time, around the year of the Lord 1260, the confederation of the Swiss first began: a people small in number, which grew with time and killed many nobles, driving others from their borders. They are warlike men, whose commonwealth is known to all the peoples of Germany. In the year of the Christians 1273, Rudolph of Habsburg was made emperor by the election of the princes, and he ruled for eighteen years: a man prudent and excellent in every respect, from whom all the later dukes of Austria descended.
