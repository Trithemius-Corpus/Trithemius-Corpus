Zachariel.

Third, Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, began to govern the world in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 780, month 8, that is, on the 26th day of the month of October, and he governed the world for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the created world 1063 inclusive. Under his direction human beings first began to claim lordship over one another, to practice hunting, to make tents, and to adorn their bodies with various garments. A great division arose between the good and the wicked: the good calling upon God, as did Enoch, whom the Lord took away; the wicked, however, running after the enticements of the flesh. Under this Zachariel's rule human beings also began to live more civilly, to endure the laws of their elders, and to grow tame from their former wildness. Under his leadership too Adam, the first man, died, leaving to all posterity the testament of necessary death. Finally, at this time various inventions and arts of human beings emerged, as the historiographers have set forth more clearly.

Raphael.

Fourth, the ruler of the world was Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, who began in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 1063, on the 24th day of February, and presided for 354 years and four months; and his dominion lasted up to the year of the world 1417 and the fourth month. In these times writing was first invented, and letters were first devised in the likeness or form of trees and plants; yet afterwards, with time and cultivation, they took on a more refined ornament, and the peoples changed the appearance of the characters according to their own judgment.

The use of musical instruments also began to multiply under the rule of this Raphael, and the exchange of merchandise was first invented among human beings. The rude boldness of navigation too was first ventured in these times, and many other things of this kind.

Samael.

Fifth, the governor of the world was Samael, the angel of Mars, who began on the 26th day of the month of June, in the year of the world 1417. He governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, up to the year 1771, month 8. Under his rule human beings imitated the nature of Mars. Likewise, under this Samael's direction, the universal flood occurred in the year of the world 1656, as is clearly evident from the history of Genesis. And it should be noted that the ancient philosophers handed down that whenever Samael, the spirit of Mars, is ruler of the world, a notable alteration of monarchy arises. Religions and sects vary, laws are changed, and principalities and kingdoms are transferred to outsiders, as we can easily find in the histories in order. But Samael does not manifest this disposition of his character immediately from the beginning of his rule, but after he has passed the midpoint of his governance. The same is to be understood of the spirits of the other planets, as is clear in the histories: they all, according to the properties of the nature of their stars, influence and act upon the lower things of this world.

In these times human beings were again multiplied, and they founded various cities. And it should be noted that the Hebrews say the flood occurred in the year of the world 1656, under the rule of Mars. But the Seventy interpreters, Isidore, and Bede confirm that it occurred in the year 2242, under the governance of the spirit of the Moon, Gabriel; and this seems to me more consonant with the truth for a reason which it is not the present time to set out.

Michael.

Seventh, the governor of the world was Michael, the angel of the Sun, who began on the 24th of February, in the year of the world, according to the common reckoning, 2126, and he governed the world for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the age of the world 2480, month 4. Under the rule of the angel of the Sun, just as the histories agree with the truth, kings first began to exist among mortals; among them Nimrod was the first who, through lust for domination, seized tyrannical command over his companions. The worship of gods too was instituted by human foolishness, and weak men began to adore their princes as gods. Various arts were also invented by human beings at this time: namely mathematics, astronomy, and magic; and worship of the one God began to be offered to different created things, and knowledge of the true God gradually fell into oblivion through human superstition. In these times too architecture was invented, and human beings began to use more refined customs and institutions.
