*Liber Octo Quaestionum ad Maximilianum Caesarem* — "A Book of Eight Questions to Emperor Maximilian" — is Trithemius's reply to eight questions the Emperor set him (by his own account at the castle of Boppard): on faith and reason, on the faith necessary for salvation, on the miracles worked by unbelievers, on witches and malefic magic, and on related theological and natural-philosophical points. Composed in 1508 and printed in 1515, it is his principal statement on witchcraft and the work through which his demonology passed into early-modern learned culture. The eight questions are enumerated at the head of the book and answered in turn.

This is the **1550 Frankfurt edition** (BSB scan) — a later reprint of the text first printed at Oppenheim in 1515.
