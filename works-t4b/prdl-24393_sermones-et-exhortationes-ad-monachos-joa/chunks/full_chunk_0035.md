That one must pray without interruption.

...to deserve to obtain mercy. For by holy prayer the conscience is cleansed through penitence; and whoever raises his heart to the Lord in true purity, his prayer does not return empty. O dearest brothers, if you knew in secret how pleasing to God are the prayers of a pure mind, and how much benefit they confer upon the soul, you would let no time pass without prayer. For if our prayer is pure and burning in the fervor of the Spirit, it is a most faithful messenger to God; it speaks to God on our behalf and obtains all things that are necessary for our salvation.

Therefore I admonish you and exhort you in true charity to pray without interruption, and not to cease even for a moment from the fervor of the Holy Spirit. Let no time be wholly empty for you of mental prayers. In the fear of the Lord, I beg you, always be watchful and solicitous, so that you may be able bravely to conquer and overcome the temptations that threaten. But when foul thoughts begin to knock at the mind, run back to the practice of prayer, and they will have no power to prevail against you. For prayer is a great fortification of the soul; at its presence all the contrivances of the opposing side fail. Through very pure prayers all useful things are granted us by God, and all adversities are driven far away. Good men are kindled by pure prayers in the love of God, and whatever lighter stains meet them, they cleanse by praying.

By holy and continual prayers the world is conquered; deadly sins are healed by devout supplications; the devil is overcome by tears and weeping; and he whom a thousand cohorts are not enough to fight is overcome by the prayer of one poor man. As wax melts and is dissolved before the face of fire, so at the fervor of pure prayer every power of demons is broken and weakened. If a foul thought, sent by a demon, enters your mind, drive it away at once, making the sign of the cross on your forehead and saying: “Save me, Jesus Christ, mediator of God and men, and drive far from me all the wickedness of the ancient enemy by your most innocent passion.” What can be shorter than this prayer, or what more powerful? For as soon as it has been poured forth with fervor of mind against the presence or suggestion of demons, every contrivance of iniquity withdraws.

The power of pure prayer is very great, since it penetrates heaven and by its constancy overthrows the powers of the air. Prayer illuminates the mind, purifies the affection, obtains delightful pardons, and strengthens the spirit in the love of God against all adversaries. Nothing so strongly draws the mind away from earthly things and raises it to heavenly love; nothing so powerfully exterminates the pleasures of the flesh; nothing so illuminates the understanding; nothing likewise confounds the adversary by the insistence of truth, as pure prayer poured out to God with the fervor of divine love and adorned with the good testimony of conscience.

If you desire to be terrible to demons, if you desire to be a friend of God, keep your conscience pure and never interrupt spiritual prayers. For devout prayer can do very much, if it is offered with uprightness of faith and purity of life. Prayer puts demons to flight, weakens the power of evil spirits, heals souls languishing in tepidity, and strengthens the weak and infirm in the fear of the Spirit. Pure prayer brings a person near to God, and makes the servant whom God accepts to be his friend. Pure prayer, fervent in the love of God, penetrates heaven, wonderfully illuminates the understanding of the one praying, closes hell, opens paradise, and brings in the souls of the faithful.

Its truth is not measured out, for in the holy Gospel he promises, saying: “Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you.” For everyone who asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks it will be opened. Which of you has ever prayed, converted to the Lord with his whole heart, and has not been heard? Or who has called upon the Lord, and the Lord has withdrawn himself from him? For Scripture says: “Know that the Lord will hear your prayers, if you remain and persevere in fasting and prayers in the sight of the Lord.”

Be mindful of Moses, the servant of the Lord, who brought down Amalek, proud in his strength, not by fighting with iron but by praying with holy supplications. For the constant prayer of a just man is very powerful, and the prayer of humility penetrates the clouds. Elijah was a man subject to suffering like us, and he prayed with prayer that it might not rain upon the earth, and it did not rain for three years and six months. And when he prayed again, heaven gave rain and the earth gave its fruit. By prayer and tears King Hezekiah obtained fifteen additional years of life, because the Lord changed the sentence by prayers. By prayers holy men shone with miracles, and by supplicating the Lord they called the dead back to life. All things are subject even to the purity of prayer, and there is nothing among us so difficult that devout prayer is not able equally to obtain it.
