For whoever perfectly loves the Lord God with his whole heart, and preserves his conscience clean and pure from every pollution of sin, always shows sincere charity to his neighbor. But the one whom envy burns, or whom hatred stirs up against a brother, is plainly shown neither to love God truly nor to have a conscience pure from sin. Therefore everyone who pursues his brother with hatred and indignation is neither a true monk nor worthy of the name Christian, but by merit and way of life a minister and companion of demons.

Far, then, from this holy congregation of yours let hatreds depart. Let there be among you no destructive contests of contention. Let no zeal of bitterness be found here, and let no word of displeasing elation be heard. Let the concord of brotherly unity flourish here continually; let the innocence of Christian purity adorn the consciences of each, so that we may live in this brief time in such a way that after death we may glory with Christ in eternity.

Omnia .xxiiij.

Nothing is so just, nothing so holy, nothing in human actions can be so honorable, as always to love God above all things with the whole heart, the whole mind, and the whole strength, and for love of him to fear neither death nor any kinds of punishments. There is no greater cause for any action in us than divine love; for since God is the author of all things, nothing is more justly loved than he, and since he is the greatest of all goods, nothing is loved more fruitfully than he.

God first loved us: we were the lowest, he the greatest; we were little, he almighty; we were weak. Therefore, my brothers, let us love the Lord God with our whole heart, whole soul, and whole strength, by whom we are most sweetly loved, who not only made us in his image and likeness, but, what is the proof of the greatest love, mercifully called us to the kingdom of eternal happiness through the teaching and death of his only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.

No bodily mother ever loved her only son so tenderly, sweetly, and strongly as almighty God loved us, who not only wished to become man for our instruction, but also deigned to undergo death in the flesh he had assumed for our salvation. No greater love ever existed, if we rightly consider who it was who endured such things for us. For love of us, he who was free from the debt of death dies voluntarily; and he who was always immune from every sin, innocent and just, is willingly condemned for our sins.

By the most just reasoning, then, we are commanded to love the Lord our God with our whole heart, who created us from his goodness alone when we did not exist, and mercifully delivered us when we had perished. The love of God is the greatest of all virtues, because whatever good, whatever virtue is done in this world, if it is not done from charity toward God, it is deprived of reward. Charity toward God purifies the conscience from every fault, since he who truly loves the Lord his God with his whole soul, his whole heart, and all his strength undoubtedly grants no place in his conscience to vices. For the heart which the love of God occupies is entered by no uncleanness of vices.

The love of God is an unfailing treasure: whoever possesses it is richer than all the wealthy of the world; whoever lacks it is more wretched than all the poor. Hence the holy apostle of Christ says: To those who love God, all things work together for good, because nothing harms those who love him.

Happy is the person in whose heart, besides the love of Christ, no bond of earthly affection dwells; who sighs after the remembrance of no human being; in whose conscience purity resides, and divine love always burns most ardently. Love him, my brothers, who for love of you descended into the womb of the Virgin. Always carry him in your hearts, who by his death opened heaven for you. Let the love of Christ, strong as death, be in you at all times, brothers, so that your life may be conformed to Christ's teaching, and, if ever it becomes necessary, you may not fear to die for love of him, just as he first deigned calmly and kindly to undergo death for us.
