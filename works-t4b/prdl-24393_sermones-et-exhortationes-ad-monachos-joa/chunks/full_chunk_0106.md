The love of Christ teaches one truly to despise the world, and to reckon all its delights and all the consolations of the flesh as nothing. The love of Christ quickly suppresses the illicit motions of the flesh and brings the purest chastity into the mind. The love of Christ sweetly lifts the mind with desire toward heavenly things, and, separated from earthly things, preserves it in spiritual exercise.

The love of God lightens everything hard and rough, because where Christ is truly loved, tribulations and any adversities at all are counted as delights. The love of God is always diligent in good works and is never idle. The love of God despises present things and strongly raises the mind to eternal things. The love of God neither seeks honors nor pursues riches, but, joined to Christ by desire, covets absolutely nothing except him. The love of God covers a multitude of sins and remits all offenses to the penitent. The love of God at once justifies the impious; and the more love grows, the more perfectly, swiftly, and strongly perfection rises up.

The love of God is a tower of strength, which all ministers of diabolical wickedness cannot approach. The love of God is the consummation of all righteousness and the end of monastic discipline. The love of God perfects the monk and makes him consummate in all good works. The love of God is the sweet yoke of inward sweetness, by which every foulness of earthly corruption is driven from the mind. The love of God honors souls and makes them pure and immaculate from all stains. The love of God perfectly illumines the mind in every knowledge of heavenly honors, and purges its affection from all unclean vanities.

The love of God makes those who truly possess it most learned, and wonderfully instructed in every Scripture. The love of God renders the monk fearsome to all enemies, and grants angelic power to those who persevere. The love of God defends a man in dangers, because whoever truly loves God fears no hostile things. The love of God makes the one who possesses it secure and safe from demons at the hour of death, and joins the soul to the heavenly companies. The love of God leads its possessor back to the heavenly fatherland, and grants eternal glory to those who love God.

The love of God is the greatest of all honorable works and the one foundation of all virtues in the faith of Christ. The love of God is the best ointment, in which all wounds of languishing souls are healed. The love of God is an abundant remedy for poverty and want, and a consolation more effective than every possession of riches. The love of Christ far surpasses all teaching of this world, and instructs those who possess it most perfectly in every knowledge necessary for salvation. The love of God prepares a delightful dwelling in the heart of man for the Holy Spirit, and preserves those who persevere in all purity.

The love of God is the swiftest of all teachers, because what carnal persons learn over time and with much study, this the Holy Spirit gives in a moment to those who love God. The love of God gives knowledge to little ones and makes rustics more learned than the educated. The love of God is stronger than all things and by its power rules over all things. The love of God commands demons by a single word, and drives them by the sign of the cross from the bodies of the possessed. The love of God bestowed eternal blessedness on the holy spirits in heaven, whom the grace of the Creator confirmed in love.

The love of God beatifies honorable men baptized in the faith of Jesus Christ in eternal happiness, because no one could ever reach the fellowship of angels unless the persevering love of God were proved to have inflamed him. For the love of God alone makes works both good and meritorious, because God receives from us only that gift of a good work which the flame of charity has made worthy in his sight. Whatever is done outside charity is judged unworthy in God’s sight.

Now attend, my brothers, to our admonitions, and consider carefully how necessary it is for you to love God above all things, if you desire to reach his kingdom and to triumph happily with him forever. Let none of you deceive his soul with empty words, so that he believes he truly loves God while he either scorns or neglects to keep his commandments. You have heard the Lord Jesus saying in the holy Gospel: “Whoever loves me will keep my words.” And, as the blessed pope Gregory says, the performance of the commanded work is the proof of the love that has been enjoined. For then we truly love the Lord our God with our whole heart, when, having despised worldly pleasures, we perfectly keep all his commandments even unto death.
