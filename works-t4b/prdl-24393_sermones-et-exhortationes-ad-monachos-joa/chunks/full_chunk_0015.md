What constitutes the armor of spiritual soldiery.

To oneself he should render holiness; to his fellow monks, love; to the enemy, patience; to the poor, mercy; to all, brotherly charity in the Lord. Therefore the most chaste one says: “We are debtors, brothers, not to the flesh, that we should live according to the flesh. For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the flesh, you will live.” Rightly, therefore, the learned soldier distributes the one thing that is his own.

After this we are commanded to have our feet shod in preparation for the gospel of peace, so that the good which we ourselves have come to know by the Lord’s gift we may also gladly communicate to others. For this warfare of ours, brothers, according to which we are commanded to serve the Lord as soldiers, is evangelical; under Christ all are enrolled.
