### scribes-002

- Previous: unknown to us by name
- Local correction: not unknown to me
- Latin (expanded/normalized): Fuit in quodam cenobio nostri ordinis mihi non incognito frater quidam devotus
- Source: BSB 9v (0020), [institutional image](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00037424_00020/full/full/0/default.jpg).
- Basis: The scan clearly has mihi non incognito. There is no by name; the adjective attaches to the monastery.

### scribes-003

- Previous: my pen trembles, looking again to Jesus
- Local correction: my sight trembles; likewise my pen
- Latin (expanded/normalized): tremet [intuitus] iterum calamus
- Source: BSB 3v (0008), [institutional image](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00037424_00008/full/full/0/default.jpg).
- Basis: The printed line describes trembling and calamus. The word spanning the line break is iterum, not a reference to Jesus. The reading intuitus is retained as an interpretive reading of the minims.

### scribes-005

- Previous: Cleanse the barn of your heart
- Local correction: Cleanse the storehouse of your mind
- Latin (expanded/normalized): Mentis vestre purgate horreum: et deus triticum faciet inferri scripturarum
- Source: BSB 19v (0040), [institutional image](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00037424_00040/full/full/0/default.jpg).
- Basis: The scan reads Mentis, not Gentis/Dentes, and horreum. The metaphor is the storehouse of the mind; neither race nor an illegible noun is needed.

### scribes-006

- Previous: the abbot should provide more abundantly than for the others in all necessities for those whom they disdain to be and to be found
- Local correction: the abbot should provide for them in all necessities more abundantly than for the others, who scorn to be such and to be found such
- Latin (expanded/normalized): abbas in omnibus eis necessariis abundantius quam ceteris provideat: qui tales esse et inveniri contemnunt
- Source: BSB 15v (0032), [institutional image](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00037424_00032/full/full/0/default.jpg).
- Basis: The scan has eis, ceteris, and plural contemnunt. The abbot provides more for the diligent brothers than for the others who disdain that conduct.
