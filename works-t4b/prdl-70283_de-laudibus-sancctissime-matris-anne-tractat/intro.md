*In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne* honours Anne as the mother of the Virgin Mary. This reading edition uses the Leipzig printing by Melchior Lotter held by the Herzog August Bibliothek. The catalogue dates the witness to 1501. Its closing imprint names the printer and place.

The Trithemius 4B and earlier translations were prepared independently from this witness. Their OCR and English can differ even where they represent the same printed line.
