Anna, behold, your newborn daughter stretches toward your milk-white breasts and with her tender hand seeks your neck.
Anna, you bare your breasts; showing your breast and bosom, you receive her tender limbs in your tender lap.
Anna, you nourish your daughter; she draws sweet juices from your breast, sounding pleasing murmurs from her rosy mouth.
Anna, if you knew what joys your daughter would bring, and what ways she would produce, you would simply be nothing but joy.
Our Father. Hail Mary.

Anna, it is dear to your heart to adorn your daughter with approved virtues, and to compose her for the most high God.
Anna, you prepare to bring your daughter to the lofty temple; but the gracious bride hastens her step toward the God above.
Anna, you follow quickly, with sensible step and labor; the ready little maiden comforts her weary mother.
Anna, why do you marvel at strength in the tender virgin? You see not human things, but the holy signs of God.
Anna, you are accustomed to give gifts to the poor and to sacred temples; no woman gave anything cleaner or more worthy.
Anna, when you wished your consecrated daughter to dwell there, she gladly entered the cloisters and the virgin house.
Anna, about to send away the holy girl, you stain your cheeks and eyes with tears; a deep love holds you unmoved.
Anna, return home; send your daughter to the Thunderer. Let her think of nothing but Jesus who is to come.
Anna, let your daughter fly through the heavenly recesses; let the love sent from the highest summit possess her.
Anna, two Marys are enough for you; the others too will honor you with no small devotion.
Our Father. Hail Mary.

Anna, taught by your own weeping, you know how to aid one who suffers; therefore be ready also to save the wretched man by his prayers.
Anna, you are accustomed to overturn the fixed laws of nature, and, if you wish, a barren woman may become a joyful mother.
Anna, even if you seek the gifts of wavering fortune, let those fallen things suddenly flee from our [unclear].
Anna, yielding to flight, you grant the gifts of fair fame; you relieve the joyful, and love yields to you.
Anna, whether hostile force terrifies anyone, or whether the aged chains of darkness hold anyone in torn bonds,
Anna, comfort and help of the gods for sick mortals, with you as guide the lost life returns to the languishing.
