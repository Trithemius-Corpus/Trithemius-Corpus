*Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim: a tetrastich on the most holy mother Anne*

Venerable Anne, mother of the gracious Virgin,
excellent in merits and powerful in piety:
no one goes away sorrowful who honors and venerates you;
for you bring to all the gentle aid of salvation.

*A distich by the same, which he placed at the image of Saint Anne*

Whoever in adversity has called upon Saint Anne
with devout prayers will be well protected.

*Conrad Celtis Protucius, poet laureate: a tetrastich on Saint Anne*

Anne has never dismissed anyone's prayers empty,
but, bright one, has always brought the desired aid.
Therefore let us heap much honor upon the pious mother,
who allows no one's prayers to be without effect.

*Theodoric Gresemund the Younger of Mainz: a tetrastich to Saint Anne*

Anne, parent of the Virgin, hail, auspicious mother of Mary,
worthy to begin the work of our salvation.
Since human redemption was begun through you, I pray,
continue, by your prayers, to cover our sins.

*Rudolf Lang, canon of Münster: a distich on Saint Anne*

Great parent Anne, mother of the fruitful Virgin, hear
the prayers that pregnant women bring to you in their womb.

*Jodocus Badius of Ghent: a tetrastich on Saint Anne*

Anne, glory of the world, mother of the mother of Jesus,
may your grandson grant you the stars for the merits of your daughter.
Holy Anne, bringing forth the first fruits of our salvation,
help us with your offspring and with your grandson.
