*On the First Angel of Saturn, Who Is the Principal One and Is Called Orifiel, Who Observes the First Quarter of Saturn*

The first and principal angel of Saturn, as we have said above, is called Orifiel; together with the other angels of the planets, in his order he governs the world for 354 years and four months. He has the first part both by day and by night; and through him the operation is performed, from sunrise by day and from sunset by night up to and including the third hour, in all matters that pertain to Saturn, and on his day and night, as we have said. Therefore, when in the first quarter of Saturn you wish to announce something to an absent person without words and without letters, then no spirit of Saturn other than Orifiel is to be called by you, because through him you will be able to announce everything most perfectly.

First consider in what sign Saturn is, and in what degree, and whether he is direct or retrograde, and with which planets he is in conjunction. Let us set down an example for the first point: behold, Saturn is now in the 25th degree of Taurus, on the 28th day of the month of April in the present year, which is the year of the Lord 1500. Now therefore let us multiply 25 by 25, and they make 600. Let us divide these by four equal quarters, and 150 will remain for each quarter. Let us complete all the degrees of Saturn in the sign of Taurus, and they will be 30. Let us then multiply 30 by 25 in all, and they make 750. Let us cast off four superfluous degrees from the middle of the third quarter, and 650 will remain. Let us now take the point of Saturn’s motion from the table for the present day and the fifth hour;
