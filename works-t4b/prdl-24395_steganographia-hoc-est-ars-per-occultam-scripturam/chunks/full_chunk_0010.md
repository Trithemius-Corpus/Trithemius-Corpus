Here in the table you have the ten angels of Camuel who preside over the day, with their substitutes in number, and as many who preside over the night, also with their own sub-servitors. The last, however, as you see, have no substitute, since 0, as you know, signifies absolutely nothing in number. Therefore, when you wish to operate through these angels, prepare your paper, as is customary; and, turned toward Eurus, write any narrative whatever, in whatever language you wish, which need not be hidden. When it is completed, read the following conjuration silently: Camuel aperoys, melym meuomanial, casmoyn eralki bufaco aeliumar photyfion theor besamys, aneal Cabelonyr thiamo vesonthy. When this conjuration has been completed in silence, make your commission to the spirits who are present, as you know; and send them forth, doing everything in silence and without disturbance, if possible. Afterwards give the letters, open as you wish, to whatever messenger you want to carry them, and let him go, because the spirits will not neglect their office, whether the messenger comes quickly or slowly. Of this let there be no doubt for you, since they are faithful. But you must know which spirit you wish to send from the aforesaid, so that in the conjuration you may express his name immediately after the principal one; and this is so in all operations in which the names of the angels to be sent are set down in the chapter itself. But now let us set forth an example of what we have said concerning those matters which pertain to the office of Camuel and of his subject spirits.

BOOK FIRST.

LET THIS BE THE MOST SECRET INTENTION OF MY MIND, which I wish to make known to someone absent through the aforesaid spirits.

[unclear]
Roth.
[unclear]

Suppose I am stationed at Rome, and I learn from the most secret council of the supreme pontiff that he himself wishes to transfer the Roman Empire to the Franks. I desire to notify the princes of Germany. I cannot do so by messengers, because I do not trust them; I cannot do so by letters, because in all the cities of Italy there are appointed men at the gates who inspect those passing through with the greatest care. Therefore I write one devout prayer, which I send open by a messenger, together with other closed familiar letters to those same princes. I summon the spirits of the third mansion and command them to follow the letters to be sent. And when they have been called by the recipient, they must not fail to reveal to him my secret intention, which I did not dare commit to letters. They come, they go, they obey.

We all die, because we have all contracted the hereditary guilt of original sin; and no one lives who will not die in a very short time. We contracted original fault in Adam and Eve. Moreover, all human beings die the death of the body because of the lamentable rashness of Mother Eve. But Jesus, the victor over death, drove away the death of the soul by freeing us. O crucified Jesus Christ, look upon our lowliness, for we are most wretched, unhappy, and afflicted by many tribulations. Therefore, most merciful King of the heavens, we ask: grant us contrition and forgiveness of all sins, because of your extraordinary humility by which you redeemed us from the sorrowful darkness of hell and from harmful sufferings. O our blessedness, most merciful Jesus, O sweetest lover of innocence, what shall I repay to you? You, holy and innocent, suffer for us. Where shall we flee when you come for the examination of our account? You will come with power to render reward to each person according to his conscience. And all things will be bare before you; nor will anything be hidden from you of which you do not have knowledge. Therefore, brothers, while you live, do penance, and forestall the strict examination of almighty God with works of mercy. Wipe away your crimes with tears, and by relieving the hardships of the poor, make God favorable to you. For he is kind to those who call upon him. Therefore, while there is time, seek his mercy. Always give thanks to him who redeemed you: Jesus Christ, crucified for the salvation of human beings who fear the Lord.
