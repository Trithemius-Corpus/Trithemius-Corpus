CHAP. XXII.
WHOSE HOUR IS CALLED MALCHO, AND WHOSE ANGEL IS CALLED THE EMPEROR IASGUARIM, who has under his rule several princes, dukes, counts, and ministers.

The tenth hour of the night is called Malcho, and its supreme angel, the emperor, is called Iasguarim. He has under his rule many princes, dukes, counts, and ministerial spirits, appointed to wondrous and various magical operations, as Solomon, surnamed Hermes, bears witness in his Secret of Magic. For Steganography, by their turns and orders, there are appointed 100 dukes, 100 counts, and many ministerial spirits, as follows:

R. R. R. R.
Lapheriel Chameray 30 10 100 100 300
Emarziel Hazaniel 30 10 100 100 300
Nameroyz Vraniel 30 10 100 100 300

Although many dukes and counts have been appointed for the operation of Steganography in the tenth hour of the night, nevertheless, since a few suffice us for all things, we have placed by name in this table three of the dukes and likewise three of the counts, setting beneath them the number of their servants, duly distributed by turns and orders. But anyone who wishes to know the names of more should summon one of the aforesaid and ask him about each one. When, however, you wish to work through these spirits in the tenth hour of the night, observe and do all the things that have been prescribed, most diligently writing everything on a clean slip, and on its back this conjuration, in the manner prescribed.

The Purpose of Any Secret.

Let the secret be whatever it may. When you cannot make it known by letters and messengers to a friend living nearby or far away, summon one or two from the spirits of Iasguarim, appoint them as your couriers, and entrust the secret to them, doubting nothing.

Let the letters be formed at will.

This is the prophecy of Nicolaus Basselius of Irbus, S. Episc., which he saw in the Spirit. And he said: Thus says the Lord to the shepherds of the churches: Soon you will perish, because a very great calamity will scatter your broad dominion and will spatter all Europe. Woe to the Ishmaelites, who break the covenants of the tabernacle, because through envy and recklessness they seize the pastures. Behold, the dominion that you have will be broken unless you hasten to fuller repentance; and you will be handed over to be torn apart. Alas, the whole Church has been spattered with the worst morals; hypocrisy has invaded its ministers. Flee, do not look back; you will be laid waste, and you will be dragged by dogs; they will spatter the whole earth with blood. Seek repentance, inhabitants of the earth. Young Benjamin will experience disturbances. Woe to the inhabitants of Genesareth, which is Europe, because on account of the multitude of their sins they will perish under the hands of the Bulgarians. Here ends the prophecy of Nicol., bishop, [unclear], 12 Kalends of May.

When the person to whom they are sent has received any letters whatsoever marked beforehand with the sign of Iasguarim, let him, being skilled in the art, do everything prescribed according to the art, observe the order of the planets, and write what must be written. And when the conjuration written above has been said, as soon as the sent spirits have appeared, let him say to the first among them: Chamerusin othriel arnotiel solais elty.
