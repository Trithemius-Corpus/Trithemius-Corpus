Here now we have forty named from the spirits of Abasdarhon, of whom the first twelve in their orders are dukes, while the remaining twelve are counts; and there are two from each order, namely one duke and one count. We have 3,200 assigned ministerial spirits, whom they use by turns in various magical operations. Solomon and Raziel, wise men and highly experienced in magic, say that the princes of Abasdarhon usually appear in whatever form they have been commanded by the operator. Since we have frequently experienced this, we acknowledge it as supported by truth. Therefore, when you wish to operate through them in Steganography in the fifth hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant, and write both him and the whole disposition of the heaven on a clean sheet, together with the other things we have taught, and on its back this conjuration.

The Conjuration.

Abasdarhon morca lafias tharuean buel dreschin tayr moaberfoty layr pornys theori mean asar penason. Then, after adding the things that must be added, as soon as you have spoken this conjuration, the spirits whom you have summoned will appear to you, ready for everything you command them.

The Argument of the Secret That Occurs.

Whatever secret you have, which otherwise you cannot safely make known to a friend living far away or nearby, entrust it, with the rite and form required according to the art, to the more powerful one among the spirits, fearing nothing, because they are well-disposed and faithful.

Let the letters be formed at pleasure.

The Catholic faith saves no one who despises the ardor of divine love. To believe and not to have zeal for divine love contributes nothing at all to the soul’s redemption. Many people are now found boasting in the Catholic faith, but few have the works of the Christian faith; idle faith dies. Many believe, but very few observe God’s commandments. Alas, how weak is the zeal of believing which even unbelieving people can have more strongly, and many do have it. The Gentiles and those people who do not believe that our savior became man, the son of the almighty, will be tormented by the punishments of hell.

But all those who believe that the Son of God came into the world and accomplished the zeal of human redemption by his murder will be exercised with heavier punishments, if they do not adorn the Christian faith with holy works, and, always entangled in sins, prefer the honors of the world to eternal blessedness, pursuing impious actions with zeal and staining the purity of faith by private morals contrary to obedience. But lovers of God, who have the right zeal of the Christian faith, prefer nothing to God, imitate Christ, flee sins, and despise riches and honors. Now therefore, you who glory in faith, attend to these words: love God, and you will have the reward of blessedness by pursuing justice with zeal. If you love the vanities of the world contrary to the honor of God, you will receive the reward of those who pursue iniquity with zeal: the horrors of hell. Written on the 15th day before the Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the person to whom the letters are sent, being skilled in the art, has received them marked with the character of Abasdarhon, let him at once consider, observe, and write down the lord of the ascendant and the whole disposition of the heaven, together with the tenor of the conjuration and the other necessary things, on a clean sheet. After adding the things that must be added and after the conjuration has been spoken, when he sees the spirits sent to him standing nearby, let him say these words to the more powerful one: Chameron massotiel caseuij renean pornas thilmeuj penason. When these have been spoken, he will at once come nearer and faithfully reveal in the operator’s ear the secret entrusted to him.

CHAPTER XVIII.

WHOSE HOUR IS CALLED THAAZARON, AND WHOSE ANGEL IS NAMED ZAAZENACH, PRESIDING OVER MANY SPIRITS.

The sixth hour of the night is called Thaazaron, and the name of its angel is Zaazenach, who has under his command princes and dukes, counts and almost innumerable ministerial spirits, who preside over various magical operations by their turns and orders. In the first order there are 10; in the second 20; in the third 30; in the fourth 40; in the sixth 50; in the seventh 70; in the eighth 80; in the ninth 90; in the tenth 100; in the twelfth 16,000. Whenever he wishes for them, they are ready and entirely obedient, as the aforesaid wise men attest, and as we have experienced.
