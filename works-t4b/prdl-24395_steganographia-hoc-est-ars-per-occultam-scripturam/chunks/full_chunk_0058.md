*Conjuration.*

Bariel mylan theory madruson alfayr dreschym taparoys mear moas layr penason. Once the things that must first be done have been done, say this conjuration as is proper, and immediately the spirits called, whom you have called, will appear. Entrust to them whatever secret you have.

*The Establishment of Any Secret Whatever.*

We have a secret for a friend, or for anyone else skilled in our art, which we cannot signify either by letters or by messenger, because we fear danger in both cases. Therefore we call a spirit; we entrust the secret to the superior one, and in all things we are safe.

*The tenor of the letters, at pleasure.*

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the Order of Saint Benedict, to brother Quirinus, a monk of Corbie in France, greetings.

Concerning the mother of the perpetual Virgin Mary I send my prayer, which you recently requested, Quirinus: Hail, most kindly mother Anna, most radiant parent of the Virgin Mary; you most peaceful helper of all the innocent; you noble refuge of those who seek you; quiet blessing of holiness. Hail, most gentle advocate of the poor, most beautiful comforter of those who groan, most adorned storehouse of virtues. Hail, happy refresher of the sorrowful, most health-giving medicine of the sick, and most renowned promoter of faithful Christians. Obtain pardon for us who pray; appease the dread judge Christ; intercede for your servants, that they may attain blessedness. With you interceding, we ask that he receive us wretched ones by his kindness. Amen. 8 Ides of April, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the one to whom the letters are sent has received them, being skilled in the art, let him first attend to the character of the hour attached to the letter. Once this is known, let him operate through the lord of the ascendant of the hour in which he received the letters, together with the rest of the precepts of the art. Then let him append the prescribed prayer in the manner and order required; and soon the spirits sent will appear. When you see them, these words must be spoken to the principal one among them: chamerusin maslotiel vear reneas liernoty trismy penason. When these words have been spoken to the spirit superior among the others, who is easily recognized by his habit and crown and always precedes the others, he will at once reveal the secret in the ear.
