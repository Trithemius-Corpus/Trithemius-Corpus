Here, from the twelve orders of the spirits of Mendrion, supreme angel of the seventh hour of the night, we have twelve dukes and six counts, who with their subordinate servants will suffice for us on this occasion for every operation. These spirits come, as Hermes the Hebrew and Raziel the Arab testify, in whatever form they are ordered by the one who works according to the art. But anyone who is not perfect in our art will obtain not obedience but danger and harm, if he undertakes to operate.

When, therefore, you wish in the seventh hour of the night to have something done by spirits skilled in this art, the ascendant to the minute and the figure of the whole heaven must be observed; write this, together with the remaining things prescribed in the art, on clean paper, and on its back write this conjuration.

*Conjuration*

Mendrion furia co breotnitus erfoy neuo, ome ar nyc o lays erfora theory pornys A zan mean lafias aftopenafon.

Then, after the required things have been added, and after the conjuration has been spoken as is proper, the spirits summoned will at once appear to you, ready for all things.

*Let the secret be whatever it may be.*

For announcing a secret, whether near or far, to an absent friend already skilled in this art of spirits, it matters little what kind it is, provided that it is not trivial or of such a sort that it could otherwise have been safely announced by messengers or letters. Nor do we care what the letters themselves are like, provided that they have the character of the principal angel of that hour in which the operation takes place, and no secret at all.

*Steganographia.*

*Let us compose the letters as we please.*

Johannes Trithemius, abbot, sends greeting to Nicolaus Basselius, monk, and transmits this prayer of Saint Nicholas.

The creator, reformer, and maker of all things created the first man, Adam, to whom he joined a most beautiful wife named Eve, the mother of all living, prefiguring the Catholic Church, to be joined to pastors, shining beautifully with upright morals, and to bear as sons men of good works, who through humility receive the kingdoms of heaven that will endure without end.

Christ redeemed us through his kindness, humbling himself for our sake. Therefore we ought to love the most gracious one, who gave us the Catholic faith; those who truly love it will rightly obtain angelic happiness. Therefore let all of us, conducting ourselves in true religion and living as Catholics, daily honor our almighty Creator.

Lord Jesus, most gracious Savior, who mercifully redeemed us all from the abyss of death, kindly regarding our frailty and weakness, conform us to the Catholic faith, so that after this transitory life we may deserve to have forever the rest of eternal consolation and happiness. Amen. 14 Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the one to whom these letters are sent, being skilled in our art, has received them marked with the character of Mendrion, let him observe the lord of the ascendant; and let him write both it and the whole disposition of the heaven on a clean sheet, and the conjuration on its back. Then, after all the required things have been added and the charm has been spoken, as soon as he sees the spirit standing by him, let him say these words to the foremost one: Chamerusyn merion nodiel burmy raueto elay reas penason.

When he has sounded these words to the one who appears to be more eminent among the other spirits, that one will at once approach peacefully and reveal the secret committed to him.
