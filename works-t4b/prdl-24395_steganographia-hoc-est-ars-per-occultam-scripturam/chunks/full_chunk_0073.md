*Chapter XXV*

*In which we shall teach the general manner and form of approaching this art of Steganography, and of operating in it without danger and with the greatest usefulness.*

Whoever desires to approach the hidden knowledge of this art of ours, whoever wishes to work marvels through it, useful to many dangers, must first be adorned with virtues, with a clean conscience and good will toward God, himself, and his neighbor, so that he is not inclined to harm anyone, nor seeks dealings of baseness. Next, he must necessarily be somewhat imbued with the good literary arts, and especially with the science of the stars, so that he knows the general motions, courses, movements, changes, orders, natures, positions, risings, settings, and effects of the stars, signs, and planets; for without competent knowledge of these things no one can have access to the depths of this art. Consequently, he must have a teacher perfect and experienced in the art, because we believe it possible to come to this knowledge without a teacher only for a few, and those most learned in many things, and especially skilled in magical matters. The teacher, however, must be not only proven and experienced in knowledge, but also faithful; for the purer he has been with God in good conduct, the safer he will be in the operation of this knowledge. For the Spirits obey virtues.

Therefore, when someone is about to approach this art, let the teacher receive him and lead him into some secret and clean place. Let the time be tranquil and serene, and the moon complete in opposition, with the sun shining clearly; and let Mercury be in the ascendant, joined to Venus or Jupiter if possible; and let Saturn and Mars be remote, because if both or either of them is with the ascendant, the instruction will not be perfect. First, he will swear this oath in the art:

I, N., swear and promise, by the power of almighty God, by the blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, by the resurrection of the dead and the last judgment, and by the salvation of my soul in the holy Catholic faith, to almighty God, to the blessed Virgin Mary, to all the Saints, and to you, N., that I will faithfully keep this art of Steganography hidden for all the days of my life, nor will I teach it to anyone without your will and consent. Moreover, by the same power I swear and promise that I will not use this knowledge against God and his commandments, nor against the holy Roman and universal Church or its ministers, nor against justice and equity. So may God help me, and so may he save me at the last judgment.

Then let the teacher read the following conjuration in the mystical language, and let him interpret it, and all the conjurations contained in this work, before the disciple, so that he may not suspect that anything diabolical, superstitious, or contrary to God, or any pact with demons, implicitly or explicitly, is in any way latently hidden there.
