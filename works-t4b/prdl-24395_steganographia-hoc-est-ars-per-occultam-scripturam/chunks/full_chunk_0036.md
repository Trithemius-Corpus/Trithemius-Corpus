Conjuration.

Buriel mastfoyt chamerusyn, noel pean Ionachym mardusan philarsij, pedarym estis carmoyboyscharonti phroys fabelronti, mear Laphany vearchas, clareson, notiel, pador aslotiel, marsno reneas, Capedon, thifinasion melro, lauair carpentor, thurneam camelrosyn.

When you have spoken this verse in silence, always looking at the ground, the spirits called will be present. But take care not to be afraid, because they will not be able to harm you if you are brave and steadfast in spirit. And note that these princes or spirits, although they are generally appointed to announce all nocturnal secrets in the night, are nevertheless especially suited and willing to carry a message to prisons and lakes, to captives, and likewise in matters that pertain to carnal love and the hidden practices of lovers, and for everything that happens in the night, whether good or evil, because they hate the light.

Let us suppose a secret which is by no means to be entrusted to anyone, as follows:

I have a secret for a friend established in this city, which I can make known to him neither by letters nor by a messenger, lest it become public. Several things prevent me from going to my friend myself. I call one of the aforesaid spirits; I entrust the mystery to him; he goes and faithfully announces what I have committed to him. I am safe.

I shall compose letters of whatever sort I wish, which should make no one suspicious, namely, such as these.

Our Savior Jesus Christ, who, dying on the Cross, redeemed the human race by his blood, is to be praised by us earnestly and without interruption. He promised that he would drive away death and give eternal life to us who fear and love him. Now therefore, dearest brothers, let us rise up from our sins, preparing our lamps with good works, so that we may meet our Redeemer joyfully in the hour of the fearful passage, when death will snatch away our souls. We now have abundant time for completing penance and adorning our lamps; but at the final hour of death, further space for repentance will be wholly denied to us. Let us consider the very brief glory of the world, since it is empty: it passes most swiftly;

When the one to whom these letters, or any such letters, are sent has received them, if he is skilled in the art, once he has recognized the sign of Buriel and sent ahead the things that must be sent ahead, let him look at the ground and say the conjuration.

Conjuration.

Buriel, Threfoy chamerontis, hayr plassu, nadiel, marso, neany, pean, fayr, fabelron, chaturmo, melros, ersoty caduberosyn.

When this verse has been spoken, the spirit, invisible to everyone but visible to you, will appear and will faithfully tell you the secret entrusted to him.

CHAPTER XXIII.

WHOSE SPIRIT AND SUPREME PRINCE IS CALLED HYDRIEL, AND DWELLS IN THE WATERS; HE HAS UNDER HIM 100 DUKES, 200 COUNTS, AND ALMOST INNUMERABLE SERVANTS.

There is another prince among the principal spirits, who is called Hydriel, and he dwells in waters, ponds, lakes, marshes, and the sea, and around springs, cisterns, and rivers. He has under his dominion at least 100 dukes, his princes, and 200 counts, his sub-dukes, and many servants. Their office in general is to announce all things both by water and by land, whether by day or by night. And know that the princes are sufficiently benevolent and willing in all things, but when they come after being conjured, they do not appear so. For they frequently appear in the form of a serpent, sometimes large, sometimes small, having the very beautiful head of a maiden, with hair spread out. None of them goes alone, but each prince brings with him at least two counts and 80 servants. So that you may not fall into error in the operation, I shall give you the names of some of the dukes.
