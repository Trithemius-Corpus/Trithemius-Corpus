Here now we have, from the twelve orders of the spirits of Zaazenach, named 11 dukes and 6 counts, with their servants duly assigned in order, to the number of 1860, who are abundantly sufficient for us in the sixth hour of the night for every operation of *Steganographia*. Therefore, when you wish to operate in this hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant; and write both him and the whole disposition of the heavens, with the other necessary things, on a clean sheet, and the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Zaazenach eneos fari neabdiel lasmy chyrmean ersoty layt pornys theor mean penason. Then, after the things that are duly to be appended have been appended, and the conjuration has been said, the spirits called will appear without delay, gentle and well-disposed, ready for all things.

*A theme for any secret whatsoever that occurs.*

Whatever secret occurs to us, which we cannot safely disclose to a friend either through messengers or through letters, let us entrust it in the sixth hour of the night to one of the prescribed dukes, or even to the counts if the duke is not present, as can often happen, in the manner and order for which instruction exists in this art, fearing nothing at all; for they are all good and faithful in everything that has been entrusted to them by the operator, and they omit nothing.

Let the tenor of the letters be set down according to the pleasure of the operator, or of the matter at hand.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, to Adam the fuller, doctor of the solitary art and of philosophy, greeting and charity.

Remember your promise, which you made when, receiving the Catholic faith in Christ, you offered it with a solemn vow to eternal joy; for to those who despise the temporal pleasures and delights of the present life in view of the future eternal rest, eternal joy is without doubt promised. But for lovers of the world, and for reprobate Christians, God has prepared the bitter punishments of hell. He who has faith, but neglects to have divine love, is faithless, and presumes in vain concerning faith also. A zealot of divine love is one who does not cease daily to adorn the Christian faith with devout and good works, humbly exercising himself in the arts of piety. Farewell. 14 Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord’s incarnation 1500.

When one skilled in the art, to whom the letters are sent, has received them sealed with the character of the spirit of the hour, after first observing the figure of the heavens, let him write it down with the remaining necessary things; and then, when everything that pertains to the operation has been completed and the prescribed conjuration has been said, when the spirit has appeared visibly, let him say: Chamerusi ferion notiel aseuomy rean, badian, laso sear vaobry hastoripeson. When these words have been said, the spirit will faithfully and most secretly reveal to the operator, in his ear, the secret entrusted to him.

*Chapter XIX.*

*Whose hour is called Venaydor, and its angel is called Mendrion, having under his command princes, dukes, counts, and almost innumerable ministering spirits.*

The seventh hour is called Venaydor, whose angel and supreme spirit is called Mendrion. He has under his command many other spirits, as princes, dukes, counts, and ministers, who are assigned to various operations in magical matters. From these, for *Steganographia* and its operations in the seventh hour of the night, many princes, dukes, and counts with their servants are assigned by their turns and orders. In the first order there are 10; in the second, 20; in the third, 30; in the fourth, 40; in the fifth, 50; in the sixth, 60; in the seventh, 70; in the eighth, 80; in the ninth, 90; in the tenth, 100; in the eleventh, 10000. All these are called by turns and come to the operation.

Ammiel
