CHAPTER I.

WHOSE KEY AND OPERATION IS HELD
by the principal spirit Pamersyel, Anoyr Madriel, through the ministry of Ebrasothean, Abrulges i Trasbiel. And Nadres Ormennitules Rablion Hamorphiel. To these let the commission of all things be made, with an exorcism.

The operation of this first chapter is very difficult and full of dangers, because of the pride and rebellion of its spirits, who obey no one unless he is highly experienced in this art. For they not only refuse to obey novices and those less tested in the art, but, if they are pressed too hard, they often injure them and afflict them with various illusions. They are malicious and faithless above all aerial spirits, and they obey absolutely no one unless compelled by the greatest sacraments; and the secret entrusted to them they often faithlessly show to others. For as soon as they have been sent out with letters, they fly away, and, bursting in disorderly fashion toward the person to whom they are sent, like a people fleeing battle without a leader, they hurry furiously and, filling the air with their shouting, often reveal the sender’s secrets to everyone in the vicinity.

We therefore advise that no one intending to work in this art compel them, nor anxiously seek their services. For they are insolent and faithless, when he can find many among those whom we shall mention below who are quite well disposed, and who of their own accord offer themselves ready for obedience. But if anyone should absolutely wish to test their insolence and prove that what we have said is true, let him know that the following procedure must be observed. Let him prepare the paper on which he is going to write with an invocation of the divine name, in the name of the Father

and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Then let him write on it whatever simple and open narrative he wishes, which all who read it may understand, in Latin, in his native language, or in any other language. But when he has sat down to write, facing the East, let him compel the spirits in this way:

Pamerfiel oshurmy delmuson Thaftoyn peano charustea melany. lyaminto colchan, paroys, madyn, moerlay, bulre atloor don melcoue peloin, ibutsyl meon mysbreath alini driaco person. Crisolnay, lemon asosle mydar, icoriel pean thalmon, asophiel il notreon banyel ocrimos esteuor naelma besro. nathaomor fronian beldodrayn bon otalmesgo mero fas el-nathyn bosramoth.

When he has said these things, and the spirits seem to him to be present to obey, let him continue the work he has begun. But if they have not yet appeared, let him repeat the words just given as often as necessary until they appear, or else let him entirely desist, lest, when compelled too much, they injure the operator. But when you have completed the letters you intend, send them by messenger to a friend tested in the art. When he has received them, let him say this conjuration:

Lamarton anoyr bulon madriel traschon ebrasothea panthenon nabrulges Camery itras-bier rubanthy nadres Calmosi ormenulan, ytules demyrabion hamorphyn.

When these words have been said, he will at once understand your mind perfectly, since the spirits will now of their own accord offer themselves with force and with shouting, so that very often other people present are able to perceive the sender’s secret. But observe that in all letters that you send written by this art you affix your own sign, so that the person to whom you write may know through which spirits you have operated. For if he himself were to operate through some spirits for understanding, but through others for sending, they would never in eternity obey him; rather, because of the improper operation, they would injure him and would by no means reveal your secret. For all the spirits whom we use in this art guard only the orders and offices entrusted to them, and in no way meddle with others.
