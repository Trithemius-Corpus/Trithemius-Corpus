*Second Book.* 127

The eighth hour of the night is called Xymalim, and its angel, the first and supreme emperor, is called Narcoriel. He has under his command many other spirits: princes, dukes, counts, and ministers, who are called by their orders to various effects by those who work in the secrets of philosophy. And from all these there are twelve orders of those who serve Steganography. In the first order there are 10, in the second 20, in the third 30, in the fourth 40, in the fifth 50, in the sixth 60, in the seventh 70, in the eighth 80, in the ninth 90, in the tenth 100, in the eleventh 1000, in the twelfth 10000. All these keep their orders in such a way that no one can work safely through them unless he has been fully instructed in all things. For whoever does not observe the proper order both exposes himself to danger and will be frustrated in the effect he desires.

Here we now have named 12 dukes and 6 counts, appointed through all twelve orders by a hidden disposition, who have under them ministers numbered XIIII. c. c., who are sufficient for us for every operation of the eighth hour of the night. Therefore, when you wish to work through them in Steganography at the eighth hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant, and write both it and the whole disposition of the heavens on clean paper, and on its back, together with the other things customary according to practice, this conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Narconiel aples pornya nauelo meas. Triome ilneasazyfan lafias my breffion ersoti penafon. Then, after adding according to custom the things that must be added, and after the conjuration has been spoken, the spirits called will be present in whatever form you have commanded them to assume, ready for all things.

125 *Steganography.* The secret meaning.

The secret that we wish a far-distant friend to know, if we trust neither letters nor messengers, let us entrust in the eighth hour of the night to the more powerful one among the spirits of Narcoriel, fearing nothing at all, since they are all faithful and most ready for obedience.

Let us form the letters at pleasure.

O most benign Jesus, I give thanks to your most holy mercy, who by your blood reconciled the human race to the heavenly Father, hanging pitifully raised upon the Cross. You subjected the flourishing youth of your most noble body to harsh wounds for our sake, so that you might give life to dead mankind. Therefore, such thanks as we can, we Catholics offer to our Redeemer, loving and praising the memory of the Passion, to be recalled in Christian fashion, of the Savior of our race. But have mercy on me, most clement Jesus, abyss of clemency, fountain of mercy, glory of the saints, light of the citizens above; and pour into me the sweetest taste of your most holy love. Deliver me from all my calamities. Give me true gladness of heart, so that I may exult in your praises, glorifying you, since by the pain of your wounds you have healed me. What shall I render to your clemency, my Redeemer, my life-giver? I suffer with your labors with fervent tears, and bless the most holy wounds of your body. Most benign Savior Jesus, I give immortal thanks, asking pardon for my crimes. In the year of the Lord 1500, on the Kalends of May, I wrote this prayer, [unclear].

When the letters, whatever they may be, marked with the character of Narcoriel, have been received, let the one to whom they are sent, if skilled in the art, observe the lord of the ascendant at the same hour; and let him write both it and the whole disposition of the heavens, with the remaining things, on a clean sheet. After all necessary things have been added according to custom, and after the conjuration has been spoken, when he sees the spirits standing by him, let him say these words to the more powerful one: Medaro Cassotiel va reneas thasny thyrmo thea penason. When these words have been spoken according to the instruction of this art, the principal spirit will soon come nearer and will disclose the secret word.
