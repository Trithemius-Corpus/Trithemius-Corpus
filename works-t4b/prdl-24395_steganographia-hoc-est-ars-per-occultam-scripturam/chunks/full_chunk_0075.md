142 Steganography

These are the seven angels of the planets according to the tradition of the ancient sages. Each of them governs the world for 354 years and four months in his own order. Orifiel is the angel of Saturn, who from the beginning of creation governed the world for 354 years and 4 months. Afterward came Venus, then Jupiter, after him Mercury, and then Mars, afterward the Moon, and last the Sun. Each one is described as having held dominion over the world for 354 years and 4 months with his angel written for him in the common table. Thus the sages say that at the time of the Flood Samael, the angel of Mars, served in the power of rule; Gabriel, the angel of the Moon, at the time of the confusion of tongues; and Michael, the angel of the Sun, at the time of Israel’s departure from Egypt. And indeed, if anyone gives credence to the aforesaid assertion, he will easily be able by himself, from the origin of the world down to his own age, to reckon the changes of times and the government of the angels in the prescribed order. But we, pursuing those matters that are useful for our purpose, have no doubt that there are 21 other spirits subject to the aforesaid seven angels of the planets, that is, three to each one, according to the instruction of this art of ours; through them we bring about the effect of our intention. We shall observe the customary order of the planets, beginning from Saturn, the highest of all, down to the Moon: first writing out the operation of each principal angel, and then the service beneath him, and joining the chapters in order.

Chapter I.

On Orifiel, the First and Supreme Angel of Saturn, and on the operations that are performed through him.

Saturn, the planet highest and supreme of all, slow in motion by nature, cold because remote, difficult, heavy, is moved by its own natural motion from the point of its beginning 650 years to the point of its first station, remaining 30 months in any given sign, 626. Its first and supreme angel, according to the opinion of the ancients, is called Orifiel. He has under him three other principal spirits, namely Sadael, Poniel, and Morisiel, concerning whom we shall speak in their own place and order.
