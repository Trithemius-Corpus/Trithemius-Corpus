When you wish to work in Steganography through Aseliel, after the customary preliminaries in the art, observe the place of his mansion, which is in the east-south, and summon one of the spirits named above according to his time, whichever you wish. This is the conjuration:

Afelic aproysy, melyn, thulnear casinoyn, mauear burson, charny demorphanon, Theomaasmeryn diuel, casponi vearly basanys, crnoti chaua lorson.

When the conjurations have been completed in the customary way, add the remaining things that are customarily to be done in the art, and the effect of your operation will follow most certainly. Let us set down an example.

Let us imagine the secret of your mind. You have a beloved woman, in whose love you are held, and she no less; but you cannot approach or speak to her because of the watch kept by her people. At last you have found a plan by which you may possess her, but it is necessary to inform her beforehand. You cannot do it yourself; you do not dare by letters, lest they come into the hands of others; you do not judge that the matter should be entrusted to messengers or old women, lest afterward they make you known. Therefore hide by the art, for a time, a matter which you are confident cannot be done openly. You summon the invisible ministers of the art, write letters that are in no way suspicious, and send them to your beloved even through the guards themselves. They praise the writing and give it to the beloved woman to read. Recognizing the sign, she calls the hidden messenger, hears the plan, sends back her consent; you approach, enter, and possess her.

CHAPTER V.

Whose supreme prince, by name Barmiel, dwells in the south, having under him ten princes who preside over the day, and twenty who preside over the night, with their servants; and they are over the announcing of secrets that pertain to the surrender of fortresses.

The prince of the fifth mansion, in the south toward midday, is called great Barmiel. He has under his dominion ten principal leaders who preside over the day with their subservants, and twenty in number who preside over nighttime operations with their servants. These are greater in number than the daytime spirits because the operation of this mansion is carried out more often by night than by day. For their office is to announce the secret surrenders of fortresses and cities, especially at night.

When you wish to work in this art through Barmiel, summon one of his princes, whichever you wish; he will come to you at once with twenty servants, because no prince of this mansion is accustomed to go about alone or with fewer. Entrust your secret not to the servants, since they are proud and malicious, but to the prince, because he is good, calm, faithful, and very benevolent. But now I shall name for you eight of the daytime princes and likewise eight of the nighttime princes, who are better and readier to obey; we need no more.
