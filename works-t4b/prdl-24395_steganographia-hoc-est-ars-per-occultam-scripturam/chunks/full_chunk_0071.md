This is the prophecy of Nicolaus Basselius, Bishop of Irbuia, which he saw in the Spirit. And he said: Thus says the Lord to the shepherds of the churches: Soon you will perish, because a very great calamity will scatter your broad dominion and will besprinkle all Europe. Woe to the Ishmaelites, who break the treaties of the tabernacle, because through envy and rashness they seize the pastures. Behold, the dominion that you have will be broken, unless you quickly show greater patience; you will be handed over to be torn apart. This whole Church has been besprinkled with the worst morals; pretense has prevailed among its ministers. Flee, do not look back; you will be laid waste, and that region will be dragged by dogs; they will besprinkle the whole earth with blood. You seek the patience of the inhabitants of the earth. Little Benjamin will experience disturbances. Woe to the inhabitants of Gennesaret, which is Europe, because on account of the multitude of their sins they will perish beneath the hands of the Bulgarians. Here ends the prophet Nicolaus, bishop. 12 Kalends of May.

THE SECOND BOOK.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Whose hour is called Aalacho, and whose supreme angel is called Emperor Dardariel; he has under his command dukes, counts, and many spirits.

The eleventh hour of the night is called Aalacho, and its supreme emperor of spirits is called Dardariel, having under his command many most powerful princes and dukes, who preside over counts and almost infinite ministerial spirits. These dukes are exceedingly benevolent, cheerful, and pleasant, and they delight in associating with human beings; and they are most obedient to those operators who have been well instructed in the art. And note that the number of these dukes is infinite, because the air, once penetrated, is filled with them, and they cannot be counted. Solomon Hermes says, and we have experienced, that among the dukes, counts, and ministerial spirits of Dardariel there are many good, useful, and most honorable spirits, who always desire to benefit human beings, always exhorting them by a certain invisible teaching toward the good, toward honorableness, peace, concord, integrity of faith, love of God, observance of his commandments, contempt of the world, desire for supreme blessedness, and every good. These are good and most holy spirits, who love, venerate, and possess God, who delight to be with holy and God-fearing human beings, because they have been appointed by God, the Creator of all things, as their protectors.

Others, however, are evil and most pestilential apostates from God, enemies of all good things, who continually tempt mortals and lie in wait for human beings. And whatever the good spirits establish for the honor of God, these always strive to destroy and revoke. The air is full of these malignant spirits; they run and roam invisibly among human beings, always inciting them to evil, and they drag the unwary with them to hell. Through these evil spirits of Dardariel there are countless acts of sorcery, not only among Christians but also among unbelievers; and they especially deceive petty women more ready for malice. And whoever wishes to devote himself to acts of sorcery through them will be, in evil things, powerful above all other sorcerers and worthy of eternal fire.
