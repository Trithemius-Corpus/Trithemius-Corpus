*The Second Book*

I have made a large *Catalogue of Ecclesiastical Writers*, which I recently presented to your fraternity to be examined. Since I brought it to conclusion too hastily, I admit that I passed over some very famous men, not because I grudged their name the benefit of the catalogue, as a swollen critic accuses, attacking the catalogue with the stupidest words. He thought that I had failed to mention his people solely out of envy, which is utterly false. Indeed, I wrote and affirmed that the conception of the most holy Mother of God was always most beautiful, without any blemish of original transgression. This the swollen critic, wishing to rebuke swollenly, said, with the emptiest rashness against Catholic observance, that the most radiant Virgin had been stained by the guilt of original fault. It is rash to wish to defile the most blessed Virgin. But the most renowned doctors of Cologne, wishing to root out the mad little brother’s opinion, which was contrary to the truth, summoned him and compelled him to retract his empty tales. They asserted and also confirmed by statute that the most blessed Virgin must be believed to have been most pure, and at no time, however brief, to have been subject to original defilement. I wanted to write these things to you, since you love the truth, so that you may avoid those who reproach the most blessed Virgin. Farewell. 16 Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the person to whom the letters are sent receives them, if he is skilled in the art, let him attend to the sign of the principal spirit, so that he may know whom he ought to summon according to the changes of the hour. Then let him observe the lord of the ascendant in the very hour in which he receives the letters, together with the remaining disposition of the heavens through the twelve houses of the Zodiac, omitting absolutely none of the things that have been prescribed. When the conjuration has been spoken, and when he sees the spirit present, let him say the following words: chamerusyn maslotiel vean reneas cralty thyrmo venear penarson. When these have been said to the principal spirit, he will at once come nearer and will reveal in his ear the secret entrusted to him.

The fourth hour of the night is called Ramerzy, whose angel is called Iefischa. He has under his command many princes, dukes, and ministering spirits, who, like those whom we described in the preceding chapter, are distributed by turns and orders into twelve ranks. For in the first order there are 10, in the second 20, in the third 30, in the fourth 40, in the fifth 50, in the sixth 60, in the seventh 70, in the eighth 80, in the ninth 90, in the tenth 100, in the eleventh 1000, and in the twelfth 10000. From these twelve orders, in their proper turns, spirits are sent to us for service in the operations of the art of steganography. Of these we shall now name some.

R.          R.          R.          R.
Armosiel    Rayziel    Lamediel    10    100    100    1000
Nedruan     Gemezin    Adroziel    10    10     100    10
Maneyloz    Fremiel    Zodiel      10    10     100    1
Ormael      Hamayz     Bramiel     10    1      100    1
Phorfiel    Iapuriel   Coreziel    10    1      100    1
Bimezyn     Iasphiel   Enatriel    10    2      100    3

Here we have named six dukes and twelve counts from the spirits of Iefischa, to whom 7,260 ministering spirits are assigned. They are sent, by their turns and orders, into the service of the one operating in the art. According to the saying of Solomon the Philosopher, these princes are multiform, and they assume whatever forms they have been commanded to take. Therefore, when you wish to operate through them in the fourth hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant and the whole figure of the heavens, and do everything according to what we prescribe for you, writing everything on a clean sheet, and on its back this conjuration.

*Conjuration*

Iefischa osiel mea pathyr lays theor dreochis fazan moab lofeas ersoti breo pornys tayr penarson. Then, after the things that must be added have been added, when you have spoken that conjuration itself in the proper manner, form, and order, the spirits will appear visibly in whatever form you have commanded, ready and willing for all things.

Let the secret be made at will. Secret.
