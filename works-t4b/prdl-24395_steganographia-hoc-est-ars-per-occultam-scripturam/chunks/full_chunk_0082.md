Once the place of the angel Sadael has been found from consideration of the fixed star at the point of Saturn's motion, make two images, in the manner we described in the preceding operation, of Orifiel, with the inscriptions, attestations, and all the other individual things that were stated there. When these have been completed, place the images themselves upon five little branches of the herb thrasnote, outside the house in a safe and secret place, and say over them: In the name of the † Father and of the † Son and of the † Holy Spirit, Amen. Just as I have placed these two images, of the angel Sadael and of N. son of N., my friend, joined together upon these five movable branches, so I command you, Sadael, by the power of Orifiel, your great prince, to bring without delay to the knowledge of the aforesaid friend the secret of my mind, which I have entrusted to you by explaining it. When these things have been said, leave the images there for 24 hours; and what you wished will be done, and your friend will know, most perfectly and secretly, everything that you said over the images. Likewise, if you wish to know concerning some absent person how he is faring, and his true condition within 24 hours, however far away he may be, you will be able to know it in this way. AND ALL THINGS THAT HAPPEN IN THE WORLD, ONCE THE CONSTELLATION HAS BEEN OBSERVED, YOU WILL BE ABLE TO KNOW THROUGH THIS ART.

On the third angel of Saturn, who is the second under Orifiel and is called Pomiel, presiding over the third quarter of Saturn.

The third angel of Saturn is called Pomiel, who is second in order under Orifiel, the principal angel of Saturn. He presides over the Saturnine seventh, eighth, and ninth hours, both by day and by night, in all things that pertain to the operation of Saturn. Since he is moved with his order toward the fixed stars 25 degrees from the East, you will find his place in this way, through the tables arranged according to his hours.
