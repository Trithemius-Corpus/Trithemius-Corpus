The person receiving these letters, or any others, to whom they are sent and who is skilled in the art, should insert the character of Armadiel; when it is recognized, the truth will be known, by which the art commands what is to be conveyed, and he should recite the song.

Conjuration.

ARMADIEL. afran meson Cafayr pelodyn, Cauoti cinameron therforuy marbeuon pheor Cafoyn myruofy lyburmy deon fabelronton Chubis archamatfon.

When this conjuration has been duly completed in the proper manner, the spirit sent will appear openly, and will faithfully reveal to him the secrets entrusted by the sender, adding nothing and taking nothing away. And if anything has then been entrusted to him by that person to report back to the operator by whom he was sent, let what is prescribed by the art be done, and he will obey.

CHAPTER XVI.

That Prince Baruchas dwells in that region from which the wind that is called Vulturnus is accustomed to blow, having under him many princes and servants, who are messengers over the hidden commissions of lords.

Over the seventeenth mansion, situated toward the Vulturnus wind, presides the great emperor Baruchas, who has under his rule many princes, dukes, and other spirits, whose entire office is to announce the hidden and most secret commissions of princes, nobles, and lords to their subjects or friends. Of these I shall name for you fifteen, who will suffice for every operator in Steganography; and they do not have divisions or orders among themselves for day and night as the others do, but all have a commission over all things in general, so that whoever is summoned by the operator at any hour is compelled to come.
