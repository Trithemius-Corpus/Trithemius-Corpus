Here we now have ten of the princes and counts of Bydiel, marked by their names, who are sufficient for us at present for every operation of ours in the art of Steganography, together with many servants who know how to observe their order according to the command of the dukes and princes. But when you wish to operate in this art through them, after first doing what must be done beforehand, say the conjuration.

Song of Conjuration.

BYdiel marchan chamerosi philtres maduse veat casmyron cralnoti: pean deuoon fabeltros eltida camean veor. Oniel veat thyrso liernory: ianos prolsato chanos elasty peanon elsathas melros notiel pen soes probys chyras lesbroy mauear iothan liernotj chrymarson.

When the song has been spoken as it should be, the spirits summoned will at once appear visibly, walking beautifully, and, like friends, leading one another in a mutual embrace; and they will be willing and obedient to you in all things.

Let the secret for me be whatever it may be.

There is a secret for me, which I wish my friend, far absent, to know in the greatest secrecy. It concerns a hidden matter which must necessarily be lost if the mystery should happen in any way to become public. Therefore I entrust it to be carried not to a man, not to letters, but to spirits alone, whom I know to be both secure and faithful.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, sends greetings to Rutgerus Sicamber. And he dedicates the present little prayer of great Basil.

Song.

BYdel maslo chameron theory madias near fabelron thiamy marfoy vear pean liernoty calmea drules: Thubra pleory malresa teorty melchoy vemo chosray.
