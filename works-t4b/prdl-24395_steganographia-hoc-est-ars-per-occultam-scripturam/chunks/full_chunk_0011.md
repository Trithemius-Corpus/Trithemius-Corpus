STEGANOGRAPHY

Roth.

LET THIS BE ANOTHER FORM OF SECRETS, WHICH IS ENTRUSTED TO THE SPIRITS.

A secret remains stored in my mind, concerning my coming soon in secret to a male friend or female friend. I do not dare write this very thing, lest it become public, which a weighty cause forbids; nor do I dare entrust it through a messenger, since there is the same danger. I summon a spirit, entrust the matter to it; it comes, goes, and carries the secret most secretly. I myself am safe in the form of the letters that I send; let it be as familiar as one likes, so that no occasion may be lacking.

Greetings in the Lord. The bearer of the present letter, brother Arnoldus Bostius, has composed many admirable volumes on various subjects, as, among many other things, you will find written at the end, where mention is made of the living, in our book sent to you some time ago, which is entitled On Ecclesiastical Writers. I commend so great a man, notable for religion and for the grace of the arts, to your sincerity, dearest patron, and I ask most earnestly that you receive him with that familiarity with which you have been accustomed to receive all those joined to us in friendship. He will disclose to you many secrets of nature, and, if you wish, will be able to delight you with the sweetest conversation. As for the codex on magical experiments which you have from me, I ask that you take care to send it back to me as quickly as possible. I shall soon send you the volume of Synesius on the power of spirits, when you have sent my experiments back to me. Take care of yourself, so that you may be well. And write back to me often. Given at Spanheim, on the sixth day before the Ides of March, in the year of the Lord’s incarnation 1501. Johannes Trithemius, abbot.

But the recipient of the letter, the one to whom you sent the spirit, should do what he knows must be done in this art; and, once the circular sign has been recognized, let him turn toward Eurus, and in silence say this imprecation:

Camuel Busarcha, menaton enatiel, meran sayr abatremon, naculi pesum nadru lasmon enotichamabet vsear lesponty a brulmy pen sayr thubarym, gonayr asmon friachar ynon otry hamerson, buccurmy pedauellon.

When these words have been said, let him take the letters again into his hand, looking toward Eurus, as you know. But it must be noted that all these spirits, together with their prince, are toward Eurus, and therefore everyone who works through them must turn himself in that direction; or, if he cannot, at least when he has received the letters he should set them down facing that direction. Observe and understand what we have said to you.

CHAP.
