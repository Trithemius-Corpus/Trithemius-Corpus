FIRST BOOK.

The first of these is called Geradiel. He has no duke or prince under him, but only many and almost innumerable servants, whom he takes with him for service according to the ranks that they have among themselves, and according to the 24 hours of day and night. Their general office is to announce all secrets, whatever they may be and to whatever region you wish.

In this table you have, numbered according to the hours and set out in their own order, 15,150 ministers and servants of Geradiel, the great prince. You should know that their arrangement and reckoning are altogether necessary for your operation, so that you may know at any given hour with how many servants they are accustomed to come, or when he himself is not accustomed to come, but only the servants assigned to the hour. I advise you, moreover, to take care in your operations to observe the hour in which the prince himself comes in person with the servants of that same hour, since otherwise, when their commander is absent, they are not always willing to obey the operator in all things. Therefore, when you wish to operate through this prince, after first doing the things that must be done beforehand, say the conjuration.

Charm of Conjuration.

Geradiel onayr bulesar modran pedarbon sazeuo nabor vielis proyn therdial masre reneal Chemarton cuhadian almona salty penoyr satodial chramiel nadiati thorays Vayr pean efriediel cubal draony myar dearyf coiludaryf menador atotiel Cumalym drafnodiar parmy sosiel almenarys satiel chulty dealny peson duaryf cuber fruony maroy stutiel, fabel merusi venodran pralso lusior lamedo syuaro larboys theory malrosyn.

When the charm has been completed, the spirit will be present, either the chief one whom you serve, with his servants, or the servants from the number of the hour without the prince, just as you wish according to the hours, as we said above.

The secret, whatever it may please you to entrust, will always be hidden.

Let there be any secret of mine, however secret, which is to be communicated through spirits to one or more friends perfectly instructed in the art of Steganography. I call the aforesaid prince at an hour suitable to me; I entrust the mystery; he receives it, goes, and conveys it altogether faithfully. I send letters merely for form’s sake, so that people may be deceived; and the one to whom I send them should know from the sign written below which spirit he ought to call, since they are not compelled.

I send any letters whatever, not secret.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, sends work to all learned and curious men reading in this art without a teacher.

Amend your life from the root. The strict examination of judgment will suddenly seize everyone, no one being safe, and in its bitterness and eternal griefs will fill the vicious; it will not dare to sadden the saints. With wailing, deplore your sins, most wretched and most foolish people. See, without good exercises you reject the name of religion. Do you not know that an examination is coming upon the guilty? The final hour approaches; we shall not live long, but all of us will be compelled horribly and unexpectedly to pass away. Therefore do not neglect the time; honor the grace granted to you by works. From Sponheim, on the 13th day before the Kalends of April, in the year 1500. X.

When these letters have been received, or any others that have been marked with the character of Geradiel, there will be no need to turn anywhere; but after the customary preliminaries in the art, the charm of conjuration must be said.

Charm of Conjuration.

Geradiel osayl chamerusin chault pemarsoniel dayr chaturmo les bornatyn ersoty camylor sayr fabelmerodan cosry damerson maltey nabelmerusyn.

When this conjuration has been completed, the spirits sent will appear visibly to you, and, speaking secretly in your ear, will make all things known to you.
