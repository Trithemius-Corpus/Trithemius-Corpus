And note that the operation through these is very different from the others, as we shall say at length concerning the lord. For in this mansion one must operate according to the twelve hours of the day and night, and four princes always preside over two hours, both by day and by night, so that it is not permitted for you to summon any other except from the order to which the hour belongs. Thus, if the operation takes place in the first or second hour of the day, call whichever you wish from the first four who preside over the day; if in the third or fourth, know that a duke must be called from the second four. And so, accordingly, it must be done through all the orders and the twenty-four hours, both by day and by night. But when you wish to operate in Steganography through the spirits of Dorothiel himself, after first performing the general preliminaries customarily set forth in the art, write whatever letters you wish, and, turning yourself toward that quarter of the world which from the wind we commonly call Chorus, call one or more of the four who preside over the hour in which you are operating. Dorothiel cusi feor madylon busar pamersy chearianothym baony Camersy vlymeor peatha adial cadumyr reneat thubra Cohagier maslon Lodierno sabeltrusyn. When these words have been spoken, you will see the appearing spirit whom you have called, ready and cheerful to obey; to him you may entrust your secret securely and faithfully. Roth.

Let us suppose any secret of the mind that is to be entrusted to letters.

You have business with a prince in any spiritual matter, very difficult and secret, which you think should be entrusted to no mortal, nor even to letters. Do you wish the prince to know it? You compose letters to him, supplicatory ones on behalf of a poor cleric, or any others at all; you call the spirit and entrust the secret to him; he faithfully carries everything out.

You will give whatever letters you wish, in which you need fear nothing.

I make known to Your Grace, most illustrious Prince, that Conrad Hummel, beneficed at the altar of Saint John the Evangelist, died last night.
