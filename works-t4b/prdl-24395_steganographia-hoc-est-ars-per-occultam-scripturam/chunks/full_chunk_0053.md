*Book Two.* 97

The sixth hour of the day is called Genapherym, and its angel is Saniel, great and powerful in his operations. Under his command he has 10 dukes, 100 counts, and 5,550 servants, appointed to the service of various operations; they will suffice for us in all the things that we wish to perform through Steganography in this hour.

The dukes, however, are summoned more rarely, although they are ready and very willing to obey our commands, because the counts are accustomed for the most part to perform their duties, since the dukes are often occupied in other magical experiments.

We have here named 5 from the dukes of Samiel, 10 from the counts, and 5,550 from the number of the servants, through whom the operation of Steganography is carried out in the sixth hour of the day. Therefore, when you wish to operate through these spirits, let it be done entirely in a secret place. And if you wish to summon one duke or more, let that be in your power, although there is no need, because the counts will suffice for making all things known to us. And so write on a clean little sheet the customary characters of the planets and signs, and on its back the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Samiel asiel thebrean pothir erfoty meat pornys layr moas, famerofyn. Then, after the things to be set forth first have been set forth, say the same conjuration according to the rite that you know; and immediately the summoned spirit will be present, ready and eager for all things. Send him far or near to a friend, so that he may convey the secret.

*Theme of the Secret.*

Whatever secret you have, you can safely send it to anyone skilled in the art of Steganography by means of the spirits of Samiel in the sixth hour of the day, because they are general for making all things known and in [unclear].
