Here I wish you to understand by East not the place where the Sun rises on any given day, but the place in which it was created at the beginning, which we call the equinoctial place. For unless you observe this consideration most diligently, you will not be able to make progress in this art. But now I shall also teach you the sequence of that table, so that from one spirit you may understand all. Therefore let us take Malgaras as an example, through whom the operation of this art is performed in the ninth chapter of the present first book. He is western; his sign is thus: ☒ ☓. He has beneath him thirty who preside over the day and have authority to put to flight the spirits of darkness. Subject to these are another thirty, who preside over the night and always dwell in darkness, nor do they come to the light except by command of their princes, to whom they are subject at all times. CHAP. II.

WHOSE PRINCE IS CALLED PADIEL, Roth.

Subsolanus, having under himself 10,000 ministers of the day, 200,000 of the night, and many other subservient ones, whose number is uncertain.

As we said in the preceding chapter, since Pamerfiel with his spirits named there is implacable and faithless, and does not readily obey all who desire to work in this art, but scarcely even the most experienced in it, in this present chapter we wish to make the art safer and to demonstrate a method of working in it through better spirits, who are benevolent, ready, and cheerful in obeying the one who calls them, and who, when called in the proper manner and at the proper time, come without delay and faithfully carry out whatever has been entrusted to them, without any deceit. Now the first principal emperor and presiding spirit of this operation is called Padiel, by name Subsolanus, of the circular mansion, which is the first mansion after the east. He has beneath himself ten thousand who preside over the day and over all daytime operations of this art; they command and bring forth the nocturnal spirits lurking in darkness, who are two hundred thousand in number.
