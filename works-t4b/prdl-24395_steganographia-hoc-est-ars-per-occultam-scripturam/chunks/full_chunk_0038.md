*Chapter XXV.*

*Whose emperor and prince is called Emoniel, having 100 subordinate leaders and princes, and many counts and sub-servitors, who have a general office over all things.*

The prince of this chapter, Emoniel, with his leaders and princes, is wandering and unstable, and frequently dwells in forests. He does not flee the light; he is benevolent and ready for all things whatsoever you command him, and, called upon, comes gladly by day as well as by night. He has under his dominion 100 princes and leaders, not fewer than 20 counts or subordinate leaders, and attendants and servants without any fixed number. From these we shall name some who will suffice for us in working, reserving the rest, together with others, to be named in their own place if there is need.

X. X. X. X.

Ermoniel Dramiel Cruhiel 10 20 100 20.
Edriel Pandiel Armesiel 10 20 100 20.
Carnodiel Vaseniel Caspaniel 10 20 100 20.
Phanuel Nasiniel Musiniel 10 20 100 20.

You now have 12 from the leaders of Emoniel, together with their subordinate leaders and servants, 1320 in number, who, according to the order of the hours, suffice for every operation of our intention, whether the operation is nocturnal or diurnal. And know that prince Emoniel, with his leaders, is accustomed to appear with a maiden’s head and body, but with a serpentine tail. Therefore, when you wish to perform something in Steganography through the spirits of Emoniel, do those things that you know must generally be done according to the art; and afterward read this conjuration, silently and under silence naming the spirit to be called.

*Song of Conjuration.*

Emoniel aproisi chamerufyn thulnear peanos meuear, pandroy cralnotiel narboy mauy fabelrontos, ariel chemorsyn nety pransobyr diuiel malros ruelty person roab chrumelrufyn. When this conjuration has been spoken in the manner that the art itself requires, the spirit whom you have called will appear, ready to obey in all things whatsoever you command him, because he is benevolent and faithful.

68.

*Steganography*

*A Secret, Whatever Kind You Wish.*

I have a friend who trusts everyone indiscriminately; in place of friends he cherishes enemies, from whom he often receives bad reputation and loss. He is credulous toward the flatteries of sycophants, and especially of one who clings to his side, whom he does not know to be opposed to him in secret, because he sees him flattering openly. I wish to accuse him, yet in such a way that the advice becomes known to my friend alone; to the rest it will be hidden.

Let the tenor of the letters be formed at pleasure.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Spanheim, to the Reverend Father and Lord Nicolaus, Bishop of Irburga, vicar of the Archbishop of Pantomora, greeting and due service.

When I call to mind your intellect and the honesty of your life, I am strongly compelled to declare you, most Reverend and excellent Father, the most worthy of all bishops. For truly, if I should speak of the innocence of your life, I would call you most just, most learned in all letters, and venerable for your most holy morals. You adorn your conduct with works of righteousness, and you always show yourself an excellent shepherd of souls; and in letters you continually exercise yourself by study, so that you may confer benefit on yourself and on others in both respects. Farewell. Given from Spanheim, on the eleventh day before the Kalends of April, in the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1500.

After the person to whom the letters are sent, perfectly instructed in the art of Steganography, has received them, let him do, as he knows, what must be done according to the art. When these preliminaries have been completed, let him add the conjuration.

*Song of Conjuration.*

Emoniel lebos chamerothy meor pemorsy dyor medulorsyn fray pean, Crymarsy melrosyne vari chabaryn dayr. Aschre cathurmo fabelron ersotus marduse. When the song has been duly completed, the spirit called will be present in the form customary to him, and will reveal to you, faithfully, all and each of the secret things that had been entrusted to him.
