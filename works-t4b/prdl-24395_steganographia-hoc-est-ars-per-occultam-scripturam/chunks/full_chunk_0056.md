Quabriel, odiel, amear, cayn alco mean chyr parcos payr peray, thubro menafry.

Then, after first doing the things customarily to be done beforehand, say the conjuration itself, as is prescribed in the art; and immediately the spirits called will be present, in whatever form you wish, ready and faithful for all things. Entrust the secret to the more powerful one.

*Argument of the Secret.*

Let our secret be whatever we wish to send to a friend, which we want to be most secret and in no way revealed to anyone except him. Therefore let us call one or two from the above-mentioned dukes and counts, according to the institution of the art, and let us entrust the secret to the more powerful one.

*Steganography*

Let us form letters at will.

John Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the Order of Saint Benedict, to the religious father John Hugo, guardian of the Friars Minor of Saint Francis in Kreuznach: greeting and charity.

Religious and venerable father, know that you freely grant to friends all things suited to them. Therefore I humbly beg that, in view of Catholic piety, you not take it badly that your zealous brothers have been kept with us rather long, with the greatest charity; for it was necessary, at my request, that they remain. Their glorious joy was a delight to us, as we all rejoiced without fault. We exulted in the Lord with spiritual joy, giving thanks to almighty God with good zeal. What gathering, I ask, is more religious than such a one? Where nothing idle is said, nothing wicked is pursued. May Christ Jesus, savior of all, make them rejoice in everlasting glory. Glorious lovers of the virtues place nothing before truth. We rejoiced with holy joy, praying that you receive the aforesaid brothers peacefully and put aside every zeal of bitterness. Farewell. From Sponheim, 3 Nones of April, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When you have received these letters, or any others, marked with the character of Quabriel, let the person skilled in the art, after the things to be done beforehand have first been done, say the prescribed conjuration. Once it has been said, he will immediately see the spirit that has been sent; and when he has seen him, let him say these words:

Mesraym paslotiel vear reneam cralt; thio phroysy ma meat hamorfy.

When these words have been said, the spirit that has been sent will approach gently and tell you the secret entrusted to him.
