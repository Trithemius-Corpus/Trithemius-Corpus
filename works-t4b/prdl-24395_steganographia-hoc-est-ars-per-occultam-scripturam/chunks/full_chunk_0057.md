But when you wish to work through them in this hour of the day, after first carrying out the customary preliminaries, and after writing what must be written on clean paper, with this conjuration arranged on its back, do the rest as you know.

Conjuration.

Oriel burnadiel irasmy crismean pormy ersoti amear medusen. After you have completed this conjuration according to the correct instruction of the art, you will see the duke present with the other spirits called, to whom you may entrust the secret.

Establishment of the Secret.

Let there be a friend of ours, whose good we favor; we fear that he is to be supplanted and deceived by some roaming impostor. We therefore wish him to be advised. We cannot do it by open words through messengers; by letters, lest they fall into someone’s hands, we likewise dare not. Therefore, so that we may be safe and our secret may always remain hidden, let us call at least one spirit, a duke of those named above. He comes, obeys, and faithfully carries the message.

166 OF THE STEGANOGRAPHY

Let the letter be composed as one pleases.

Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Spanheim, to Heinrich of Banau, gilded knight, distinguished philosopher and mathematician, sends greeting.

Since you are most devoted to the study of the liberal arts, you will rejoice exceedingly, I think, that you have found a new companion in studies. The man who will deliver this letter to you is a man of great knowledge, a supreme philosopher, a most subtle arithmetician, a most eloquent rhetorician, and a most profound mathematician, and in every way most excellently learned. He speaks most elegantly, shining with Ciceronian eloquence. If you were not especially my friend, I would have kept so great a man entirely for myself, since through experience of him I found him to be a most learned philosopher. I favor you greatly; receive with gratitude the philosopher whom I send to you, moved by singular affection. O that all our students were such, who with fearful heart despise noble knowledge! That man continually studies philosophy and the other sciences, and the loftiest depths of nature; he treats them constantly and penetrates them most acutely. Therefore remember, Heinrich Bunau, for Christ’s sake, to deal with the man with a generous countenance. Farewell from Spanheim, 2 November, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the friend to whom the letter is sent has received it, being skilled in this science of Steganography, let him do everything that must be done according to the art, and let him add the prescribed conjuration, as is customary. And when he has seen the spirit sent present, let him say these words to him, as to the more powerful among the spirits: Camyn aparsy aslotiel omeat reneas vean triamy cralty penafon. When these have been uttered, the spirit will immediately come nearer and will reveal the secret to him.
