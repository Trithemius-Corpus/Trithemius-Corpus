Conjuration.

Amenadiel aprolsy chametonta nostoy throen mestro salayr chemaros noe pean larsy freueon ionatiel peltroyn rathtroy Caser malusan pedon Cranochyrn daboy seor marchofyn lauo pedar venoti gestoy phernotiel Cabron.

When the conjuration has been duly completed, the spirit whom you have called will be present, ready and eager for everything that you have commanded him to perform.

A Secret Very Hidden and Necessary for Me to Send to a Friend.

I have a certain secret for a friend, which I do not trust to be conveyed either by a man or by letters, since if it came into public view it would create great danger and harm for me. I cannot endure delay; therefore I call a spirit, and write letters that are not at all suspicious, and which he himself is not thought even to understand, since he is ignorant of the Latin language. I send the spirit; he reveals the secret.

I Send Letters of Whatever Sort, Which an Invisible Spirit May Accompany.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, to the noble and vigorous knight Alberto Goeler of Ravensburg, prefect of Kreuznach, greetings.

We believe that our Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, is to come for judgment, who will render to each one according to his works. We believe this, and yet, wretched as we are, we neglect ourselves. Therefore let us strive to anticipate his face with continual tears, amending the negligences we have committed, lest that terrible coming of our Redeemer overtake us unprepared; all of us must necessarily meet him, to receive the reward of our deserts. Why, then, do we now make little of the opportune time of salvation granted to us, which the Almighty has conceded to us as so useful and so acceptable? Why do we not emulate men distinguished in morals, who seek out for us examples of justice, honorable conduct, humility, chastity, and all the virtues? Let us do penance, emulating their name, since the end of our life is near. Horrible death will come, which does not know how to spare, but will devour young men and maidens, and old men together with the young. Let us live in mourning, washing away our excesses with continual tears, lest unexpected death seize us and subject us to a horrible judgment. Let us amend our past life, praying God that after

When you have received these letters, or any others committed by me to a spirit, the friend to whom they are sent, being tested in the art, after the sign of the prince of this region has been recognized and after the preliminaries have first been performed, should say the conjuration:

Conjuration.

Amenadiel bulurym chameroy eriscoha pedarmon flusto pean truarbiel fabelron greos malgoty nabarym stilco melros suar pelatyso chitron amanacason.

When this conjuration has been completed according to what is required, the spirit, otherwise invisible, will appear visibly and reveal the secret.

CHAPTER XX.

Whose Prince Demoriel Dwells toward the North, Having under his dominion 400 greater dukes, and subdukes or counts with many subordinate servants and attendants, whose number is uncertain.

Toward the North dwells the great prince Demoriel, having under his dominion 400 chief princes and dukes, whom he sends into the ministry of this art according to their orders, together with subdukes and counts, whose number is not less than 600, for announcing all secrets that we wish to send toward the North to friends skilled in this art. From all those mentioned above, let us set down only as many names as are necessary for us at present, with the number of their counts and servants according to their orders in the hours.
