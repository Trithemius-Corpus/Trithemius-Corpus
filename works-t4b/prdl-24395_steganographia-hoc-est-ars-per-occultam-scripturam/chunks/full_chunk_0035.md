CHAPTER XXII.

Whose prince is Byriel, nocturnal, a shunner of light, dwelling in lairs and openings of the earth; and no operation is performed through him except at night.

After Geradiel follows Buriel, a great prince indeed, but perverse and wicked, who hates, persecutes, and detests all the other spirits, especially the princes; but he is no less hated by them. He fears and flees the light with all his dukes and attendants, and is never accustomed to come except at night, and this often with great horror and fear for the operator, especially if he is not perfect in the art, steadfast, and strong in spirit. And he often appears in the form of a serpent, having a maiden’s head, but a serpent’s tail and whole body, hissing terribly. When adjured in the proper manner, he speaks with human words. He has under him dukes and princes, whom he is accustomed to send into service with their attendants and servants, whose multitude is almost innumerable. Of these we shall set down the names of some who may suffice for our operation.
