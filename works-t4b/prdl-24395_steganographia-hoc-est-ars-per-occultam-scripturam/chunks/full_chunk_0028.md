STEGANOGRAPHY

Since I know, dearest brother, that you are a lover of the good arts, it greatly delights me to exhort you to persevere in searching out and continually turning over the secrets of books, which show us the road to happiness and the open path back into the homeland that we lost by sinning. To that homeland the burning love of our most gentle and clement Savior, hidden for us in the holy Scriptures, will lead us back, if we adorn the mind with virtues and exercise ourselves in holy study; this will without doubt be granted. For what could be more wholesome or sweeter for those wandering in miserable exile than to devote themselves diligently to letters? Through their study our eyes are lifted up to seek truth, and our mind is raised to the desire for future blessedness. Without them, the soul, submerged in temporal and worldly affairs, is compared to beasts and will never arrive at the knowledge of God. Therefore despise the pleasures and commotions of the world, and devote yourself to letters day and night; for religion is adorned by virtues, and virtues in turn by the commerce of letters. Thus one cannot value letters without the ornament of noble virtues, nor good virtues without knowledge of the Scriptures. Therefore I exhort you always to devote yourself to the study of letters. Farewell from Spanheim, 16 Kal. Apr., in the year of the Lord M.D.

After the one to whom the letters are sent has received them, once the sign of Baruch has been recognized and the necessary preliminaries have first been performed, let him turn to the quarter of Vulturnus, which is nearest before the eastern quarter, and let him say this conjuration in silence.

Conjuration of the Spirit.

Baruchas Mularchas chameron notiel pedarfy phroys lamafay myar chalemon phorfy fabelrontho theras capean Vearalmonym lierno medusan therfiel peatha thumar nerosyn cral nothiel peson segalry madon scoha bulayr. Once the conjuration has been said according to the precept of the art, the spirit will appear, and will bring forth into the ear, without deception, the secret entrusted to him.

CHAPTER XVII.

WHOSE PRINCE AND SUPREME SPIRIT is called Carnesiel, and he dwells in the East, having under him many dukes and princes with their servants, whose office is to announce everything that is sent toward the East.

SINCE the affairs of men are so many, and more may arise, for communicating which to friends far away no prince or spirit has been specially assigned in the aforesaid mansions, lest any difficulty should occur to those working in this art of Steganography in bringing their work to completion: after we have made mention of certain special spirits who preside over special secrets, we also wish to set forth certain general princes of spirits for general announcements. Thus, if anything to be announced should perhaps occur to someone, concerning which no commission is found in the foregoing, it may be referred to these, who in their own way have commission over all matters; yet so that the general does not derogate from the special, nor the special, if not expressed clearly enough, refuse the office of the general.

There are therefore four great princes who, with their dukes and subordinate servants, preside over all secrets to be announced that have not been expressed above. The first of them, Carnesiel, presides over everything that we wish to announce toward the East; Caspiel toward the South; Amenadiel toward the West; and Demoriel over those things that are sent generally toward the North. Therefore, when you wish to announce some secret through a spirit to a friend far away toward the East, call one or more, as many as you wish, from the princes of Carnesiel, turning yourself throughout the whole operation toward the eastern quarter, as you know according to the art. I shall describe for you the names of the princes, with the number of servants, as many as suffice for the present business; and take care not to err in anything.

R.S.R.S.
Myrefyn   Benoham   Armany      600000000000      R. S. R.
Ornich    Arisiel   Capriel     10   30   100   300
Zabriel   Cumeriel  Bedarys     10   30   100   300
Bucafaes  Vadriel   Laphor      10   30   100   300

You have twelve from the princes of Carnesiel, with a very great multitude of servants; here note that the large number 600000000000 belongs to other dukes and princes, who generally preside over all diurnal matters. From them a fixed number is called, as the operator wishes, with the duke.
