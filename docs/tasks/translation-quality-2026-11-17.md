## Correction priorities

1. **Seven Secondary Intelligences.** The print starts Michael’s period in 2126 and ends Samael’s in 4252. The English gives 2116 and 4152. Across a page turn the Latin says Solomon built the temple at Jerusalem. English segment 9 assigns that act to Gabriel. Read the preceding and following paragraphs together when editing these three passages.
2. **On the Commonwealth of the Church.** T4B turns Plato’s ten books into an “inadequate work”. The print counts Plato’s ten, Aristotle’s eight and Tullius’s six.
3. **Saint Anne and Hucbald.** In the Mainz hymn the petitioners are to be pure. The English calls the prayers pure. In Hucbald the earlier edition’s “knowing flesh” comes from a word meaning cut or torn. Recheck the two verse lines together in both tracks.
4. **Parallel alignment.** In the Dilibri *Sigilla* the Latin for English segment 6’s green-jasper cross is in segment 5. In *Vindicator* the sentence about Trithemius’s fear is one segment later in the Latin. Correct the links at passage level and preserve the existing English anchors. Institutional canvas and PDF page numbers also need a documented crosswalk before treating source links in other witnesses as exact.
