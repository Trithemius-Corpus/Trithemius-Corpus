There are also said to be several letters of his to Basilides. There is extant his notable letter against Paul of Samosata, which he had written a few days before his death. He also composed many other works for ecclesiastical usefulness, which are unknown to us. He flourished under the rulers Gallus and Volusianus, in the year of the Lord 255.

Novatian, priest of the Roman church, tried to seize the episcopal chair against Cornelius; when he could not do so, he stirred up the first schism in the Roman church, established the heresy of the Novatians, refusing to receive apostates who repented, and rebaptizing those duly baptized. The author of this error was Novatus, Cyprian’s priest.

He wrote:

On the Sabbath: a book.
On circumcision: a book.
On prayer: a book.
On Jewish foods: a book.
On perseverance: a book.
On the holy Trinity: a book.
Several letters to various persons.

It is said besides that he wrote some other works, which have not come to my knowledge. He flourished in the times of the emperor Philip, in the year of the Lord 250.
