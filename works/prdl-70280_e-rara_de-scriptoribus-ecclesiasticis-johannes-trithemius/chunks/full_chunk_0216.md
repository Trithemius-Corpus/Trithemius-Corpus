*Heinricus of Hesse the Younger*, of the Carthusian order, prior of the house of [unclear] Mary, a man learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular philosophy, subtle in intellect and clear in eloquence. He published many very elegant volumes, by which he left to posterity the memory of his name, and many of them he publicly lectured on in the universities before entering religion. Among these the following are reported:

On the Sentences, three books.
On Genesis, one book.
On Exodus, one book.
On the Proverbs of Solomon, one book.
On the Apocalypse of John, one book.
A Dialogue between a bishop and a priest, one book.
Useful Sermons, one book.
And certain others.

He died under Emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1428, in the sixth indiction.

*Johannes de Gerson*, a Frenchman by nation, chancellor of Paris, formerly a disciple of Peter d’Ailly, cardinal of Cambrai; a man most learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular philosophy, subtle in intellect, almost scholastic, sure and firm in judgment, cautious in counsel, and a very clear interpreter of doubts; distinguished in life and conduct, by far the foremost of the theologians of his time. He wrote, in verse and prose, many most excellent little works, by which his name will always obtain immortality among posterity; the reading of them is no less useful than pleasing to devout and religious men. Among these there are extant:

On the Praise of Writers, one book: “To search the Scriptures.”
On the Concordance of the Evangelists, one book: “That the Gospel has been spread abroad.”
On Ecclesiastical Power, one book: “Ecclesiastical power ought.”
On the Removability of the Bridegroom, one book: “But the days will come when.”
On the Manner of Holding Faith in Schism, one book: “For removing certain.”
On Ecclesiastical Unity, one book: “Ecclesiastical unity.”
On Ecclesiastical Estates, one book: “Peace, which all.”
On the Manner of Living of the Faithful, one book: “The first rule which.”
On Ecclesiastical Defects, one book: “Perhaps the Church says to me.”
On Schism, one book: “In the inquiry of truth.”
On a General Council, one book: “About to give occasion.”
A Trialogue on Schism, one book: “Very often the lofty.”
