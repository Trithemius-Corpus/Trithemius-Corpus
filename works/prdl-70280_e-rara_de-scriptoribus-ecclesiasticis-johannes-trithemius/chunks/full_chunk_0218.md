In addition he published many other minor works, indeed, but very useful ones, of which no certain knowledge has come down to us. He flourished in person at the Council of Constance. There, laboring gloriously for the extirpation of heresies, at length, when it had been dissolved, he was deprived of home, homeland, kinsmen, friends, dignities, and his own possessions for the defense of truth; he was beset by many plots. Hence, compelled by necessity, fleeing to the city of Lyon, he died there after a few years under the emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1429, in the seventh indiction, in the sixty-sixth year of his age, on the fourth day before the Ides of July. He was buried there among the Celestines with honor.
