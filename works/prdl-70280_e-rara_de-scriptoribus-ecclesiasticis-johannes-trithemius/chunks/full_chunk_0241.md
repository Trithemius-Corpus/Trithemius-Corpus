Finally, on many books of the Bible he composed elegant commentaries, and other notable treatises and almost innumerable solutions of questions. He flourished under Frederick III and Pope Pius II, in the year of the Lord 1460.

Johannes, abbot of the monastery of Neuzelle, studious and learned in the sacred scriptures, nobly learned in canon law, subtle in intellect, distinguished in eloquence, wrote many diverse little works of his intellect, by which he transmitted to posterity with glory the memory of his most celebrated name.
