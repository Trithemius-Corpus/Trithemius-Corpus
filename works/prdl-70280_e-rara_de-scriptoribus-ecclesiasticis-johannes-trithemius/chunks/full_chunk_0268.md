Robertus Gaguinus, a Frenchman by nation, minister general of the Order of the Holy Trinity, an outstanding doctor of Decrees, and most learned both in the divine Scriptures and in secular letters, a distinguished poet and a most eloquent orator, who often left the Italians stunned by his eloquence, excellent in talent, devout in life and conduct, wrote many excellent little works in both kinds, by which he made his name known far and wide.
