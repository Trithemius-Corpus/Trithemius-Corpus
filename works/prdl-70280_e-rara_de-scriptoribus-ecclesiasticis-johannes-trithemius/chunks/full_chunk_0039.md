He flourished under the emperors Arcadius and Honorius, in the year of the Lord 400.

Ambrose, bishop of Milan, a Roman by birth, a man most celebrated for faith and holiness, easily the most learned of all in divine Scriptures and in secular letters, perfectly trained in Greek and Latin speech; his merit the whole world proclaims, and holy mother Church confirms his works and books with a great proclamation of authority. In expounding and explaining the divine Scriptures he surpassed all teachers; no earthly power could ever break his faith, so that, though many bishops had been banished into exile for the faith, no one ever dared to touch Ambrose. For he was like a hammer of heretics, a most steadfast assailant; no human authority could ever impose silence on his tongue when it spoke in the faith of Christ.

Always ready to undergo any dangers for the faith, with the Lord protecting him, he escaped everywhere free and unharmed. He wrote many excellent little works, of which I have found only the following:
