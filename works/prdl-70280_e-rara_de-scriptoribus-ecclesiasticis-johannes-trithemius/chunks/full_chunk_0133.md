Peter, archbishop of Sens, erudite by continual study in the divine Scriptures, rich in the reading of the ancients, and moreover nobly learned in secular letters, subtle in talent, renowned in eloquence: he wrote little works of no small usefulness, by which he both cultivated his own talent and prepared material for the edification of others. He wrote very subtly, with the sentences of the fathers appended:
On all the Epistles of Paul: 14 books.
