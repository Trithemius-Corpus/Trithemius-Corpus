*BOOK.*

Alexander of Alexandria, of the order of Friars Minor, the sixteenth minister general, a man most erudite in the divine Scriptures, and in Aristotelian philosophy second to none of his time, subtle in talent, scholastic in speech, profound in sense and understanding. He composed certain little works in either science, by which, exercising his talent, he brought his name to the notice of posterity. Of these are reported:
On the Sentences: 3 books.
Various Questions: 1 book.
On the Metaphysics of Aristotle: 12 books.
On the books On the Soul: 3 books.
He commented also on other books of Aristotle, and wrote various [things].
He flourished under Henry VII, emperor, [in the year 1]310.

Eckhart, a Teuton by nation, of the order (as some will have it) of Preachers, a man [versed] in the divine Scriptures, subtle in talent and renowned in eloquence; but, while pursuing philosophy too much, he curiously mingled everywhere a novelty of terms against the custom of the theologians, [and] introduced various errors—among which there are extant seventeen articles condemned by the supreme pontiff, and revoked by the same master Eckhart at the end of [his] life; many of which are contained in his exposition on the Gospel of John. Finally, the master-theologians of the faculty of the gymnasium of Heidelberg, in the year of the Lord [1]430, denounced the same errors, with certain others, as publicly condemned. But eleven articles were left as suspect, which all are wont to be appended separately to his little works as a caution. Nevertheless, where he wrote catholically, he gave dogmas profound and useful enough. Of his books I have found:
On the Sentences: 3 books.
On Genesis: 1 book.
On Exodus: 1 book.
On the book of Wisdom: 1 book.
On the Song of Songs: 1 book.
On the Gospel of John: 1 book.
On the Lord's Prayer: 1 book.
A sermon in the chapter of the Preachers: 1 book.
Of his Positions: 1 book.
Sermons on the [seasons of the] year: 1 book.
Sermons on the saints: 1 book.
[The incipits of these works:] "This beginning of Scripture"; "These are the names, etc.… because I feared"; "Love, etc.— The judge always"; "In the beginning, etc.— Aquila [an eagle]"; "Our Father— before the Lord's [day]"; "I, as the vine, [brought forth] fruit"…
