Besides these he published several other works, which have not yet come into our hands. He flourished in the times of the emperor Louis IV and of Pope John XXII, in the year of the Lord 1320.

Guido Perpignanus, a Catalan by native land, of the Order of the Brothers of the Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, the twelfth bishop, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures, not ignorant of secular literature, outstanding in ability, sweet in speech, an acute disputant, distinguished no less in conduct than in knowledge. He wrote many very distinguished little works, by which he commended his name to posterity.
