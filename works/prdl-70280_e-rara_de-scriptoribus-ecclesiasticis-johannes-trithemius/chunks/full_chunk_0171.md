But he is also said to have composed certain other works, which have not come to my notice.

Petrus, of the Order of Friars Preachers, a man learned in the divine Scriptures through long practice, and a subtle and profound interpreter of scholastic philosophy, an acute disputant, an excellent preacher of sermons, sharp in intellect, scholastic in speech, published certain little works not to be despised. By fruitfully and usefully exercising his talent in them, he made his name known not only to those then living but also to future generations. Of these there are extant:

On the Sentences, four books; Sermons on the Season, one book; Sermons on the Saints, one book.

In addition, he adorned many books of Aristotle with most elegant commentaries. He flourished under Emperor Louis IV, in the year of the Lord 1320.
