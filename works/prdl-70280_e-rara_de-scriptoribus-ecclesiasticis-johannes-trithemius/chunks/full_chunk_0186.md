Hilmannus of Aachen, a German by nation, of the Order of the Brothers of the Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, a distinguished professor of sacred letters, outstanding in talent and an excellent declaimer, and also quite sufficiently learned in secular philosophy, which they call Aristotelian, wrote, for the use of readers, treatises not to be despised, by which he sent the fame of his name to distant places. Of these, one particularly noteworthy lecture is reported, which he read publicly at Cologne:

*On the Gospel of Matthew*, one book; also *On the Sentences*, four books. He is also said to have composed various sermons and commentaries on the Bible; about these, since I am uncertain, I can judge nothing certain.

Bartolus of Saxoferrato, once a hearer of the doctors Cinus and Jacobus Bothigarius, had few equals in the age: subtle and acute in talent, polished and eloquent in speech. He published certain excellent volumes, especially on the laws; how great their authority is today among jurists, no one even slightly learned and experienced is ignorant. Among them are reported the following distinguished commentaries on civil law:

*On the Code*, nine books; *On the Old Digest*, twenty-four books; *On the New Digest*, twelve books; *On the Infortiatum*, thirteen books; *On the Institutes*, three books; also *Tiberias*, one book; also *Counsels*, one book; *On the Guelphs and Ghibellines*, one book; and many others.

He died under Charles IV, emperor, and Pope Innocent VI, in the year of the Lord 1355, in the eighth indiction, and in the fifty-sixth year of his age.

Bertrus Thomas, of the Order of the Brothers of the Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, first bishop of Patti, then archbishop of Crete, and most recently patriarch of Constantinople, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures, most holy in life and conduct, since indeed, alive and dead, he is read to have shone with many signs and miracles, was of excellent talent in delivering sermons to the people.

He wrote certain excellent volumes, of which the following are reported:

*On the Sentences*, three books; also various sermons, one book; and certain similar things.

He flourished in the time of Charles IV, emperor, and Pope Clement VI, in the year of the Lord 1350. And he was buried in Cyprus.
