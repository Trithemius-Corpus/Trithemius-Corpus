Ugolinus, patriarch of Constantinople, of the order of the Hermit Friars of Saint Augustine, a man practiced by long study in the divine Scriptures and sufficiently learned, subtle in intellect, scholastic in speech, distinguished in conduct and life, is said to have written certain excellent little works, not to be despised in reading, by which he transmitted his name to the knowledge of posterity. Among these the following are said to be more important than the others: *On the Sentences*, three books; *Quodlibets*, one book; *On the Difference of Idioms*, one book; *Sermons on the Season*, one book; *Sermons on the Saints*, one book. He flourished under Emperor Adolf, in the year of the Lord 1290.

[Arlotus?], minister general of the order of the Friars Minor, Tuscan by birth, a man excellently learned in the divine Scriptures and sufficiently instructed in secular philosophy, a subtle dialectician and a very acute disputant, who won great praise for his learning by writing various works; in delivering sermons to the people he was of excellent talent. He is said to have brought together the New and Old Testament into one: *Concordances*, one book; *Various Sermons*, one book; and certain other things. He flourished in the times of Emperor Adolf, in the year of the Lord 1290.

Jacobus, bishop of Genoa, of the order of the Friars Preachers, a man practiced in the divine Scriptures, who because of his frequent citations of them is called a whirlpool; ready in intellect and clear in eloquence, he was sufficiently suited to delivering sermons to the people. He wrote not a few little works for the benefit of readers, by which he made his name known; of these a few have come to my knowledge, and I have set them down below. A volume of great size on the life and deeds of many saints, which he entitled *The Golden Legend* or *Mirror of Saints*, but which is now commonly called the *Legend* or *Lombard History*, one book, “The whole time of the present...” *Mariale on Saint Mary*, one book, “As I was thinking, the days of old...” *Chronicle of the Genoese*, one book, “Sacred Scripture, divinely...” *On the Little Works of Saint Augustine*, one book; *Sermons on the Season*, one book; *Sermons on the Saints*, one book, “He followed in his footsteps...” *Lenten Sermons*, one book. He also published some other things besides, which have not come to my knowledge. He flourished in the times of Emperor Adolf, in the year of the Lord 1290.
