These are the works from the writings of the most blessed teacher Jerome which at present have occurred to us as needing to be recorded. Nor do we doubt that he wrote more things which are not contained in this catalogue, especially because they either are unknown to us, or remain scattered among many things. For among all the doctors of the Church none was of more benefit than Jerome: besides innumerable little works, he translated the Old Testament from Hebrew into Latin, corrected the New according to the Greek truth, and faithfully offered it to the Church to be read. He also made many volumes of the Greeks Latin, namely those of Origen, Josephus the Jew, Eusebius of Caesarea, Didymus of Alexandria, Victorinus the martyr, and others.
