*ON ECCLESIASTICAL WRITERS. Fol. 8.*

Alexander, bishop of Cappadocia: when out of desire for the holy places he was going to Jerusalem, there, by divine revelation, he was ordained bishop after Saint Narcissus; he shone forth illustrious for great sanctity and learning; and at last, for the faith of Christ, under the most impious tyrant Decius, he completed [his] martyrdom. He wrote many elegant letters to various [persons], of which the following are reported foremost:
On the peace of the Church, to the [Antiochenes?]: a letter.
To the Antiochenes: a letter.
For Origen against Demetrius: a letter.
To Origen: several letters.
He suffered at Caesarea under Decius, in the year of the Lord 253, in the 1st indiction.

[Julius] Africanus, a most learned man, philosopher, distinguished historiographer and theologian, and a most ardent lover of the divine Scriptures and of books: by his own studies and at his own expense he founded a notable library at Caesarea in Palestine, in which he gathered many volumes, both of secular and of divine Scriptures. He composed, moreover, several little works of great usefulness, of which the following are reported:
Of Chronicles: 5 books.
On the Holy Trinity: 1 book.
On circumcision: 1 book.
On Attalus: 1 book.
On the Pasch: 1 book.
On the Sabbath: 1 book.
To Origen, on Susanna: 1 book.
To Aristides the philosopher: 1 book.
And he is said to have composed many others, which have not come to my knowledge.
He flourished under Marcus Aurelius, in the year of the Lord 220.

Gregory, bishop of Neocaesarea in Pontus, hearer and disciple of Origen, a learned and holy man, who also wrote much, and, living, shone with miracles. While still a youth, he—together with his brother Athenodorus, seeking the study of secular philosophy—submitted himself to the school of Origen, by whom, little by little and imperceptibly, he was converted, together with his brother, to the faith of Christ, and became equally a most learned and most holy man. Of his writings there is extant, [addressed] to Origen:
A Panegyric: 1 book.
Of Interpretations on Ecclesiastes: 1 book.
And several letters.
He flourished under the prince Gordian, in the year of the Lord 240.
