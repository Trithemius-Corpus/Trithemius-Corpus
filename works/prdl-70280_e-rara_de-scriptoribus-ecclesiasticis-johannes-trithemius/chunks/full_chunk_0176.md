Albertus Brixianus, of the Order of Friars Preachers, a man zealous and learned in the holy Scriptures, and not slightly learned in both laws; also an outstanding declaimer of sermons, and no less holy than learned, since he is said to have shone even in life with miracles. Among the other monuments of his talent he wrote the notable:

Summa on cases, a book.
Various sermons, a book.
And certain other things.

He flourished under Emperor Louis IV, in the year of the Lord [1340?].

Ludolphus, of the Carthusian Order in the house of Strasbourg, a German by nation, sprung from the Saxon land, a man zealous and learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular letters; gentle in disposition, sweet and well-ordered in speech, and also distinguished in life and conduct. He produced certain excellent little works of great devotion and deep learning, by which, nobly exercising his talent, he bestowed his name upon immortality. Of these I have seen only the following: a great and distinguished volume on the Gospel, which he entitled:

The Life of the Lord Jesus, “No one [can lay] another foundation.”
Also on the Psalter, “As once manna [unclear].”
Other works of his are also reported.

He flourished under Emperor Louis IV, in the year of the Lord [unclear].

Hermannus de Schildis, of the Order of Hermit Friars of Saint Augustine, a man most zealous and very skilled in the divine writings, outstanding in talent, and no less distinguished in life than in learning and knowledge. He produced many excellent volumes, by which he showed the abundant vein of his talent and transmitted his name to the knowledge of posterity with great praise and glory. Of these the following are reported:

On the First Book of the Sentences, a book.
A reading on Genesis, a book.
A double Hexaemeron, a book.
On the Song of Songs, a book.
On the subject matter of the Song of Songs, a book.
On the Lord’s Prayer, in two ways, a book.
On the Ave Maria, a book.
A Breviloquium, a book.
On the four senses of Scripture, a book.
A Manual for Priests, a book.
On the capital vices, a book.
The Cloister of the Soul, a book.
On the Conception of Saint Mary, a book.
On the Method of Studying, a book.
An Introduction to Law, a book.
On True and False Friendship, a book.
On the Canonical Hours, a book.
