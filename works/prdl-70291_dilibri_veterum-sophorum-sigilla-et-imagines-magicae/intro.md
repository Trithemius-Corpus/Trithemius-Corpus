*Veterum Sophorum Sigilla et Imagines Magicae* — "The Seals and Magical Images of the Ancient Sages" — is a short astral-magical text: instructions for engraving planetary seals and images on stones and gems, keyed to the divine name Tetragrammaton and the courses of the planets, "for producing miraculous effects." It belongs to the later esoteric corpus that attached itself to Trithemius's name after his death.

> **Attribution note.** This text is **not by Johannes Trithemius**. It is a 17th-century astral-magic grimoire — planetary seals and images attributed to Zoroaster, Solomon, and Hermes Trismegistus — published under Trithemius's name by the Marne / Aubry press in 1612. It is a *pseudo-Trithemian* work of the Hermetic tradition, riding his posthumous occult reputation, and it should not be cited as Trithemius's own writing. It is retained here because it circulated under his name and forms part of the Trithemius *legend*.
