The image of the Ship is with a curved prow and a raised sail, and sometimes without oars and sometimes with them. It is contained by Leo, and in the southern part; it is of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Carved on a stone, it makes the bearer secure in business carried on at sea and on water, and does not permit him to suffer harm from water.

The image of the Dog is the figure of a greyhound-like dog with a curved tail. It stands in the sign of Cancer, and in the southern part, having the nature of Venus. If it is carved on a stone, they say that it has this very power: to free lunatics, madmen, and the demon-possessed entirely.

The image of the Hare is in the likeness of a hare, with ears and feet stretched out as if it were running. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part. Its nature is Saturnine and Mercurial. Its virtue is to cure the frenzied and to be powerful against the wiles of demons; nor will the bearer be able to be harmed by a malignant spirit.

The Centaur is the figure of a bull as far as the neck, the rest being of a man, holding in his left hand a spear resting above his left shoulder, with a hare hanging from it; but in his right hand a little beast lying on its back, with a vessel hanging from it. It is situated in Libra and in the southern part, and is of the nature of Jupiter and Mars. The virtue of this carving is to make the bearer steadfast and in perpetual soundness of body.

The image of a seated Dog in Cancer is of the nature of Venus. When it has been carved on a stone and you carry it with you, you will not fear dropsy, and you will be freed from plague and from the dog’s disease.

The figure of a sacred vessel or censer, with fire kindled, is of the nature of Venus and Mercury. When it is carved on a stone, it makes the bearer fit to summon spirits and to be in converse with them, and renders them obedient. They also say that it adorns the bearer with perpetual virginity, since it induces chastity.

The image of the Hydra or Serpent is the figure of a serpent having above it an urn about its head, and a raven about its tail. It is of the nature of Saturn and Venus. Its virtue is to make the bearer extremely fierce, clever, cautious, prudent, and free from harmful things, and it resists every harmful [unclear].

The figure of the Southern Crown is likened to an imperial crown. It is of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its virtue lies in increasing riches and in making a man cheerful and pleasant.

The image of the Charioteer or Wagoner is the figure of a man in a chariot, holding a he-goat on his left shoulder. It is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the north, having the nature of Mercury. This figure, carved on a stone, makes the bearer a hunter and fortunate in catching animals.

The image of the Banner is made in the likeness of a banner spread out at the end of a spear. Carved on a stone, it makes the bearer fortunate in military service and victorious in battle in the open field.

The image of the Lion, carved in gold when the Sun is in Leo and the Moon does not aspect Saturn, preserves from stone and from hot illnesses.

The image of the Scorpion, impressed or carved on a gold ring and on a bezoar stone when the Moon is in Scorpio, and Scorpio is in one of the angles of the world, is effective against the sting of a scorpion.

The image of Aquarius benefits those who have false imaginings, such as melancholics, those afflicted by quartan fever, and madmen. It must be made on the day of the Sun and in its hour, and Jupiter must not be combust, nor must the Sun be higher than Jupiter; nor must Saturn be in the ascendant, nor in the 19th degree of Aries; the 3rd, 15th, or 27th of Taurus; the 11th of Gemini; the 3rd, 4th, 12th, 15th, or 19th of Cancer; the 2nd, 5th, or 19th of Leo; the 3rd, 13th, or 30th of Virgo; the 3rd, 5th, or 21st of Libra; the 7th, 18th, 20th, or 21st of Scorpio; the 13th or 20th of Sagittarius; the 12th, 13th, or 24th of Capricorn; the 7th, 18th, or 20th of Aquarius; or the 13th or 29th of Pisces.

The image of Pisces, when the Sun is in Pisces in the twelfth house and the Moon in the ascendant, and when the lord of the ascendant and the lord of the sixth house behold one another by sextile or trine aspect, or at least when the Moon beholds either one of them, is said by experience to free the bearer from gouty pains under the soles of the feet.
