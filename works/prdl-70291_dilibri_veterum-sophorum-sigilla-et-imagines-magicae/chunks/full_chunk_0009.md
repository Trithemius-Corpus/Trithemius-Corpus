The figure of a tree, or of a vine, or of an ear of grain, carved on some stone, makes the wearer abound in food and friendship, and renders princes favorable.

If armed Mars, or a robed maiden with a flowing garment, holding a laurel in her hand, is found on a stone, it makes the wearer powerful in accomplishing deeds of arms, and frees him from violent death, from drowning in water, and from adverse misfortunes.

The figure of an armed man on horseback with a lance, carved on amethyst, makes the wearer victorious in field warfare.

If you find the figure of Hercules holding a club in his right hand and killing a lion, or some other monster, carved on a stone, you should know that it gives victory in pitched battle.

The figure of Jupiter, as it were in the form of a man with a ram’s head, makes the wearer lovable, and quickly obtaining whatever he wishes.

A maiden or girl with a lamp, carved on crystal, has the virtue of preserving the light of the eyes.

The figure of the fiery triplicity, that is, of Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius, carved on a precious stone, is said to be effective against all cold illnesses, and to help against phlegmatic fevers; and, because of the dignity of the planets that hold dominion in those signs, to render the wearer pleasing to God and to men, eloquent, clever, and able easily to obtain honors and dignities. It must be carved in such a way that, in the triangle, Leo holds the apex, Aries the right side of the base, and Sagittarius the left.

The image of the earthy triplicity, that is, of Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn, engraved on a stone, will provide help against all hot and moist illnesses, and will incline the wearer toward rural works, such as plowing, planting, sowing seed, cultivating vineyards, and the like. Capricorn must be at the apex, Taurus on the right side of the base, and Virgo on the left.

The image of the airy triplicity, that is, of Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius, carved on a stone, frees one from all cold and dry illnesses arising from melancholic humor, especially quartan fevers; and Libra will be at the point of the triangle, Aquarius on the right side of the base, and Gemini on the left.

The figure of the watery triplicity, that is, of Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces, engraved on a stone, frees one from hot and dry diseases, such as hectic fever, and from all diseases of choler; but it renders the wearer inconstant, unjust, and a hater of truth. Cancer must be at the crown of the triangle, Pisces on the right side of the base, and on the left [unclear].

The figure of Saturn carved on stones is of this kind: an old man holding a sickle in his hand, having a beard that is not very hairy. This figure, carved on a stone of the nature of Saturn, renders the wearer powerful, with continual increase of power.

The image of Jupiter is that of a man seated on a throne or on a four-footed chair, holding a rod in one hand and a ball in the other; or holding the image of Cancer or a fish, and having an eagle before his feet. But among the teachers of magic he is represented otherwise: for they carve the figure of a man with a ram’s head, wrinkled heels, and a slender breast. If this is carved on a stone, especially [unclear: Krabatus], it renders the wearer fortunate, lovable, pleasing to religious men, and raises him to honors and dignities.

The image of Mars is represented on a stone in many ways: sometimes with a standard in his hand, sometimes with a lance or another instrument of war, yet always armed, and sometimes mounted on horseback. The virtue of this stone, if so carved, renders the wearer victorious, bold, and warlike, especially if it is carved on a stone of similar virtue.

The figure of the Sun is found in different forms: sometimes in the likeness of the Sun, with rays all around; sometimes [unclear].
