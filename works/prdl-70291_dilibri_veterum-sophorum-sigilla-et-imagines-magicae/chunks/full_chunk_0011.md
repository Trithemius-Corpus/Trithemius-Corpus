The image of a swan or a hen, with wings spread and neck bent, makes the bearer pleasing to the people, learned, rich, and drives away gout, paralysis, and quartan fever.

The figure of Cepheus, having a girded sword and his arms and hands spread out, carved in stone, makes one cautious and learned; and when it is placed beneath the pillow of a sleeper, it causes delightful things to appear in sleep.

Cassiopeia is the image of a woman sitting in a chair, having her hands stretched out in the manner of a cross, sometimes with a triangle on her head. It is situated in the sign of Taurus and in the northern part; its nature is of Saturn and Venus. When this has been carved on a suitable stone, its virtue will be to give health to people, to restore weak and weary bodies, to provide rest and tranquility to bearers after labor undertaken, and to bestow pleasant and sweet sleep.

Andromeda is the image of a girl with disheveled hair and lowered hands. She is contained by Taurus in the north and is of the nature of Venus. Its virtue, if it is carved in stone, is to reconcile quarrels and disputes that have arisen between husband and wife, to strengthen steadfast love, and to protect from very many illnesses.

Perseus is an image having a sword in the right hand and the head of the Gorgon in the left. It is placed in Taurus and in the northern part; it has the force of Saturn and Venus. If it has been carved in stone, it turns misfortunes away from those who bear it, preserves not only the bearer but also the places where it is kept from lightning and tempest, and also dissolves enchantments.

Serpentarius is the figure of a man holding a serpent, with its head in his right hand and its tail in his left. It is in the sign of Scorpio and in the north, and has the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its virtue, when carved in stone, is to be effective against poisons, to cure the bites of venomous animals, and, if the water in which it has been washed is drunk, to make poison be vomited out without injury.

The Eagle, or falling Vulture, is the image of an eagle flying with an arrow beneath its feet. It is placed in Cancer and in the northern part; it is of the nature of Jupiter and Mars, but the arrow is of the nature of Mars and Venus. When these constellations are carved in stone, they are said to preserve the bearer’s old ranks of honor, to acquire new ones, and to contribute to victory.

The Fish, or Dolphin, is the image of a humpbacked fish. It is in the sign of Aquarius and in the north, having the nature of Saturn and Mars. When it is carved in stone and bound on with the proper arts, it makes the bearer fortunate in fishing.

Pegasus, or the winged horse, or, according to some, the half of a horse with wings, according to others a whole horse with wings and without a bridle, is in the sign of Aries and in the northern part, having the nature of Mars and Jupiter. When it has been carved in stone, it gives the bearer victory in battle on open ground and makes him swift, cautious, and bold. But when it is hung from a horse’s neck, or put in water where the horse drinks, it will free him from many illnesses.

Caetus is the figure of a great fish with a curved tail and a large mouth. It is contained by Taurus and in the southern part, and obtains the nature of Saturn. This figure, carved in stone, when it has a crested serpent upon its back, makes the bearer fortunate at sea, renders him prudent and lovable, and restores things that have been lost.
