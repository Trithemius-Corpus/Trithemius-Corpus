*MAGICA. 17.*

…the head of a dragon, if it be carved in crystal, or in some precious stone, and be set in orichalc, and under the stone be musk and amber—if anyone [carry it] with himself… .

*SIGILLA.* [page 20]
…[carry] the stone in a ring, [and] all will obey; and if you touch a woman with it, she will love you; and if you put [it] under [your] head when you go to sleep, you will see whatever you wish.

The image of a man sitting upon a fish, and over the man's head a peacock: if it be carved in a red stone, and you place it under the table at a banquet, no one eating will be glad with [his] right hand.

The figure of a naked man standing, and at his right a girl standing, whose hair is bound about [her] head, and the man holds his right [hand] over the girl's neck and [his] left over [her] breast, and gazes at her face, but she [gazes] at the ground: when it be carved in some stone, and set in an iron ring, and under the stone be a little of the tongue of a sparrow, of a hoopoe, of myrrh, of alum, and of the blood of a man—he who carries this ring will be invincible, nor will anyone be able to resist him, nor will weapons [or other things] harm him. It is good also for epileptics, if you mark red wax with it; and if it be placed on a dog's neck, it does not permit [it] to bark.

The image of a man holding flowers in [his] hand, if it be carved in carnelian, and set in a tin ring, and the ring be made on the day of the Moon [Monday] or of Venus [Friday], at the 4th, 8th, or 12th hour—touch whom you wish with this ring, and they will straightway obey you.
