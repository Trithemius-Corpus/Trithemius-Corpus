## 2026-11-18 · sigilla-title-authors

The title gives Solomon’s title in full. In the two-column list the plural Iudaeis groups Raphael and Chael. Aegyptio aligns with Hermes and Persa with Thetel. These are the identifications printed in this witness. AI scan collation. No human verification of this correction is documented.

Previous wording: Solomon, [unclear];
Raphael, the Jew;
Chael, the Egyptian;
Hermes, the Persian;
Thetel.

Revised wording: Solomon, king of the Jews;
Raphael and Chael, Jews;
Hermes, the Egyptian;
Thetel, the Persian.

*Salomone, Rege Iudaeorum; Raphaele, Chaele, Iudaeis; Hermete, Aegyptio; Thetele, Persa.*. [Stadtbibliothek Trier, Dilibri, [5]](https://www.dilibri.de/i3f/v20/466744/full/full/0/default.jpg).
