The same Madelgarius founded the monastery of Soignies. He also built the monastery of Maubeuge. Saint Trudo founded the monastery of Hasbania, where he too is buried.

In the second year of the reign of Lotharius, king of the Franks, Saint Gertrude died, abbess of the monastery of Nivelles in Brabant, of the order of Saint Benedict, daughter of the elder Pippin, mayor of the palace of the kingdom of the Franks in Austrasia.

But the aforesaid Lotharius, king of the Franks, died in the fourth year of his reign, which was the year of the Lord’s nativity 666, in the ninth indiction.

*The fifty-fourth. Theodoricus reigned one year.*

Ebroin, mayor of the palace, a man excessively wicked.

Theodoricus is deposed from the kingdom as most insolent.

*The fifty-[unclear].* Childericus, brother of the two preceding kings, who was reigning in Austrasia, after the deposition of Theodoricus, was, by the common election of the nobles, taken up as king and monarch of the whole kingdom of the Franks. He reigned twelve years. His mayor of the palace was Wolfradus, a noble man and powerful in deed and word.

Theodoricus is deposed from the kingdom of the Franks in the first year of his reign, which was the year of the Lord’s nativity 667, in the tenth Roman indiction, and his brother Childericus, who was reigning in Austrasia, is elected by common consent.

Childericus the king is killed together with his wife.

In the ninth year of his reign, King Childericus, through the levity of his own conduct, kindled against himself the hatred of many nobles. Saint Leodegarius, formerly a monk of our order, bishop of Autun, having been driven from his see, he thrust into Luxeuil.
