Your race will first destroy me; long after this it will keep the lion captive by the sword. Take care to hear from its mouth what remains further. The lion-like head then said to King Marcomirus: Strongest king, stranger who will come into my [unclear] with your people, soon you will conquer the toad with toil, over much time; then my rights will yield everywhere and of their own accord to yours, by wars and by craft. Afterwards the Eagle will crown me, the right-hand victim, having on the left the toad bound by your seed. But what will be afterward, our conjecture shows you. When this lion-like apparition had uttered these things, the three heads were suddenly turned into one, and the three-headed monster stood transformed into the image of a man, and not of a simple condition, but of a king crowned with a golden diadem, holding a scepter in his left hand and a sword in his right; and, murmuring in a shrill voice, it said in the Scythian tongue:

Alruna, taught by songs to raise the shades and to bring down the highest gods from heaven, so that they may bring responses to your incantations, hear what we foreannounce is to be for the people of King Marcomirus, and understand, lest we speak in vain. As the transformation of our image confirms the form and makes certain this very voice of the prophecy, so to this people a varied and broad kingdom is promised for the future, which long time and the multitude of the ruling lineage will vary, so that it will neither always be one nor divided among many. Now one, then two, now three, then four and beyond. Now it returns into one. At last it will stand divided in two, when the eagle has been joined as a friend to the lion, and the toad has held the left-hand part of the eagle in ambush. When all these things had been said, the vision disappeared.

King Marcomirus asked Alruna what the meaning of the prophecy was. She said to him: It is the ancestral god, O king, who accompanies you and your people; informed by his predictions concerning the future for your people and for the kings who will be born from you, you should know from this that you must migrate as quickly as possible toward the farthest lands of the lion, toward the west. The lion designates the people who dwell on this side of the river Rhine, sprung from the ancient stock of the Trojans.

On the other side of the river, however, dwells the people of the toad, inhabiting a wide and fruitful region. The other, third people, designated by the eagle, are the Romans, whose pride labors to subject the whole world to its dominion. Therefore go with your people toward the west, and you will dwell between those two peoples, of the lion and of the toad, between the sea and the Rhine. For it is to come to pass that many battles will be fought between your people and the people of the toad, and many on both sides will fall, slain by the sword.

But after many years, at last your seed and your people will take by arms the whole land of the toad, and they will possess it for many years, after the toad has been killed.
