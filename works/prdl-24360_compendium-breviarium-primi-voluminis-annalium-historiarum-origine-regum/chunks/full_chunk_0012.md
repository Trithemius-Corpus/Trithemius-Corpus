Then they will also subject to themselves the land of the lion, from the western river as far as the great eastern river of many nations. And finally they will possess the kingdom of the eagle with many nations, and there will be one king of several kingdoms from your seed, until after the third generation unity will be thrown into confusion by division.

Marcomir the king, therefore, after richly rewarding Aliruna, commanded her to keep silence about the secrets, and ordered her to depart. On the next day, having again summoned the nobles to him, he praises the counsel, and by public edict warns that their settlements must by all means be transferred. And so, an embassy having been sent to the Saxons in Germany, who themselves too are said to have been born from the Trojans, they asked them as brothers to grant the Scythians dwelling-places in some corner of their land. The Saxons readily gave consent to their request, promising that they would give them excellent settlements, and adding nonetheless that they should hasten to come as quickly as possible. A reason pressed the Saxons, on account of which they gladly consented to their Scythian brothers: namely, because they greatly desired to set them between themselves and the Gauls as a wall and bulwark.

*Concerning the arrival of the Scythians within the borders of Germany, in the regions on this side of the Rhine, with their king Marcomir; they were first Trojans, then Scythians, afterward Sicambri, and in the final generation were called Franks.*

Therefore in the four hundred and third year before the birth of Christ, which was the beginning of the seventh year of King Marcomir, around the middle of the month Hecatombaeon, which we call April, King Marcomir himself, with Sunno, Panthenor, and Priam his brothers, having left dukes beyond the Danube and the sea of Scythia within the borders, returning on their journey and circling northward, and then turning their road toward the region of the Teutons, began their migration westward with the whole people of the kingdom, with wives, children, and all their household goods, with great labor, difficult and long-lasting.

There were, apart from women and little children, one hundred seventy-five thousand six hundred fifty-eight men most ready for war. And the names of the princes who were with Marcomir and had descended from his lineage were: Duke Nicanor, paternal uncle of King Marcomir; Sunno, Panthenor, and Priam, brothers of Marcomir, now said to be king; Duke Helenus; Duke Antenor; Duke Menander; Duke Edrasius; Duke Gethenus; Duke Priam; Duke Marenus; Duke Ermo; Duke Nicanor; Duke Pyndar; Duke Helan; Duke Marcomir; Duke Colan; Duke Masan; Duke Antenor; Duke Ancrasius; Duke Bengoth; Duke Calomer; Duke Rodanus; Duke Malda; Duke Kylan; Duke Solas; Duke Colo; Duke Bremus; Duke Sunno; Duke Melanus; Duke Nicamor; Duke Thaphon; Duke Salon.

Number of Franks coming: 175,658 fighters. Dukes of the Franks under the king: 24.
