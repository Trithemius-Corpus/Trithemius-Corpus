The thoughts of men are not in God, nor does he change his counsel according to time; but, since he is unchangeable and eternal, willing all things together and at once and always, he has from eternity unchangeably foreordained them. God speaks once, as sacred Scripture bears witness, and does not add, because whatever he wills he wills immovably, and he admits no change. Therefore God permits, by most just reason, that men be born good and evil, pious and impious, faithful and faithless, sinners and saints, those to be saved and those to be damned, for he has assigned this order to all things. Nothing perishes to God, who is Lord of all, and who everywhere holds firm dominion no less over evil men and spirits than over good ones.

Christ Jesus, God without time from God the Father, and man in time from the Virgin, appointed mediator between God and men, is himself the one through whom we have access to God. He is the savior of all the faithful; whoever rashly grants that anyone can be saved without Christian faith thinks wrongly of him. The omnipotence of God has regard to the order of things which he established; according to the inviolable sequence of its rectitude, no one will be saved without faith in Christ, no one who does not believe in the Son of God is justified.

Therefore those are moved by vain and most foolish pity who think that anyone in the whole world and in any sect is to be saved outside the faith of Christ, since his judgment is manifestly the contrary. Let them hear the Savior himself again saying in the Gospel: “No one comes to the Father except through me. For I am the way, the truth, and the life.” If no one comes to the Father except through the Son, certainly no one will be saved except through him. Who will save a Jew or a pagan, when no one can be saved except through Christ? But Christ will undoubtedly save only those who believe in him with heart, speech, and deed. He will not save a Jew, not a Saracen, not a pagan, not any other unbeliever, because they do not believe in him, but despise his doctrine, scorn the faith, and, blaspheming his holy name, hold it in contempt all day long. For he himself said in the Gospel: “Whoever is ashamed of me and of my words, of him I also will be ashamed before my Father and his holy angels.”

Tell me now, you who worship Christ and contend that someone can be saved in his own sect, which he thinks true, outside the Christian faith: do you believe the Gospel of Christ is true or false? If true, why do you hold an opinion contrary to it? For Christ says that whoever has not believed in the Son of God, the wrath of God remains upon him, and he is already judged and will be condemned, and that “no one comes to the Father except through me.” But you, seduced by I know not what rashness, thrust in the contrary, saying that a man outside the faith of Christ, trusting in the religion of his fathers which he believes to be true and to please God, or living innocently according to the law of nature, especially when perhaps he has never heard anything of the faith of Christ, is to be saved in that confidence. Christ did not say this, but: “He who has believed in the only-begotten Son of God will be saved; but he who has not believed will be condemned.” He excepted no one, admitted no condition, because he who had come to suffer for all had provided for the salvation of all through faith alone. If faith in the Savior is lacking, no confidence will now save the unbeliever.

For the holy bishop Augustine said in On Faith to Peter: “Hold most firmly and by no means doubt that not only all pagans, but also all Jews, heretics, and schismatics who end this present life outside the Catholic Church will go into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.” You now see where foolish compassion drags you, you who measure God by human laws and, so that in all things you may make his only-begotten Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, merciful, do not fear to judge him a liar. But Christ is truthful, who says that those who do not believe in him are damned; but you are rash and lying, who trust that anyone can be saved outside his faith. Whoever truly believes in Christ cannot at all allow anyone outside the unity of the Church of Catholic Christians to be said to be saved. Whoever says the contrary shows that he is not a true and good Christian. For this is the inviolable judgment of Catholic tradition, which the Lord Jesus himself, the Son of God, defined in the holy Gospel: namely, that no one is to be saved outside his faith, which he himself established and taught.
