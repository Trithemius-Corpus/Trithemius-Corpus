Therefore whoever asserts that a man is to be saved in any sect whatever outside the faith of Jesus Christ, or believes this in his heart, is not a good Christian, but rather an apostate and a disciple of Antichrist; and unless he comes to his senses, let him know that he is to be tormented forever in hell. For he is an Antichrist, not a Christian, who says that one who does not believe in the Lord Jesus is to be saved without the Savior.

Therefore, most wise Caesar, let no one appear in the sight of your Majesty, let no one feel that you cleave to him, who thinks that men are to be saved outside the faith of Jesus Christ. For everyone who believes that anyone is to be saved outside the Christian faith has denied both Christ and his Gospel.

But when we say that all who are not Christians are to be damned, this is not a sign of cruelty but of true piety. For it is better to believe firmly in the truth than to agree falsely with a man. For that all Jews, pagans, gentiles, Saracens, schismatics, heretics, and unbelievers are without doubt to be damned forever with the demons, Holy Church testifies; and she has taken and confirmed this judgment from no other source than from the words of Christ.

For thus he said: “He who believes in the Son has eternal life,” by which good Christians in faith and work are designated. “But he who is unbelieving toward the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of God remains upon him.” Here are denoted all Jews, pagans, Saracens, gentiles, and all who do not believe in our Lord Jesus Christ.

Concerning schismatics and heretics, Christ’s judgment is plain in the Gospel, when he says: “If he will not hear the Church, let him be to you as a heathen and a publican.” Heathens are gentiles, and publicans are manifest and public sinners; and Holy Church does not doubt their most certain damnation. For what Christian would doubt that gentiles and gross, manifest sinners are to be damned? Since, therefore, all schismatics and heretics despise Holy Church, they are most rightly said to be like the gentiles and publicans who are to be damned for their offenses and demerits, and they will justly undergo equal punishments of everlasting damnation with them.

Behold: those whom the just sentence of Christ the Savior has condemned, the foolish compassion of men, not to say impious cruelty, presumes are to be saved. They desire to be merciful toward lost men, and they do not fear to show themselves cruel toward God. Those whom he has assigned by justice to death, these men, with the worst presumption, assert are to be recalled to life. It is not good or useful for a Christian, whether in earnest or in jest, to contradict Christ.

He is impious, not Christian, whoever says that unbelievers are to be saved. When Adam, the first man, perished, we all sinned, and the wrath of God remained upon the whole human race from the beginning of the world until Christ. Christ came, born of a virgin; being God and man, innocent of sin, he voluntarily underwent the death of the cross, and, becoming mediator between God and man, removed the wrath due to original guilt from those who believe in him. For thus he says: “He who believes in the only-begotten Son of God has eternal life. But he who is unbelieving toward the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of God remains upon him.”

Indeed, without faith in Jesus Christ no one will be saved, however justly and holily he may seem to live in his own religion. For almighty God can save a believer without material baptism, but he cannot make one who denies the faith safe without faith. No one is saved unwillingly, because no one believes except willingly.
