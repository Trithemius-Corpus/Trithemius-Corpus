Secular learning has more power to exercise the intellect than to enlighten it for true wisdom. Therefore, without doubt, divine Scripture is clear and bright to purified minds through continual practice of the fear of God and frequent devotion to reading, though to filthy minds, swollen with worldly wisdom, it seems confused and obscure. Hence he understands the holy Scriptures well and rightly who, with purified affection and constant in their study, explains them in the same sense as the one who wrote them. For if they are read in any spirit other than that by which he is believed to have inspired them to be written, the mind must be wholly shut out from true understanding.

But when we meet with any discordance in them, let us understand that it belongs only to the words, not to the meanings; and this arises in ordered fashion not by chance but by the providence of God, so that the mind, exercised longer in the Scriptures, may be strengthened and not grow altogether sluggish. For God’s counsel cannot be deceived; but the divine Scriptures are Scriptures by the counsel of his will. It follows necessarily that they are true and in no way false. They were men by whom the divine Scriptures were handed down to us, and certainly words customary in human usage. They wrote with words by which they might signify to posterity what that supreme excellence of divinity wished to be known to mortals.

Therefore you now, who are devoted to worldly wisdom and attack the divine Scriptures, say: the Scripture of the New and Old Testament, which the Church has received and venerates as the true, authentic, and holy Scripture of Christians, from where do you believe it was received? From God, or from men? If from God, why do you not believe it? For everyone who attacks Scripture which he believes to be inspired by God most plainly resists the divine ordinance. But if it is from men, why do you hope in it for the eternal salvation promised there, which man cannot give? Unless perhaps, madmen, you go on to say that all things are fabricated, and that nothing remains after death except ashes, and that the mind perishes together with the body; by such a profession you would show yourselves no less insane than foolish.

For if you truly believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten Son of God, you will necessarily say that the divine Scriptures were inspired by the Holy Spirit. But if, made one with apostates in a reprobate mind, you say they were fabricated by men, you impiously show how far you are from true wisdom, wandering in darkness. You openly reveal yourselves to be ignorant and rash judges, since you condemn a Scripture which you do not understand.

But because it is more familiar to you to despise wisdom than to learn it, it is not the purpose of this work to weary the mind any longer against the rashness of the ignorant. This one thing I wish you to know: no one understands unless he receives; but because you place the filth of your affections in the way, so that you may not receive, therefore you do not have the understanding of true wisdom.

For almighty God deigns to be present as illuminator through grace to those who, stripped of carnal desires, burn with the fire of inward love, who desire nothing in this world except God. To them the Spirit of God brings help by his presence; those whom he finds rooted in the love of truth and purified from every foreign thing, he brings into the knowledge of all things. For to those who truly love God all things are open, all things naked, all things manifest, since whoever through love beholds in faith Jesus Christ, creating and governing all things, knows all things that are necessary for his salvation.
