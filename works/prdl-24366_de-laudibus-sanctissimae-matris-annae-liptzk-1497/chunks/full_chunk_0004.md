We believe without doubt that he came forth from her daughter’s womb without the seed of man. Whatever praise and veneration we bestow on her, let us firmly believe that it belongs to the honor of the highest King. For the honor of the most holy mother Anne, all faithful, come together; pay praises to the most high Lord in her memory; rejoice with religious devotion out of reverence for her.

Believe me, for I have experienced many of her benefits: you will have her ready as an advocate in every tribulation, if you celebrate her memory with faithful devotion. Therefore she is to be honored by all, for at her merits the Son of God grants favors at her nod; her honorable name the court of the citizens above honors with the greatest reverence; the queen of heaven, the mother of God, regards her as her sweetest parent. Whoever chooses her as patroness, whoever perseveres devoutly in her service, shall be saved both now and forever. For the Son of God, who is accustomed to grant benefits to mortals through his beloved mother, cannot deny them to the merits of his grandmother; indeed, he sometimes grants greater things through her, so that he may draw us, as it were by gifts, to her memory, long neglected. The Son of God can never despise his own parents, he who so earnestly commanded us to honor the parents of others.

Invocation of the author to God and Saint Anne and all the saints above, that the grace of writing may be granted.

O God, true light, who enlightens every person coming into this world; God, who justifies the ungodly and calls sinners to repentance; God, who gives wisdom to little ones and makes eloquent the tongues of those who stammer: grant me, I beseech you, pardon for my sins; grant me the wisdom of your praise; grant me light, through which I may understand, know, and love you, and may burn so with love for most blessed Anne that I may worthily embrace her praises in this work.

O God, who brought forth water from the hardest rock for your thirsty people in the desert, soften the hardness of my heart, that I may be able to praise you with such devotion of mind in your saints, in the grandmother of your Son, in the parents of your Only-Begotten, that, through your mercy and her intercession, this little night-work of ours may serve for devotion not only for me, but also for all who read it.

O God, who opened the mouth of the beast, so that it uttered human words against the foolish man, mercifully water the dryness of my tongue by the infusion of your grace. Strengthen my trembling hand with the Holy Spirit, that concerning most holy Anne I may write nothing except what is right, persuade nothing except what is holy, utter nothing except what is fitting. Grant me, I ask, almighty and eternal God, the help of heavenly grace in this work, you who have granted me the intention to undertake it. For I am a worm and not a man, wretched and unhappy, sold under my sin.
