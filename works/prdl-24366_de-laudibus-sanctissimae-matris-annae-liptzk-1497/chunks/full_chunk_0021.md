Let the man hardened in heart and reprobate in piety blush, who claims that he loves the Mother of God and is shown not to love Anne, her mother. It is foolish and ruinous presumption to place confidence in the daughter’s patronage while scorning to honor her venerable mother. For he who has the mother hostile to him will not be able to find the daughter appeased. For the daughter loves her mother, and the whole host of the heavenly court venerates Anne. If anyone has Anne as his advocate, he will have all the saints in heaven as patrons. Therefore, dearest brothers, let us love the mother, who will be able to bring us into the sight of God. For she is pleasing and very near to God.

On the feast and commemoration of the most holy mother Anne, to be held by us especially in honor.

Finally, if we love Anne, the most blessed parent of the Mother of God, if we wish to honor her with fitting honor, it is fitting that we celebrate with yearly devotion her honorable feast, which is on the seventh day before the Kalends of August. Nor should we believe that this is sufficient for our devotion, unless we take care often and frequently to observe the commemoration of this most singular patroness.

And if an adversary wishes falsely to object to us that this feast is new, let us answer: We are not introducing new feasts, but renewing ancient ones. And on this matter I believe I have argued sufficiently above; I do not doubt that satisfaction has been given to the devout worshippers of Anne. To wish to satisfy curious and proud men is superfluous, since piety does not move them, devotion does not stir them, charity does not inflame them.

Therefore let the devout worshippers of blessed Anne believe and hold without any hesitation that her feast is ancient, and that it was customarily celebrated by the ancients with the greatest reverence, as was fitting for the mother of the most holy parent of God. For in Judea and in the regions overseas, among the faithful Christians, her commemoration was very celebrated, until at last, after the land had been occupied by unbelievers, it was neglected there for a long time. But in the regions of Europe her feast was not so celebrated, because many there came to the faith later. Yet in certain places of Europe there was always a solemn remembrance of Anne, which had been established by the faithful coming from Palestine. The altars erected in her honor long ago in various churches prove this; very ancient oratories also prove it, and the various writings of the fathers on the praises of Anne.

But why do we try to defend the antiquity of this feast, which no good and devout person will wish to attack? We have no need at present to dispute against noisy quarrelers, whom we could never persuade to the worship of most holy Anne.
