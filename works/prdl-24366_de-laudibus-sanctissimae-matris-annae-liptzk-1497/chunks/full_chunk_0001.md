The letter of Lord Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, on the book concerning the praises of the most holy Anne. To the venerable and most religious father, Lord Rumuld Laupach, prior of the convent of Frankfurt, of the Order of the Brothers of Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel: Johannes of Trittenheim, Abbot of the monastery of Saint Martin in Sponheim, of the Order of the divine father Benedict, sends greeting and charity.

Desiring to satisfy our vow and your requests, excellent father, we have written with sincere devotion a book on the praises of the most holy Mother Anne, and have dedicated it to your name, since before others you seemed worthy to us, as being a very great worshipper and most devout lover of the same venerable Anne.

But from the history about her which is rather widely circulated we have taken little or nothing, because it seemed to us both less well proven and, in very many places, apocryphal. Yet we did not wish to refute those accounts, lest we give simple people an occasion for murmuring, if, as they themselves think, we were to censure with new presumption things approved by antiquity. We have therefore left that history untouched, and whatever had been known as most fitting by faith, truth, and reason, we have brought together by God’s gift for the honor of holy Mother Anne.

We have also excluded the miracles of Saint Anne from this volume, lest we make our treatise suspect to learned men, who do not easily give credence to such things. And so at another time, if you wish, a history of them will be composed. If you should happen to hesitate over anything, write back; you will find us ready to show you and everyone the reason for each of the things we have written. After the end of this work we have added certain poems on the praises of the holy Mother, which have been sent to us by various learned men. Farewell.

From Sponheim, on the Kalends of July, in the year of the Lord 1494.
