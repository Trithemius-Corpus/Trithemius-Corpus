The merits of Lady Elizabeth are rightly set forth; Saint Elizabeth bore a soldier, Anne bore the Mother of God. There is no comparison where there is no likeness. If Elizabeth was full of grace because of John, I believe Anne obtained grace much more because of Mary.

Anne was holy before she conceived the Mother of God, yet in that conception she was sanctified still more abundantly, because she deserved to become the tabernacle of her who was full of grace above all creatures. We call a casket or ark in which the pledges of the saints are kept sanctified and honorable; how much more ought we to venerate Anne as venerable, honored, and sanctified, in whom the Mother of God lay without stain. Truly holy is she who bore her without the guilt of original stain, than whom no one was ever holier. Truly holy is she who was chosen to be the mother of the God-bearer before she was born.

O womb never to be named without honor, in which the ark of God deserved to be fashioned without stain. Blessed the belly that carried the Lady of heaven; happy the breasts that deserved to nurse the Mother of God. Run, peoples, from every side, and receive the blessing from the merits of your holy patroness.

For indeed, if the holy devotion of the faithful is directed toward her, we believe that through her the Almighty will give great gifts to mortals, and no smaller ones than he is accustomed to give through her daughter. Therefore you, faithful peoples, who believe the daughter of Saint Anne to be the Mother of God, who rest under the sanctified shadow of both, if you desire to please in the love of God, if you wish to come to the heavenly homeland, do not neglect the memory of this most holy mother. For after the Queen she is most powerful in the court of the heavenly Emperor, because from her blood the Son of God took flesh from the Virgin.

When in tribulation you cry to her, you will find help. She is rich enough to grant you what you can ask for in faith. She heals the sick, purges crimes, gladdens sorrowful hearts with holy exultation, calms sadness, smooths rough things, puts to flight the phantoms of demons. As ice is dissolved by fire, so by the merits of the most holy mother Anne the ancient enemy flees in confusion. Anne, ark of piety, drives away disgrace, preserves a fair reputation, and wondrously removes shameful love from the minds of her faithful.

Therefore honor Anne, you who pass through the toilsome life of this world. Anne obtains pardon for sinners, roots out vices, plants virtues. Anne cures diseases, heals wounds, drives out pestilence. Honor this mother, then, O children, for she is powerful to bring you into the sight of God. Whoever has her as advocate before God will fear no adversities of this world.

Therefore whoever desires to lead a tranquil and holy life on earth, whoever earnestly seeks forgiveness for his sins, let him never depart from the service of this most holy mother.
