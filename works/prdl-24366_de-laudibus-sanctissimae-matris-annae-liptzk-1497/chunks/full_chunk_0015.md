Take my counsel, and change your position. Return at last to yourselves, for it is time, and do not presumptuously defend an uncertain opinion, which one may pass over undiscussed without sin. If you love God, if you love Jesus Christ, the Savior of all, do not do so great an injury to his most holy mother, concerning whom no one is permitted to think anything except what is most holy. To believe in her conception as always most pure belongs not to necessity but to piety; not to believe belongs to human slowness. But, while not believing, to attack it and to wish to defend contrary opinions is not only not free from fault, but is also a matter of great rashness and folly. Indeed rashness is rightly rebuked where piety is rewarded; and let no one think it a small sin that is committed against God and his mother. For whoever loves the Son of God does not open his mouth against the honor of his mother, does not detract from her purity, does not stubbornly defend an uncertain opinion; and where the devotion of the humble receives reward, the presumption of the proud will find torment. For the Mother of God is believed to have been worthy to be conceived without the stain of original sin, she who, as a virgin, was to bear him who would blot out the sins of the whole world. Blessed mother Anne also proved worthy of such a conception, whose renowned way of life was most pleasing to God. Do not wonder that the Mother of God, beyond the usual course of nature, was preserved from original sin, since God, the pourer-in of grace, had prepared her as a dwelling for himself. Consider the one born to be the Mother of God, and you will not wonder at her conception without stain. Great are the things that they say, but they are true, holy, and fitting for the Mother of God; grace forestalled her conception, and at this nature marvels. We too marvel, because we faithfully believe; but her adversaries, not believing, do not marvel, but are confounded. If you can grasp these things, believe; if you cannot grasp them, do not examine them, if you do not wish to sin. For it is better to keep reverent silence concerning the purity of the most blessed Mother of God than to speak unworthy things. Therefore take care always to honor with humble silence the things that you cannot understand.

How the most blessed mother Anne presented her sweetest daughter to the Lord in the temple. Chapter 8.

At the beginning of the book of Kings we read that Anne, the wife of Elkanah, offered in the temple, for the service of God, her son Samuel, whom she had asked from the Lord, after the years of his weaning. Samuel’s offering was so pleasing to God that he not only chose him as priest after Heli had been rejected, but also filled him with the spirit of prophecy and wisdom. What, brothers, what else is indicated to us by Samuel’s offering than that the most blessed Mother of God was presented to the Lord in the temple by her mother? O how pleasing to almighty God was that offering, in which already then a most worthy dwelling was being prepared for the Only-Begotten.
