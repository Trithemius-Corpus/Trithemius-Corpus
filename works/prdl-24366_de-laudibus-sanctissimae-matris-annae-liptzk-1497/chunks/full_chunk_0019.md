That the blessed mother Anne is powerful before God to come to the aid of those who devoutly invoke her.

Chapter.

Since Anne, the holy and most blessed mother of the Mother of God, is in the kingdom of heaven very near to the Son of God and to his mother in merit and charity, there is no doubt that she is powerful to obtain help for our miseries, provided that the devotion of our mind is diligently directed toward her. She cannot fail to be most merciful, who bore the mother of mercy in her womb. How merciful and powerful and ready she is to drive away human miseries, there are almost as many witnesses as there are devout worshippers of her. For she is powerful in the palace of the supreme emperor: God hears her when she prays, out of reverence for her, and whatever she asks he grants without delay.

Let him doubt her authority and power who does not know that she gave birth to the queen of angels. Let him hesitate over the mercy of this most blessed mother who does not know that the Savior of the world came forth from her most noble blood. She is powerful and near to God; she is merciful and inclined toward our necessities. Believe me, brothers, for I am certain: God is accustomed, through her merits, to grant many benefits to human beings, so that they may learn always to keep her memory in honor.

Therefore approach such a merciful patroness with confidence; knock upon her with diligent prayers, for she is powerful to obtain pardon for you, powerful to lead you to eternal life. She lacks nothing by which she can provide for our necessities; there is nothing she does not have by which she can aid us, since she always has the ability to act and the mercy to will it. Therefore let us doubt nothing, brothers, let us hesitate in nothing, for her sure intercessions will be present to us if firm faith is not lacking. From whatever tribulation we cry out to her, we shall find ready help, if we devote service to her name.

Do you wish to know, brothers, what this most holy woman is able to obtain for you? Listen. Anne drives away sadness; she destroys the lust of the flesh; she relieves poverty; she cures infirmities; she softens adversities; she quiets opposition; she removes vices; she plants virtues; she enlightens the mind; she purifies the affections; she remits crimes. Therefore, whoever you are who have fortune opposed to you in good endeavors, whoever you are who feel the storm of this pilgrimage against you, invoke holy Anne with devout and diligent prayers; knock upon the mother with prayers, and you will find remedies of unexpected consolation.

She herself is a safe refuge for the wretched, a sure remedy for the sick, an unfailing comfort for the desolate. Why do you hesitate to approach her, who is both powerful and willing to help you? She requires nothing earthly from you: not gold, not silver, but only a heart stretched out in devotion toward God and Saint Anne. Why, then, do you fear? Why do you waver?
