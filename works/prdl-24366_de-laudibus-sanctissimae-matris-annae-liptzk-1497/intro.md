*De Laudibus Sanctissimae Matris Annae* — "In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne" — is Trithemius's *tractatus* honouring St Anne, mother of the Virgin Mary, written at Sponheim with a dedicatory letter to Rumold Laupach dated 1 July 1494. It circulated with a set of commendatory poems on the Holy Kindred and belongs to the early, hagiographic side of his Sponheim output — the world of late-medieval Marian and Anna-devotion that the Reformation would soon dismantle.
