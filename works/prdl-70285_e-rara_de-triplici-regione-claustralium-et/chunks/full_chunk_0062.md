All these things are full of mysteries; if they are examined with diligent consideration, they will magnificently commend this supreme region of the spirit. For Mount Sinai signifies the height of this region; the smoking darkness represents the object of contemplation; Moses expresses the affection burning in love; the people show the crowd of thoughts and phantasms. Moses alone, the leader of the people, is permitted to climb the mountain and enter the darkness, because the affection, inflamed with divine love, alone is raised to the summit of highest contemplation. But the people are forbidden, under threat of death, to approach the mountain, because no intellect, no thought, no imagination or reasoning will be able in any way to penetrate the secret of the divine majesty. The people stand far from the mountain, terrified with fear, because while the mind alone enters through the affection of love into the abyss of contemplation, the whole crowd of thoughts, utterly failing, is disturbed. The intellect does not dare to draw nearer when the affection ascends, but, standing far off and shaken with fear within itself, is forced to remain. Hence, yielding to the majesty, astonished, it says with the prophet to the Lord: “Truly you are a hidden God.” But when Moses is on the summit of the mountain, he is wrapped in darkness: he does not grasp God by the light of the intellect, but passes into contemplation of him only through love and desire. Moses, and not the Lord, speaks to the fearful people, because the purified affection enters the dwelling-place of love, through which the powers of the spirit are enlightened.

*By what methods or steps one must ascend to this supreme summit of this region.*

*Article IX.*

Although in many preceding places of this little work I have briefly set forth the way of ascending to this supreme region of the spirit, nevertheless, as I think, it will not be useless to repeat more often things well said, so that we may be able to retain them more closely. For it seems to me of little profit to describe the beauty of contemplation unless we also know how to show those who desire it the way of coming to it. Therefore let one who wishes to ascend, through the desire of love, to the pleasantness of this supreme region strive to exercise himself diligently in the labors of the middle region; for unless in that region he has been purified from sensual passions through the pursuit of holy compunction, he does not approach this one without danger.

For the completion of this exercise, let him often recall our Lord Jesus Christ to memory; let him weep from delight in divine love; and let him learn, by frequent attentive meditation, to run through the text of his Passion and to stir himself to compassion for the Savior. And because the body that is corrupted weighs down the soul, so that the mind may be able to raise itself powerfully in this meditation on the Lord’s Passion, let him strive, if need be, to present to his thoughts images that are truly sensible. Therefore, withdrawing from crowds, in silence let him begin by recollection to turn over the order of the Passion. Let him see himself with the eyes of the mind as present with the suffering Lord at each event; let him hear Jesus speaking in imagination; let him go with him as he goes through all the places of the Passion. Then let him apply his affections to each article out of the desire of compassion; let him gaze with faithful eyes upon the suffering Jesus set before him, weeping because of anguish of heart. Let him grow accustomed in spirit to address him as though bodily present, and through the desire of love to embrace him wounded at the pillar and on the cross. For the mind will quickly be able to be kindled in the love of the Lord Jesus if it truly becomes accustomed to suffer with him through the tears of meditation.

But I remember having written elsewhere at greater length about the most efficacious exercise of the Lord’s Passion; therefore here I have passed over it briefly. For it is enough to have given the beginnings to those who do not know how to meditate on it, since those who know have higher things.
