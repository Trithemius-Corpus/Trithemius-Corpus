There your most indulgent father will receive you as you return joyfully; he will clothe you, the first robe of innocence having been recovered; and he will give you the ring of most holy covenant upon your hands, and the shoes of true mortification of evil affections upon your feet, so that, adorned with these ornaments like a faithful bride, you may cleave continually to him and henceforth in no way withdraw from his love. Finally, for food he will kill for you the fatted calf; he himself will be the server and at the same time the banquet; by the greatness of his surpassing love he will give his flesh for food and his own blood for drink. There the Holy Spirit of the Lord will be present; he will fill you with his mystical gifts. Then, rejoicing in the Lord, you will sing: The fruit of Jesus is sweet to my throat, because his spirit is sweeter than honey.

Therefore this highest region of the spirit is great by way of restoration, since the citizens whom it receives into its habitation it leads back to the delight of the heavenly fatherland. We recognize the citizens of this most holy region infallibly from the fruits of the Spirit, concerning which we know that the holy Apostle spoke: The fruit of the Spirit is charity, joy, peace, patience, goodness, long-suffering, gentleness, faith, modesty, continence, chastity. These fruits the citizens of this third region bring forth, because, filled with God and united to him, they walk always joyful in the Lord and most fervent in charity. For whatever may befall a just man dwelling on this mountain of love will not sadden him. For the mind, always peaceful and tranquil in the love of God, is lifted up by no prosperity and disturbed by no adversity.

*That This Highest Region of the Spirit Is by Treasuring Up, Article Six*

Joyful and most true. For this reason this most beautiful region of the spirit is called joyful and most true by treasuring up, because whatever is acquired through the exercise of good work is possessed in simplicity of spirit. The treasure of the soul is the love of God; when the purified mind is kindled by its fervor, it is raised to this region as to its own storehouse of love.

For the Lord says in the holy Gospel: Where your treasure is, there will your heart be also. This is the treasure hidden in the field of the Scriptures; when a man finds it, he hides it, and going off sells everything, so that he may be able to buy the field with the treasure.

The field is this highest region of the spirit; the treasure is divine love. When a man finds it, he ought to despise all other things, so that he may be able joyfully to possess the field with the treasure. There is no possession in the world more joyful than divine love; there is no greater beauty in this life, because whoever has this treasure of love requires nothing more. And just as fire is naturally borne upward, so love is raised into this region of the spirit as to its own proper dwelling-place.

For the mind, set aflame by divine love, is always lifted up to God with burning affections, and with wondrous sweetness is moved above every thought into its own beginning. For, purified in the exercise of the second region, it prepares itself for the ascent of this third region through loving affection; it longs to come to him whom it loves so greatly; it thinks of nothing other than Jesus, the treasure of its heart; by the greatness of surpassing love it adores him with burning sighs. But when the most sweet office of contemplation has been completed, suddenly the beloved slips away; the mind, languishing with love, is recalled to the exercise of the second region, in which the true lover of God is seen indeed to dwell bodily among men, but in affection and desire he lives with his most beloved Jesus.

And although that anagogical action of the created spirit takes place by the impulse of the Spirit of God, nevertheless the mind, purified through the exercise of the second region, must advance itself toward the ascent and, disposed through most fervent affection, must concur with the divine Spirit. For indeed almighty God, who bestowed on us freedom of choice, will not raise anyone unwilling to this highest region of his contemplation. Therefore our will is required for our own salvation; love is demanded for the ascent.
