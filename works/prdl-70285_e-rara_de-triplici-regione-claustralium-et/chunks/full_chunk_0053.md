No one at all understands what it begets in the heart of the lover, except the one who, having despised all the things and events of the world, has loved God alone for a long time. The heart of one who loves God loves none of the things that are in the world; it despises the riches, honors, and favor of the age; it is not afraid to undergo any adversities for the love of its Lord. The love of God is never idle; it always desires to come to its source; it refuses to endure no labors in this world. Therefore let him love who does not wish to become slothful. One's neighbor too is to be loved sweetly on account of God, and by no reasoning is he to be offended against God or justice. Let each person always show himself to his brother, in words and deeds, such as he expects others to be toward himself. For he truly loves his neighbor who, for his sake, exposes his goods, body, and soul. This Christ taught; this the Son of God did for us. Indeed, he who says that he truly believes in Christ ought himself also to walk just as He walked. He does not truly love Christ who does not keep His commandments. For He says: This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you. We are members of one head, Jesus Christ; we then believe in Him when we observe His commandment concerning love toward God and toward our neighbor. Let us therefore love our neighbor, because he is a member in Christ together with us, so that just as one member in the body suffers with another, so a brother may be humbly helped by a brother for the love of Christ. The second mode commands you to love wisely, and to seek nothing else in divine love but God. Extend your affection toward that Word which was in the beginning with God; lift your mind above everything that is created, because unless you leave behind yourself and all things that are in the world, you will not be able to come to Him whom you love. Finally, if you desire to love God wisely, do not think yourself, O man, to be anything, because where a man thinks himself to be something, he already loves unwisely. The third mode commands that you love strongly, and never be afraid to expose body and soul for the love of God.

He is not a strong lover of God who is afraid to undergo death for Him. For love is strong as death. One's neighbor is loved wisely if not the man but the vice is hated. But he is loved strongly if every injury is forgiven from a heart intending to do good to him.

*The second region ends.*
