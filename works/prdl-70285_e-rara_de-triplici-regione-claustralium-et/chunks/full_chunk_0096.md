Most loving and sweetest Lord Jesus, who at the first hour, falsely accused, truly confessed yourself to be the Son of God: have mercy on me and grant me, through your innocence, the virtue of true confession, so that I may humbly endure any words of those who accuse me, and may not be driven away by any reproaches from the inward tranquility of my mind. You who live and reign with God the Father.

*After terce.*

After terce you will call to mind how many reproaches, how many mockeries and derisions the most innocent Lord Jesus endured for your redemption; in memory of this, take upon yourself the desire for the sixth degree of humility, praying thus.

*Prayer.*

Sweetest Lord Jesus, who for us were pitiably scourged, mocked, derided, crowned with thorns, and spat upon at the third hour: grant me, by the power of all your sufferings, through the shedding of your most holy blood, always to be content, for love of you, with every abasement and extremity; so that, in consideration of my sins, I may humbly regard myself even as your unworthy workman, and yet may in no way grow lukewarm in your holy service.

*After sext.*

After sext, consider the Lord Jesus bearing his cross before all and then hanging pitiably upon it, together with the other particulars of his punishments; and, inflamed with desire for the seventh degree of humility, pray to him with fervor of spirit. *Prayer.*

Sweetest Lord Jesus, who for us wretched sinners, after the countless pains of your passion, carried your cross to the place of your death while your most sorrowful mother looked on; on which, cruelly fastened, you were given gall and vinegar to drink and were mocked in many ways: I beseech you, through the pain of your wounds, grant me pardon for all my sins, the grace of holy compunction, a fountain of tears, the virtue of deep humility, and continual remembrance of your most bitter passion, so that all that I am and am capable of may always serve you, and my mind may continually burn with your love.

*After none.*

After none, be mindful of sweetest Jesus dying on the cross; and, falling down in spirit at his knees, with tears, giving thanks with as much devotion as you can, you will pray.

*Prayer.*

I adore you, sweetest Lord Jesus Christ, King of heaven and earth, dying for wretched me on the cross, and I humbly give you thanks for your holy passion and most innocent death. O what shall I render to you, most loving Savior, for all that you have rendered to me? Behold, I offer myself to you; I continually desire to dwell in your most holy wounds. I beseech you, through the power of your most innocent death, have mercy on me as you know how; pierce my flesh with fear of you, and restrain in me all disordered motions, so that, inflamed with the fervor of your most holy love, I may love you with a pure heart, confess you as praiseworthy with my mouth, and follow you in deed, according to my strength, in humble conduct. I beseech you, most merciful Lord Jesus, through that most holy anguish of your heart which you endured on the cross when your most holy soul departed from your body: have mercy on my soul at its departure.

*The first hour after the meal.*

At the first hour after the meal, unless you have been occupied with higher exercises, you will pray to the Lord in such or a similar manner, to obtain knowledge of your own frailty.

*Prayer.*
