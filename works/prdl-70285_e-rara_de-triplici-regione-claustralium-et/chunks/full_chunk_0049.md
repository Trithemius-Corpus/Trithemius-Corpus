Rare is the true repentance of one who scandalizes the little ones, because, when he neglects to make restitution for the harm done to another, he by no means attains to the fruit of saving repentance.

Fourth, consider the negligence of your ungrateful heart, since you, who were created in the image of God and ought to have loved your maker above all things, dishonor him in many ways by your sins. To Christ the Son of God, by whose blood you have been redeemed, you ought to render continual service of love; but you do not fear to love the flesh more than Jesus Christ your savior. Finally, with a compassionate mind you ought daily to turn over in your mind his most holy passion, and yet you are accustomed to think of nothing less than of it. Adopted among the sons of God by mercy alone, you ought to have shown yourself worthy of the heavenly calling as an imitator, and still you do not fear to lie through sloth in the lowest things. And why do I say much? Behold, you who have been marked with the Holy Spirit, who have been counted with the angels, are called to the inheritance of the sons of God; you are invited by all the saints to the feasts of paradise; you partake daily of the body and blood of our savior Jesus Christ; and yet you do not observe the commandments of God and of the rule which you promised, so that you might be blessed. Examine your conscience carefully, therefore, and you will find that there are many good things you ought to do and omit, and many evil things you ought to omit and commit against God's good pleasure.

Fifth, consider whom you offend by sinning: not a man, but God; not a creature, but the Creator. He against whom you have sinned is great and immeasurable, terrible and glorious; his majesty the angels tremble at, his power all the heavenly spirits marvel at. To the highest and unchangeable good, God, the beginning and end of every creature, the rational spirit ought to devote love and the desire of adhesion beyond human understanding, and to raise itself wholly to that truly one thing, as to its own center, despising all phantasms; and it can lift itself up to this so much the more easily, the more, leaving all other things behind, it has striven to unite itself to itself alone. For by the power of that one itself, wherever union occurs, nor for any other reason are things more alike more easily united to one another than because they are one in quality. Therefore the soul is then at last most rightly said to be united with the one principle of things when it, having been made [most purely?] one in itself, is separated from every multitude and, above all imagination, reason, and understanding, its unity alone escapes, enjoying through loving affection the unity of God, joining, so to speak, its own center with the center of the universe. But when the human soul itself, suffering badly along with its flesh as if with its spouse, is turned toward any animal passions, then that very unity is badly changed into a multitude contrary to the one alone, and it becomes so much the more remote from its principle as it has been divided from itself. Finally, the soul itself contracts a great difficulty in returning to that one ineffable divine thing whenever, confused in itself, it falls into the sin of apostasy. Think now how great and ineffable he is against whose honor sin is committed; and since you are wholly unable to understand the greatness of his majesty, learn humbly to fear his power.

Sixth, consider what you have lost by sinning, so that the recognized greatness of the loss may hasten repentance and restore through tears what has been lost. What, then, wretched man, have you lost by sinning? Hear what you have lost. You had been made like God through grace, the companion of angels, a member of Christ, and chosen heir of eternal happiness; you ought to have lived and rejoiced without end in the goods of the Lord with all the saints. But what has happened with your sin? You have been compared to senseless beasts, and have been made like them. For when you were in honor, you were unwilling to understand; you abandoned the fountain of life; by sinning you have been made the companion and member of the devil, the harlot of Antichrist, the heir of hell. You abandoned life; you chose everlasting death. If you fear the sentence, change your will, and you will find remission.
