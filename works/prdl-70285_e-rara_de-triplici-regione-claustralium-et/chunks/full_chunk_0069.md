Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of your faithful, *Prayer*, and kindle in them the fire of your love: you who, through the diversity of all tongues, gathered the nations into the unity of faith. Send forth your Spirit and they shall be created. And you shall renew the face of the earth.

O God, to whom every heart lies open, *Prayer*, and every will speaks, and from whom no secret is hidden: purify, by the infusion of the Holy Spirit, the thoughts of our heart, that we may merit to love you perfectly and praise you worthily. Through Christ our Lord. Let him add “O flowering rose,” and, if he wishes to add anything else out of devotion, let him do so.

*At the first signal.* Then at the first signal of any hour of the day, or also at the striking of the clock, if he has not been occupied with higher meditations, let him say: Blessed be the name of the Lord, from this time forth and forevermore. And let him add some brief little prayer formed from the ardor of his mind, in this or a similar manner:

Receive me, most sweet Lord Jesus, as your perpetual servant, that I may always have you as my only good. Another: I beseech you, most merciful God, grant me to live so innocently in holy religion that I may be able to come to you, the beginning and end of every good. Another: O that my ways may be directed to keep your justifications at all times. Another: Most loving Lord Jesus, I ask you, by the merit of your most holy passion, teach me to despise all things, that I may continually merit to find you, the highest good of the soul. Then the general preparation for prayer shall be observed.
