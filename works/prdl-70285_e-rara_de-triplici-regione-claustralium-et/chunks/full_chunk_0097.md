Lord God, who by your great kindness created me in the first parent from the clay of the earth, so that, mindful of my humble condition, I might glorify you, my creator, in true humility: grant me, I beseech you, truly to know my frailty at all times, so that, while I consider how vile and almost nothing I am by my own merit, I may deserve to please you by your gift. Through Christ.

*The Second Hour.*

At the second hour after noon, let our devout cenobite summarily recall to memory the gravity of his sins, by which he has sinned against the rule, especially while living in holy religion; and, turned to the Lord, let him pray thus.

*Prayer.*

Almighty everlasting God, who have deigned, by the incarnation and death of your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to give life, from your mercy alone, to us wretched people dead in sins: grant me, through the power of his most innocent passion, pardon for all my sins, so that henceforth I may deserve to serve you with a pure conscience. Through the same our Lord.

*The Third Hour.*

At the third hour after the meal, let the faithful cenobite remember his vows which he promised to the Lord, and especially the renunciation of all ownership, namely in things, judgments, and will; and, moved to compunction before the Lord in fervor of spirit, he shall pray thus.

*Prayer.*

Almighty and merciful God, who with kindly favor sanctify and receive the vows of your servants offered to you from faithful devotion: grant me to render all that I have promised, through the power of humility, chastity, obedience, and poverty, with such purity of mind that I may deserve, by your mercy, to come to your kingdom promised to the poor in spirit. Through our Lord.

*After Vespers.*

After Vespers, let our good cenobite consider how vain all visible things are; and, with his mind raised to the love of invisible goods, let him cry to the Lord with as much devotion as he can.

*Prayer.*

Almighty everlasting God, who wished to lead us through the visible things which you created to the knowledge and love of invisible things: grant me so to know the vanity of present things that I may always deserve to think with a pure mind upon the eternity of things to come. Another.

O God, protector of those who hope in you, without whom nothing is strong, nothing holy: multiply your mercy upon us, so that with you as ruler, you as guide, we may so pass through temporal goods that we do not lose eternal ones.

*Another Prayer.*

O God, who have prepared invisible goods for those who love you: pour into our hearts the affection of your love, so that, loving you in all things and above all things, we may obtain your promises, which surpass every desire. Through our Lord.

*After Supper and Collation.*

After supper or collation, let the faithful cenobite clothe his desire with what fervor of charity he can, for good zeal and especially for souls; and, praying to the Lord, let him say.

*Prayer.*

Lord God, greatest lover of faithful souls, who for the salvation of human beings willed your Son to die on the gibbet of the cross: grant me, by the example of the same your Son, to be strengthened in good zeal, and to fear no labors, no adversities, for the salvation of souls, so that, strengthened by the fire of your love, I may dare at the proper time to resist those who contradict you, and manfully set myself as a wall for your house. Through the same our Lord.

*After Compline.*

After Compline, with his strength gathered, let the devout cenobite enter alone into the secrecy of his heart; and let him leave outside all cares and anxieties of the world, so that, free and released from all things, he may deserve to speak with the Lord Jesus through the desire of compunction, and to be sweetly united with him through the fire of love. Therefore, when the faults of the day have been recalled summarily to the memory of examination, let him pray thus to the Lord.

*Prayer.*

Most merciful Lord Jesus Christ, who for our redemption willed to undergo a most shameful death: grant me, I beseech you, through the pain of your wounds, through the shedding of your most holy blood, and through the power of your most innocent death, pardon for all my sins, so that, with my conscience cleansed, I may henceforth cling continually to you through the desire of love, make daily progress in holy religion, and humbly submit myself in all things to your most holy will.

*To the Son.*

My body and my soul I commend to you, Lord Jesus Christ, this night and at all times, in the power and union of that love by which, hanging on the cross, you commended your beloved mother, the Virgin Mary, to the disciple; in which love, I beseech you, guard me from all snares of the ancient serpent; suppress the illicit movements of the flesh; drive far away foul and evil dreams; and repel the phantoms of the nights, so that I may sleep secure in your peace, and rise cheerfully at the hours appointed for me to your praises. Amen.
