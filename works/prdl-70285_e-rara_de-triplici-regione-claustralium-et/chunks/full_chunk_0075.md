If he still finds himself a cultivator of the [first?] region, he ought not to approach the altar unless, through contrition and confession and a firm purpose of amending himself, he first strives to cleanse himself. But if he finds himself a citizen of the second region, if he has found his powers reformed and ordered toward the cenobitic end, he can readily dispose himself for celebrating, since by emptying out his own affection day by day he has learned more often to lift himself upward through desire. For divine goodness quickly enters the affection of a person who is stained by no desire of his own.

Therefore, wishing to perform this most holy priestly office with devotion and the fervor of divine love, let him first set forth some ardent prayers by which he may be able to stir himself to greater compunction. For devotion is rarely granted at the altar if the mind is not prepared beforehand for fervor. For this reason our fathers, desiring each of us to advance in his own free devotion, did not wish us to be occupied, after the manner of certain people, with many psalms before approaching the altar; not because anyone ought therefore to approach without recollection, but so that each one might be more freely able, in secret, to devote himself to exercises or prayers more suited to his devotion.

Therefore, desiring to commend to those who celebrate an elevation of the mind to the Lord Jesus before approaching the altar, we have compiled the following prayer from that which is said to be Saint Ambrose of Milan's, which we believe to be quite efficacious by virtue of the Lord's Passion. Accordingly, a devout cenobite about to approach the altar, if he has leisure and wishes, will be able to set forth this or a similar prayer beforehand, or to prepare himself in whatever ways he wishes.

*Prayer before the altar*

Highest priest and true pontiff, Jesus Christ, who offered yourself to God the Father as a pure, holy, and immaculate victim on the altar of the cross for us miserable sinners; who also gave us your flesh to eat and your blood to drink, and established that mystery in the power of the Holy Spirit, saying: “As often as you shall do these things, you shall do them in memory of me.” I beseech you by the pain of your wounds and by the pouring out of your most precious blood, and by the power of your most innocent death, and by your very body and blood, the price of our salvation; I beseech you by this wondrous and ineffable charity by which you so loved us miserable and unworthy ones that you washed us from our sins in blood: [unclear] all my sins, and teach me, your unworthy servant, whom among your other gifts you have also deigned to call to the priestly office, not by any merits of mine but solely by the gracious condescension of your mercy, through your Holy Spirit, to handle this mystery with that reverence and honor, devotion and fear with which it ought and is fitting.

Grant me, I beg, always to believe and understand, feel and firmly hold, say and think concerning so great a mystery that which pleases you and that which is expedient for the salvation of my soul. Let your good Spirit enter into my heart, to sound there without sound, and without the noise of words to speak within me the truth of such great mysteries. For they are very deep, and covered with sacred veils. Because of your great mercy, sweetest Lord Jesus, grant me to celebrate the solemnities of masses with a pure heart and a clean conscience. I beseech you, by the sweetness of your most holy heart, most loving Savior, free my heart from all vain, unclean, harmful, and illicit thoughts, so that it may become a dwelling-place of your love, now and without end forever.

I beseech you, O lover of humankind, sweetest Lord Jesus, by every power of your most holy Passion: drive away from me the spirit of elation and pride, vainglory and envy, lust and blasphemy, and fill my soul with the virtue of charity and innocence. King of virtues, Jesus Christ, lover of holy chastity and integrity, I beseech you by the most holy virginity of the most blessed Mother of God and undefiled Virgin Mary, and by the purity of your most reverend body: extinguish in me, with the dew of heavenly blessing, the tinder of burning lust, so that the brightness of all purity may be and remain in me.
