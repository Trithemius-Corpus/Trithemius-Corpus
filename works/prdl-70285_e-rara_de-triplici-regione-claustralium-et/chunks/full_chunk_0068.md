Since, after matins have been completed, it is our custom that those who are to celebrate during the day prepare themselves by confession, therefore the devout monk, once his confession has been made at whatever time, should at once devoutly perform the penance enjoined on him; and he should not approach the altar unless sacramental confession has always first been made, so far as possible.

*On Leaving the Church*

On leaving the church after each hour, when the divine office has been completed, or also at any other time, he may repeat the order and manner of praying at the entrance of the church described above, and observe the same rite in his several places. But if he wishes to vary this manner for greater devotion, he shall pray the following little prayers, or similar ones.

Bowing before the high altar, let him say:

Blessed be the holy Trinity and the undivided Unity; we shall confess him, because he has shown us his mercy.

O most holy majesty, beginning of all things, have mercy on me. Amen.

*Returning to the Cell*

Then after matins and the several hours of the day, when the devout lover of Christ has returned to his cell, let him fall on his knees before his bed; let him humbly give thanks to almighty God, because he has deigned to admit him, an insufficient minister, to his service, and there to number him among his sons. Let him therefore say this or a similar prayer.

*Prayer*

I give thanks to you, almighty and eternal God, because you have deigned to call me, an unworthy sinner, to your service, by no merits of mine, but by the worthy consideration of your mercy. I ask you, through the pain of the wounds of our Lord Jesus Christ, your only-begotten Son, and through the shedding of his most holy blood and the power of his most innocent death: receive this sacrifice of my service for the glory of your majesty, for my sins, for your holy Church, and for the souls of all the faithful departed who are placed in the pains of purgatory, in that union of charity in which you accepted the same our Lord Jesus Christ dying for us on the cross; and grant me, through the merit of the same passion, to love you in truth and to remain ever faithful in your service. Amen.

*Morning Recollection*

Then enter for a little while into the examination of your conscience, and see whether you still have in yourself the conditions of a good cloistered religious described in the seventh article of the second region. If you recognize that anything is lacking, do not neglect to reform it as quickly as possible, lest little by little through sloth you fade away. For it is written: He who neglects the least things gradually falls away. And the holy lover of God Augustine says: No sin is so small that it does not grow by neglect. Therefore, once the conscience has been examined and reformed in holy fervor, say:

*Prayer for Perseverance*

Grant, we beseech you, almighty God, through the merit of the passion of our Lord Jesus Christ, and through the intercession of blessed Mary ever virgin, and of all the holy heavenly powers, and of the blessed patriarchs, prophets, apostles, martyrs, confessors, virgins, and all your elect, perseverance for us in your will, so that we may be able continually to render service pleasing to you. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

Entering the bed, let him fortify himself with the sign of the cross and say with attention of spirit: In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ I intend to lay myself down, so that, strengthened, I may be able more fully to praise his divine majesty throughout the day. Sitting in bed: The peace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the purity of blessed Mary ever virgin, the sign of the holy cross, the sorrow of the Lord's passion, and the intercession of all the saints preserve me now and always. In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Let him lay himself down while signing himself.
