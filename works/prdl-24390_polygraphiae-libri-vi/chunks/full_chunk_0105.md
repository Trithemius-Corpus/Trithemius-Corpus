Alchemy has many domestic attendants, who guard their mistress with perpetual vigilance and put themselves forward under her name, so that they may preserve her forever untouched from intercourse with so many importunate lovers. Vanity, fraud, deceitfulness, deception, sophisticating, greed, lying confidence, foolishness, want, poverty, despair, flight, outlawry, and beggary are the handmaids of alchemy; while pretending that their mistress is loved, they guard her inviolate, and gladly prostitute themselves to the moneyed, avaricious, greedy, and silly seekers of her.
