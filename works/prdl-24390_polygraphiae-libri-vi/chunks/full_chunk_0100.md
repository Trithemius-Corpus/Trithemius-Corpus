We have thus far set forth, by way of example, the ancient manner of the Franks of Germany in writing numbers with Latin letters, so that we might also provide knowledge of it to those who do not know it. But the fact that, in setting down the figures of algorism, we have departed from the common rule of computation was done not by mistake but for a reasonable cause, namely so that we should not be burdened by an excessive progression. There were also some who wrote larger numbers in this manner.

*The sixth book of the Polygraphia of Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Saint James the Greater the Apostle at Würzburg, begins.*

Before every invention and use of letters, in the beginning of the world, when human beings were still rough in understanding and uncultivated in manners, under the governance of a succession shared among three, as certain persons think, of intelligences succeeding one another in order for one thousand sixty-three years, the influences of the planets were in the world, acting in their own way upon bodies on earth. The ancient investigators of this matter say that Saturn, from the beginning of the world, presided by his influence over the things sprouting and growing on earth, principally through the governance of his intelligence, which they called Orifiel, that is, the life-giving one of God, for three hundred fifty-four lunar years, likewise four months, and as many days and hours. These things we recount as poetic; we do not affirm them.

After Saturn, Venus, and after Venus, Jupiter is held to have assumed dominion. After Jupiter, however, Mercury, fourth in order as ruler, adorned the talents of human beings with various arts. But under the three intelligences mentioned, namely while Saturn, Venus, and Jupiter were ruling, they assert that human beings did not have the use or knowledge of letters. Hence whenever one person wished to announce some secret to another, he did so not by writings, since the function of letters was lacking, but symbolically, by certain likenesses of animals, trees, plants, or other sensible things, according as each had agreed among themselves.

The age of Saturn, as they say, gave human beings manners suited to the influence proper to it. For it made farmers, hunters, founders of cities, rough men, woodland-dwellers, gloomy men, simple men, uncultivated in life and manners, living in the flesh for a long time.
