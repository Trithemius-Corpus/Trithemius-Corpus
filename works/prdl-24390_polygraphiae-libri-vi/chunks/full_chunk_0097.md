*The fifth figure of the expansion of the reversed table,*

|a | w | . | 3 | c | m | n |
|b | z | b | w | d | o | p|
|c | y | c | x | e | q | r|
|d | u | d | y | f | s | t|
|e | k | e | t | g | i | u|
|f | u | f | u | h | j | v|
|g | t | g | s | i | l | x|
|h | r | h | i | k | m | y|
|i | q | k | p | l | n | z|
|k | p | k | n | o | m | w|
|m | n | m | o | p | q | b|
|n | m | n | l | r | s | a|
|o | l | o | q | t | u | b|
|p | k | o | r | v | x | c|
|q | i | p | s | y | z | d|
|r | b | q | t | a | w | e|
|s | g | r | u | b | a | f|
|t | g | u | x | b | c | d|
|x | d | . | 3 | b | d | e|
|y | c | . | 2 | b | e | f|
|z | b | . | 1 | b | f | g|
w | a | . | 0 | b | g | h|

In this fifth figure of the expansion of the reversed table there are only the first two alphabets in order looking back upon it, in the number of which, first, a is eclipsed. But we have set down the remaining four as an example, so that one who wishes may have material for coining names according to his desire.
