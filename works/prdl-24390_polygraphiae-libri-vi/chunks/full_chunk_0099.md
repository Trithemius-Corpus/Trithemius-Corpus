Behold, reader, you have the method by which you can adequately mark down and write every writable number with a few letters; their range, as you see, proceeds to infinity. But, as people once used to say in a proverb, “The weary ox plants his foot firmly”: whoever wishes there to be a limit to the preceding things, let him follow the ancient method.

So that those who do not know it may understand it more easily, it will not be tedious for me to give in what follows the form which Hunibald the Frank, historiographer of the Franks, and also Bede, Rabanus, the monks Helpericus, and many of the ancients used in chronicles and ecclesiastical computations, as follows.
