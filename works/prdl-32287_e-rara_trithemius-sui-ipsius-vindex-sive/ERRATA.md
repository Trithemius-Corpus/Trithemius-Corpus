## 2026-11-18 · vindex-fear

Bonus describes Trithemius. Quam marks the degree of his fear, sibi refers to his concern for himself, and the following clause explains his fear of offending readers. AI scan collation. No human verification of this correction is documented.

Previous wording: how much good Trithemius feared for himself

Revised wording: how deeply the good Trithemius feared for himself

*quam sibi timuerit bonus Trithemius, ne quempiam suo hoc opere, praesertim indoctos et imperitos, offendat*. [Stiftung der Werke von C. G. Jung, Zürich, e-rara, 41](https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/1761232/full/full/0/default.jpg).
