*Sermones et Exhortationes ad Monachos* — "Sermons and Exhortations to Monks" — gathers Trithemius's addresses to his community as a reforming Benedictine abbot, pressing for stricter observance of the Rule against the lax monasticism of his day. They sit with his other reform texts — *De Triplici Regione Claustralium*, *De Statu et Ruina Monastici Ordinis* — and together they form the pastoral engine of his Sponheim programme: the sermons are where the reform treatises are preached.

This is the **1516 Strasbourg edition** (BSB scan) — the second of two witnesses of the *Sermones et Exhortationes* in this corpus (the other is `prdl-24393`), a separate Strasbourg printing of the same year.
