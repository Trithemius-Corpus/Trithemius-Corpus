*With the grace and privilege of the Imperial Majesty.*

Maximilian, by the favor of divine clemency elected Emperor of the Romans, ever Augustus, and King of Germany, Hungary, Dalmatia, Croatia, etc.; Archduke of Austria; Duke of Burgundy, Brabant, etc.; Count Palatine, etc.: to the honorable and devout John Trithemius, beloved by us, Abbot of Saint James the Greater at Wurzburg, imperial grace and every good thing.

Since you have humbly set forth to us that you have composed various editions of both ancient and modern authors, collected not without great labor, vigils, and diligence, which you desired with your whole mind to be brought forth into the light; and since, for a matter of this kind, our beloved subject and that of the Holy Roman Empire, Master Johannes Haselberg of Constance, offered himself with a most ready spirit to be the agent, and therefore procured at great expense the forms by which he may be able to print the aforesaid works: we, therefore, desiring that provision be made for the indemnity of the aforesaid Master Johannes and for the ornament of your honor, lest your works, composed by you with great labors and vigils, should lie hidden as though covered with rust, but rather should shine forth more and more each day, have given and granted to the aforesaid Master Johannes Haselberg of Constance full faculty and authority in every respect, as by the tenor of these presents we give and grant it.

We decree that he may print, sell when printed, and dispose wherever he wishes of whatever, among treatises and little works, has been edited by you, the aforesaid Trithemius, Abbot of Saint James the Greater at Wurzburg, and has been reviewed and approved by the honorable Conrad Peutinger, doctor and our faithful beloved councillor. We forbid all and singular chalcographers and printers of books, of whatever condition, under the penalty written below, commanding more strictly that they shall not dare or presume to print or publish such works, or similar ones, for ten years after they have been published by the said Master Johannes, nor to publish, buy, or sell them when printed by others, throughout the whole Roman Empire and in our hereditary lands.

We command all and singular our beloved faithful subjects and those of the Holy Roman Empire, of whatever estate, condition, dignity, or office, whether ecclesiastical or secular, under penalty of our indignation and that of the Holy Roman Empire most grievously, and of ten marks of pure gold, to be applied, one half to our treasury and the other half to the aforesaid Master Johannes Haselberg, as often as anything shall have been done to the contrary, that they assist the same Master Johannes of Constance with their favor and aid in their places and lands, and by no means permit the said works and treatises to be printed, or those printed elsewhere to be sold. Rather, let them immediately declare in our name that all who presume to do anything against this our grace and prohibition have incurred the sentence of this our indignation and that of the Holy Roman Empire, executing in this matter our expressed will.

In testimony whereof, these letters have been authenticated with our seal impressed on the back. Given in our town of Innsbruck, on the tenth day of the month of November, in the year of the Lord 1513, in the twenty-ninth year of our Roman reign, and the twenty-fifth of Hungary.

By the king himself.

N. Ziegler.

By command of the lord Emperor himself.

Etc., through Paulus Obersteiner.
