O, dearest brothers, if you knew how pleasing to God are the prayers of a pure mind, and how much benefit they confer upon the soul, you would allow no time to pass without prayers. For if our prayer is pure and burning in fervor of spirit, it is a most faithful messenger to God: it speaks to God on our behalf, and obtains all things necessary for our salvation while it prays.

For this reason I admonish you and exhort you in true charity to pray without interruption, and never to cease from the fervor of the Holy Spirit. Let no time be empty for you, or idle in sluggishness of mind. In the fear of the Lord, I ask you, always be watchful and solicitous, so that you may be able bravely to conquer and overcome the temptations that threaten. As soon as foul thoughts begin to knock at the mind, hasten back to the practice of prayer, and they will by no means be able to prevail over you. For prayer is a great fortification of the soul; in its presence all the contrivances of the opposing side fail. For through most pure prayers all useful things are granted us by God, and all adversities are driven far away. The mind is powerfully kindled in the love of God by pure prayers; and whatever slighter stain may arise is cleansed by praying.

By holy and continual prayers the [world?] is conquered; the plagues of the soul are healed by devout prayers; by tears and weeping the devil is overcome; and he for whom a thousand cohorts are not enough for battle is weakened by the prayer of one poor man. Just as wax, melting before the face of fire, is dissolved, so at the fervor of pure prayer all the power of demons is crushed and weakened. If a foul thought has been sent into your mind by demons, immediately make the sign of the cross upon your forehead and say: Save me, Jesus Christ, mediator of God and men, and drive away from me all the wickedness of the ancient enemy, through your most innocent passion. What can be shorter than this prayer, or what more powerful? For as soon as it has been uttered with fervor of mind against either the presence or the suggestion of demons, every contrivance of iniquity withdraws.

For the power of pure prayer is very great, since it both penetrates heaven and by its constancy defeats the powers of the air. Prayer illuminates the mind, purifies the affection, obtains pardon for sins, and strengthens the spirit in the love of God against all adversaries. Nothing so raises the mind from earthly things to the love of heavenly things; nothing so quickly and powerfully destroys the pleasures of the flesh; nothing so illuminates the understanding; nothing also so greatly confounds the adversary of truth as pure prayer, which is poured out to God with fervor of divine love and is adorned by the testimony of a good conscience.

If you desire to be terrible to demons, if you wish to be a friend of God, keep your conscience pure, and never interrupt spiritual prayer. For devout prayer can do all things, if it is adorned with uprightness of faith and purity of life. Prayer puts demons to flight, weakens the power of malignant spirits, heals the soul languishing in tepidity, and strengthens the weak and feeble spirit in the fear of God. Pure prayer makes one near to God; and the one whom it had received as a servant it makes to be a friend of God. Pure prayer, fervent in the love of God, penetrates heaven and wonderfully illuminates the understanding of the one praying. It closes hell, opens paradise, and brings in the souls of the faithful.

For the Truth does not lie, who promises in the holy Gospel, saying: Ask, and it shall be given to you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives; and he who seeks finds; and to the one knocking it shall be opened. Which of you has ever turned to the Lord with his whole heart and prayed, and was not heard? Or who has called upon the Lord, and he has withdrawn himself from him? For Scripture says: Know that the Lord will hear your prayers, if you remain and persevere in fasts and prayers in the sight of the Lord.

Be mindful of Moses, the servant of the Lord, who cast down Amalek, confident in his own strength, not by fighting with iron, but by praying with holy prayers. For the continual supplication of the just man avails much, and the prayer of one who humbles himself penetrates the clouds. Elijah was a man subject to suffering like us; and he prayed with prayer that it might not rain upon the earth, and it did not rain for three years and six months. And again he prayed, and heaven gave rain, and the earth gave its fruit. By prayer and tears King Hezekiah obtained fifteen years of longer life, and by prayers changed the sentence of the prophet. By prayers holy men became renowned for miracles, and, supplicating the Lord, called the dead back to life.
