*Sermons and Exhortations to Monks, by Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim, afterward of Wuerzburg. Of these sermons there are two books: the first is called the book of Homilies, the second the book of Sermons.*

*The Decastich of Ottomar Luscinius of Strasbourg on the homilies of Johannes Trithemius to monks*

If it is not enough for anyone to follow your gentle laws, O Christ, laws of equity, unless he recalls the monks: Serapion, Paulius, and Antony, who once set foot upon the path that bristles with countless rough places; who strives to show that the body is tormented by a rough cloak, and does not allow a feather-bed on the couch; that fasts may drive away the swollen pride of the mind; that brief rest may cherish the half-sleeping limbs: these things they taught silently by their deeds. But Trithemius is more apt than they, for he has power both with tongue and hand.
