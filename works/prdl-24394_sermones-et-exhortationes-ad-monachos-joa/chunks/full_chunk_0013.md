*What the armor of spiritual warfare is.*

As James says in his epistle. But works of piety must be joined to the Christian faith. You believe in one almighty God, creator of heaven and earth: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. You do well. The demons also believe and tremble. What then? Where are the works by which your faith may be shown to have been formed in Christ? Idle and dead is the faith that is not commended by holy and necessary works. We believe in Christ: it is well, if we follow Christ's examples. For if we do not follow Christ in works of piety, in vain do we flatter ourselves about faith alone. You, I say, believe, and the demons believe. With love faith belongs to a Christian; without love faith belongs to the devil. You believe rightly if you love God with all your heart according to your powers. But if you do not truly love God, you have an idle and dead faith. The proof of love, as Saint Gregory testifies, is the performance of work. For thus the Lord speaks in the Gospel: If anyone loves me, he will keep my word. This, therefore, is true faith: ordered according to Christ's teaching by good and holy works. If with faith and work together you believe in the Lord Jesus, surely you hold the shield of salvation in your hands, in which, by the Lord himself granting it, you will easily overcome all adversaries.

Of this armor it is written: By faith the saints conquered kingdoms, worked righteousness, obtained the promise. John also says: Everything that is born of God conquers the world; and this is the victory that conquers the world, our faith. And the prince of the apostles, Peter, thus admonishes us, saying: Brothers, be sober and watchful, because your adversary the devil, like a roaring lion, goes about seeking whom he may devour; resist him, strong in faith. True faith and trust in our Lord Jesus Christ is an unconquerable and most powerful armor, against which no adversary has ever been able to stand, as is written in the book of Chronicles. King Jehoshaphat said to his army: Believe in the Lord your God and you will be secure. In Ecclesiasticus also we read thus: You who fear the Lord, believe him, and your reward will not be made void. And again: In your work believe from the faith of your soul, for this is the practice of the commandments. He who believes God attends to the commandments; and he who trusts in him will not be diminished.

Take care now, therefore, dearest brothers, and always strive with the highest zeal to carry in your hands this shield of faith, formed by holy works, against all attacks of your enemies, because he who is fortified by this unconquerable shield is never conquered by the adversary. Confirming this, Saint Augustine says on the words of the Lord: No riches are greater, no treasures, no honors, no substance of this world is greater than the Catholic faith, which saves sinners, enlightens the blind, heals the sick, baptizes catechumens, justifies the faithful, restores the penitent, increases the righteous, crowns martyrs, preserves virgins and widows and married persons in chaste modesty, ordains clerics, consecrates priests, and places believers in the eternal inheritance with the holy angels. Just as in the root of a tree absolutely no appearance of beauty is visible, and yet whatever beauty or comeliness is in the tree proceeds from it, so from faith [unclear] is; since without faith it is impossible to please God. For one who comes to God must believe that he exists and that he is a rewarder of those who seek him.

But the more anyone loves, the more he advances in faith. For faith without charity is like a useless shield thrown away and without a strap. O faith, treasure richer than all things, when formed in the mind of a human being, you make the mortal equal to the immortals, conquer demons and kingdoms, and bravely overcome all hard and adverse things. Faith is the foundation of all virtues; without it no spiritual building stands. Faith is the enlightener of minds and the chief instrument of spiritual warfare. For without the shield of faith the devil is not conquered, nor is the attack of wanton flesh overcome. Let us consider, therefore, brothers, how necessary for salvation faith formed by charity is for us, without which, as we fight in this warfare, neither victory is granted to us nor confidence reserved. For faith is the shield in which we most easily conquer the enemy of our salvation, if we attach the handle of divine love.
