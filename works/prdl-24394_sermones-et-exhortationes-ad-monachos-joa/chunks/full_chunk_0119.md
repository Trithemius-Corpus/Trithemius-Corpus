I give thanks to you, sweetest Lord Jesus; I praise, bless, and adore you for all your labors, journeys, and wearinesses; for hunger, thirst, cold, nakedness, poverty, and want; and for the necessities, injuries, insults, mockeries, and slanders which you sustained throughout your whole life for us, voluntarily and not under compulsion. And as often as you moved your hand to work, your foot to walk, or as often as you opened your mouth to speak, so often do I bless, praise, glorify, and humbly adore you, and with all the powers of my heart I always give thanks to you, mediator of God and human beings, sweetest Jesus Christ. I give thanks to you, Lord Jesus Christ, most merciful king of the heavens, for your most holy teaching, for the establishment of your faith and of the evangelical sacraments, for the calling of the nations, for the justification of sinners, and for all your wonders and powers, and for all your benefits, which you have bestowed either on me in particular or on all others together with me in general. I give thanks to you, Lord Jesus Christ, most kindly savior, for your charity which you had toward the human race, and for your whole most bitter Passion and all the pains which you sustained for us wretched ones. I give thanks to you, Lord Jesus; I praise, bless, and adore you for the sorrow and sadness of your most innocent heart, which, when the time of the Passion was at hand, you sustained for us, when you said to your disciples following you:

“My soul is sorrowful even unto death.” I beseech you, through this sadness of your heart, O Lord God, have mercy on me now and at the hour of my death, and take from me all melancholy and disordered sorrow, granting me at all times a salutary sadness for my sins. I give thanks to you for the sorrow of your most holy heart when, placed in agony, you prayed to the Father as many as three times that he would transfer from you the chalice of the Passion, and, with sweat poured forth in wondrous anguish, you wholly resigned yourself to his will for us. I give thanks to you, O king of heaven and earth, for your captivity, for the insults, injuries, and mockeries which you sustained throughout the whole time of the Passion for our salvation and that of all. I give thanks to you

with immeasurable grief. I adore you, Lord Jesus Christ, crowned with thorns, and I give thanks to you for the piercings and pains of your most holy head, for the blows, spittings, and all the mockeries cruelly inflicted by the impious upon your majesty. I adore you, Christ Jesus, Son of the living God, bearing the very heavy weight of your cross; I adore you, stripped before the multitude looking on; I adore you, stretched out by hands and feet and very cruelly fastened to the cross with iron nails. I adore you, Jesus Christ, eternal king, hanging and dying on the cross for me; and I give thanks for the pains of your wounds, I give thanks for the pouring forth of your most precious blood, I give thanks for your most innocent death, which you voluntarily sustained for our sins and for those of the whole world. I give thanks for your immeasurable goodness, because you called me from fear to faith, because you caused me to be baptized in the name of the Holy Trinity, and because you deigned to call me away from the vanity of the world to serve you in true tranquility of mind, into this holy congregation.
