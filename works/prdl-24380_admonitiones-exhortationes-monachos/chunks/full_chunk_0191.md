No one reaches the homeland, no one will attain eternal life. Let us firmly believe that our Lord Jesus Christ is the one mediator between God and human beings, true God and true man; outside faith in him no one will be saved, however innocently he may seem to live. For he himself says in the holy Gospel: “He who believes in the only-begotten Son of God has eternal life abiding in himself. But he who does not believe in the Son, the wrath of God remains upon him.” What does “the wrath remains upon him” mean, except that the first original sin is not removed from him? For after the institution of the New Testament, original sin is removed from those who believe in him through faith in Christ alone. Circumcision was given to the fathers as a sign of future salvation; yet none of them could enter the kingdom of heaven before Christ the Savior rose from the dead. Therefore outside faith in Christ there is no salvation, since, as he himself said, whoever does not believe is already judged and will be condemned.

Let us remain in Christ through faith and love, for he is the way of salvation, he is truth without error, he is life without death. If we have the Lord Jesus as the guide of our journey, we walk with confidence. Secondly, it is necessary for travelers to pass through the lands of their journey without injury to anyone; for if they commit theft, robbery, or any other similar sin against the commonwealth, they would at last come to be punished with the ultimate penalty and would not reach the intended destination of their journey. But we, brothers, placed on the road by which we strive toward the homeland of the heavenly kingdom, must most carefully beware lest we commit anything by word, deed, or thought contrary to the justice of God; rather, let us render to each his right and his due: first to God, piety, worship, and charity; to our neighbor, love; to ourselves, the holiness of perfect purity. Indeed, if we live otherwise than Christ taught, we do not remain on the right way of salvation, which leads to the kingdom. But we shall remain on the way if we keep the commandments of God. We shall walk in truth if we love the Lord our God himself with the whole strength of our mind. We shall attain life if, turning away from evil, we continually persevere in the practice of good.

Thirdly, it is necessary to have provision for the journey, because if travelers’ supplies fail, they will not be able to reach the place intended. A prudent traveler, before he leaves his house, calculates for himself the length and difficulty of the journey, and according as necessity requires, prepares provisions and expenses. A great and arduous journey remains for us; therefore we must prepare supplies for many years, lest we fail on this road by which we are going. The provision for this journey of ours to the super-heavenly homeland is not gold, not silver, not the deceptive riches of the world, but faith in Christ and love of God. The holy virtues are an unfailing provision of the soul for the kingdom of heaven; strengthened by them, it walks the way of God in gladness. The soul faithful to Christ, and accompanied by the holy virtues, joyfully completes the arduous road of this long pilgrimage.
