In this life repentance is fruitful, and voluntary satisfaction also highly meritorious; but after death there will be no place for repentance, and then each person will receive according to what he has done in his body, whether good or evil. Think on these things, brothers, while you live in health, lest, overtaken by the command of death, you at last grieve too late. Present salvation should not wait for an uncertain tomorrow; and he who can pay his debt with grace while free should not allow himself to be dragged to prison with torment.

As much as each person can in his whole life, so much let him take care to make satisfaction for his sins, since the spirit returns securely to the Lord when, while still existing in the body, it is daily purified by the fire of divine love. Here you have a place in which daily expiation is made for the guilt of public offenses; if you keep true and humble patience in your soul, you will without doubt obtain certain remission of all. As for secret and hidden sins, what you ought to do is taught to you not only by the Rule, but also by daily sacramental confession, which instructs you in all things.

For each day we are accustomed devoutly, one by one, to offer the Son of God at the altar; and none of us approaches that most holy mystery unless he has first, according to custom, made confession, because the greatest purity is required where the salvation of the whole world is renewed through the Mass. Amid these things, dearly beloved brothers, who among us would not daily make progress in the love of God, unless he disgracefully and utterly laid aside every desire for progress? For I dare to say that the monk who does not make progress in this holy congregation is plainly proved to have utterly forgotten his own salvation.

Believe me, brothers: God will save no one unwillingly. If you neglect your own progress, it will be imputed not to him but to you. For God is the intellectual light that enlightens every man coming into this world, as high-flying John says; but those who do not receive the light necessarily remain in darkness. He who turns his mind away from the faith that is in the Son of God remains in darkness; and if he does not do the works of light, faith will profit him nothing for salvation.

Outside the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ there is no salvation; and whoever thinks otherwise is not a Christian. Do not let the consideration of foolish pity seduce you, O man, into believing that anyone outside the faith of Christ, in any religion whatever, will be saved, however innocently he may live, even according to the law of nature; for just as outside the ark of Noah there was no life for human beings, so outside the Christian faith there is no salvation for anyone unto eternal life. For Christ, the only Son of God, is the sole savior of the world; whoever does not believe in him, the wrath of God remains upon him, and he will be condemned with the devil.

But you, my brothers, who now believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, I beg you: henceforth live in such a way that, Christians in name and in deed, you may be able to come to fellowship with him who lives and reigns with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, forever and ever.
