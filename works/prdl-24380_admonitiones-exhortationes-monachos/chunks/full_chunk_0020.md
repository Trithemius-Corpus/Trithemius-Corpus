For the righteous man says: “With those who hated peace I was peaceable; when I spoke to them, they attacked me without cause.” Hence Saint Augustine says: “Among the true worshippers of God even wars themselves are peaceful, when they are waged not from greed or cruelty, but from zeal for peace, so that the wicked may be restrained and the good assisted.” He has peace within himself who, illumined by purity and holiness, lives in his own conscience secure, without error, whom anxious synderesis does not gnaw, nor any unjust or wicked thought disturb. Hence Augustine again says: “Peace is serenity of mind, tranquillity of soul, simplicity of heart, the bond of love, the fellowship of charity. It is this that removes enmities and rivalries, restrains wars, suppresses anger, treads down the proud, loves the humble, calms those at odds, reconciles enemies, is pleasing to all, burdensome to none, troublesome to none, oppressive to none; it does not know how to exalt itself, it does not know how to be puffed up. Let him who receives it hold it fast; let him who has lost it seek it again; let him who has let it slip search for it, because whoever is not found in it is cast off by the Father, disinherited by the Son, and likewise made a stranger to the Holy Spirit. He can never come to the inheritance of the Lord who has refused to observe the testament of peace.”

For the place of God is in peace, but the dwelling of the devil is in anger and disturbance. Therefore every man who does not have this threefold peace in his heart, who has not offered it in speech, nor shown it in works, is to be called neither a monk nor a Christian. Hence the Lord says in the Gospel: “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God.” If those who make peace are called sons of God, beyond doubt those who confound peace are sons of Satan. Therefore, my most beloved brothers, have among yourselves that peace which the world cannot give, which is the peace of God and of our Lord Jesus Christ, of which Isaiah the prophet said: “And the work of justice shall be peace, and the practice of justice silence and security forever.” For the Lord is the God of peace and love; hence whoever violates peace and brotherly love shows that he is not a true lover of God.

If the true peace of Christ is in your hearts, you will fear no adversities of this world. Among you also let there be true peace, not counterfeit, because Christ desires peace not from the mouth but from the heart, and wishes one’s neighbor to be greeted with affection rather than with the lips. Therefore, if anyone among you has in any way grieved his brother, let him make satisfaction to him as soon as possible before sunset, and all of you take care that the sun not go down upon your anger, because anger grown old begets hatred and strips the soul of every adornment of the virtues. Therefore, monk, if you have sinned against your brother, do penance before him at once, and falling at his knees beg pardon. “And the sword,” he says, “of the Spirit, which is the word of God,” through every prayer and supplication: as soldiers of Christ, let us always hold it in our hands.
