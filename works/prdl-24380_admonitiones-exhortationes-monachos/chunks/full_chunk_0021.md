For Scripture says: “Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” Therefore, when you rise from your bed in the morning, at once make that protestation against the adversary of your salvation which I have described; and neither the devil nor a perverse man will be able to harm you by enchantment or sorcery, whether it be [unclear] or anything else. Do this same thing when you are about to sleep at night, and as often as you perceive any temptation of the evil spirit rising up in your thought. And thus you will be safe in all your ways, and the devil will flee far from you; nor will he dare to come nearer to you, unless your own sloth first trips you up, so that you either cast away the sword or commit some mortal sin. For Scripture says: “Those who commit iniquity and sin are enemies of their own soul.” Solomon also says in Proverbs: “His own iniquities ensnare the wicked man, and he is bound fast by the cords of his own sins. He shall die because he had no discipline, and in the multitude of his folly he shall be deceived.” Therefore, most beloved brothers, if you wish to dwell securely under the protection of almighty God, flee sin, because sin separates us from the Lord’s help, just as purity and cleanness of conscience join us to God, as the prophet Isaiah says: “Behold, the hand of the Lord is not shortened so that it cannot save, nor is his ear heavy so that it cannot hear; but your iniquities have made a division between you and your God, and your sins have hidden his face from you, so that he would not hear.” Jeremiah also says: “Our iniquities have turned these things away, and our sins have withheld good from us.” For everyone who sins mortally opens the door of his heart to the devil and closes it to God, nor can he trust in the help of the Most High as long as he remains lying in wickedness without repentance.
