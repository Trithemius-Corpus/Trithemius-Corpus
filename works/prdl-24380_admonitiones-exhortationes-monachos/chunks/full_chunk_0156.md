By so much the more swiftly and strongly does the perfection of the soul rise up. The love of God is a tower of strength, which all the ministers of diabolical depravity cannot approach. The love of God is the consummation of all justice and the end of monastic discipline. The love of God perfects the monk and makes him complete in all good works. The love of God is the sweet yoke of inward sweetness, through which every filth of earthly corruption is driven out from the mind. The love of God honors souls and makes them pure and immaculate from all stains. The love of God perfectly enlightens the mind in every knowledge of heavenly goods, and purges the very affection from all unclean vanities. The love of God makes the one who truly possesses it most learned and wonderfully instructed in every Scripture. The love of God renders the monk formidable to all enemies and grants angelic power to those who persevere. The love of God defends a man in dangers, because whoever truly loves God fears no adversities. The love of God makes the one who possesses it secure in the hour of death and safe from demons, and joins the soul to the heavenly companies. The love of God brings its possessor back to the heavenly fatherland and grants eternal glory to those who love God. The love of God is the greatest of all good works and the single foundation of all virtues in the faith of Christ. The love of God is the best ointment, in which all the wounds of languishing souls are healed. The love of God is an abundant remedy for poverty and want, and a consolation more effective than every possession of riches. The love of Christ far surpasses all the learning of this world, and most perfectly instructs those who possess it in all knowledge necessary for salvation. The love of God prepares a delightful dwelling for the Holy Spirit in the heart of man, and preserves him, if he perseveres, in all purity. The love of God is the swiftest of all teachers, because what fleshly people learn over time and with much study, the Holy Spirit hands on in a moment to those who love God. The love of God gives knowledge to little ones and makes rustics more learned than the educated. The love of God is stronger than all things, and by its virtue rules over all. The love of God commands demons by a single word, and by the sign of the cross drives them from the bodies of possessed men. The love of God has bestowed eternal blessedness on the holy spirits in heaven, whom the grace of the Creator confirmed in love. The love of God beatifies, in eternal happiness, men baptized in the faith of Jesus Christ, because no one has ever been able to reach the fellowship of the angels unless he was proved to have been inflamed by persevering love of God. For the love of God alone is what makes works good and meritorious, since God accepts from us as an offered gift of good work only that which the flame of charity has made worthy in his sight. Whatever is done outside charity is judged unworthy in God's sight. Attend now, my brothers, to our admonitions, and consider diligently how necessary it is for you to love God above all things, if you desire to come to his kingdom.
