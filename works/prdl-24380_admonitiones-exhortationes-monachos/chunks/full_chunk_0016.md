Homily III.

Certainly you hold in your hands the shield of salvation, by which, with the Lord himself granting it, you will easily overcome all adversaries. Of this armor it is written: The saints through faith conquered kingdoms, worked righteousness, and obtained the promise. John also says: Everything that is born of God overcomes the world; and this is the victory that overcomes the world, our faith. And Peter, the Prince of the Apostles, likewise admonishes us, saying: Brothers, be sober and watch, because your adversary the devil, like a roaring lion, goes about seeking someone to devour; resist him, strong in faith.

True faith and confidence in our Lord Jesus Christ is an invincible and most powerful armor, which none of the adversaries has ever been able to withstand, as it is written in the book of Chronicles: King Jehoshaphat said to his army: Believe in the Lord your God, and you will be secure. In Ecclesiasticus too we read as follows: You who fear the Lord, believe him, and your reward will not be made void. And again: In your work believe from the faith of your soul, for this is the keeping of the commandments. He who believes God attends to his commandments, and he who trusts in him will not be diminished.

Therefore now take care, dearly beloved brothers, and always labor with the greatest zeal, so that you may carry in your hands this shield of faith formed by holy works against all the attacks of your enemies; for he who is protected by this unconquerable shield is never overcome by the adversary. Confirming this, blessed Augustine says concerning the words of the Lord: There are no greater riches, no treasures, no honors, no substance of this world greater than the Catholic faith, which saves sinful men, enlightens the blind, heals the sick, baptizes catechumens, justifies the faithful, restores the penitent, increases the righteous, crowns martyrs, preserves virgins and widows and married persons in chaste modesty, ordains clerics, consecrates priests, and places believers with the holy angels in the eternal inheritance.

Just as in the root of a tree no appearance of beauty at all is seen, and yet whatever beauty or comeliness there is in the tree proceeds from it, so from the humility of faith, whatever merit, whatever blessedness the soul is to receive, proceeds wholly from the foundation of faith. For, as the Apostle says: Whatever, or everything, that is not from faith is sin, since without faith it is impossible to please God. For one who comes to God must believe that he exists and that he is a rewarder of those who seek him.

Moreover, the more each person loves, the more he advances in faith. For faith without charity is like a useless discarded shield, a shield without a strap. O faith, a treasure richer than all, which, formed in the mind of man, makes the mortal comparable to the immortals, conquers demons and kingdoms, and bravely overcomes all hard and adverse things. Faith is the foundation of all virtues, without which no spiritual building stands. Faith is the enlightener of minds and the chief instrument of spiritual warfare. For without the shield of faith the devil is not conquered, nor is the assault of wanton flesh overcome.
