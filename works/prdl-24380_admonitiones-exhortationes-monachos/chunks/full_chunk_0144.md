Chastity is supported by two companions, namely fasting and temperance; if it is ever deprived of them, it very quickly, without doubt, begins to totter. Chastity is the greatest adornment of monks; adorned with it, they will be brought into the company of the angels. Nothing is more beautiful than chastity: it makes clean what was conceived from unclean seed, it makes a member of the household out of an enemy, and an angel out of an earthly man.

Chastity is the most beautiful virtue of all virtues; when it is promised to be kept even until death for the love of Christ, it is rewarded with an inestimable prize. Chastity is nearest to the fellowship of angels, and is the highest medicine of the Christian mind. Chastity is the beloved dwelling place of Jesus Christ, the eternal King, and the wondrous beginning of future incorruption. Chastity is the fullness of heavenly grace, through which every corruption of nature is restored. Chastity is the most powerful virtue of all, through which, in a fragile vessel, nature is conquered in a wondrous contest. Chastity imitates the angelic life, and knows no sprinkling of stain in the midst of the mire.

Blessed is the man who has truly deserved to receive from the Lord God the gift of chastity, and has kept it inviolate until death in Christian humility. He is chaste who, pure in mind and body for the love of Jesus Christ, abstains from every defilement of the flesh; who, with the fervor of the Holy Spirit, burns sweetly in the love of God; who desires absolutely nothing in this world; who always longs to be and to live with Christ.

He is chaste who, mindful of his profession in spiritual warfare, restrains the movements of the flesh by reason, and at once, from the very beginning, firmly checks all the itching sparks of lust. He is chaste who, as soon as a carnal thought has begun to strike the mind, implores divine help with tears, and does not cease from prayers until he feels that the fire of itching ardor has been utterly extinguished.

He is chaste who flees every matter and occasion of lust; who has made a covenant with his eyes that he should think nothing about a woman, and sets a guard over his senses, lest any foul or immodest things be thrust upon them. He is chaste who avoids the company of the wanton, and always turns his eyes away lest he behold vain things. He is chaste who detests the frequent company of all women, and who at all times regards his own frailty with fear and vigilance as suspect.

He indeed is chaste who in mind and body imitates the angelic nature, and who admits no corruption in holy conduct. Chastity is a gift of grace, not a work of nature or of human effort. Chastity makes a humble monk most pleasing to God, since he who lives in mortal flesh without corruption is a companion of the angels. He who has attained true chastity is always humble; and he who understands its nature does not arrogate its possession to himself, but to the Lord who gave it. For no one is chaste by his own strength, since frail man cannot conquer nature.
