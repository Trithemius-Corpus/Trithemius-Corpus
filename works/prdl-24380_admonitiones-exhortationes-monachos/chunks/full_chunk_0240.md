A spider’s web, made as if of blood, is easily broken if any weight is placed on it; but you apostates tell falsehoods for truths, and under the fiction of poverty you deceive the vicar of Christ, indeed you deceive your very selves. Would that the poverty you allege were true; would that, as you mendaciously pretend, you had less earthly substance. Why do you allege poverty, you who possess very great riches? Who does not know that the condition of the monastery of S. Albani in temporal goods before its transfer was most abundant? What shall I say of the once distinguished monastery of Elwangen, whose riches could have sufficed for a prince? Could the monastery of S. Burchard have been transferred because of poverty, when it always abounded in many riches? Ought the monastery of Camberg to have been dismembered from the order because of poverty, when to this day it surpasses all the monastic houses of that province in riches? What shall I say of Selsz? What of the monasteries of Clingenmünster, most abundant as far as moderate measure is concerned? Could poverty truly have been alleged in their transfer?

O betrayers of your order and transgressors of your vow, do you believe that lying words can remain hidden? Your deceits are not hidden from us, nor are we ignorant of the riches that you deny. If you were poor, as you pretend, some of you could not lay out two, others three, others four thousand florins, and some still more, for your transfer. Why, then, do you allege poverty, when you are not poor? Why do you say that you have nothing from which you can live as monks, when you can lay out such great sums of money in order to become canons?
