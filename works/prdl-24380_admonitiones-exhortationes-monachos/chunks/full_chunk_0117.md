For it is difficult to cure an illness that is long and has become inveterate through length of time. Let us consider with how great diligence and attentiveness servants stand before earthly kings and princes, so that we may not be ignorant of how it befits us to stand in prayer before God, who alone is King of all and Lord of the universe. The eyes of all look to the Lord, and all together and each one singly depend most carefully upon his nod, lest, when he commands, anything escape those who attend him through negligence. From this, therefore, let us consider with how great purity we ought to stand before the Lord God, day and night, in the assembly of prayer, from whom we desire to obtain the kingdom of eternal blessedness.

Not voice, not shouting, but an intention fervent in the love of God merits being heard. Therefore flee drowsiness and sluggishness of mind as a deadly poison, because sleep and the [unclear] of the eyes are the source of all vices. But the watchful eye is the servant of inward devotion, and the monk who truly loves Jesus Christ guards himself from every excess. He who is watchful sharpens his mind with understanding; but the monk who sleeps much shamefully blinds his own soul. A monk watchful in prayer and fasting is formidable to the devil; but one who is sated with food and sleep is not permitted to rest by the spirit of fornication.

For a vigil dedicated to prayers breaks the heat of lust, powerfully extinguishes the fire of desire, purifies the inward parts of the heart, enlightens the mind, and inflames the affection toward love of heavenly things. Vigil is the parent, nurse, and guardian of good thoughts, the sure victory over the temptations of the ancient enemy, the flight of phantasms, the death of vices, the life and fountain of all virtues. But much sleep is the companion and fosterer of lust, the robber of a man’s life, the image of death, the bringer of forgetfulness, the spouse of sloth. Immoderate sleep corrupts the stomach, harms the brain, and weakens both body and soul. A monk often given over to sleep despises wisdom, prostitutes chastity, neglects the salvation of his soul, loses precious time, kills the mind, and nourishes vices.

Therefore, whoever among you, most beloved brothers, desires to reach the rest of heavenly blessedness, let him vigorously drive away from himself all sluggishness and cowardice of mind. Let him learn first of all to conquer himself, so that thereafter he may be able to overcome the devil, the enemy of his salvation. For truly, unless you first root out the evil that is in you, when will you overcome that which comes from outside? Learn to rule your passions, and with the Lord’s help you will easily overcome everything that happens outwardly. Cleanse your mind from every disordered desire, so that in truth you may love Jesus alone above all things, with whom you hope to rejoice forever.

But no one will reign with Christ in heaven who does not imitate him on earth by holy conduct, as holy Scripture says: “He who says that he remains in Christ ought himself also to walk just as he walked.” For the Lord Jesus, who by his death freed us from the destruction of eternal damnation, himself willingly became mediator between God and men; he himself is the way by which the path of life must be continued for us; he himself is the powerful guide of the journey; he himself is the pattern and rule of saving conduct for Christians. No one comes to God the Father except through Christ; no one will see the Father except through the Son.

Therefore, if you wish to come to salvation, you must pass through the Savior. For he himself said in the holy Gospel: “No one comes to the Father except through me.” And also: “I am the door; if anyone enters through me, he will find the pasture of eternal life.” And again: “I am the way, and the truth, and the life.” By this way, living with pious and holy conduct, I beg you, my brothers, always walk; love this truth fervently with a pure heart; hasten ardently toward this life, which alone is lasting.

Let the teaching of Christ never depart from your hearts, but remain in memory at all times, since he refuses to become a true imitator of him who has not been exercised day and night in meditation on his words. Therefore, always watchful in prayers, live with such purity of mind that at the hour of death you may deserve to hear with joy our Lord Jesus Christ calling you, who lives and reigns with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God almighty, one, forever and ever. Amen.
