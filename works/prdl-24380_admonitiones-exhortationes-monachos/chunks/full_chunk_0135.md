On the True Humility of Monks

Humility makes the one who has it a servant of God; but pride makes him a slave of the devil. Humility makes a man wise in the law of the Lord; but pride renders a man foolish, away from the knowledge of the Most High. Humility brings the poor man into the fellowship of God; but pride makes the rich man members of the devil. Humility is the mother and nurse of all virtues; but pride is the source of all vices. Humility, through charity, covers a multitude of sins; but pride alone brings in the fullness of all vices.

Humility makes the good works of monks most pleasing to God; but pride renders the efforts of the arrogant idle before the Creator. Humility receives from the Lord whatever it asks; but almighty God resists the prayers of the proud. Humility always has devout confidence, with cheerfulness of mind, in the mercies of the Lord; but pride, with the sting of despair, begets disturbance in a man. Humility always has joy of spirit in the love of Christ; but destructive fear always occupies pride. Humility, the fosterer of good works, neglects none of the spiritual exercises; but pride despises all pursuits of salvation.

Humility justifies sinners; but pride condemns its followers. Humility makes a man near to God; but pride makes him alien from the grace of God. Humility diligently obeys the commandments of God with a peaceful will; but pride struggles against the precepts of the Most High. Humility heaps up the merits of everlasting happiness; but pride prepares for itself the most grievous torments of hell. Humility grants security at the moment of death; but pride brings horrible fear and calamity upon the dying man.

Humility frees the soul of its worshiper from every anguish in the dread judgment of God; but pride accuses before the Judge all the arrogant whom it has deceived in the world. Humility will place the servants of God at the Judge’s right hand; but pride will set those to be damned at his left. Humility will plead the cause of its own and prevail in judgment; but pride, utterly confounded, will hand the arrogant over to the devil. Humility will repay its friends with the reward of eternal happiness; but pride will give the torments of perpetual damnation to all the arrogant.

Humility will be crowned without end in heaven; but pride will suffer immeasurable torment with the demons forever in hell. Humility, crowned, will reign with Christ in heaven; but pride, despised, will be tormented with the devil in punishments. Humility, joyful and glorious, will sit forever at the right hand of God; but pride, sorrowful and shameful, will lie without end in the torments of hell. Then the humble will exult in glory, and all the proud will cry out in punishment through everlasting ages with exceedingly great grief.

There the humble will stand in the sight of God and of all the saints, in great gladness and everlasting exultation, receiving for the voluntary poverty of this life unfading riches; for meekness, a kingdom; for sorrow, joy; for the love of God, dominion; for contempt of the world and voluntary mortification of the flesh, heaven.

But the proud and arrogant, placed in hell, seeing such great glory of the humble whom they despised in the world, will marvel at so sudden a change of the saints. They will be troubled with horrible fear, groaning from anguish of spirit, and saying in the fury of pride: “These are the poor and humble monks, once clothed in mean garments, separated from men, abstaining from the eating of meat and from every filthiness of the flesh, whom we once held in derision and as a likeness of reproach. We senseless men judged their life madness and their end without honor. Behold how they have been counted among the sons of God, and their lot is among the saints. Therefore we have strayed from the way of truth, and the light of justice has not shone for us, and the sun of understanding has not risen for us. What does pride now profit us, by which we were then puffed up and despised the humble? And what does the boasting of riches possessed in the world bring us? All things have passed away, and we, because of our stubbornness, have been counted among the demons, since we always hated humility, and we suffer immeasurable torments without end. But the humble will live forever, and their reward is with the Lord, and their thought is with the Most High.”

Think on these things always, my brothers, because without doubt they are going to happen in this way. Therefore take up true humility of mind; flee every sin; keep your conscience pure, so that you may deserve to obtain the rewards of eternal life. Amen.
