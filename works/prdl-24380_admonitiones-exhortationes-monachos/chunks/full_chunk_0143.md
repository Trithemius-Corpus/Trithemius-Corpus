Stand in humility and true obedience, and no adversities will ever prevail against you. Just as a tree that sends many great roots into the earth is not easily torn from its place, so a monk who possesses charity and obedience in humility, together with the other virtues, is by no means overcome by demons. Obedience is a great virtue, brothers; it plants the other virtues in the mind, and, once planted, guards them through the ministry of humility. May our Lord Jesus Christ himself, who was made obedient for us unto the death of the cross, enlighten your hearts by his grace, and sweetly inflame your minds at all times for the observance of the regular life; he who lives and reigns with God the Father, in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.

On Continence and True Chastity. Homily XXI.

I would greatly wish, my dearest brothers, that I had obtained from God that grace by which I might be a worthy and suitable pastor for you in deed as well as in word. For I know, and it does not escape me, how dangerous a thing the governance of souls is for a young and inexperienced man such as I am, when one who has not yet learned to direct and govern himself according to the rule of justice presumes to undertake the teaching of others. I blush to open my mouth among you to teach, since after only a short time as a disciple I am compelled, before maturity, to be a teacher. Yet since it has so pleased you, and you have wished to have me, a young man, as your master, spare me, for necessity compels me to fulfill, according to my strength, the office entrusted to me. Help me with your prayers, I beg you, brothers, so that the mercy of God may supply the strength that the inexperience of age denies.

For I desire you to walk before God in all purity of mind and body, so that you may please him to whom you gave yourselves from the beginning. Look to our Lord Jesus Christ as the guide of the journey you have undertaken toward heaven: born of the ever most chaste Virgin Mary, he preserved forever the most perfect chastity of mind and body. Follow this teacher, into whose service you have consecrated your hands, so that you may preserve continence for yourselves not only in body, but also perpetually guard in mind a chastity brighter than sapphire and carbuncle. For chastity is a most beautiful virtue, especially fitting for those in the cloister; through it every impulse of lust is curbed under the yoke of reason, and, the madness of vices being extinguished, the body’s disposition is preserved in complete honorableness.

An incontinent and wanton monk, who follows the filthy lust of the flesh, is counted with the demons who withdrew from the Lord. For there are three things without whose perpetual observance no one is to be reckoned worthy of being named among monks. The first of these is the obedience of humble and true subjection; and the second, the renunciation of everything of one’s own.
