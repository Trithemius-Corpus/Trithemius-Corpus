My discourse is now directed to you, O abbots, who do not correct the evil pursuits of your monks, who consent to their apostasy, and who yourselves apostatize with them from the order. O abbot, shepherd and idol, you who make wolves out of sheep, secular men out of monks. What will you answer to the supreme Shepherd when you are compelled to render an account of your stewardship? What will you say to Saint Benedict when he demands his sheep back from you? He will require monks from you, not canons. What then will you answer him? Are monks canons?

When that day comes, to be feared by all mortals, on which the just Judge will repay each one according to his works, Benedict, the lawgiver of monks, do not doubt it, will accuse you gravely; against you he will bring forth as witnesses all the true monks of his order. With one voice they will cry out to the Judge: “Just Judge, Jesus Christ, you who know all things, behold the madness, crime, sacrilege, and cruelty of this apostate, who destroyed the holy order, recalled monks to the world, broke faith, and with unheard-of presumption violated the vow made to you and to us.”

O wretched man, what will you do when so gravely accused? Which of the saints will you invoke, when you have made them all your adversaries? Where will you flee from the face of the Lord’s wrath? To whom will you flee for refuge? There will be no place for flight; rather, you will stand before the tribunal of Christ, about to receive a harsh sentence, unless here, through repentance and tears, you take care to appease the face of the Lord. No room will be granted for excuse when you have been accused in the judgment of him who knows all things. For whatever you may wish, it will be nothing, because, accused outwardly, inwardly by the testimony of conscience you will be judged condemned.

You seize the things that belong to divine right, though you had been appointed their guardian. Therefore you will hear the Lord saying: “Pay back what you owe.” That is: “Give back the monks whom I entrusted to your faith; give back the monastery which you received to govern; give back faith; give back the vow; give back holiness; give back the gain; give back the merit; give back the honor. Pay back what you owe. I appointed you shepherd of my sheep, so that them in holy…”
