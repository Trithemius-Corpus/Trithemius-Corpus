Paul, the lover, says in the epistle: I beseech you, dearly beloved, by the mercy of God, that you present your bodies as a living, holy, God-pleasing sacrifice. Whoever chastens his body by the exercises of holy abstinence and brings it under the service of the spirit has consecrated a living sacrifice to the Lord. Whoever has now learned to keep his heart clean and pure from every pollution of sin has beyond doubt offered a holy sacrifice to the Lord. And whoever perseveres in the purpose of holiness to the end has without doubt offered a pleasing sacrifice.

We gird our loins when we keep ourselves pure from every form of lust, unstained in body and in mind. The virtue of holy chastity is of great merit before God, and because of the greatness of the struggle it is to be given a singular reward in the recompense of the righteous, as the Lord says through the prophet Isaiah: Thus says the Lord to the eunuchs who keep my sabbaths, and choose the things that I have willed, and hold fast my covenant: I will give them in my house and within my walls a place and a name better than sons and daughters. I will give them an everlasting name that shall not perish.

And of these the Lord says in the holy Gospel: There are eunuchs who were born so from their mother’s womb; and there are eunuchs made by men; and there are eunuchs who have castrated themselves for the kingdom of heaven. He who can receive it, let him receive it.

But why did he say, He who can receive it, let him receive it? I think that this mention of eunuchs, who castrate themselves for love of the kingdom of heaven, must not be referred to the cutting off of the genital members, as many have thought and so have strayed from the truth of the Gospel, but rather to the command of the mind, by which chastity, conceived from the love of Christ, is kept whole. For those castrate themselves for the kingdom of God who, for the love of God, preserve true chastity of mind and body inviolate. And just as no one is easily castrated in body without the pain of great torment, so no one is able to attain the perfection of chastity without the greatest application of labor.

And certainly no one is chaste by his own strength; no one is modest by his own merits. It is God who gives the good will; it is he also who joins virtue to the will, so that it may have power. But because it is not for many to live unmarried, he leaves each one to his own judgment, saying: He who can receive it, let him receive it. The cutting off of members does not make the mind chaste, because the root of lust is within, and cannot be removed by the amputation of the private parts. But if the mind is chaste and whole, it will easily restrain the insolence of the members. For nothing is stronger than the mind of a man who with a pure heart sweetly loves God above all things.

Now consider, most beloved brothers, the future rewards of beauty, and the labor of chastity will not burden you. For the great rewards of exaltation are reached by the persevering effort of arduous labor. Therefore whoever shrinks from the labor of the contest will never come to the height of honor. The time is short now in which we labor, resisting the devil; but very soon the eternal time will come, in which, reigning, we shall rejoice without end.
