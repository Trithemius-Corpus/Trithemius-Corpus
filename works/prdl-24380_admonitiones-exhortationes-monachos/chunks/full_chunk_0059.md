And who truly loves God does not utter an idle word. Apply every care and attention to your thoughts, because evil must be watched with chief intent there where it takes the beginning of its progress, so that as soon as it begins to arise it may be cut off at the root. For there is no thought of evil so weak that it does not grow with time, if it is nourished by the consent of the one thinking it. Therefore evil, while still weak, must be conquered at once from the beginning, because when delay is interposed, evil gains strength, however small it may seem at its outset.

A great fire often arises from a small spark, and from a tiny thought a great sin sometimes rises up. When thought gives birth to delight, it is soon accustomed to break out into consent, and consequently even into deed, if it can, and to cast itself headlong. But when the will has given consent through delight to an evil thought for the deed, even if what the soul desires is not carried out, nevertheless the sin has already been consummated before God. But what does Scripture say? Sin, when it has been consummated, begets death.

For almighty God looks upon the will of man, whether he wills good or evil. Therefore he says in the holy Gospel: Whoever looks at a woman in order to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart. God counts the will as the deed whenever a man cannot bring into effect what he wished. For this reason again and again I admonish you, brothers, and with as much diligence as I can I urge you, that according to the holy Apostle you walk cautiously, not as the unwise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

Watch most carefully for the snares of the evil demon, and resist evil and unclean thoughts immediately from the beginning, because remedy is thought of far too late when the sickness has gained strength by the course of time. Accustom yourselves at all times to dwell on good and holy thoughts, and you will easily be able to overcome the insolence of evil ones. For just as vessels filled with good liquids receive nothing of external corruption, so minds filled with good thoughts do not admit the excess of evil thoughts.

The eye of the heart is pure, by which God is seen, and through purity of conscience one comes to the glory of eternal happiness. For the Lord says in the holy Gospel: Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God. A pure heart is the dwelling place of the Holy Spirit, and where an evil thought is not admitted, God beyond doubt abides. A pure heart is the seat of wisdom, and where purity of mind is guarded, no dangers of adversity will do harm.

Be mindful of Scripture when it says: Perverse thoughts separate from God, but proven virtue rebukes the foolish. For wisdom will not enter a malicious soul, nor will it dwell in a body subject to sins. For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit and will withdraw itself from thoughts that are without understanding.
