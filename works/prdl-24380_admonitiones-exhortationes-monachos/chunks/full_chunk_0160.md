Homily XXIV.

Nothing is more pleasant, nothing, finally, more precious than a good conscience; for where the mind has within itself the manifest testimony of its own uprightness, it suffers no remorse of destructive unrest. An upright and good conscience is an incredible exultation of the mind and a sure comfort in every adversity. The monk who has obtained the testimony of a good conscience neither refuses to live nor fears the coming arrival of death. A good conscience is always glad, always pleasant, always fearless, and truly tranquil in the peace of Jesus Christ. In a good conscience there is always peace, piety, goodness, light, virtue, honor, joy, gladness, sweetness, and delight without dissipation. Conscience is the treasury of the soul, because whatever is placed in it is kept as provision for the future life. A good conscience is the best treasure; if anyone has neglected to possess it, he must be judged the most wretched and unhappy of all men. A good conscience is the dwelling place of the Holy Spirit and the earthly paradise of all graces.

A good conscience does not fear the slanders of the wicked, and a mind conscious of what is right despises the lies of reputation. A good conscience is always cheerful, and he who works the justice of God is not uselessly sad. A good conscience always advances in the love of the Lord, and he who lives innocently will not dread danger. A good conscience has peace and concord with all in God, and in humble simplicity takes care for its salvation. A good conscience strengthens the soul; but he who is constant in evil will fall into despair. A good conscience confidently rebukes the foolish; but he who is evil keeps bitter silence. A good conscience gladdens the soul; but he who is conscious of evil in himself suffers continual desolation of mind. A good conscience safely reproves the impious; but he who has lost the testimony of uprightness always fears being accused. A good conscience is the most beautiful adornment of the soul; and he who does not care for wisdom in this life will die in poverty.

A good conscience is the dwelling place of virtues and the true abode of heavenly mysteries. A good conscience is the consummation of all philosophy and the fullness of all sciences. A good conscience is the best teacher of virtues and the completed discipline of all studies. A good conscience bears the best fruit of holiness, and, standing founded in the fear of the Lord, admits no sin. A good conscience is more precious than all the riches of the world, and far sweeter than all desirable things that can be possessed in this life. A good conscience is the merit of Christian perfection and the humbly presumable beginning of future happiness. A good conscience is the fertile field of virtues and the sweet and pleasant fragrance of the Paradise of God. A good conscience is the sweetest companion of this life and a glorious mediator between God and man. A good conscience is very fearful to demons, but a pleasing friend to the holy angels. A good conscience makes the dying man secure against the envy of demons, and deserves to have the escort of angels. Conscience
