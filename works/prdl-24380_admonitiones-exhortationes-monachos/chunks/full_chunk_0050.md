If a foul thought has been cast into your mind by demons, then, after making the sign of the cross upon your forehead, say at once: Save me, Jesus Christ, mediator of God and men, and drive away from me all the wickedness of the ancient enemy through your most innocent passion.

What can be shorter than this prayer, or more powerful? For as soon as it has been uttered with fervor of mind against either the presence of demons or their suggestion, every contrivance of iniquity withdraws. For the power of pure prayer is very great, since it both penetrates heaven and by its steadfastness defeats the powers of the air. Prayer enlightens the mind, purifies the affections, obtains pardon for sins, and strengthens the spirit in the love of God against all adversaries. Nothing so raises the mind from earthly things to the love of heavenly things; nothing so swiftly and powerfully exterminates the pleasures of the flesh; nothing so enlightens the understanding; nothing also so greatly confounds the adversary of truth as pure prayer, which is poured out to God with the fervor of divine love and is adorned by the testimony of a good conscience.

If you desire to be terrible to demons, if you wish to be a friend of God, keep your conscience pure, and never cease from spiritual prayer. For devout prayer can do all things, if it is adorned with uprightness of faith and purity of life. Prayer puts demons to flight, weakens the power of malignant spirits, heals the soul languishing in tepidity, and strengthens the spirit that is weak and infirm in the fear of God. Pure prayer brings one near to God, and makes him whom it had received as a servant of God to be his friend. Pure prayer, fervent in the love of God, penetrates heaven, wonderfully enlightens the understanding of the one who prays, shuts hell, opens Paradise, and brings in the souls of the faithful.

For Truth does not lie, who promises in the holy Gospel, saying: Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives; and he who seeks finds; and to the one who knocks it shall be opened. Which of you has ever turned to the Lord with his whole heart and prayed, and was not heard? Or who has called upon the Lord, and he withdrew himself from him? For Scripture says: Know that the Lord will hear your prayers, if you remain steadfast, continuing in fasts and prayers in the sight of the Lord.

Remember Moses, the servant of the Lord, who cast down Amalek, trusting in his own strength, not by fighting with the sword, but by praying with holy prayers. For the continual supplication of a righteous man avails much, and the prayer of one who humbles himself penetrates the clouds. Elijah was a man subject to suffering like us, and he prayed with prayer that it might not rain upon the earth, and it did not rain for three years and six months. And again he prayed, and heaven gave rain, and the earth gave its fruit. By prayer and tears King Hezekiah obtained fifteen years of longer life, and by prayers changed the sentence of the prophet. By prayers holy men became renowned for miracles, and, supplicating the Lord, called the dead back to life.
