*A Sapphic Poem by Rutger Sicambrius, for a Hymn in Praise of Saint Anne*

We sing your praises, venerable Anne;
favor the measures of those who praise you,
obtaining pardon for all, granting
the rewards of salvation.

You, Anne, parent of Christ’s mother,
shining in the highest citadel of heaven,
obtain whatever you ask of the offspring
of the omnipotent one.

You heal the sick; you wipe away stains;
you make faithful servants attain their vow;
at last you grant them to climb
to the heavenly kingdom.

By your approved morals you moved
the highest Lord, Anne, and by many prayers,
that he should grant you to become the parent
of so great an offspring in the world.

To the highest Father, to the heavenly Son,
to the Holy Spirit be honor and power,
brightness, virtue, devotion, and strength
through all ages.

*Another by the Same*

Let us all resound the diligent praises
of Anne, mother of the Virgin Mother,
with all our powers, from the marrow of the soul,
with sonorous voice.

She brought forth for the wretched race,
stained by the blemish of Eve our mother,
a noble offspring, the radiant beam that
she gave to the world.

This light of the world drove out
the darkness of the blackening world,
shining on all with heavenly light,
as holy John resounds in the sacred book.

Anne, mother of the divine maiden,
whose grandson you call the groaning one,
only according to the flesh: do not spurn
the people who pray.

Virgin, favor us by the intent prayers
of Anne, your mother, who entreats for us;
you, grandson Jesus, graciously fulfill
her prayer.

To the Son born of Anne’s daughter,
together with God, be the highest honor and power;
to the blessed Spirit let equal honor be given
for all time.

*Another Iambic Dimeter Poem by the Same, Which Is Sung to the Melody of the Hymns of Ambrose*

Bent low, let us honor
the feast of blessed Anne,
singing due thanks with our voices
and with our inmost hearts.

She is the mother of the nurturing Virgin,
who brought forth the light of the world;
she is the one who by her virtues
made herself most pleasing to God.

She drives away delays; she confers grace
by her prayers;
she relieves the most dreadful diseases,
both of body and of spirit.

In praise of her, O King of heaven,
to you with sonorous songs,
and to the Son and likewise to the Spirit,
we pour forth pious praises. Amen.

*An Alcinian Poem by the Same, Which Is Sung to the Melody “Crux fidelis” or “Pange lingua”*

With harmonious voice, with vows
and with pious hearts,
this feast of Anne the mother,
so illustrious, grows eager to set forth.
We beg that you be gently present
to those who praise you.

You are the parent of Mary’s mother;
you are most pleasing to God.
You preserved the ordinances of the law
which Moses once delivered
to the fathers of old
and to the Hebrew peoples.

Wondrous, gracious gifts
you obtain for the faithful,
by prayer pleasing
before the Son of the high Father.
You heal the sick, and grant
that hearts be chaste.

We ask you, Father,
and the Son with the Spirit,
that you grant joyful things to us
who sing together the praises
of the renowned, most sweet Anne,
the mother of the nurturing Virgin. Amen.

*A Poem by Rutger Venray, a German Regular Canon of Heinsberg, on the Book Concerning the Praises of Saint Anne*

Whoever you may be, reader of this excellent book,
put aside empty fear; no frivolous things hold it.
This book, pregnant with wondrous meaning and sweet with charm,
Abbot Trithemius brought forth by his art,
watching over books, guardian of the sheep of Spanheim,
who cultivates the sacred writings of the threefold tongue.

Here you will find the titles of Anne, full of reason and art;
only turn them over in a pious heart.
He does not follow vain and doubtful little tales everywhere;
he teaches the clear streams of sacred eloquence.
Therefore, I pray, read lovingly the worthy praises
of venerable Anne, which the learned man has brought forth for you.
