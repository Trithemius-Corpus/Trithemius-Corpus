If you love and honor the venerable mother with all your heart, you will know by experience how much the heavenly Emperor defers to her. She has such power with God that whatever she has asked of him, she is able to obtain without delay. Just as the King of heaven is believed to honor his own mother above all, so after her he venerates mother Anne before all the saints. And very often Anne obtains for her faithful ones what the daughter denies: not because she does not know how to grant what is asked, but so that, deferring to her mother, she may seem to call us to veneration of her. Thus also the supreme Emperor is moved to mercy by the prayers of the saints: not because he begins to will what before he did not will, but so that, deferring to his elect, he might instruct us in honoring them. For God is not moved in the human manner, nor is he ever stirred to anger from disturbance, but, always steadfast, wills what he wills; when he shows mercy at the prayers of the saints, he does not change his counsel, but mitigates the sentence. Therefore, to obtain God’s mercy, since our merits are not sufficient, the intercessions of the saints must be implored by us, so that what we cannot do by our own merits, [unclear] by theirs. Yet the citizens of the heavenly fatherland, even if they are unlike in the quality of their merits, nevertheless, in coming to the aid of our wretched condition, according to the variety of those who supplicate, if devotion demands it, are believed to be concordant in act and will; because, when the faith of the petitioner aids the advocate, it renders the merit for obtaining doubled. This, moreover, we attain not by conjecture but by experience. But when two blind men approach in supplication to some heavenly patron’s merit, one is given sight, the other is left in his blindness. No contempt of the saint is believed to be present, but the distrust of the suppliant is charged. Without faith devotion merits nothing. I have set these things forth with sure consideration, so that if anyone should not feel most blessed Anne to be present to him with as much readiness as we promise, he should not accuse us of falsehood, but recognize his own culpable distrust. For the greatest confidence before God and the saints is constant faith in their aid. Moreover, no Christian doubts that those who are more excellent in merits can do more before God. Therefore the most blessed mother of God, ever-virgin Mary, just as she surpasses all the saints in merits and holiness, so in power and majesty, as queen of heaven, she is rightly believed to excel them all. At her right hand, so to speak, we trust that her most blessed mother stands, whose life we know was most holy. There was not in Israel another holier than she, who in the conception of the mother of God constructed for the Holy Spirit a vessel without stain. Finally, if Elizabeth, the mother of the Lord’s forerunner, is believed to have been filled with the Holy Spirit and ineffable joy, how great an exultation, joy, and grace must have been fitting for the most holy mother of the parent of God, one is compelled [to say].
