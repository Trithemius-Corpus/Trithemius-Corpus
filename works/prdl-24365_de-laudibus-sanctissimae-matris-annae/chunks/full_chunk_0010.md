Very ancient records of this matter are found in the said monastery. Do these things seem new to you? Cease, then, to disparage the feast of the most holy mother; cease to tear with impious tooth at her most worthy cult. Do not spread personal hatred against all; do not, because of displeasure with a few, be angry with everyone; do not carry an injury from a man up against the heavenly ones. Why, perhaps injured by a man, do you call the saints of God into blame? Cease, I beseech you, cease wishing to exact vengeance for one man or a few from the most holy mother Anne. I do not want you to revile her sanctity; I do not want you to deprive her of her honor; I do not want you to assail the devotion of her servants. If a man sins against a man, God will be able to appease him; but he who sins against the saints of God, against the parents and son of God, against the mother of the mother of God, who will pray for him? How can he hope to have him propitious, who has offended his saints, and indeed the most holy? For Saint Anne is worthy of every honor, she who deserved to bear the Mother of God without stain of guilt; whoever lessens her honor does injury to God. Therefore rather, if you are wise, if you understand, withdraw from this folly of yours, flee from this false madness, turn aside from this most wicked blasphemy, and undertake repentance for your former error, so that through tears of compunction you may wash away so unspeakable a fault. But if you do not do this, if you persevere in your obstinacy, you will not hinder the will of God, but you will have a share in the lot of the devil. God has resolved to honor Saint Anne in his Church; who will prevent him? Behold, kings and princes celebrate the memory of Saint Anne with religious devotion; behold, faithful peoples everywhere invoke her name, and you with the devil waste away, are confounded, and rave in your envy. Monks and religious of both sexes, the whole clergy of the churches of God, celebrate the feast and memory of the holy mother Anne with singular devotion; and you, envious dog, presume to bark against all. Why do you bark impiously? Why do you mutter wickedly? Be silent, go away, withdraw; not without grave chastisement will this impiety of yours be punished. For whoever disparages the honor and sanctity of the most blessed mother Anne, whoever blasphemes her worshippers, will not depart from this life without confusion. He will be confounded with evil confusion before God and men; reproaches and the worst ignominies will cover his head; he will be wretched and pitiable, and there will be no one to grieve over his calamity, all the days that he shall be upon the earth. *That Almighty God [unclear] Saint [unclear]*
