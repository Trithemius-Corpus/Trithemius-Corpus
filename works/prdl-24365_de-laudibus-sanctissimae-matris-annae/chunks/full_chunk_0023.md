For rightly is she interpreted as “grace,” since she was to give birth to the mother of grace. Rightly “grace,” through whose intercession the grace of God is obtained for the faithful. She is also interpreted as “giving” or “gift,” because those whom she finds devoted in her honor she makes rich with many gifts of grace. Rightly “gift,” through whom remission of sins is given. Whoever, therefore, is weighed down by the burden of sins, whoever is struck by the darts of temptations, let him flee trustingly to the aid of mother Anna; let him offer prayers and gifts of devotion, and there will be nothing that can prevail against him for evil. For Anna is called “grace”; according to her name she does not cease to obtain the grace requested, if she is found with devout faith. Therefore, faithful people, celebrate most devoutly the feasts of so great a mother; rejoice in her commemoration, and pour out diligent prayers to her, because she is able to free you from every tribulation. Rejoice in the Lord our God, all you who love Saint Anna. Praise the name of the Lord, all faithful peoples, who love the mother of the God-bearer and Virgin. Take Anna as your patroness, honor her with devout prayers, and you will always find her a faithful advocate.

*With what exercises and devotions we ought to honor the most blessed mother Anna. Chapter 13.*

We have heard how greatly the most blessed mother Anna, by her merits, has power with almighty God for those who pray; we know how worthy she is of honor. It remains now that we briefly say how she is to be honored by us. And although, because of the diversity of devotion, no general rule concerning the manner of veneration can be set down by us, nevertheless we shall try, as far as we can, to persuade everyone, so that no one may be able to excuse himself through ignorance from the praise of so great a mother. And indeed, first of all, devotion will establish for each person the manner of praising her; the greater that devotion is, the more useful the manner will be. Just as we are accustomed to venerate the other saints with diverse ceremonies, so also we honor her, but more abundantly. You have the manner: apply your desire. If perhaps I seem too brief to you, receive a fuller manner. If kings, princes, and bishops of churches honor blessed Anna, let them erect chapels and oratories in her memory, institute prebends, and found monasteries. Let them also make diligent prayers to her, celebrate her memory, and feed the poor and needy out of reverence for her. With whatever zeal the faithful venerate the other special saints, let them know that the divine mother Anna is to be honored not with less, but with more abundant zeal. Likewise, let the prelates of the faithful themselves have the same manner of veneration, and let them institute that the feast of the most holy mother Anna be celebrated by all in their dioceses.
