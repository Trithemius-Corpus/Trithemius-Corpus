*Letter of Lord Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, concerning the book on the praises of the most holy Anne.*

To the venerable and most religious father, Lord Rumold Laupach, prior of the Frankfurt convent of the Order of the Brothers of Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, Johannes of Trittenheim, abbot of the monastery of Saint Martin in Sponheim, of the Order of the divine Father Benedict: greeting and charity.

Desiring, excellent father, to satisfy our own wish and your requests, we have written, with sincere devotion, a book on the praises of the most holy mother Anne, which we dedicate to your name, since you seemed to us worthy before others, as being a very great worshipper and most devoted lover of the same venerable Anne. From the history, however, which is sufficiently widespread, we have taken nothing, or little, because it seemed to us less approved, and in very many places apocryphal. Yet we did not wish to refute it, lest we give simple people occasion for murmuring if, as they themselves think, we were to attack with new presumption what has been approved by antiquity. Therefore we left the history untouched, and those things that had been known by most evident faith, truth, and reason, by the gift of God, we have brought together for the honor of the holy mother Anne. We have also excluded the miracles of Saint Anne from this volume, lest we make our treatise suspect to learned men, who do not readily give credence to these things. Therefore, if it should happen that you hesitate about anything, write back: you will find us ready, who will demonstrate to you and to all men the reason for each of the things we have written. After the end of this work we have added certain poems on the praises of the saint, which were sent to us at Basel by learned men on the other side. From Sponheim, on the Kalends of July, in the year [unclear].
