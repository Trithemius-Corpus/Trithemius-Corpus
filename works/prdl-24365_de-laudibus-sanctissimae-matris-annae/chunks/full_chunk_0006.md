Let no one, therefore, more learned detract from me as I praise you, Anne; let no equal disparage me; let no inferior mock me. What is true prevails, and what proceeds from the true does not fail. Let no one think me contemptible on my own account as I write about this most holy woman. Let no one [unclear] my devotion. Let no one murmur against the praises of the holy mother Anne. I do not fear the barking of dogs; I do not fear the censures of the ignorant; I do not dread the torches of envy. For I write for the devout, not for the wicked; for the studious, not for the ignorant; for the humble, not for the proud. May the present work please those for whom it is written; may it profit those to whom it is offered; may it be of use to those to whom it is presented. What have I to do with those who are outside? What is it to me if they do not think rightly? What is it to me if they gnaw at holy things contrary to reason? I do not fear displeasing those whose censures and rash judgments I know.

*General exhortation to all the faithful.*

Hear me, you who love what is good and seek the Lord. I perform an embassy; I am led by devotion; I am driven by charity. The mother of the Mother of God has sent me, that I may proclaim her most holy merits. And now I come to you not with lofty speech, but as a simple man to the simple, as a devout man to the devout. If you are Christians, if you are good, if you love the grace of the Lord’s incarnation, why do you not love his ministers, that is, his parents? For they are those from whom the Son of God was born according to the flesh; if their way of life had not been most holy, it would by no means have been so acceptable to God. But when he willed his only-begotten Son to be born from them, he showed openly how holy and pleasing to himself they were. From these, one woman, most pleasing to God above all, chosen as the mother of the unique father, ever-virgin Mary, shone forth; she was borne without stain by the most blessed Anne, wife of Joachim, and was brought up by her most chastely in the fear of the Lord. The son of this most sacred Virgin is God, who redeemed us by his most holy passion. If you love the daughter, why do you not honor her mother? If there had been no mother, you would not have the daughter. To you, therefore, my discourse is now directed, you who dwell in the valley of tears. I address you, O sons of Adam, who are exiles from your homeland. I write to you who have been redeemed by the precious blood of the Son of God. I address you; I warn and exhort you that, if you love the Son of God, you should devoutly honor his kinsfolk according to the flesh. Among them, after his most holy mother, holy Anne is set before you as first of all to be honored; her holy merits are shown daily to the human race through many miracles. Take her as your patron; celebrate her memory more devoutly.
