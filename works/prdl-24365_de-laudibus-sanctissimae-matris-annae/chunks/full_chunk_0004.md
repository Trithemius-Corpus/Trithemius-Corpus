Whatever praise we bestow upon her veneration, let us firmly believe pertains to the honor of the highest King. For the honor of the most holy mother Anne, let all the faithful come together. Render praises to the most high Lord in memory of her; for reverence of her, rejoice with religious devotion. I, who have experienced many of her benefits, [say that] you will have her as a ready advocate in every tribulation, if you celebrate her memory with faithful devotion. Therefore she is to be honored by all, by whose merits the Son of God grants all things at her nod; whose honorable name the court of the heavenly citizens honors with the greatest reverence; whom the queen of heaven, the mother of God, observes as her sweetest parent. Whoever has chosen her as patroness, whoever has persevered devoutly in her service, he shall be saved both now and forever. For all the benefits that the Son of God is accustomed to grant to mortals through his beloved mother, he cannot deny to the merits of his grandmother; indeed, at times he bestows greater things through her, so that he may draw us, as if by gifts, to the memory of her, long neglected. Nor can the Son of God ever despise his own parents, who commanded us so earnestly to honor the parents of others.

*The author’s invocation to God and Saint Anne and all the heavenly ones, that they may bring the grace of writing. Chapter 2.*

God, true light, who illuminates every man coming into this world; God, who justifies the impious and calls sinners to repentance; God, who gives wisdom to little ones and makes eloquent the tongues of those who stammer: grant me, I beseech you, forgiveness for my sins; grant me wisdom for your praise; grant me light by which I may understand, know, and love you, and be so conquered by love for most blessed Anne that I may worthily embrace her praises in this work. God, who for the thirsty people in the desert brought forth water from the hardest rock, soften the hardness of my heart, so that I may be able to praise you in your saints, in the grandmother of your Son, in the parents of your only-begotten, with such devotion of mind that, through your mercy and her intercession, it may profit unto devotion not only for me but also for all who read this little night-work of ours. God, who opened the mouth of a beast so that it uttered human words against an unwise man, graciously water the dryness of my tongue by the infusion of your grace; strengthen my trembling hand with the Holy Spirit, so that concerning most holy Anne I may write nothing except what is right, persuade nothing except what is holy, and utter nothing except what is worthy. Grant me, I ask, almighty and eternal God, the aid of heavenly grace in this work, you who gave me the resolve to will it. For I am a worm and not a man, wretched and unhappy, sold under sin.
