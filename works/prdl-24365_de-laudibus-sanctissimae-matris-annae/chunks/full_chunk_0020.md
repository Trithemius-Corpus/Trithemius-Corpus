*That the blessed mother Anne is powerful before God to aid those who devoutly call upon her.*
*Chapter X.*

Since the holy and most blessed mother of the Mother of God is, in the kingdom of heaven, very near to the Son of God and to his mother in merits and charity, there is no doubt that she is able to obtain help for our miseries, provided that the devotion of our mind is earnestly directed toward her. She cannot fail to be most merciful, who bore the mother of mercy in her womb. How pious, how powerful, how ready she is to repel human miseries, there are almost as many witnesses as there are devout worshippers found for her. For she is powerful in the palace of the highest emperor, whom God hears as she supplicates, out of reverence for her, and whatever she has asked he grants without delay. Let him doubt her authority and power who does not know that she gave birth to the Queen of Angels. Let him hesitate over the piety of the most blessed mother who is ignorant that the Savior of the world came forth from her most noble blood.

She is powerful and very near to God; she is pious and inclined toward our necessities; she is accustomed to grant benefits to human beings, so that they may learn continually to hold her many merits in memory and honor. Therefore approach the pious patroness confidently; knock upon her with assiduous prayers, because she is able to obtain pardon for you; she is able to lead you to eternal life. Nothing is lacking to her that can profit our necessities; she has everything that can help us, since the capacity to be able and the piety to be willing are always present in her. Therefore, brothers, let us doubt nothing, let us hesitate at nothing, since sure intercessions will be at hand for us, if firm faith is not lacking. In whatever tribulation we shall cry out to her, we shall find ready help, if we devote faithful service to her name. Do you desire to know, brothers, what this most holy woman is able to provide for you? Listen. Anne drives away sadness, expels the lust of the flesh, relieves poverty, cures infirmities, mitigates adversities, calms oppositions, eliminates vices, plants virtues, enlightens the understanding, purifies affection, remits sins. Therefore, whoever you are who, in good pursuits, have fortune contrary to you; whoever you are who feel the adverse storm of this pilgrimage, call upon Saint Anne with devout and assiduous prayers, knock upon the mother with supplications, and you will find remedies of unexpected consolation. For she herself is a safe refuge for the wretched, a sure remedy for the sick, an unfailing solace for the desolate. Why do you hesitate to approach her, since she is both powerful and willing to help you?
