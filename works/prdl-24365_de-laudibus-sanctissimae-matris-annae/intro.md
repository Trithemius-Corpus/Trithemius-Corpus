*De Laudibus Sanctissimae Matris Annae* — "In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne" — is Trithemius's treatise honouring St Anne, mother of the Virgin Mary. He wrote it at Sponheim with a dedicatory letter to Rumold Laupach dated 1 July 1494; it circulated with commendatory poems on the Holy Kindred, the extended family of Christ that the late-medular devotion to Anne had made its centre. It is a characteristic piece of his early Marian-hagiographic output.

This is the **Leipzig edition, ca. 1497** (BSB scan) — a reprint of the 1494 Mainz first edition.
