*To Maximilian Caesar.*

...of [unclear] and of the faithful, the judgment of permission would rightly seem to be just. But now, more often, the good and innocent are weakened by the harmful arts of demons, and sometimes are even deprived of life, to pass over in silence the loss of temporal goods which those bewitched often suffer. It troubles many why the Almighty permits these things, he who permits nothing unjustly.

For this proposed question, I shall set forth in order, as briefly as I can, the necessary reasons, no other than those I have received from the ancient and Catholic doctors.

And the first reason is the testing of the faith of Christians and the experience of their integrity: that, scourged by the harmful arts of demons, they may learn, in the sufferings of the flesh, what faith and love toward God they possess in the hidden place of the mind. In prosperity it is not difficult to be long-suffering; but in adversity, to await help from the savior of the faithful with humble patience belongs not to the soft, but to the strong and truly faithful Christians. In his tribulation most holy Job learned how great a constancy of faith toward God he had, who before, in tranquillity, had preserved piety without the dart of temptation. Hence it must also be noted that those whom the harmful arts of demons or men have in any way made ill, who, in order to recover health, either approach or consult any sorcerers and enchanters, whether by themselves or through others, act against the precepts of God and of the Church, and have neither true faith in God nor love, seeking health not from Christ but from the devil and his minister. For they prefer to live healthy, but unhappily, in the name of the devil, rather than to suffer a little here for the love of Christ and afterward to rejoice with him forever.

*Johannes Trithemius, Eight Questions.*

But he who does not approach sorcerers on these grounds, because God has forbidden it, preferring to live, with observance of God's commandments, in poverty and sickness rather than against God in riches and bodily health, truly loves God and rightly preserves the integrity of the Christian faith. Among Christians, most sacred Caesar, men corrupt in mind and reprobate concerning the faith are not to be tolerated: those who cure the bewitched by harmful arts, who reveal thefts and adulteries by the ministry of demons, who foretell future things by inspection of stones or mirrors. Do not hear them, I pray, who, ignorant of divine law, would wish ministers of demons of this sort to exist under your command, because they know how to undo spells by spells; for such pernicious treatment of bodies brings about the death of souls, and makes men withdraw from God, the creator of all things, and adhere to impious apostate demons, with whom after death they will be damned forever.

Secondly, the reason why almighty God permits the innocent to be injured by witchcraft is the increase of merits, namely that their patience, proved in tribulation, may deserve a worthier crown of glory in heaven. For, as holy Augustine says: man is unknown to himself, as to what he can bear and what he cannot; sometimes he thought he could not bear what nevertheless he can. When temptation comes, as if some interrogator, a man is found by himself, who before, lying hidden, did not know himself. The divine chief apostle Peter, before temptation came upon him, was hidden both from himself and from others, how inconsiderate and weak he was; he received it, and the weakness that lay hidden appeared.
