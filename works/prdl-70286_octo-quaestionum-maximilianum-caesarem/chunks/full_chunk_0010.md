*To Maximilian Caesar.*

... rashly concedes that he can be saved. The omnipotence of God has regard to the order of things which he established; according to the inviolable sequence of its rectitude, no one will be saved without faith in Christ, no one who does not believe in the Son of God will be justified. Therefore they are moved by vain and most foolish piety who think that anyone in the whole world and in any sect whatever is to be saved outside the faith of Christ, since this is manifestly contrary to his sentence. Let them hear the Savior himself again saying in the Gospel: No one comes to the Father except through me; for I am the way, the truth, and the life. If no one comes to the Father except through the Son, certainly no one will be saved except through him. Who will save the Jew and the pagan, since no one can be saved except through Christ? But Christ will undoubtedly save only those who in heart, speech, and deed believe in him. He will not save the Jew, not the Saracen, not the pagan, not any other unbeliever, because they do not believe in him, but despise his teaching, scorn the faith, and, blaspheming his holy name, hold it cheap all day long. For he himself said in the Gospel: Whoever is ashamed of me and of my words, I also will be ashamed of him before my Father and his holy angels. Tell me now, you who worship Christ and contend that someone can be saved in his own sect, which he thinks true, outside the Christian faith: do you believe the Gospel of Christ to be true or false? If true, why do you think contrary to it? For Christ says: he who does not believe in the Son of God, the wrath of God remains upon him, and he has already been judged and will be condemned. And because no one comes to the Father except through me. But you, led astray by I know not what rashness, press the contrary, saying that a man outside the faith of Christ, trusting in his paternal religion, which he believes to be true and pleasing to God,

or living innocently according to the law of nature, and especially since perhaps he has never heard anything at all about the faith of Christ, is to be saved in his confidence. Christ did not say this, but: Whoever has believed in the only-begotten Son of God will be saved; but whoever has not believed will be condemned. He excepted no one, he adds no condition. Since he had come to suffer for all, he had provided for salvation through faith alone. If faith in the Savior is lacking, now no confidence will save the unbeliever. For the holy prelate Augustine said in *On Faith to Peter*: Hold most firmly, and by no means doubt, that not only all pagans, but also all Jews, heretics, and schismatics who end this present life outside the Catholic Church, will go into the eternal fire prepared for the Devil and his angels. You now perceive where foolish pity drags you, who, measuring God by human laws, so that in all things you may make his only-begotten Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, merciful, do not fear to judge him a liar. But Christ is truthful, who says that those not believing in him are to be damned. But we are rash and liars, who trust that anyone can be saved outside his faith. Whoever truly believes in Christ in no way permits anyone at all outside the unity of the Catholic Church of Christians to be said to be saved; whoever says the contrary shows that he is not a true and good Christian. For this is the immovable sentence of Catholic tradition, which the Lord Jesus himself defined in the holy Gospel: namely, that no one is to be saved outside his faith, which he himself established, taught, and confirmed with his own blood.
