The second kind of wicked men who wish to command demons is that of those whom we commonly call necromancers. They profess arts full of execrations, by which they promise that they can summon demons into a circle, or show them openly visible, enclosed in crystal or some other vessel. These most pernicious men, full of falsehood and superstitions, commit evils so great that no good man could think them up.

For, in order to entice demons into service to themselves, they generally use most shameful sacrifices and enter into an express pact with them. They circulate many and various little books, full of filth, impiety, and falsehood, and deceitfully inscribed with the names of ancient philosophers and wise men; by displaying these they deceive many unwary and curious persons, and draw them with themselves into the pit of demons. They promise things great and altogether incredible, and by the hearing of these they falsely hold men suspended in wonder.

They say that by rings, pendants, images, exorcisms, and conjurations they can constrain malignant demons so that they appear visibly in a circle or in crystal, or in some vessel, and answer distinctly to all questions asked.
