1268.

In this year, which was the fourth of Peter our abbot, William, who, as we said above, had in the dissension been elected against Peter as abbot of this monastery by one part of the convent, but had been rejected by Werner, archbishop of Mainz, as having fewer and weaker votes in the election, after he had long troubled the monastery and the abbot, was seized by that same archbishop of Mainz while running about here and there, and was committed to prison, not to come out until his death. And that very disturbance ceased, which he had carried on against Peter for three years, with certain of the brethren adhering to him, though secretly, because of fear of the bishop.

*William the pseudo-abbot is captured.*

1269.

In this year a grave discord arose between Bishop Berthold and the counts of Henneberg, Castell, and Hohenlohe; at length, coming together for battle near the town in Franconia which is called Kitzingen, they fought fiercely. Many on both sides were slain, wounded, and captured.

*The Bishop of Würzburg is at odds with certain counts.*

In the same year the proscribed fugitives from the city of Cologne, having made a hidden passage underground and wishing to occupy and seize Cologne, inflicted many evils on the citizens; and, fighting against one another, they mutually caused themselves irrecoverable losses.

*The Cologne sedition.*
