*Chronicle of Johann Trithemius* 31: he constructed [unclear] with magnificent workmanship; Hatto, the son of his sister, succeeded him, and afterward became archbishop of Mainz.

In the same year Rupert, archbishop of Trier, died. Henry succeeded him; he built the regular workshops and cloister of Saint Peter around the church, and ordered the rule of the canons to be observed there. This is the greater church of Trier, in which at this time, as also in the other ancient churches, the canons lived in common according to the rule prescribed for them, having one table, one purse, and one common dormitory, just as today we live among ourselves according to the norm set before us.

*DCCCCLVIII.*

In the sixth year of Abbot Siger, which was the year of the Lord 958 and of the reign of Otto the great emperor, crosses appeared on people’s garments, terrifying many and bringing them back to salutary repentance. The garments of some also appeared as if marked by leprosy and infected with spots.

Around these times also a monastery of our order was founded in Saxony, called Hildesleuen, and many convents of nuns under the rule of our holy father Benedict were built in various regions, especially in Saxony. In this year also Willa, formerly the wife of King Berengar, after he had died under the emperor’s custody at Bamberg, despised the world, entered a monastery, and lived religiously under the habit of holy religion, in the eighth year of Abbot Siger. Envoys of the Turks came to King Otto, asking of him some learned man who might instruct them in the way of truth; for them, at their request, he sent Adalbert, formerly a monk of the monastery of Corvey, a bishop, a learned and holy man, who scarcely escaped from their hands.
