*Johannes Trithemius of Spanheim, first abbot of Spanheim, then of St. James the Greater at Würzburg, a man most learned in his age: two distinguished chronicles of the second part.*

*I. Of the monastery of Hirsau, in the diocese of Speyer: comprising its foundation and progress from the year of Christ 830 down to the year 1370.*

*II. Of the monastery of Spanheim, in the diocese of Mainz: comprising its foundation and progress from the year of Christ 1143 down to the year 1526; or rather the memorable events carried out in the whole Church and Empire during those eight centuries.*

*Of these, the former is issued more correctly and fully through collation with the archetype written in the author’s own hand; the latter, now for the first time rescued from darkness, is brought to light in the best faith because of its outstanding usefulness.*

*There are added his familiar letters, written after his departure from the monastery of Spanheim to princes and distinguished men, full of the histories of that age.*

*From the library of the distinguished Marquard Freher, Palatine councillor.*

*With a very full index.*

*To the most reverend and most illustrious prince, Lord Johann Adam, Archbishop of Mainz, Archchancellor of the Holy Roman Empire throughout Germany, and Prince Elector, etc., my most gracious lord.*
