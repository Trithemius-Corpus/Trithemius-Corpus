*Chronicle of Trithemius*

In the same year also Hermann Beude, knight, father of our abbot Philip, obtained for the benefit of this monastery letters of indulgences from the twelve bishops who follow.

To all the sons of holy mother Church to whom the present letters shall come: We, by divine mercy Nicholas of Belcastro, Raphael of Archadia, Remigius of Pistoia, Richard of [Bisancientis], Peter of Valona, John of Potenza, Garcias of [Correnchensis], James of Sebaste, Peter of [Otthonensis], Arnold of [Surrensis], Augustine of [Salubriensis], Boniface of [Fodiensis], bishops, eternal greeting in the Lord. The splendor of the heavenly light, which illumines the world with its ineffable brightness, follows with kindly favor the pious vows of the faithful who hope in the clemency of his majesty, especially when the devout humility of [unclear] has deserved and is aided by the prayers of the saints.

Desiring therefore that the monastery at Sponheim, of the order of St. Benedict, in honor of St. Martin, and the chapel of St. Mary the Virgin, founded within the precinct of the said monastery, in the diocese of Mainz, be visited with due honors and be continually venerated by Christ’s faithful: to all who are truly contrite and confessed, who come to the said monastery or chapel on the individual feasts of their patrons, and on all the other feasts written below, namely Christmas, the Circumcision, Epiphany, Good Friday, Easter, Ascension, Trinity, Corpus Christi, the Finding and Exaltation of the Holy Cross, St. Michael the Archangel, on all feasts of the blessed Virgin Mary, the Nativity and Beheading of St. John the Baptist, the blessed Apostles Peter and Paul, and all the Apostles and Evangelists, on the feast of All Saints, on the commemoration of souls, and on the dedication of the said monastery or chapel, of Saints Stephen or Lawrence, Nicholas, George, of Saints Mary Magdalene, Margaret, Catherine, Elizabeth, Lucy, and Agatha, and throughout the octaves of all the aforesaid feasts that have octaves, on each Sunday and Saturday of the whole year, for the sake of devotion, prayer, or pilgrimage; or who shall be present there at masses, sermons, matins, vespers, and other divine offices and [unclear], and at the burials of the dead; or who shall celebrate there or have or procure celebration there; or who shall go around the cemetery of the said monastery or the precinct, praying for the dead; or who shall follow the Body of Christ or the holy oil when they are carried to the sick; or who at the evening ringing of the bell, on bended knees, shall say the Ave Maria three times; and also who shall extend helping hands to the fabric of the monastery or chapel itself, for lights, books, chalices, vestments, or any other necessary ornaments; or who shall give, bequeath, or procure to be given or bequeathed to the same church or altar gold or silver or any of their property in their wills; and who shall devoutly pray to God for the salutary state of the lord archbishop and for the state of lord Philip Meysz, now abbot of the said monastery at Sponheim, and of Hermann Beude, knight, and Hildegard his wife, and their sons and daughters, the petitioners for the same, while they live, and for the same when they have departed from this light; and who, for the soul of the reverend father and lord Wilhelm, abbot, predecessor of the aforesaid monastery at Sponheim, who is buried in the aforesaid chapel, shall say beside his tomb the Angelic prayer, the seven penitential psalms, or any other devout prayers, and shall piously beseech God: as often as they shall devoutly do the aforesaid things, or any of the aforesaid things, trusting in the mercy of almighty God and in the authority of his blessed Apostles Peter and Paul, each one of us remits forty days of indulgences from the penances enjoined upon them, provided that the will and consent of the diocesan bishop intervene for this. In witness whereof our seals are appended to the present letters. Given at Avignon on the twentieth day of May, in the year of the Lord 1358, in the sixth year of the pontificate of lord Pope Innocent VI.

These indulgences Gerlach, Archbishop of Mainz, diocesan of this monastery, confirmed by his authority, and added forty days to all the aforesaid matters, as likewise appears in his letters:

Gerlach, by the grace of God archbishop of the holy see of Mainz, archchancellor of the Holy Empire throughout Germany, to all Christ’s faithful to whom the present letters shall come, eternal greeting in the Lord. The indulgences of the venerable fathers, the bishops, to which this present charter of ours is attached, given and granted for the monastery at Sponheim of the order of St. Benedict, founded in honor of St. Martin, and for the chapel of St. Mary the Virgin situated within the precinct of the said monastery, of our diocese, we hold to have other equal graces, and we confirm them in the name of the Lord by these presents; and, trusting in the mercy of almighty God and in the merits of his blessed Apostles Peter and Paul and of St. Martin our patron, we add to them forty days of indulgence in like form, in God’s name, in these writings. Yet we do not wish, and under penalty of excommunication strictly forbid, that anyone should in any way carry or export letters of this kind of indulgences outside the monastery and chapel for which the collection is made. In witness whereof our seal is appended to the present letters. Given on the Ides of November, in the year of the Lord 1358.

The total, therefore, of these indulgences of the twelve bishops and of the aforesaid Archbishop of Mainz, for any of the aforesaid works and on any Sunday throughout the circuit of the year, or on any other day expressed in the letters, joined together, makes 520 days of remission from the penance enjoined for those who merit it.
