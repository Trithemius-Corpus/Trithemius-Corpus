They profess themselves imitators of the apostles: men without God, without heart, full of the Devil. They are, however, very cunning men, and in their sect wonderfully skilled in teaching, disputing, and defending, so much so that they cannot easily be overcome by anyone in disputation by arguments. No one is found of such dull understanding, provided he has the free use of reason, who, once he has professed this sect, does not immediately become, in defending it by arguments, most learned, as it seems to him. But lest anyone think the matter some kind of miracle, we shall state the cause of so great a change, so that all may understand that it is not God but a malignant spirit working in them. Whoever desires to be admitted by prudence into that secret society first enters a church while some Catholic priest is celebrating, and, standing in a corner or in some secret place, throughout the whole Mass speaks certain insulting words, which it is not fitting for us to express on account of the weak, against the sacraments under his breath, with the intention proposed to him by his teacher. When these have been completed after the end of Mass, a fly of large body flies to him, and, by the noise of its wings indicating that it is present, seeks entry into his mouth. But as soon as he opens his mouth, it enters him, and the man is filled with a diabolical spirit, and becomes learned and wonderfully cunning in that sect. Yet they themselves affirm that the evil fly is not a demon but the Holy Spirit, who, descending upon them, as upon true imitators of the apostles, just as once on the day of Pentecost upon those men, makes them learned and steadfast. And in truth they become not steadfast but obstinate, nor could they ever be brought back from their errors to the right way by any kinds of torments. We knew one convert who, deceived by the counsel of the heretics, did and said under Mass the things we have mentioned; but when he heard the noise of the fly circling his head, he became terrified and fell to the ground, and was scarcely able to drive away the fly so that it would not enter his mouth.
