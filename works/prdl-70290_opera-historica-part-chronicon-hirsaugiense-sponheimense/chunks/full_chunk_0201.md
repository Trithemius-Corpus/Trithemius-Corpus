Some of them received the word with reverence; others, however, did not, but spoke ill of the angel who was familiar to me, saying that he was a deceiving spirit and transformed into an angel of light. Therefore the lord abbot himself bound me by my obedience, ordering that, when he appeared to me again, I should adjure him by the name of the Lord to tell me whether he was a true angel of God or not. But I, judging this presumptuous, accepted this command with great fear. Therefore one day, when I was in my ecstasy, in the usual manner he presented himself to me and stood in my sight.
