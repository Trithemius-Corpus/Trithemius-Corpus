manifest toward me. With the Lord’s help I have completed the work of my Polygraphy, divided into six parts, or books, which is known to be dedicated to your majesty. I shall take care that it is presented to your sacred sight as soon as possible. May Almighty God long preserve your Highness, illustrious prince, unharmed and perpetually happy; to him I commend myself with humble supplication. From Würzburg, on the tenth day of the month of October, in the Christian year 1507.

*LXI. Johannes Trithemius, abbot of the monastery of St. James in the suburb of the city of Würzburg, sends greetings to Jacob Trithemius, his dearest brother.*
