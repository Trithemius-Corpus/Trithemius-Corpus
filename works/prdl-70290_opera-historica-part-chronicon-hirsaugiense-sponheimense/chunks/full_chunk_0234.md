*MCCXXXI.*

In the fifteenth year of Abbot Eberhard, which was the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1231. *Ludovicus, duke of Bavaria, is killed.* Ludovicus, duke of Bavaria, returning from overseas regions, when he had come to Kelheim, was stabbed through with a knife and killed in the sight of his household by a certain messenger of the Old Man of the Mountains, sent for this purpose; Frederick the emperor, as was reported, having procured this crime. For the aforesaid Old Man of the Mountains, prince of the Saracens, allied with the emperor, wishing to avenge the many injuries which the same duke had inflicted on the emperor, sent his servant to kill him. *Concerning Ernest, twenty-third abbot of this monastery, who presided for 13 years, one month, and three days, and concerning the various deeds of that time.*

After the death of the most reverend and God-beloved abbot Eberhard, the brothers of this monastery, assembling together on the last day of the month of December, which was the second day after the death of their shepherd, began to have discussion among themselves about the necessary substitution of a new abbot. Therefore, after the customary prayers had first been said, at length they unanimously elected Ernest, a good man, born of respectable parents in Swabia, who, ordained abbot by Berengar, bishop of Speyer, presided over this monastery for 13 years, one month, and three days. *The life and character of Abbot Ernest.* And he was, as we have said, a good man of honorable conduct, yet more inclined to temporal affairs than to the observance of monastic discipline. Hence it came about that the integrity of discipline gradually failed under him, since he cared less for the inner life of the brothers, but rather for external things...
