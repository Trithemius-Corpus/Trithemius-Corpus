But very many other monasteries also, under the title of mutual confederation, were received by the holy abbot Wilhelm into the same confraternity of the monks of Hirsau; excessive antiquity of time has not allowed their names to come down to our memory. Canons also of secular churches, and likewise of cathedrals, established in various provinces, and many noble princes and powerful bishops in the world, having heard of the most holy way of life of Abbot Wilhelm and of the monks of Hirsau, commending themselves most devoutly to their prayers, prayed the more urgently to be received into their confraternity. For such great good things were spoken everywhere, in Wilhelm's time, by all who loved God, about that most holy congregation, that all who had been admitted into their confraternity believed themselves blessed. Nor among many at that time was this famous remembrance of the monasteries, amassed [unclear], idle; for the souls of the dead [unclear] many noble [unclear] and powerful bishops in the world, having heard of the most holy way of life of Abbot Wilhelm and of the monks of Hirsau, commending themselves most devoutly to their prayers, prayed the more urgently to be received into their [unclear]. For these agreements stipulated that whenever anyone deceased from a monastery received under the aforesaid confraternity had been announced in the monastery of Hirsau, the appointed prayers, vigils, masses, disciplines, and alms should immediately be made for him.

Therefore, when the most holy abbot Wilhelm had happily died, the abbacy was vacant for twenty-seven days because of the absence of Gebhard, the future abbot, then indeed prior, whom, as we have said, had been sent to Cluny for the relics of St. Peter the Apostle.

*Concerning Gebhard, thirteenth abbot of this monastery, who presided for fourteen years and three months; concerning the deeds of his time.*

After the most reverend and most holy abbot had happily entered the way of all flesh, and after the abbacy of this monastery had been vacant for 27 days, the brothers came together as one for the election of a new shepherd, on the day which is called the feast of Peter in Chains, when, as we have said before, the hairs of the same Apostle, in the presence of many abbots, monks, and faithful, had been brought into the monastery [unclear].

The abbacy was vacant for 27 days.
