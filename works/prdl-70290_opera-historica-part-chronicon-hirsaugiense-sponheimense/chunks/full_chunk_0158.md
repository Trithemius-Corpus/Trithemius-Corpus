Many bishops were present: Saint Otto, bishop of Bamberg; Adelbert, archbishop of Mainz; Buggo of Worms; Sifridius of Speyer; Embricho of Wurzburg; the bishops of Augsburg, Eichstatt, and Hildesheim; and many others. To this council also Wolmarinus, abbot of this monastery of Hirsau, was summoned by the archbishop of Mainz,

In the same year died Frederick, archbishop of Cologne, founder of the monastery of nuns of our order on Rolandswerth, a man magnificent in all things; through his munificence and authority that most reverend abbot of Deutz, the great Rupert, composed almost countless works. He died in his own castle, which he himself had newly built on the highest summit of a mountain, and therefore called Wolenberg. He was buried in the monastery of Siegburg of our order, in the chapter-place. In the archbishopric Bruno succeeded him, dean of Saint Gereon, provost, son of Count Adolf of Althene, ordained by the judgment of Emperor Lothar, of the princes, and of three cardinals, or legates of the Apostolic See, then present at Cologne, against the will of many.
