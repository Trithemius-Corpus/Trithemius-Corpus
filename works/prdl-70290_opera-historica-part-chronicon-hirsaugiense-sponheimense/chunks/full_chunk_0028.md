In this holy synod the bishops sitting with the abbots of the monasteries enacted many things for ecclesiastical advantage, and especially against certain laymen who were striving to depress and diminish episcopal authority. On these matters, therefore, and on many others, they established decrees, which they strengthened by common subscription, as is the custom.

In the ninth year of Abbot Haderad, Regino, abbot of Prum, as he writes in his chronicle, was deposed. He did not, however, set forth the causes of his deposition, but says thus: In the year of the Lord 899, Richar was appointed abbot of the monastery of Prum; but how it was done toward me I have therefore deferred noting in this place, lest perhaps, provoked by injuries, I should [unclear]. Dividing day and night into three parts, he spent the first eight hours in reading, writing, meditating, and praying, another eight in arranging the affairs of the kingdom, and the remaining eight in care of his body, by a most prudent distribution.
