*Chronicle of Jo. Trithemius. 201*

In the same year, after the apostolic see had been vacant for ten months, the cardinals at last assembled at Perugia and elected as pope Jerome, cardinal priest of St. Sabina, of the Order of Friars Minor. He was called Nicholas IV and presided for 4 years, 1 month, and 8 days, a good man, a lover of religion and peace.

*Nicholas IV is elected pope.*

*1289.*

In the 7th year of Abbot Crafto, and in the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1289, King Rudolph again led an army into Burgundy; and first, besieging the city of Payerne, he took it. Afterwards he captured the count of Burgundy, a rebel and perjurer, and put him in chains. He likewise captured the count of Savoy and the count of Montbeliard, and compelled them to swear to his dominion.

*The king again into Burgundy.*

*Henry, duke of Bavaria, dies.*

In the 8th year of Abbot Crafto, which was the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1290, Henry, duke of Bavaria, son of Otto I from the dukes of Bavaria, count palatine, brother of Ludwig, of whom mention was made above, died. He had as wife Elisabeth, daughter of King Bela of Hungary; she bore him Otto, king of Hungary, and Ludwig and Stephen, dukes of Bavaria. These two, after their father’s death, held the lordship in Landshut.

*Peter, abbot of Sponheim, died.*

*1291.*

In the same year, on the sixth day before the Nones of May, Peter, the eighth abbot of my monastery of Sponheim, died. John II succeeded him and presided for 7 years and 11 months; with every zeal he took care to adorn the church, and in his first year he also had it dedicated anew, together with the altars. In these times, while Siegfried, archbishop of the church of Cologne, was for seven years, as we have said, captive under the custody of the count of Berg, Walram, count of Julich, raging with sword and fire through the lands of the church of Cologne, inflicted many harmful losses on the poor. Among other things he took a very strong castle of the church called Zulpich and utterly destroyed it. Stirred by a similar madness, Eberhard, count of Mark, entered Westphalia with a powerful army of his men, and devastated by burning and plunder the duchy of Angria belonging to the lordship of that archbishop, and subjected to himself many fortifications of the land, castles, and towns.

*Assembly of the princes.*

*King Rudolph dies at Speyer.*

*1291.*

In the 9th year of Abbot Crafto, and in the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1291, Rudolph, king of the Romans, held a solemn assembly of the princes at Frankfurt, where with many entreaties, means, and methods he tried to induce the princes to elect his son Albert, duke [of Austria], as king. Yet he could not obtain this, although after the death of Adolf of Nassau that same son was at last made king, as we shall say in its place. Rudolph therefore went with his friends to Strasbourg for the sake of refreshing his spirit, and fell into an illness that could not be cured. Therefore he had himself quickly brought to Speyer, where his predecessors, kings and emperors, had been buried. When he had arrived there, he soon died in the aforesaid year, on the octave of the Division of the Apostles, and was buried there in the greater church; and the empire was vacant for almost one year, because of the discord of the princes.

*1292.*

In the 10th year of Abbot Crafto, in the year of the Lord’s Nativity 1292, at the beginning of the month of May the princes assembled at Frankfurt, after the empire had been vacant for almost a year, in order to elect a king of the Romans. After many counsels and negotiations had been held among them, they [unclear] Adolf [unclear], who, having received the kingdom, ruled for 6 years. Against him Albert, duke of Austria, son of the late King Rudolph, exerted himself with the aid of certain princes, saying that the empire belonged to him by paternal right, and that no one else should be elected so long as someone fit for the kingdom could be found from the royal lineage. Therefore, on all the days that Adolf lived, Albert regarded him as an enemy, until at last, deserted by the princes, he was killed by him in the field of Worms.

*Adolf, count of Nassau, becomes king for 8 years.*
