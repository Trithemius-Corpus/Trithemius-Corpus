Now this Godfrey was count in Bouillon and duke in Lotharingia, a brave and vigorous man, who, about to undertake this arduous pilgrimage for love of the heavenly kingdom, sold his county of Bouillon, with all its revenues and appurtenances, to the bishop of Liege for thirteen hundred marks of purest silver, which he gladly spent on that same expedition.

For around the Nones of October a comet appeared, standing toward the southern quarter, extending its mild brightness obliquely like a sword. Another star also was seen in the east on the sixth Kalends of March; it changed its place over a long interval with frequent leaps.

Many writers of that time testify that they saw blood-red clouds rising both from the west and from the east, meeting one another on their way in the middle of the sky; and, at about midnight, fiery [unclear] often rising from the north; and also torches flying through the air.

Provoked by these words, not only peasants and persons of no rank, but also kings, dukes, princes, bishops, abbots, monks, priests, laymen, nobles, the wealthy, the poor, men, women, virgins, and nuns, a nearly innumerable multitude, after selling in part their possessions, or even [unclear], with the cloisters wholly neglected, as if driven by some divine frenzy, all resolved with one mind and on one road to go to Jerusalem, to rescue the holy sepulcher of the Lord, together with the whole surrounding region, from the hands of the infidels and restore it to the Christian world. I marvel at that wondrous spirit which impelled so many thousands of people to so extraordinary an undertaking, especially all the women. The women, virgins, and nuns without number who went out on this expedition were dressed in male clothing and walked armed among the men.

Gathering together as one, they took up the proposed journey; and when they had come as far as Pannonia, a great part of them was killed, and not undeservedly. For as the men marched with women and nuns, priests and monks practiced lust in the army, defiling one another with unclean fornication, and provoked against themselves the wrath of the just judge, Jesus Christ.
