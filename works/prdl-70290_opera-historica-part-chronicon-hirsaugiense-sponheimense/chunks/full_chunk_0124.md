*Chronicle of Johannes Trithemius.*

... in the book. And so that each year you pay one gold piece to the Apostolic See as census, and that it be subject to no other mortal in any earthly matter. And now what remains to be added and completed by us? Chiefly concerning the appointment of an abbot, and concerning the advocate or defender of the place.

Therefore, whenever it shall happen that an abbot is to be ordained, we do not wish, indeed we wholly forbid, any person to be present at his election except the monks, only those who have professed obedience to the title of the monastery. Let the man whom they have chosen receive the pastoral staff from one of the senior brethren, so that no ecclesiastical or secular authority be sought for this. After he has received investiture of the office, let him be sent on behalf of the congregation to whichever of the bishops seems most suitable, who for charity’s sake will come, as is customary, to bless him as abbot. Concerning the other brethren to be ordained to any order of this kind, as to the priesthood or the diaconate, let what is more opportune be done: either let them be ordained in the monastery itself, or let them be directed to an absent bishop making ordinations elsewhere. For this reason we most earnestly beseech all our fellow-bishops, especially all those in Germany together, that they not disdain to favor and consent to this our decree.

And if it was not unworthy in the very text of his ordination to profess obedience to the Apostolic See of the Roman Church, whoever shall have dared to cavil at or contradict our command, as though it were known to be unlawful for anyone to profess obedience, we admonish and ask them, or, if they prefer this, by the authority of the Apostolic See we command them, that in whatever we have ordained concerning this, so far as lies in them, they not in any way show themselves disobedient. If any of them has been asked to come for the blessing of the abbot, let him not fail to come, if he can, unless prevented beforehand by ill health or by some other necessity of this sort.
