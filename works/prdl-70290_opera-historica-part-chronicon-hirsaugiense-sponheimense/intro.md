*Opera Historica*, Part II — the *Chronicon Hirsaugiense* and *Chronicon Sponheimense* — is the second half of Trithemius's collected historical works: his two great monastic chronicles. The *Chronicon Hirsaugiense* narrates the foundation and progress of the monastery of Hirsau, in the diocese of Speyer, down to 1520; the *Chronicon Sponheimense* does the same for his own former house of Sponheim, in the diocese of Mainz. Together they are the fullest record of the Benedictine reform world he inhabited — though, like his Frankish history, they must be read with caution for the fabricated sources woven into the genuine documentary labour.
