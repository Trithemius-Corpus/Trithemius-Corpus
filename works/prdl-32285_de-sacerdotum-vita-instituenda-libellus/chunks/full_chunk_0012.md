Or what benefit will a priest be able to bring to his hearers in preaching, if he does not know the Scriptures? Woe now to our times, in which, as is the people, so is the priest. Our priests cast aside the study of the Scriptures, neglect learning, and in place of books of Scripture they rear birds and dogs. Let the bishops see to it, who promote such idiots and ignorant men to the dignity of the priesthood, who entrust the care of Christ’s sheep to unskilled shepherds. Withdraw from the company of those who cast aside knowledge, who neglect the reading of the Scriptures, who despise study. They savor nothing but the worldly, love nothing but vanity, understand nothing unless it is carnal. They will quickly overthrow you if you often pass into their gathering.

They sit in ambush, with drinkers in taverns; they devote themselves to games and feasts. There is no fear of God before their eyes. They grow angry if anyone begins to speak of the Scriptures in their presence; they disdain to hear a reading, but turn instead to tales. What have you to do with them? Are not priests asses in their manner of life? They understand absolutely nothing of the Scriptures; they despise learning. They do not know how to write or speak the Roman language; they have scarcely learned to explain the Gospels in the vernacular. How many errors, tales, and heresies they proclaim to the people in the Church by preaching, who could believe unless he had experienced it? In place of books they get children for themselves; in place of study they love concubines. These are the blind and leaders of the blind, who do not instruct the people of God in righteousness, but rather lead them astray. O how many poor Christians live in the countryside who, because of the ignorance of their priests, do not know what befits a Christian.

Amid these things there is no one who takes notice, no one who grieves over the danger to souls, no one who applies a remedy. All seek the things that are earthly, all seek their own interests, all run after profits. But what are these matters to us? Each one stands or falls before his own Lord. And although it does not belong to me to correct these things, nevertheless I cannot help grieving, seeing every evil in the Church proceed from the ignorance of priests. For who would not grieve that those who have been appointed to instruct others do not know the commandments? See, no knowledge is sought in the ordaining of priests; those who are not called are allowed to approach; the life of the man to be ordained is never examined, as it ought first of all to have been. For this reason the Christian people is led away captive with the cords of sins; the harmony of brotherly love is violated. Our priests overflow with worldly desires; they do not dare to correct the vicious, because they know that they themselves have sinned in greater matters. For there are few who now shine in the Church by holy morals, few who duly instruct the flock entrusted to them by word and example. Nor is it strange if lesser men are free from the study of the Scriptures, when among the prelates you find few who adhere to the examples of the ancients. For very often those who are not more learned are chosen. They have either no codices of Scripture or few, since they pursue knowledge with hatred. Nothing so diminishes ecclesiastical dignity and renders it contemptible as the ignorance of prelates. When once bishops, abbots, and the other prelate priests turned their minds to the Scriptures, access was not so easy for crimes and sins.

For love and knowledge of the Scriptures teach one to despise the world, to flee vices, and to love the most holy virtues. As long as the Israelite people remained in the law of the Lord, no power of the enemy prevailed against them. But after it cast aside knowledge of the law, it was subjected to many adversities. For Scripture says: Because they did not have wisdom, they perished through their own folly. Today also priests and rectors of churches are in great number, but few of them attend to knowledge of the law and Scripture. And because, as the Lord speaks through the Prophet, the priests have cast aside knowledge of the law, for that reason they have been made contemptible among the people. They do indeed cry to the Lord in tribulation, but he does not hear them, because whoever turns his ears away so as not to hear the law, his prayer will be abominable.
