Therefore, as soon as the lust of the flesh begins to attack the spirit, let the cultivator of chastity lift his hands to heaven and say: Lord Jesus Christ, lover of chaste counsel, you know that my strength to resist is slight; help my infirmity, through your bitter passion. I believe in the Lord that, if he perseveres in prayer with devotion, he will easily obtain victory against the flesh.

But I wish you, most beloved brother, to avoid not only shameful deeds but also shameful words. For evil conversations corrupt good morals. Nor should you wish to imitate those who, giving themselves to filthy talk, think themselves chaste in heart. For it is an indication of the abundance of the heart. What each person is like hidden within himself, his speech when uttered proves.

There are some, and indeed many priests, who by their wanton conduct violate the purity of their consciences, although they do not bodily proceed to the act of lust. They sit with women, speaking shameful things; they mix itching touches and kisses; amid these things they boast that they are chaste. And where remains the saying of the Lord: Whoever has looked at a woman to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart? If it is not lawful for a chaste man to look at a woman in order to desire her, how much less to touch her immodestly, to kiss her, to handle her with his hands.

For the Apostle says: Abstain from every appearance of evil, as befits saints. Does it not seem to you an appearance of evil for men who are chaste to sit among others and to be maddened by the kisses and touches of young women? I would be lying if I had not seen priests, prelates of higher rank, spend the whole day on ships in obscene touches and kisses of women, and have no reverence for their dignity. It is an indication of inward lust when someone is accustomed to speak shameful words and to join touches to them. Amid these things, will chastity of mind remain uninjured?

But you, dearest Nicolaus, if you desire to show yourself a minister worthy of God, if you wish to escape the most harsh yoke of lust, abstain not only from shameful deeds but also from shameful words and from the touch of women. It is impossible for the mind not to be wounded when, acting wantonly with kiss and hand, you touch a woman. Remember your last things, the wise man warns, and you will not sin forever. For nothing restrains so much as the intent remembrance of death.

*On Reading and the Study of the Scriptures. Chapter Four.*

I know and have experienced that the most effective remedy against all temptations is study and the reading of the Scriptures. For blessed Jerome says: Love knowledge of the Scriptures, and you will not love the vices of the flesh. For Scripture alone is what promises us a heavenly remedy in tribulation. What will we be able to know or believe about the heavenly homeland if we do not have the Scriptures? For the Apostle says: All Scripture is divinely inspired, so that through patience and the consolation of the Scriptures we may have hope.

Therefore I advise you to take up the study of the holy Scriptures as a solace in this pilgrimage, since if you are assiduous in reading them, you will easily despise the world with all its vanities. For nothing so raises the mind from vain solicitude to heavenly things, nothing so calms the fires of the flesh, as diligence in sacred reading. Finally, study is especially fitting to your office, as Malachi says: The lips of the priest keep knowledge, and they will seek the law from his mouth, because he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts. For, as we said a little before, it is necessary for the priest of Christ to be learned in the divine law, both that he may know and that he may have a source from which to bring forth his sermon. For he who has undertaken to rule souls, for whom he is to render account to God the supreme judge, must necessarily read and understand many things.
