We read that Saint Augustine fled women to such an extent that he would not allow even his own sister to enter his episcopal house. We ourselves have also known many priests who for many years have innocently governed both the household and the people committed to them without the service of a female servant. For it will not be difficult for you to imitate what you see others have repeatedly done. Therefore take care not to come near even the company of young women, since it is difficult to live chastely even among old women. Remember with whom you speak at the altar; and do not pour out a mouth consecrated to divine ministries in kisses of prostitutes. It is most disgraceful, with hands consecrated by the oil of holy anointing, by which the Son of God is held and sacrificed, to touch a woman with polluted contacts. Do not give a stain to your glory, nor make your name a reproach to all. All people will tear at you fiercely in public, and country ploughmen and vine-dressers will tear at you daily in the field, if you, being yourself a young man, presume to live with women, especially younger ones. You will become a proverb and a laughingstock to all the people if you have frequently entered the dwellings of young women. Against your invisible enemy, which always flies round your side laying snares, keep watch at all times; and, according to Naso, “resist the beginnings of evils,” when the temptations are still weaker. For, as we see, after a cleric has once given his body over to the embraces of prostitutes, he rarely or never returns to perfect amendment. For all who are troubled by perverse love, the longer they have lain in the filth of lust, the more difficultly they return to health of mind. A vice strongly grown old fixes its roots in the human heart. O how many priests there are today in the Church of God who, sunk in the depth of lust, despise the long-suffering of God, as it is written: “When the impious man has come into the depths of evils, he despises.” There are without doubt many among the clergy defiled by lust who, although at the beginning of their promotion they resolved to live chastely, deceived through negligent custody of the senses, now without any fear of God, as though lawfully, from habit mingle with prostitutes.
