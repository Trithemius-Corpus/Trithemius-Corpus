Nothing diminishes ecclesiastical dignity so much, and renders it contemptible, as the ignorance of prelates. When formerly bishops, abbots, and the other prelates and priests devoted their minds to the Scriptures, an entrance did not lie so easily open to crimes and sins. For love and knowledge of the Scriptures teach one to despise the world, to flee vices, and to love the most sacred virtues. So long as the Israelite people remained in the law of the Lord, no power of the enemy prevailed against them. But after it cast away knowledge of the law, it was subjected to many adversities. For Scripture says: “Because they had no wisdom, they perished on account of their own folly.” Today also priests and rulers of churches are very numerous, but few of them attend to knowledge of the law and the Scriptures. And because, as the Lord speaks through the prophet, the priests have cast away knowledge of the law, therefore they have been made contemptible among the people. He does indeed cry to the Lord in tribulation, but he does not hear them, because whoever turns his ears away so as not to hear the law, his prayer will be execrable. Therefore let priests not wonder that laymen despise them, since they themselves spurn the commandments of Christ. But I greatly fear that more grievous things will soon happen against the clergy. But enough of these matters. You, however, should consider not what others are doing, but what you yourself must do. For many have been called to the priesthood in the church of God, but few are chosen. Therefore we must take the greatest care that we who are enrolled in the lot of the Lord may also be chosen. What profit is it to exercise the priesthood here for the briefest time, if it should happen that one is separated from the sight of God? Nothing, therefore, is better than to fix one’s desire on heavenly things, to be diligent in the study of sacred reading, to despise present things for the love of God, and to think on the things to come. Hence Peter says in the Aurora: “Nothing is better for a man than, by reading divine things, to fix his mind there whence life comes to him.” Accordingly continual study must be devoted to reading the Scriptures, because reading nourishes the mind and supplies sustenance to the virtues. Therefore, so that you may not lose the price of time, read the sacred Scriptures; write books; persist in devout prayers; at the proper times also labor with your hands.
