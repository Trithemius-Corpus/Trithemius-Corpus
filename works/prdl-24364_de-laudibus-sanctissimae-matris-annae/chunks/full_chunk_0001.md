*Quatrain of Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, on the most holy mother Anne.*

Venerable Anne, mother of the bountiful Virgin,
Surpassing in merits, and powerful in loving-kindness:
No one goes away sorrowful who honors and venerates you;
For you bring to all the gentle aid of salvation.

*Distich by the same, which he placed by the image of Saint Anne.*

Whoever in adversity has called upon Saint Anne
With devout prayers, he will be well protected.

*Quatrain of Conrad Celtis Protucius, poet laureate, on Saint Anne.*

Anne has never dismissed anyone’s prayers empty,
But in her purity has always brought the desired aid.
Therefore let us heap much honor upon the loving mother,
Who allows no one’s prayers to be in vain.

*Quatrain of Theodoric Gresemund the Younger of Mainz, to Saint Anne.*

Anne, parent of the Virgin Mary, hail, propitious,
Worthy to begin the work of our salvation.
Human redemption, which was begun through you, I beg you
To continue, covering our sins with your prayers.

*Distich of Rudolf von Langen, canon of Münster, on Saint Anne.*

Great Anne, parent of the fruitful Virgin, hear
The pregnant women who bring vows to you with the womb.

*Quatrain of Jodocus Badius of Ghent on Saint Anne.*

Anne, glory of the world, mother of the mother of Jesus,
May your grandson grant the stars for the merits of your daughter.
Holy Anne, bearing the first-fruits of our salvation,
Help us with your offspring and with your grandson.

*Rodolphus Agricola, in his poem on Saint Anne, among other things, thus.*

Anne, parent, venerable mother of the highest parent,
You who first open the way of salvation to the peoples:
All diseases flee you, pallor and pain,
And all harmful things that lodge in mind and body.
Whatever it is among men that wearies cares and hearts
Flees you, as clouds, driven on, are wont to flee the winds.

*Rutgerus [Venrai?], canon regular of [unclear], to the image of Saint Anne.*

Here pour forth devout prayers with knee bent,
Where Anne the parent sits with her blessed offspring.
Do not doubt: you will obtain whatever you have asked of her;
For Anne shines ever powerful in prayers.

*Distich by the same on Saint Anne.*

Anne, parent of the mother of Jesus Christ, hail;
We ask: drive every sin away from us.

*Rosary of Jodocus Beysselius, patrician of Aachen, on Saint Anne, to the reverend lord Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the order of the divine father Benedict.*

Whoever wishes, out of devotion, to read this rosary in honor of the most blessed mother Anne should first say the Lord’s Prayer with the salutation of Saint Anne, which he should always repeat after two verses; this salutation is as follows. Hail, venerable Anne, mother of the mother of God, most pleasing to the Holy Trinity, honored above all women, blessed are you by the Lord, and blessed is the offspring of your womb, and Jesus Christ, the virgin-born son of your offspring. Our Father. Hail, mother of God. Amen.

Anne, may I, wretched as I am, be permitted to utter your praises;
For, if you allow it, I shall bring you garlands and roses. Hail.

Anne, you shine as the venerable offspring of the kings of Judah,
And also renowned through Joachim, your pious husband. Hail.

Anne, for a long time you wept over sterile loves in wedlock,
The singular glory of marriages, and their one honor. Hail.

Anne, you seek a pious child, and not for empty uses. Hail.
But that, with holy posterity, it may worship God. Hail.

Anne, the swift messenger, standing by you from the heavenly court,
Says, Rejoice; behold, as mother you will have offspring, you will exult. Hail.

Anne, you will bear offspring blessed with such great virtue
That, as a virgin, she may shine with a mother’s honor. Hail.

Anne, you will bear a daughter whose chaste womb
God himself, whom the world does not contain, may willingly enter. Hail.

Anne, at last from your blood will arise the blood
Which, poured out, may bring hope always to wretched sinners. Hail.

Anne, so great an expectation befitted your birth-giving;
It is no small thing to have the mother of the mother of God. Hail.

Anne, the praises of your offspring will fly above the stars;
All ages too will proclaim you blessed.
Our Father. Hail, mother of God.

Anne, you are believed to have conceived Mary pure from evil,
And soon to have had her holy for God.

Anne, in your womb you carry her whom figures marked out
As coming, whom the holy fathers sang.

Anne, you bear an enclosed paradise, blessed with the tree
Of life, and sacred with all the honor of the countryside.
Hail. 2
