*Liber Octo Quaestionum ad Maximilianum Caesarem* — "A Book of Eight Questions to Emperor Maximilian" — is Trithemius's reply to eight questions the Emperor set before him: on faith and understanding, on the faith necessary for salvation, on the miracles worked by unbelievers, on witches and malefic magic, and on related theological points. Composed in 1508 and among the most widely read of his shorter treatises, it became his central statement on witchcraft and was drawn on heavily by later demonological writers. The eight questions are set out in an enumerated table at the head of the book, and Trithemius answers each in turn with his characteristic mixture of scholastic authority and pastoral caution.
