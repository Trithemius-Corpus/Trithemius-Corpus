And although the pain of my heart afflicted me beyond measure, nevertheless I willingly endured all things for love of you, so that I might rescue you from the power of the ancient enemy and join you forever to myself as a bride. See, my dearest one, at what a price I redeemed you, I who did not hesitate to die for you by a most shameful death. What, O sweet beloved, do you give back to your lover, and what do you repay? He does not ask for gold and silver; he does not care for precious stones. In place of all these things he requires faith and charity from you; he desires the dwelling-place of your love. Therefore give your savior the habitation of your heart, he who opened heaven for you in his most sacred blood. Open to him the chaste chamber of your mind, so that he may grant you the secret of his sweetest love. For the Lord Jesus goes nowhere empty, whom the ineffable gifts of his grace accompany. And although the little gifts that Jesus, king of heaven, brings with him are many and various, nevertheless they are all very precious; among them one is most precious, to be purchased from him only with a human heart. What is that? It is he himself, who said in the holy Gospel: I came to cast fire upon the earth, and what do I wish except that it burn? No one understands the greatest love who has not first received it in secret. For indeed Jesus, beautiful in form beyond the sons of men, is always present to those who love him; he meets those who meditate intently on his passion; he loves the simple and upright in spirit; he does not admit curious lovers of the world into the sweetness of his love. Therefore I would deservedly call those blessed who, having despised the affairs and cares of the world, desire to lie hidden in monasteries with themselves in the tranquility of a pure and simple soul; who love absolutely nothing in this world except God alone; who despise empty honors and the deceptive prosperity of the world; who, rooted in divine love, love nothing in this life except God himself, fear nothing. For the one who loves, God is sufficient for all things.
