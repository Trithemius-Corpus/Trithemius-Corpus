*De Operatione Divini Amoris* — "On the Working of Divine Love" — is one of Trithemius's devotional-contemplative works, treating the action of divine love in the soul. It belongs with the inward, affective side of his output rather than the polemical reform tracts or the cryptographic books, and stands near his other contemplative pieces in the corpus — *De Vera Conversione Mentis ad Deum* and *De Vanitate et Miseria Humanae Vitae*. Where the reform writings address the discipline of the cloister, this one turns to the inner motion of charity that the discipline is meant to serve.
