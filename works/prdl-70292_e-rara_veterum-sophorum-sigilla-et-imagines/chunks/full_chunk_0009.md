*Treatise IV*

*The Images, or Seals, of Thetel*

Thetel, a very ancient doctor, treating of carvings on stones, says that these carvings, when found on suitable stones, possess very great power.

The image of a man carved on jasper, holding a shield in his left hand and in his right an idol or something warlike, with vipers in place of feet and, in place of a human head, the head of a cock or a lion, will give power against enemies and renders one victorious; it is effective against poisons, and also stops blood from whatever part it flows.

The figure of a man having a bundle of herbs at his neck, when carved on jasper, has the power of discerning or recognizing illnesses; it restrains blood from whatever place it flows. They say that Galen possessed this stone.

They say that a cross carved on green jasper has the power of freeing the wearer from drowning in water.

A woman having a bird in one hand and a fish in the other: when carved on chrysolite, it is of great value for doing business.

The figure of a wolf carved on jasper is effective against cunning, and prevents one from uttering insane words.

The figure of a stag carved on jasper has the power of healing and freeing lunatics and phrenetics.

The figure of an emperor carved on jasper, with head held upright, renders the wearer lovable to every creature, and whatever he seeks he will obtain.

The figure of a maiden with flowing garment, holding laurel in her hand, when carved on jasper, makes the wearer safe from drowning in water; nor will he be troubled by a demon, and it makes him powerful and able to obtain all things.

The figure of a man having in his hand an inscribed palm, when it is found on jasper, renders the wearer powerful and pleasing to princes. Thus far Thetel.

*Treatise V*

*The Images, or Seals, of Solomon*

[unclear]

A very ancient little book was found in the desert among the children of Israel; because many works of Solomon were found in it, it was ascribed to Solomon. The title of the book was: *On the Seals of Stones According to Thy Name, O Lord, and According to the Course of the Stars*.

The figure of a man sitting upon a plough, having a small neck and a long beard, and four men lying on his neck, holding in one hand a fox and in the other a vulture: know that this seal, hung from the neck, is effective for all plantings and for every discovery of treasures. You shall make the proof of it in this way. Take pure black wool without dye, wrap the stone in it, and place it among wheat straw; put it under your head. Then you will see in sleep all the treasures of that region in which it is. It also has the power of healing all illnesses of beasts, if they drink water in which it has been washed.

A man carved on green jasper, having a shield hanging from his neck and above his head a helmet with an upright sword, and treading a serpent under his feet: place it on your neck, and you will fear no enemy; in all things you will be victorious, especially in martial affairs. It must be bound in bronze.

A horse bearing a crocodile on its back, carved on white hyacinth, makes one lovable to men and beasts.

The figure of a seated man, with a woman standing before him, having hair extending down to the thigh and raising her eyes upward: when it has been carved on carnelian, know that its power is to render every person, whether male or female, obedient. It must be bound in gold of as much weight as the stone itself has; underneath, place betony and amber.

A foaming horse with a man upon it holding a sceptre in his hand, carved on amethyst: know that this seal is useful for all the affairs that the person who wears it has; and all princes and nobles will be obedient to him. It must be bound in gold or silver in double quantity to the seal.
