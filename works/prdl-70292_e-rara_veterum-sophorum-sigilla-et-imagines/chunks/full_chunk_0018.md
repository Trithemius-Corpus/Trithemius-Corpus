But so far am I from attributing to these [unclear] any power in themselves, or from affirming that characters or named constellated [things] possess virtue by reason of their shape, since shape, number, magnitude, position, and the division of the continuous are not principles of any action, that, if there is no other agent and mover by whose power the characters are effective, I plainly deny it. God through the prophets heals men by the name alone when it is uttered: for God is living, and the name of the living God is also living, and the letters of the living name are also living; God lives on account of himself, and the letters live on account of the name; and just as God has life in himself, so he also gave to his name to have life in itself, and further gave it to the letters.

Thus images or characters are effective by reason of the virtue or office appointed by God or nature to such a name or character. For there is no power, either in heaven or on earth, which does not descend from God. Thus medicines are visible bodies, words invisible bodies; whether an herb or a word heals, God is the cause, that is, the spirit of God assisting nature through the word “Fiat.” Hence the herb itself is not medicine, but this lies hidden in it invisibly, implanted by God, namely. Therefore things that are more remote from the senses and have hidden causes are not immediately to be condemned all the way to hell, nor foolishly mocked.
