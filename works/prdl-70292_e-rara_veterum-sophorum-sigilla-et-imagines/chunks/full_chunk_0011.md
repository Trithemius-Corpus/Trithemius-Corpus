Also, if red wax is sealed with it and hung from a dog’s neck, it will prevent it from barking.

Know that a soldier riding upon a running horse, with a horn at his neck and with a tree before him, engraved on some precious stone, is effective for all hunting. Know that a woman holding a bird in one hand and a fish in the other, engraved in crystal, is effective for catching birds; and it ought to be bound in silver. The figure of an eagle engraved in crystal makes the bearer rich, victorious, and eloquent. A horned animal that carries a horse beneath it, and a horse that draws behind itself half of a goat, engraved on some rather precious stone, know to be very effective for taming any beast; and it ought to be bound in a leaden ring.

*Magic.*

When you find the figure of a turtle engraved in [Pangon?], you shall wear it in a leaden ring: the bearer will never be wounded, nor will he receive harm, but he will be honored by all, especially by elders.

When you find engraved in gold the figure of a bird holding a leaf in its mouth, and in its view there is the head of a man or of a vulture, the bearer will acquire profit from all the things that he is going to buy or sell, and will be sought out by buyers.

When you find engraved in crystal the figure of the face of a lion or dragon having two heads with a delicate tail, or of a man in whose right hand, with a staff, he strikes the head of a lion, and you place it in orichalc, and beneath the stone, indeed, musk and amber, and carry it with you, all will obey you, and you will increase your wealth; wax sealed with it will have the same power.

When you find the figure of a winged horse, which is called Pegasus, engraved on some stone, it is excellent for soldiers: in battle on open ground it gives boldness and eagerness.

When you find engraved on some stone the figure of a man with serpents surrounding his sight, know that it gives victory in battle on open ground to the bearer.

The figure of a tree, or vine, or ear of grain engraved on some stone makes the bearer abound in food and clothing, and renders princes well-disposed.

Mars armed, or a maiden in a stole with a flowing garment, holding laurel in her hand, if found in jasper, renders the bearer powerful for accomplishing deeds of arms, and frees him from violent death, from drowning in water, and from adverse accidents.

The figure of an armed man on a horse with a lance, engraved in amethyst, makes the bearer victorious in battle on open ground. If you find the figure of Hercules holding a lion or some other monster engraved on a stone, know that it gives victory in battle on open ground.

The figure of Jupiter, which is made in the form of a man with the head of a ram, makes the bearer lovable and quickly obtaining whatever he has wished.

A virgin or girl with a lamp, engraved in crystal, has the power of preserving the light of the eyes.
