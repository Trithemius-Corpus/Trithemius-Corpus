*Magic.*

...is: a man fallen to his knees, holding a club in his hand and killing a lion; sometimes a man with a lion’s skin in his hand. Such figures, carved on a suitable stone, contribute to victory in war, especially if it is worn in agate.

The image of the swan or hen, with wings spread and neck bent, makes the bearer pleasing to the people, learned, and drives away gout, paralysis, and quartan fever.

The figure of Cepheus, having a sword girded on, with arms and hands spread out, carved on a stone, makes one cautious and learned; when it is placed beneath the pillow of a sleeper, it causes delightful things to appear in sleep.

Cassiopeia is the image of a woman seated in a chair, having her hands stretched out in the manner of a cross, sometimes with a triangle on her head. It is placed in the sign of Taurus and in the northern part; its nature is from Saturn and Venus. When this has been carved on a suitable stone, its power will be to provide health to people, to provide [unclear] and tranquillity after conceived labor to those who bear it, and to furnish pleasant and sweet sleep.

*Seals.*

Andromeda is the image of a girl, having her hair loose and her hands lowered. She is contained by Taurus in the north, and is of the nature of Venus. Its power, if it has been carved on a stone, is to reconcile quarrels and disputes arising between husband and wife, to strengthen stable love, and to guard against very many infirmities.

Perseus is an image having a sword in the right hand and the head of the Gorgon in the left; it is located in Taurus, in the northern part, and has the force of Saturn and Venus. If it has been carved on a stone, it turns misfortunes away from the bearer and preserves him from lightning and storm; it also frees not only the bearer, but the places where it is kept, from enchantments.

Serpentarius is the figure of a man holding a serpent, holding its head in the right hand and its tail in the left. It is in the sign of Scorpio, in the north, and has the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its power when carved on a stone is to avail against poisons, to cure the bites of venomous animals; if its washing is drunk, it makes one vomit up poison without injury.

The eagle or falling vulture is the image of a flying eagle with an arrow beneath its feet. It is placed in Cancer, in the northern part, and is of the nature of Jupiter and Mars, but the arrow is of the nature of Mars and Venus. When these constellations have been carved on a stone, they are said to preserve the bearer’s old ranks of honors, to acquire new ones, and to contribute to victory.

The fish or dolphin is the image of a humped fish. It is in the sign of Aquarius in the north, having the nature of Saturn and Mars. When this has been carved on a stone and bound to the limbs, it makes the bearer fortunate in fishing.

Pegasus, or the winged horse, or, according to some, half a horse with wings, according to others a whole horse with wings, without a bridle, is in the sign of Aries, in the northern part, having the nature of Mars and Jupiter. When carved on a stone, it gives the bearer victory in war on the open field, and makes him swift, cautious, and bold. But when it is hung from a horse’s neck, or placed in water where the horse drinks, it will free him from many infirmities.

Cetus is the figure of a great fish with a curved tail and a great mouth. It is contained by Taurus, in the southern part, and possesses the nature of Saturn. When this figure, carved on a stone, has on its back a crested serpent, it makes the bearer fortunate at sea, renders him prudent and lovable, and restores things taken away.

The image of Orion, an armed man with a sword or sickle in his hand, is placed in the sign of Gemini and in the southern part; it has the nature of Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars. This figure, carved on a stone, makes the bearer victorious, and causes him to carry off victory from his enemies.

The image of a ship is one with a twisted prow and raised sail, sometimes [unclear] with oars. It is contained by Leo, in the southern part, and is of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Carved on a stone, however, it makes the bearer secure in conducting affairs on the sea and water, and does not permit him to take harm from water.

The image of the dog is the figure of a hunting dog with a curved tail. It exists in the sign of Cancer, in the southern part, having the nature of Venus. If it has been carved on a stone, they say that it has the very power of freeing lunatics and maniacs entirely. [unclear]

The image of the hare, in the likeness of a hare, has extended ears and feet, as if it were running. It is placed in the sign of Gemini, in the southern part. Its nature is to cure the frantic, to avail against the wiles of demons, and the bearer will not be able to be harmed by a malignant spirit.
