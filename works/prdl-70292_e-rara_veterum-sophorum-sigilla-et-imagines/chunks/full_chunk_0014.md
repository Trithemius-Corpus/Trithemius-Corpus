The figure of a shrine, or censer, of the nature of Venus and Mercury, with fire kindled, when engraved on a stone, makes the one who carries it able to summon spirits and to be in their conversion, and renders them obedient. The image of the Hydra, or serpent, is the figure of a serpent having above it an urn about its head and a raven about its tail, of the nature of Saturn and Venus. Its virtue is to make the bearer very rich, shrewd, cautious, free from harmful things; it resists every harmful color.

The figure of the Southern Crown is made like an imperial crown, of the nature of Saturn and Mars. Its virtue is in increasing riches, and in making a man cheerful and pleasant.

*Seals.*

The image of the driver, or charioteer, is the figure of a man in a chariot holding a goat on his left shoulder. It is placed in the sign Gemini and in the north, having the nature of Mercury. This figure, engraved on a stone, makes the bearer a hunter and fortunate in catching animals.

The image of a banner is made in the likeness of a banner spread out at the end of a spear. This, engraved on a stone, makes the bearer fortunate in military service and victorious in battle in the field.

The image of a lion engraved in gold, when the Sun is in Leo and the Moon is not aspecting Saturn, preserves from stone and from hot diseases.

The image of a scorpion, impressed or engraved on a gold ring with a bezoar stone, when the Moon is in Scorpio and Scorpio is thus in one of the angles of the world, is effective against the sting of a scorpion.

The image of Aquarius is useful for those who have false imaginings, such as melancholics, quartan-sufferers, and maniacs. But it must be made on the day of the Sun and in its hour; Jupiter must not be combust, nor must the Sun be higher than Jupiter; nor must Saturn be in the ascendant, nor in the 19th degree of Aries; 3rd, 15th, 27th of Taurus; 11th of Gemini; 3rd, 4th, 12th, 15th, 19th of Cancer; 2nd, 5th, 19th of Leo; 3rd, 13th, 30th of Virgo; 3rd, 5th, 21st of Libra; 7th, 18th, 20th, 21st of Scorpio; 13th, 20th of Sagittarius; 12th, 13th, 24th of Capricorn; 7th, 18th, 20th of Aquarius; 13th, 29th of Pisces.

The image of the fishes, when the Sun is in Pisces in the twelfth house, and the Moon in the ascendant, and the lord of the ascendant and the lord of the sixth are in sextile aspect or aspecting each other, or at least the Moon aspecting either one, is said by experience to free the bearer, under the soles of the feet, from gouty pains.

*Seals.*

*On the Method of Making Seals and Magical Images. Treatise VI.*

Having set forth the magical seals and images, and the method of making them, insofar as it has indeed been handed down by the most ancient writers themselves, it remains that in this place there also be added a more exact method of making them, and that the foregoing be explained more accurately.

And indeed, before all things, in their composition the time must be considered: I mean the time of election, to be carefully observed in inscribing or engraving figures on stones. For they receive greater virtue from heaven if they are engraved at fixed and opportune times, since in this way the stars exert more influence.
