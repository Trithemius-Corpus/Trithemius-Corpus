*Magic.*

... in Capricorn or Aquarius; Jupiter's in Sagittarius and Pisces; Mars's in Aries and Scorpio; the Sun's in Leo; Venus's in Taurus and Libra; Mercury's in Gemini and Virgo; the Moon's in Cancer. Thus the image of Leo or Cancer ought to be set into a ring or stone while the Sun is traversing Leo and the Moon Cancer, yet in such a way that each is free from every impediment, especially from the rays of Saturn or Mars.

It must also be held that any sign, so long as the Sun is in it, becomes living, rules over the others, and has its effect felt before the others; and therefore the planet must always be directed to the place, that is, the sign, face, and degree, which the Sun illumines.

Thus far concerning the magical images of the ancient sages, which I have chosen to enumerate here chiefly for this reason: that it may be clear that they were constructed from magical philosophy, that is, from the principles of true natural magic, not of goety or necromancy, although it is quite certain that superstition has crept into many of them through abuse: Use has praise; abuse has blame. For if anyone should wish, with superstition, to refer them to evil action, he will be contrary to the divine order of the art itself and to the principles from which they flowed; and he must beware lest, ensnared by delirious superstitions, he become wholly delirious and superstitious.

*Seals.*

Moreover, from almost the same foundations and causes, gilded and silver plates, round, square, or triangular, have even now begun to be made; on them certain signatures and characters of the planets are engraved according to certain positions, configurations, or constellations of the heavens, in order to win the favor and influence of each planet. For that the influence of the planets is impressed, received, and retained in such figures, likenesses, and images marked with a certain form is consonant with reason and experience.

Meanwhile this must be observed most carefully: that the planet whose influence you seek be strong and fortunate, that is, in its own domicile, exaltation, or triplicity, in the angle of the east or in midheaven, free from the evil rays of Saturn or Mars, supported by the trine or sextile of Venus or Jupiter, or joined to them, direct, swift in course, not combust, ascending in the circle of its apogee.

*The erection of Mercury's signature, for sharpening the memory.*

Let Mercury serve as an example. If anyone desires to procure its influence for sharpening understanding, talent, and memory, let him observe at what time of the year Mercury, in Gemini or Virgo, in which signs it has its domicile, is strong and fortunate in the east or in midheaven. Once the hour and minute of the hour have been found, let a careful and skillful engraving of the celestial figure, together with a delineation of the aspects, be made in pure silver; afterward it is enclosed in white silk and kept, and is sewn into a cap at the part where it precisely touches the cerebellum, with some grains of the best musk added. It has been noticed that it performs its operation chiefly at night, and more evidently at that hour and time at which the engraving was both begun and completed.

In this year 1608 Mercury was found strong and fortunate around 2 May, at the second hour of the morning; and again it will be strongest and most fortunate around the fourteenth and fifteenth day of the month of September, shortly before and after the fifth morning hour, at which time it precedes the Sun in the eastern cardinal point, in its own domicile, exaltation, and the favorable trine of Jupiter.
