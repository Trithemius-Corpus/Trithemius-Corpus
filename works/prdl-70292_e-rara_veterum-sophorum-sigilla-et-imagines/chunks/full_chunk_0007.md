If you find the figure of a standing man wearing a breastplate, a helmet on his head, and in his hand a drawn sword, carved on some stone, and bind it in an iron ring of so great a weight, no one will be able to resist the one who carries it in battle.

If you find the figure of a bearded man with a long face and curved eyebrows, sitting on a plough between two bulls, carved on some stone, it is effective for plantings and for every kind of cultivation, for finding treasures, and for waging war; it will turn enemies into friends; it is effective in many illnesses. If anyone carries it, serpents will flee from his face. It also cures epileptics; if an infant carries it at his neck, it drives fear from him, and [unclear] the vexation of evil spirits. If you find the figure of a standing eagle carved in an aetites-stone, and put it in a lead ring, the person who carries it will be accepted and loved by men; animals will obey him.

If you find the figure of a ram together with a lion carved on some of the more precious stones, and bind it in a silver ring, if you touch persons at odds with it, they will be reconciled.

*Treatise III. The Seals or Images of Hermes.*

Hermes, in the four-part book, records these fourteen images.

The image of a man, standing and of great stature, carved in diadochos, holding in his right hand an obol and in the other a serpent; and above the man’s head let there be the figure of the Sun, and let him hold a lion prostrate beneath his feet. When it has been put in a lead ring with a little artemisia and root of fenugreek, and you have it with you on a riverbank, and call upon the aquatic spirits, you will receive from them answers concerning the matters asked.

When the figure of a man standing and holding a bundle of herb hung from his neck, with thickness of the loins and breadth of the shoulders, has been carved in green jasper, know that it gives aid to those suffering fever and frees them from fever. And if anyone practices the art of medicine and carries it with him, it grants the greatest favor in distinguishing illnesses, in knowing medicines and herbs, and in giving potions; and in every hemorrhage and flow of blood it helps and at once checks it.

*16 Seals.*

If the image of a sea turtle is carved in a marine stone and is carried bound in a lead ring, the bearer will not be able to be harmed by anyone, and he will be loved by elders and lords of lands.

If the figure of Aquarius has been carved in green jasper, and someone carries it with him, it provides profit in selling and buying goods; merchants will seek counsel from him and will bring merchandise into his dwelling.

If a bird that holds a leaf in its mouth, and in its sight there is the head of a man looking at that bird, is carved in a Pangon stone, and you bind it in gold and carry it with you, you will be rich and wealthy, and venerable to all.

The figure of Jupiter, which is that of a man sitting on a four-footed chair, and let there be four men standing there before Jupiter, and let Jupiter’s hands be raised toward heaven, and let him have a diadem on his head: if it has been carved in hyacinth and bound in gold, and if someone carries such a ring with him, or the figure of this ring impressed in wax hung from his neck, he will obtain from kings and wise men whatever he wishes.
