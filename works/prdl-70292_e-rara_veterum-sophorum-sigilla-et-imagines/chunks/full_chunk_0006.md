*Seals*

in a silver ring, you will be invited to many banquets, and all will look at you with wonder and astonishment.

The figure of a ram and of the half of a lion, when it has been carved on some precious stone: when you have touched discordant persons with it, they will be made loving and harmonious; and it must be set in silver.

The figure of a woman in the upper part, but in the lower part of a fish, holding a mirror in one hand and a branch in the other hand, carved in hyacinth and worn on the finger in a gold ring, renders one invisible. The ring with the stone must be turned toward the palm, and the palm must be closed.

The figure of a basilisk, of which one part is that of a woman, but the lower part that of a serpent, when it has been carved on some precious stone, will have the power of putting all venomous animals to flight.

The figure of a basilisk fighting with a dragon, with a man’s head made above, carved in cornelian: when it is hung from the neck, it will give the wearer the power of overcoming every beast, both terrestrial and marine.

The figure of a naked, swollen man, with another figure of a well-dressed man, holding a cup in one hand and a branch of an herb in the other, when it has been carved in jet stone, you should know to have the power of freeing from all fevers, when he has carried it with him for three days.

A statue of a man having an ox’s head and eagle’s feet, carved on some stone: when you have carried it with you, no man will speak ill of you.

A man bending his knees and looking upward, and holding one cloth, carved in turquoise, you should know to be effective for buying and selling things.

The figure of scorpions and Sagittarius fighting each other, carved on some stone, set in an iron ring and impressed on wax, immediately makes [unclear] among discordant persons, whomever you touch with it.

The figure of a ram with the half of an ox, carved on some gem, you should know to have the power, when it has been set in a silver ring, that whomever you touch with it, such persons immediately become harmonious.

The figure of a woman in the upper part and in the lower part of a fish, holding a mirror in one hand and a branch in the other, when it has been carved in hyacinth and set in a gold ring, renders one invisible whenever you wish. The seal, however, must be covered with wax and worn on the finger turned toward the palm.

The figure of a man ploughing; above him let there be the hand of the Lord making a sign; beside it let a star be carved: if it has been carved on some gem and you have worn it most honorably, in whatever region you are, you will not perish from a storm, and the fruits of the earth likewise in that place will not perish.

The figure of a young man holding a crown on his head and sitting upon a four-footed throne, and under each foot let there be a man standing, holding the chair by the neck; and let there be a circle around the head of the one sitting, and let his hands be raised to heaven. If you find this figure carved under white hyacinth, and place this stone in a silver ring of equal weight to the stone, and beneath the stone a little mastic and turpentine, and make a seal in wax, and wear it at the neck, you will obtain from kings and princes whatever you wish; but it must be worn most justly and chastely.

The figure of a serpent having a man on its back, and upon the same a raven: if you find it carved on some stone, whoever carries this stone with him will abound in all things, and will be shrewd and prudent.

The figure of a man standing upon a dragon, holding a sword in his hand: if you find it carved in hematite, place it in a lead or iron ring, and all subterranean spirits will obey him, and will reveal to him all treasures
