*Liber de Scriptoribus Ecclesiasticis* — "On Ecclesiastical Writers" — is Trithemius's foundational bio-bibliography of Christian authors from the apostles to the fifteenth century, in roughly a thousand entries pairing each author with a short life and a list of writings. First printed at Basel by Johann Amerbach in 1494, it was the first large-scale alphabetical bio-bibliography of Christian literature and the work that made Trithemius's reputation as a scholar in his own lifetime.

This is the **1546 Cologne edition** — printed by Peter Quentel, a mid-century reprint of the 1494 first edition (the first edition itself is `prdl-70280` in this corpus). The Latin was re-OCR'd from the page scans and translated by GPT-5.5. No complete modern English translation of the *De Scriptoribus Ecclesiasticis* exists; the catalogue form — a sequence of compact author entries — translates cleanly, and this witness sits in the top tier (S).
