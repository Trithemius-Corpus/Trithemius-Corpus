He flourished under the emperor Anastasius, in the year of the Lord 490.

Fulgentius, bishop of Ruspe in Africa, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures and nobly instructed in secular letters, subtle in intellect, deep in understanding, sweet, eloquent, and polished in speech, ready in teaching, sharp in argument, fit and very gentle in persuasion, shone out most brightly no less for the holiness of his life than for his knowledge of the Scriptures.
