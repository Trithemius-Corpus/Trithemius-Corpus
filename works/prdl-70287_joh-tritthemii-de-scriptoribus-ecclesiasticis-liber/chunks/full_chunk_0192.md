*The Abbot of Sponheim.*

a count, perfectly trained in Greek and Latin letters, a very celebrated philosopher, rhetorician, and theologian. Among other little works of his talent he wrote a large and distinguished volume, which he entitled *Commentaries on Grammar*, one book.

Also *On Dialectic*, one book.

He published various letters, one book.

And many other things.

He translated the *Life of Athanasius of Alexandria* into Latin for Pope Eugenius, and rendered the historian Appian word for word. And other things.

He flourished under the emperor Sigismund, in the year of the Lord 1420.

Hermann Petra of Stutdorp, of the Carthusian order, a German by nation, a man devoted to and learned in the divine Scriptures, devout in life and conduct, vicar and rector of the house of nuns of his order, of Saint Anne near Bruges in Flanders, where for twenty-nine years he was distinguished by much religion and learning, wrote certain devout little works of his talent. Of these the following are reported: a notable work divided into six volumes over the cycle of the year, containing

*Sermons on the Season*, one book.

*Sermons on the Saints*, one book.

Fifty sermons *On the Lord’s Prayer*, one book.

*On the Governance of Nuns*, one book.

And very many other things.

He died under the emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1428, on the ninth day before the Kalends of May, in the sixth indiction.

Henry of Hesse the Younger, of the Carthusian order, prior of the house of the monks of Mary, a man learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular philosophy, subtle in intellect and distinguished in eloquence, published many very elegant volumes, by which he left the memory of his name to posterity; and he publicly lectured on many of them in the universities before his entry into religion. Of these the following are reported:

*On the Sentences*, four books.

*On Genesis*, one book.

*On Exodus*, one book.

*On the Proverbs of Solomon*, one book.

*On the Apocalypse of John*, one book.

*A Dialogue between a Bishop and a Priest*, one book.

*Useful Sermons*, one book.

And certain other things.

He died under the emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1428, in the sixth indiction.
