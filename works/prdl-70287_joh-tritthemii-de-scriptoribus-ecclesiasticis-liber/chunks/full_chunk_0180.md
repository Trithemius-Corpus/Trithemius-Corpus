*Abbot of Spanheim.*

Nicolaus, a physician of outstanding learning, Italian by nation, Florentine by homeland, a man highly learned not only in medicine but also in the other letters of secular philosophy, distinguished in talent, polished in eloquence; how great his learning is among the physicians of this time is proved not by my words, but by their testimonies. He published certain notable little works in that faculty, among which an excellent work is reported:

*Summa medicinae*, one book.

And certain other things.

He died in the times of Rupert, king of the Romans, and of Pope John the twenty-third, in the year of the Lord 1412, in the fifth indiction.

Johannes de Duren, of the Order of Friars Minor of St Francis, German by nation, a man studious and nobly practiced in the Holy Scriptures, of great and distinguished reputation in delivering sermons to the people, is reported to have composed certain little works for the direction of simple priests as preachers, by which he left the memory of his name to posterity. Of these there survive:

*Sermones de tempore*, one book.

*Sermones de sanctis*, one book.

*Quadragesimale*, one book. “Sanctificate ieiunium.”

*De septem peccatis mortalibus*, one book. “Cum confessor idoneus.”

*De occultis vitiis*, one book. “Est via quae videtur ho-”

*De confessione*, one book.

He is also said to have written many other things, which have not come to my knowledge.

Tilmannus de Hachenberg, of the Order of Friars Minor, German by nation, from the diocese of Trier, a man studious in the divine Scriptures, learned through long practice, having a tongue polished by use in speaking, shone forth as great and most renowned in delivering sermons to the people. He wrote certain little works not to be despised by simple people, by which, profitably applying his talent, he left the memory of his name to posterity. Of these there survive:

*Sermones de tempore*, one book. “Erunt signa hic praedi-”

*Sermones de sanctis*, one book.

*Sermones per quadragesimam*, one book.

And certain other things.

He flourished in his convent at Koblenz, where he died and was buried.
