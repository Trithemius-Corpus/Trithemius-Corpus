*Abbot of Spanheim.*

He composed, besides, many other works which have not come to our knowledge. He flourished under Antoninus Pius, in the year of the Lord 150.

Quadratus, an Athenian, bishop after the martyr Publius, once a hearer and disciple of the holy apostles, a man steadfast and of incomparable learning, labored much for the usefulness of the Christian faith, made much progress, and gathered into the unity of the faith the church dispersed by fear of persecution. Since he was an eminent teacher, he wrote, among other things, on behalf of the Christians to the emperor Hadrian, an Apologeticum full of instruction, 1 book. And other works unknown to us. At last he suffered for the defense of the catholic faith on the seventh day before the Kalends of June. He flourished under the emperor Hadrian, in the year of the Lord 120.

Aristides, an Athenian philosopher, a man most learned in secular letters and very studious in the divine scriptures, shone forth distinguished by such eloquence that he offered the nations a marvel of himself; under the garb of a philosopher he bore both Christian faith and Christian conduct, and shone forth renowned by reputation, not without holiness. He wrote an Apologeticum on behalf of the Christians to the emperor Hadrian, 1 book. And certain other works. He flourished under the emperor Hadrian, in the year of the Lord 120.

Melito the Asian, bishop of Sardis, a man learned in the divine scriptures and venerable no less for holiness than for doctrine, shone forth renowned among catholic preachers. For the usefulness of the church of the faithful he wrote many distinguished volumes, of which these are reported:

On Easter, 2 books.
On the Life of the Prophets, 4 books. On the Church, 1 book.
On the Fallen, 1 book. On the Lord’s Day, 1 book.
On the Body and the Soul, 1 book. On the Senses, 1 book.
On Baptism, 1 book. On Faith, 1 book.
On Truth, 1 book. On Hospitality, 1 book.
On the Generation of Christ, 1 book. The book entitled Key, 1.
On the Prophet, 1 book. On the Devil, 1 book.
Of Selections, 6 books. On the Apocalypse of John, 1 book.
On the Passing of Saint Mary, 1 book. Melito, servant of Christ,

He also published other small works, which age has not permitted to come down to our memory. Tertullian, mentioning in a certain passage this man’s elegant and declamatory talent, says that he was regarded by our people as a prophet. It should indeed be noted that the book On the Passing of Saint Mary ascribed to him is reckoned among the apocrypha, in distinction 15, Sancta Romana. He flourished under the emperor Marcus Antoninus Verus, in the year of the Lord 160.
