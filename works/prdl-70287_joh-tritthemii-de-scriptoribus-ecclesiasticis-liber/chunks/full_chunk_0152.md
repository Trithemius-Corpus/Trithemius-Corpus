*The Abbot of Spanheim.*

On the books of the Ethics, 10 books.

On moral philosophy, 1 book.

He also composed the office of the stigmata of Saint Francis, founder of the Order of Minors. Finally, he is said to have published commentaries on many books of the Bible, which have not yet come into our hands. He flourished under the emperor Louis the Bavarian, the fourth, and Pope John XXII, in the year of the Lord 1390.

Nicholas of Lyra, English by nation, of the Order of the Friars Minor, a man most devoted to the holy Scriptures and skilled through long practice, scholastic in speech, and no less venerable in conduct than in knowledge, wrote many distinguished little works on the sacred volumes, by which he brought his name to the knowledge of posterity. How useful and how necessary these are for all who desire to learn sacred Scripture no one knows better than one who has exercised himself in reading them. For there are some who think him to be despised because of the simplicity of his eloquence; they seem to me both vain and unlearned, and unworthy of the sacred knowledge of saving philosophy, since they disparage ecclesiastical simplicity and embrace words, not things.
