*Of the Reverend Father in Christ and Lord, Dom Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Spanheim, On Ecclesiastical Writers, one book, with two supplements down to these times.*

Clement, the fourth bishop of the holy Roman Church ordained after blessed Peter, as some think, a Roman by nation, a holy man and beloved of God, adorned also with beauty of character, whose life and passion are contained in ecclesiastical writings, sat in the pontificate nine years and ten days. He was banished into exile for the faith of Christ with steadfast spirit, in the one hundred third year of the Lord. This most blessed martyr is said to have written, in the person of the Roman Church, several letters full of faith and learning, of which the principal ones are extant:

Two letters to the church of the Corinthians. Clement to the lord James.

Besides these, ten books of the itinerary of his life. When in the city of Rome.

Also one book of the disputation of Peter.

These last two volumes, however, are counted among the apocrypha, as is clear in distinction 15, *Sancta Romana*. Other letters of his are also circulated, but they are apocryphal.

*Addition:*

He compiled the Apostolic Canons, an epitome of the *kerygmata*. All are extant in Greek in the Vatican Library.

Dionysius the Areopagite, an Athenian, a distinguished philosopher, converted to the faith by blessed Paul the Apostle, was ordained the first bishop of Athens, as some think, a man of eminent holiness and incomparable learning. After the death of the apostles, blessed Clement, bishop of the city of Rome, sent him with Rusticus the priest and Eleutherius the deacon into France to preach the word of God to the nations, another bishop having been ordained in his place.
