In certain places, especially where he treats of the impassibility of the mortified spiritual man, he is censured by modern theologians, as is found in certain treatises in John Gerson. He flourished around the times of Constantine, Constantius, and Constans, brothers and emperors, who reigned in the year of the Lord 346.

Ildephonsus, bishop of the see of Toledo after St. Eugenius, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures, and not ignorant either of secular literature, excellent in verse and prose, subtle in intellect, eloquent in speech, and above all the bishops of his time fluent and eloquent, worthy of God and most holy in life and conduct. For he was endowed with constancy in the fear of God, devoted in religion, abundant in compunction, grave in gait, praiseworthy in honor, singular in patience, renowned for his talent in discourse, supreme in wisdom. When, while still a boy, he had been touched by the divine spirit, despising the guilty world and the affections of his parents, he sought the monastery of Agali, and there for many years fittingly showed himself a monk. He also built a convent of virgins in the little village of Deibia, which he endowed from the wealth of his parents. Then, made abbot of the monastery of Agali, he trained the manners of the monks, administered its property, and preserved its life. After this, by princely violence he was brought back to Toledo, and there, after the death of his predecessor, was appointed bishop in his place. He wrote many distinguished little works, of which the following are reported:

*On the Holy Trinity*, one book.
*On His Own Weakness*, one book.
*Various Hymns*, one book.
*Annotations on Sacred Matters*, one book.
*Many Epigrams*, one book.
*Annotations on the Sacraments*, one book.
*Letters to Various Persons*, one book.
And very many other things.

When this most blessed prelate Ildephonsus had completed to the end the book on the virginity of Mary, the most pure Mother of God, whom he was always accustomed to serve most devotedly with all the zeal he could, the most holy Lady of the world appeared to him, holding that book in her hands and giving him thanks for such service, which she affirmed was most pleasing to her.
