*The Abbot of Sponheim.*

*Pope Hormisdas, a Campanian by birth, after Symmachus presided over the Church as pontiff for nine years, a good, learned, and vigorous man. When a synod had been assembled at Rome, he condemned the Eutychian heresy; and the Manichaeans, springing up again, he banished into exile after their books had been burned. Among the other little works of his talent he wrote:*

*A synodal decree, one book.*

*To the Emperor Justin, letter 1.* Among those things which pertain to unity

*To the clerics of Second Syria, letter 2.* Having read the letters of your affection

*Several letters to various persons, two books.*

He died under the elder Emperor Justin, in the year of the Lord 521, in the fourteenth indiction, on the eighth day before the Ides of August; John succeeded him.

*And certain other things.*

*On the Catholic Faith, one book.*

*Evodius, bishop of Ticinum, a man learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular letters, of singular eloquence both in Greek and in Latin, was sent by Pope Hormisdas to Constantinople in matters of the faith to the heretical emperor Anastasius. After receiving many wrongs, he was driven from the city together with his companions, Anastasius saying: “Why do you deliver mandates to us, when we are under no one’s rule? The Roman pontiff ought to obey us, not also to command.” Evodius wrote to Pope Symmachus.*

*He flourished under the emperor Anastasius, in the year of the Lord 510.*
