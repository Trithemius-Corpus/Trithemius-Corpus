*On Ecclesiastical Writers*

On all the epistles of Paul, one book. “From Corinth, the metropolis ...”

On the canonical epistles, seven books. On the Acts of the Apostles, one book. On the Apocalypse, one book. “We read in the Church [unclear] ...”

On the Variety of Books, one book. “Since you are the only and most holy ...”

On the Holy Trinity, one book.

On the Memory of Christian Affairs, one book.

He is also said to have written many other things, letters, sermons, and various treatises, which have not come to my knowledge.

He died under the emperor Louis, son of Charlemagne, in the year of the Lord 854, in the twelfth indiction. He was buried in the church of St. Stephen at Halberstadt.

Claudius, a monk of the Order of Saint Benedict, once, as they say, a disciple of Bede in England, and a colleague of Albinus the deacon and abbot, a Scot by nation, a man very zealous and learned in the divine Scriptures, and not ignorant of secular learning, practiced in verse and prose, composed many short works on the sacred books, of which the following are reported:

On the Five Books of Moses, five books. Also on Judges, one book.

On the Book of Ruth, one book. On the Gospel of Matthew, [unclear] books.

And many other things.

Freculphus, bishop of Lisieux in the province of Rouen, a monk of the Order of Saint Benedict, for whom Hrabanus, while still abbot, exercised his talent in many volumes, a man most zealous and very skilled in the divine Scriptures, and well suited to teaching the people, distinguished no less by his way of life than by his knowledge, is said to have written certain excellent short works. Of these I have read only a large and notable volume, containing, from the beginning of the world down to the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ,

A History of Deeds, seven books. “While at some time ...”

I have seen nothing of the others.

He wrote the aforesaid work to Helisachar; in it he also explained the more difficult questions of the Scriptures, and set down in clear discourse the deeds of both the Jews and the gentiles, from the beginning of the world down to the Virgin’s childbearing.

He flourished under Louis, in the year of the Lord 840.
