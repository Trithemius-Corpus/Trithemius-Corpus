Peter Maurocenus, a Venetian patrician, bishop-cardinal of the sacrosanct Roman Church, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures and in canon law, and not ignorant of secular letters, is known to have fashioned, by his excellent talent, many excellent little works, in which the liveliness of his intellect shines forth. But I have not deserved to see any of them up to the present. There is reported as his notable volume:

On the Sixth Book of the Decretals, one book.
And various determinations.

Nicholas of Dinkelsbuhl, German by birth, ornament and rector of the University of Vienna in Austria, most learned in the holy Scriptures, very erudite in secular philosophy, distinguished in talent, scholastic in discourse, outstanding in life and conduct, wrote many excellent volumes, by which he transmits his name to posterity with glory.
