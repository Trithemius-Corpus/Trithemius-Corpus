He flourished in the times of the emperor Rudolf, in the year of the Lord 1270.

Gerard, a monk of the monastery of St Quentin in the Isle, a man devoted to the Holy Scriptures and skilled through continual exercise, and sufficiently instructed in secular letters, ready in intellect and distinguished in eloquence, wrote certain little works not to be despised in reading, by which he made his name known to those present and those to come. Among these is said to be a history of the translation of the crown and of part of the Lord's cross, which relics Louis, king of France, obtained from the emperor of Constantinople and transferred to Paris. He also wrote concerning blessed Elizabeth the Landgravine:

Miracles after her death, four books.
Responsories and chants concerning the same, one book.
And certain other things.

He flourished under the emperor Rudolf, in the year of the Lord 1280. And in both kinds of literature he composed various little works.

William Lamarensis, of the order of Friars Minor, a man learned in the divine Scriptures by long use and exercise, and also, according to the custom of that time, sufficiently skilled in secular philosophy, distinguished in intellect, scholastic in speech, composed various little works on the Scriptures, by which he transmitted his name to the knowledge of posterity. Among these the following are reported:

On the Sentences, four books.
Correctory of the works of Thomas, one book.
Additions on Bonaventure, one book.
And certain other things.

He flourished under the emperor Rudolf, in the year of the Lord 1289.

Richard, archbishop of Reims in Gaul, of the order of Friars Minor, excellently learned in the Holy Scriptures, inferior to none of the doctors of his time in delivering sermons to the people, subtle in intellect and distinguished in eloquence, is said to have written many excellent volumes, by which he rendered himself useful to those then present and known to those to come; but I do not recall that any of them has come into our hands. The following are reported as his:

Sermons for the season, one book.
Also sermons on the saints, one book.

He flourished under the emperor Rudolf, in the year of the Lord 1270.
