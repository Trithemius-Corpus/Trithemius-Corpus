*In Praise of This Most Noble Work, an Epigram by Dom Sebastian Brant.*

Whoever you are who wish to know the approved writers of the Church, and whatever distinction each one has, read this epitome of the learned Abbot Johannes of Sponheim, whom his eloquent life commends.

As he restores the ancient doctrines of the fathers of the order, and renews the sacred rights of Benedict, so he takes care to bring together the scattered names of the writers of old and to collect them in one book.

He sets down a catalogue, by which he reckons the times, and what that venerable company of authors wrote. He rescues from decay so many worthy names of men, whom devouring time had lately held beneath its feet.

And it is a wonder that one man could either read in order so many worthy men, or call to mind such great men; so that I am quite inclined to doubt whether, if perhaps he did not read them all, yet a great part of them all may have seemed to him to have been seen.

Therefore I rightly place this great-souled and distinguished man also among the number of the learned.
