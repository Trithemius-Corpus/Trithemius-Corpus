*De Vanitate et Miseria Humanae Vitae* — "On the Vanity and Misery of Human Life" — is Trithemius's contribution to the *contemptus mundi* tradition, the medieval meditation on the transience and wretchedness of earthly existence descending from Innocent III's *De Miseria Humanae Conditionis*. It belongs to the devotional-contemplative strand of his output, alongside *De Operatione Divini Amoris* and *De Vera Conversione Mentis ad Deum*, and it rehearses the familiar themes — death, judgement, the emptiness of honours and riches — in the sober monastic register of his shorter treatises.
