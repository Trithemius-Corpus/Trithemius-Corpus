And what is so slight as man, who lives today and tomorrow does not appear? If man is not vanity, where are all our forebears? Where are the men once famous in their generations, who subjected many peoples and kingdoms to themselves? Where is Methuselah, the old man of years? Where is Nimrod, the most powerful of tyrants? Where is Samson, the strongest? Where is Absalom, the most beautiful? Where is Solomon, the wisest? Where is Croesus, richest in gold? Where is Alexander the Great, tamer of the world? Where is Plato, the arch-philosopher? Where is Homer, the greatest of poets; where the remaining cohort of philosophers? They all died many years ago, and perished in their vanity. What now profits them all the power once possessed, what honors, what riches, what fame flying through the world? Behold, now they sleep in dust, and have perished from the memory of men as though they had not existed. Vain, therefore, are all things that are seen in the world: vain is man, vain riches, vain honors. For the things that are seen are temporal, but the things that are not seen are eternal. For the holy Apostle says: The creature has been subjected to vanity, and groans and labors in birth, and awaits the revelation of the sons of God. So long are all things vain, until that which is perfect comes. Therefore all things are liable to vanity that are contained upon the earth within the circuit of heaven. Nothing temporal remains; nothing earthly endures; nothing mortal is long-lasting. What if you are a great prince and live many years on earth? For the judgment of death, even if you put it off for a time, you will not be able to escape. Soon one day will come, suddenly rushing from ambush, which will take away all your hope and your life together. Today a prince, today a lord, today powerful on earth; and tomorrow, dead, he will be hidden beneath the earth, a rotting corpse. Behold, yesterday a mighty emperor; today he is carried dead to the tomb, and he who was clothed in gold and purple is cast almost naked into the pit as food for worms.
