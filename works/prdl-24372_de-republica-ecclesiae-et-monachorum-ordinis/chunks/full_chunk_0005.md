[Con]fessors formerly in your tribulation you sent on to Christ with joy; but now in peace your bitterness has become most bitter. Once, in tribulation for the faith of Christ, you were full of bitterness, yet because of the countless multitude of holy sons you were joyful, glad, steadfast, cheerful, and comforted by the Lord; but now in your peace, because of the paucity of good sons, you are filled with most bitter bitterness.

Nor is it surprising. Your shepherds, with few exceptions, are dumb dogs unable to bark. They buy for a price the leadership of your sheep and sons; they acquire it with innocent blood; with sword, spear, and lance they violently invade it, not from love of Christ, not for your honor, but because of avarice, pride, arrogance, and an insatiable lust for ruling. They come to prelacies uncalled; unelected they approach; unworthy men fraudulently thrust themselves in; and under the name of ecclesiastical liberty they wickedly defend themselves as though they had done well.

These are ravening wolves, who come in sheep’s clothing, destroyers and enemies of the Lord’s flock, who unworthily covet ecclesiastical dignities, buy prelacies with gold and blood, and by right and wrong acquire rule over the people. These are blind leaders, evil guardians, perverse servants, unjust judges, who destroy the commonwealth of the church, who kill the sheep of Christ by perverse conduct, who give laymen occasion to persecute the church, who grant the devil boldness to rage against the flock of the Lord.

These are the foxes that demolish the vineyard of the Lord, namely the holy church, because while those set as watchmen carelessly neglect to stand watchfully at their guard-post, they offer their adversaries the power to do harm. From this there follows among the people a plague of crimes and the destruction of souls. Because of a wicked leader an army is often exterminated. As we now see priests to be toward God, so too we see laymen to be toward them. Contempt of God is now, quite justly, punished in the priests of the church by contempt from the people, and the negligence of his ministers is avenged by laymen through the plundering of temporal goods.

Believe me, venerable priests: God permits nothing against you without cause. He would certainly defend his church from the assault of plunderers, unless our conduct provoked him to vengeance. Therefore let us impute to ourselves what we suffer; let us ascribe to ourselves the fact that we are despoiled by laymen of temporal goods. For almighty God once, because of the holiness of priests, magnificently endowed the church with temporal goods through good princes; now, because of the crimes of his ministers, he again despoils it through evil men.

What more shall I say? You see with your own eyes what the church suffers in these times, since those who would confer anything more upon it are lacking; only those who would seize what was once conferred by others come running. No one now builds churches; no one now raises monasteries, because where greed is seen to abound in priests, the honor and customary worship are denied by laymen.

To this end the commonwealth of the church has been brought down: that those who despoil it think they are offering service to God. For they say: “Behold, priests and monks, sinners, have obtained wealth; behold, with the worship of God despised, they consume it with prostitutes. They have no care for honor; no solicitude for holiness of life. Why then do we delay? Why do we not invade their temporal goods, so that such wicked pride may be humbled? Come, let us oppose the bishops and priests of the Lord; let us seize the tithes; let us take away revenues and pensions; let us despoil houses; let us drive off the livestock; and let there be no one who can escape our hands.”

Thus speak the satellites of the world; thus they act, and they omit no kind of plunder that may harm priests. Everywhere the cruelty of the devil’s satellites rages against the priests of the Lord; and those whom they ought to have pursued with honor and reverence, they persecute with hatred and plunder because of the offenses of a few.

Where now is the privilege of clerical immunity preserved, when because of innumerable declarations of hostility no one is secure, no one goes anywhere safe? Truly, fathers, it is not to be wondered at if we are abandoned by the Lord in necessity, since by evil morals we resist his commandments. Sins alone have made us contemptible before God and men. And so the estate of the ecclesiastical commonwealth descends from Jerusalem to the Jericho of defection, because, falling away from its holy institution, it falls among robbers, while, at the devil’s suggestion, it rushes as if into a certain spring of vices. This long before the Lord announced in the Gospel.
