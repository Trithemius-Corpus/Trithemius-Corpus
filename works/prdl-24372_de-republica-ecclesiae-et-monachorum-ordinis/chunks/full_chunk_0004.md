There is no one who grieves over your ruin; there is no one who pours wine and oil into your wounds; there is no one who entrusts care of you to a faithful innkeeper. Behold, those who are entrusted to judge crimes, lest their own be reproved, gladly overlook those of others. Justice is sold for a low price of silver, and virtue is valued by the protection of statute. The frenzy of Christians at variance with one another rages, and, peace broken among citizens, quarrels are stirred up. Kings and princes once were accustomed to build and endow monasteries and churches; but now, with few exceptions, turned into robbers, they violently seize ecclesiastical property, despise the priests of God, and scorn the commands of the prelates of Christ.

The love of God has withdrawn from the hearts of men. Reverence for priests has departed; the honor of the sacraments of the Church, alas, has expired; and as the people, so the priest has become, because, as among the laity, so also among the priests, avarice reigns, lust rages, pride runs mad, anger is savage. Uncleanness of mind as much as of body renders them contemptible. O priests, priests, who by your sins and crimes throw the ecclesiastical commonwealth into confusion; who devote yourselves to avarice and luxury; who neglect the worship of God; who shamefully consume the alms of the faithful: how harsh a sentence of sudden damnation you are about to undergo, you who should have instructed the people by an example of truth and holiness, but go before them into guilt. Woe to you, ministers of pleasure, how near is your overthrow. There is no fear of God before your eyes; no dread of the laws; no terror of the inner Judge. Your damnable conduct has given laymen an occasion for sinning, while because of your evil life you do not dare to teach well. How shall an avaricious priest rebuke avarice?

I lie if I have not known priests, sons of peasants, so inflamed by the torches of avarice that some possess ten, others twenty, some thirty ecclesiastical benefices, both with cure of souls and without cure; yet they neither feed nor visit the flock committed to them. O what will you answer to the supreme shepherd, you hirelings, you avaricious priests, you deserters of the sheep of Christ? You not only snatch the wool from the sheep, but also, what is unspeakable, scatter the Lord’s flock. You have no care for the sheep, you who receive the wool and the milk and place their keeping with unfaithful hirelings. You avaricious, unclean, proud pastors, you devour the blood of the poor; by the plurality of your benefices you despoil poor priests better than yourselves in learning and holiness. Behold, you shamefully consume the substance of Christ’s patrimony; behold, day and night you devote yourselves to the pleasures of the flesh, and you do not assist needy, learned, and holy priests in the least. Therefore the wrath of the just Judge, Jesus Christ, will burn against you; he will require the blood of his sheep from your hands.

I do not wonder at the hatred of laymen against clerics, who by their ways deserve no love; for, as the Lord says through the prophet, because of their crimes the priests have become contemptible among the people. Deservedly we suffer, O priests, deservedly we suffer the reproaches of the people, since we despise the precepts of God. For the Wisdom of the Father says: Those who despise me shall be ignoble. And would that the unlearned common people spread the crime of a few to all, and that the good priests might somehow repair what the bad have ruined in the ecclesiastical commonwealth. But now the just are condemned among the ignorant together with the impious, and because of the hatred owed to the wicked, even the customary honor due to the good is withdrawn.

Such now is your commonwealth, O holy mother Church, that among your priests and judges those who correct the wicked are lacking; only those who are corrected come before them. Woe to the pastors who feed themselves, who neglect the Lord’s sheep, who serve earthly profits, who seek their own things, not those of Jesus Christ. Alas, mother and virgin, bride of Christ, Church, to what misery you have come. You once grew and expanded amid the storms of persecutions; you humbled the necks of the proud; you trod down the savagery of tyrants; you subjected the kings and princes of this age to your command; now you are torn by your sons, despised, and almost reduced to nothing.
