Johannes Trithemius, at Peapolis, which is now Würzburg in ducal Franconia, formerly abbot of the monastery of Saint James and of the abbey of Sponheim: On the Seven Secondary Causes, that is, the Intelligences, or Spirits That Move the Spheres, a truly most precious little book, happily dedicated to the Emperor Caesar Maximilian Augustus Pius.

Epigram of Johannes Marquardus

This little book, teaching earlier deeds with pleasing brevity,
from fixed numbers foretells how great future things will be.
Calchas, son of Thestor, filled with Apollo,
could not have reported more certain things, nor Helenus the seer.
Great was the trust once placed in the Cumaean song;
but I do not trust your leaves, Sibyl.
Our venerable abbot possesses more credibility here,
whose page has an everlasting divine power.

With the privilege of the imperial majesty that it is not to be printed in his kingdom, empire, and lands, nor copies printed elsewhere sold there, within ten years, under the penalties expressed in the privilege: ten marks of pure gold and the loss of all such books.
