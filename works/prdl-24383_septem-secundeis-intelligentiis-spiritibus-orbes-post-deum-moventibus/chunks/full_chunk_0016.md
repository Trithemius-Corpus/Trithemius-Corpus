In the year of the Christians 1244, a certain Jew at Toledo, while digging, found a book in which it was written: “In the third world Christ will be born of the Virgin Mary, and will suffer at the hands of the Jews.” Soon believing, he was baptized. The third world: the third revolution of Saturn’s [sphere?] was that of which it was said above, at the beginning of which Christ was born of the Virgin.

The Roman pontiffs, annulling Frederick as impious, said that it had been vacant for 28 years, until the election of Rudolf, count of Habsburg, setting among the kings by election of the princes first Henry, count of Schwarzenburg, a Thuringian; William, count of Holland; Conrad, son of Frederick; Alfonso, king of Castile; and Richard, count of Cornwall, brother of the king of England. And evils were multiplied on the earth.

In these times, around the years of the Lord 1260, the confederation of the Swiss first began: a people small in number, which in time grew, and many of the nobles they killed, others they drove from their territories. They are warlike men, and their commonwealth also is known to all the peoples of Germany.

In the year of the Christians 1273, Rudolf of Habsburg was made emperor by election of the princes, and he reigned 18 years, a man prudent in all things and very good; from him all the dukes of Austria afterwards descended. The Tartars, invading the lands of the Christians, Constantinople, and Greece, brought many losses upon the Christians. The Saracens occupy many cities in Asia and kill more than four hundred times a hundred Christians.

After Rudolf had died, Adolf of Nassau was elected king and reigned 6 years. Albert, son of Rudolf, afterwards overcoming him in battle near Worms, killed him. And he was elected emperor in the year 1298, and reigned 10 years, and was killed by his brother’s son. The order of the Templars, [unclear], Pope Clement V destroyed. The island of Rhodes was taken by the Christians from the hands of the Saracens in the fourth year of the siege and of continuous war against them.

After Albert had been killed by his nephew, Henry VIII was made emperor, count of Luxembourg, who reigned 5 years. When he had died, Louis IV of Bavaria reigned as emperor for 32 years, beginning in the year 1315. To him the Roman pontiffs gave the crown. Frederick, duke of Austria, opposed himself to him, but was overcome by him. After Louis, Charles IV of Bohemia was made emperor; he raised the bishopric of Prague to an archbishopric. He reigned 31 years. There were very great earthquakes. This Charles newly instituted many things concerning tolls in favor of the prince-electors. Günther, count of Schwarzenburg, calling himself king, opposed Charles as emperor, but he never prevailed. After Charles, his son Wenceslaus reigned 22 years; after him Jodocus, margrave of Moravia. They succeed Sigismund.
