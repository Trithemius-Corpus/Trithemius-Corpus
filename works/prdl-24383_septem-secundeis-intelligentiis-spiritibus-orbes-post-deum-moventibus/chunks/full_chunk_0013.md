The description of Francia was great in length and breadth. Its metropolis: Mainz. But now only Wurzburg. Bavarians, Swabians, Rhenish people, Saxons, Thuringians, and the districts of the pontificate today occupy a great part of Francia in Germany. Likewise, in the 280th year of the government of this Anael, the Romans declined, the head of the empire, whose city was captured and burned by the Goths, the first imperial seat having been transferred under Constantine into Greece; this was hostile, and the cause of the whole decline of the monarchy. For around the end of this rule of Anael there arose Radagisus, Alaric, Athaulfus, kings of the Goths, and afterward Genseric of the Vandals, Attila also of the Huns, who, fighting through all Europe, miserably tore the empire apart, as is clear in these histories. After Anael, in order the seventeenth, Zachariel, spirit of Jove, took up the rule of the world for the third time on the first day of June, in the year of the world 3669, the fourth month, but of the Lord’s nativity 463, the seventh month, and he continued for 354 years and 4 months, namely to the year of the world 6023 and the eighth month, and of the Lord 817. In these times many, for love of Christian philosophy, withdrew to the desert; many portents also appeared: comets, earthquakes, rain of blood. Merlin, born in [Tumba?], foretold wondrous things at the beginning of this rule. Arthur, whom the common people call [Arcum], the most famous king of Britain, defeated the barbarians, restored peace to the Church, performed many very great deeds as victor, enlarged the faith of Christ, and subjected all Gaul, Norway, Dacia, and many provinces to his empire. He was the most glorious of all the kings of his time; after many excellent deeds had been performed, he appeared nowhere, being awaited for many years by the Britons to return. About him once the [unclear] composed wonderful little songs. While he reigned, England was in flower; thirty kingdoms served him. In these times the monks in the order of the Church of God began to be multiplied. Theodoric, Arian king of the Goths, possessed all Italy and killed the consul Boethius. All things were full of disturbance, both the empire and the Church: Zeno and Anastasius, Arian emperors in the East; Theodoric and his successors in Italy; Honorius, king of the Vandals, exercised no small tyranny in Africa. At last Clodoneus, king of the Franks in Gaul, having become Christian, overcame the Goths and imposed peace upon affairs, although not everywhere on earth, in the time of Saint Benedict, in the five-hundredth year of the Christians, or thereabout. At the beginning of the rule of this angel Zachariel, of Jove, whose own spirit it is, he began to change empires and kingdoms, as the histories variously declare was done in this revolution; and what he himself could not do, he ordained to be done by Raphael, the angel of Mercury, his successor, in Charles of the Franks.
