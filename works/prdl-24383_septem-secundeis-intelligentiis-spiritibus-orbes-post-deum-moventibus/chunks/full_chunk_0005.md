They began to practice hunting, to make tents, and to adorn their bodies with various garments; and a great division was made between the good and the wicked. The good called upon God, as did Enoch, whom the Lord translated; but the wicked ran after the enticements of the flesh. Under the rule of this Zachariel, men also began to live more civilly, to endure the laws and customs of their elders, and to grow gentler from their former savagery. Under his leadership also Adam, the first man, died, leaving to his posterity the testament of the necessity of dying. Finally, in this time various inventions and arts of men emerged, as the historiographers have expressed more clearly.

The fourth ruler of the world was Raphael, spirit of Mercury, who began on the twenty-fourth day of February in the year 1063 from the creation of heaven and earth, and he presided for 354 years and four months; his dominion lasted until the year of the world 1417 and the fourth month. In these times writing was first invented, and letters were first devised after the manner or form of trees and plants; afterward, with time, they took on a more cultivated adornment and changed the appearance of their characters according to their peoples. The use of musical instruments began to multiply under the rule of this Raphael, and the exchange of merchandise was first invented among men. The rude boldness of sailing was first attempted in these times, and many other things of that kind.

The fifth governor of the world was Samael, angel of Mars, who began on the twenty-sixth day of the month of June in the year of the world 1417. He governed the world for 354 years and four months, until the year 1771 and the eighth month. Under his rule men imitated the nature of Mars. Likewise, under the governance of this Samael, the flood of Noah occurred in the year of the world 1656, as is clearly evident from the history of Genesis. And it should be noted, according to what the ancient philosophers have handed down, that whenever Samael, spirit of Mars, is ruler of the world, a notable alteration of monarchy arises. Religions and sects are changed; the laws of principalities are changed; kingdoms are transferred to foreigners. This we can easily find in order from the histories. Yet Samael does not display this disposition of his character immediately from the beginning of his rule, but after he has passed the middle of his governance. The same is also to be understood of the spirits of the other planets, as is clear in the histories: all of them, according to the properties of the nature of their stars, influence and act upon the lower things of this world.

The sixth governor of the world was Gabriel, angel of the moon, who began after Samael of Mars on the twenty-eighth day of the month of October, in the year of the world 1771 and the eighth month, and governed the world for 354 years and four months, until the year of the world 2126.
