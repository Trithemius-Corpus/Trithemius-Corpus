Gabriel built the temple in the midst of Jerusalem. From this time the Spirit of God, illuminating his prophets with a fuller light of his grace, caused them to foretell not only the future incarnation of the Lord and Savior, but also many other things, as the sacred histories testify. Among them were Nathan, Gad, Asaph, Ahijah, Jeremiah, Azariah, and many others. Homer, the Greek poet and writer of the fall of Troy, and Dares the Phrygian and Dictys of Crete, who were present at that fall and likewise described it, are read to have lived in these times.

In the thirteenth order Gabriel, the spirit of the moon, again began to govern the world on the thirtieth day of January, in the year from the origin of the universe 4252, and he presided over his rule for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the world 4606 and the fourth month. In these times many prophets flourished among the Hebrews, namely Elijah, Elisha, Micah, Obadiah, and many others, and there were various changes in the kingdom of the Hebrews. Lycurgus gave rights and laws to the Lacedaemonians. Capetus Silvius, Tiberinus Silvius, Remulus Silvius, Procas Silvius, and Numitor, kings of Italy, flourished under the guidance of this spirit.

Many new kingdoms also took their beginning under him, such as those of the Lydians, Medes, Macedonians, Spartans, and others. The monarchy of the Assyrians failed in Sardanapalus, and the kingdom of the Macedonians arose. Various laws were imposed upon men, and the worship of the true God was neglected; the religion of false gods spread exceedingly. The city of Rome was built under the rule of this spirit in the year 1484, which was the 239th year of Gabriel’s rule in his order. The kingdom of the Silvii failed, and that of Italy and the Romans began.

In these times also Thales, Solon, Chilon, Periander, Cleobulus, Bias, and Pittacus, the seven sages, flourished in Greece, and from then on philosophers and poets began to be held in esteem. Romulus, the first founder of the city, ruled Rome for thirty-seven years, a fratricide and a lover of sedition. After him Numa Pompilius ruled for forty-two years; he enlarged the worship of the gods and, in the time of Hezekiah king of the Jews, maintained the kingdom in great peace.

Toward the end of the governance of this spirit of the moon, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon captured Jerusalem, destroyed King Zedekiah, and led the whole people away captive. Jeremiah the prophet flourished, who foretold this destruction, and likewise their future liberation from Babylon. After Gabriel, Michael, the spirit of the sun, assumed the fourteenth rule of the world again. He began on the first day of the month of May, in the year of the world 4606 and the fourth month, and ruled the world in his order for 354 years, up to the year from the creation of the world 4960 and eight months. In the time of this rule Evil-merodach king of Babylon restored liberty and a king to the people of the Hebrews.
