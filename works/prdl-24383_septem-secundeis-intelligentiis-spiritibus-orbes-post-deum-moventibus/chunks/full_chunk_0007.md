They introduced elaborate adornments of the body and various kinds of musical instruments, and people again began to pursue lust and the pleasure of the flesh excessively. In these times, too, people first began to establish and dedicate statues and temples to their gods. Incantations and works of sorcery were devised in these times by Zoroaster, the first king of the Bactrians, and by various others; Ninus, king of the Assyrians, defeated him in war.

In the tenth order, Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, again began to govern on the last day of February, in the year 3189 from the creation of heaven and earth, and he governed, as usual, for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 3543 and the fourth month. These were joyful times, which could truly be called golden, in which abundance of all things brought increase to the human race and gave the world its greatest adornment. In these times also God gave Abraham the law of circumcision and first promised the redemption of the human race through the incarnation of his only-begotten Son. Under this rule the holy patriarchs, founders of justice, became renowned, and the just were separated from the impious by their own will and conduct.

In these times also Jupiter became famous in Arcadia, who is also Lisanias, son of Heaven and of God, a king who first gave laws to the Arcadians, made them very civilized in their customs, taught the worship of God, built temples, established priests, and procured many useful things for the human race. For these reasons he was called Jupiter by them, and after death was regarded as a god. He derived his origin from the sons of Heber, namely Gerar, as the histories relate.

Prometheus also, son of Atlas, is said under this angel’s rule to have made human beings, because from rude people he made learned, humane, gentle people, polished in manners and knowledge. By art he made images move of themselves. He first devised the use of the ring, and likewise invented the scepter, the diadem, and the royal insignia. Other Jovian people also became renowned in these times, most wise men and women, who by their own genius handed down many inventions useful to the human race; because of the greatness of their wisdom, after death they were thought to have been numbered among the gods: namely Phoroneus, who first established laws and judgments among the Greeks; Sol, Minerva, Ceres, Serapis among the Egyptians; and many others.

In the eleventh order, Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, again undertook the government of the world on the first day of July, in the year of the world 3543 and the fourth month, and he presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year 3897 from the creation of heaven and earth, and the eighth month.
