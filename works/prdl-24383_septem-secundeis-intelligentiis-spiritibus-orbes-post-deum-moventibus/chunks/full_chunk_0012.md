On both sides, while Orifiel, the spirit of Saturn, governed it, heavenly things were mingled with earthly, and two sceptres of the world were given to men, namely of spiritual and worldly things; of these the Most High seems to have conferred one on Caesar, the other on the pope. For many Christians were cruelly killed by the rulers of the world for the faith which they preached. Around the end of Orifiel’s rule Jerusalem was destroyed by the Romans, and the Jews were scattered into every land. After eleven hundred thousand had been killed and 80,000 sold, the rest fled; and thus the Romans utterly destroyed Judea.

*In the sixteenth order*, after Orifiel, Anael, the spirit of Venus, resumed the governance of the whole world for the third time, on the last day of January, in the year 5315 from the creation of heaven and earth, but 109 from the birth of Christ; and he governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, that is, until the year of the world 5669, month 4, but of the Lord’s nativity in the flesh 463. And it should be noted that through almost the whole time of this rule of the angel of Venus the church of the Christians flourished in persecutions and prevailed, with countless thousands of men killed for the faith of Christ. Moreover, several heresies began to spring up in the church in these times; these, with time, were extinguished not without the labor and blood of good men. In these times many men most learned and most eloquent in every kind of knowledge became renowned: theologians, astronomers, physicians, orators, historiographers, and others like these, not only among the Gentiles but also among the Christians. At last the persecution of the unbelievers against the church ceased, after Constantine the Great Caesar had taken up the faith, in the year of the world 5539, after the middle of the rule of Anael himself, the angel of Venus; although even afterward it was at times disturbed in part by the impious, nevertheless for a long time it remained very quiet throughout. From this time the human race, which from the time of King Ninus had wandered miserably for 1300 years in the worship of idols, was mercifully recalled to the knowledge of the one God. In these times arts of varied subtlety were increased, and in accordance with the nature of Venus took on adornment and growth; for they change men’s morals with the time, and dispose lower bodies according to the influence of the higher ones. The mind indeed is free, and does not receive the influence of the stars, except insofar as, through the too great commerce which it has with the body, by inclining itself it stains its own affection. For the angels who are movers of the orbs destroy or overturn none of those things which nature has established. A comet of unusual magnitude preceded the death of Constantine. The Arian heresy disturbed the holy church in many places. At the end of this rule, in the time of Julian Caesar, crosses appeared on the linen garments of men.
