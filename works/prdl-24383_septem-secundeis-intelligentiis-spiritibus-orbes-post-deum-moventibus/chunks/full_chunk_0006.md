In these times human beings again multiplied and founded various cities. It should be noted that the Hebrews say the Flood occurred in the year of the world 1656, under the rule of Mars. But the Seventy translators, Isidore, and Bede confirm that it occurred in the year 2242, under the governance of Gabriel, the spirit of the moon; and this seems to me more consistent with the truth, because of a calculation which it is not the present time to set forth.

The seventh governor of the world was Michael, the angel of the sun, who began on February 24, in the year of the world, according to the common reckoning, 2116, and governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world’s age 2480 and the fourth month. Under the rule of the angel of the sun, as histories in accord with the truth relate, kings first began to exist among mortals. Among them Nimrod was the first who, from lust for domination, seized tyrannical power over his companions. The worship of gods also was established through the folly of human beings, and the poor began to worship their own rulers as gods. Various arts too were invented by human beings at this time: mathematics, astronomy, and magic; and the worship of the one God began to be offered to different creatures, and the knowledge of the true God gradually fell into oblivion through human superstition. In these times also architecture was invented, and human beings began to use more refined customs and institutions.

Thereafter, in the eighth order, Orifiel, the angel of Saturn, again began to govern the world on the twenty-sixth day of June, in the year from the origin of the world 2480 and the fourth month. He governed the world a second time for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 2834 and the eighth month. Under the governance of this angel the nations multiplied, the earth was divided into regions, many kingdoms were established, the tower of Babel was built, the confusion of languages took place, and human beings were scattered over the whole earth. Human beings began to cultivate the earth more carefully, to lay out fields, sow grain, plant vineyards, set trees in the ground, and acquire more diligently whatever pertains to food and clothing. From this time first among human beings there began to be a distinction of nobility, since men who excelled the rest in morals and ability received the marks of glory from the elders of the earth according to their merits. From this point the whole world first began to come into human knowledge, since, as peoples multiplied everywhere, many kingdoms arose and various differences of languages followed.

But in the ninth order Anael, the spirit of Venus, again began to govern the world on the twenty-ninth day of October, in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 2834 and the eighth month, and he presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 3189. In these times human beings, consigning the true God to oblivion, [worshiped] dead men and their statues [unclear].
