*De Septem Secundeis, id est, Intelligentiis sive Spiritibus Orbes post Deum Moventibus* — "On the Seven Secondary Intelligences, that is, the Spirits Moving the Orbs after God" — is Trithemius's astrological philosophy of history. Its thesis is that the successive ages of the world are governed in turn by seven planetary angels — the *secundeis* — each ruling for a fixed period in the order of the celestial spheres, yielding a chronological scheme that runs from the Creation down to his own century. Addressed to Emperor Maximilian I, it is the work in which Trithemius brought his reading of the celestial-intelligence tradition (transmitted through Arabic and scholastic astronomy) to bear on universal history, and it had an outsized afterlife in Renaissance historical thought.
