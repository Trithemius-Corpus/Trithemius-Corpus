*De Laudibus Sanctissimae Matris Annae* — "In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne" — is Trithemius's *tractatus* honouring St Anne, mother of the Virgin Mary, written at Sponheim with a dedicatory letter to Rumold Laupach dated 1 July 1494, and issued with commendatory poems on St Anne and the Holy Kindred — the extended family of Christ that the late-medieval devotion to Anne had made its centre. It is a characteristic piece of his early Marian-hagiographic output.
