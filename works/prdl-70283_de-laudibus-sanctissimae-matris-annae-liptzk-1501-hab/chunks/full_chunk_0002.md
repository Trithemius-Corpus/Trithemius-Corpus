*John Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim: a tetrastich on the most holy mother Anne*

Venerable Anne, mother of the nourishing Virgin,
excellent in merits and powerful in piety,
no one who honors and venerates you goes away sad;
for you bring to all the gentle help of salvation.

*A distich by the same, which he placed beside the image of Saint Anne*

Whoever in adversity shall have appealed to Saint Anne
with devout prayers, he shall be well protected.

*Conrad Celtis Protucius, poet laureate: a tetrastich on Saint Anne*

Anne never dismissed anyone’s prayers empty,
but, bright one, always brought the desired aid.
Therefore let us heap much honor upon the pious mother,
who allows no one’s prayers to be without effect.

*Theodoric Gresemund the Younger of Mainz: a tetrastich to Saint Anne*

Anne, parent of the Virgin, hail, auspicious mother of Mary,
worthy to begin the work of our salvation;
since human redemption was begun through you, I pray
that you continue, by your prayers, covering our sins.

*Rudolf Lang, canon of Münster: a distich on Saint Anne*

Great parent Anne, hear the prayers which pregnant women
bring to you, mother of the fruitful Virgin.

*Jodocus Badius of Ghent: a tetrastich on Saint Anne*

Anne, glory of the world, mother of the mother of Jesus,
may your grandson grant you the stars for the merits of your daughter.
Holy Anne, bringing forth the first fruits of our salvation,
help us with your offspring and with your grandson.

*Johann Theutheinus: [On the praises of the most holy mother Anne]*

Anne, parent, venerable mother of the highest parent,
you who first open to peoples the path of salvation:
from you all diseases flee, and pallor and pain,
and all harmful things that sit in the mind and in the body.
Whatever there is among human beings that wearies cares and hearts
flees from you, as clouds driven away are wont to flee the winds.

*Rugerus Venrai, canon regular of Hain, before the image of Saint Anne*

Speak deep prayers, devoutly, with knee bent,
where mother Anne sits blessed with her offspring.
Do not doubt: you will receive whatever you have asked of her;
for Anne continually shines, powerful in prayers.

*A distich by the same on Saint Anne*

Hail, Anne, mother of the mother of Jesus Christ;
and, we pray, drive away from us every sin.

*The rosary of Saint Anne by Jodocus [Beissellius?], patrician of Aachen, and that to the reverend father lord Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim, of the order of the divine father Benedict*

Whoever wishes to read this rosary in honor of the most blessed mother Anne with pious devotion should first say the Lord’s Prayer with the salutation of Saint Anne, which he should always repeat after two verses. This salutation is as follows:

O venerable Anne, mother of the mother of God, most pleasing to the Holy Trinity, honored above all women: blessed are you by the Lord, and blessed is the offspring of your womb, and blessed is Jesus Christ, born of the most pure Virgin. Amen.

Our Father. Hail, [mother] of God.

Anne, may I, wretched man, be permitted to set forth your praises,
for if you allow it, I shall bring you garlands and roses.
Amen.

And, Anne, you shine, venerable offspring of the kings of Judah,
and also renowned through your pious husband Joachim.
Amen.

And, Anne, for a long time you wept over barren loves in marriage,
the unique glory of married women and their one honor.
Amen.

And, pious Anne, you do not seek offspring for vain uses,
but so that, in holy posterity, it may worship God.
Amen.

And, Anne, a swift messenger, standing by you from the heavenly court,
says, “Rejoice; behold, exultant mother, you shall have offspring.”
Amen.

And, Anne, you will bear offspring blessed with such great virtue
that she may shine with the honor of a mother, while remaining a virgin.
Amen.
