Nothing is lost to God, who is lord of all, and who holds sovereignty everywhere, strengthened no less over evil than over good men and spirits. Christ Jesus, God from God the Father without time, and man from the Virgin in time, appointed mediator of God and human beings, is himself the one through whom we have access to God. He is the savior of all the faithful; of him he thinks wrongly who rashly grants that anyone can be saved without the Christian faith. The omnipotence of God has regard to the order of things which he established; according to its inviolable sequence of righteousness no one will be saved without faith in Christ: no one who does not believe in the Son of God is justified. Therefore they are moved by vain and utterly foolish piety who think that anyone outside the faith of Christ, in the whole world and in any sect whatever, is to be saved, since this is plainly contrary to his sentence. Let them hear the Savior himself again saying in the Gospel: No one comes to the Father except through me. For I am the way, the truth, and the life. If no one comes to the Father except through the Son, certainly no one will be saved except through him. Who will save the Jew and the pagan, since no one can be saved except through Christ? But Christ will undoubtedly save only those who believe in him with heart, speech, and deed. He will not save the Jew, not the Saracen, not the pagan, who do not believe in him, but despise his teaching, contemn the faith, and, blaspheming his holy name, vilify it all day long. For he himself said in the Gospel: Whoever is ashamed of me and of my words, I too shall be ashamed of him before my Father and his holy angels. Tell me now, you who worship Christ and contend that someone can be saved outside the Christian faith in his own sect, which he thinks true: do you believe the Gospel of Christ to be true or false? If true, why do you think the contrary of it? For Christ says: He who has not believed in the Son of God, the wrath of God remains upon him; and he has already been judged and will be condemned; and that no one comes to the Father except through me. But you, seduced by I know not what rashness, force in the contrary, saying that a man outside the faith of Christ, trusting in his ancestral religion, which he believes to be true and pleasing to God, or living innocently according to the law of nature, and especially since perhaps he has never heard anything of the faith of Christ, is to be saved in his confidence. Christ did not say this, but: He who has believed in the only-begotten Son of God will be saved; but he who has not [unclear]. He excepted no one; he admitted no condition, since he who had come to suffer for all had provided for the salvation of all through faith alone. If faith in the Savior is lacking, then no confidence will save the unbeliever. For the holy prelate Augustine said in On Faith to Peter: Hold most firmly, and in no way doubt, that not only all pagans, but also all Jews, heretics, and schismatics who end this present life outside the catholic Church will go into the eternal fire, which has been prepared for the devil and his angels. You see now where foolish compassion draws you: you who measure God by human laws, so that you may make him merciful in all things, do not fear to judge his only-begotten Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, a liar. But Christ is truthful, who says that those who do not believe in him are to be damned; you, however, are rash and liars, because you trust that someone can be saved outside his faith. Whoever truly believes in Christ does not permit anyone at all outside the unity of the catholic Church of Christians to be said to be saved. Who says the contrary? For this is the inviolable sentence of catholic tradition, which the Lord Jesus himself, the Son of God, defined in the holy Gospel: namely, that no one is to be saved outside his faith, which he himself instituted, taught, and confirmed by his own blood.
