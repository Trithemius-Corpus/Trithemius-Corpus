*The Sixth Method, after two nulls, always takes two significatives, whether they are words or are not.*

The sixth method is that, with the first two being nulls to avoid suspicion, the two following in the order of the secret signify; and if by words of equal syllables they had completed the hidden word, that word itself is, in the usual way, closed off by two idle ones; if not, until the narration falls equally through two or more mysteries, where the nulls end some hidden word, presently what follows is begun from the significatives.
