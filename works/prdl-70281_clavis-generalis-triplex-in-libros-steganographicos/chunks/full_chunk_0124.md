*Example of the Second.*
Creator of the human race, God, look upon your servants. Govern them with zeal for righteousness, so that, living well, we may obtain the reward, the joy of the heavenly ones. Help us, frail and wretched human beings, and adorn us with holy morals; [unclear], we beseech you, illumine our minds with light, so that we may diligently call to mind your passion, with tears and good works. Jesus Christ, Savior, free us by the power of your shed blood. Ruler of Catholic unity, save your gravely afflicted people, whom long ago, dying on the cross, you redeemed by your most blessed shed blood. See how grievously the people is afflicted under the most cruel Turk. Free us wretches through the [love?] by which you redeemed us.

Amen. John Trithemius, Abbot, in the year of the Lord 1500.

*The Third Method proceeds through individual signifying words, and the letters are transferred to the third preceding letter.*

The third method of this second chapter proceeds through individual signifying words, as the first does, and the first letter of each word is transferred to the immediately third preceding letter. Thus for A, x is taken, which is third before it through z and [y?]; and so consequently, as is clear from the letters themselves and the numbers placed in the little wheel. This method can also be varied just as the others can, and not only by proceeding through individual signifying words, but also through alternate words, pairs, threes, fours, or more, at the pleasure of any writer.
