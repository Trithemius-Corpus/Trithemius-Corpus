*Key of Steganography. Method XXXIII. Proceeding by alternate words like the preceding methods, it shifts the first significant letter to the fourteenth.*

Method 33 begins the second narration with one idle word, and, like the preceding method, proceeds in turn by alternate words. For the first letter of each significant word it takes the letter following it in the fourteenth place, so that for A, p is taken; for b, q; for c, r; and so on according to the order of the following alphabet.

*Example XXXIII.*

Johannes Trithemius, to the holy nuns on the mountain once of Rupert, who adorn the Catholic faith with bright virginity: happiness. With continual zeal for most holy religion, for the sake of the aforementioned greatest king, we send a servant, requested by us for a long time, a great man, a man [unclear] in temporal matters. Johannes, known to your charities, zealous with fervor for the religion of the monastery of [Uleidorf?], for a long time abbot, and freely leaving a new abbacy, I approached therefore, just as I had committed to you the monastery of Limpurg, in which he served Christ, occupied with continual prayers and holy readings of the Scriptures, when he went down with the monks of the same place and took up the care of temporal affairs, and became procurator and cellarer. The abbot of Limpurg, changed in mind by his usual instability, finally consents to leave. I begged with the greatest urgency, and scarcely could obtain consent from him. Christ willing, he at last consents, though timidly, because he fears to serve nuns, since they are lightly changed. He fears not undeservedly; for, as experience has taught us, some nuns have given their stewards, for their beneficence, a reward not praiseworthy. A woman’s mind is suddenly changed to bitterness. Yet, Christ granting it, I trust that he will be useful to you. [unclear] Therefore, recognizing womanly changeableness, I admonish you to remember to be constant, receiving him in holy honor and sincere charity, putting aside every levity of womanly mind. Especially help us and recover the state of temporal affairs, collapsed in your monastery. It would be of benefit, with Christ our redeemer, the zealous lover of chastity, helping. Farewell, 7 Ides. Johannes Trithemius.

*Method XXXIV. It similarly uses alternate words and shifts the first letter of each significant word to the fifteenth.*

Method 34, like the preceding methods, begins with one idle word and proceeds similarly by alternate words, so that the first is idle, the second significant, the third again idle, the fourth in turn significant, and so on successively to the end of the proposed narration. Further, for the first letter of each significant word there is taken the letter which, in the following alphabetical order, comes after it in the fifteenth place, so that for A, q is taken; for b, r; for c, s; and so on, as is clear in the alphabet that follows.

*Alphabet.* A-q, b-r, c-s, d-t, e-u, f-x, g-z, h-a, i-b, k-c, l-d, m-e, n-f, o-g, p-h, q-i, r-k, s-l, t-m, u-n, x-o, z-p.

*Hidden tenor.* Tomorrow at 9 the bailiff will come and take the peasants’ cows; keep yours at home.
