*The eighth method proceeds alternately by three empty words and the same number of significant words.*

As the sixth does by two, so namely that any single mystical word of equal syllables completes the meaning of the open narrative in such a way that, when three empty places have been left, the following one begins not from [unclear], but from three significant words. If the word of the secret has unequal syllables, it will receive [unclear] into its company, until the syllables before the period in some way at last become equal.

*Eighth example.*

O almighty, eternal God, most kindly rewarder of justice, who willed your Son to become a participant in our race, that he might redeem those long placed in most wretched perdition, overflowing with goodness alone, assuming our form incorrupt from the flower of the virginal womb, as the holy Archangel Gabriel made known; which Virgin, destined to conceive by the most blessed Spirit, was to remain a virgin forever, free from crime, more eminent than all men and angels, bore the almighty King, God and man: the most holy and most reverend Virgin Mary, wholly ignorant of male companionship, giving birth without pain, and always remaining such, most high and immaculate.
