*Of Steganography*

*Let us form letters at will.*

O most gracious Jesus, I give thanks to your most holy mercy: you who by your blood reconciled the human race to the heavenly Father, hanging piteously raised upon the Cross; you subjected for our sake the flourishing youth of your most noble body to harsh wounds, so that you might make dead man live. Therefore, let us Catholics give such thanks as we can to our redeemer: groaning and praising, recalling in Christian fashion the memory of the passion of the savior of our race. Have mercy on me, most merciful Jesus, fount of mercy, abyss of compassion, glory of the saints, light of the citizens above; and pour into me the sweetest taste of your most holy love. Deliver me from all my calamities. Give me true gladness of heart, that I may exult in your praises, glorifying you, since by the pain of your wounds you have healed me. What shall I render to your mercy, my Redeemer, my giver of life? I have compassion for your sufferings with burning tears, and, blessing the most holy wounds of your body, most gracious Savior Jesus, I give thanks, asking pardon for my crimes. In the year of the Lord 1500, on the thirteenth day before the Kalends of May, I wrote this prayer, *τυμβοὶ λαοῦ τε γῆ μου*.

When the person to whom letters, of whatever sort they may be, marked with the character of Narcoriel, have been sent receives them, being skilled in the art, let him observe the lord of the ascendant at that same hour, and write both it and the whole disposition of the heavens, with the rest, on a clean sheet; and when all necessary things have been added beneath according to custom, and the said conjuration has been spoken, when he sees that the spirits stand by him, let him say these words to the more powerful one: Medaro Castotiel va reneas thafny thyrmo thea penason. When these have been said according to the instruction of this art, immediately the chief spirit will come nearer and will disclose the secret word.
