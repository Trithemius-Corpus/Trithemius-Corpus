The ninth mode begins from three vacant ones with the eighth, and through as many significations alternately [unclear] concludes the individual mystical words before the period with an equal syllable, that is, it is concluded by three and three through as many idle ones; but the redundant ones, if two, are ended by two, and if one, by one similar vacant letter, in such a way, if you wish, that the following mystical word is always begun with three vacant ones. Of this let us set down the following example.

*The ninth example.*

Greeting and affection, most beloved and most singular friend, [bearer] of the present letters and more glorious, whom I ask you to receive humanely; he will set forth to you his necessity. Since I desire, as far as I can, to relieve here his old-time necessity and the defects pressing upon him, I should wish to be able to apply consolation to his misfortunes; but since, as is clearer than daylight to you, we are exhausted by heavy losses and, as is indicated, burdened by various debts, I am strongly confident in his zeal of piety, you who hold the force and bonds of friendship, that you would willingly show yourself humane to a friend, just as you have always been accustomed to be a helper of the needs of friends. And so I ask that you lend him, for a fixed term, two hundred florins, since he is placed in the greatest necessity, as he will inform you. I trust that you will restore all things with ready integrity after he has at last been moved by you. But if you do this, I give immortal thanks; act so that we may prove your sincere love toward us in the matter.
