*The ninth method uses all signifying words, and every letter is transferred to the ninth preceding one.*

*Alpha*

The ninth method of this preceding chapter, in concealing the proposed mystery, directs the arrangement or order through all the signifying words, and admits absolutely no empty word. But all letters in the secret narration are transferred to the ninth preceding letter, so that for d one takes o, which is ninth in the order of the alphabet before it, as appears plainly both in the wheel and in the alphabet. For b one takes p; for e, q; and so following. This method, like all the others, can be varied into many others, not only by alternate words, pairs, or triplets, but also by other arrangements so diverse that infinite methods can be made from any one method.

*Example IX.*

*Zealot of religion, Savior Jesus, most gentle of all, receive those invoking you;* [unclear] *free the weak and humble servants; pardon the suppliants their crimes; make blessed* [unclear] *calm disturbances; grant true humility to those zealous for the Lord’s salvation.*

*The tenth method uses individual signifying words, and every letter is transferred to the tenth preceding one.*

The tenth method of this chapter, in the manner of the preceding ones, uses individual signifying words in concealing the mystery, while no word at all is mixed in. But since it would be difficult, for the concealment of the intention or the congruity of the speech, to compose by individual words, you will be able, both in this and in almost all the other methods, to use alternate words, pairs, triplets, quadruplets, or even more. But since all letters are signifying, after the pattern of the preceding methods, in this tenth method they are taken by anticipation into the tenth preceding letter, so that for A one takes n, because in the order of the alphabet, according to the demonstration of the wheel, it is ten places before it; for b one takes o; for c, p; for d, q; and so consequently through the whole alphabet, as is clear to one looking at the middle circumference.
