Remember, I beseech you, what Christ our Lord cried out in the holy Gospel: Blessed are the pure in heart, that is, those who preserve purity of soul in a muddy body for God’s sake. Let purity of heart not be without continual and most humble prayers, since prayer purifies the mind, cools those who are burning, and drives far away the temptations of the devil. Hence the most holy Apostle Paul teaches you to persist in diligent and devout prayers, so that you may overcome harmful temptations. We join you in marriage, so that you may build up your lineage, propagated by decree of law, not so that you ought to overflow with harmful pleasures. For the pleasures of the flesh kill the soul and corrupt the noblest dignity of human substance [for] things to come. Therefore, mindful of these things, know that you are wayfarers who will return to the fatherland to which you have been called back. The present life is very brief, and the delight of the world comes most swiftly to an end. Therefore live in marriage in such a way that after death you may come to the kingdom of heaven with [exultation?]. Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the order of God’s father Benedict, issued [this] on the day before the Ides of March, in the year of the nativity of the Lord Jesus Christ 1500.

When these letters, or any others, are sent to someone, once the sign of the great Usiel has been recognized, let him act secretly as he knows from the art must be done; then let him turn toward the Subcircius and say the song: Usiel asoyr paremon cruato madufyn saueyr realdo chameron ilco paneras thurmo pean elsoty fabelrusyn iltras charson frymasto chelmodyn. When this song has been said, the spirit will show himself visibly and will report the things committed to him; if anything has been entrusted to him by that man, he will report it to the sender.

You now have, out of the hundred princes of the great Cabariel, twenty, and they are sufficient for you at present. The first ten of them are appointed for daytime operations with 500 servants, and the other ten for nighttime operations, likewise with their 500 attendants. And you should know that the spirits presiding over daytime operations are much more well disposed than the nighttime ones, and readier to obey, so that it is often necessary to compel the nighttime spirits themselves with a certain violence, and more often to repeat the conjuration. When commanded, they bring their servants with them; if they have not been ordered, they conceal them. When you wish to operate through the ministry of these spirits, turn yourself toward the region that we have mentioned and, after the customary preliminary things have been performed, say this conjuration:

Cabarielon ear chameron fruani, parnaton fofiel bryosi nagreal fabelrontyn adiel thortay nofruau pena afefiel chufy. When the song has been completed, if it is during the day, the spirit called will be present at once, without delay. But if it is at night and the spirit whom you have called delays in coming, press him as often as needed until he comes upon repetition of the song, which they are accustomed to revere greatly.
