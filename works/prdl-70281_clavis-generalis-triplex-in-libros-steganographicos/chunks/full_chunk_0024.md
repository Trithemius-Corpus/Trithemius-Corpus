*32. STEGANOGRAPHIA.*

[Dorothiel spirit table: Carba 40, Lomor 400, Paniel 40, Abael 400; Merach 40, Cesael 400, Curfaes 40, Meroth 400; Althor 40, Busiel 400, Aliel 40, Cadriel 400; Omael 40, Larfos 400, Maziel 40, Lobiel 400.]

Behold, you have, of the princes of Dorothiel, from each order—the daytime, namely, and the nocturnal—24 dukes, with the number of servants, ten thousand one hundred and sixty. And note that the operation through these is much different from the rest, as we shall say at large concerning the lord [spirit]. For one must operate in this mansion according to the 12 hours of the day and night, and always four princes preside over two hours, as much in the day as in the night: so that it is not permitted you to summon another except from the order which the hour concerns. So that, if the operation is in the first or second hour of the day, call whom you wish from the first four who preside over the day; if in the third or fourth, know that a duke is to be called from the second four. And so consequently it is to be done through all the orders and the 24 hours, as much in the day as in the night. When, however, you wish to operate in Steganography through the spirits of Dorothiel himself: the general [things] customarily premised in the art being premised, write whatever letters you wish, and, turning yourself to that quarter of the world which from the wind we commonly call Chorus [north-west], call one or more of the four who preside over the hour in which you operate. Dorothiel, cusi, feor, madylon, busar, pamersy, chearianothym, baony, Camersy, vlymeor, peatha, adial, cadumyr, reneat, thubra, Cohagier, maslon, Lodierno, sabeltrusyn. These [words] said, you will see the spirit appearing whom you called, prompt and cheerful to obey, to whom you may commit your secret securely and faithfully. Look at the preceding table.
