Then, when everything prescribed in the art has been completed, say the conjuration itself, taking the greatest care not to omit anything required for the perfection of the operation. When the conjuration has been spoken, the named spirits will at once appear visibly in the form which you ordered them to assume. For all whom we have named are benevolent, good, prompt, and willingly obedient to one who operates duly, and faithful in the tasks entrusted to them.

*Subject of the Secrets.*

Whatever secret occurs to us, which otherwise we cannot safely make known to a friend either by letters or by messengers, we first entrust to the spirits appearing to us, and we are secure. The more eminent one among the others is distinguished by the appearance of a crown.

*Let the letters be formed at will.*

Wail, mortals, you who now laugh: by your very grave sins and horrible crimes you have in many ways most ungratefully spurned the kindness also of our glorious redeemer. Behold, the Turk, the deadly enemy of Christians, will move his army from Hungary; he will slaughter men like beasts. Savage, kindled with zeal for impiety as if with fire, he has crossed the Danube. Therefore mourn, most wretched ones, doomed to die in horrible slaughter; you will die in bitter zeal, because your crimes will seize you. You have despised the laws of God, your most gracious redeemer. Therefore you will be handed over to most cruel enemies, who will devour you like locusts. We have heard how cruelly the Turk invaded Hungary, Bohemia, and Poland; he destroyed very strong cities; women and virgins consecrated to God, holding chastity in zeal for holiness, he most shamefully violated, horribly slaughtering all Catholic men, raging everywhere like cruel beasts.
