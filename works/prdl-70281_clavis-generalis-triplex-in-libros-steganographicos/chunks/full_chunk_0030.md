After his friend has received these letters, or any others whatsoever, let him be skilled in the art; when the sign of Raysiel is recognized, after the customary preliminaries have first been performed, let him turn toward the north and summon the spirit with this chant.

*Chant of Conjuration.*

[unclear] Raysiemyltran, fruano fiar chasmy clymarfo pean Sayr pultho chultusa medon veputsandly tusan axeyr affion. When these words have been spoken, the spirit sent will at once appear visibly, and will report to you each and every thing that I have entrusted to his ear. And I wish you to know that some spirits presiding over nocturnal operations do not willingly come at night to the operation unless they are compelled by the greatest sacraments and conjurations; yet after they have come and have been sent to a friend, they are accustomed quickly to appear and obey the one calling them, unless perhaps he himself, deceived by some occasion, has neglected something prescribed in the art.

*Chapter XIV.*

*Whose supreme prince is called Symiel, who dwells toward the north, having under him ten princes who preside over diurnal operations, with many servants; their office is to carry secret familiar communication.*

[unclear] The fourteenth station is toward the north, distant from the beginning of the north by 21 degrees and a little more; its supreme emperor is Symiel, who has under him ten dukes who preside over diurnal operations, with many of their servants. Over nocturnal operations many dukes preside, whose certain number I have not been able to discover; they also have many servants under them. The office of these is to announce among friends the most secret hidden matters, which are never to be made manifest to any men. But so that you may be able to work through them, if need be, I shall set forth to you ten of the diurnal and as many of the nocturnal, and they will suffice for the present.

You now have ten named princes for diurnal actions, with many sub-servants, and ten for nocturnal operations, also with many substitutes, through whom you will be able to operate safely and without danger, provided you are well instructed in Steganography. For whoever, not well instructed for the operation of this art, has presumed to approach its precepts will either accomplish nothing or will scarcely escape danger. For the princes do not obey everyone. But when you wish to work with this very science, after the things that must first be done have duly been done, turn yourself toward the north, and in the manner I have handed down to you, conjure the duke to come by means of this chant.

*Chant to the Spirits.*

Symiel myrno chamerony the or pasrô ãdiueal fanerosthi sofear Carmedon Charnothiel peasor sofitrã fabelrusy thyrno pamerosy trelno chabelron chymo churmabon, asiel peasor carmes nabeyros toys Camalthonty. When these words have been spoken in the manner necessary, the spirit summoned by you will come to obey you in all your commands, if the operation is to be carried out through nocturnal spirits; and if they do not appear quickly, do not on that account desist, but press them by repeating the chant, and, compelled, they will obey.
