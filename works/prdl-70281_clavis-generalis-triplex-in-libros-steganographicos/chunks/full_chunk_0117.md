*Example XXXI.*

S. D. Everyone who, with regard to temporal things, gives alms [unclear] by virtues, and before a transitory thing no one can be a lover of justice, since true charity divides [unclear] under no condition. Finally, holy religion, the Christian faith, forbids love of the world to its people, because he who loves worldly joys loses eternal happiness; a Catholic who does not live in Catholic fashion does not love Christ.

And he who loves temporal things is not said to be a good Catholic; he who loves the Creator rejoices in spiritual things. A lover of Christ despises all the vanities of the world, and does not care for the joys of wicked men. A lover of the world hates holy virtues; he cares nothing at all for the joys to come. [unclear] Christ [unclear] not the thought intent upon one thing which are not honorable. Do not, wretched men, I beseech you, do not do things contrary to religion; do not love the vanities of this world, soon to perish. Rather take care to love Christ, so that he may receive you into the heavenly kingdoms. Farewell. From Spanheim, 7 Ides of January, in the year 1499. Johannes Trithemius, abbot.

*Example XXXII.*

S. D. Almighty God, eternal creator of all things, who through your Son Christ redeemed us Christians, gave the zeal of love to Catholics through the light of grace, instilling piety in us who believe, and most generously gave your beloved Son as a man; free all of us believers from prisons, mourning under the power of the Tartarean one, destroying the most dark prison and conquering the [unclear].

What, O Christ, shall we Christians, weak men without works of justice, be able worthily to render to you? Professing the Catholic faith, therefore, most devoutly, we celebrate you, our redeemer, with the highest praises, saying: Praise be to you, eternal, most gracious savior of all, who redeemed us by your blood. Strengthen our frailty with the zeal of your love; save us, sweetest savior Jesus, and pour the zeal of faith into our hearts. Holy is your zeal; illumine with compunction for works the hearts of those who seek you, most gracious Christ Jesus. Most gracious Christ Jesus, eternal light, illumine the darkness of your servants with your accustomed goodness. Savior Jesus, save our souls, bestowing eternal joys. Christ, hear your humble servants, and pardon the sins of all who pray; save your Christian servants, though most unworthy; seal us with your zeal, we humbly ask. Amen. Farewell from Spanheim, 6 Ides of January, in the year of the Lord 1499. Joh. Trith.
