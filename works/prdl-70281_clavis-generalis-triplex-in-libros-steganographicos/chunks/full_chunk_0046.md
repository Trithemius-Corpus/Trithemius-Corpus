Here we have, from the 100 commanders of Icosiel, 15 named; but 50 sub-commanders, and 2,150 servants, who, together with their commanders, according to the order assigned to them in the 24 hours of day and night, are accustomed to come, when summoned by the operator, into the service of Steganography. They are suitable and willing for everything you may wish, provided only that you are perfectly instructed in the art, constant and undaunted in the operation; for they come in a serpentine form and likeness, having maidenly heads. Therefore, when you wish to operate through any one of them, do what must be done according to the art, and add this conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

ICosiel aphorsi chamersyn thulneas ianotiel menear peanos erasnotiel medurfan matory fabelron erfonial cathurmos laernoty beffaym alphayt lamedontinael cabelron. When the conjuration has duly been completed, the spirits summoned will be present without delay; to them you will be able safely to entrust your secret message for a friend.

Make the letters to be sent as you please. Let the secret be whatever you wish.

A friend of mine, far absent from me, is skilled in the art of Steganography; I have for him a great secret and hidden matter, which must not be entrusted to anyone, even by letters, nor even hinted at. Therefore, so that all things may remain secret, I send as secret messenger a Spirit, who is to reveal the secret to my friend and keep my business secret.

Let us amend our life, humbling ourselves before the highest maker of the universe. Humility obtains Heaven; it annihilates the harmful temptations of demons; it restrains the pernicious swelling of the mind, and powerfully exterminates all fires of vices. The path of humility deserves to receive a crown and to possess a noble kingdom. I establish for you as a path innocence and uprightness of conduct; by guarding it continually, you will not sin. This alone is the way for us to heap up the merit of blessedness: that, giving thanks to God, we fear him with a most upright heart, fleeing the tumult of the world; let us never grow weary of the exercise of humility, seeking the life above. Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Spanheim, writer, 10 Kalends of April, 1500.

When he to whom the letters are sent has received them, being skilled in the art, once he recognizes the sign of the prince, let him do what he knows must be done; then let him say the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

I Cosiel osayr penarizo chulti meradym phrael melchusy dayr pean cathurmo fabelron ersoti chamorusan iltham pedaly fuar melrosyn crymarsy phroyfon. When this conjuration has been completed, the spirit will be present to the one who calls, revealing the secret.
