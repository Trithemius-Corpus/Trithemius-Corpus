*Of the Steganography*

I shall devise letters of whatever kind I wish, which need fear no one, namely such as these.

[unclear]

Our Savior Jesus Christ, who, dying on the Cross, redeemed the human race by his blood, must be praised by us earnestly and without interruption. He himself will drive away death, and he has also promised that he will deliver eternal [life] to us who fear and love him. Now therefore let us rise up, dearest brothers, from our sins, setting our lamps in order with good works, so that we may joyfully meet the Redeemer in the hour of the fearful passage, when death will seize our souls. We now have abundant time for fulfilling penance and for adorning our lamps; but in the final hour of death the space for repenting further will be altogether denied us. Let us consider the very brief glory of the world, since it is vain: it passes away most swiftly, and we with it are corrupted every hour. Therefore let us humble our hearts at all times, never failing. In the year 1513, on the Kalends of April.

[unclear]

When the man to whom they are sent, skilled in the art, has received these or any other letters whatever, once the sign of Burci has been recognized, after the things that must be sent ahead have been sent ahead, let him inspect the earth and say the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Buriel, Thresoy chamerontis, hayr plassu, nadiel, marso, neany, pean, sayr, fabelron, chaturmo, melros, ersoty caduberosyn. When the charm has been spoken, a spirit invisible to all, but visible to you, will appear, and let him faithfully speak the secret entrusted to him.
