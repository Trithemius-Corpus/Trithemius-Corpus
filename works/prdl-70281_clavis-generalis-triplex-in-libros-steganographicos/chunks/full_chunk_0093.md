*On the first Angel of Saturn, who is principal, and is called Orifiel, who observes the first quarter of Saturn.*

[unclear]

The first and principal angel of Saturn, as we have said in the preceding sections, is called Orifiel; he, with the other angels of the planets in his order, rules the sphere for 354 years and four months. He has the first part both in the day and in the night; and through him the operation is performed from sunrise in the day, and from sunset in the night, up to the third hour inclusive, in all things that pertain to Saturn, and on his day and night, as we have said. Therefore, when in the first quarter of Saturn you wish to announce something to one absent without words and without letters, then no Spirit of Saturn other than Orifiel is to be summoned by you, because through him you will be able to announce everything most perfectly. First consider in what sign this Saturn is, and in what degree, and whether he is direct or retrograde, and with what planets he is in conjunction. Let us set down an example of the first point: Behold, Saturn is now in the 25th degree of Taurus, on the 28th day of the month of April in the present year, which is the year of the Lord 1500. Now therefore let us multiply 25, and they become 600; let us divide these by the four equal quarters, and there will remain to each quarter 150. Let us complete all the degrees of Saturn in the sign of Taurus, and there will be 30. But let us multiply 3 by 25 in all, and they become 750. Let us cast off the four superfluous degrees from the middle of the third quarter, and 650 will remain. Now let us take the point of Saturn’s motion from the table for the present day and hour; and we shall be able to know what is the rising, elevation, and setting of the Angel Orifiel with Saturn in the first quarter. Once these are known, our operation will be easy.
