*Mode XXXVIII. It uses alternating words in turn, and the first letter of the significant word is transferred to the 19th.*

Mode 38 proceeds in turn by alternate words, in the manner of the preceding ones, whose first letter of the significant word is transferred in order to the 19th following, so that for capital A, u is taken; for b, x; for c, z; and so consecutively, as is clear in the alphabet written below. *Mode XXXIX. It also uses alternating words, and the first letter of the significant word is transferred to the 20th.*

Mode 39 uses alternating words in turn, beginning from one vacant word, or after a preceding vacant one it appends one significant word, then again one vacant word and again a significant word, and so consecutively up to the end of the proposition. Furthermore, the first letter of each significant word is transposed to the 20th next following, so that for A, x is taken; for b, z; for c, a; and so consecutively, as is clear in the alphabet itself.
