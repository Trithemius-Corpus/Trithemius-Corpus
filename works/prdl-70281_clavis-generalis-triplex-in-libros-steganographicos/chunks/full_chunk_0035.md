*Song.*

*Carnesiel aphroys chemeryn mear aposyn. Layr peã noema ouear ma fere cralty caleuo thorteam chameron ianoar pe lyn Layr, baduson iesy melros ionatiel delassar rodiuial meron fauean fabelron clumarsy preos throen benarys sauean demosy non laernoty chamedonton.* When these things have been duly completed, the general messenger of secrets whom you have summoned will be present, ready, faithful, and utterly secret.

*I should like secretly to warn a friend about a bad man.*

There is a certain man, evil under the appearance of respectability, a prattler: knowing that I have influence with a friend, he asks me for letters of recommendation to him. I cannot refuse, nor do I wish my friend to be deceived fraudulently by him. I write the letters; I praise the wanderer wonderfully. He reads them, rejoices, and promises himself that an open path of treachery has been offered to him. I give him the letters; he goes off gladly. I call the spirit and send it to my friend, so that it may warn him and reveal my secret.

*I send letters of recommendation pleasing to the man who is recommended.*

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, sends greetings to Johannes Vigilius, lord Wackor, most distinguished Doctor of both laws.

I send the bearer of the present letters to you, *most beloved Vigilius*, a man indeed adorned with all virtues, esteemed most learned of all in the Scriptures, and graced with such charm of manners that I scarcely remember ever having seen with these eyes anyone like him: an excellent mathematician, most acute in philosophy, to be compared with the most learned men. In wisdom and study he surpasses all the most practiced Germans; hence he has rightly seemed to us worthy to be received with the praises of learned men and of all good men: for he is noble and most learned, able to be useful to all in handing down the various teachings of wondrous things. Farewell. From Sponheim, 15 days before the Kalends of April, in the year 1500.

When a friend proved in the art of Steganography has received these or any similar letters, once he has recognized the sign of Carnesiel, let him turn toward the east and, after the necessary preliminaries have been performed, say this conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

*Carnesiel aptysi chameron to pemalroyn phroys Cadur mearmol benadron Vioniel faviron army pean arnotiel fabelronthusyn throe chabelron sauenear medaloys vear olmenadab cralty sayr.* When the song has been spoken, the spirit sent, now visible, will appear and will reveal the secret faithfully entrusted.
