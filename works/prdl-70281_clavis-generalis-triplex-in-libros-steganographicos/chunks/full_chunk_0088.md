*Preface.*

The secret of this art is of such a kind, and wrapped in such great obscurities, that it is not easily accessible to anyone. For Menastor enveloped the mystery very obscurely and in few words, and he used such powers to keep it secret that after him no one has yet been found who has dared to make his discourse clearer by any instruction. But I, fearing that so marvelous a work, because of its excessive obscurity, might be despised by the unskilled, or at last burned in the fire, since I have long since tested it by many proofs, wished to commit it to writing in clearer letters, so that, with God's help, it might in some measure become accessible to learned men and to those most devoted to magical studies; and yet it should at all times remain hidden from unskilled Rapophagi, and in no way become known to their dull understanding.

But Menastor said: There are seven planets, over which seven angels preside, and under them are subject twenty-one spirits, through whom secrets are announced. Let us set out their names by means of a table.

*Preface.*

These are the seven angels, according to the tradition of the ancient sages, each of whom guides the world for three hundred fifty-four years and four months in his order. Orifiel is the angel of Saturn, who from the beginning of creation governed the world for 354 years and 4 months. Afterwards Venus, then Jupiter, after him Mercury, and then Mars, afterwards the Moon, afterwards the Sun. Each is described as having held dominion over the world for 354 years and 4 months, with his angel written in the table common to him. And so the sages report that at the time of the flood Samael, the angel of Mars, served the authority of rule; Gabriel, however, the angel of the Moon, at the time of the confusion of tongues; Michael, the angel of the Sun, at the time of Israel's departure from Egypt. And indeed, if anyone gives credence to the aforesaid assertion, he will easily be able by himself, from the origin of the world down to his own age, to calculate the changes of times and the governance of the angels in the order prescribed. But we, pursuing those things that serve our purpose, do not doubt that under the seven aforesaid angels of the planets there are subject twenty-one other spirits, that is, three to each one, according to the instruction of this art of ours; through them we accomplish the effect of our intention. We shall observe the customary order of the planets, beginning from Saturn, the highest of all, down to the Moon, first writing down the operation of each principal angel, and then of those serving under them, and joining the chapters together in order.
