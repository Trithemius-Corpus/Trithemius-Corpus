*Book One.*

*Conjuration.*

Armadiel marbeuo pelrusan neor chamyn aldron pemarson Cathornaor pean lyburmy Caueron thorty abesmeron ve-ar larso charnoty theor Caueos myat drupas Came dortys ly pa ruffes ertnory mesoryn elthy chaor atiel, lamesayn rouemu fabel rusin, friato chasalon pheor thamorny mesardiel pelusy madiel baseroty sarreon prolsoyr afenosy cameltruson.

When you have completed this conjuration in the required manner, the spirit will soon be present, ready for your command.

Let us form for ourselves, for the sake of example, such a secret as is to be entrusted to no one.

I have a certain secret for my prince, to be communicated absolutely to no one. I do not dare to entrust it to letters, lest through negligence or forgetfulness they be scattered and read; much less do I wish to announce it through a man, lest it somehow happen that it be revealed by him also to others. Therefore it is pleasing to test the mystery of this art, by which my secret will remain hidden at all times.

Let us write letters, of whatever kind one pleases, which need fear no one’s gaze.

To the most serene Prince, Lord Philip, Count Palatine of the Rhine, Duke of Bavaria, Arch-Steward of the Holy Roman Empire, Prince Elector, Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, offers service with prayers.

Most illustrious prince, with humble submission, my service. The kindness of your renowned dukedom commanded me to commit to writing certain most hidden matters that had been explained in words. I would rejoice, not without devout exultation, if powers equal to my will were present, to assign a more fitting and most honorable place to the benefits shown, and service by the grace due
