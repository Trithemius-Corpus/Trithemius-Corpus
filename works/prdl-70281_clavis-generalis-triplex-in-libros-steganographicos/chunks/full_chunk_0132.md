*The General Key of Steganography*

The sign 0, or the arithmetical [unclear], as often as it is set down, will indicate to you that many significant letters, and the number immediately following will signify that many significant letters.

The first mode is indicated thus: 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10 etc.
01 01 01 01 01 01 01 etc.
The second mode thus: 200 200 200 200 200 200 etc.
The third thus: 001 001 001 001 001 001 etc.
The fourth mode thus: 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 0001 etc.
The fifth mode thus: 1000 1000 1000 1000 1000 1000 etc.
The sixth mode: 10000 10000 100
The eighth mode thus: 20000 20000 20

The second general key is one that is made in this way, namely that the writer goes before and the reader follows, as in this example: “Have mercy, almighty God, on the souls of your servants,” etc.; that is: lhrdqdqđ nlmbrpsdmr edtr zmhlzatr ezlknqlstnqtl, etc. Since these letters, joined in this way, would signify nothing, so likewise through the preceding eight examples or modes, as is contained in our book.

The third and last general key is made thus: if you wished to write, “Have mercy, God,” etc., as above, you will do as follows: you will count all the letters of your meaning, and, however many there are, into that many parts you will divide them; for example, if you wish to divide two by three, for one part of the whole sentence set above you will have 45 letters. Once divided, 22 will come to one part; one remains, which you will always attach at the end to the rest. You will write them in the similar way indicated below; afterwards you will vary them by the rules stated above.

Mainsiemraebuers osmanmiuplotreuns iducourfu. And this verse is divided by two; by 3, however, it is made thus: Mefinaffme dnrcleuors reau onmitmnu raopbrouutsm. By 4, however, thus: Mi auipnlfoio et mr reanenbmrs ut edfu oesu muar nsmu. And thus by dividing we proceed up to eight parts; afterwards, for making the letters signify something to the reader, with other vowels inserted, you will have this by the preceding eight rules, proceeding in our book, except that you examine each conjuration at the beginning through each of our [unclear], which will soon be clear to your industry with this key. Therefore we ask that, for the salvation of our soul, you be willing to say the Lord’s Prayer.
