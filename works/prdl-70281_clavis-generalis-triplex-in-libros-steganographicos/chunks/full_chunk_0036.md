You now have twelve of the more principal leaders of Caspiel, with counts, subordinate leaders, and servants, 100,640, through whom every general operation of announcing toward the South is performed. But when you wish to announce something hidden to a friend dwelling toward the South, do what is required according to the art, and say the conjuration.

*Conjuration of the Spirit.*

Caspiel aloyr chameron noeres padyr diuiel prolsyn veat maduson cralnoti fruon phorsy larsonthon thiano pemarson theor. Caueos adeueos friato briosi panyeldrubon madiel sayr fabelrusyn gonear pean noty nabusran.

When the conjuration has been duly completed, you will see present the one whom you called, and ready to obey your commands in all things. All the leaders of this Caspiel are very benevolent and willing, but their subordinate leaders are somewhat harder; nevertheless they yield to conjurations, if the operator has been steadfast and undaunted, speaking to him harshly, as though with the greatest authority. For none of them is so hard that he does not become tame when compelled and adjured by the virtue of authoritative power.

*Book One.*

Let us posit some secret of whatever kind, which is to be entrusted to no one.

I have business with a friend which, if it passes into public knowledge, will bring great danger to both of us. All messengers are watched, so that letters may be read by those lying in wait for us. Therefore, that we may be safe and the business remain secret, I write letters that need fear no one’s inspection, and send them to the aforesaid friend with an invisible spirit accompanying them.

It does not matter of what kind the letters are, if they have been entrusted to the spirit.

Consider, mortals, the brevity and misery of the present life; do saving penance while you have time. Woe to you, most senseless lovers of worldly glory, rashly rejecting almighty God and eternal joy. Why do you not love those heavenly banquets which have been promised to those who humbly seek God? For you reject the humble [unclear], you seek the very brief joys of the world, you do not love the excellence of eternal blessedness; you live with contentious zeal, you stain honor with your actions, and you do not know the praises of the high God. Day and night you are intent on worldly actions and temporal profits; always stretched out in hunting after novelties, you neglect to hear readings that sharpen the memory; you do not care for the time of grace granted to you, nor for the expectation of eternal blessedness. Behold, the Judge, terrible to all criminals, draws near. Behold, omnipotent Majesty hastens in severity. Behold, the last judgment is appointed. Behold, eternal laments are set before us, and everlasting goods will be withdrawn from us. In the year 1500, the fifteenth day before the Kalends of April.

When he to whom these letters are sent has received them, let him send before those things which, according to the art, must be sent before, and, turning himself toward the South, let him say the song.
