*Chapter XXII*

*Whose prince is Buriel, nocturnal, shunning the light, and dwelling in lakes and in holes of the earth; and no operation is performed through him except at night.*

[unclear]

After Geradiel follows Buriel, a great prince indeed, but perverse and wicked, who hates, persecutes, and detests all the other spirits, especially the princes; but he is no less held in hatred by them. He fears and flees the light with all his dukes and servants, and is never accustomed to come except at night, often with great horror and fear for the operator, especially if he is not perfect in the art, steadfast and strong in spirit. And he often appears in the form of a serpent, having a maiden’s head, but a serpentine tail and whole body, hissing terribly. When adjured in the proper manner, he speaks in human words. He has under him dukes and princes, whom he is accustomed to send into service together with their attendants and servants, whose multitude is almost innumerable. From these let us set down the names of some who may suffice for us for the operation.
