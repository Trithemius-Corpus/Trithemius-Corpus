*Book Two.*
*Chapter XV.*
*Whose hour is called Quabrion, and its supreme angel is called Serquanich, having under his rule many princes, dukes, counts, and servants without number.*

[unclear]
The third hour of the night is called Quabrion, whose emperor and supreme angel is Serquanich, having under his rule many princes, dukes, and counts, distinguished through twelve orders, with a great continual company of servants. In the first order there are 10; in the second, 20; in the third, 30; in the fourth, 40; in the fifth, 50; in the sixth, 60; in the seventh, 70; in the eighth, 80; in the ninth, 90; in the tenth, 100; in the eleventh, 1000; in the twelfth, 10000. From these, from whose order, by turns, sufficient dukes, counts, and servants are called and sent into the service of various experiments.

Here now we have named twelve of the more powerful spirits of Serquanich, from whose order one, according to their turns, receives the dignity both of dukes and of counts. The remaining six never change their office, but are always dukes.
