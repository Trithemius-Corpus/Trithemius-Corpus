All these are sent, by their turns and ranks, into the service of this art; among them are dukes and counts, with their servants, very many, whose names it is not easy to set forth, nor indeed is it necessary, since it is enough to know the names of a few, and the operator in the art can always, from the report of one spirit, know more names of others. And note that, according to Raziel, all these spirits form and transform themselves according to the will of the operator, so that in whatever form he wishes to see them, he should command it before the Conjuration in the manner prescribed by the art, and they will obey him most promptly in all things. *Conjuration.*

Bariel mylan theory madruson alfayr dreschym taparoys mear moas layr penason. When the preliminaries have been put first, pronounce that conjuration as is proper, and at once the spirits called, whom you have called, will appear; entrust to them whatever secret you have.

*The appointment of any secret whatever.*

We have a secret for a friend, or for anyone else skilled in our art, which we cannot signify either by letters or by a messenger, because we fear danger in either case. Therefore we call a spirit, entrust the secret to the stronger one, and in all things we are safe.

*The tenor of the letters at pleasure.*

Ioannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the Order of Saint Benedict, to Brother Quirinus, a monk of Corbie in France, greeting.

Concerning the mother of the perpetual Virgin Mary, I send my prayer, which you recently requested, Quirinus: Hail, most gracious mother Anna, most radiant parent of the Virgin Mary; you most faithful helper of all who call upon you; you noble refuge of those who seek you; quiet benefit of holiness. Hail, most gentle advocate of the poor; most beautiful consoler of those who groan; most adorned storehouse of virtues. Hail, [Iuda?], restorer of the sorrowful; most healthful medicine of the sick; and most celebrated promoter of faithful Christians. Obtain pardon for us as we pray; soften Christ, the dread judge; intercede for your servants, that we may attain blessedness; with you interceding, we ask that in his kindness he receive us miserable ones. Amen. The eighth day before the Ides of April, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When the person skilled in the art to whom the letters are sent has received them, let him first attend to the character of the hour attached to the letters; when this has been recognized, let him work through the lord of the ascendant of the hour in which he received the letters, together with the other things prescribed in the art. Then let him add the prayer written above, in the manner and order in which it is proper, and immediately the spirits sent will appear. When you have seen them, these words are to be spoken to the chief one among them: chamerusin maslotiel vear reneas liernoty trismy penafon. When these words have been spoken to the spirit stronger than the others, who is easily recognized from his habit and crown and always precedes the others, he will immediately reveal the secret into the ear.
