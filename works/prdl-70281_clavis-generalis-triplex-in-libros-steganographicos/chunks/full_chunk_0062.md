*Book Two.*

Fool, what are you doing? The time is at hand for correcting these negligent acts of yours more swiftly, zealously, according to justice; and unless you hasten humbly to us to break the zeal of bitterness by the scourges of humiliations, we are compelled by office to be zealous against all the things you are doing that are seditious against the faith and Catholic morals. Farewell from Spanheim, the day before the Kalends of April, in the year 1500.

When he to whom the letters are sent has received them, being skilled in the art, once the character of Sasquiel has been recognized, and the things customarily to be sent ahead have first been sent ahead, let him pronounce the aforesaid conjuration over it; and immediately the spirit sent will appear and present himself visibly. When he has been seen, let him at once say these words: Fabelmerusyn aueual vear cralti penafon. When the said spirit has heard these, he will immediately approach and reveal the secret.

*Chapter VI.*

*Whose Hour Is Called Genapherym.*

Its supreme emperor spirit is called Saniel, who has under him 100 dukes, with many servants.

The sixth hour of the day is called Genapherym, and its angel is Saniel, great and powerful in his operations; he has under his command 10 dukes, 100 counts, and 5,550 servants assigned to the service of various operations. These suffice for us for everything that we may wish to perform through Steganography in this hour.

The dukes, however, are more rarely summoned, although they are ready and very willing to obey our commands, because their counts are accustomed for the most part to carry out their duties, since they are often occupied in other magical experiments.
