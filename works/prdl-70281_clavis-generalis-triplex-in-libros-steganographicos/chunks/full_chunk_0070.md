*Book Two.*

...despises pleasures and does not care for glory and unstable riches. But the lover of the world hates the virtues; he cares nothing for future joys. Alas, what are you doing, Christians, intent on vain things? You are thinking things that are not honorable and will do things contrary to religion. Do not, I beseech you, do not love the soon-to-perish vanities of this world. Take care rather to love Christ, so that he may receive us into the heavenly kingdoms. Ioan. Tritt. Abb.

When one skilled in the art has received this foregoing little prayer, or any others whatever, confirmed by the character of Berathiel, let him diligently do everything that is prescribed in the art, and let him say the conjuration. And when he has seen that the spirits, who will come without delay, are present, let him say to the more powerful one, who advances crowned: Famerusyn melyfno alny vemoby dreary drymes charfony. When these words have been spoken, the spirit will come nearer and will faithfully tell the secret into the ear of the operator. But he must be steadfast; they harm no one well instructed in the art. If, however, anyone of the unskilled should presume to approach, he would not escape danger.

*Chapter XIII.*

*Whose hour is called Omalharien; its supreme angel is named Sabrathan, who has under his command many princes, dukes, counts, and servants.*

The first hour of the night is called Omalharien; its angel is named Sabrathan, who has many other spirits under him, serving at various times in magical operations according to their orders, as Hermes and Solomon the Jew, most experienced in magic, attest. From these, many have been deputed in turn according to their orders for the operation of our art. For in the first order there are 10; in the second, 20; in the third, 30; in the fourth, 40; in the fifth, 500; in the sixth, 600; in the seventh, 70; in the eighth, 80; in the ninth, 90; in the tenth, 100. All these are sent into the service of secrets in their turns and hidden orders, about which we have neither full knowledge nor do we speak.

Here now we have from the spirits of Sabrathan 5 dukes and 10 named counts, who have 2,000 servants deputed to themselves in their turns and orders. Therefore, when you wish to work through these, observe the lord of the ascendant, and write its character, with the other necessary things, on a clean sheet, and this conjuration on the back.

*Conjuration.*

Sabrathan odiel melros rhupis othia elroz adiel methiel mear nasutiel lafian irsoti brestion dreor chamerson. Then, after the things customarily to be sent ahead have first been sent ahead, as soon as you have said the conjuration written above, you will see the summoned spirits present; with them do whatever you wish, according to the precepts of the art.

*The Secret Is Done at Will.*

It will be a great secret and an arduous secret, which would do harm if made public; and for this reason it must be entrusted neither to letters nor to a notary, but must pass into the service of the spirits who preside over the hour in which you are working. It is not safe to summon spirits in trivial causes; but where human services fail, let theirs be sought in the art.
