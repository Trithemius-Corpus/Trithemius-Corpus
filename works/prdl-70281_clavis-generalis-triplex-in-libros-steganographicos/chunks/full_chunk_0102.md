The keys of the Steganography can all be varied with manifold diversity, according as it shall have seemed suitable to any diligent person for the concealment of his secret; but now let us set down below an example of this sort.

*Seventh Example.*

[unclear]

Unfailing light, blessedness of all the saints and creator [unclear], I.C. [unclear] of the universe and ruler, have mercy on us. We, weak, groaning, and thirsting for your mercy, ask you: grant us the strength to overcome the temptations of the Devil, so that, after hard labors for you, we may deserve rest. O sweetest Savior, I pray, look upon your humble servants and useless servants; be merciful to us, since we desire with all our longings to serve you. Make us have the favor of your love always burning within us. Give us the strong bond of brotherly charity. Jesus, almighty King of heaven, make us truly humble and vigorous despisers of worldly things, so that, always burning with love for you, we may love nothing here and regard nothing temporal as pleasant to us. O teacher of humility, most gentle Jesus, teach us to imitate your doctrine; for we are subject to various evils, intent on few goods. Save us, Lord, lest we perish. O supreme truth, give us life; sweet refuge of souls, be to us a tower of strength. For you are our strongest virtue; you are the salvation of my soul and the most healthful refreshment of my mind; you are the ineffable joy of the angels and the rest of all the faithful. You are the holy gladness of those who love you. You are the inextinguishable name of the citizens above. You are the giver of life to the dead and the most merciful sustainer of the frail. You, sweetest Jesus, help us, your frail servants, so that we may deserve to walk along the way of truth on the right path without offense. O most loving Lord Jesus, mitigate my very hard exile; and when the end of this life of mine begins to draw near, deign mercifully to come to our aid then, since [unclear], and fleeing far away all those who intend evils, they will soon be destroyed, since you are our protector. We ask by the virtue of your most noble death, sweetest one: do not despise your servants on the day of our departure, but may Jesus the Lord redeem and wash each of us with your blood, and may all [unclear] of our transgression graciously grant us, as we wait, the heavenly joys of the celestial homeland. Amen.
