Therefore, when you wish to work through these angels, prepare your paper, as is customary; and, turned toward the Eurus, write whatever narrative you wish, in whatever language you wish, which need not be concealed. When it has been completed, read the following conjuration silently: Camuel aperoys, melym meuomanial, casmoyn cralty busaco aeli lumar photirion theor besamys, aneal Cabelonyr thiamo vesonthy. When this conjuration has been completed in silence, give your commission to the spirits who are present, as you know; send them forth, doing everything in silence and without commotion, if it can be done. Afterwards give the letters, to be carried through, to whatever messenger you wish, open, so that he may go freely, because the spirits will not neglect their duty, whether the messenger comes quickly or late; of this let there be no doubt for you, for they are faithful. But you must know which spirit from those named above you wish to send, so that in the conjuration you express his name immediately after the principal one; this applies in all operations in which the names of the angels to be sent are set down in the chapter itself. But now let us set down an example of what we have said concerning those things that pertain to the office of Camuel and of his subject spirits.

*Roth.*

*Let This Be The Most Secret Intention Of My Mind,* which I wish an absent person to know through the aforesaid spirits. When I am situated at Rome, I understand from the most secret counsel of the supreme pontiff that he wishes to transfer the Roman Empire itself to the Franks. I desire to dare [unclear] the princes of Germany: by messengers I cannot, since I do not trust them; by letters I cannot, because in all the cities of Italy there are men appointed at the gates who examine passers-by most exactly. Therefore I write one devout prayer, which I send open by a messenger, along with other familiar letters closed, to the same princes. I constrain the spirits of the third mansion and command them to follow the letters to be sent. And when they have been summoned by the recipient, they are not to fail to reveal to him my secret intention, which I did not dare entrust to letters. They come, they go, they obey. We all die, because we have all contracted the hereditary right [unclear] of original guilt; and no one lives who is not going to die.
