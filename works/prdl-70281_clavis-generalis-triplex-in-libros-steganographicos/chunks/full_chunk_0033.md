*50. STEGANOGRAPHIA.*

…[with your] face placed toward the south-east, call him whom the hour concerns, observing most diligently the letters of the servants, without which they nowhere proceed; and beware lest you err in the number, order, [or] lordship of them.

*Conjuration.* Baruchas, maluear, chemorsyn, charnotiel, bason, ianocrimedusyn, aprilty, casmyron, fayr, pean, cauoty, medafon, peroel, chamyrsyn, cherdiel, auenos, nofear, penaon, fayr, chauelon, tigenayr, pamelron, frilcha, madyrion, onetiel, sabelronthos. This conjuration completed in silence as is fitting, soon there will be present one principal spirit, whom you called, with the servants deputed to him according to the series of the time. For example: It is committed to me to survey the orders [and] to amend [and] correct in the monasteries [things] wrongly done. Hence to a friend, one of the monks, far absent, I desire to send such a secret as, [if] published, would bring upon him prison and the hatred of [his] pastor, and upon me [and] the order and the common good, detriment. Whence [it is] neither to be entrusted to letters, nor to be committed to any man. That we may both be secure, I summon the minister of the art, a spirit; I commit the secret.

*I transmit such letters as I wish, which I will not refuse to be read by all.*

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the order of Saint Benedict, to the religious brother Nicholas of Dürckheim, monk of the monastery of Hirsau of the same order: sincere charity in the Lord.

Since I know, dearest brother, that you are a lover of the good arts, it delights me greatly to exhort you that you would persevere in inquiring into and continually unrolling the secrets of books, which show us the way of felicity—to return by an open path into the homeland which by sinning we lost, to which the love of our most mild [and] most merciful Savior, hidden for us in the holy Scriptures, will lead us back, burning [with desire], if [these secrets] adorn the mind, exercising us in holy study—[a thing] without doubt to be conferred.
