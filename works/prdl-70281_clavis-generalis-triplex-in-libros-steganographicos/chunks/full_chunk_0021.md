*Of the Steganographia*

*Roth. Schw. R. S. X. S.*

Arach 30, Sarmiel 30, Baros 30, Rabiel 30; Maras 30, Amoyr 30, Eliel 30, Atriel 30; Noguel 30, Badiel 30, Paras 30, Saluax 30.

You have, indeed, twenty-four of the thirty principal spirits of Maseriel, with seven hundred and twenty servants; of these, the first twelve preside over operations by day, and the remaining twelve over those by night. Therefore, when you wish to accomplish something in Steganography through them, turn yourself toward the west wind, and, after first performing the necessary things required by the art, call whichever one you wish from those named above, as is fitting, and he will come without delay. The conjuration: Maseriel bulan lamodyn charnoth iabrun caresathroyn afulroy beuefy Cadumyn turiel bufan Seuear; almos lycadufel ernoty panier iethar care pheory bulan thorty paron Venio Fabelronthufy. When the spirit whom you have called appears, entrust your secret to him to be carried through safely, hesitating not at all, speaking silently to him, if you are among others, as you know.

*Roth.* Let the secret of your mind be such. [unclear]

You have secrets in the occult sciences, philosophy, the magic of the stars, arithmetical Cabala, or any arts whatsoever, which you desire a friend, absent near or far, to know; but they are such that it is not safe to entrust them either to letters or to messengers; or certainly that he should send secrets to you, [unclear]; or even to commend a philosopher, yet so that he not know those of others. Call one or two of the above-mentioned spirits; set forth the secret; he accepts the burden, will carry it to the friend, and will report back to you what has been entrusted by him.

*Roth.* Write whatever prayer you wish, or any letters you like, containing nothing secret.

Almighty, everlasting God, most just rewarder of the good, who willed your Son to be a partaker of our race, that he might redeem us most wretched from devilish envy; who, by kindness alone, overflowing, took on the uncorrupted form of our [nature] from the flower of a virginal womb, the holy archangel Gabriel announcing that the Virgin, about to conceive by your most blessed Spirit, would remain a perpetual Virgin, immaculate, brighter than men, more eminent than spirits.
