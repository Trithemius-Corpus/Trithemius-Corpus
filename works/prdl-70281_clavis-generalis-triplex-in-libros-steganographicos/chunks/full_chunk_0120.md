*Example 36.*
Catholic faith does not save [unless] it is supported by the ardor of the divine name. Believe also that zeal for the love of God is by no means entirely useless here for the redemption of the soul, for many people are now found boasting in the Catholic faith, but few are performing the works of the Christian faith. Without works faith dies; they believe a great deal, but very few observe God's commandments. O weak zeal of faith, which stronger unbelieving men could have by natural uprightness; the Gentiles who do not believe that the Savior of the world was made the Son of God and made man through zeal for human redemption will be tormented by the pains of hell. But they do not in the least adorn the Christian faith with most holy works, and, always entangled in sin, put [unclear] honors before eternal blessedness, staining the purity of faith with impious actions and depraved morals against obedience. But the lovers of God who have the right zeal of the Christian faith, setting nothing before Christ, will have him as an appeased judge, and he will give them everlasting honor. [Unclear], therefore, from these discourses, you who boast of faith: love God and you will have the reward of blessedness of those who are zealous for righteousness. If you love the vanity of the world against the honor of God, you will receive the reward of those who are zealous for iniquity: the horrors of hell. Johannes Trithemius wrote this on the fourth day before the Ides of January, in the year 1500.
