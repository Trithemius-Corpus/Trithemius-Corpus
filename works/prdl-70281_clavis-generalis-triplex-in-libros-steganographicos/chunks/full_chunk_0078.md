Without doubt it is promised to those who, in view of the eternal rest to come, despise the temporal pleasures and joys of the present life, and who love the eternal God. We know that the best rewards have been promised to the lovers of God; but for the lovers of the world and reprobate Christians God has prepared the most bitter punishments of hell. *Chapter XIX.*

*Whose hour is called Venaydor, and its angel is called Mendrion, having under his rule princes, dukes, counts, and ministerials almost innumerable.*

The seventh hour is called Venaydor, whose angel and supreme Spirit is said to be Mendrion, who has under his rule many other Spirits, as princes, dukes, counts, and ministerials, who are appointed to diverse operations in magical matters. Of these, for Steganography and its operations in the seventh hour of the night, there are appointed by their turns and orders princes, dukes, and counts with their servants, many in number. In the first order there are 10; in the second 20; in the third 30; in the fourth 40; in the sixth 60; in the seventh 70; in the eighth 80; in the ninth 90; in the tenth 100; in the eleventh 1000; in the twelfth 10000. All these are called by turns and come to the operation.

Here we have, from the twelve orders of the Spirits of Mendrion, the supreme angel of the seventh hour of the night, 12 dukes and 6 counts, who suffice for us at this time for every operation, together with their subordinate servants. These Spirits come, as Hermes the Hebrew and Raziel the Arab testify, in whatever form they have been ordered by him who works according to the art. But he who has not been perfected in our art [unclear]. But when you wish to work something through these Spirits in the seventh hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant and the figure of the whole heaven, which you are to write, together with the rest prescribed in the art, on clean paper, and on its back this conjuration.
