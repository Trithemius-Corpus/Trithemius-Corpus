*Conjuration.*

A Menadiel aprolfy chameronta nostroy throen mestro salayr chemaros noe pean larsy freueon ionatiel pelroyn rathroy Cafer malusan pedon Cranochyran daboy phernotuel Cabron. *A matter of mine is very secret, necessary for a friend.*

I have a certain secret for a friend, which I do not entrust to be carried either by a man or by letters, since if it came into public view it would create great danger and loss for me. I cannot endure delay; therefore I call a spirit, I write letters in no way at all suspect, which indeed he himself is not thought to understand, as being ignorant of the Latin tongue; I send the spirit, and he reveals the secret.

*I send letters of whatever sort, to be accompanied by an invisible spirit.*

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, to the noble and valiant knight Albert Goeler of Ravensburg, prefect of Kreuznach, greetings.

We believe that our Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, will come to judgment, who will render to each one according to his works. This we believe, yet we, wretched men, neglect ourselves. And so let us strive to come before his face with continual tears, correcting the negligences that we have committed, lest that terrible coming of our Redeemer overtake us unprepared; we shall all necessarily meet him, to receive the reward of evils. Why, then, do we now despise the season of salvation granted to us, so useful to us and so acceptable, which the Almighty has bestowed? Why do we not emulate men distinguished in conduct, who left us examples of upright life, honorable humility, steadfastness, and all virtues? Let us, emulating their name, do penance, since the limit of our living is at hand. Horrible death will come, which knows not how to spare, but will devour young men and maidens, and old men with the younger. Let us live in mourning, washing our transgressions with continual tears, lest death, seizing us unexpectedly, submit us to a horrible judgment. Let us amend the life lived in the past, praying God that after the brevity of our present sojourn he may grant us eternal blessedness. Amen. Farewell. Given from Sponheim, the 14th day before the Kalends of April, in the year 1500.

When you have received these letters, or any others entrusted by me to a spirit, the friend to whom they are sent, being approved in the art, and having recognized the sign of the prince of this region, after first performing what must be performed beforehand, should say the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

A Menadiel bulurym chamerym erisco ha pedarmon flusro pean truarbiel fabelron greos belor malgoty nabarym stilco melros fuar pelatyso chitron amancason. When this conjuration has been completed according to the proper manner, the spirit, otherwise invisible, will appear visible and will reveal the secret.
