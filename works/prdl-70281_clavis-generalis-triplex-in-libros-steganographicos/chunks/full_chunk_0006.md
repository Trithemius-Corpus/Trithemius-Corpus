*The author's protestation or oath concerning the innocence of this art, etc.*

And lest any future reader of this work, when in the course of it he has often encountered names, offices, orders, differences, properties, prayers, and any other operations of spirits, through whose intelligences all these secrets of this science are closed and opened, should believe or think me a necromancer and magician, or that I have entered into a pact with demons, or that I have used, or use, any other superstition: I have considered it necessary and opportune, by vindicating it with truth, to preserve my reputation and name from so great a stain, injury, fault, and blemish by a solemn protestation in this prologue.

Therefore I say before Almighty God, from whom absolutely nothing can be hidden, and before Jesus Christ, his only-begotten Son, who will judge the living and the dead; in truth I say, swear, and protest: all and every one of the things which I have said in this work, or am about to say, and all the properties, modes, figures, operations, receptions, formations, inventions, institutions, mutations, alterations of this science or art, and all things whatsoever which pertain to its speculation, invention, attainment, practical operation, either in part or in whole, and everything contained in this volume of ours, rest upon true, Catholic, natural principles; and all and every one of them are done with God, with a good conscience, without injury to the Christian faith, with the integrity of ecclesiastical tradition, without any superstition, without idolatry, without any explicit or implicit pact with evil spirits; without suffumigation, adoration, veneration, worship, sacrifice, or offering to demons; without any fault or sin, whether venial or mortal.
