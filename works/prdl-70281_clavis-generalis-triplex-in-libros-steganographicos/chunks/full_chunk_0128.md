*70. The Key of Steganography of Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim.*

*The eleventh method, like the preceding one, uses single signifying [items], and the letters are advanced by eleven.*

The eleventh method proceeds, in concealing the mystery, [like?] the preceding ones, with single so-called signifying entities, to which no idle one at all is to be mixed in. Furthermore, although all are immediately transposed into the eleventh preceding one, as is clearly apparent to one looking at the alphabet in the little wheel.
