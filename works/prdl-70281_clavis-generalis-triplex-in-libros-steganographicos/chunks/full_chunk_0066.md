*Book Two.*

Here now we have named 5 dukes and counted 10 counts, and 650 from among his assigned servants, who will suffice for us. But when, in the ninth hour of the day, you wish to work through these spirits, observe the lord of the ascendant, and write on clean paper those things that are prescribed according to the instruction of the art, and on its back the conjuration.

Quabriel, Odiel, Amear, Cayn Alco Mean Chyr Pareos Payr Peray, Thubro Menafry. Then, after the things usually to be sent ahead have first been sent ahead, say that conjuration itself, as is prescribed in the art; and at once the spirits called will be present, in whatever form you wish, appointed and faithful for all things. Entrust the secret to the more powerful one.

*The Theme of the Secret.*

A secret of whatever kind comes to us for a friend, which we wish to be most secret and in no way revealed to anyone except him. Therefore let us call one or two from the aforesaid dukes and counts, according to the instruction of the art, and let us entrust the secret to the more powerful one.

Let us form letters at pleasure.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, of the Order of Saint Benedict, to the religious father Johannes Hugo, guardian of the Friars Minor of Saint Francis in Kreuznach, greeting and charity.

Reverend and venerable father in religion, I know that you gladly grant suitable things to all friends; therefore I humbly beseech you, that in view of Catholic piety you not take it ill that your [unclear] brothers, detained rather long with us with the greatest charity, [unclear]; for it was necessary that they remain, at my entreaty. It was a glorious joy for us, while we all rejoiced without offense. We exulted in the Lord with spiritual joy, giving thanks to almighty God with good zeal. What gathering, I ask, is more religious than such a one? where nothing idle is said, nothing unjust is pursued with zeal; may Christ Jesus, the savior of all, make us rejoice in everlasting glory.

Glorious zealots of the virtues place nothing before truth. Filled with holy joy, we pray that you receive the aforesaid brothers peacefully and lay aside every zeal of bitterness. Farewell from Sponheim, 3 Nones of April, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When you, being skilled in the art, have received these letters or any others marked with the character of Quabriel, after the things to be sent ahead have first been sent ahead, say the prescribed conjuration. When it has been said, you will soon see the spirit sent; and when you have seen him, say these words: Mestaym Paslotiel Vear Reneam Cralty Thio Phroysy Ma Mear Hamorty. When these have been said, the spirit who has been sent, approaching peacefully, will tell you the secret entrusted to him.

*Chapter 10.*

*Whose hour is called Lamarhon, and its supreme emperor-spirit is called Oriel, who has under his rule many dukes, counts, and servants.*

The tenth hour of the day is called Lamarhon, and its supreme angel is called Oriel, a great and powerful emperor, who, according to Solomon the Jew, has under his rule many dukes and counts who, together with their attendants, are assigned to the operation of Steganography. In the first order there are 10, in the sixth 30, in the fourth 40, in the fifth 500, in the sixth 600, in the seventh 700, in the eighth 800, in the ninth 900, in the tenth 1000. But we, taking only 10 counts from the first five orders and only five dukes from the last, are content in our operations. If it pleases anyone to know the names of more, let him call one of those named in the table and question him; and he will hear whatever he wishes, and however many he wishes to know, he will know them perfectly from him.
