*Of Steganography.*

When the mean motion of Saturn for the second day, degree, and hour has been learned from the common tables, know that it must first be carefully observed whether it is direct or retrograde; pure, proper, mixed, or confused. But you will not find this from the common tables. For that reason we have arranged the prescribed table, in which you will be able to find the point of the beginning and [end?] of its motion at any hour, the degrees being divided, with intervening [unclear], into particles of minutes. That is, each minute is to be divided into two, three, or four hourly parts, according to the proportion of the pure or confused motion, that is, with the conjunction or separation of the other planets observed. For it is impossible, either through the stars or through the spirits of the stars, for you to arrive at the effect of this art unless, with the knowledge of the punctual motion most diligently observed, you know how to order your operations precisely according to the proper qualities of the planets. For in different degrees, signs, days, hours, minutes, seconds, thirds, and fourths, the effects of the planets, with respect to this science, vary in different ways. For in each degree we have observed and found twenty-five different changes; hence it seemed necessary to us to divide each degree into just as many principal parts. This division, however, does not apply throughout all the degrees, but only in those in which the stars conducive to this art rise for us, with which the operation is performed for signifying to the absent everything that we wish, by the ministry of spirits, without letters, as we shall say. Therefore the first table of the division of the degrees of the motion of Saturn, which we have set forth above, serves perpetually for the first quarter of the day or night in those things which pertain to the operation of Saturn and its beginnings; the second, however, for the second three hours; the third for the third; the fourth for the fourth. Then we have added the punctual order of the motion of Saturn itself in the ascendant, namely when it shall have been in one of the first four signs, which are Aries, Taurus, Gemini, and Cancer. We shall also arrange a following table concerning the remaining eight signs.
