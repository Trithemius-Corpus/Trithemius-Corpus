*Chapter XVI.*

*Whose hour is called Ramerzy, and whose angel, the supreme emperor, is called Iefischa, who has under his command many princes, dukes, counts, and ministering spirits.*

The fourth hour of the night is called Ramerzy, whose angel is named Iefischa, having under his command many princes, dukes, counts, and ministering spirits; these, like those whom we described in the preceding chapter, are distributed by turns into twelve orders.
