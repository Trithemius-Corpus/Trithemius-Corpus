But let someone object to me, saying: If you wanted this knowledge of yours to be hidden, why did you wish to commit it to writing? To him I say, if an answer must be given, that I neither ought nor could hide this knowledge from the most excellent prince, by means of which he may, if occasion arises, be able to free his dependants, of whose number I am one, from many dangers. For by means of this knowledge he can make known the conception of his mind to his own people, to whomever he wishes, secretly or openly, securely, without in the least fearing anyone's knowledge to the contrary. But whoever wishes to write anything by this art should diligently observe the methods, signs, and proportions of each thing; if he neglects to consider these, he himself will easily be able to forget what he has written. But now, with the Lord's help, let us proceed to the demonstration.

*Method*

*The First Method of Writing Secretly is Carried Out by the Individual First Letters of Individual Words; Its Sign Is This: I.*

The first, then, of the methods for writing secretly is such as this: when you wish to write something secretly to a friend, so that no one understands your intention except the one to whom you are writing, then take any Latin narrative you wish which need not be hidden, arranging all its words in such an order that the first letter of each word yields the word you wish, just as I shall teach you in the argument or example placed below. Yet this device is not sufficiently safe, because it could easily be detected by an industrious man, [unclear]. In reading, indeed, it is easy, but it is written with difficulty, because it is laborious always to have at hand words suitable to the proposition.
