*Book Two.*

*The secret may be of whatever kind.*

Concerning the announcing of a secret to a friend, absent near or far, who is already skilled in this art of spirits, it matters little what sort it is, provided it is not slight, or such as could otherwise have been safely announced by messengers or letters. We are also not concerned what the letters are like, provided they have the character of the principal angel of that hour in which the operation is performed: no secret at all.

*Let us form letters at pleasure.*

*Johannes Trithemius, Abbot, sends greeting to Nicolaus Basselius, monk, and transmits this prayer of Saint Nicholas.*

Creator of all things, reformer and maker, created the first man, Adam, to whom he joined a very beautiful wife named Eve, the mother of all living things, prefiguring the Catholic Church, to be joined to shepherds, shining beautifully with the morals of uprightness, and destined to bear sons of good works, men who by humility obtain the kingdoms of heaven that will remain without end. Christ redeemed us through his most kindly love for us; he gave us the Catholic faith, and those who truly love it will rightly obtain angelic happiness. Therefore let us all, living in true religion and living as Catholics, daily honor our almighty creator. Lord Jesus, most kindly savior, who mercifully redeemed us all from the abyss of death, kindly regarding our frailty and weakness, conform us to the Catholic faith, so that after this transitory life we may deserve to have forever the rest of eternal consolation and happiness. Amen. 14 Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 1500.

When you have received these letters, marked with the character of Mendrion, the person to whom they are sent, being skilled in our art, should observe the lord of the ascendant; let him write both it and the whole disposition of the sky on a clean sheet, and on its back the conjuration. Then, after adding all the things that must be added, and after the chant has been spoken, as soon as the spirit has seen his altar, let him say these words to the first one: Chamerusyn merion nodiel burmy raueto elay reas penafon.

When he has sounded these words to him who appears to be the more eminent among the other spirits, immediately he will approach calmly and will reveal the secret entrusted to him.

*Chapter 20.*

*Whose hour is called Xymalim, and its angel, the supreme emperor, is called Narcoriel, having under him princes, dukes, counts, and servants.*

The eighth hour of the night is called Xymalim. Its angel, the first supreme emperor, is called Narcoriel, who has under his command many other spirits, princes, dukes, ministerial counts, who in their orders are called to various effects by those who work in the secrets of philosophy. And from all these there are twelve orders of those who serve Steganography. In the first order there are 10; in the second 20; in the third 30; in the fourth 40; in the fifth 50; in the sixth 60; in the seventh 70; in the eighth 80; in the ninth 90; in the tenth 100; in the eleventh 10,000. All these observe their orders in such a way that no one can safely work through them unless he has been fully instructed in all things. For whoever does not observe the due order exposes himself to danger and will be frustrated of the desired effect.

Here now we have named 12 dukes and 6 counts, established through all twelve orders by a hidden disposition, who have under them numbered ministerials, xiii. and [unclear], who suffice for us for every operation of the eighth hour of the night. Therefore when you wish to operate through them in Steganography at the eighth hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant, and write both it and the whole disposition of the sky on clean paper, and on its back, with the other things customarily used according to custom, this conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Narconiel aples pornya nauelo meas. Triome ilneas azyfan lafias my bression ersoti penason. Then, after the things to be added according to custom have been added, and after the conjuration has been spoken, the spirits summoned will be present in that form which you have instructed them to assume, ready for all things.

*Secret argument.*

The secret that we wish to learn from a friend far absent, if we trust neither letters nor couriers: let us entrust it in the eighth hour of the night to one of the more powerful spirits of Narcoriel, fearing absolutely nothing, since all are faithful and most ready for obedience.
