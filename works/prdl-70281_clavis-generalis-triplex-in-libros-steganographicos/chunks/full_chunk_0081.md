*Whose hour is called Zeschar, and its spirit is called Pamiel, having many princes under him.*

The ninth hour of the night is called Zeschar, and its angel, the supreme commander, is called Pamyel; after the manner of the preceding ones, he has under his command many other spirits, good and bad, princes, leaders, counts, and ministering spirits assigned to him by turns and ranks. For in the first order there are 10, in the second 20, in the third 30, in the fourth 40, in the fifth 50, in the sixth 60, in the seventh 70, in the eighth 80, in the ninth 90, in the tenth 100, in the eleventh 10,000.

But in the operations of Steganography we do not need all these, since a few are sufficient for us.

Here now we have 18 princes from the spirits of Pamiel, who in their turns are sometimes leaders, sometimes counts; anyone who does not know their ranks will labor in vain in this art. They have 1,320 ministers assigned to them by turns and moments of the hour, with whom they work marvelous effects. Therefore, when you wish to operate through them in Steganography in the ninth hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant of that same hour, or the whole figure of the heavens, and write everything on clean paper, and on the back of that little sheet this conjuration.
