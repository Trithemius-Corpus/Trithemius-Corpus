*Book Three.*

When these things have been most diligently considered, it is clear that Orifiel of Saturn is separated from Saturn by 25 degrees and 15 minutes; he is toward the east, distant from the first point of Saturn’s motion by 625 gradations, and from the end of the motion by 25 minutes. When these things are known, make an image out of wax, or paint on new paper the figure of Orifiel in the likeness of a bearded and naked man, standing upon a bull of varied color, holding in his right hand a book, and in his left a reed-pen. While you are making it, say: Let this image of great Orifiel be full, perfect, and fit to announce the secret of my mind to N., son of N., my friend, securely, faithfully, and completely. Amen. Write on the forehead your own name with ink tempered with oil of roses, and on the breast the name of your absent friend, saying: This is the image of N., son of N., to whom through Orifiel, angel of Saturn, the conception of my mind is announced. Amen. On the forehead of the image write Merion, and on the breast Troesda. Then join both images together, saying: In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Hear, Orifiel, prince of the star of Saturn, and by the powers of almighty God I adjure you, listen: I command and order you, by the power of this image of yours, that you announce to N., son of N., this intention of mine (let the intention be explained) as quickly as possible, securely, secretly, and faithfully, omitting nothing of those things which I want him to know, and which I have entrusted to you. In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Then wrap those images, thus fastened together, in a cloth washed with water of white [wine?], and put them in a movable vessel, which the wise call Indix pharnat alronda; cover it carefully above with shaved leather, and put it in the entrance of some closed dwelling, wherever you wish, for the space of 24 hours; and without any hesitation your desire will be perfectly fulfilled within those 24 hours. And your absent friend will know your intention in every form most perfectly, just as you spoke it over the image, however long it may have been; and whatever you wish him to know from you, he will know within those 24 hours perfectly and most secretly, so that no man will ever be able to perceive or know this for all eternity without your will or that of your friend. And it is a great secret, which none of the wise before us dared to commit to writing. But that friend of yours, if he wishes you to know anything from his affairs, can make you more certain through the same spirit within the same space of 24 hours, provided he is well perfected in this art. But you, when 24 hours have elapsed, take the images from the place in which they were put, and put them away, because at every time of Orifiel you can operate through them not only for that friend of yours, but for any others whatsoever, merely changing the name of that friend into the name of the person whom you wish to know something. And note that the images themselves need not be painted beautiful or elaborate; however simple they may be, it makes no difference, provided they have some proportionate likeness, so that they are recognized as images of men. But if anyone wishes and is able to paint them beautifully, it will not hinder, nor does it help.

*Concerning the second angel of Saturn, who is the first under Orifiel and is called Sadael, presiding over the second quarter of Saturn.*

The second angel of Saturn is called Sadael, who is appointed first under Orifiel in order, and has the second part after his prince, both by day and by night, in the works and times of Saturn: that is, the fourth, fifth, and sixth unequal hours. Therefore, when in these three hours you wish to announce something to an absent friend within twenty-four hours, without letters, without words, and without a messenger, do it in all respects and by the sign which we stated in the preceding operation; and observe the point of the second motion of Saturn in its degrees toward the sign in which that planet itself is moved, and what the motion is like, and where the spirit moving the star is, how far it has receded, and how far it is elevated from the center of its motion, however far it is distant from any point of the whole circle or sphere.
