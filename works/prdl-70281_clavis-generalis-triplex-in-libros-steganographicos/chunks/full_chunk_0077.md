*Chapter XVIII.*

*The sixth hour is called Thaazaron, and its angel is called Zaazenach, presiding over many spirits.*

*The sixth* hour of the night is called Thaazoron; the name of its angel is Zaazenach, who has under his command princes, dukes, counts, and ministerial servants almost innumerable, who preside by turns over various magical operations according to their orders. In the first order there are 10, in the second 20, in the third 30, in the fifth 50, in the sixth 60, in the seventh 70, in the eighth 80, in the ninth 90, in the tenth 100, in the eleventh 1000. But when you wish to call them, in whatever form it pleases you, they are entirely ready and obedient, as the aforesaid sages testify; we have experienced it.

Here now we have, from the twelve orders of the spirits of Zaazenach, 12 dukes, named counts with their servants, duly assigned in order, numbering 1860, who in the sixth hour of the night are abundantly sufficient for us for every operation of Steganography. Therefore, when you wish to operate in this hour of the night, observe the lord of the ascendant; and write both that very one and the whole disposition of the heaven, with the other necessary things, on a clean slip, and the conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Zaazeuach eneos fari nabdiel lasmy chyrmean ersoty layr pornys theor meã penason. Then, when the things that must duly be added have been added, and the conjuration has been spoken, the spirits called will appear without delay, placid and well-disposed, ready for everything.

*The outline of any secret whatsoever that occurs.*

Whatever secret occurs to us, which we cannot safely disclose to a friend either by messengers or by letters, in the sixth hour of the night let us entrust it to one of the prescribed dukes, or even to the counts, if the duke is not present, which can often happen, in that manner and order in which the instruction exists in this art, fearing nothing at all; because all the good ones are faithful in all things that have been entrusted to them by the operator, and they omit nothing.
