*Book Two.*

When the person to whom these letters are sent has received them, being skilled in the art, after first doing what must be done beforehand, let him say the conjuration; and immediately the spirit sent will appear. When he has seen him present, let him say these words:

Charmes pormeniel vear saseuij liernoty noty ersy melron.

When these have been said, as is prescribed in the art, the spirit will come nearer and will faithfully and secretly reveal the secret committed to him.

*Chapter VII.*

*Whose hour is called Hamarym, and whose spirit is Barquiel, having under his command 10 dukes, 100 counts. The number of servants is not certain to me.*

The seventh hour of the day is called Hamarym; its angel is named Barquiel, who has under his command 10 dukes, 100 counts. The number of servants is great and uncertain. The services of all these, each in turn according to their ranks, are amply sufficient for us for every operation of Steganography in the seventh hour of the day. But when you wish to operate through the spirits of the aforesaid emperor in the seventh hour, do not summon fewer than at least one duke and one count, who have servants assigned to them, each in his own rank. But so that you may have at hand the names of some dukes and counts through whom you may operate, attend to the table.

| R. | Abrasiel | Harmiel | Pasriel | 10 | 10 | 100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R. | Farmos | Nastrus | Venefiel | 10 | 10 | 100 |
| R. | Nestorii | Varmay | Euarym | 10 | 10 | 100 |
| R. | Manuel | Tulmas | Drufiel | 10 | 10 | 1000 |
| R. | Sagiel | Crofiel | Kathos | 10 | 20 | 100 |

Here now you have named 5 from the dukes, 10 from the counts, and 600 counted from the order of servants. The dukes and counts are called by name, and they are quite ready to obey the one who summons them in all things that pertain to the service of this art. Therefore, when you wish to operate through them, write on clean paper the characters of the lord ascending in the twelfth house, with the rest to be written according to custom; and on its back write this conjuration according to the precepts of the art, as you know.

*Conjuration.*

L Amedon mosco vrsoty tharuean: dayr Lays emel thebrean rasotv bamerson.

Then, after adding what according to the custom of the art must be added, say this conjuration with strong intention, as is fitting, and at once the spirits summoned will appear visibly. Entrust the secret not to the count but to the duke, unless perhaps you have summoned a count without a duke; and although I do not deny that this can be done, nevertheless according to the precept of the art at least one duke must be summoned, with as many counts as you wish.

*The subject of the secret, as you wish.*

Let the secret be for you whatever sort of matter to a friend that you do not wish to be shared with anyone else. Therefore one should trust neither letters nor notaries, because fraud can intervene everywhere. Summon from the above-named spirits as many as you wish; entrust the secret to the more powerful one, fearing nothing, because they are good and faithful for all things.
