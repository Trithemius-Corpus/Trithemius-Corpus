*The Thirteenth Method.*

It begins its narrative with five null letters and concludes the mystic word, through the same number of significant letters, [unclear] if they were of the same letter with the eleventh. Further, if there are fewer than five letters of the mystery, or if any remain after five, they must be concluded with as many null letters as the significant letters that immediately preceded them in number; but the mystic word, however many significant letters it has, is nevertheless begun from five null letters. Let us also devise the following example of this method.

*Example XIII.*
