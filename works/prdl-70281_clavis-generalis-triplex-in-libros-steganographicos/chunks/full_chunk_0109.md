*28. The Key of Steganography.*

The hidden tenor of the above: [German] "Peter Kleipfeyel, the bearer of this letter, is a wicked rogue, full [drunk] day and night, a despiser of the name of God."

*Modus XIX:* after each single vacant [letter] it sets two significant [letters], with the letters transposed in the aforesaid manner.
Modus 19: you premise one vacant [letter], to which it always subjoins two significant [letters] with the letters transposed at pleasure, so that always after one idle, transposed [letter], two significant and transposed [letters] follow. And this transposition can be varied as if to infinity, as we shall say in what follows; and of this modus let us set down the following example, in which we shall observe the transposition of letters said above, so that in place of any letter the one always immediately following it be taken.
