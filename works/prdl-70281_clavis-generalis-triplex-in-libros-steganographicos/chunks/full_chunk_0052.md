*Of Steganography*

*Song of conjuration.*

BYdiel marchan chamerosi philtres maduse veac casmyron cralnoti: pean deuoon fabelios eltida camean veor. Oniel vear thyrso liernoty: ianos prolsato chanos elastry peanon elsathas melros notiel pen soes probys chyras lesbroy mauear iothan liernoti chrymarson?

When the song has been spoken as is proper, the spirits that have been summoned will immediately appear, visible, walking beautifully, and as friends leading one another while mutually embracing; and they will be willing toward you and obedient in all things.

*Let it be secret to me, whatever it may be.*

It is a secret to me, which I desire to know, a very secret friend absent far away: it concerns a hidden substance, which must be lost if the mystery happens in any way to be made public. Therefore I entrust it to be carried not to a man, nor to letters, but to spirits alone, whom I know to be safe and faithful.

Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, sends greetings to Rutger the Sicambrian. And let him say the following little prayer of great Basil.

The glory of ineffable eternal blessedness is acquired by good and humble exercises. Now, mortals, wipe away your errors with diligent tears: love God, adoring his holy name. Strive humbly to imitate the path of humble Jesus Christ, crucified, always rendering honor to him, sweetly burning with love of our most benign Savior. Glorify, exalt his praiseworthy goodness; bless the holy name, zealously pursuing the fire of goodness. Despise empty honors; learn to render praises in adversities; love the exercise of devotion; cultivate humility, the most noble thing; exalt Jesus, rescuing and saving us from death. Farewell. 7 days before the Kalends of April, in the year of the Nativity of the Lord 1500.

But when the friend receives the letter, being previously skilled in the art of Steganography, once the character of Bidiel is recognized and those things have first been said which are customarily to be said beforehand, let him say the conjuration generally.

*Book One.*

*Song.*

BYdiel maslo chameron theory madias near fabelron thiamy martoy year pean liernoty calmea drules: Thubra pleory malresa teorty melchoy vemo chosray. When the song has been spoken, the spirit will stand by, visible only to him who summoned him; he will report to you the things entrusted to him completely and faithfully.

*Chapter 32.*

*In which a certain necessary recapitulation of the foregoing is made. Several cautions are inserted, which must be observed by those wishing to practice the art of Steganography.*
