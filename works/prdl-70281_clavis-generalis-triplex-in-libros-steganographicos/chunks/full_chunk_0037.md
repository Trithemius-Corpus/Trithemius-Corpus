*Chapter XIX*

*Whose supreme prince is called Amenadiel, dwelling in the West; he has under him 300 dukes, 500 counts, and almost innumerable servants.*

Amenadiel the emperor, the supreme spirit of the West, has under his dominion 300 more powerful princes or dukes, and no fewer than five hundred sub-dukes or counts; servants, sub-servants, and attendants from among the innumerable, whose names I have found to be no fewer than 30,000, besides innumerable others whose names I have not yet found. For these are sufficient for announcing to friends all secret matters toward the West. But I shall now state the names of the dukes who are to be summoned for the service of this art, as many as are sufficient.

Through these twelve chief dukes you will be able to accomplish everything that has to be done by the command of Amenadiel in Steganography toward the West. But observe carefully, so that you know, when you call any one of the dukes, how many counts, servants, and attendants he has under him according to the twenty-four hours of day and night, which these 1,550 keep, assigned to themselves by equal division in their order. If you err in these matters, you will accomplish nothing; perhaps you would fall into some danger. For one who wishes to work in Steganography must not only be skilled in our art, but also in [unclear].
