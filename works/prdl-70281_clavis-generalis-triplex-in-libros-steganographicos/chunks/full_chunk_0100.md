The Passion of Jesus Christ, giving life to the human race, whose life, innocently afflicted, freed us from every calamity, because [unclear] shall be saved in necessity; but [unclear] by the vicious, and the death of the good shall be abundant. Let us speak the praise of C. J., who redeemed our souls. Therefore let us give thanks to our most pious redeemer, who redeemed all who were subject to tyranny; all have praised his blessed name, and let us do penance for our excesses. Daily we shall hasten to live virtuously in love of the redeemer, whose fear cleanses the conscience, justifying us sweetly. Let us amend our life, lamenting with devotion, so that, turned to justice, we may deserve release from sins, persevering continually in charity and humility; we shall not fail.

Joh. Trith., Abb.

*The fifth mode proceeds with the first null through two signifying ones; it concludes each thing with an alternate null.*

The fifth mode, not very dissimilar to the fourth, proceeds in the customary way with the first null through two signifying words. Each word of the secret, containing one null, is always enclosed, if they are equal, by two signifying words and one null; but if the matter is among unequal ones, a solitary signifying word will stand between two nulls. Let us also set down an example of this kind.
