*Chapter V.*

Roth. *Whose supreme prince, named Barmiel, dwells in the South, having under him 10 princes who preside over the day, and 20 who preside over the night, together with their keepers; and they are over the announcing of secrets that pertain to the surrender of fortresses.*

The great prince of the fifth mansion in the South, toward midday, is called Barmiel. He has under his dominion 10 principal dukes who preside over the day, together with their sub-keepers, and 20 in number who preside over nocturnal operations, with their servants. These are more numerous than the diurnal ones because the operation of this mansion is performed more often by night than by day. For their office is to announce secret surrenders of fortresses and cities, especially at night. But when you wish to operate in this art through Barmiel, call one of his princes, whichever you wish: he will immediately come to you with 20 servants, because no prince of this mansion is ever accustomed to proceed alone or with fewer. Commit your secret, however, not to the servants, because they are proud and malicious, but to the prince, because he is good,
