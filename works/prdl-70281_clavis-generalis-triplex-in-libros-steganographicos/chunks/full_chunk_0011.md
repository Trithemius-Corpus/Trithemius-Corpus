*3 Of the Steganography*

Let them be kindly disposed, quiet, and calm; they are more often solitary wanderers than noisy in a crowd. Therefore, when you wish to operate through them, and through them to make known the secret of your mind to someone, however absent he may be, do this. Write on paper previously prepared in the proper manner, as you know, whatever account you wish, and in whatever language, since it makes no difference: for he will perceive your mind not from the paper, but from the Spirits. While writing it, turn toward the east; when it is completed, say this conjuration: Padiel aporsy mesarpon omeuas peludyn malpreaxo. Condusen, vlearo thersephi bayl merphon, paroys gebuly mailthomyon ilthear tamarson acrimyon peatha Casmy Chertiel, medony reabdo, lasonti iaciel mal arti bulomeon abry pathulmon theoma parhormyn. When you have completed these words, you will see two, or at least one, of the Spirits very peacefully present and ready to obey your will. And to him you will entrust your secret, and he will faithfully carry it to the place you have commanded.

But when the letters come by messenger to the one to whom they are sent, if he is alone, well; if he is with others, let him withdraw apart if he can, or, if he cannot, let him turn toward the east and say this conjuration in silence: Padiel ariel vanerhon chio thatson phymarto merphon amprisco ledabarym, elsophroy mesarpon ameorsy, paneryn atle pachumgk thearan vtrul vt solubito beslonty las gomadyn triamy mefarnothy. When these words have been duly spoken, immediately the Spirit sent will appear, to reveal it without deceit and without any danger. No one malicious and wicked will easily be able to operate in this art; but the better and purer anyone is, the more willingly and with greater cheerfulness the Spirits obey him. And the more each person conceals this knowledge, the more easily he will operate. And note that this Padiel with his Spirits is appointed to announce secret advisories for the correction of evildoers, imprisonment, and punishment, as we have shown in the example set below.
