When the place of the angel Sadael has been found from consideration of the fixed star at the point of Saturn’s motion, make two images, in the manner which we described in the preceding operation, of Orifiel, with the inscriptions, adjurations, and all the other individual things that were stated there. When these have been completed, place those images upon five little branches of the herb thrasnote, outside the house in a safe and secret place, and say over them:

In the name of the + Father and + the Son and + the Holy Spirit. Amen. Just as I have placed these two images of the angel Sadael and of N., son of N., my friend, joined together upon these five movable branches, so I command you, Sadael, by the power of Orifiel, your great prince, that without delay you convey to the knowledge of the aforesaid friend the secret of my mind which I have entrusted to you by explaining it.

When these words have been said, leave the images there for 24 hours; and what you wished will be done, and your friend will know, most perfectly and secretly, everything that you said over the images. Likewise, if you wish to know about someone absent, how he is faring, and his true condition within 24 hours, however far away he may be, you will be able to know it in this way.

*And, with the constellation observed, through this art you will be able to know all things that are done in the world.*

*Steganography, Book III.*

*Concerning the third angel of Saturn, who is second under Orisiel and is called Pomiel, presiding over the third quarter of Saturn.*

The third angel of Saturn is called Pomiel, who is second in order under Orisiel, the principal angel of Saturn. He presides over the seventh, eighth, and ninth Saturnine hours, both in the day and at night, in all matters that pertain to the operation of Saturn. Since he moves with his order among the fixed stars 25 degrees from the East, you will find his place, once his hours have been set out in tables, as follows.
