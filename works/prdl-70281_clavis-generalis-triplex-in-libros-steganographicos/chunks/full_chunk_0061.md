*Steganography*

is sufficient for every operation of the present hour. Therefore, when you wish to work in Steganography through any one of the aforesaid in the fifth hour of the day, write the character of the lord of the ascendant, of the twelfth house, together with the other things which are required for the art, on a clean little sheet; and on its back write this conjuration in the manner that you know.

*Conjuration.*

Sasquiel adres rhotrofei rosiel emeltto satu olmeniel irsoth sauear nauediel liernoti chameson. When these things have been completed, and the other necessary things added, say the conjuration itself, as is proper; and at once you will see the spirit whom you have called visibly present, to whom you can safely entrust the secret.

*Method of announcing the secret.*

Let there be for us any sort of secret for a friend, which we wish no one except him to know; and let it be such that it may not safely be entrusted either to letters or to messengers. Let us call one of the companions of the aforesaid Sasquiel; let us entrust the secret to him; let us attach letters sealed with his character, and we shall be safe.
