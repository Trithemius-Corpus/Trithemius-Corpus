*30. Key of Steganography. Method XXI. It uses alternating significant words, with the letters transposed into the second place.*

Method 21 uses alternating words, of which the first is empty, the second indeed significant, the third again empty, the fourth by no means idle; and so on in succession down to the end of the proposed narrative. All the letters in the significant word are transposed into the second following place, so that for the letter A, c is to be taken; for c, e is to be taken; and so on in succession to the end of the alphabet. Further, for x, a is to be taken, but for z, b. The Greek y, however, is not necessary for us for an ordinary name, and therefore is to be omitted in all the following alphabets. But now let us set down an example of this kind.

*Example XXI.*

I should rejoice to answer your letters; so may Christ make me rejoice, and I would send the requested volumes, if at last I had had them. I have been zealous, I think, for my nearest and most serious hidden handlers lately with a good zeal, as the most exact philosophers had it; moreover I shall have for you thanks far from mortal, my sweetest brother, you who keep mutual glory inviolate. For in this familiar matter, which you seem to have attended to most diligently with grave modesty, I have plainly learned this: that I praise what has been done and shall have thanks that will never die. And therefore, complying with your requests, I should soon have wished to be, Christ knows, the things requested, if I had them. Here I have nowhere seen a Latin translation of Master Ficino; I confess I have read at Venice the beloved letter of Pico della Mirandola. Therefore do not take it hard that your petitions are so frustrated. The highest truth of your Christ conceals nothing in darkness, and, as examiner of hearts, that I could not oblige you. To hide the other best things, to scorn virtue and very many gains swiftly, at last you think true, to love Christ purely. I ask, see to it that you exist as a priest in works, and that the lover of Christ more truly love Christ purely, whom you are commanded to fear. He is preserved by zeal for souls, and sets nothing before Christ's commands, since he knows that the fear of God is the best zeal of the soul, the love of blessedness, God zealous by loving. For the true royal knowledge of the Savior, and the pious observance of the catholic faith, and increasing thanks to almighty God Jesus Christ, the more glorious giver of all good things, may confer on you loves of eternity, and inscribe you in the catalogue of faithful Christians. Farewell.

*Method XXII. With the transposition of letters already mentioned preserved, after two idle words he sets down the same number of significant ones.*

Method 22 begins with idle things and at once adds two significant ones, and so, by means of two empty words and two significant words, it completes the whole open and hidden narrative in alternating turns. The first letters of all the significant words are placed in the third position, so that for a, d is to be taken; for b, e is to be taken; but for c, f; and so on in succession to the end, just as for the first letter of the alphabet A, x is to be taken; for b, [unclear]; and for z, c, as in secret matters the alphabet showing [unclear] their red visited black, hidden this time.
