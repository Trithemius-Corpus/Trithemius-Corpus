The general keys of our book, and the principal ones, are three; on them depends the variation of all the rest. And it consists in the permutation of vowels with consonants, as you will see in the following examples:

This method of writing must evidently be understood as follows: where, in your secret sense, the vowel *a* happens to have to be written by you, you should put in place of *a* the consonant *b*, and conversely, if *b* happens to have to be written, you should write in place of *b* the vowel *a*; thus in the remaining modes also, which are for variation, namely for *ag*, for *ef*, for *id*, which, just as it is the middle one, so always remains fixed and will never be changed.

Now, by way of example, if the word *Omnipotens* is to be written, it is written in this way by the first mode: *Fmndpftens*; by the second, *Moulpmkus*; but by the third mode, thus: *Smnrasuqno*. Conversely, namely by the three later modes: by the first thus, *Cmndptfns*; by the second thus, *Qmnruqasne*. And thus we proceed in complete sentences.

Now from these six aforesaid modes the following rules are set up, according to which you will regulate yourself in reading our book. Any one of the following modes is varied by any one of the aforesaid rules. And you will observe their variation in many parts of our book, namely in the conjurations of demons, in which our secrets are hidden, and revealed by our rules to those who know the seals.

And, as an example for you, let the following sentence serve: just as it can be written by all the aforesaid rules, so it has pleased us to put it here by the first mode. “Have mercy, almighty God, on the souls of your servants, resting piously in you.” This sentence by the first becomes thus:

*Mdscrec fmndpftens icgs bndmbags obmglfrgm tgfrgm dn te pdc reqgdesecntdgm*

And since, because of the lack of vowels, no word signifying anything is given to you here for mixing into our book, among so many consonants we have followed above this rule of the example, namely by taking the aforesaid consonants:

*Midosa coracir ocafati co iu sa*

Of these letters only the first or initial one is significant for you; immediately after it follows one that is not significant, or is vacant, and so alternately, as the points marked above over the significant letters show you.
