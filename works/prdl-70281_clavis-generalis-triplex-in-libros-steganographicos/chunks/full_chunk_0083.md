R. R. R.

Laperiel, Chameray: 30, 10, 100, 100, 300
Emarziel, Hazaniel: 30, 10, 100, 100, 300
Nameroyz, Vraniel: 30, 10, 100, 100, 300

Although there are many dukes and counts appointed for the operation of the Steganographia in the tenth hour of the night, nevertheless, since a few suffice us for everything, we have set down by name in this table three from the dukes and likewise three from the counts, adding beneath them the number of their servants, lawfully distributed by turns and orders. But whoever wishes to know the names of more of them should summon one of the aforesaid and ask him about each one. But when you wish to work through these spirits in the tenth hour of the night, observe and do most diligently everything that has been prescribed, writing everything on a clean sheet, and on its back this conjuration, in the manner in which it has been prescribed.

*Conjuration.*

Iasguarim apornys vesale moes labiel throe. Tadrys ascel cachylos thubla nailso thirmel vear. Turiel cralty solmys aslotiel naemes renhar, vear thirmo cralnoti faon dremlion lauiel odres, notiel pornys. Pornis meir moab sayraslotiel lo raytu lian afeuo, Bian eory churio bays afropenason. Then, when the things to be added have been added, and the conjuration has been spoken as is proper, immediately the summoned spirits will be present.

*The intention of any secret whatsoever.*

Let the secret be whatever it may. When you cannot make it known by letters and messengers to a friend living near or far, summon one or two from the spirits Iasguarim and appoint them as your couriers; entrust the secret, doubting nothing.

*Book Two.*

*Letters are to be formed at will.*

This is the prophecy of Nicolaus Basselius of Hirsau, bishop and saint, which he saw in Spicatus. And he said: Thus says the Lord to the shepherds of the churches. Soon you will perish, because a very great calamity will scatter your broad dominion and will sprinkle all Europe. Woe to the Ishmaelites, who break the covenants of the tabernacle, because through envy and rashness they seize Easter. Behold, the dominion that you have will be broken unless you hasten greater repentance; and you will be handed over to be torn apart. Alas, the whole Church has been overturned by the worst morals; pretense has invaded its ministers. Flee; do not look back. You will be laid waste, and you will be dragged by dogs; they will sprinkle the whole land with blood. Therefore, inhabitants of the earth, do penance. Young Benjamin will experience disturbances. Woe to the inhabitants of Gennesaret, which is Europe, because on account of the multitude of their sins they will perish under the hands of the Bulgarians. Here ends the prophecy of Nicolaus, bishop of Hirsau, 12 Kalends of May.

When letters of whatever kind, marked beforehand with the sign Iasguarim, have been received by the person to whom they are sent, one skilled in the art should do everything that is prescribed according to the art, and should observe the order of the planets, and write what must be written. And when the conjuration written above has been spoken, immediately, when the spirits have appeared mingled together, let him say to the first among them: Chamerusin othriel arnotiel solais elty. Naeles proy vear sato cralnoti, penason. When these words have been said, the spirit, immediately approaching, will faithfully and calmly reveal the message entrusted to him.
