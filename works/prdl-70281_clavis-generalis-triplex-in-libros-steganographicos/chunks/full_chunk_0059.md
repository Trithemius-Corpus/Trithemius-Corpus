Therefore write on a new sheet the character of the lord of the ascendant, and the others in the order of the planets, with the sign of the twelfth house, in the manner which the instruction of the art requires. On the back of the sheet write this conjuration.

*Conjuration.*

Vequaniel, odiel mesrij reuoj sotiel mear iamy otiel aslofian yrsoti breotion drearij fabel merusin. Once the conjuration has been written and those things which are required by the art have been added beneath, say it in a low voice, so that it is not heard by anyone; and immediately the spirits summoned will come. When you see them, entrust the secret to the more powerful one, whom you will easily recognize from his attire, because the one who is first among the other spirits always appears crowned.

*Any Kind of Secret Is Effected.*

If you have any secret for an absent friend, which must not be entrusted either to letters, lest they be read by someone, or to a courier, lest on some occasion, either corrupted by payment or by fear, he betray you publicly: summon the spirit assigned to the hour, entrust the secret to him, fearing nothing; he carries it faithfully.

*You Will Form Letters at Will.*

Johannes Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim, to Arnoldus Bostius, Carmelite of Ghent, fellow philosopher: greeting and charity.

Since I have known you to be a lover of the humane arts, and a devout [unclear] of the Christian faith, and the greatest supporter of all philosophers, I gladly comply with your requests, moved by the wondrous pleasure of friendship. Behold, at your urging I send you the book on writers, marked beforehand with ecclesiastical and Catholic doctors, so that you may have something with which to delight your mind. But send back to me the codex of Hermes, the books of Dionysius on spirits, and the great work of Euencius on winter questions. May Christ deign to adorn the eyes of our heart with the most fervent zeal of his love; and may he make us have love of heavenly goods; and, having stripped us of the basest desires, may he teach us to despise vain honors, so that we may be able to penetrate the sublime things of the art, and also the great secrets of nature; and may we have a continual desire for philosophy, to purify our affections, so that we may always adorn the Catholic faith with the most honorable morals. Hold fast the great word: you are a philosopher; give up everything, and you will find it. Keep watch for the hour of your approaching death, so that you may meet it prepared. Farewell. 3 Kal. Apr. 1500.

When you have received letters sealed with the character of Vequaniel, in whatever manner, write the figure of the lord of the ascendant, of the twelfth house, together with the other things required for the art, on a new small sheet, and on its back the aforesaid conjuration; immediately the spirit sent will appear visible. As soon as you see him, say this song.

*Song of Conjuration.*

E Ameron aprois liernoti stadiueat diuiel sauean Lamerfy. When these words have been spoken, receive the word from the mouth of the spirit without fear, because he is sufficiently faithful. And beware of working in public before others.
