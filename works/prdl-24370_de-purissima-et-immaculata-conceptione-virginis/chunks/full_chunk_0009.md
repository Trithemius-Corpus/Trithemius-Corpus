Thus, by your pious office and by divine authority, resist steadfastly our enemies, or rather the enemies of the blessed Mary. Drive them from your territories and expel them after punishing them, so that the religious devotion of the Christian people toward the most glorious Mother of God, the unique, special patroness and mother of our race, and toward piety and charity, may be more and more fostered, increased, and kindled. This you will without doubt make most pleasing and most delightful to her most loving Son, our Lord and Redeemer Jesus Christ, and also most fruitful for this people whose care you bear. Given at Paris, in the general congregation celebrated with such solemnity at Saint-Mathurin, in the year of the Lord 1497, on the sixteenth day of the month of September.
