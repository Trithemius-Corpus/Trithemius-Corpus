The grace of God had gone before both; it had filled both; but it was earlier in the mother, more abundant in the daughter. The holy mother taught her daughter what she herself had first learned from her parents, although she had no need of human instruction whom the Holy Spirit had filled from conception. For she did not need maternal training, since she was full of heavenly grace. Learn, O mothers, to train your daughters for the honor of God: not to love the pomps of the world, to flee the public gaze of the crowd, to remain at home. For the Holy Spirit hates the public place; he loves the secrecy of a pure mind. Why do you run about through streets and squares, if you love virginal honor? The archangel came to Mary not in public but in private; not as she was speaking in the street, but as she was silent in her chamber. The apostles received the Holy Spirit not in the marketplace, but enclosed in a house. What the most blessed Virgin learned from her mother, indeed from the Holy Spirit, she carefully kept. That woman had been accustomed to avoid wandering excursions; Mary remained alone at home. Look to both of them: mothers to the mother, virgins to the virgin. Believe me, it does not befit your honor to delight in public; it is not advantageous for virgins to be greeted in the marketplace, because one who often goes into public diminishes reverence for her honor. Since virginity is modest, it flees the public place. Without modesty there is no sincere virginity, nor can a thing too public remain pure. Anna offered her sweetest three-year-old daughter to the Lord. She taught the little child concerning the fear of God; she presented her to the temple to be instructed in the fear of the Lord. But you do not do this; rather, you train your daughters for dances and the vanities and love of the world. You say nothing to them about the fear of the Lord; you persuade them of nothing about eternal life, since you yourselves learned nothing good from your parents. Let not a woman glory in the multitude of children if she has neglected to train them properly in the love of God, because it is not multitude but uprightness that commends women. Therefore holy Anna is deservedly to be honored, who bore for us the mother of God without blemish, through whom the Son of God came as savior to the world. Just as, therefore, we venerate and honor the most blessed parent of God because, an immaculate virgin, she bore for us the Redeemer, so also it teaches us to honor Anna because she brought forth for us the mother of the Savior without fault. Unless we had the mother, whence would the daughter have come? Let no one doubt, let no one waver, let no one seethe with wicked thoughts, because if Anna had not been most holy she would not have been chosen as mother of the mother of God.
