*De Purissima et Immaculata Conceptione Virginis* — "On the Most Pure and Immaculate Conception of the Virgin" — is Trithemius's defence of the Immaculate Conception of the Virgin Mary, then a contested theological question rather than defined dogma, with Dominicans and Franciscans sharply divided. Trithemius argues for the Franciscan, pro-Immaculist side in the scholastic manner, and the work is continuous with his St Anne and Marian writings — the devotional-hagiographic strand of his early Sponheim output carried into doctrinal polemic.

This is the **Nuremberg edition, after 1497** (BSB scan) — the second of two witnesses of the *De Purissima* in this corpus (the other is the 1506 Strasbourg edition, `prdl-24369`).
