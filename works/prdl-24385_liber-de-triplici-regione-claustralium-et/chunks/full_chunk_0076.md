by the shedding of your blood and by the power of your most innocent death, and by your very body and blood, the great price of our salvation, I beg you, by that wondrous and unspeakable charity with which you so loved us, wretched and unworthy though we are, as to wash us from our sins in your blood: have mercy on me and forgive me all my sins. Teach me, your unworthy servant, whom among your other gifts you have deigned to call to the priestly office, through no merits of mine but solely by the gracious condescension of your mercy, through your Holy Spirit, to handle this mystery with that reverence and honor, devotion and fear, with which it ought and fittingly should be handled.

Grant me, I beseech you, always to believe, understand, feel, firmly hold, say, and think concerning so great a mystery that which pleases you and is profitable for the salvation of my soul. Let your good Spirit enter into my heart; let him sound there without sound, and without the noise of words speak within me the truth of such great mysteries. For they are exceedingly deep, and covered with a sacred veil.

For the sake of your great mercy, sweetest Lord Jesus, grant me to celebrate the solemn rites of Mass with a pure heart and a clean conscience. I beg you, by the sweetness of your most holy heart, most loving Savior, free my heart from all vain, unclean, harmful, and unlawful thoughts, that it may become a worthy dwelling place of your love, now and without end forever.

I beg you, lover of humankind, sweetest Lord Jesus, by all the power of your most holy passion: drive away from me the spirit of pride and arrogance, vainglory and envy, lust and blasphemy, and fill my soul with the power of charity and innocence. King of virtues, Jesus Christ, lover of holy chastity and integrity, I beg you by the most holy virginity of the most blessed mother of God and undefiled Virgin Mary, and by the body of your most reverend purity: extinguish in me, with the dew of heavenly blessing, the burning heat of lust, so that the brightness of all purity may be and remain in me. Choke in me, I beseech you, the goads of the flesh, and all lustful stirrings and disordered affections; and give me true and perpetual chastity, together with the other good things which truly please you, so that at all times, with an unstained body and a clean heart, I may be able to offer to you the sacrifice of your body and blood.

I know, Lord, and truly know, and I confess this very thing to your goodness, that I am not worthy to approach so great a mystery because of my excessive sins and my many negligences. But I also know, and with my whole heart believe and confess to you, that you, since you are almighty, can make me worthy; you alone justify the ungodly, and from one unclean you can make one clean. Therefore, by your omnipotence I pray you: grant to me, your unworthy servant, true contrition for my sins, pure devotion, and a most fervent compunction in your love, so that I may be able to offer this most holy sacrifice to you with fear and reverence, with purity of heart and a fountain of sweet tears, with spiritual gladness and heavenly joy, with humility and the fervor of charity.

O sweetest Lord Jesus, grant me in this hour, by your mercy, that my mind may feel the sweetness of your most blessed fullness, and the devotion of the holy angels around me, and a peaceful lifting up into you.

Take away from me, Lord Jesus, all my iniquities, and mercifully kindle in me the fire of your love. Be not angry with me, O Jesus, ineffable good, nor restrain over me the multitude of your mercies. For I do not pour out my prayers before your face, Lord, in my own justifications, but in your many mercies. Take from me, Lord Jesus, a heart of stone, hard and unyielding, and give me a heart of flesh, contrite, which may fear you, love you, love you above all things, delight in you alone, follow you, and enjoy you forever.

O most merciful Lord Jesus, I beg you by the most sacred mystery of your body and blood, by which in your holy Church we are daily fed and given drink, washed and sanctified, and made sharers in your one supreme divinity: give me your holy virtues, adorned with which, in a good conscience, I may be able to approach your altar, so that these heavenly sacraments may become for me salvation and life. For you said with your holy and most blessed mouth: The bread which I shall give is my flesh, for the life of the world. If anyone eats of this bread, he shall live forever. O sweetest bread, heal the palate of my heart, that I may taste the sweetness of your love.
