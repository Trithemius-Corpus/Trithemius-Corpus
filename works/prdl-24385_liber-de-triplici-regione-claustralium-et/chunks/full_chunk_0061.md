There the most indulgent Father will receive you as you return with joy; he will clothe you with the first robe of innocence recovered; he will give you the ring of most holy covenant on your hands, and shoes of true mortification of evil affections on your feet, so that, adorned with these ornaments, like a faithful bride you may cling to him continually and never thereafter withdraw from his love. Finally, for your food he will kill the fatted calf for you; he himself will be at once the server and the feast: because of the greatness of his love he will give his flesh as food and his own blood as drink. There the Spirit of the holy Lord will be present and will fill you with his mystical gifts. Then, exulting in the Lord, you will sing: The fruit of Jesus is sweet to my throat, for his spirit is sweeter than honey.

Therefore this supreme region is magnificent in the spirit’s return, because it leads back to the pleasantness of the heavenly fatherland those citizens whom it receives into its dwelling. And we unfailingly recognize the citizens of this most holy region by the fruits of the Spirit, of whom we know the holy apostle spoke. Now the fruit of the Spirit is charity, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, long-suffering, gentleness, faith, modesty, continence, chastity. These fruits the citizens of this third region bring forth, because, filled with God and united to him, always joyful in the Lord and most fervent in charity, they walk onward. For whatever happens to a just man dwelling on this mountain of love will not sadden him. For the soul always peaceful and tranquil in the love of God is not lifted up by any prosperity and is not disturbed by any adversity.

That this supreme region of the spirit is joyful and most true in its treasuring up.

Article Six.

Behold, this most beautiful region of the spirit is called joyful and most true in its treasuring up, because whatever is acquired through the exercise of good work it possesses in simplicity of spirit. The soul’s treasure is the love of God; when the purified mind is kindled by its fervor, it is raised to this region as to the very storehouse of love. For the Lord says in the holy Gospel: Where your treasure is, there also your heart will be. This is the rare treasure hidden in the field of Scripture; when a man has found it, he hides it, and going away sells everything, so that he may be able to buy the field with the treasure. The field is this supreme region of the spirit; the treasure is divine love. When a man finds it, he ought to count all other things as worthless, so that he may joyfully possess the field with the treasure. There is no possession in the world more delightful than divine love; there is no greater beauty in this life, for whoever has this treasure of love seeks nothing more. And as fire is naturally borne upward, so love is raised into this region of the spirit as to its own proper dwelling.

For the soul, kindled by divine love, is always lifted up to God with fiery affections, and beyond every thought is moved with wondrous sweetness toward its own beginning. For, purified in the exercise of the second region, it prepares itself for the ascent of this third affection of lovers; it pants with all its strength to come to him whom it loves; it thinks of nothing but Jesus, the treasure of its heart; in the greatness of its love it adores him with burning sighs. But when the most sweet office of contemplation has been completed, suddenly the beloved slips away; the mind, languishing with love, is called back to the exercise of the second region. In this exercise the true lover of God sees that his body indeed dwells among men, but in affection and desire he lives with his most beloved Jesus.

And although this anagogic action of the created spirit comes about by the impulse of the Spirit of God, still the mind, cleansed through the exercise of the second region, must advance itself toward the ascent, and, disposed by a most fervent affection, concur with the divine Spirit. For almighty God, who has bestowed on us freedom of choice, will raise no one unwilling to this supreme region of his contemplation.
