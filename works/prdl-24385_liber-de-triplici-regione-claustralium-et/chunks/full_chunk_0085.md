After vespers or the vigils of the dead, return to your cell with the customary prayers and bows. There, on bended knees, give thanks to your most merciful preserver, because he has not only graciously snatched you from the vanities of the world, but has also guarded you in the order from many evils and dangers. Then call to mind how many bodily and spiritual goods the kindly Father has bestowed on you, though you deserve nothing, and how, as the greatest reward, he has promised you himself if you faithfully persevere in his love. Be grateful, and always watchful in prayer, so that you may burn sweetly in the love of God.

At the meal.

When the signal for supper has been given, prepare yourself humbly to go to it, and observe what we marked above concerning dinner. After supper you may return to your cell and devote yourself to prayer or meditation until the signal is rung for the conference.

At the conference.

After supper, when you have heard the signal for the spiritual conference, take care to go without delay and with gravity, and to hear the last reading of the day with fervor of spirit. For the word of holy exhortation that has been heard last will cling more tenaciously to the mind throughout the whole night. But if it is a fast day, the conference is customarily observed in the refectory while drinking. Therefore be attentive to sacred reading in every place, because where the word of holy exhortation is gladly received with attention, it cannot happen that the mind is not often lifted up.

At compline.

When you come to compline, pray to the Lord that he may always make you despise all worldly and carnal things, and grant you to remain continually in his most holy love, so that, when the course of this present life has been completed, you may deserve to enter the temple of God not made by hands, and rejoice forever in the Lord with all the saints. At the end of compline, when you hear the signal of the angelic salutation being rung, humbly greet the most blessed Mother of God and Virgin Mary, that by her merits she may guard you through the night and continually intercede with her Son for your salvation.

After compline.

When compline is finished, return at once to your cell, and after a few prayers take care to prepare yourself most diligently for the following recollection of mind.

On recollection after compline. Consideration nine.

Since we cannot remain altogether unmoved in one holy state of mind, it is necessary for us often to renew our holy purpose, so that as often as during the day, by wandering through the byways of sins, we withdraw from the one principle of our soul, the most high God, so often we may return to him through contrition and compunction, with amendment firmly purposed. For this reason our holy fathers established for us that we should devote ourselves to recollection after compline and examine ourselves concerning the negligences committed during the day, so that the purpose of holiness, which we know to be quite weak and infirm, we may renew toward fervor of spirit by the continual exercise of recollection. Therefore our devout monk, wishing to recollect himself fruitfully and with useful continuance, ought principally to examine himself in order on five points.

First in recollection.

The demanding of an account.

The second is the demanding of an account, by which the monk is ordered to bring back before the eyes of the mind, for correction, whatever he remembers himself to have committed during the day by failing negligently or badly or less well. Let him turn himself to the Lord, lie before his bed with hands joined, and say in this or a similar manner:

I give thanks to you, almighty and merciful God, for all your benefits, which to me, an unworthy sinner deserving absolutely nothing, from my youth [unclear] of good things.
