*After Compline*

After Compline, having gathered his powers, the devout cenobite should enter alone into the secret place of his heart; let him leave all cares and anxieties outside, bare of them, so that, free and released from all things, he may deserve to speak with the Lord Jesus through the desire of compunction, and sweetly to be united through the burning of love. Thus, having recalled the faults of the day in summary to the memory for examination, let him pray to the Lord as follows.

*Prayer.*

O most merciful Lord Jesus Christ, who for our redemption willed to undergo a most shameful death: grant me, I beseech you, through the pain of your wounds and through the shedding of your most holy blood, and through the power of your most innocent death, pardon for all my sins, so that with conscience cleansed I may hereafter cling to you continually through the desire of love; may make daily progress in holy religion; and may humbly submit myself in all things to your most holy will.

*To the Son.*

My body and my soul I commend to you, Lord Jesus Christ, this night and at every time, in the power and union of that love by which, hanging on the cross, you commended your beloved mother, the Virgin Mary, to the disciple. In that love, I beseech you, guard me from all the snares of the ancient serpent; suppress the unlawful motions of the flesh; drive far away foul and evil dreams; and repel the phantasms of the nights, so that I may sleep secure in your peace, and at the hours appointed for me may rise gladly to your praises. Amen.

*To the Father.*

Lord, holy Father, almighty, eternal God, who kindly received us into your grace in the passion of your only-begotten Son: receive, I beseech you, mercifully, all the good things, bestowed by you, that I have done on this day, in words, deeds, and thoughts, in the power and union of that charity and love by which you received the same your Son hanging and dying on the cross for us. And to you, holy Father, in the faith and union of that charity I commend myself this night and at every time, by which your Son, our Lord, when now about to die, commended his spirit to you on the cross.

*To Saint Mary.*

Holy and immaculate mother of God, Virgin Mary: I beg you, by the bitter passion and death of your sweetest Son, do not reject me, a wretched sinner, because of my unclean conscience, but remember that you are the mother of mercy; and pray for me to God, whom you bore, that he may mercifully forgive me my sins; make me his fervent lover; guard me this night from all snares and evils; and after the death of the flesh bring my soul to eternal life. To your protection, most blessed Virgin Mary, I commit myself this night and at every time, in the power and union of that commending by which your beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, when now about to die on the cross, commended you to the beloved disciple.

*On the Memory of the Lord’s Passion.*
