Lord Jesus Christ, king of heaven and earth, have mercy on me, for whom you died on the cross; and I humbly give thanks to you for your holy passion and most innocent death. O most loving Savior, what shall I render to you for all the things that you have rendered to me? Behold, I offer myself to you; I continually desire to dwell in your most holy wounds. I ask you, by the virtue of your most innocent death, have mercy on me as you know how; fasten my flesh with your fear, and restrain in me all disordered motions, so that, inflamed by the fervor of your most holy love, I may love you with a pure heart, confess you praiseworthy with my mouth, and follow you in deed, according to my strength, in humble conduct. I beseech you, most merciful Lord Jesus, by that anguish of your most holy heart which you endured on the cross, when your most holy soul departed from your body: have mercy on my soul at its departure.

*The first hour after dinner.*

*The first hour after dinner.* Unless you are occupied with higher exercises, for first obtaining knowledge of frailty, pray for the day in this or a similar manner.

*Prayer*

Almighty God, who out of your great goodness created me in the first parent from the mud of the earth, so that, mindful of my humble condition, I might glorify you, my creator, in true humility: grant me, I beseech you, truly to know my frailty in all things, so that, while I consider that I am both vile and almost nothing by my own merit, I may first deserve to please you by your gift. Through Christ.

*The Second Hour.*

At the second hour after midday let our cenobite bring back to memory the gravity of sins, both his own and those by which, especially while living in holy religion, he has sinned against the rule; and, turned toward the Lord, let him pray thus.

*Prayer*

Almighty everlasting God, who deigned, from your mercy alone, to make us wretched ones, dead in sins, alive by the incarnation and death of your only-begotten Son, our Jesus Christ: grant me, by the virtue of his most innocent passion, indulgence for all my sins and my own, so that henceforth I may deserve to serve you with a pure conscience. Through the same our Lord.

*The Third Hour.*

At the third hour after dinner let our faithful cenobite remember his vows and what he promised the Lord, and especially the renunciation of all property, namely in things, [unclear], and will; and, compunct in fervor of spirit, he shall pray thus to the Lord.

*Prayer*

Almighty and merciful God, who by kindly favor sanctify and receive the vows of your servants, offered to you from faithful devotion: grant me to render all that has been promised to you, and the virtue of humility, chastity, obedience, and poverty, with such purity of mind that I may deserve, with your mercy, to obtain your kingdom promised to the poor in spirit. Through our Lord.

*After vespers:*

After vespers let our good cenobite think that all visible things are vain; and, raised in mind to the love of invisible goods and to the Lord, let him cry out with as much devotion as he can.

*Prayer.*

Almighty everlasting God, who wished to lead us through the visible things which you created to knowledge and love of invisible things: grant me so to know the vanity of present things that I may deserve to think on the eternity of future things with a pure and [unclear] mind.

*Another.*

God, protector of those who hope in you, without whom nothing is strong, nothing holy: multiply your mercy upon us, so that, with you as ruler, with you as guide, we may so pass through temporal goods that we do not lose eternal goods.

*Another prayer.*

God, who have prepared invisible good things for those who love you: pour into our hearts the affection of your love, so that, loving you in all things and above all things, we may obtain your promises, which surpass every desire. Through the Lord.

*After supper and collation:*

After supper or collation let the most faithful cenobite put on desire for good zeal, and especially for souls, with what fervor of charity he can; and, praying to the Lord, let him say.
