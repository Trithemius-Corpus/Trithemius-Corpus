## 2026-06-09 — targeted chunk re-translation (Fable 5 revision pass)

### full_chunk_0071

Re-translated from scratch against the source page-scans, superseding the earlier machine rendering, and independently re-graded against the Latin; the superseded text is retained in version control.

**What was wrong:** The graded rendering added "sweat" to the prayer for the dead; the print (p145) reads only "p sanctissimi sanguinis tui effusionē" — wounds, blood, and death, no sudor anywhere in the segment. Beyond that, the public version mistranslates the print's "capitulum passiuum" (the passive chapter) as "chapter of faults" in both the heading and first sentence, reads "codicis" ("in the book") where the print has "cōditionis" (a saint of any other condition), and paraphrases "per conditoris misericordiam" as "the aid of mercy" while omitting the "¶ Oratio." rubric and the prayer's "Obsecro" opening.

**Key corrections (verified against the scan):**
- Verified the prayer invokes wounds, blood-shedding ("sanctissimi sanguinis tui effusionē"), and death only — no "sweat" in the print (p145).
- "alterius cōditionis sancti" = "a saint of any other condition", not "of any other saint in the book" (OCR/public read "codicis"; print clearly "cōditionis", p145).
- Heading "Primū capitulū passiuū est" and "Primū cap'lm passiuū dicit'" = "the passive chapter", confirmed by the contrasting heading "Secundū capitulū actiuū" on p147; public had "chapter of faults" (a different Latin word, culparum).
- Restored the rubric "¶ Oratio." (*Prayer.*) before the prayer for the dead — omitted in the public version (p145).
- Prayer opens "Obsecro dulcissime dn̄e iesu" = "I beseech you, most sweet Lord Jesus", not "O most sweet Lord Jesus" (the OCR's "O Vscro" is Obsecro).
- "per ɔditoris miscd'iā" = "per conditoris misericordiam" — "through the mercy of the Creator"; public had "through the aid of mercy" (p146).
- "in cenobijs bene reformatis ... delinquit': quotidie emendat'" — plural and passive: "whatever is done amiss in well-reformed monasteries ... is amended daily"; public had singular "a well-reformed monastery" and "can be corrected".
- "ne a dn̄o ... durius iudicetur" — "judged more harshly by the Lord" (public: "by God"); and "Dicit enī sanctus dei ap'ls" — "For the holy apostle of God says" (public: "says to him").
- "pia p̄sumptione cōfido" rendered literally as "with pious presumption I trust" (public paraphrased "pious confidence"); "Deniq3 nouerit" = "Finally let him know" (public: "Then").
- Segment end made honest: the sentence breaks off mid-clause at "qui consortiū" before signature K ij (continues in the next chunk); signature mark recorded as an editorial line; public had "[unclear] fellowship".

**Surviving cruxes (flagged in the chunk's translator's note):**
- "ɔditoris" expanded as "conditoris" (Creator) — the ɔ con-abbreviation is clear but the expansion is flagged as conjectural in the note.
- "corā se" read as "coram se" ("set before him"); abbreviation expansion.
- "vt tāq̄3 bene purgatus" — abbreviation read as "tanquam bene purgatus" ("as one thus well purged").
- Final sentence is incomplete in the print at this segment boundary: "qui consortiū" breaks off at the page end (signature K ij); the continuation ("expectas...") belongs to the next chunk and was not absorbed.
