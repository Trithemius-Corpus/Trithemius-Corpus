*Sermones et Exhortationes ad Monachos* — "Sermons and Exhortations to Monks" — collects Trithemius's addresses to the monastic community at Sponheim during his reforming abbacy (1483–1506). The sermons are practical spiritual direction for Benedictine monks: on obedience, humility, the discipline of the choir, the dangers of private property, and the interior life that the Rule is meant to form. They sit at the heart of the monastic-reform cluster, alongside *De Statu et Ruina Monastici Ordinis* and *De Triplici Regione Claustralium* — less a systematic treatise than the abbot's own voice, pressing his monks toward the observance he wanted for them.
