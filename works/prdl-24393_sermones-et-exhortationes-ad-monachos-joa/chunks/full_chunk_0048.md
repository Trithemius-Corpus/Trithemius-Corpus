Be consenting to the spirit, O flesh, because the apostle says: “But those who are Christ’s have crucified their flesh with its vices and desires.” And you, O men, brothers, who profess the warfare of Christ, who have voluntarily sworn yourselves to the law of monastic life, who have promised to imitate the footsteps of the Savior: consider your calling, so that you may walk carefully, not like fools who are driven by every wind, but as true lovers of Jesus Christ, who by the spirit have put to death the deeds of the flesh. You, I say, my most beloved fellow-soldiers, always keep watch in good works, mortifying for the love of Christ our bodies which are upon the earth, constant in fasts and prayers, and continually occupied in meditation on the divine Scriptures. Always stretch yourselves forward to what lies ahead; every day renew with fresh zeal the fervor of your holy purpose. Flee idleness and all sloth, and diligently consider how brief is the time of this life. Let the brief and moderate labor of the hard contest not frighten you; let the wickedness of the enemy not make you fainthearted, since every labor is quickly overcome by voluntary endurance, and the devil, however savagely he rages, is overcome as soon as you call upon Christ.
