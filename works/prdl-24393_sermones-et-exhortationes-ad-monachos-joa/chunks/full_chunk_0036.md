*That one must pray without ceasing.*

...that you may deserve to obtain mercy. For by holy prayer the conscience of the penitent is cleansed; and he who raises his heart to the Lord in true purity, his prayer does not return empty. O, if you knew, dearest brothers, how pleasing to God are the prayers of a pure mind, and how much benefit they confer upon the soul, you would allow no time to pass without prayers. For our prayer, if it is pure and burning in the fervor of the spirit, is a most faithful messenger to God; it speaks to God on our behalf, and obtains all things that are necessary for our salvation while it prays.

Therefore I admonish you and exhort you in true charity to pray without ceasing, and never to cease from the fervor of the Holy Spirit. Let there be no time for you empty or idle in sluggishness of mind. In the fear of the Lord, I beg you, always be watchful and attentive, so that you may be able bravely to conquer and overcome the temptations that threaten. As soon as foul thoughts begin to knock at the mind, run back to the practice of prayer, and they will be in no way able to prevail against you. For prayer is a great fortification of the soul; in its presence all the contrivances of the opposing side fail. For through the purest prayers all useful things are granted to us by God, and all adversities are driven far away.

The mind is powerfully kindled in the love of God by pure prayers, and whatever occurs of a lighter stain is cleansed by praying. By holy and continual prayers the world is conquered; the plagues of the soul are healed by devout petitions; by tears and weeping the devil is overcome; and he for whom a thousand cohorts are not sufficient for battle is weakened by the prayer of one poor man. Just as wax, melting before the face of fire, is dissolved, so at the fervor of pure prayer all the power of demons, crushed, is enervated.

If a base thought has been cast into your mind by demons, then at once, with the sign of the cross impressed upon your forehead, say: Save me, Jesus Christ, mediator of God and men, and drive away from me all the wickedness of the ancient enemy through your most innocent passion. What can be briefer than this prayer, or more powerful? For as soon as it has been uttered with fervor of mind against the presence or suggestion of demons, every contrivance of iniquity withdraws. For the power of pure prayer is very great, since it both penetrates heaven and by its steadfastness overthrows the powers of the air.

Prayer illuminates the mind, purifies the affection, obtains pardon for sins, and strengthens the spirit in the love of God against all adversaries. Nothing so raises the mind from earthly things to the love of heavenly things; nothing so quickly and powerfully destroys the pleasures of the flesh; nothing so illumines the understanding; nothing likewise so greatly confounds the adversary of truth, as pure prayer, which is poured out to God with the fervor of divine love and is adorned by the testimony of a good conscience. If you desire to be terrible to demons, if you wish to be a friend of God, have a pure conscience and never interrupt spiritual prayer.

For devout prayer can do all things, if it is [offered?] with uprightness of faith and purity of life. Prayer puts demons to flight, enervates the power of the malign, heals the soul languishing in tepidity, and strengthens the spirit that is weak and infirm in the fear of God. Pure prayer makes one near to God, and him whom it had received as a servant it makes to be a friend of God. Pure prayer, fervent in the love of God, penetrates heaven, wonderfully illuminates the understanding of the one praying, closes hell, opens paradise, and brings in the souls of the faithful.

For Truth does not lie, who in the holy Gospel promises, saying: Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to the one knocking it shall be opened. Which of you has ever turned to the Lord with his whole heart and prayed, and has not been heard? Or who has called upon the Lord, who cast down Amalek, trusting in his own strength, not by fighting with iron but by praying with holy prayers? For the continual supplication of the just man avails much, and the prayer of one humbling himself penetrates the clouds.

Elijah was a man subject to suffering like us; and he prayed with prayer that it should not rain upon the earth, and it did not rain for three years and six months. And again he prayed, and heaven gave [rain?], and the earth gave its fruit. By prayer and tears King Hezekiah obtained fifteen years of longer life, and by prayers changed the prophet’s sentence. By prayers holy men shone with miracles, and, supplicating the Lord, recalled the dead to life.
