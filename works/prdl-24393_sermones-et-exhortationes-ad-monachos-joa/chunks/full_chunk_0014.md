There are no greater riches, no treasures, no honors; there is no greater substance of this world than the Catholic faith, which saves sinful human beings, enlightens the blind, heals the sick, baptizes catechumens, justifies the faithful, restores the penitent, increases the just, crowns martyrs, preserves virgins and widows and married persons in chaste modesty, ordains clerics, consecrates priests, and places believers in the eternal inheritance with the holy angels.

Thus in the root of a tree there appears absolutely no form of beauty, and yet whatever beauty or comeliness there is in the tree proceeds from it; so also from the humility of faith, whatever of merit, whatever of blessedness the [unclear] soul has, all proceeds from the foundation of faith. For as the Apostle says, whatever, or everything, that is not from faith is sin, since without faith it is impossible to please God. For one who approaches God must believe that he exists and that he is the rewarder of those who seek him. But the more anyone loves, the more fully he advances in faith. For faith without charity is like something cast away as useless, and a shield without a strap.

O faith, a treasure richer than all things: when formed in the mind of mortal man, you make him equal to the immortals; you conquer demons and strongly overcome all hard and adverse kingdoms. Faith is the foundation of all virtues, without which no spiritual building stands. Faith is the illuminator of the mind and the principal instrument of spiritual warfare. For without the shield of faith the devil is not conquered, nor do you overcome the [unclear] of wanton flesh. Therefore, brothers, let us consider how necessary faith formed by charity is for us, and [unclear], because in this warfare, while we fight, neither is victory granted to us nor is confidence preserved. For faith is a shield, with which we very easily conquer the enemy of our salvation, if we are bound to it by the handle of divine love.
