The continual meditation on necessary death makes a man not trust in earthly things, but with foresight await his departure in every place and time. Meditation on death always awaits the end of life in every place, because it is wholly ignorant of the moment of dying. He who daily meditates on the coming of future death disposes himself at all times for dying with as much diligence as he can. For death is certain to us; all of us are ignorant of the hour of dying. Hence, brothers, we urgently admonish your charities as much as we can, that you always think on death; with such foresight prepare yourselves diligently for dying, so that when the hour of departure comes you may not fear. Let no one promise himself a long life in this world, because nothing so deceives mortals as hope of a longer life. For what does a more advanced age accomplish in the good, except that it makes them liable for more offenses, and always less disposed for dying? The longer we live in this world, the more we fear death. A longer age accumulates greater debts of spiritual obligation, so that an old man fears all the more to come before the sight of the strict judge, the more he knows himself bound before the Lord to render account in many matters. For the soul leaves its body unconquered when it is driven by the heavier goads of its conscience. But he who is pure from every malice of iniquity and adorned for salvation with the necessary comeliness of virtues does not fear to die, because through death he goes to eternal life. Therefore, O monk, live now such as you wish to die, because death cannot be evil when the integrity of a good life has gone before. O reader, whoever loves the Lord Jesus above all things dies [well], because, despising all the goods of this world for love of him, he truly keeps his words. Securely does he depart from this life who always lives in the love of God without stain of conscience. Securely does he die whose life is defiled by no pleasures of this world. For the death of the righteous is a most sweet sleep, because it is a tranquil entrance into eternal life. But the death of sinners is very evil, whose evil life and one contrary to divine institutions has gone before. For him who lives in sins, contrary to God, death becomes a dreadful entrance into hell. Such a man does not die who lives well; rarely does he die well who has always lived badly.
