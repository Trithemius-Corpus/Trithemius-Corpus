*Sermons and Exhortations to Monks by John Trithemius, abbot of Sponheim, later of Wurzburg. Of these sermons there are two books: the first is called Homilies, the second Sermons.*

*Ottomarus Luscinius of Strasbourg: a decastich on the homilies of John Trithemius to monks*

If for anyone it is not enough to follow your gentle laws of right, O Christ, unless he should speak of monks: Serapion, Paul, and Antony once tried the path, which bristles with countless rough places. He strives to show how a rough cloak may torment the body, and does not allow down to be in the bed; so that fasts may drive out the swollen pride of the soul, and brief, [half-slumbering?] rest may cherish the limbs. These things the silent men taught by deeds; but Trithemius is better suited than they, since he is strong in tongue and hand.
