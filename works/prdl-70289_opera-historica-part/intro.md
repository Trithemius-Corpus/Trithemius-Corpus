*Opera Historica* — "Historical Works," Part I — is the collected historical magnum opus of Trithemius's last years, assembling his chronicle of the Franks from their mythical Trojan origins through the Merovingian and Carolingian dynasties to the late medieval period. It follows the universal-chronicle tradition — year-by-year entries assembled from over two hundred manuscript sources — and is an important, if at times credulous, source for early medieval history. (Part II, the Hirsau and Sponheim monastic chronicles, is `prdl-70290` in this corpus.) Like much of Trithemius's historical writing it mingles genuine documentary labour with fabricated sources, the most notorious being the invented *Hunibald*; readers should weigh it against later scholarship, but the breadth of compilation is real.

This is the **1601 Frankfurt edition** — printed by Marne, the standard early-modern collected edition of the historical works (dilibri witness).
