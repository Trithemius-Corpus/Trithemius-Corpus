Under the rule of this Zachariel men also began to live more civilly, to endure the laws of their elders, and to grow tame from their former wildness. Under his leadership, too, Adam, the first man, died, leaving to all his posterity the testament of necessary death. Finally, various human inventions and arts emerged at this time, as the historiographers have expressed more clearly.

*III.*

The fourth ruler of the world was Raphael, spirit of Mercury, who began on 24 February in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 1063, and presided for 354 years and four months; his dominion lasted until the year of the world 1417 and the fourth month. In these times writing was first invented, and letters were first devised after the manner or form of trees and plants; later, however, with time, they both took on a more cultivated ornament, and peoples changed the appearance of the characters according to their own judgment. The use of musical instruments also began to multiply under the rule of this Raphael, and the exchange of merchandise was first invented by men. The rough daring of navigation, too, was first attempted in these times, and many other things of that kind.

*V.*

The fifth governor of the world was Samael, who began on the 26th day of the month of June, in the year of the world 1417. He governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year 1771, the eighth month. Under his rule men imitated the nature of Mars. Likewise, under the governance of this Samael, the universal flood occurred, in the year of the world 1656, as is clearly evident from the history of Genesis. And it should be noted that the ancient philosophers handed down that whenever Samael, spirit of Mars, is ruler of the world, then a notable alteration of monarchy arises. Religions and sects are varied, laws are changed, and principalities and kingdoms are transferred to foreigners, as we can easily find in the histories in order. But Samael does not at once from the beginning of his rule manifest this disposition of his character, but after he has passed the middle of his governance; the same is to be understood of the spirits of the other planets, as is evident in the histories, all of whom, according to the properties of the nature of their stars, influence and work upon the lower things of this world.

*VI.*

The sixth governor of the world was Gabriel, angel of the Moon, who began after Samael of Mars, on the 28th day of October, in the year of the world 1771, the eighth month, and governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 2126. In these times men were again multiplied, and founded various cities. And it should be noted that the Hebrews say that the flood took place in the year of the world 1656, under the rule of Mars. But the Seventy interpreters, Isidore, and Bede confirm that it took place in the year 2242, under the governance of Gabriel, spirit of the Moon, which also seems to me more consonant with truth, by a multiplication which it is not for the present time to express.

*VII.*

The seventh governor of the world was Michael, angel of the Sun, who began on 24 February, in the year of the world, according to the common reckoning, 2126, and governed the world for 354 years and four months, until the year of the age of the world 2480, four months. Under the rule of the angel of the Sun, as the histories agree with the truth, kings first began to exist among mortals; among them Nimrod was the first, through lust for domination, to seize tyrannical rule over his fellows. The worship of gods, too, was instituted by the folly of men, and the humble began to worship their princes as gods. Various arts were also invented by men at this time, namely mathematics, astronomy, and magic; and the worship of the one God began to be offered to diverse creatures, and the knowledge of the true God gradually fell into oblivion through the superstition of men. In these times, too, architecture was invented, and men began to use more refined customs and institutions.

*VIII.*

Thereafter, in the eighth order, Orifiel, angel of Saturn, again began to govern the world on the 26th day of the month of June, in the year from the origin of the world 2480, four months; and for 354 years and four months he governed the world a second time, until the year of the world 2834 and the eighth month. Under the governance of this angel, peoples were multiplied, the earth was divided into regions, many kingdoms were established, the Tower of Babel was built, the confusion of languages took place, men were scattered over the whole earth, and men began to cultivate the earth more carefully, to establish fields, to sow grain, to plant vineyards, to set trees in the ground, and to acquire more diligently whatever pertains to food and clothing.
