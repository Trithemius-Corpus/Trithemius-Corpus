*Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, On the Seven Secondary Beings, that is, on the Intelligences or Spirits that move the spheres after God: a little book, or Mystical Chronology.*

*Comprising within itself many secrets worthy of being known, with marvelous brevity.*

*The Letter of Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, on the little book concerning the heavenly intelligences that govern the spheres after God.*

*To the divine Maximilian I, Emperor Caesar, pious and fortunate.*

[unclear]

It is the opinion of very many of the ancients, most wise Caesar, that this lower world is governed, by the ordering of the first intellect, which is God, through secondary intelligences. Agreeing with their opinion, the Conciliator of the Physicians says that seven spirits were appointed over the seven planets from the beginning of heaven and earth; each of them governs the world for three hundred fifty-four years and four months in sequence. Very many most learned men have hitherto given their assent to this position; I make it known to Your Most Sacred Majesty not by asserting it, but only by reporting it.

*I.*

The first angel, or spirit of Saturn, is called Orifiel. God entrusted the world to him to govern from the beginning of creation, and he began his rule on the fifteenth day of the month of March, in the first year of the world, and it lasted 354 years and four months. Orifiel, however, is a name of office, not of nature, attributed to the spirit by reason of his action. Under his rule human beings were rude and rustic, living in solitude after the manner of beasts. This does not need proof from me, since it is manifest to all from the text of Genesis.

*II.*

The second governor of the world is Anael, the spirit of Venus. After Orifiel, he began to rule the influence of his planet in the year of the world 354, in the fourth month, that is, on the twenty-fourth day of the month of June, and he governed the world for 354 years and four months, down to the year 780 from the founding of the world, as is clear to one who calculates the period. Under the rule of this Anael, human beings began to be more cultivated, to build houses and cities, to discover manual arts, external work, wool-working, and things similar to these; also to indulge far more in the pleasures of the flesh, and there to take beautiful wives, to forget God, and in many respects to depart from natural simplicity; to invent games and songs, to play the lyre, and to devise whatever pertains to the nature and cult of Venus. This wantonness of life among human beings lasted until the Flood, a sufficient proof of its depravity.

*III.*

The third, Zachariel, angel of Jupiter, began to govern the world in the year 780 from the creation of heaven and earth, that is, on the twenty-sixth day of the month of October, and governed the world for 354 years and four months, down to the years of the founded world 1063 inclusive. Under his direction human beings first began to usurp lordship over one another, to practice hunting, to make tents, and to adorn their bodies with various garments; and there arose a great [unclear] among [unclear].
