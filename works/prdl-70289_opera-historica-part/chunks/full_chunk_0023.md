*Mystical Chronology.*

The kingdoms and provinces of the Christians are devastated and captured by the Turks. At this time Christians had many very grave wars among themselves in Gaul, England, Saxony, Westphalia, Prussia, Flanders, Swabia, and other places. In these times the art of printing was newly invented at Mainz, the metropolis of Germany, by wondrous industry, a gift of divinity. In the year of the Christians 1456, the Turks were overthrown in Hungary by the faithful, and many perished. The pilgrimage of children to Saint Michael was remarkable. There were earthquakes in the kingdom of Naples; more than 4,000 men perished. In the year of the Lord 1462, Mainz, the metropolis of the Franks, was captured and plundered. Charles, duke of Burgundy, defeated the French in the year 1465. Afterwards, in the year 67, he destroyed Dinant and Liège. In the year 1473 he entered Guelders and powerfully obtained it, likewise the whole duchy of Lorraine. A comet appeared through the month of January in the year of the Lord 1472. Charles, duke of Burgundy, besieged the town of Neuss for the space of a year, in the year 74; afterwards, in 1477, he was killed in battle, a magnanimous prince.

In these times the Turks took many cities of the Christians: Negroponte in Euboea, also the Bosnian kingdom, the duchy of the [Despot?], Achaia, Mitylene, and several kingdoms in the East. In 1476 a gathering of fools took place at Niklashausen in the Franconia of Germany, full of errors. In the year 1480 the Turks besieged the Rhodians with a great army, but did not prevail. In the same year, departing from Rhodes, they took Hydruntum, more than 12,500 Christians being killed and only 22 escaping by flight. And in the following year the emperor of the Turks, Mehmed, died; Bayezid, his first-born, succeeded him in the kingdom, now ruling into his twenty-seventh year. In the year of the Christians 1486, Maximilian, son of Frederick, was established king of the Romans at Frankfurt; he was greeted as Caesar by Pope Julius in the year 1508. He founded the military order of Saint George against the heretical Turks; he subdued the Swiss in war; he attacks the rebellious Sicambri in war even to this day, and will prosper against all treaty-breakers. The king of the French, persecutor of the Empire in his usual manner, has been found to be devising new plots. May the Almighty protect those assigned to the dominion of Samael in the year [1508?]. The Venetians, rebels against Caesar’s empire, have been marked by ban and war. There will be punishment for obstinacy, and reward for prudent satisfaction.

Around the end of this third revolution of Samael, the image of alteration will pass to the first; it will be the destruction of many. For unless Y., with God ministering, is brought back to Algos, there will be the monarchy of someone, or the transfer of some great kingdom. A great sect of religion will arise, the destruction of old religions. It is to be feared lest the fourth beast lose one head. In Samael, Mars first foretold the Flood, in the second the destruction of Troy; in the third, near the end, there will be great damage to unity. For from preceding things let the future things that follow be judged. This third revolution of Mars will not be completed without the prophecy of some new institution of religion. From here, in the year of the Christians 1508, there remain years up to the year of the world 7086, the eighth month; but of the Lord’s nativity, 1879, likewise two months. The future sequence of this revolution requires prophecy.

I have not written these things assertively, most sacred Caesar; they are by no means to be believed with any injury to the orthodox faith. There are, however, those who have reckoned the months in these matters as lunar; if you decide that they should be agreed with, those things which I have written otherwise must be changed. With my own hand also I protest, and with my mouth I confess, that in all these things I believe nothing, admit nothing, except what the Catholic Church believes; the rest I reject and despise as vain, fabricated, and superstitious.

Praise to almighty God.
