*Of Johannes Trithemius*

...a remarkable change and the new founding of many cities, so that Paris, Mainz, Carthage, Naples, and others arose anew, and kingdoms arose anew, such as those of the Lacedaemonians, Corinthians, Hebrews, and many others. In these times there were very great wars and battles of kings and peoples throughout the whole world, and various changes of empires. From this time the Venetians reckon the beginning both of their people and of their city from the Trojans. And it should be noted that very many other nations, both in Europe and in Asia, claim that they took their origin from the Trojans; to them I have thought fit to grant only as much credence as they themselves can persuade me of the truth by sufficient testimony. Trifling are the things they bring forward about their nobility and antiquity, wishing openly to boast, as though there had been no peoples in Europe before the destruction of the Trojans, and as though there had been no one of ignoble birth among the Trojans themselves. Likewise, under the rule of this planet Saul was made the first king of the Jews; afterward David, whose son King Solomon built at Jerusalem the temple of the true God, most famous in all the world. Hence the Spirit of God, illuminating his prophets with a fuller light of his grace, caused them to foretell not only the future incarnation of the Lord Savior but also many other things, as the sacred histories testify. Among them were Nathan, prophet of King David, Gad, Asaph, Ahijah, Shemaiah, Azariah, Anan, with many others. Homer, the Greek poet and writer of the destruction of Troy, Dares the Phrygian, and Dictys of Crete, who were present at that destruction itself and likewise described it, are read to have been alive in these times.

*XIII.*

In the thirteenth order, Gabriel, the spirit of the Moon, again took up the government of the world on the thirtieth day of January, in the year from the origin of the universe 4252, and continued in his rule for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 4606 and the fourth month. In this time, moreover, several prophets flourished among the Hebrews, namely Elijah, Elisha, Micah, Obadiah, and very many others. There were also various changes in the kingdom of the Hebrews. Lycurgus gave statutes and laws to the Lacedaemonians. Capetus Silvius, Liberius Silvius, Procas Silvius, and Numitor, kings of Italy, flourished under the guidance of this spirit. Many new kingdoms also took their beginnings under him, such as those of the Lydians, Medes, Macedonians, Spartans, and others. The monarchy of the Assyrians came to an end in Sardanapalus. Likewise the kingdom of the Macedonians was consumed. Various laws were imposed on human beings, and the worship of the true God was neglected; the religion of false gods was propagated exceedingly. The city of Rome was built under the rule of this spirit in the year 1484, which was the 239th year of the government of this Gabriel in order; and the kingdom of the Silvii of Italy failed, and that of the Romans began. In these times also Thales, Solon, Chilon, Periander, Cleobulus, Bias, and Pittacus, the seven sages, flourished in Greece, and from this time philosophers and poets began to be held in esteem. First to rule at Rome was Romulus, founder of the city, for 37 years, a fratricide and cultivator of sedition. After him Numa Pompilius ruled for 42 years; he enlarged the worship of the gods, and in the time of Hezekiah king of the Jews he maintained the kingdom in great peace. Toward the end of the governance of this spirit of the Moon, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, took Jerusalem, destroyed King Zedekiah, and led away the whole people captive. Jeremiah the prophet flourished; he foretold this destruction, and likewise the future liberation from Babylon.

*XIV.*

After Gabriel, Michael, the spirit of the Sun, again assumed the fourteenth rule of the world. He began on the first day of the month of May, in the year of the world 4606, in the fourth month, and ruled the world in his order for 354 years, until the year from the foundation of the world 4960 and 8 months. In the time of this governance Evil-merodach, king of Babylon, restored liberty and a king to the people of the Hebrews, at the direction of Michael, who, as Daniel writes, stood for the people of the Jews, to whom they had been entrusted by God. Likewise in these times the monarchy of the Persian kingdom began. Its first king was Darius, and its second Cyrus; in the days of Belshazzar they utterly destroyed that most powerful kingdom of Babylon, as Daniel and the prophets had foretold. In these times the Cumaean Sibyl flourished; she offered nine books to King Tarquinius Priscus to be bought at a price. In them were contained the sequence of future warnings and the plan of the whole commonwealth of the Romans. But when the king refused the price, the Sibyl burned the first three books in his sight, and for the remaining six she demanded the same price. When he again refused it, she also handed over another three to the fire to be burned; and she would have done the same with the last ones, had not the king, persuaded by the counsel of his men, redeemed them from destruction by paying the price that had been demanded for all. Likewise, after abolishing kings, the Romans appointed two consuls for each year. Phalaris the tyrant occupied Sicily in those times. Magic too, in those times, was held in great esteem among the kings of the Persians. Pythagoras the philosopher and many others then flourished among the Greeks.
