*Johannes Trithemius*

... founded for the people of Würzburg across the bridge; he died, however, on the fifth day before the Ides of November, in the year of the Lord’s nativity 1159, under Caesar the third.

*Siegfried.* 53. Siegfried, prelate and duke, presided for five years and six months, and died in the year of the Lord 1155, under Frederick the first emperor, in the second indiction.

*Gebhard.* 54. Gebhard the second, prelate and duke, presided for six years, five months, and twenty-four days, and died in the year of the Lord 1161, under Frederick the first emperor.

*Henry.* 55. Henry the second, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for six years and four months, and died in the year of the Lord 1167, under Frederick the first emperor.

*Herold.* 56. Herold, prelate and duke, presided for seven years, ten months, and eleven days, and died in the year of the Lord 1174, under Frederick the first emperor.

*Reinhard.* 57. Reinhard, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for four years, ten months, and fifteen days, and died in the year of the Lord 1189, under Frederick the first emperor.

*Gottfried.* 58. Gottfried of Pistenberg, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for five years, eight months, and twenty-four days. Having set out for Jerusalem, he died in the army of the Christians and was buried at Antioch, in the year of the Lord [unclear], under Frederick the first emperor.

*Henry.* 59. Henry of Biebelrith, the third of that name, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for six years, eight months, and as many days. He died in the tenth indiction of the Romans, in the year of the Lord 1162, under Henry the sixth.

*Gottfried.* 60. Gottfried of Hoenlo, prelate and duke, a lover of the clergy, presided for six years, four months, and eleven days. He died in the year of the Lord 1198, in the last year of King Henry.

*Conrad.* 61. Conrad, prelate and duke, chancellor of King Philip, presided for five years, three months, and twelve days, and was killed on the public road at Würzburg by Bodoner and Henry, knights of Rabenspurg, and by Herold and Conrad, their servants, on the third day before the Ides of December.

*Henry.* 62. Henry the fourth, a doctor of Paris, called de Taseo, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for four years, seven months, and seven days. And he died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 1210, in the third indiction, under Otto the fourth emperor.

*Otto.* 63. Otto of Lodenburg, prelate and duke, presided for thirteen years and five months, and died on the fifth day of the month of December, in the year of the Lord 1223, under Frederick the second emperor.

*Theodoric.* 64. Theodoric, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for one year, two months, and sixteen days, and died in the year of the Lord 1124, under Frederick the second emperor.

*Hermann.* 65. Hermann of Lodenburg, prelate and duke, presided for nineteen years, and died in the year of the Lord 1240, under Frederick the second, in the thirteenth indiction of the Romans.

*Eringus.* 66. Eringus of Reinstein, prelate and duke, presided for thirty-six years, and died in the year of the Lord 1276, under Emperor Rudolf, in the fourth indiction.

*Berthold.* 67. Berthold of Sternberg, prelate and duke, presided for thirteen years. He died in the year of the Lord 1288, under Emperor Rudolf, in the first indiction.

*Manegold.* 68. Manegold, prelate and duke, presided for fifteen years, and died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 1303, under Albert the first king, in the first indiction.

*Andrew.* 69. Andrew of Gundelfingen, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for fifteen years and died in the year of the Lord 1322, under Louis the fourth emperor.

*Gottfried.* 70. Gottfried the third of Hoenlo, prelate and duke, presided for four years, and died in the year of the Lord 1322, under Louis the fourth, in the seventh indiction.

*Wolfram.* 71. Wolfram of Grunbach, prelate and duke, presided for two years, and died in the year of the Lord 1333, on the octave of the Apostles, under Louis the fourth.

*Otto.* 72. Otto Wolffskell, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for twelve years, one month, and three days. Against him Hermann of Lichtenberg, a canon of Speyer, promoted by the emperor, violently occupied the episcopate for the first four years. Otto died on the tenth day before the Kalends of September, in the year of the Lord 1343, under Louis the fourth emperor.

*Albert.* 73. Albert of Hoenlo, prelate and duke, presided for four years: not elected, but appointed by Pope Clement the sixth against Albert of Hoenlo, who had been canonically elected. But afterwards, when the pope was better informed about the case, he transferred his Albert to the church of Freising in the year of the Lord 1349, and confirmed Albert of Hoenlo, the provost, previously canonically elected, to his own church.

*Albert.* 74. Albert of Hoenlo, prelate and duke, presided for two years, and died on the Sunday after the Nativity of Saint John the Baptist, in the year of the Lord 1352, under Charles the fourth emperor, in the tenth indiction. After him Albert of Hesberg, canon, was unanimously elected by the chapter, but Pope Gregory the eleventh, at the emperor’s request, annulled the election and installed Gerhard of Svvartzenburg.
