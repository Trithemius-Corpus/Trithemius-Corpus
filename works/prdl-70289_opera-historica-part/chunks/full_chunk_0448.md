348 *The Abbot of Spanheim* He dies under Emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1428, indiction 6.

Johannes de Gerson, a Frenchman by nation, chancellor of Paris, [unclear] of Peter de Ailly the cardinal, not ignorant of philosophy, subtle in intellect, scholastic in speech, firm in judgment, and not ignorant of secular philosophy, subtle in intellect, a most distinguished interpreter of sermons, notable in life and conduct, by far the foremost of the theologians of his time. He wrote in verse and prose many very excellent little works, by which his name will always obtain immortality among posterity; the reading of them is no less useful than pleasant to devout and religious men.
