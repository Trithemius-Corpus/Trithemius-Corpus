IX.

In the ninth order Anael, the spirit of Venus, again began to govern the world on the 29th day of October, in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 2834, in the 8th month; and he presided for 354 years and 4 months, down to the year of the world 3189. In these times human beings, consigning the true God to oblivion, began to worship and honor the dead and their statues as God; this error occupied the world for more than two thousand years. Introducing elaborate adornments of the body and various kinds of musical instruments, human beings again pursued lust and the pleasure of the flesh to excess; in these times also people established and dedicated statues and temples to their gods. Incantations and malefic arts were devised in those times by Zoroaster, first king of the Bactrians, and by various others; Ninus, king of the Assyrians, defeated him in war.

X.

In the tenth order Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, again began to govern on the last day of the month of February, in the year of the foundation of heaven and earth 3189; and he governed, as usual, for 354 years and 4 months, down to the year of the world 3543 and the 4th month. These were happy times, which could truly be called golden, in which the abundance of all things both brought increase to the human race and gave the greatest adornment to the world. Likewise at this time God gave Abraham the law of circumcision, and first promised the redemption of the human race through the incarnation of his only-begotten Son. Under the rule of this spirit the patriarchs, founders of justice, were illustrious, and the just were divided from the impious by their own will and deed. In these times also Jupiter was illustrious among the Arcadians; he is also Lisania, son of heaven and of God, a king, who first gave laws to the Arcadians, made them very civilized in their customs, taught the worship of God, built temples, instituted priests, and provided many useful things for the human race. For these reasons he was called Jupiter by them and, after death, was regarded as God. He derived his origin from the son of Eber, namely Jerah, as the histories relate. Prometheus also, the son of Atlas, under the rule of this angel is said to have made men, because from rude people he made them learned, humane, gentle, and refined in morals and knowledge; by art he made images move by themselves. He first devised the use of the ring; likewise he invented the scepter, the diadem, and the royal insignia. Other Jovian persons also were illustrious in these times, very wise men and women, who by their talent handed down many inventions useful to the human race; and because of the greatness of their wisdom, after death they were thought to have been numbered among the gods, namely Phoroneus, who first established laws and judgments among the Greeks; Sol, Minerva, Ceres, Serapis among the Egyptians; and very many others.

XI.

In the eleventh order Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, again assumed rule on the first day of the month of July, in the year of the world 3543, the 4th month; and he presided for 354 years and 4 months, down to the year of the creation of heaven and earth 3897 and the 8th month. In these times, as is clearly evident from the histories of the ancients, human beings devoted themselves more to wisdom; among them were at last very illustrious persons: Mercury, Bacchus, Omogyrus, Isis, Inachus, Argus, Apollo, Cecrops, and very many others, who by their inventions benefited both the world at that time and posterity. Various superstitions also in the worship of idols were established by human beings in these times, and incantations and the arts of diabolical images were wonderfully increased; and whatever subtlety and ingenuity is customarily attributed to Mercury was then augmented. Moses, the very wise leader of the Hebrews, skilled in many matters and arts, worshiper of the true and one God, delivered the Hebrew people from the servitude of the Egyptians into freedom. At this time also Janus first reigned in Italy; after him came Saturn, who taught that fields were enriched with dung, and was regarded as God. Around this time Cadmus invented Greek letters, and Carmentis, daughter of Evander, Latin ones. God almighty, under the rule of this Raphael, angel of Mercury, gave his people the law in writing through Moses, which gives manifest testimony to the future birth of Christ in the flesh. A wonderful diversity of religions also arose in the world. Many Sibyls, prophets, soothsayers, haruspices, magi, and seers also flourished in these times, such as the Erythraean Sibyl, the Delphic, and the Phrygian, with the rest.
