*Abbot of Spanheim*

The *Hammer of Witches*, 5 books. He lives to this day in his convent at Cologne, under Maximilian, most clement king of the Romans, in the year of the Lord in which we wrote these things, 1493, indiction 12.

Johannes Tholosus, a German by nation, born in Eastern Francia, which is called Franconia, canon of Regensburg and provost of Forchheim, a man devoted to and learned in the divine Scriptures, and excellently instructed in secular letters, a distinguished doctor of pontifical law, a very celebrated astronomer, cosmographer, and poet, subtle in intellect and eloquent in speech, wrote certain excellent little works, both in verse and in prose, by which he commended his talent to posterity. But I do not remember that any of them have come into our hands. Yet those who knew the man well report that he has, on various subjects:

Elegant Letters, 1 book. And certain works in mathematics.

And certain other things. He lives to this day in the city of Regensburg, in the province of the Norici, and writes various works, a very great supporter of learned men, under Maximilian, king of the Romans, and Pope Alexander VI, in the year of the Lord 1493, the twelfth indiction.

Adam Werner of Themar, a German by nation, a man excellently instructed in secular letters and not ignorant of the divine Scriptures, a philosopher and no trivial poet, and a most distinguished interpreter of imperial law, outstanding in intellect and eloquent in speech, writes various works, playing in verse and prose, by which he has made his talent known far and wide. There are extant many of his writings, and poems addressed to me, 1 book, beginning: “To the reverend father in Christ.”

Also many letters. He composed for me, On Saint Anne, 1 book.

In addition he has written, and daily writes, many other works to be published. He lives to this day at Heidelberg, tutor of young princes, under Maximilian, king, in the year of the Lord in which we composed these things, 1493.

Johannes of Düsseldorf, a German by nation, of the order of the brothers of the Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, prior of Strasbourg, a man devoted to and learned in the divine writings, and not ignorant of secular philosophy, a very celebrated interpreter of the faculty of theology, outstanding in intellect and distinguished in life, is said to write many things, but [unclear]. I saw certain treatises of his, whose titles I have not now been able to recall to memory. He lives to this day at Strasbourg, under Maximilian, most clement king of the Romans, in the year of the Lord 1493, the twelfth indiction.

Theodericus Visenius, a Frisian man of singular learning, persistent in poetry and oratory. Among his works are extant:

Cultivated Elegies. And other epigrams, 1 book. Of the rest I have seen nothing.

And many other things which will come down to posterity. He is still living under Maximilian, king of the Romans, in the year of the Lord 1494.

Hartmann Schedel of Nuremberg, a German by nation, doctor of arts and medicine, and not ignorant also of the divine Scriptures, outstanding in intellect and clear in speech, compiled and wrote, among other little works of his talent, from Jacobus of Bergamo and other historiographers, adding some things, especially concerning German affairs, a great and distinguished work, which contains:

Histories of the Times, 10 books. Of the rest I have seen nothing.

He lives to this day at Nuremberg, under King Maximilian, in the year of the Lord 1494. [unclear]
