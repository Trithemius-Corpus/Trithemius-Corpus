He also published many other smaller works besides, indeed, but very useful ones, of which no certain knowledge has come down to us. He was personally prominent in the Council of Constance, in which, laboring gloriously for the extirpation of heresies, at length, when that council was dissolved, he was deprived, for defending the truth, of home, country, relatives, friends, dignities, and his own possessions, and was sought by many hostile persons. Hence, compelled by necessity, he went to the city of Lyon, and there after a few years he died, under the emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1429, the seventh indiction, in the sixty-sixth year of his age, on the fourth day before the Ides of July. He was buried there with honor among the Celestines.
