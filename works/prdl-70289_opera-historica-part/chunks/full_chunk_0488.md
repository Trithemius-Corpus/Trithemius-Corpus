*Harynghus* Sifridi Sinnama, from The Hague, of Frisian origin, who is commonly called Hermannus, German by nation, professor of both laws and a most celebrated interpreter, and excellently learned both in the divine Scriptures and in secular philosophy; subtle in talent, scholastic in speech, ready and steady in counsel, whose authority is held in great esteem among princes and prelates of churches, easily holds the first place in teaching, writing, and giving counsel in the university of Cologne. *Raphael Regius*, an Italian by nation, a man most learned in every respect, a renowned philosopher, a celebrated poet, and a distinguished orator, skilled in both languages; excellent in talent and eloquent in speech. In the university of Padua he acquired the highest praise for his learning by teaching, disputing, and composing. He is said to have produced many distinguished little works, by which he made his name and talent known with glory. Of these the following are extant: Corrections on Quintilian, one book, “High-minded men are accustomed [unclear].” *On the Praises of Eloquence*, one book, “If ever anyone of distinguished [unclear].” On one letter of Pliny, one book, “In the inscription of this work.” *Against Johannes Calphurnius*, one book, “Although with very weighty [unclear].” A dialogue against the same, one book, “Yesterday you disturbed the boys.” Various orations, one book. Also many letters. And several other things. *An Introductory Notice to the Fasti*, one book. And certain commentaries. He is still living at Padua, as I have learned, and is composing various works, under Maximilian, king of the Romans, and the supreme pontiff Alexander VI, in the year of the Lord 1494.

*Laurentius Bonincontrius*, a philosopher and distinguished astronomer, and excellently learned in the other disciplines of the liberal arts; outstanding in talent and clear in speech, strong in verse and prose. He is said to have produced many distinguished little works, by which he has transmitted his name to the knowledge of many, but up to now I have not been able to see any of them. There is said, however, to be a notable work of his, *through the circle of the year*.
