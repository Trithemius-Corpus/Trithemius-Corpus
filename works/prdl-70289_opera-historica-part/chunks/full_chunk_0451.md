*On Ecclesiastical Writers*

On perseverance, seven books: “Since grave matters often, as happens...”
On the penitential Psalms, one book. In praise of Saint Monica, one book.
A thirteenth book of the Aeneid in heroic verse, one book. Several epigrams, one book.
On military affairs. A dialogue, which is called Philaletes.
Many letters. And very many other works.
He flourished in the times of the Emperor Sigismund and Pope Martin V, in the year of the Lord 1430.

Johannes Plaeth, German by nation, the ornament and pillar of the school of Heidelberg, a man most learned in the divine Scriptures and nobly instructed in Aristotelian philosophy, subtle in intellect, scholastic in speech, distinguished in life and conduct, insofar as the secular state allows, an excellent doctor in delivering sermons to the people. Among other things he wrote a great and distinguished volume, which he read in ordinary and public lectures at the aforesaid University of Heidelberg.

On the first chapter of the Decretals, one book: “Venerable fathers...”
Against the Bohemians, one book: “As the wise man says...”
Questions on the Sentences, one book. Several sermons.
Various questions. And certain other things.
He flourished under the Emperor Sigismund, in the year of the Lord 1430.

Johannes Dieppurg, called “of Frankfurt,” German by nation, a doctor of Heidelberg, a man learned in the divine Scriptures and not ignorant of secular philosophy, an acute disputant and an excellent declaimer of sermons, formerly chaplain and secretary of Ludwig, duke of Bavaria and count palatine of the Rhine, and often an envoy in many difficult cases. He wrote little works not to be scorned in reading, by which he left the memory of his name to posterity. Of these, the following are reported:

Against the Hussites, one book: “When recently, in the year of the Lord...”
Against the Feymeri, one book: “The law of the Lord for external [unclear]...”
On contracts, one book: “As in the forum of the soul, where in...”
On predestination, one book: “Glory to God in the highest...”
Many sermons, one book: “I have a word for you...”
An oration before the king of England, one book. An oration before the king of France, one book.
An oration before the duke of Savoy, one book. Several letters.
Various questions, one book. [unclear]
Various orations. He flourished in the times of the Emperor Sigismund and Pope Eugenius IV, in the year of the Lord 1430.

Raymundus Sebeide, Spanish by nation, a man devoted to and learned in the divine Scriptures, and excellently instructed in secular letters, a distinguished doctor of arts and medicine, who by teaching and writing in the school of Toulouse gave great proof of his learning. He is reported to have written many splendid little works; of these I have seen only a large volume, which he had read publicly in the aforesaid university, and at its beginning he promises many and glorious things, concerning the truth of which some applaud themselves as experienced men, because he set it forth beforehand.

Of creatures, or On man, one book: “To the praise and glory...”
Disputed questions, one book. The rest are unknown to me.
He flourished in the times of the Emperor Sigismund and Pope Eugenius IV, in the year of the Lord 1430.

Franciscus Barbarus, a Venetian patrician, a pupil in Greek letters of the Byzantine philosopher Chrysoloras, a man most learned in secular letters and not ignorant of the divine Scriptures, a famous philosopher and a supreme orator in both languages. While still a young man he wrote certain little works not unworthy of reading, by which he transmitted his name to the knowledge of posterity. Of these there survives the work which he entitled:

On wifely duty, one book. A collection of letters to various persons, sixteen books.
And certain other things.
From Plutarch he translated into Latin, for his brother Zacharias, the life of Aristides and of Cato, and of many illustrious men. Likewise also other things.
He died in the times of the Emperor Frederick III and Pope Nicholas V, in the year of the Lord 1454, in the second indiction.
