Muhammad the Arab introduced the sect of the Saracens in these times, around the year of Christ 600; through it the Roman empire in Asia is now seen to have been wholly taken away. *XVIII.*

In the eighth place after Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, took up the government of the world for the third time, on the second day of the month of November, in the year from the creation of the universe 6023, in the eighth month, but in the year of the Lord’s nativity 817; and he presided over the world for [354?] years and 4 months, until the year of the world 6378, but of the Lord 1171. At the beginning of this revolution, as we have said, the monarchy of the Roman empire was transferred to Charlemagne. After Charles, his son Louis ruled for 25 years. Under Louis the Second, blood rained from heaven in Italy for three days. In Saxony a certain village, with all its buildings and people, was swallowed up in a moment by a horrible opening of the earth. Around the years of the Lord 910, great disturbances occurred in Italy, and Italy broke away from the empire of the Franks and set up kings of its own; the first of them was Berengar, duke of Friuli, and seven succeeded him in order, for almost fifty years, down to the transfer of the empire to the Germans. Otto became the first emperor, and from this point the monarchy began to be restored; his son Otto and his grandson Otto succeeded him in the empire. Under them the Hungarians were converted to the Christian faith. Otto the Third, dying without children, established the electors of the empire after him, in the year of the Christians 1002, as they remain to the present day. Jerusalem, however, was taken by the Saracens. Many portents were seen in heaven, air, earth, sea, and waters. When Otto the Third had died, Henry was the first to succeed by election of the princes; he ruled for 20 years. He founded the Church of Bamberg, and, dying a virgin together with his wife Kunigunda, shone with miracles. After him Conrad was elected, the first duke of the Franks, and he ruled for 20 years. Likewise Godfrey, count of Bouillon, recovered the Holy Land and Jerusalem from the hands of the infidels. Before the end of the revolution many signs and portents were seen, and after a short time had elapsed the people of the Tartars, going forth from their own borders, did many evils to the Roman empire. Famines, pestilences, and earthquakes occurred in the empire. Three suns were seen in the East, and the same number of moons, in the year of the Lord 1153. Frederick I, called Barbarossa, began to rule, and he ruled for 33 years. The beginning of his rule was in the 336th year of Raphael. He performed many deeds magnificently, enlarged the forces of the empire, and successfully waged many wars; in his time [unclear] and [unclear] were converted to the faith of Christ.

*XIX.*

In the nineteenth order Samael, the spirit of Mars, took up the universal empire of the world for the third time, on the third day of March, in the year of the world [6378?], and governed for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 6732, in the fourth month, but of the Lord 1525. Under his rule there were many wars throughout the world; in them countless thousands of people perished, and very many kingdoms lost their former boundaries. Between Frederick the First, emperor, and the Roman nobles there were many discords and great battles, and many thousands of Romans perished. The said Frederick overthrew Milan from its foundations. Jerusalem was again taken by the Saracens. The empire of all the Tartars, the greatest in the world at this time, took its beginning in these times; they inflicted a very great scourge on the world, and they do not cease. After Frederick, his son Henry was chosen emperor. When he had died, schism threw the empire into confusion under Philip and Otto, and many endless battles followed in Germany, at Strasbourg, Cologne, Liege, Worms, Speyer, and throughout the whole kingdom.
