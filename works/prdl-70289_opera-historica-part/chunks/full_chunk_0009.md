They, indeed, the more learned and more renowned for sanctity they were, always had all the sharper and more numerous detractors. *The Much Celebrated Medicinal Powder of the Same Trithemius*

Many have always praised up to this point this powder of Trithemius, very useful for the brain and for students. For not a few, by using it, have reached the point that they could easily disregard those inspectors of urine, arteries, tongues, and excrements. Many have extended sound life almost to the hundredth year; finally they have attained such keenness of memory that they easily surpassed any men, even the greatest. And this without pills, electuaries, [unclear], ointments, plasters, electuaries, chewing preparations, gargles, [unclear], fumes, preserves, syrups, waters, theriacal preparations, and the like.

For indeed it is effective against many illnesses, and is a most proven and efficacious powder. It strengthens the stomach, purges the brain, clears the eyes and sight, sharpens the memory, consumes bad and superfluous humors in the whole body without danger, soothes and opens, drives away constriction, nausea in the [unclear], prevents excessive fatness, preserves from epilepsy and apoplexy, dissolves the matter of stone without pain and does not allow it to harden into stone, and does not permit chiragra, podagra, or sciatica to prevail.

When Paulus Scalichius wished to have the Duke of Prussia, to whom he was then counselor, use this powder, the prince’s physicians forbade it. From this arose a dispute among them, in which they fought in writing for both sides concerning this powder. That inquiry is extant in book two of the *Philosophical Satires* of the same Scalichius.

*Effigy of Abbot Trithemius*

Above human comprehension and the senses of the wise,
and minds, a genius to whom genius belonged;
Could Trithemius not surpass by wondrous art
whatever is highest on earth, whatever is in the vault of heaven?
Cease to wonder. So great a man, learned in all things,
by nature’s instinct was wholly keenness.

Melissus made it.
