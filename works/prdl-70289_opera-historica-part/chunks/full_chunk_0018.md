And it should be noted that through almost the whole period of this rule of Anael, angel of Venus, the Church of the Christians flourished and prevailed amid persecutions, countless thousands of men being killed for the faith of Christ. Finally, many heresies in these times began to spring up in the Church, which in time were extinguished not without the labor and blood of good men. In these times there flourished many men most learned and eloquent in every kind of knowledge: theologians, astronomers, physicians, orators, historiographers, and others like them, not only among the nations but also among Christians. At length the persecution of unbelievers against the Church ceased after Constantine Caesar the Great accepted the faith, in the year of the world 5539, after the middle of the rule of this Anael, angel of Venus; although even afterwards it was sometimes disturbed in part by impious men, nevertheless it remained very quiet for a long time. From this time the human race, which from the time of King Ninus had wandered wretchedly in the worship of idols for 2300 years, was mercifully called back to the knowledge of one God. Various arts of subtlety were increased in these times, and through the harmonies of nature received adornment and growth. For the morals of men change with time, and lower bodies are disposed according to the influence of the higher ones. The mind indeed is free, and does not receive the influence of the stars, unless, by reason of the too great commerce which it has with the body, it stains its own affection by inclining itself. For the angels, who are the movers of the spheres, destroy or overturn nothing of those things which nature has established.

A comet of unusual magnitude preceded the death of Constantine. The Arian heresy troubled the holy Church in many places at the end of this rule. In the time of Julian Caesar, crosses appeared on men’s linen garments. In Asia and Palestine there followed wars, plagues, and famines in those places in which the crosses had appeared. In these times also, around the year of the Lord 360, the Franks took their beginning in Germany; afterwards, devastating Gaul, they called the conquered land Francia by their own name. The extent of Francia was long and broad in size; its metropolis was Mainz, but now only Würzburg. The Bavarians, Swabians, Rhinelanders, Saxons, Thuringians, and the surrounding bishoprics today occupy a great part of Francia in Germany. Likewise, in the 280th year of the governance of this Anael, the Roman empire began to decline, when the city was captured by the Goths and burned, the imperial seat having previously been transferred under Constantine to Greece; this was the beginning and cause of the decline of the whole monarchy. For around the end of this rule of Anael there arose Radagaisus, Alaric, and Athaulf, kings of the Goths; likewise afterwards Genseric of the Vandals, and also Attila of the Huns, who, roaming through all Europe, wretchedly tore the empire apart, as is clear in the histories.

*XVII.*

After Anael, in the seventeenth order, Zachariel, spirit of Jupiter, resumed the universal rule for the third time on the first day of January, in the year of the world 5669, the fourth month, but of the Lord’s Nativity 463, the seventh month; and he presided for 354 years and four months, up to the year of the world 6023 and the eighth month, but of the Lord [unclear]. In these times many, through love of Christian philosophy, betook themselves to the desert; many portents also appeared: comets, earthquakes, rain of blood. Merlin, born in Tumba, foretold marvelous things at the beginning of this rule. Arturus, whom the common people call Arthur, the most famous king of Britain, defeated the barbarians, restored peace to the Church, fought many battles as victor, enlarged the faith of Christ, and subjected all Gaul, Norway, Dacia, and many provinces to his rule. He was the most glorious of all the kings of his time; after accomplishing many outstanding deeds, he appeared nowhere, though the Britons expected for many years that he would return. Of him the mimes once produced marvelous songs. In his reign England was in flower, and thirty kingdoms served him. In these times the orders of monks in the Church of God began to multiply. Theodoric, king of the Goths, an Arian, possessed all Italy and killed the consul Boethius. Everything was full of disturbance, both the empire and the Church. Zeno and Anastasius, Arian emperors in the East, Theodoric and his successors in Italy, and Honorius, king of the Vandals, in Africa, exercised no small tyranny. At length Clovis, king of the Franks in Gaul, having become a Christian, both overcame the Goths and imposed peace on affairs, though not everywhere on earth, in the time of Saint Benedict, in the five-hundredth year of the Christians, or thereabouts, at the beginning of the rule of this angel Zachariel of Jupiter, whose proper function as a spirit is to change empires and kingdoms; that this was done in this revolution the histories declare in many ways.
