*Compendium, Book I.*

[in the] times of the aforesaid Childeric, king of the Franks. After Odoacer’s departure from the Rugii, the Lombards invaded Rugium; over them at that time a fifth king, named Gudeoch, held rule, and they lived in that kingdom for some time.

But Childeric, king of the Franks, after he had performed many deeds bravely, died in the twenty-third completed year of his reign, but the twenty-sixth if the interruption is counted, and in the year of the Lord’s nativity 484, in the seventh Roman indiction.
