*On Ecclesiastical Writers*

I hear that he is composing for me a work on the calamities of these times, which I have not yet seen. He has written very many letters, both to me and to others, in verse and in prose, not without elegance. He is living to this day and composes various works under Maximilian, king of the Romans, and Pope Alexander VI. In the year of the Lord in which we wrote these things, 1494.

Jodocus Beysselius, a patrician of the royal city of Aachen, a German by nation, a man devoted to the sacred Scriptures and most learned in secular letters, a distinguished philosopher, eloquent orator, and notable poet, although he is necessarily distracted by care for his wife and household estate, nevertheless burns with an incredible love of the Scriptures, writing and composing many things every day. Of these the following are extant, by which his name is everywhere spread with great glory. Of these the following are extant:

A very beautiful dialogue addressed to Hermolaus Barbarus, in the manner of the Platonists, *On the Best Kind of Musicians*, one book. “I have brought you, O Hermo[laus]...”

*On Christian Ambition*, one book. “Even if all things are at hand for you...”

*Epitome of the Goad of Love*, one book. “Since through these, Christina...”

*On the Rose-like Crown of Mary*, one book. “Most august rose-like...”

*A Metrical Rosary*, one book. “Whoever loves the rose-like...”

*On the Mystery of the Rosary*, one book. “Go forth, daughters of Sion...”

*Rosary on the Sacrament of the Altar*, one book. “Under this small bread...”

*Deeds of the Flemings*, one book.

Most elegant orations.

Also many letters. Many poems and epigrams.

And certain other things.

He lives to this day at Aachen, a most distinguished interpreter of both laws, and writes various works under Maximilian, king of the Romans. In the year of the Lord in which we were about to write these things, 1494.

Philippus Callimachus, an Italian by nation, a Florentine by homeland, secretary of the king of Poland, most learned in every respect, a distinguished philosopher, an eloquent orator and fluent in speech. He is said to have published many excellent little works, by which he left his memory to posterity.
