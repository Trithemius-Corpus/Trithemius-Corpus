And so King Conrad, intending to go into Italy with Frederick, duke of the Swabians, against the pope and Roger, encountered Welf, duke of Bavaria, opposing him with a great army of Bavarians and others. Duke Henry, however, Welf’s brother, had remained at home because of old age. And so a very severe battle took place between them in the diocese of Augsburg, near a certain monastery of the order of Saint Benedict, which is called Neresheim. There Frederick, duke of the Swabians, on the king’s side, obtained the victory, and many on Welf’s side fell slain, and he himself barely escaped with a few.

Again Duke Welf, having gathered a great army, fought a battle against Duke Frederick near Weinsberg, around the village of Ellenhofen in the diocese of Wuerzburg, in which he too fell back, and the greater part of his army was laid low.

Therefore Henry, duke of Bavaria, surnamed the Proud, excommunicated by the judgment of the princes and deposed from the duchy of Bavaria by King Conrad, came poor and on foot from Bavaria with his wife and children into Saxony, and remained there until death. Leopold, margrave of Austria, received his duchy from King Conrad.

Leopold, margrave, duke of Bavaria: this Leopold, margrave of Austria, received the duchy of the Norici after Duke Henry had been deposed by King Conrad. He was the son of the elder Margrave Leopold, by Agnes, daughter of Emperor Henry IV, who before Leopold had had Frederick, duke of the Swabians. When this Duke Leopold died not long afterward, the king conferred the duchy upon Henry his brother.

Henry, margrave, duke of Bavaria, was the brother of Leopold. After his brother’s death, King Conrad gave to him as wife the widow left by Henry, duke of Bavaria, whom he had deposed, Gertrude, daughter of Emperor Lothar, and established him as duke of Bavaria.

Otto, bishop of Bamberg, son of Count Berthold, a bold man and of most holy conduct, was held renowned in these times.

*Restoration of the Duchy of Bavaria to the Legitimate Heirs*

When Conrad, king of the Romans, had died, Frederick, the first of this name, son of Duke Conrad, succeeded. In the year of the Lord 1156, around the feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Mary ever Virgin, at the request of the princes he restored the duchy to Henry, duke of Saxony, son of the elder Henry the Proud, once duke of Bavaria, removing Henry, brother of Leopold, margrave of Austria. These things were done at Regensburg, in the year as above.

At the same time Emperor Frederick raised the march of Austria into a duchy, appointing as the first duke in Austria the aforesaid Henry, brother of Leopold, whom he had removed from the duchy of Bavaria, releasing him from all jurisdiction of the duke of Bavaria, and giving several counties of Austria to Henry, duke of Bavaria, as recompense and exemption. For previously the margraves of Austria, whenever they had been summoned by the dukes of Bavaria to their court, were accustomed to appear as subjects to them, just as counts and other nobles are still bound to obey. The same Emperor Frederick I raised the duchy of Bohemia into a kingdom.

Henry, duke of Bavaria and Saxony, son of Henry the Proud, restored to the paternal inheritance of both duchies, took the greatest care to rule and govern the people subject to him justly and according to the laws, in all peace and tranquillity. Whoever violated the laws issued by him was punished with death. Therefore, peace having been established for all throughout all Bavaria together with justice, Duke Henry had praise and honor in everyone’s mouth above all the princes of that time.

*Transfer of the Duchy of Bavaria to the Counts of Scheyern*
