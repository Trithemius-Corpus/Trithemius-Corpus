14. Gotfridus succeeded his father for twenty-four years. He was a pious prince, yet he was not able to convert all his subjects to the faith of Christ. For at that time the Franks were stiff-necked in accepting the faith, and the greater part of them was still pagan in the duchy. Duke Gotfridus died in the year of the Lord 695.

15. Genebaldus succeeded his father for twenty years. In his time Saint Gregory the Pope sent preachers into England. This duke died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 665.

16. Clodomirus succeeded his father in the duchy of the eastern Franks for twenty-three years. In his first year Anastasius the Persian, a monk, was martyred. He died in the year of the Lord 639.

17. Heribertus, nephew of Clodomirus, succeeded in the duchy for thirty years. In his time Saint Furseus, abbot in Ireland, flourished. This duke died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 668.

18. Clodoveus succeeded Heribertus his uncle for twelve years. In his time Clodoveus was king of the Franks in Gaul. Duke Clodoveus died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 680.

19. Gosbertus succeeded his father for twenty-six years. In his sixth year, Pope Canon ordained Saint Kilian, a Scot by nation and a monk by profession, as bishop, and sent him into eastern Francia to preach the faith of Christ. After he had spent two years at Herbipolis in preaching, together with his ministers, Colman the priest and Totnan the deacon, he was martyred by order of Geilana, the duke’s wife, in the year of the Lord 689. Duke Gosbertus died in the year of the Lord 806.

20. Hetanus succeeded his father in the duchy for thirteen years. In his time Saint Aegidius, abbot, flourished in Gaul. This duke died in the year of the Lord’s nativity 320.

21. Hetanus succeeded his father in the duchy for twenty years and died without sons, in the year of the Lord 761, leaving one daughter named Immina, who was a nun and of holy conduct. And so Pippin, mayor of the palace and afterward king of the Franks, took the vacant duchy to himself and entrusted it to his son Charles to administer for ten years. Then, in the year of the Lord 751, when Pippin was already king, with the consent of his son Charles and of all the nobles of the whole kingdom of the Franks, he gave the duchy as perpetual property to Saint Burghard, first bishop of the Wirtzburgers, and to his successors.

22. Therefore Burghard, established by Pippin as duke and bishop of the eastern Franks, presided for forty years. He was upright in morals and most holy in conduct; from a monk of Saint Benedict he was ordained bishop by Boniface, archbishop of Mainz. Burghard resigned the episcopate, together with the duchy of the Franks, to the church and to his successor, in the eleventh year of his rule, but in the year of the Lord 791; and in that same year the same Saint Burghard died.

23. Megengaldus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for four years; he too was a monk. Others, in the manner of the Hollanders, turning I into V, call him Mergingaudus, a holy man. He died in the year of the Lord 752, on the third day before the Kalends of March, under Charles the Great, emperor.

24. Bernwolphus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for six months, one month, and twenty-three days, and died on the eighth day before the Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 800, under Emperor Charles.

25. Ludericus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for five years. And he died in the year of the Lord 753, on the third day before the Kalends of March, under King Arnulf among the Saxons, killed in time of war during the solemnities of the Mass.

26. Agilvardus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for five years, one month, and eighteen days, and died on the eighth day before the Kalends of May, in the year of the Lord 715, under Emperor Lothar.

27. Wolfgerus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for twenty-two years, six months, and twelve days, and died on the day before the Nones of November, in the year of the Lord 931, under Louis.

28. Hunibertus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for ten years, three months, and seven days. He died on the seventh day before the Ides of March, in the year of the Lord 822, under Emperor Lothar.

31. Rodulphus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for [unclear] years, and died on the third day before the Nones of August, in the year of the Lord 1108, under Louis III.

29. Gosswaldus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for thirteen years, five months, and eighteen days, and died on the twelfth day before the Kalends of October, in the year of the Lord 756, under Emperor Louis II.

30. Arno, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for thirty-six years, six months, and twelve days. Famous for miracles, he died on the third day before the Ides of July, in the year of the Lord 792, under King Arnulf among the Saxons, in time of war, killed during the solemnities of the Mass.

32. Dracolfus, prelate and duke of the Franks, presided for five years and four months. Sent by Emperor Conrad I into Greece, he died on the journey, in the year of the Lord 913.
