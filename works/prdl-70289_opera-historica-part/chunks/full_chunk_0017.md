*XV.*

After Michael, fifteenth in order, now for the third time Orifiel, the spirit of Saturn, assumed the rule of the world on the last day of the month of September, in the year 4960, month 8, from the creation of the universe. He presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 5315. Under his rule the Punic War began between the Romans and the Carthaginians. The city of Rome was almost wholly consumed by fire and water. The Colossus, a cast bronze image 126 feet high, fell in an earthquake. In this one year there was peace for the Romans after the Punic War; for 440 years they had never been without war. Jerusalem, together with the temple, was burned and destroyed by Antiochus Epiphanes. The history and wars of the Maccabees were carried on. In these times, in the year 606 from the founding of the city, Carthage was destroyed; it burned for 17 continuous days. In Sicily there was a conspiracy of 7,000 slaves against their masters. Great portents were seen in Europe in those times: domestic animals fled to the woods; blood flowed; a fiery globe flashed from heaven with a crash. Mithridates, king of Pontus and Armenia, waged war with the Romans for 40 years. The kingdom of the Hebrews was restored, which had been interrupted for 575 years, from the time of Zedekiah down to Aristobulus. Likewise the German Teutons invaded the Romans; after many battles they are said to have been defeated, and 160,000 were killed, besides innumerable others who killed themselves together with their households, under the consuls Gaius and Manlius. Nevertheless, many of the Romans had previously been destroyed by them. Finally, the civil wars, which lasted 40 years, greatly wore down the Romans. Three suns appeared at Rome, but not for long, and soon they were reduced to one. A few years later Julius Gaius Caesar usurped the empire of the Romans; Octavian Augustus after him enlarged it, and united Asia, Africa, and Europe into one monarchy. He ruled for 36 years; through him God bestowed peace on the whole world. In the year 751 from the founding of the city, the 42nd of Octavian Caesar Augustus, in the 245th year and 8th month of the aforesaid rule of Orifiel, spirit of Saturn, on the twenty-fifth day of the month of December, JESUS CHRIST, Son of God, was born of the Virgin Mary in Bethlehem of Judaea. Note how beautiful was the ordering of divine providence. For the world was first created under the rule of Orifiel, and under his third rule also was mercifully redeemed, restored, and renewed, so that so great a harmony of events seems to furnish no small credibility to this description of the rule of the world by the seven spirits. For under the first rule of Orifiel there was one monarchy of the whole world; under the second, as we said above, it was divided among many; again under the third, as is seen, it was recalled into unity. It is clear, if we measure rightly, that it is also manifest that under the second rule of Orifiel there was one monarchy of the whole world, when the tower of Babylon was being built. From this time the kingdom of the Jews was taken away, and the victims and continual sacrifice ceased; nor will liberty be restored to the Jews before the third revolution of Michael the spirit; and this will happen after the birth of Christ in the year 1880, month 8, that is, in the year of the world 7170.
