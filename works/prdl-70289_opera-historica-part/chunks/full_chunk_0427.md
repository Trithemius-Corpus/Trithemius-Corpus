Johannes Balistatii, a Catalan by homeland, of the order of the brothers of Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, the seventeenth prior general, was a man devoted to and learned in the divine Scriptures, who governed his order strenuously in deed and word for sixteen years. He composed distinguished monuments of his talent, of which the following are reported:

On the Sentences, four books. On the War of the Church Militant, one book. Various sermons, one book. And certain other works.

The aforementioned notable work on the fierce war of the Church Militant and of Antichrist attacking her he sent dedicated to Pope Gregory XI. He died in the times of Emperor Charles IV and Pope Gregory XI, in the year 1374, in the twelfth indiction, in his convent of Majorca.

Johannes of Hildesheim, a German by nation, of the order of the brothers of Blessed Mary ever Virgin of Mount Carmel, learned in the holy Scriptures and nobly educated in secular letters, an eminent theologian, philosopher, and poet, was of excellent talent in delivering sermons to the people. He was formerly at Avignon the familiar and devoted servant of blessed Peter Thomas, the general, a most holy man, of whom we made mention above. He wrote in verse and prose many distinguished little works, by which he transmitted his name with glory to posterity. Of these the following are reported.
