[People?] change, customs also vary, minds change, and garments vary; the simplicity of languages is altered, the ancient proper character of idioms is varied; nothing stable is found in human affairs. For there is change in all things; variety succeeds to simplicity of minds. Who will be able to bring this perpetual flux, so to speak, back to the concord of unity? For many times pass away, as Scripture says, and knowledge will be manifold. But the fact that we add a third book to the two preceding books of the Polygraphy has not lacked a special reason. For, since human wills are various, many methods are rightly given for honorable operations. In the former, night illuminates day; in the latter, however, day lacks light. There light leads shadow around in light; here, however, the blind man illuminates the blind, being and remaining sight without eyes, and serves to darkness what he does not possess. Here night clouds day with perpetual darkness, and, walking in darkness without light, furnishes guidance to the blind. These paraphrasmata serve all men throughout the world; in every language they transmit any secret safely, securely, and without danger to the cabalistic binary. Latin makes barbarians laugh; barbarism itself generates suspicion among Latins. Yet it matters nothing, or little, since however great the suspicion may have been, it will nonetheless contain every mystery in the utmost secrecy. Let the diligent operator recall to mind the rules and precepts that we have handed down, lest he labor uselessly and in vain for himself and for others. For understanding is needed; without it the usefulness of no art will stand. In a few words we have shown the path to the greatest things; with finite things we have given a method for infinite things. From what is given and received, greater things are easily prepared for friends. Reader, remember that the retrograde mark can be fastened in every place, until you attain the desired rest. Farewell. From Peapol, on the first day of March, in the year of the Lord 1508.
