Although in the Latin language we have no need of k, y, and w, nevertheless, because we use k and w in German, we have brought this alphabet also to the twenty-fourth number for extending it. I have also found another alphabet, newly devised by a certain alchemist, which he was accustomed to use in concealing his secrets, making a great estimate of his art, which contains little in itself besides words. For alchemy is loved by many, and is chaste.

Alchemy has many domestic handmaids and familiars, who guard their mistress with perpetual vigilance, and put themselves forward in her name, so that they may preserve her from intercourse with so many importunate lovers, untouched for all time. Vanity, fraud, deceitfulness, deception, sophification, greed, falsity, lying confidence, stupidity, want, poverty, despair, flight, proscription, and beggary are the attendants of alchemy, who, pretending to be the beloved mistress, keep her inviolate, and gladly offer themselves in her place to her moneyed, avaricious, greedy, and foolish seekers.
