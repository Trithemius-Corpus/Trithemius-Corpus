We do not expand the *orchemata*, being moved by a rational cause, because, since it ranges more widely in its progression, the immense labor has frightened the mind. We have also deliberately passed over unity, not assigning it a place; to set it before our inventions in due order we judge rash. For from those things which we have already shown by way of example, anyone who is fit in understanding will easily attain greater things. All things serve secrets.
