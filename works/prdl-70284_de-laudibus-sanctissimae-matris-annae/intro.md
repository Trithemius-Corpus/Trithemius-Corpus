*De Laudibus Sanctissimae Matris Annae* — "In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne" — is Trithemius's treatise honouring St Anne, mother of the Virgin Mary, written at Sponheim with a dedicatory letter to Rumold Laupach dated 1 July 1494, and issued with commendatory poems on St Anne and the Holy Kindred. It belongs to the early, hagiographic side of his Sponheim output — the world of late-medieval Marian and Anna-devotion. The text was much reprinted in the decade after 1494.
