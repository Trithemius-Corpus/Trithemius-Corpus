# Errata — prdl-70284

## 2026-06-09 — targeted chunk re-translation (Fable 5 revision pass)

### full_chunk_0004

Re-translated from scratch against the source page-scans, superseding the earlier machine rendering, and independently re-graded against the Latin; the superseded text is retained in version control.

**What was wrong:** The public version left three [unclear] gaps that the print resolves (socius, pia sis, semper) and carried OCR-driven errors: all three colophon dates were wrong (1497 for M.cccc.xciiij = 1494; "Ides of July" for Nonis iulias = 7 July; "second day before the Kalends" for iij. kalendas = 30 January), "her countenance" for Vita ("her life"), "women's minds" for miseris ("wretched minds"), and an unsupported "in old age" from misreading Nate ("of your daughter").

**Key corrections (verified against the scan):**
- All three colophon years read M.cccc.xciiij (1494), not 1497 — verified at maximum zoom on scan pages 8, 9, and 11; consistent with the Mainz 1494 edition (BSB bsb00008347)
- First colophon: 'iij. kalendas februarij' = 30 January, not 'second day before the Kalends' (public followed OCR 'ii')
- Trithemius Sapphic colophon: 'Nonis iulias' = Nones of July (7 July), not 'Ides of July'
- 'Christo qui socius cum patre flamini regnat' — resolves first [unclear]: 'who reigns with the Father, companion of the Spirit' (print 'socius' clear)
- 'Nate parturijt' — 'the holy chastity of your daughter brought forth'; removes the public version's 'in old age' (the initial glyph is the same capital N as in 'Nobis' two lines below, which OCR also rendered as A)
- 'In festo pia sis germine cum tuo' — resolves second [unclear]: 'on this feast, gracious one, be present with your offspring' (print 'sis' clear; OCR 'lis')
- 'Ergo licet sp̄ fuerit iesus' — resolves third [unclear]: sp̄ = semper, 'though Jesus has existed forever' (fits the hexameter)
- 'Cuius... Vita laudatur' — 'her life is praised', not 'her countenance' (OCR 'Utta' = print 'Vita')
- 'Dote signorum miseris leuamen mentibus auges' — 'relief for wretched minds', not 'women's minds' (print 'miseris' clear; OCR 'muleris')
- 'summam deitatem coniunxit carni' — 'he joined the highest Godhead to flesh' (accusative object), not 'in the highest deity he joined himself to flesh'
- 'Tu diuam dño genuisti sancta puellam' — 'the divine maiden' restored (public dropped the word)
- 'Gaude simplicitas angelico sagax' — 'keen-eyed' (sagax) restored, final x verified
- Rutger rubric: print reads 'in heyna' — rendered 'Heyna' as printed, not 'Heinau'
- 'lucis inacte' — the print does not support 'uncreated' (increatae, which also breaks the hexameter); read intactae 'inviolate', flagged as conjecture

**Surviving cruxes (flagged in the chunk's translator's note):**
- 'rubith' (p8, 'Gaude que opprobrio pontificis rubith') — clearly printed but not a Latin word; translated 'blushing', perhaps for 'rubens'; flagged in text and note
- 'lucis inacte' (p9) — read 'intactae' (inviolate) on metrical grounds; conjectural
- 'Ex titulos' (p10) — print clearly 'Ex' but ungrammatical; translated as 'Et' ('and... the titles'), flagged
- 'diuam' (p9) — minim order slightly ambiguous (diuam/duiam); 'divam' is the only viable reading and scans
- sp̄ expanded as 'semper' (abbreviation, metrically secure)
- Hymn incipit printed 'Gaude viscibus' for 'Gaude visceribus mater in intimis' — print reading preserved with bracket
- 'Heyna' (Rutger's house near Worms) given as printed; identification left open
