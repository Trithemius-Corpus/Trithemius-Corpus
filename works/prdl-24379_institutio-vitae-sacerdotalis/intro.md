*Institutio Vitae Sacerdotalis* — "The Formation of the Priestly Life" — is Trithemius's treatise on the discipline, study, and conduct proper to priests, part of the clerical-formation strand of his writing that accompanies the Benedictine reform tracts. It addresses the clergy's interior and moral preparation rather than monastic observance as such — how a priest is to live, read, pray, and bear his office — and it belongs with *De Cura Pastorali* and *De Sacerdotum Vita Instituenda* among his works on the priestly life.

This is an **Augsburg edition, after 1494** (BSB scan) — the second of two witnesses of the *Institutio Vitae Sacerdotalis* in this corpus (the other is the Mainz edition, `prdl-24378`).
