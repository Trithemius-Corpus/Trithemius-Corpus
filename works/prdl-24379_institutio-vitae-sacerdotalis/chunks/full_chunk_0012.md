Therefore I admonish you and beseech you to flee the company of women, lest you lose the reputation of a good name. For a priest once defamed for fornication will never recover the honor he has lost. But also avoid conversations with women; decline frequent greetings in the marketplace, because through trifling words one comes to the worst deeds. For the incautious speech of women has deceived many.

Be watchful at all times, and never, as long as you live in the flesh, think that you should put confidence in yourself. For we have known several who fell in old age, who seemed secure to themselves because of their past victory. For temptation is never lacking to a man so long as he lives in this life. But if you cannot manage household care without the service of a maidservant, take an upright old woman from your own kin, from whose living with you no suspicion can arise among the people. Yet guard against this before all things: do not take a married woman as a maidservant. For what God has joined, let no man separate. The living together of another man’s wife is not free from suspicion of evil.

I want you, insofar as can be done, to have good testimony from all. Let the dangers of others make you cautious, and where you see many to have fallen, do not wish to walk incautiously. Remember how many very strong and very wise men, from the beginning of the world down to this day, have perished through the company of women. A woman deceived Adam, the first-formed, and, when he had been overthrown, killed us all. From the beginning of the created world the sons of God sinned through women, and by their crimes brought the cataclysm upon the whole world. Women of the [unclear] also laid low in the desert the children of Israel as they came out of Egypt.

Why do we recount these women, when a vile little harlot, after capturing Samson, the strongest of all men, delivered him over by her loves to death and disgrace? Let no one presume on his own strength; let no one trust in former chastity, since often he who today is seen to stand strongly is tomorrow seen to fall into the depth of fornication. The most wise Solomon could not beware the deceits of women; who now will be safe? The strong and wise man falls by the sword of women, and we, fragile and foolish, presume upon security. For he who fell says: Better is the iniquity of a man than a woman doing good. It is safer to live with an unjust man than with a good woman. For the devil, the adversary of chastity, never ceases to lay snares for the chaste man, if he can find material for deceiving him. Since he is cunning, he knows how to dissemble for a time, until at last, in a single hour, he can overturn all the labor and reward of continence already passed.
