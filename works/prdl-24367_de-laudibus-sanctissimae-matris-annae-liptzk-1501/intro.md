*De Laudibus Sanctissimae Matris Annae* — "In Praise of the Most Holy Mother Anne" — is Trithemius's *tractatus* honouring St Anne, mother of the Virgin Mary, composed at Sponheim with a dedicatory letter to Rumold Laupach dated 1 July 1494. It travelled with commendatory poems on the Holy Kindred, the extended family of Christ, and it is a central piece of his early Marian-hagiographic writing.

This is the **Leipzig (Liptzk) edition, ca. 1501** (BSB scan) — a later reprint.
