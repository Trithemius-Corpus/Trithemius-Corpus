Whatever praise we bestow upon her veneration, let us firmly believe that it pertains to the honor of the highest King. To the honor of the most holy mother Anne, come together, all the faithful. Pay praises to the most high Lord in her memory. In reverence for her, rejoice with religious devotion, for I have experienced many of her benefits. You will have her as a ready advocate in every tribulation, if you celebrate her memory with faithful devotion. She is therefore to be honored by all; through her merits the Son of God grants all things at her nod; her honorable name the court of the citizens above honors with the greatest reverence; the queen of heaven, the mother of God, attends to her as her most sweet parent. Whoever chooses her as patroness, whoever perseveres devoutly in her service, he shall be saved both now and forever. For the gifts which the Son of God is accustomed to grant to mortals through his beloved mother, he cannot deny to the merits of his grandmother; indeed, he sometimes grants greater things through her, so that he may draw us, as if by gifts, to the memory of her, long neglected. Nor can the Son of God ever despise his own parents, since he so earnestly commanded us to honor those not related to us.

*The Author's Invocation to God and Saint Anne and All the Heavenly Ones, That They May Bring Him the Grace of Writing. Chapter 2*

God, true light, who enlighten every man coming into this world; God, who justify the ungodly and call sinners to repentance; God, who give wisdom to little ones and make the tongues of stammerers eloquent: grant me, I beseech you, pardon for my sins; grant me the wisdom of your praise; grant me light by which I may understand, know, and love you, and may be so conquered by love of most blessed Anne that I may worthily embrace her praises in this work. God, who brought forth water from the hardest rock for the thirsty people in the desert, soften the hardness of my heart, that I may be able to praise you in your saints, in the grandmother of your Son, in the parents of your Only-Begotten, with such devotion of mind that, by your mercy and her intercession, this little night-work of ours may profit toward devotion not only for me but also for all who read it. God, who opened the mouth of the beast so that it uttered human words against the foolish man, kindly water the dryness of my tongue with the infusion of your grace; strengthen my trembling hand with the Holy Spirit, so that concerning most holy Anne I may write nothing except what is right, persuade nothing except what is holy, utter nothing except what is worthy. Grant me, I pray, almighty and everlasting God, in this work the help of heavenly grace, you who have given me the will for the undertaking. For I am a worm and no man, wretched and unhappy, sold under sin.
