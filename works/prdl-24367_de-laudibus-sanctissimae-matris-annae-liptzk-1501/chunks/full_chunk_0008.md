...prefer the merits to the gifts of Elizabeth. Saint Elizabeth bore a soldier; Anne bore the Mother of God. There is no comparison where there is no likeness. If Elizabeth was full of grace because of John, I believe that Anne obtained grace much more because of Mary. Anne was holy before she conceived the Mother of God; yet in the conception she was sanctified still more abundantly, because she deserved to be made the tabernacle of her who was full of grace above all creatures. We call a chest or ark in which the relics of saints are kept sanctified and honorable; how much more ought we to venerate venerable Anne as honored and sanctified, in whom the Mother of God lay without stain. Truly holy is she who bore her without the guilt of original stain, than whom none holier ever was. Truly holy is she who was chosen as mother of the Mother of God before she was born. O womb never to be named without honor, in which the ark of God deserved to be fashioned without stain. Blessed is the belly that bore the Lady of heaven; happy the breasts that deserved to nurse the Mother of God.

Run, peoples, from every side, and receive a blessing from the merits of so great a patroness. For truly, if the holy devotion of the faithful is directed toward her, we believe that almighty God will give great gifts to mortals, and no smaller ones than he is accustomed to give through her daughter. Therefore you, O faithful peoples, who believe Anne’s daughter to be the Mother of God, who rest beneath the shadow of the holiness of both, if you desire to advance in the love of God, if you wish to reach the heavenly homeland, do not neglect the memory of the most holy mother. For she herself, after the Queen, is most powerful in the court of the heavenly emperor, because from her blood the Son of God took flesh from the Virgin. From whatever tribulation you cry to her, you will find help. It is enough for the Lady to grant you what you will be able to ask in faith. She heals the sick, cleanses crimes, gladdens the hearts of the sorrowful with holy exultation, calms sadness, smooths rough things, drives away the phantoms of demons. As ice is dissolved by fire, so the ancient enemy is troubled by the merits of the most holy mother Anne.

Anne, the ark of piety, drives away defilement, preserves a reputation beautiful, and wondrously banishes base love from the minds of her faithful. Therefore worship Anne, you who carry on the wretched life of this world. Anne obtains pardon for sinners, roots out vices, plants virtues. Anne cures the blind, heals wounds, exterminates pestilence. Therefore honor this mother, O children, who has power to introduce you into the sight of God, because whoever has had an advocate before God has feared no adversities of this world. Therefore whoever desires to live a tranquil and holy life on earth, whoever earnestly asks pardon for his sins, let him never depart from the service of this most holy mother.
