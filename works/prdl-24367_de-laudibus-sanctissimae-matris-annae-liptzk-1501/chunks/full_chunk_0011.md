It is said that Saint Longinus, soldier of Christ, first founded it, and, carrying those most precious relics with him from Jerusalem, placed them in the same place. Very ancient records of this matter are found in the said monastery.

Do these things seem new to you? Cease, therefore, to disparage the feast of the most holy mother. Cease to tear at her most worthy cult with an impious tooth. Do not spread a personal hatred against all. Do not, because of the displeasure caused by a few, be indignant at everyone. Do not carry an injury done by a man up against those above. Why, perhaps injured by a man, do you call the saints of God into blame? Cease, I beg you, cease to wish to exact vengeance for one man or a few from the most holy mother. Do not attack her holiness by [revilings?].

If a man has sinned against men, God will be able to be appeased for him. But he who sins against the saints of God, against the parents of the Son of God, against the mother of the Mother of God, who will pray for him? How can he have God propitious who has offended His saints, and indeed the most holy ones? For Saint Anne is worthy of every honor, since she deserved to bear the Mother of God without stain of sin; whoever diminishes her honor does injury to God.

Rather, if you are wise, if you understand, withdraw from this foolishness of yours; flee from this false madness; turn aside from this most wicked blasphemy, and undertake penance for your former error, so that with [tears of compunction?] you may wash away so heinous a fault. But if you do not do this, if you persevere in your obstinacy, you will not hinder the will of God, but you will have a share in the lot of the devil.

God has resolved that Saint Anne be honored in His Church. Who will forbid Him? Behold, kings and princes celebrate the memory of Saint Anne with religious devotion. Behold, prelates of churches everywhere consecrate altars in her honor. Behold, faithful peoples everywhere invoke her name. And you, in your envy, waste away with the devil; you are confounded and rave.

Monks and religious of both sexes, the whole clergy of the churches of God, celebrate the feast and memory of the holy mother Anne with singular devotion; and you, envious dog, presume to bark against all. Why do you bark impiously? Why do you mutter wickedly? Be silent; begone; withdraw. This impiety of yours will not be punished without grave chastisement. For whoever detracts from the honor and sanctity of the most blessed mother Anne, whoever blasphemes those who venerate her, will not depart from this life without disgrace. He will be confounded with evil confusion before God and men; reproaches and the worst ignominy will cover his head; he will be wretched and pitiable, and there will be no one to grieve over his calamity, all the days in which he shall be upon the earth.
