*Letter of Lord Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Sponheim, prefixed to the book on the praises of the most holy Anne.*

To the venerable and most religious father, Lord Romuldus Jaupach, prior of the convent of Frankfurt of the order of the brothers of the Blessed Mary, ever Virgin, of Mount Carmel, Johannes of Trittenheim, abbot of the monastery of Saint Martin in Sponheim, of the order of the holy father Benedict: greeting and charity.

Desiring, excellent father, to satisfy our vow and your requests, we have written with sincere devotion a book on the praises of the most holy mother Anne, which we dedicate to your name, since you seemed to us worthy before others, being a very great worshipper and most devoted lover of that same venerable Anne. But from the history, which is sufficiently widespread, we have taken nothing, or little, because it seemed to us both less approved and in very many places apocryphal. Nevertheless we did not wish to refute it, lest we provide the simple with occasion for murmuring, if, as they themselves think, we should attack an approved account with novel presumption. We have therefore left the history untouched, and have brought together, from the gift of God, for the honor of the holy mother Anne, those things that were known by the clearest faith, truth, and reason. We shall also reject the miracles of Saint Anne from this volume, lest we make our treatise suspect to learned men, who do not easily give credence to these things. And so, at another time, if you wish, a history concerning them will be woven. In whatever matters you may happen to hesitate, you will find us ready to demonstrate to you and to all, concerning each of the things that we have written, the reason for it. After the end of the work we have appended certain poems on the praises of the holy mother, which have been sent to us by various learned men. Farewell. From Sponheim, on the Kalends of July, in the year
