What is true prevails, and what proceeds from truth has not failed. Let no one think that I, writing about the most holy one, am to be despised on my own account. Let no one [unclear] my devotion. Let no one whisper against the praises of the divine mother Anne. I do not fear the barking of dogs. I do not fear the censures of the inexperienced. I do not shudder at the torches of envy. For I write for the devout, not for the wicked; for the studious, not for the ignorant; for the humble, not for the proud. May the present work please those for whom it is written; may it profit those to whom it is offered; may it benefit those to whom it is presented. What is it to me, because these things are outside? What is it to me, because they gnaw at holy things beyond reason? I do not fear displeasing those whose censures and rash judgments I know.

*General exhortation to all the faithful, that they venerate the memory and feast of the most holy Anne. Chapter 3.*

Hear, you who love what is good and seek the Lord. I perform an embassy; I am led by devotion; I am impelled by charity. The mother of the mother of God has sent me to proclaim her most holy merits. And now I come to you not with lofty speech, but as a simple man to the simple, a devout man to the devout. If you are Christians, if you are good, if you love the grace of the Incarnation, why do you not love its ministers, that is, its parents? For they are those from whom the Son of God was born according to the flesh. If their manner of life had not been most holy, it could by no means have been so acceptable to God. But since he wished his only-begotten Son to be born from them, he clearly showed how holy they were to him. From these, one woman, most pleasing to God above all, shone forth: Mary, mother of the one Father, chosen virgin of the Spirit, whom Anne, the most blessed wife of Joachim, bore without stain and educated most chastely in the fear of the Lord. The son of this most sacred virgin is he who redeemed us by his most holy Passion. If you love the daughter, why do you not venerate her mother? If there had been no mother, you would not have had the daughter. Therefore my discourse is directed to you who dwell in the valley of tears. I address you, O sons of Adam, because you are exiled from your homeland. I write to you who have been redeemed by the precious blood of the Son of God. I address you; I admonish and exhort you that, if you love the Son of God, you devoutly honor his relatives according to the flesh.
