*... chose as mother of his mother before the foundation of the world. Chapter V*

Just as almighty God, before the foundation of the world, appointed the time at which he would be born in the flesh, so also from eternity he foreordained the parents from whom he would be born. For the Son of God did not find parents, but sent them beforehand: he who, without time, ordained them for time. He did not come upon them by chance accident, but by wondrous dispensation chose them beforehand from eternity.

Therefore most blessed Anna could confidently say that which is written of the eternal Wisdom of God: “The Lord possessed me at the beginning of his ways, before he made anything from the beginning. From eternity I was ordained, and from ancient times.” Before the foundation of the world she was in the divine mind, made not by nature but foreordained by grace. Truly she is worthy to be honored, whom the grace of God, by going before her, appointed as mother of the Mother, and, by always preserving her, made most holy.

Truly holy and most worthy of every honor is the parent who gave us the rule of a holier life, and brought forth from her own breast the fountain of inexhaustible piety. She could only have been most holy who carried so precious a treasure in her womb. She was the most noble Ark of the Testament, splendid with the adornment of all virtues, in which was stored that urn which was the true manna: the virgin who was to give birth.

O with how great an honor Anna’s womb must be believed worthy, which, made the treasury of God, bore a treasure of inestimable price. It is not to be doubted, most beloved brothers, but to be believed with firm devotion, that blessed Anna was then filled with ineffable joy, when for nine months she carried in her most blessed womb the future Mother of God, made worthy and preserved clean from every stain by the Holy Spirit from her conception.

O who could tell the joys of that most holy mother Anna, which she sensibly perceived in her breast from the presence of the Holy Spirit? What voice can express, what tongue disclose, what mind of a mortal wayfarer comprehend, with how great a joy and exultation the inmost parts of most blessed Anna were filled, when she merited to feel the grace of the Holy Spirit in the heart of her daughter not yet born?

If Elizabeth is said to have been filled with the Holy Spirit from the presence of the soldier [unclear], that this was done more abundantly in venerable Anna is proved by manifold argument. For the former conceived in her womb the herald of the supreme King, sanctified; the latter conceived the mother. Mary too was preserved in conception. Finally, the Holy Spirit, choosing Mary from the hour of her conception as his purest bride and inhabiting her, never afterward abandoned her, not even for a moment, but, always guarding and directing her, illuminated her above all mortals with a special gift. We believe that the most blessed Virgin’s mother was a sharer in this most sacred illumination, not only when she was carrying the Virgin, still enclosed, in her womb, but also in all things afterward
