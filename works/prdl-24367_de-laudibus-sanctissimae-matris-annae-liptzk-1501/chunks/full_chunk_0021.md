Therefore it is fitting that we always be devout in venerating them. We, I say, who still live in the flesh and journey in exile from the Lord, ought to honor those citizens of the heavenly homeland with especial devotion, so that, helped by their intercessions, we may be able to reach the joys which they inhabit. Among all the saints, however, we believe that the Mother of God is preeminent, and Anne, her venerable parent; if we are devoted to them and honor them, no adversities will harm us.

*[That Saint Anne] aids before God those who devoutly invoke her. Chapter 10.*

Since Saint Anne, mother of the holy and most blessed Mother of God, is in the kingdom of heaven nearest in merits and charity to the Son of God and to His Mother, there is no doubt that she is able to obtain help for our miseries, if only the devotion of our mind is diligently directed toward her. She cannot fail to be most compassionate, who bore the mother of mercy in her womb. How compassionate, how powerful, and how ready she is to drive away human miseries, there are almost as many witnesses as there are devout worshippers of her to be found. For she is powerful in the palace of the supreme Emperor; God hears her when she supplicates, out of reverence for her, and grants without delay whatever she asks. Let him doubt her authority and power who does not know that she gave birth to the Queen of Angels. Let him hesitate over the compassion of the most blessed mother who is ignorant that the Savior of the world came forth from her most noble blood. She is powerful and very near to God; she is compassionate and inclined toward our needs. Believe me, brothers, for I am certain that God is accustomed, through her merits, to grant many benefits to human beings, so that they may learn always to hold her memory in honor.

Therefore approach the compassionate patroness with confidence. Knock upon her with diligent prayers, because she is able to obtain pardon for you; she is able to lead you to eternal life. Nothing is lacking to her that can be of profit to our needs; she lacks nothing that can help us, for there is always present to her the capacity to be able, and the compassion to be willing. Therefore let us doubt nothing, brothers; let us hesitate at nothing, since certain intercessions will be ours, provided firm faith is not lacking. From whatever tribulation we cry to her, we shall find ready help, if we render devoted service to her name.

You desire to know, brothers, what this most holy woman [can] provide for you. Listen. Anne drives away sadness; she roots out the lust of the flesh; she relieves poverty; she heals infirmities; she softens adversities; she calms opposing things; she removes vices; she plants virtues; she illuminates the intellect; she purifies the affections; she remits crimes. Therefore whoever you are who have fortune opposed to you in good pursuits, whoever you are who feel the adverse storm of this pilgrimage, invoke Saint Anne with devout prayers; knock upon the mother with diligent supplications, and you will find remedies of unexpected consolation. For she herself is a safe refuge for the wretched, a sure remedy for the sick, an unfailing solace for the desolate. Why do you hesitate to approach her, who is both powerful and willing to help you?
