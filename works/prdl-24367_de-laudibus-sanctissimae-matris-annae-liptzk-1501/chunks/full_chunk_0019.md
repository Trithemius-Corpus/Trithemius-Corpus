God had chosen her before the foundation of the world, and had preferred her; and what he arranged outside time he fulfilled by this presentation. How great a joy, we think, there was then in heaven, when Mary the Virgin, a young girl, was being joined to the heavenly bridegroom. The most holy virgin is led by her mother into the temple, she who was to bear the Lord of the temple; and now, a little child of three years, she is prepared for divine nuptials. Who will be able to recount your joy then, O mother Anna, when, devotedly, you handed over your daughter, received from the Lord, to serve the temple, nay rather to bear the Lord of the temple? No one ever offered God so pleasing a gift; no one ever presented to the temple a treasure of so great a price. The holy parents marvel at the manners in their daughter beyond her age; they marvel at her grace, they marvel at her eagerness. For she was full of grace from the womb, nor was she ever subject to any stain, however slight, she who was the temple of God. Why should one marvel at the mother in the daughter? The grace of God is not enclosed under the law of nature; the power of the Holy Spirit does not require age in order to do what it has willed; the gift and will of the Most High is not constrained beneath many years. The Most High sanctified his tabernacle, and the ark in which he arranged to place the true manna he adorned most beautifully within and without with the purest gold. That the mother was perfect in the love of God he declared in this offering of her sweetest daughter. For if she had been solid in the fear of the Lord [unclear], she would never have allowed her beloved daughter in turn, tender and noble, to be torn away from herself. But love of the Creator conquered a mother’s inmost affections; and the pledge which above all created things she held most dear she offered to the Lord with cheerful face, to be consecrated. Nothing here is done by chance; custom effects nothing; all things have been arranged by the Most High outside time; all things have been preordained from eternity by the counsel of God. O Joachim and Anna, most blessed parents, who were worthy to bring forth her who, without male seed, merited to procreate the Son of God. Truly an offering always to be honored with worthy praises, which furnished a mother to God, a queen to the angels, and a savior to the world long since lost. And we believe that this offering was adorned with such purity that no purer could exist under God. For she has been made queen of the angels, mother of God, lady of the world. These most blessed parents too must be believed to be in the greatest glory before God, who begot a mother for the Son of God. Parents, learn to set the fear of God before your children; learn to form them as young people in the love of God, lest, if you neglect them as youths, you later find them stubborn. When Anna offered her sweetest daughter, three years old, she taught the little child to fear God; she presented her to the temple to be instructed in the fear of the Lord. But you do not act so. You instruct your daughters for dances and love of the world’s vanities; you say nothing to them about the fear of the Lord; you persuade them of nothing about eternal life, since indeed you yourselves learned nothing good from your parents. Let not a woman glory in the multitude of her children if she has neglected to establish them properly in the charity of God; for it is not the multitude of children that commends women, but uprightness.
