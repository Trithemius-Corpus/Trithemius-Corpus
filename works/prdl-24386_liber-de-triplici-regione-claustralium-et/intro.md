*Liber de Triplici Regione Claustralium et Spirituali Exercitio Monachorum* — "On the Threefold Region of Cloister-dwellers and the Spiritual Exercise of Monks" — is Trithemius's most systematic treatise on the monastic spiritual life. It divides the interior life of a cloistered monk into three stages — beginners (purification), proficients (illumination), and the perfect (union with God) — drawing the scheme from Pseudo-Dionysius and the Victorines and applying it to Benedictine observance. Completed in manuscript in 1497, it is one of the central texts of his Sponheim reform programme.

This is the **1516 Leipzig edition** (BSB scan), a later reprint of the text first printed at Mainz in 1498. It is one of three witnesses of the *De Triplici Regione* in this corpus: the 1498 Mainz edition (`prdl-24385`), the 1498 e-rara witness (`prdl-70285`), and this 1516 Leipzig printing.
