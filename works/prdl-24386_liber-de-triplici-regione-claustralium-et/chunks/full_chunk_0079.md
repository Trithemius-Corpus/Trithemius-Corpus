In this, when we return with the prodigal son, he mercifully receives us; through love he rushes into our embraces; he clothes us with the first robe; he grants the garment of charity to those who love him. Why, then, do we neglect to return as quickly as possible to the most indulgent father, whom we see to be ready for our embraces even after our contempt? We wretches have departed through the rashness of our own will; we have squandered the portion of our inheritance with the harlots of carnal affections; we have been shamefully stripped of all beauty and paternal adornment. Why do we not return to the father, whom we know to be ineffably merciful? Let us at least return, I beg, after our fault, since we scorned to avoid ruin. Return, return, wretched Shulamite; return, grievously captive in your affections; return, mortified in sorrows. Why delay? Why do you tarry? Return, O faithful soul, to Jesus, the guide of your youth; return to your former husband; return to the most loving father who awaits you with the longing of love. For behold, he awaits you with patience, and through the prophet Isaiah cries out to you: Return to me, for I have redeemed you. I have blotted out your iniquities like a cloud, and your sins as though a mist. What is this, O soul, that you are in the land of enemies? You have grown old in a foreign land; you have been defiled with the dead; you have been numbered with those who go down into hell. Remember that almighty God created you from nothing; consider that he formed you in his own image and likeness; be mindful whence you have fallen; see where, cast among the swine, you are trampled under the feet of unclean affections. Your creator, your bridegroom, placed you from the beginning in this highest region; he entrusted to you the natural exercise of his love; he also bestowed on you the hope of life ever-abiding. But you have forsaken the fountain of wisdom; you have gone out into the lowest region of unlikeness; you have squandered the goods of the promised inheritance. But return, even late at last, to the exercise of penitence in the highest region. There the most indulgent father will gladly receive you as you return; he will clothe you with the first robe of innocence, recovered for you; he will give you the ring of most holy contemplation on your hands, and the shoes of true mortification of evil affections on your feet, so that, adorned with these ornaments like a faithful bride, you may cling to him continually and henceforth in no way withdraw from his love. Finally, for food he will kill for you the fatted calf; he himself will be both steward and banquet; by reason of the greatness of his love he will give his flesh for food and his own blood for drink. There the Holy Spirit of the Lord will be present; he will fill you with his mystical gifts. Then, exulting in the Lord, you will sing: The fruit of Jesus is sweet to my throat, for his spirit is sweeter than honey.
