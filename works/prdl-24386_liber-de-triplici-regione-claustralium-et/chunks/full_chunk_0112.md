*At supper.*

When the signal has been given for going to supper, humbly prepare yourself to attend, and observe what we marked out above for dinner. After supper you may return to your cell and devote yourself to prayer or meditation until the signal is rung for the collation.

*At the collation.*

After supper, when you hear the signal for the spiritual collation, you should at once hasten to attend it with gravity, and hear the last reading of the day with fervor of spirit. For the word of holy exhortation that has been heard last will cling more firmly to the mind throughout the whole night. But if it is a fast, the collation is customarily observed in the refectory while drinking. Therefore be attentive to the sacred reading in every place, for where the word of holy exhortation is willingly received with attention, it cannot happen otherwise than that the mind is often lifted up.

*At Compline.*

When going to Compline, beseech the Lord that he may always make you despise all worldly and carnal things, and grant you to remain continually in his most holy love, so that, when the course of this present life has been completed, you may deserve to enter the temple of God not made by hand, and rejoice eternally in the Lord with all the saints. At the end of Compline, when you hear the signal of the Angelic Salutation being rung, you shall humbly greet the most blessed Mother of God and Virgin Mary, that by her merits she may guard you through the night and continually intercede with her Son for your salvation.

*After Compline.*

When Compline is finished, return at once to your cell, and, after the customary prayers have been said, take care to prepare yourself most diligently for the following recollection of the soul.

*On recollection after Compline. Consideration IX.*

Since we cannot persist altogether immovable in one state of mind, it is necessary for us to renew our holy purpose more often, so that as often as during the day, by wandering through the byways of sins, we withdraw from the one principle of our soul, the Most High God, so often by contrition and compunction we may return to him, with correction firmly purposed.
