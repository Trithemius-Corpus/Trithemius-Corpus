*The Second Hour.*

At the second hour after midday, let our devout cenobite briefly recall to mind the gravity of his sins, by which, especially while living in holy religion, he has sinned against the rule; and, turning to the Lord, let him pray thus.

*Prayer.*

Almighty, everlasting God, who by the incarnation and death of your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, have deigned, out of your mercy alone, to bring us wretched persons, dead in sins, to life: grant me, through the power of his most innocent passion, pardon for all my sins, so that henceforth I may deserve to serve you with a pure conscience. Through the same our Lord.

*The Third Hour.*

At the third hour after the meal, let our faithful cenobite remember his vows which he promised to the Lord, and especially the renunciation of all property, namely in possessions, judgments, and will; and, moved to compunction, let him pray thus to the Lord in fervor of spirit.

*Prayer.*

Almighty and merciful God, who with gracious favor sanctify and receive the vows of your servants offered to you from faithful devotion: grant me to render all things promised to you, through the virtue of humility, chastity, obedience, and poverty, with such purity of mind that I may deserve, by your mercy, to reach your kingdom promised to the poor in spirit. Through our Lord.

*After Vespers.*

After Vespers let our good cenobite consider that all visible things are vain; and, his mind lifted up to love of invisible goods, let him cry out to the Lord then with as much devotion as he can.

*Prayer.*

[unclear]

Almighty, everlasting God, who wished to lead us through the visible things which you created to the knowledge and love of the invisible: grant me so to know the vanity of present things that I may always deserve with a pure mind to contemplate the eternity of things to come.

*Another.*

O God, protector of those who hope in you, without whom nothing is strong, nothing holy: multiply your mercy upon us, so that with you as ruler, you as guide, we may pass through temporal goods in such a way that we do not lose the eternal.

*Another Prayer.*

O God, who have prepared invisible goods for those who love you: pour into our hearts the affection of your love, so that, loving you in all things and above all things, we may obtain your promises, which surpass every desire. Through our Lord.

*After Supper and Collation.*

After supper or collation let the faithful cenobite clothe himself with desire for good zeal, and especially for souls, with whatever fervor of charity he can, and, praying to the Lord, let him say:

O Lord God, greatest lover of faithful souls, who for the salvation of men wished your Son to die on the gibbet of the cross: grant me, through the example of the same your Son, always to make progress in good zeal, and to fear no labors, no adversities, for the salvation of souls, so that, strengthened by the fire of your love, I may dare to resist opportunely those who contradict you, and manfully set myself as a wall for your house. Through the same our Lord.

*Prayer.*

After Compline, his powers gathered together, let the devout cenobite enter alone into the secret place of his heart, and leave outside all cares and anxieties of the world, so that, free and released from all things, he may deserve to converse with the Lord Jesus through desire of compunction, and to be sweetly united to him through the fire of love. Therefore, having in summary recalled to the examination of memory the faults of the day, let him pray thus to the Lord.
