Woe to those who, buried in the habit of sin, do not fear the censure of strict examination; who do not depart from the blindness of mind through the remedy of contrition, since just as now they despise the long-suffering of God’s goodness, so in that terrible hour they will undergo the sentence of eternal death. What will confession of the mouth be able to profit, where there is no true contrition of the heart? Without contrition there is no saving confession, nor can there be contrition without a purpose of amendment. The sign of contrition is to be unwilling to sin any more.

Fifth, the disordered citizens of the lowest region, having through the habit of sinning become despisers of almighty God, always fall into graver dangers for the soul, while they always approach wickedly the most holy sacred mysteries of the body and blood of the Lord. They make a superficial confession from habit; they allow herds of great vices to run about in their consciences. And now thereafter, as if confederated with hell, they do not fear God; they despise the paths of amendment of life; they think themselves well ordered; though inwardly they are full of vices, they do not care. Sometimes, sensibly affected by a fanciful devotion, they think the Holy Spirit is now present; they affirm that they are beloved of God and holy. They also shamelessly thrust themselves upon the divine mysteries; not discerning the body and blood of the Lord, they take them to themselves unto judgment and condemnation. Finally, such people, communicating unworthily, are more gravely hardened, because, while they think themselves very secure and to be living well, they do not take due care to cleanse their conscience of vices. Thus Judas the apostate, the evil of betrayal which previously, as someone says, he was afraid to carry out against the Savior, after the morsel, when the devil had entered into him, did not fear boldly and without shame to accomplish. O father, many are the citizens of this lowest region: carnal monks, puffed up, proud, envious, murmurers, and afterwards it is effected by various [unclear]; who nevertheless are not afraid to approach the most holy altar almost every day.

Behold, father, the evil company of all who dwell in this lowest region, whose end is destruction, for whom by the death of the body a descent to hell is prepared. This is the far-off region of bestiality and unlikeness, into which that prodigal son went, who squandered the portion of his inheritance luxuriously with harlots. In this region dwell carnal citizens, concerning whom the holy apostle testifies: The animal man does not perceive the things that are of God. In this dark region, deadly and wrapped in the densest darkness, no spiritual good dwells; in it not peace but bestial discord reigns, as the prophet says: There is no peace for the wicked, says my Lord.
