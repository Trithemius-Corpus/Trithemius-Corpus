I praise my [unclear] so negligently. Behold, my Lord, who suffered most bitterly for me through the whole night, had no rest; and could I not watch with him for one hour? Help me, O Lord my God, that I may be able to sing psalms to you prudently.

Finally, under each psalm, at a suitable time, let him stir himself to compunction by some ardent words of fervor, so that by mental exhortation he may inflame his affection still more. Would that, you will say, O Lord God, I could love you with all my heart above all things, and cleave continually to you alone. O good God, kindle my heart in your most holy love. My love is crucified; and what am I doing upon the earth? O sweet Jesus, my love, possess my heart, for you are the treasure of my heart, my king and my God.

*After matins.*

When the office of matins has been completed, and confessions have been made or heard, let him not neglect immediately to read the hour concerning the Holy Cross; when it has been completed, let him add this little prayer.

*Prayer.*

I beseech you, most sweet Lord Jesus, by the pain of your wounds, and by the shedding of your most holy blood, and by the power of your most innocent death: have mercy on me now and in the hour of my death. Amen.

To the holy and undivided Trinity, to the crucified humanity of Jesus Christ our Lord, to the most blessed Mary ever virgin, and to Saint Anne, her venerable mother, with all the saints, be everlasting praise and glory through infinite ages. Amen.

Our custom is as follows. The custom at matins. After confession.

Indeed, when each of the hours has been finished, after the divine office has been completed, or also at any time whatever, he can repeat the order and manner of praying at the entrance of the church described above, and observe the same rite in its several places. But if he wishes to vary this manner with greater devotion, he will pray the following little prayers, or similar ones.

On leaving the church.

Bowing before the high altar, let him say:

Blessed be the holy Trinity and undivided Unity; we confess to him, because he has shown his mercy to us. O highest majesty of all things, have mercy on me. Amen.
