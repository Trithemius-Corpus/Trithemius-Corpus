Therefore our will is required for our own salvation; love is demanded for the ascent. If you wish to ascend to this highest summit of the divine darkness, you will have to love very greatly. The will, purified by love, receives; love, greatly kindled, makes ecstasy. Therefore, if you desire to fly up to the mountain of contemplation, you must, by loving God most fervently, pass entirely beyond yourself. But if you truly love God, you despise both yourself and all things except him alone. For he is truly the treasure of the soul; if anyone has attained him through love, he will think always of Jesus. This treasure is acquired by loving; no one understands its usefulness who has not received it through love. Whoever desires to have this inestimable treasure should fix his heart continually in the love of sweetest Jesus. For indeed he who loves, has; he who has, loves. The mind that has deserved to have this treasure of the love of Jesus abides continually with him in affection in this supreme region.

*That this third and supreme region of the soul rejoices in the greatest freedom of spirit.*

*Article VII.*

Seventh, this most holy region of the soul overflows with great freedom of spirit; in it the soul, free from the passions of the flesh and united to God through love, reigns. For the holy apostle says: Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom. And again: Whoever are led by the Spirit of God, these are the sons of God. For indeed, one abiding in this region of the spirit is made free from every defilement of flesh and blood; and, outwardly stripped and alone, without every phantasm of thought, he is raised into the darkness of divine contemplation. This is the true freedom of the soul: to rest in the fervor of divine love and to love none of the consolations of this world. Therefore let a man consider himself and diligently examine his love; and if he finds himself piously disposed, frequently raised to divine love by burning desires even without prior thought, let him trust that he can laudably be enrolled among the citizens of this region. For the Spirit of God bears witness to our spirit; and the mind which he raises into the ray of divine love he also causes to know the condition of its own state. This freedom of soul is acquired through the fervor of divine love, because the supremely holy Spirit inhabits the heart that loves God and gives true freedom to those who love. In this most holy freedom divine wisdom is learned; the sweetness of the divine goodness is tasted beforehand with inestimable sweetness; the mind, made most free, is carried above itself; it rests in the silence of the divine darkness.
