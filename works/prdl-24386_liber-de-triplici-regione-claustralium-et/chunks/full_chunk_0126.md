*After Matins.*

When Matins have been completed, while you are still remaining in the church, or even when placed in your cell, take up for yourself the disposition of true resignation, praying thus:

*Prayer.* Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, who at the morning hour, placed in the greatest anguish of your most holy heart, after threefold prayer most fully resigned yourself to the will of your Father: grant me, by the power of that charity of yours, perfectly to despise my own will for love of you in all things, and truly at all times to resign myself to the ordering of your will and of those whom you have set over me. Who lives and reigns.

*After Prime.*

After Prime, when, leaving choir, you enter the chapter-room, in memory of your most innocent Savior accused before Pilate, you shall form your disposition toward humble confession, praying thus to the Lord:

*Prayer.* Most loving and sweetest Lord Jesus, who at the [unclear] hour truly confessed yourself the Son of God: have mercy on me, and grant me, through your innocence, the virtue of true confession, so that I may humbly bear any words of those who accuse me, and by no reproaches withdraw from the inward tranquility of mind. Who lives and reigns with God the Father.

*After Terce.*

After Terce you will recall to mind how many reproaches, how many mockeries and derisions the most innocent Lord Jesus endured for your redemption; in memory of him take up for yourself the desire of the sixth degree of humility, praying thus:

*Prayer.* O sweetest Lord Jesus, who for us at the third hour were miserably scourged, mocked, derided, crowned with thorns, and spat upon. Grant me, by the power of all your sufferings, through the shedding of your most holy blood, always to be content for love of you with usefulness and the utmost [unclear], so that, in view of my sins, I may regard myself as an unworthy worker of humility, and nevertheless in no way grow lukewarm in your holy service.

*After Sext.*

After Sext, consider the Lord Jesus carrying his cross before all and then hanging miserably upon it, together with the other articles of his punishments; and, inflamed with desire for the seventh degree of humility, pray to him with fervor of spirit.

*Prayer.* Sweetest Lord Jesus, who for our sake, miserable sinners, after the countless pains of your Passion, carried your cross to the place of your death while your most sorrowful mother looked on; upon which, cruelly fastened, given gall and vinegar to drink, and mocked in many ways, you were: I ask you, through the pain of your wounds, grant me pardon mindful of all my sins, the grace of holy compunction, a fountain of tears, the virtue of deep humility, and continual remembrance of your most bitter Passion, so that all that I am and can do may always serve you, and my mind may burn continually in your love.

*After None.*

After None be mindful of sweetest Jesus dying on the cross; and, falling in spirit at his knees, giving thanks with tears with as much devotion as you can, you shall pray thus:

*Prayer.* I adore you now, O sweetest Lord Jesus Christ, King of heaven and earth, dying for me, a wretch, on the cross; and I humbly give you thanks for your holy Passion and most innocent death. O what shall I render to you, most loving Savior, for all that you have rendered to me? Behold, I offer myself to you; I desire to dwell continually in your most holy wounds. I ask you, through the power of your most innocent death: have mercy on me as you know how; pierce my flesh with your fear, and restrain in me all disordered movements, so that, inflamed with the fervor of your most holy love, I may love you with a pure heart, confess you as praiseworthy with my mouth, and in deed follow you, according to my strength, in humble conduct. I beseech you, most merciful Lord Jesus, through that anguish of your most holy heart which you endured on the cross when your most holy soul went out from your body: have mercy on my soul at its departure.
