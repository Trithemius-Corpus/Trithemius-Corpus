*Prayer.*
I offer to you, almighty God, the life, Passion, and death of the same our Lord Jesus Christ for my sins; for the holy Roman and universal Church; for the supreme pontiff; for our bishop; for our abbot; for all my confreres; for the unity of our observance; also for our whole order; for the salvation of all the faithful; for the conversion of unbelievers; and for the redemption of the souls of the faithful who are in the pains of purgatory. Receive, I beseech you, holy Father, this my offering in union with that charity by which you received the same our Lord Jesus Christ dying for us on the cross, and may it be pleasing to you.

*During the Preface.*
[Strive?] to raise yourself from all earthly things to heavenly things, and to prepare yourself to praise the Lord with the holy angels. Remember that by the merit of the Lord's Passion you have been called to the fellowship of the angels, and take care to arrive at it through observance of the commandments.

*At the Sanctus.*
With your mind lifted upward, when the Sanctus is sung, glorify the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, God and Lord; humbly adore his majesty at all times with the holy angels.

*During the Canon.*
After the holy praise, during the Canon, you may take up meditation on the Lord's Passion, and offer it through each of the greater principal articles to God the Father for yourself, or for others, or for any need. Say, therefore, in this way:

Holy Father, I offer to you the bloody sweat, or in similar fashion, and the tears of our Lord Jesus Christ, your most beloved Son, in that charity and union in which he voluntarily offered himself to you unto death for us: for my sins, or for that brother, or for this or that need. And I ask you, by the pain of the wounds of the same your most sweet Son, and by the shedding of his most holy blood, and by the virtue of his most innocent death: have mercy on me, or on that person; or grant us this or that, etc. Through the same our Lord.

You may also act similarly concerning the other articles of the Lord's Passion, and enlarge them by offering them according to the intention of your devotion.

If your mind has not been occupied in the higher things of our Lord Jesus Christ, you may pray to the Lord, who is truly present, in this way:

You are the King of glory, O Christ. You are the everlasting Son of the Father. You, to deliver, etc., to the end. For it is read that Saint Thomas Aquinas prayed this part of the hymn to the Lord at the elevation.
