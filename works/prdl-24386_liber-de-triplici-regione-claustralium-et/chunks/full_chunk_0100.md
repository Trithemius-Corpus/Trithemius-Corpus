May your good Spirit enter into my heart, sounding there without sound, and without the noise of words speaking within me the truth of such great mysteries. For they are exceedingly deep, and covered with a sacred veil. Because of your great mercy, sweetest Lord Jesus, grant me to celebrate the solemn rites of the Masses with a pure heart and a clean conscience. I pray you, by the sweetness of your most holy heart, most loving Savior, cleanse my heart from all vain, impure, harmful, and unlawful thoughts, so that it may become a worthy dwelling-place of your love, now and without end forever.

I beg you, O lover of humankind, sweetest Lord Jesus, through all the power of your most holy passion, drive away from me the spirit of self-exaltation and pride, vainglory and envy, lust and blasphemy; and fill my soul with the virtue of charity and innocence. King of the virtues, Jesus Christ, lover of [sacred?] chastity and integrity, I beg you, by the most holy virginity of the most blessed mother of God and undefiled Virgin Mary, and by the purity of your most reverend body, extinguish in me, with the heavenly dew of blessing, the tinder of burning lust, so that the brightness of all purity may be and remain in me. Mortify in me, I beseech you, the goads of the flesh, and all lustful stirrings and disordered affections; and give me true and perpetual chastity, together with the other gifts of yours that truly please you, so that at all times, with an unstained body and a clean heart, I may be able to offer to you the sacrifice of your body and blood.

I know, Lord, and truly know, and I confess this very thing to your goodness, that I am not worthy to approach so great a mystery, because of my very many sins and my many negligences. But I also know, and with my whole heart believe and with my mouth confess, that since you are almighty, you can make me worthy: you who alone justify the ungodly, and can make the clean from the unclean. Therefore I pray you by your omnipotence: grant to me, your unworthy servant, true contrition for my sins, and pure devotion, and most fervent compunction in your love, so that I may be able to offer to you this most holy sacrifice with fear and reverence, with purity of heart and a fountain of sweet tears, with spiritual gladness and heavenly joy, with humility and fervor of charity. O sweetest Lord Jesus, grant me your mercy in this hour, so that my mind may feel the sweetness of your most blessed presence, and the devotion of the holy angels around me, and its own peaceful elevation in you.

Take away from me, Lord Jesus, all my iniquities, and graciously kindle in me the fire of your love. Do not be angry with me, O Jesus, ineffable good, nor withhold from me the abundance of your compassion.
