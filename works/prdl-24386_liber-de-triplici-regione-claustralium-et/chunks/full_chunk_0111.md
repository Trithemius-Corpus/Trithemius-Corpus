Therefore, as much as you can, have compassion on the desolation of the souls of the faithful groaning in the prison of purgatory, and pour out diligent prayers for them to the Lord. For although the almighty and merciful Father, by his justice, defers them from the kingdom, nevertheless he loves them with inestimable charity. Whatever good, therefore, you now do by praying for them, you offer as a pleasing sacrifice to almighty God. And when you come there, you will receive multiplied whatever you have done here for them in charity. For Scripture says: It is holy and wholesome to pray for the dead, that they may be released from sins.

*Passing Through a Cemetery or Cloister.*

But when passing through a cemetery, cloister, or another place of burial of the dead, do not forget your brothers and all the faithful departed; but, moved by charity, have compassion on them, and strive to aid them before the Lord in this or a similar way. Prayer.

Hail, faithful souls; rest in holy peace. May he who redeemed you with his precious blood deign to free you from punishments and bring you to eternal joys, where you may rejoice and exult forever, blessed ones. Another.

May God the Father, who created you, bless you. May Jesus Christ, who redeemed you with his most precious blood, bless you. May the Holy Spirit, who by his mercy and gift preserved you from eternal damnation, bless you. Amen.

*Read the Following Prayer Often.*

I beseech you, sweetest Lord Jesus, Son of the living God, through the pain of your wounds, and through the shedding of your most holy blood, and through the power of your most innocent death: have mercy on the souls of the faithful departed existing in the punishments of purgatory, and grant them the dwelling of eternal light with all your saints.
