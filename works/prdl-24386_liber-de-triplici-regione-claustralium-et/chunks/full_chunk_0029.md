But no one can rise again from the depth of hell with a lukewarm will; no one can overcome a habit without a contrary habit, since, unless a certain violence intervenes, the habit of sinning will always strengthen vice. Rightly, moreover, this lowest region is called hell, for by a certain likeness of disturbance and aversion it is compared to hell. Now after this life there are three dwellings of souls, which receive their citizens, emigrating from this world, from the three regions. For the first dwelling is called hell, and this is compared to the lowest region. For as in hell no order dwells, but perpetual horror, so in this lowest region the fury of the mind, blinded by carnal affections, disturbs all things. In hell all are evil, and in the lowest region nowhere are good men found. Whoever fall down to hell have been from the lowest region. For the lowest region always sends its citizens to the lower world, unless, led by repentance, while living they have taken care to renounce dead works.

The second dwelling is called purgatory, and this is compared to the second region, the rational. For just as purgatory purges and perfects souls, and sends them, once purged, to the kingdom of heaven, so this second region washes its citizens, persevering in it through continual exercise, from the filth of sins, and disposes them for ascent to the third region, which is called spirit. In this dwelling all are good, but still to be purged so that they may become better; and in the second region none are evil, but nevertheless they are always exercised so that they may be made better. This region sends its citizens, dying in it, to purgatory, since, even if all are good, nevertheless they do not yet seem fit for the kingdom without temporal purgation. For indeed those who inhabit this middle region, although they appear to be very near the highest, nevertheless are not yet wholly removed from the dust of the lower one. But the third dwelling of separated souls is called the kingdom of heaven, and this is compared to the highest region of the spirit. For as in that heavenly fatherland there is the highest rest and eternal peace, so in the region of the spirit there is continual tranquility of mind.

*Here ends the first region.*
