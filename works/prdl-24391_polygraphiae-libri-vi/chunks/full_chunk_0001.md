## Six Books of Polygraphy

*By Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Würzburg, formerly of Sponheim*

Addressed to Emperor Maximilian I

This edition also includes the *Key*, Adolf von Glauburg’s observations, and Trithemius’s booklet *On the Seven Secundeans, or Intelligences Moving the Spheres after God*.

Strasbourg: Lazarus Zetzner, 1600

*The Printer to the Reader, Greeting.* When, at the urging of certain persons, candid Reader, I had resolved to publish anew, at my own expense, the Polygraphy of Johannes Trithemius, I diligently applied myself to this: that it might come forth both more correct and enlarged by a new addition. And so, having sought out various editions from everywhere, I diligently compared them with the most recent; and besides, I added also a manuscript exemplar of Jacob Trithemius, brother of Johannes, and, a collation having been made with the remaining editions, I found it nearly similar to them, a few [readings] excepted. Secondly, I appended the same author's booklet on the Secundeans, or Intelligences moving the sphere after God. It is yours, kind Reader, to take in good part this our endeavor, such as it is. Farewell.
