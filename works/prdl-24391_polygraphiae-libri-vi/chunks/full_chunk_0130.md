*On the Secondary Intelligences.*

Many men shone in those times, most learned and most eloquent in every kind of knowledge: theologians, astronomers, physicians, orators, historiographers, and the like, not only among the gentiles but also among the Christians. At last the persecution of unbelievers against the Church ceased, after Constantine the Great Caesar took up the faith, in the year of the world 5539, after the midpoint of the rule of Anael himself, the Angel of Venus; although even afterwards it was at times disturbed in part by the impious, nevertheless on the whole it remained very quiet for a long time. From then on the human race, which from the time of King Ninus had miserably wandered for 2300 years in the worship of idols, was mercifully recalled to the knowledge of the one God. Various arts of subtlety were increased in these times, and through the correspondences of nature took on adornment and growth. For men's customs change with time, and lower bodies are disposed according to the influence of the higher. The mind indeed is free, and does not receive the influence of the stars unless, through the excessive commerce which it has with the body, by inclining itself it stains its own affection. For the angels who are the movers of the spheres destroy or overturn none of the things which nature has established. A comet of unusual magnitude preceded Constantine's death. The Arian heresy disturbed the holy Church in many places at the end of this rule. In the time of Julian Caesar crosses appeared on the linen garments of men.
