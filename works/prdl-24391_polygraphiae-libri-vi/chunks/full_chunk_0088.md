With the help

With the help of our Lord Jesus Christ, savior of faithful souls, who lives in the sacred ternary unity with the Father and the Holy Spirit, threefold in persons and one God in substance, incomprehensible, omnipotent, and everlasting, from whom, in whom, and through whom proceeds every good, beautiful, and true thing, we have brought this second book of the Polygraphy to its end. To him be praise, honor, virtue, and glory: he who, through the innocence of his death, freed our race from the destruction of eternal perdition, and, with perpetual mercy, opened again the entrance to eternal life for all who believe in him with a pure conscience. Line by line we have set down three hundred and eight paraphrases, deliberately avoiding a longer wandering [unclear]. For it seemed enough to us at the beginning of this lucubration if we gave those wishing for larger matters a reason to seek them out, since both time and method for these things were lacking to us. Farewell, friendly reader. From Peapolis, on the penultimate day of the month of February, in the year of the Lord 1508. IOAN-
