The fifth governor of the world was Samael, the angel of Mars, who began on the 26th day of the month of June, in the year of the world 1417, and governed the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year 1771, as is clearly evident from the history of Genesis. And it should be noted that the ancient philosophers handed down that whenever Samael, the spirit of Mars, is ruler of the globe, so often a notable alteration of monarchy arises. Religions and sects vary, laws and principalities are changed, and kingdoms are transferred to foreigners, as we can easily discover in order from histories. But Samael does not immediately from the beginning of his rule manifest this [unclear]; the same is to be understood concerning the spirits of the other planets, as is evident in histories, all of whom, according to the properties of the nature of their stars, influence and act upon the lower things of this world.

The sixth governor of the world was Gabriel of the Moon, who began after Samael of Mars on the 28th day of the month of October, in the year of the world 1771, in the eighth month, and governed the globe for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 2126.

*On the Secondary Intelligences*

In these times human beings were again multiplied and founded various cities. And it should be noted that the Hebrews say the flood occurred in the year of the world 1656, under the rule of Mars. But the Seventy interpreters, Isidore, and Bede confirm that it occurred in the year 2242, under the governance of Gabriel, spirit of the Moon; and this seems to me more consonant with the truth, by reason of the multiplication, which it is not the present time to explain.

The seventh governor of the world was Michael, angel of the Sun, who began on the 24th of February, in the year of the world, according to the common computation, 2126, and governed the globe for 354 years and four months, until the year of the world's age 2480 and 4 months. Under the rule of the angel of the Sun, as histories agree with the truth, kings first began to exist among mortals; among them Nimrod was the first, through lust for dominion, to seize tyrannical rule over his companions. The worship of gods was also instituted by human folly, and lesser men began to adore their princes as gods. Various arts too were invented by human beings at this time, namely mathematics, astronomy, magic; and the worship of the one God began to be offered to diverse creatures, and knowledge of the true God gradually passed into oblivion through human superstition. In these times, too, architecture was invented, and human beings began to use more refined customs and institutions.

*The Source of Idolatry.*

Next, in the eighth order, Orifiel, angel of Saturn, again began to govern the world on the 26th day of the month of June, in the year from the origin of the world 2480 and four months; and for 354 years and four months he governed the globe a second time, until the year of the world 2834 and the eighth month. Under the governance of this angel, peoples were multiplied, the earth was divided into regions, many kingdoms were established, the Tower of Babel was constructed, the confusion of tongues occurred, human beings were scattered over all the earth, and human beings began to cultivate the earth more carefully, to institute fields, sow grains, plant vineyards, set trees in the ground, and acquire with diligence whatever pertains to food and clothing. From this time the distinction of nobility first began to be made among human beings, when men who excelled the rest both in character and in ability received marks of glory from the greater men of the earth according to their merits. From this point the whole globe first began to come into human knowledge, since, as peoples were multiplied everywhere, many kingdoms also arose, and various differences of languages followed.

In the ninth order, Anael, spirit of Venus, again began to govern the world on the 29th day of October, in the year of the creation of heaven and earth 2834, in the eighth month, and presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 3189. In these times human beings, consigning the true God to oblivion, began to worship and honor the dead and their statues as God; this error occupied the world for more than two thousand years, introducing the increase of idolatry.
