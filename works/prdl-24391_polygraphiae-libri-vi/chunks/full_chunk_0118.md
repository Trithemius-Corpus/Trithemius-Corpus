(*) *Numerical orchema for letters.*

[table of letters and numbers]

Whoever correctly understands the table of the orchema will compose for himself from it as many alphabets as he wishes. For example, we have placed the letters of the Latins crosswise, meeting one another from the four corners, so that we might provide the leap of transposition to infinity. Those writing will need diligence, lest the multitude of numbers confuse memory or understanding.
