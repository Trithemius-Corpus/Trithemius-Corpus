*606. The Second Table of Anomalous Expansion.*

[letter-number expansion grid, as printed]

*607.* [header row of zodiac signs: Aries, Taurus, Cancer, etc.]

[letter-number grid, as printed]

To each of the twelve divisions of the Zodiac we have assigned alphabets singly by numbers, so that the sign of Aries for a has the numeral cipher 6; Taurus, 7; Gemini, 8; of Cancer, 9; [Leo,] 10; [Virgo,] 11; and so throughout the whole alphabet.

*609.* [header row of further signs]

[letter-number grid, as printed]

In this figure we have arranged the alphabets of the remaining six signs singly by numbers, just as they are seen set, in order, under the letters of the Latins, so that whoever wishes [to write] by them may the more safely veil his mysteries.
