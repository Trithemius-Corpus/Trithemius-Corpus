*18 On the Secondary Intelligences*

*A comet, an earthquake, a rain of blood. Merlin. Arthur. Thirty kingdoms served Arthur. The orders of monks. Boethius. The disturbed condition of the Church and the empire. Clovis, good king of France.*

A comet, an earthquake, a rain of blood. Merlin, born in [Tumba?], foretold wondrous things at the beginning of this rule. Arthur, whom in the common tongue they call Arthur, the most famous king of Britain, conquered the barbarians, restored peace to the Church, victoriously fought many battles, enlarged the faith of Christ, and subjected all Gaul, Norway, Denmark, and many provinces to his empire. He was the most glorious of all the kings of his time; after many splendid deeds had been accomplished, he appeared nowhere, though the Britons expected him to return for many years. Concerning him the mimes once produced marvelous songs. While he reigned, England was in flower, and thirty kingdoms served him. In these times the orders of monks began to multiply in the Church of God. Theodoric, Arian king of the Goths, possessed all Italy and killed the consul Boethius. Everything was full of disturbance, the empire and the Church alike. The emperors Zeno and Anastasius, Arians in the East, Theodoric and his successors in Italy, and Honorius, king of the Vandals, in Africa, were exercising no small tyranny.
