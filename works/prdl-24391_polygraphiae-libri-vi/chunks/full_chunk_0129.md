*Booklet.*

After Michael, fifteenth in order, now for the third time Orifiel, the spirit of Saturn, took up the government of the world on the last day of the month of September, in the year of the creation of the universe 4960, month 8, and he presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 5315. Under his government the Punic War began between the Romans and the Carthaginians. *The Punic War.* The city of Rome was almost utterly consumed by fire and water. *The calamity of Rome.* The Colossus, a cast bronze image 126 feet high, fell in an earthquake. *The Colossus is destroyed.* In this one year there was peace for the Romans after the Punic War; for 440 years they had never been without war. Jerusalem, together with the temple, was burned and destroyed by Antiochus Epiphanes. *Antiochus Epiphanes.* The history and wars of the Maccabees took place. In these times, in the year 606 from the founding of the city, Carthage was destroyed and burned for 17 continuous days. *Carthage is destroyed.* In Sicily there was a conspiracy of 70,000 slaves against their masters. In those times great portents were seen in Europe: domestic animals fled to the woods; blood flowed; a fiery globe flashed from heaven with a crash. *Many portents.* Mithridates, king of Pontus and Armenia, waged war with the Romans for 40 years. *Mithridates.* The kingdom of the Hebrews was restored, which had been interrupted for 575 years, from the time of Zedekiah until Aristobulus. Likewise the German Teutons invaded the Romans, and after many battles are said to have been defeated, and 160,000 slain, besides innumerable others who killed themselves along with their households, under the consuls Caius and Manlius. *The Teutonic disaster against the Romans.* Nevertheless many of the Romans had previously been destroyed by them. Finally the civil wars, which lasted 40 years, greatly wore down the Romans.

*On the Secondary Intelligences*

Three suns appeared at Rome; not for long, but soon they were reduced into one. A few years later Julius Caius Caesar usurped the empire of the Romans; after him Octavian Augustus enlarged it and united Asia, Africa, and Europe into one monarchy. He ruled for 36 years; through him God bestowed peace on the whole world. In the year from the founding of the city 751, in the 42nd year of Octavian Caesar Augustus, in the 245th year and 8th month of the prescribed government of Orifiel, the spirit of Saturn, on the twenty-fifth day of the month of December, Jesus Christ, the Son of God, was born of the Virgin Mary in Bethlehem of Judaea. Note how beautiful is the ordering of divine providence. For the world was first created under the government of Orifiel, and also under the third was mercifully redeemed, restored, and renewed, so that it may seem to furnish a harmony for this description of the sevenfold government of the world. For in the first government of Orifiel there was one monarchy of the whole; under the second, as we said above, it was divided into many. Again under the third, as is seen, it was recalled into unity; although, if we measure rightly, it also becomes manifest that in the second government of Orifiel there was one monarchy of the whole world, when the tower of Babylon was being built.
