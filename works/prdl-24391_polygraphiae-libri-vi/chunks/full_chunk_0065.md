For it is written: Where there is no hearing, never pour forth speech. Therefore, adding a second book to the first of the Polygraphy, we keep its form and method, setting in the light of day three hundred and eight paraphrases in simple progression, for the honorable remembrance of the most chaste Mother of God; if a suitable time had been available, [unclear] to give more elegant things, so that it might both add brilliance to the matter and sufficient progression to the form. The first inventor of any art transmits greater things to posterity, because, since the beginnings of an art are hard and unfamiliar, no subtle invention is found complete from its origin. From [Peapoli?], which is also Herbipolis, a city of the Eastern Franks, on the twelfth day of the month of February. In the year of the Lord 1508.
