Incantations and sorceries were devised in those times by Zoroaster, first king of the Bactrians, though others say differently, whom Ninus, king of the Assyrians, defeated in war.

In the tenth order Zachariel, the angel of Jupiter, again began to govern on the last day of the month of February, in the year from the creation of heaven and earth 3189, and governed, as usual, for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world itself 3543 and the fourth month. These were joyful times, which could truly be called golden, in which he brought abundance of all things and increase to the human race, and gave the world its greatest adornment. Likewise at this time God gave Abraham the law of circumcision, and first promised the redemption of the human race. Under his rule the patriarchs, founders of justice, shone forth in spirit, and the righteous were separated from the impious by their own will and deed. In these times also Jupiter was illustrious among the Arcadians, whom [Lisania?] calls the son of Heaven and of God, a king who first gave laws to the Arcadians, made them very civil in their customs, taught the worship of God, built temples, instituted priests, and procured many useful things for the human race; because of these things he was called Jupiter by them, and after death was held to be a god. Moreover, he took his origin from the sons of Heber, namely [Jerari?], as the histories report. Prometheus also, son of Atlas, under the rule of this angel was said to have made men, because from rude men he made them learned, humane, gentle, and polished in morals and knowledge; by art he made images move by themselves. He first devised the use of the ring, and likewise invented the sceptre, the diadem, and royal insignia. Other Jovian men also flourished in these times, very wise men and women, who by their own genius handed down many inventions useful to the human race; and because of the greatness of their wisdom, after death they were thought to have been numbered among the gods: namely Phoroneus, who first established laws and judgments among the Greeks, Sol, Minerva, Ceres, Serapis among the Egyptians, and many others.

In the eleventh order Raphael, the spirit of Mercury, again undertook the governance of the world on the first day of the month of July, in the year of the world 3543 and the fourth month, and presided for 354 years and 4 months, until the year from the creation of heaven and earth 3897 and the eighth month. In these times, as is clearly evident from the histories of the ancients, men devoted themselves more to wisdom; among them were these most illustrious men: Mercury, Bacchus, Omogyrus, Isis, Inachus, Argus, Apollo, Cecrops, and many others, who by their inventions benefited both the world of that time and the later world. Various superstitions too in the cultivation of idols were established by men in these times, and incantations and the arts of diabolical images were increased in a wondrous manner; and whatever subtlety and genius is usually attributed to Mercury was then being increased. Moses, a very wise leader of the Hebrews, skilled in many matters and arts, a worshipper of the true and one God, vindicated the Hebrew people from the slavery of the Egyptians into freedom. Around this time Saturn, who taught that fields should be fertilized with dung, was held as a god. Cadmus, inventor of letters, first reigned in Italy; after him Saturn, who was powerful under the rule of this Raphael, angel of Mercury. He gave the law to his people through Moses in writing, which bears manifest witness to the future birth of Christ in the flesh. A wondrous diversity of religions also arose in the world. Many Sibyls too flourished in these times, prophets, soothsayers, haruspices, magi, seers, such as the Erythraean Sibyl, the Delphic, the Phrygian, and the others.
