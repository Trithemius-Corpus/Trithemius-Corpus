Hence the prudent man, understanding and experienced in the science, will satisfy the operation at every time and place. The binary receives from unity, whence it produces the ternary. Knowledge of this cryptology is easy at first sight, after the moon has been illuminated by the sun with even a single point. Although it can also happen that the knower becomes skilled without the guidance of a teacher. Yet I think this is to be understood only in this book, and in none of the preceding ones. Concerning those that follow, judgment will be given in its proper place. Among all the cabalistic modes of secrets that we have presented, the progression of this fourth book, easier than the rest, can be extended to infinity. Therefore it is enough that I have handed down to those desiring fuller things both the form and the cause of the example. The light of understanding easily penetrates all these things. Written at Peapoli, on the ninth day of March, in the year of the Lord 1508.
