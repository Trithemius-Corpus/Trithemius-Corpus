*The booklet of Johannes Trithemius, Abbot of Spanheim, on the celestial intelligences governing the spheres after God.*

It is the opinion of very many of the ancients, most wise Caesar, that this lower world is governed, by the ordering of the first intellect, which is God, through secondary intelligences. Agreeing with their opinion, the Conciliator of the Physicians says that seven spirits were set over the seven planets from the beginning of heaven and earth. Each of these governs the world for three hundred and fifty-four years and four months in order. I make this position known to Your Most Sacred Majesty, not by affirming it, but only by reporting it.

[unclear]

The first angel, or spirit of Saturn, is called Orifiel; to him God entrusted the world to be governed from the beginning of creation, and his rule began on the fifteenth day of the month of March, in the first year of the world, and lasted 354 years and four months. Orifiel, however, is the name of an office, not of a nature, attributed to the spirit by reason of his action. Under his rule men were rude and rustic, dwelling in solitude after the manner of beasts. This needs no proof from me, since it is made plain to all from the text of Genesis.

The second governor of the world is Anael, spirit of Venus, who after Orifiel began to rule the influence of his planet in the year of the world 354, in the fourth month, that is, on the twenty-fourth day of the month of June; and he governed the world for 354 years and four months, down to the year 780 of the created globe, as is clear to one who calculates the age. Under the rule of this Anael men began to be more cultivated, to build houses and cities, to discover manual arts, weaving, [laxificium], and things like these; also to indulge more abundantly in the pleasures of the flesh, to take beautiful wives for themselves, to forget God, and in many things to depart from natural simplicity, to invent games and songs, to play the cithara, and to devise whatever belongs to the principle and cultivation of Venus. This wantonness of life endured among men until the flood, taking the occasion of its own depravity.

*On the Secondary [Intelligences]*

The third, Zachariel, angel of Jupiter, began to govern the world in the year 780 from the creation of heaven and earth, in the eighth month, that is, on the twenty-sixth day of the month of October; and he governed the world for 354 years and four months, down to the year 1063 of the created globe inclusive. Under his governance men first began to claim lordship for themselves over one another, to practice hunting, to make [sentoria], and to adorn their bodies with various garments; and a great division arose between the good and the wicked, the good calling upon God, like Enoch, whom the Lord translated, while the wicked ran after the enticements of the flesh. Under the rule of this Zachariel men also began to live more civilly, to endure the laws of their elders, and to grow tame from their former savagery. Under his leadership, too, Adam, the first man, died, leaving to all posterity the testament of necessary death. Finally, various inventions and arts of men emerged at this time, as the historians have set forth more clearly.

The fourth ruler of the world was Raphael, spirit of Mercury, who began in the year 1063 from the creation of heaven and earth, on the twenty-fourth day of February, and presided for 354 years and four months; and his dominion lasted down to the year of the world 1417 and the fourth month.
