When Rudolf had died, Adolf of Nassau was elected king and ruled for six years; afterward Albert, son of Rudolf, defeated him in battle near Worms, killed him, and was elected emperor in the year 1298. He ruled for ten years and was killed by his brother's son. The Order of the Templars is destroyed by order of Pope Clement V. The island of Rhodes was taken by Christians from the hands of the Saracens, in the fourth year of the siege and of continuous war against them. After Albert was killed by his nephew, Henry VIII, count of Luxembourg, was appointed emperor; he ruled for five years. When he had died, Louis IV of Bavaria ruled for thirty-two years, beginning in the year 1315; to him the Roman pontiffs gave the crown. Frederick, duke of Austria, opposed him, but was defeated by him.

After Louis, Charles IV, king of Bohemia, was appointed emperor; he raised the bishopric of Prague to an archbishopric, and ruled for thirty-one years. There were very great earthquakes. This Charles newly instituted many things concerning tolls in favor of the prince-electors. Gunther, count of Schwarzenburg, calling himself king, opposed Emperor Charles, but prevailed in nothing against him. After Charles, his son Wenceslaus ruled for twenty-two years; after him Jodocus, margrave of Moravia, succeeded Sigismund, brother of Wenceslaus.

*Little Book*

Wenceslaus was deposed. Leopold, duke of Austria, fighting in battle with eight counts and more than 4,000 men, was killed by the Swiss. Under Wenceslaus, king of the Bohemians and emperor, the doctrine of the Hussites took its beginning. When he had been deposed, Rupert, count palatine of the Rhine and duke of Bavaria, was elected and ruled for ten years. In the year of the Lord 1369 the Christians brought war upon the Saracens, but it turned out badly because of the arrogance of the French, since more than 100,000 of our men perished, besides captives, among whom was John, duke of the Burgundians. There were many wars in these times.

In the year 1407 Sigismund becomes emperor for twenty-seven years. He attempted to lay waste Bohemia for the extirpation of heresies, but made little progress. The kingdom of France is cruelly ravaged and afflicted by the English and Burgundians. When Sigismund had died, Albert, duke of Austria, his son-in-law, succeeded him in the year of the Christians 1438, and ruled for only two years, a very excellent man and worthy of empire. When he had died, Frederick III, duke of Austria, [son?], was appointed emperor by election of the princes, and ruled for fifty-six years, a man divine in intellect and peaceful in spirit. He began to reign in the year of the Lord 1440.

In the year of the Christians 1453 Constantinople is taken by the Turks through the betrayal of a certain Genoese, and gradually all Greece fell away from the Christian faith.
