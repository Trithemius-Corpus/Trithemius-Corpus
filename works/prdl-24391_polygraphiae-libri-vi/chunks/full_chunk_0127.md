In the twelfth order, Samael, the angel of Mars, began again to rule the world on the second day of the month of October, in the year of the world 3892, in the eighth month; and his rule lasted 354 years and 4 months, down to the year 4252. Under his rule occurred that great and most famous destruction of Troy in Asia Minor, and there took place a remarkable change of monarchy and of many kingdoms, and the founding anew of many cities, such as Paris, Mainz, Carthage, Naples, and many others.

Many kingdoms also arose anew, such as those of the Lacedaemonians, the Corinthians, the Hebrews, and very many others. In these times there were very great wars and battles of kings and peoples throughout the whole world, and various changes of empires. From this time the Venetians reckon the beginning both of their people and of their city from the Trojans. And it should be noted that very many other nations, both in Europe and in Asia, claim that they took their origin from the Trojans; I have judged that only so much credence should be granted them as they themselves will be able to persuade me, by sufficient testimony, belongs to the truth. Frivolous are the things they adduce about their nobility and antiquity, wishing openly to boast, as though there had not been peoples in Europe before the destruction of the Trojans, and as though no one among them were ignoble.

Likewise, under the rule of this planet Saul was made the first king of the Jews, and afterward David, whose son King Solomon built in Jerusalem the temple of the true God, most famous in all the world. From this time the Spirit of God, illuminating his prophets with a fuller light of his grace, caused them to foretell not only the future incarnation of the Lord Savior, but many other things as well, as the sacred histories testify. Among them were Nathan, prophet of King David, Gad, Asaph, Ahijah, Shemaiah, Azariah, Anan, with many others. Homer, the Greek poet and writer of the destruction of Troy, Dares the Phrygian, and Dictys of Crete, who were present at that destruction and likewise described it, are read to have been alive in these times.

In the thirteenth order, Gabriel, the spirit of the Moon, again took up the rule of the world on the thirtieth day of January, in the year from the origin of the universe 4252, and he presided in his empire for 354 years and 4 months, down to the year of the world 4606 and the fourth month. In that time many prophets flourished among the Hebrews, namely Elijah, Elisha, Micah, Obadiah, and very many others. And there were various changes in the kingdom of the Hebrews. Lycurgus gave rights and laws to the Lacedaemonians. Capetus Silvius, Lyberius Silvius, Romulus Silvius, and Numitor, kings of Italy, flourished under this spirit. Many new kingdoms also took their beginnings under him, such as those of the Lydians, the Medes, the Spartans, and others. The monarchy of the Assyrians failed with Sardanapalus. Likewise the kingdom of the Macedonians came to an end. Various laws were imposed upon men, and the worship of the true God was neglected; the religion of false gods was propagated exceedingly. The city of Rome was built under the rule of this spirit in the year 1484, which was the 239th year of the rule of Gabriel himself in this order; and the kingdom of the Silvii of Italy failed, and that of the Romans began. In these times also Thales, Solon, Periander, Cleobulus, Bias, and Pittacus, the seven sages, flourished in Greece, and from then on philosophers and poets began to be held in esteem. The first to rule at Rome was Romulus, founder of the city, for 37 years, a fratricide and cultivator of sedition.
