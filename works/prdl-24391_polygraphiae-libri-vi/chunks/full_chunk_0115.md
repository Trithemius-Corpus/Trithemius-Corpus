Besides that alphabet of the Northmen which we placed at the beginning of this sixth book, we have also found the following written out in Bede.

From Latin letters also multiple alphabets are made, when, either reduced to number according to the model which we handed down in the preceding fifth book, or otherwise, in imitation of the Northmen, together with Greek letters they provide a method even for our own letters, for communicating most securely whatever we wish. Let us consequently set down, according to the number and order of our usual alphabet, the letters themselves changed in an unusual way, and we shall have a secret and mystical method of writing, sufficiently fine and elegant, confirmed by usage. This method of numbering, however, is different from the one which we placed in the fifth book, and is more suited to our purpose.
