*On the Seven Seconds, little book.*

1508. There remain seventeen years until the end of the government of Samael, in which figures signifying the beginning of evils will be given. For in the year of the Christians 1525, the crosses seen on men's clothing before the decade that has passed will show their effect; but thirteen [years] from now, duly summoned, you will give place to one who does not know; greater after death you will rise again, although you may be sent, covered in cloud.

In the twentieth order Gabriel, angel of the Moon, will receive the government of the world, in the year of the world 6732, in the fourth month, on the fourth day of the month of June, in the year of the Christians 1525, and he will govern the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 7086 and the eighth month; but of the Lord's nativity, 1879, and likewise 11 months. The future course of this revolution requires prophecy.

I have not written these things assertively, most sacred Caesar; they are in no way to be believed, since that would be an injury to the orthodox faith. There are, however, those who have reckoned the months in these matters as lunar months; if you should judge that they are to be agreed with, those things which I have written otherwise must be changed. With my own hand also I protest, and with my mouth I confess, that in all these matters I believe nothing, I admit nothing, but reject and despise them as vainly devised and superstitious.

Praise to almighty God.

*END.*
