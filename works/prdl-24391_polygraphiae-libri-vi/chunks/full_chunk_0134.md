At that time very grave [movements] of wars occurred among themselves in France, England, Westphalia, Prussia, Flanders, Swabia, and other places. In these times the art of printing was newly invented at Mainz, the metropolis of Germany, by wonderful industry, a gift of divinity. In the year of the Christians 1456, the Turks were overthrown in Hungary, and many infidels perished. The pilgrimage of boys to Saint Michael was remarkable. Earthquakes occurred in the kingdom of Naples, and more than 40,000 men perished. In the year of the Lord 1462, Mainz, the metropolis of the Franks, was captured and despoiled. Charles, duke of Burgundy, defeated the Franks in the year 1465. Afterwards, in the year 67, he destroyed Dinant and Liege. In the year 1473 he entered Guelders and powerfully obtained it, and likewise the whole duchy of Lorraine. A comet appeared through the month of January in the year of the Lord 1472. Charles, duke of Burgundy, afterwards besieged the town of Neuss for the space of a year, in the year 74; he was afterwards killed in war in the year 1477, a magnanimous prince. In these times the Turks took many cities of the Christians: Negroponte in Euboea, also the kingdom of Bosnia, the duchy of [Spetis], Achaea, [Mytsena], and several kingdoms in the East. In the year 1476 a gathering of fools occurred in the Franconia of Germany, at Niklashausen, full of errors. In the year 1480 the Turks, with a great army, besieged the Rhodians, but did not prevail; in the same year, departing from Rhodes, they took Otranto, after more than 12,000 Christians had been killed,

only 22 escaping by flight. And in the following year Mehmed, emperor of the Turks, died; Bayezid, his firstborn, succeeded him in the kingdom, now ruling into his twenty-seventh year. In the year of the Christians 1486 Maximilian, son of Frederick, was appointed king at Frankfurt, and was saluted as Caesar by Pope Julius in the year 1508. He established the military order of Saint George against heretics and Turks; he subdued the Swiss by war; he attacks the rebellious Sicambri in war up to the present day, and will be fortunate against all treaty-breakers. The king of the French, in his manner, a persecutor of the empire, has been found to be devising new plots. May the Almighty protect those described under the dominion of Samael in the year 1508. The Venetians, rebels against the empire of Caesar, have been marked by ban and war. There will be punishment for obstinacy, and the reward of advised satisfaction. Around the end of this third revolution of Samael, the image of alteration will pass to the first, and there will be the destruction of many. For unless T., with God ministering, is brought back to Algos, there will be a transfer of some monarchy, or of some great kingdom. A great sect of religion will arise, the destruction of old religions. It is to be feared lest the fourth beast lose one head. In Samael, Mars first foretold the flood; in the second, the fall of Troy; in the third, around the end, there will be loss of unity. For from the preceding things let the future things that follow be judged. This third revolution of Mars will not be completed without prophecy and without the institution of some new religion. From this point, in the year of the Christians 1508.

*Maximilian, Emperor.*
