*The discovery of a book by a Jew.* In the year of the Christians 1244, a certain Jew at Toledo, while digging, found a book in which it was written: “In the third world Christ will be born of the Virgin Mary, and will suffer for the salvation of men.” Soon believing, he was baptized. The third world was the third revolution of the spirit of Saturn, of which mention was made above; at its beginning Christ was born of the Virgin.

*Frederick deprived of the empire.* The Roman pontiffs, removing Frederick from the empire, said that it had been vacant for twenty-eight years [unclear] Wilhelm, count of Holland; Conrad, son of Frederick; Alfonso, king of Castile; and Richard, count of Cornwall, brother of the king of England; and evils were multiplied on the earth.

*The confederation of the Swiss.* At this time, around the year of the Lord 1260, the confederation of the Swiss first began: a people small in number, which grew with time. They killed many of the nobles, and drove others from their territories; warlike men, whose commonwealth is known to all the peoples of Germany.

*Rudolf of Habsburg. Origin of the dukes of Austria.* In the year of the Christians 1273, Rudolf of Habsburg was made emperor by the election of the princes, and he ruled for eighteen years: a man in every respect prudent and excellent, from whom all the later dukes of Austria descended.
