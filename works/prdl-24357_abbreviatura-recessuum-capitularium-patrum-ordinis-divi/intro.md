*Abbreviatura Recessuum Capitularium Patrum Ordinis Divi Benedicti* — "An Abridgment of the Chapter Decrees of the Fathers of the Order of St Benedict, for the Province of Mainz and the Diocese of Bamberg" — is Trithemius's digest of the *recessus*, the closing decrees of the Benedictine reform chapters. It is a working document of the Bursfelde-aligned observant reform rather than a treatise: a codified summary of the chapters' rulings on monastic discipline, the sort of administrative-legislative text an abbot kept to hand. As such it is the documentary counterpart to his reform polemics — the *De Statu et Ruina Monastici Ordinis* and the *Sermones ad Monachos* — showing the machinery behind the rhetoric.
