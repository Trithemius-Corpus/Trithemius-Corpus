*How visitors are to be received and treated in the monasteries. The time for visiting. Chapter 11.*

The time for visiting is to be fixed by the presiding visitors themselves, as is stated in Const. Bene., XII, chapter 2. Yet we find in the older statutes of the fathers the command that, within a year from the time of the chapter that has been held, they should fulfill the office of their visitation. It belongs to bishops, however, to visit every year, 10 q. 1, Relata, and c. Decrevimus.

Concerning the time of visitation, in chapter 7 held at Bamberg, it was laid down thus: "Let the visitors faithfully complete within the year the office of visitation enjoined upon them." Thus there.

And how often ought visitation to be made? It seems that every year, as in Extra, De statu monachorum, Attendentes, in the Clementines. On this Franciscus de Zabarella poses the following question formally: "I ask," he says, "with respect to its saying that visitation is to be made every year, whether it is enough that it be made at the end of the first year and at the beginning of the second." The gloss on whether it suffices refers to the note in the same title, In singulis, where it is concluded that it does not, because in this way it could, in the case of chapter In singulis, be deferred up to six years, to the prejudice of the monastery; nor would that satisfy the law, which wishes it to be done from three-year period to three-year period.

Stephen says the same, because visitation is made for correcting things committed, not for things to be committed. Therefore, if the first was sufficient, the second will have nothing to correct. Paulus says the same, because if this were done at the end of the first year and at the beginning of the second, there would not be a year between the first and the second, but a two-year period. Hence it seems that they ought to be equally spaced; argument from De consanguinitate, Quod dilectio, as Paulus thinks. This is precisely true with respect to chapter In singulis, because the text there says "from three-year period to three-year period." Thus Franciscus Zabarella in the said chapter.
