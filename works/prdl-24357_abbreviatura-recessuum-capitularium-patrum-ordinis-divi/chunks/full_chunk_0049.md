In the same bull of the aforesaid council all the abbots and prelates of the whole province of Mainz and of the dioceses of Bamberg of this order were summoned, so that on the day before the Kalends of March, in the year of the Lord 1417, in the city of Constance, in the monastery of Petershausen, they should appear in person to hold such a provincial chapter. This was also done in that manner. And from this time down to the present, in the aforesaid province, a chapter has been held every three years in accordance with the constitutions of the supreme pontiffs.

*The first provincial chapter was held at Constance in the year of the Lord 1417.*

In the year of the Lord one thousand four hundred and seventeen, on the last day of February, in the tenth indiction, while the apostolic see was vacant, in the third year of the Council of Constance, the first provincial chapter of our order of the province of Mainz and of the dioceses [unclear] was held; the abbots were Thomas of the monastery of Eboracum of this and the diocese, Sifridus of Elwangen of the diocese of Augsburg, and Johannes of St George of the dioceses of Constance.

*Concerning the statutes issued in the same chapter.*

In this provincial chapter many fathers were present, having been summoned by the general Council of Constance; and those who could not attend in person sent mandates through their proctors. And indeed in the same chapter several statutes for regular observance were issued, which, divided under individual rubrics, I have inserted below.

*With what reverence and discipline the work of God is to be performed.*

Directing the eyes of our mind toward the reform of the order, we ordain that the day office and likewise the night office be celebrated devoutly, deliberately, with pauses, not hurried through, not syncopated, and at suitable hours, as is contained in the Rule of our most learned father Benedict. To this, as soon as the signal has been heard, all things that are in their hands being left behind, they are to hasten together with the greatest speed, yet with dignity, lest levity find fuel; and so nothing is to be put before the work of God. And lest they be able to excuse themselves on the pretext of ignorance, saying that the signal was not heard, we will that the tract of Matins, which we wish to begin in the middle of the night, and that of Prime and Vespers, last at least beyond a fourth part of an hour. But anyone who does not come to the aforesaid divine office is to be punished without remission, as is contained in the Rule.

We further will and ordain that every day, in places where there are six monks, and in other places where it is customary, after Prime only one Mass is to be said; but if there are two, after the early Mass the other members of the aforesaid order are to celebrate, consecrate, and communicate, as is contained in constitution 12 concerning the celebration of Masses.

*Concerning the chapter of faults.*

We further will and ordain that whatever pertains to divine worship be clean.

*The ornaments pertaining to divine worship are clean.*

We will that each one, when he comes to his monastery, take care within a month concerning the judgments and firm leases [unclear] and the place where the Eucharist is kept there. And the books, with the other ornaments concerning the divine office of the monastery, are to be preserved with diligent custody.
