We nevertheless admonish and exhort in the Lord Jesus those who up to now have made a beginning in holy religion in the observance of a stricter life, that they persist in their good beginnings and advance more and more from day to day, and that, not turning aside from their purpose under pretext of this norm, they turn themselves to laxer things; for the constitution of this bull was issued as a beginning of regular life for those who hitherto have been more negligent, until the best God grant them greater gifts.

The aforesaid bull also here commands that an authentic copy be kept in every monastery, in good and legible writing, and be read publicly once in each quarter in convent, as above in the preceding chapter.

*On the procurators of the visitors.*

It has further pleased us, and we decree, that the appointed procurators of the absent shall see whether the visitors have fulfilled their visitations, and shall report the negligent to the nearer presidents, who shall compel them, by ecclesiastical censure and other penalties if need be, to fulfill their office.

*That no one be procurator for more than three.*

We also forbid anyone henceforth to dare to be procurator for more than three, except because of evident necessity, under a penalty to be imposed by the lord presidents.

*On the viaticum of three florins to be paid to the visitors.*

The whole community of the fathers has also decreed that every visited abbot shall pay his visitors three Rhenish florins for viaticum, unless the visitors themselves, for good reason, wish to receive less; in this matter we burden their consciences.

*That prelates not absent themselves from the chapter.*

We also strictly forbid, under the penalties contained in the Benedictina, that prelates who are bound to come to the provincial chapter should henceforth presume to absent themselves from such a chapter.

*On the accounting to be made by the former presidents to the new ones concerning receipts and expenditures.*

We further will and ordain that, when their office has expired, the presidents shall render to the new presidents a full and clear account of each and every item received and expended on behalf of the chapter.

*The twelfth provincial chapter, celebrated at Erfurt at St. Peter’s for the second time, in the year of the Lord 1444.*

In the year of the Lord 1444, on the tenth day of the month of May, which was Cantate Sunday, the twelfth provincial chapter was celebrated in the monastery of St. Peter at Erfurt. In it presided the venerable fathers, the lord abbots of St. Peter there, of St. Gall, of St. Giles of Nuremberg, and of St. Stephen of Würzburg. In it procurators were appointed who were to accuse the negligence of the visitors before the presidents.

*On the old statutes here renewed and repeated.*

In this sacred chapter, as also in the two immediately preceding, the bull of the Council of Basel concerning reform was accepted in the same words as above. And that it should be kept in every monastery and read four times in the year, as above in the preceding chapter. And that the brothers of a stricter life should not wish, under pretext of this bull, to descend to laxer things, but should persevere by advancing toward the better, as in the same place. Likewise the statute concerning the authority of the presidents over the rebellious, and concerning the investigating of the negligence of the visitors by procurators, is set down here in precisely the same words in which it is contained above in the same chapter 10, together with certain other matters.

*Royal monasteries are to be visited by the presidents.*

Royal monasteries shall henceforth be visited by one of the presidents, with the visitors of that diocese taken along with him, as above in chapter 10.

*On the monasteries of nuns to be visited.*

But also the monasteries of nuns in each diocese shall be visited by the visitors of that diocese; and every visited monastery shall pay its visitors, when they depart, three Rhenish florins for viaticum.
