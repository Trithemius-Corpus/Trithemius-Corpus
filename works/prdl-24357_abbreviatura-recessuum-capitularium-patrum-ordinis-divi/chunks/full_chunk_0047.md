The other presidents, with the consent of the greater part of those then holding the chapter, or, if none of the presidents is present, the others assembling for the celebration of the chapter, may appoint other suitable persons as presidents for these occasions only. By apostolic authority, by the tenor of the presents, we grant this.

We likewise grant, by the same authority, that the aforesaid presidents may change the place chosen for holding a chapter of this kind, and afterwards, for certain reasons, [unclear] as inconvenient, and may choose another place nearer the former one, with postponement of the fixed time for holding the chapter, as shall be expedient.

The same, in the same constitutions. Those coming to those chapters shall have with them respectable servants. And they shall take care, under penalty of excommunication latae sententiae, not to stir up or cause, by themselves or through others, disturbances or impediment of chapters of this kind.

Again we decree that all and each who come to the same chapters shall not lead, or cause or permit to be led, mounts or household attendants excessive in number or in any way excessive; but, with the number of mounts and attendants suited to the condition of each of them, we will and command that they be content.
