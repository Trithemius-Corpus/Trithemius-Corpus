It pleased us that, when any one of the presidents or visitors dies, the person who succeeds him in the abbacy should also succeed, without any new summons, in the presidency or visitation, both in the office and in the dignity of the presidency.

*Concerning the feast of the commemoration of Saint Benedict.*

We further will that, in accordance with the apostolic prohibitions, the feast of the translation of our most holy father Benedict shall henceforth not be observed; but in place of the translation, the feast of his commemoration shall be kept in the hours and divine offices with all the customary ceremonies.

It was also decreed: *A new penalty for negligent visitors.*

That all visitors who have not fulfilled the office of visitation enjoined upon them shall, for each monastery not visited, be bound to pay three Rhenish florins to the chapter as a penalty, unless a legitimate cause has intervened.

*Concerning the form of the procuratory.*

And because negligence often occurs in the procuratories of absent fathers, when they are not sent in proper form, ignorance, as we believe, being the cause of this: therefore, to obviate those errors, we unanimously receive and approve this form of a sufficient procuratory inserted here.

*Form of the procuratory.*

To the reverend fathers in Christ, the lords presidents of the sacred provincial chapter of the province of Mainz and of the diocese of Bamberg, of the black monks of the Order of Saint Benedict, to be celebrated in the monastery of N., of the diocese of N., on the coming Sunday Jubilate: N., called abbot in N., of the same order, of the diocese of N., obedience, reverence, and honor.

Venerable fathers and lords, because according to the laudable statutes of the fathers a provincial chapter is to be celebrated, to which, as I ought and am bound, I cannot come in person, being prevented by such a cause. Let the cause be expressed. Hence it is that I appoint the venerable father lord N., abbot in N., or the religious brother N., professed of our monastery of N., as my undoubted procurator, in the better form, with full mandate to appear in my stead in the aforesaid chapter, to treat, vote, conclude, and do all and singular other things which I myself could and ought to do if I were present in person; with power to swear on my soul, if need be, that the cause of my absence alleged above is true. Further, if it should happen that a monetary collection be imposed by a chapter of this kind, he is to accept it in my person, which also I shall lawfully pay when I have been required. Promising, under the witness of these present letters, that I shall hold ratified and acceptable whatever shall have been established and ordained in the same chapter, and that, so far as I can, I shall observe it, both myself and those subject to me. In faith and witness of all these things, my abbatial seal is impressed below upon the presents. Given in my aforesaid monastery, in the year etc.

*The twentieth provincial chapter, celebrated at Erfurt for the fourth time, in the year of the Lord 1470.*

In the year of the Lord 1470, in the third indiction, on the fifteenth day of the month of May, which was Sunday Jubilate, the twentieth provincial chapter was celebrated at Saint Peter’s in Erfurt for the fourth time, with the customary protestations, that the fathers do not intend to admit excommunicates, and that neither they themselves nor the procurators should depart before the end of the chapter, under penalty of excommunication, namely of a sentence to be issued. In it there presided the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Gunther, abbot there; Melchior of Saints Afra and Ulrich in Augsburg; Sebald of Saint Giles in Nuremberg; Paul of Saint Mary in Elchingen. In it it was ordained that the individual acts of the individual preceding chapters should be brought together into one summary compendium, as has here been attempted in the present compilation.

*Concerning the old statutes which were here again renewed.*

In the present chapter many statutes of the old chapters were renewed and inserted again in the recess as though anew. Among them are these. First, the form of the procuratory is inserted and confirmed word for word. Likewise, that the presidents of each chapter, together with the visitors, should come on the Wednesday before the appointed day for celebrating the chapter, as above, c. 17.
