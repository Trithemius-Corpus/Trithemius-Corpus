*LXI*

In the year of the Lord 1435, in the tenth indiction, in the fifth year of the pontificate of lord Pope Eugenius IV, on the twenty-sixth day of the month of June, which was the Sunday after the Nativity of blessed John the Baptist, the ninth provincial chapter was held in the monastery of the Friars Preachers at Basel, while the general council was being held there at the same time. At it there presided the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Ortwin of Saint Peter at Erfurt, Frideric of Saint Mark in Reichenau, John of Saint George in Stein, and Heinrich in Faucibus, of the dioceses of Mainz, Constance, and Augsburg.

*Concerning the statutes of the former chapters.*

Moreover, first of all we approve and commend the statutes, and we will that they be observed by all as though expressed here.

*The statutes of the preceding chapters repeated here.*

In this chapter of the fathers almost all the statutes issued in the preceding three chapters were again repeated word for word. First, namely, the statute concerning the common life of those coming to the chapter, and concerning the number of dishes at the same, as is set forth precisely above, chapters 6, 7, and 8. And there it was ordained that the treasurers themselves should provide for the refreshment during the three days of the provincial chapter. Likewise concerning the power of the lord presidents, namely that each of them individually should have authority to proceed against rebels. Concerning the visitors also, who were compelled under penalty of excommunication to fulfill their office faithfully within the year, and to make a report of the whole matter to one of the nearest presidents; and that they should first visit and reform themselves and their own, and only then proceed to the reformation of others. Likewise that mendicants should henceforth not be received into our order, under penalty of excommunication, as above. Under the same penalty it is decreed, as above, that abbots should seek out their fugitives and should not give anyone permission to reside or govern in parishes except as is permitted above, chapters 7 and 8. Likewise the statute concerning fugitive monks not to be received to obedience without letters of recommendation is repeated as above. All these things which I have briefly reviewed, just as they are contained above in form word for word, were again issued and made as though anew.

*Decree concerning the penalty for those in contumacy.*

It was further decreed that the presidents, and each one of them, should compel by ecclesiastical censures, with appeal removed, according to constitution 12, the abbots and others who did not come to the chapter in person nor send lawful proctors with sufficient mandate for paying the double amount, and also for paying the contributions imposed by the chapter.

*Concerning the observance of the precepts.*

We also command all visitors appointed and to be appointed that in each monastery which they visit they leave a recess or visitation charter. We command that all and singular the statutes and mandates written above be observed inviolably by all, reserving, however, what is to be added, decreed, or changed in the sacred Council of Basel for the reformation of our said order. For the cause of this reformation, in the said sacred council twelve prelates of the aforesaid order from the said province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg have already been appointed and elected.

*The tenth provincial chapter was held at Nuremberg, in which the bull of the Council of Basel was accepted, in the year of the Lord 1439.*

In the year of the Lord 1439, in the second indiction, in the ninth year of the pontificate of lord Pope Eugenius IV, on the twenty-sixth day of the month of April, which was Jubilate Sunday, the tenth provincial chapter was held in the monastery of Saint Giles at Nuremberg, of the same order. At it there presided the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Hartung of Saint Peter at Erfurt, George of Saint Giles at Nuremberg, Nicholas of Saint Blaise, and Heinrich in Amerbach. In this chapter the bull of the Council of Basel concerning the reformation of our order throughout the province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg was presented and accepted.
