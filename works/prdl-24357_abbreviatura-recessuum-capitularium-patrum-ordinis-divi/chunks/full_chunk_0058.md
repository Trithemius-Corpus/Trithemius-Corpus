*Those being visited are to guard carefully the goods and horses of the visitors.*

Furthermore, when visitors have come to some monastery for the purpose of visiting, the abbot and convent of that monastery shall cause the horses, property, and goods of the visitors to be guarded with the utmost diligence. But if anything of their property, goods, or horses should happen to be lost or taken away, before all else the abbot and convent of that place itself, without any objection, shall pay the value of the lost goods or property to those visitors without delay; and nevertheless they shall be punished in chapter, at the discretion of the presidents, for such negligence.

But if, which God forbid, the visitors have been robbed or harmed in any respect on the road while they are going to make a visitation, the provincial chapter shall make satisfaction to them for such loss, as is fitting. *On the safe-conduct of visitors.*
