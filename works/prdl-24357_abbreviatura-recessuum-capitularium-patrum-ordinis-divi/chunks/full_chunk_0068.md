In the year of the Lord 1451, on the twenty-fourth day of the month of May, which was Cantate Sunday, the fourteenth provincial chapter was held at St Stephen’s in Würzburg, the most reverend father lord Nicholas of Cusa, cardinal priest of the title of St Peter ad Vincula and legate, being present and presiding over that chapter. In it the venerable fathers, the lord abbots of St Egidius in Nuremberg, St Burkhard in Würzburg, St Peter in Erfurt, and St Godehard in Hildesheim, presided.

*Of the old statutes repeated.*

Besides those statutes which the aforesaid lord cardinal Nicholas issued, the present holy chapter enacted certain things, and repeated word for word many things issued in previous chapters, namely:

Concerning safe-conduct and travelling expenses for visitors, as in the immediately preceding chapter.

Likewise, that the names of deceased brothers are to be announced in each chapter, as above, chapter 12. And many other things which it would seem superfluous to insert here in order.

*Concerning collations and drinkings not to be held in the monasteries.*

It has come to our hearing by truthful report that in some monasteries, even reformed ones, by a certain singular gracious permission of prelates, collations before compline have customarily been held; and now the brothers in certain monasteries contend that these are held daily as if by right, taking them up on their own authority, to no small prejudice and loss of the purity of reform and of the peace of the same, with detractions, frivolities, reports of secular affairs, and even sometimes extending to excessive contentions, and at times in the presence of laypeople. Therefore we prohibit those same collations from henceforth being held without the special favor of those same prelates; and we also will that the visitors be particularly admonished about them.

Besides these things nothing new was enacted in this chapter. But the lord cardinal issued certain constitutions for the visitors, some of which are contained here separately.

*The fifteenth provincial chapter, held at Seligenstadt for the second time, was celebrated in the year of the Lord 1454.*

In the year of the Lord 1454, on the twelfth day of the month of May, which was Jubilate Sunday, the fifteenth provincial chapter was held in the monastery of the holy martyrs Marcellinus and Peter in Seligenstadt. In it the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Wolfram of Hirsau, Christian of St Peter in Erfurt, Ulrich in Wiblingen, and Berthold of St Stephen in Würzburg, presided.

*They approve the statutes of the lord cardinal.*

Following in the footsteps of the lord cardinal and legate, we command that all and each of the things enacted, issued, and ordained by him for the reform of our order in the provincial chapter most recently held at Würzburg, and then unanimously accepted by the lord presidents and the whole chapter, under the penalties and censures contained and expressed in the letters made concerning them by the said lord cardinal, be strictly observed by all, with the sacred chapter approving.

*That the presidents, visitors, and treasurers come before the day of the chapter.*

We further will and ordain that, for provincial chapters to be celebrated henceforth in the future, the lord presidents, visitors, and treasurers come together on certain preceding days, meanwhile being supported also at the chapter’s expense, to treat those matters which shall arise as necessary and opportune for the more useful and speedier celebration and dispatch of that chapter.

*How visitors are to be received and treated.*

We also strictly command all prelates and convents that they, if required concerning this, provide for their visitors, at their own expense and to the best of their ability and knowledge, safe-conduct from the princes of the region and of that place or dominion, as far as another monastery to be visited. And they shall receive and treat those visitors with all reverence and humility, charitably, and shall diligently observe and obey their admonitions and ordinances.
