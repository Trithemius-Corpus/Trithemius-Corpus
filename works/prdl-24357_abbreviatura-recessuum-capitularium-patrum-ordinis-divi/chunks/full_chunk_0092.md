These and other things handed down by the holy fathers and by canonical and regular institutions, as the character of the time and the condition of persons require and as seems salutary to the visitors, they shall renew by establishing them in monasteries and places that have fallen away from regular observance, for the introduction of holy reform. And they shall take care to entrust them to due execution, causing each and all of them to be firmly observed by ecclesiastical censure, by our authority, which by these presents we commit to them.

*To the present volume, divided and apportioned into a form for visiting the monasteries, both of nuns and of monks, of the order of Saint Benedict throughout the province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg; into a manner of holding their provincial chapter every three years; into an abridgment of the recess of each provincial chapter held from the year of grace 1417 up to the present; into a summary of the privileges granted to the aforesaid order; into an epilogue expressing the foregoing matters in ordered brevity: at the request of the reverend father in Christ, the lord abbot of the monastery at Saint Giles in Nuremberg, a prelate of the said order, whose wish he greatly desires to accommodate, Georg Stuchs of Sulzbach, citizen of Nuremberg, with his art as guide and fortune as companion, gave the work its preconceived and intended end, in the year 1493 of the incarnation of the intelligible divine Word by the Holy Spirit from Mary ever virgin, on the twelfth day before the Kalends of October [20 September]. May [the merits?] of most blessed Benedict aid us in obtaining his clear intellectual and most blessed vision. Amen.*
