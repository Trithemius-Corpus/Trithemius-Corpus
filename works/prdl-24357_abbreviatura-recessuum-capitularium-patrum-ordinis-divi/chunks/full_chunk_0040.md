Nor may that division, nor any other custom whatever, avail in opposition for that abbot or for any officials whatever. On this matter we wish the visitors of the chapter especially to be attentive.

*Concerning places not to be leased out. Article 19.*

Let places of the order not be leased out, except with observance of the Benedictine constitution concerning places not to be leased out; and we wish this to be firmly observed, both with respect to the persons to whom they are leased and also with respect to the other circumstances.

*Concerning loans. Article 20.*

We also wish that, when loans have to be made, provision likewise be made according to the constitution of the aforesaid Pope Benedict XII concerning loans and sales; and we most strictly command that this likewise be observed, under the penalties added there and under others to be imposed by the aforesaid visitors of the chapter.

*Concerning inventories of monasteries. Article 21.*

So that the squanderers of the goods of a monastery, or their restorers, may be more plainly seen [unclear] and known, we wish that, at the assumption of any abbot or other presiding officer, inventories be made, as is contained in the constitutions of the aforesaid Pope Benedict [XII?] concerning inventories.

*Concerning monks who own property. Article 23.*

Against the vice of private ownership, let what is contained on this matter in [unclear], in the same title, in the constitution of the lord pope [XII?], be observed, and according to the form of common law.

*Concerning the resignation of officials. Article 25.*
