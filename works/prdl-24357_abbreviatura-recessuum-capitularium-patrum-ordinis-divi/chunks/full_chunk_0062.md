*The eighth provincial chapter was celebrated at St. Ulrich's in Augsburg in the year of the Lord 1432.*

In the year of the Lord 1432, in the tenth indiction, in the second year of the pontificate of the lord Pope Eugene IV, on the eleventh day of the month of May, which was Jubilate Sunday, the eighth provincial chapter was celebrated in the monastery of Saints Ulrich and Afra in the city of Augsburg. In it the venerable fathers, the lord abbots Ortwin of St. Peter's at Erfurt, Johannes in Stein of the diocese of Constance, and Heinrich in Amorbach, of Mainz, presided. In it provision was made concerning persons to be sent to study.

*The old statutes are renewed and approved.*

First, by common consent, we approve, ratify, and renew each and every statute issued in the preceding chapters, and we will that they be inviolably observed by all those subject to our chapter, so far as each one is concerned. These statutes, set out above, were set down here again.

Finally, in this chapter almost all the statutes were repeated and inserted in the recess; but, to avoid tedium, it will suffice to note them briefly. First, the statute that the fathers coming together to the chapter should live in common in the refectory during those three days, and concerning the number of dishes, as above, chapter 7. Likewise the statute concerning the visitors, that they should first reform themselves and their own. And, beginning with the word of God, they should fulfill their office without exacting an oath, as above, chapters 6 and 7. A mandate was also given to the abbots that they should be diligent in reforming themselves and their own in the three especially substantial matters, concerning abstinence from meat and the other things that were issued above, chapters 6 and 7; they are similarly set down here. Furthermore, the statute concerning the authority of the lord presidents, as above, chapters 6 and 7, is likewise set down here in the same words.
