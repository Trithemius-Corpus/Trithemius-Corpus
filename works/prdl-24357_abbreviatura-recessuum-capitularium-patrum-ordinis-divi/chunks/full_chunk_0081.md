*The executorial proceedings of the same bull,* in the year as above.

*Another executorial bull of the named council.* In the year as above.

*Another executorial bull of the aforesaid council.* In the year as above.

The Council of Constance gave executorial bulls against those disobedient and rebellious toward the provincial chapter or its presidents, in the year of the Lord 1420.

*A bull of Pope Martin V:* that at the time of the chapter others may be deputed and taken in the place of absent presidents; and that the presidents, for reasons, may change the place of the chapter, with [unclear] faculty of absolving from all censures and penalties as regards those who submit themselves to regular observance with the mind and intention of continuing such observance. In the year of the Lord 1439. And this privilege is very broad with respect to irregularity, infamy, and other penalties and censures; and it does not extend except to those who submit themselves to regular observance with the mind and intention of continuing such observance. And it lasts until the express revocation of another general council or of the Apostolic See.

*Statute of the lord Henry, cardinal priest of the title of Saint Eusebius of the Holy Roman Church, Englishman, legate of the Apostolic See throughout all Germany and the kingdoms of Hungary and Bohemia,* that the aforesaid presidents should compel rebels and contemners of the statutes of the chapter, and hear whatsoever cases have been moved on that account. Done at Frankfurt, on the eleventh day before the Kalends of December, in the eleventh year of the pontificate of the most holy father in Christ and our lord, lord Pope Martin V.

*Statute of the lord Louis, cardinal priest of the title of Saint Cecilia of the Roman Church, of Arles, legate de latere appointed for the regions of Germany by the holy general synod of Basel, lawfully assembled in the Holy Spirit and representing the universal Church:* that at the time of the chapter divine services may be celebrated even in an interdicted place; and that the aforesaid presidents and visitors may absolve persons subject to the chapter from all censures and penalties, confirm elections, impart the office of blessing, and crown nuns. And this statute has effect when those to whom confirmation pertains, having been solemnly requested a third time before a public notary and witnesses, with no reasonable cause preventing it, have refused, maliciously declined, or neglected to confirm the same election gratis according to the decrees of the aforesaid synod. Likewise, if those to whom this will belong by law, similarly requested, have refused, declined, or neglected to bless abbots or abbesses and to impart to them the office of blessing, and also to crown nuns or even bless them according to the customary manner, gratis and according to the decrees mentioned. Done at Frankfurt, in the year of the Lord 1446.

*Penal statutes for the reform of the abbots and brothers of the Order of Saint Benedict of the province of Mainz, of the lord Nicholas, cardinal priest of the title of Saint Peter ad Vincula of the Holy Roman Church, legate of the Apostolic See throughout Germany.*

*Statute of the same:* that those entering monasteries of nuns without permission and contrary to the provision of common law incur the sentence of excommunication. Done at Mainz, on the twentieth day of the month of November, in the year of the Lord 1451.

*A bull of Pope Pius which confirms all the statutes of the said lord cardinal.* Done in the year of the Lord 1459, in the first year of his pontificate.
