*Concerning the brief of the dead.* We further decree that each abbot, or their procurators, whenever in all future time they come to the provincial chapter to be held, shall bring with them briefs containing the names of the deceased brothers of their monasteries who have died between the chapter held and the chapter to be held, so that they may be commended to the prayers of the fathers. This concludes this fifth chapter. Many other things also were decreed and repeated in the present chapter, which are not here expressed one by one, because they have once been set down above in the same words, and it would be tedious to repeat the same things so often in writing.

*The sixth provincial chapter was held at St. Peter’s in Erfurt in the year of the Lord 1426.*

In the year of the Lord 1426, in the fourth indiction, in the ninth year of the pontificate of Pope Martin V, on the twenty-first day of the month of April, which was Jubilate Sunday, the sixth provincial chapter was held in the monastery of St. Peter in Erfurt. At it the venerable fathers and lord abbots presided: Johannes in Vrauwe, Henricus of St. Giles in Brunswick, Georgius of St. Giles in Nuremberg, and Nicolaus in Bretzenhausen.

*First of all the old statutes are praised.* With the common consent of all, first of all we approve, ratify, and renew all and singular the statutes issued in previous chapters, and we wish them to be considered expressly confirmed, as if inserted here.

*Here the sentence of excommunication and the censure are removed.* It pleased the lord presidents and all those residing in chapter to remove and relax the sentences of excommunication and censures pronounced in past chapters, lest they should beget a snare for souls. Whereupon they said: The penalties and sentences of excommunication, suspension, and interdict issued by the presidents in past chapters, lest they should bring forth a snare for souls, are, in the name of God, removed.
