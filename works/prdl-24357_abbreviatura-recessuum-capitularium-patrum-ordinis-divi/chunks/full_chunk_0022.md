*The notary should subscribe himself to this citation according to custom, and should make execution under vespers or compline.*

The one who is to deliver the sermon on the morrow, on Saturday, should present it in writing to the lord presidents for examination and correction, if need be, lest scandal arise.

The former presidents should, before the day of chapter, render to the future presidents a full account of receipts and expenses, which they may read in the prior writings if there is need. And therefore on Wednesday both are ordered to arrive at the chapter, as was lately established in chapter 17.

On Sunday let the sign for terce be made earlier, in the year of the Lord 14[unclear]9, according to the ordinance of the presidents; and the abbot appointed to sing the mass should vest himself with the ministers, and at the introit should enter the choir, as is customary. And before the presbytery with the ministers he should read the Confiteor; and the mass of the Holy Spirit should be sung solemnly, at which all the priors are to be present.

When the sermon is finished, let them enter the chapter place and let each sit: first the abbots, then the provosts and priors, and the proctors of the absent, according to the placing of the promoter. *XXII* Then the promoter, standing while all listen, should say:

In the name of God, amen. By the mandate of the venerable lord presidents, I warn and require all and singular here present, to whom it does not pertain to be present at this our sacred provincial chapter, under penalty of excommunication and suspension, to go out from this chapter place and withdraw. *The notary shall remain with the witnesses; yet even they too may be ordered to go out while secret matters are being treated. These, afterward, if they are to be written down in recess, shall also be recited to the notary. Then, according to the usual custom, let them read from the martyrology, and let the Precious be added by the prior of the convent, after first asking a blessing from the president, saying: Lord, bid a blessing. And the president who gives the word should answer: May the almighty and merciful Lord direct us in the way of his commandments. And all should answer: Amen.* *And for the regular reading let him read from the second chapter of the Rule,* concerning what sort the abbot ought to be. *Chapter 2 of the Rule.*

The abbot who is worthy to preside over a monastery should always remember what he is called, and should fulfill the name of superior by his deeds; he is believed to act in Christ's place in the monastery, since he is called by his own title, as the Apostle says: You have received the spirit of adoption of sons, in which we cry, Abba, Father. Therefore the abbot ought neither to teach, nor establish, nor command anything outside the commandment of the Lord, which God forbid; but his command or teaching should be sprinkled like the leaven of divine justice in the minds of the disciples. *This section is [unclear] most often customarily read as an opening.* The abbot [unclear], because an examination must be made in the dread judgment of God concerning both matters, his teaching and the obedience of the disciples.
