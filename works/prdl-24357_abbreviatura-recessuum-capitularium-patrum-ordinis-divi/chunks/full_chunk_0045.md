We strictly enjoin and command that no man, of whatever estate, dignity, and preeminence, even if he be exempt, under penalty of excommunication latae sententiae, which we pronounce in these writings, unless he has license according to the disposition of common law from the ordinary or from him whom it concerns, and otherwise in cases permitted by law, shall hereafter and after the execution of the present mandate presume in any way to enter any monastery of nuns of the city and province of Mainz. *Likewise, nuns are forbidden under penalty of excommunication to go out from the monasteries.*

Under penalty of latae sententiae and interdict, we enjoin all and each of the nuns of the aforesaid monasteries that, after notice of the present letters, they shall in no way, even when license has been given by the abbess and prioress or by any other woman whatsoever, unless as common law permits, dare to leave their monastery, nor shall any of them dare to do so. Rather, we will that a woman doing the contrary be excommunicate, and the place wherever she shall be be under interdict, without further declaration.

Commanding, under a like penalty of latae sententiae, all chaplains and priests who have certain knowledge of this, that they publish that nun as excommunicate and that place where she shall be as under interdict.

XLV

*Constitutions of the provincial chapters of the Order of Saint Benedict throughout the province of Mainz and the diocese of Bamberg.*

*Preface.*

Since, for one who wishes to know the individual statutes of the order, namely what statutes and of what kind have been issued by our most devout predecessors, the fathers, in provincial chapters, it would be laborious to go through all the acts and recesses of the same because of their length; yet lest so pious a desire of knowing be hindered by so great a labor, since it is also full of tedium for anyone to carry with him the larger volumes of the aforesaid recesses in celebrating chapters according to custom, I undertake to bring together the same statutes, abbreviated into one. And this is the order of this labor: I shall pass over the sanctions of the supreme pontiffs and also the decrees of general councils made for the celebration of chapters of this kind, so that what is to be done or thought in the remaining statutes may be recognized more easily. Then I shall summarize, with whatever brevity and diligence I can, the times, places, and statutes of the individual provincial chapters celebrated in our province from the time of the Council of Constance. And I believe that this labor will be sufficiently useful to posterity, if they should decide to add to these things what ought to be established. But if it should please no one, which I fear more, I nevertheless pray that I be forgiven, since I have satisfied, would that I had done so, not my own will, but that of the fathers who desired and requested it.

*Innocent III, in the general council. That a provincial chapter be observed every three years.*

In individual kingdoms or provinces, every three years, saving the right of diocesan pontiffs, let there be held a common chapter of abbots and priors not having their own abbots, who have not been accustomed to celebrate such a chapter; to which all who do not have a canonical impediment shall come together at one [unclear].
