*That they should not receive mendicants into the order*

We forbid all abbots and prelates of our chapter, under penalty of excommunication, to receive anyone from the order of mendicants into obedience; and they are to bring back to their monasteries as soon as possible those already received who are now wandering. See c. 2 and c. 3; and this has often been decreed in many other places.

*Concerning fugitive monks*

Under the same penalty of excommunication we command all abbots and prelates subject to our provincial chapter to gather back their fugitive monks, and not henceforth to give anyone permission to dwell in parishes or to govern them, unless they are annexed to monasteries.

*What sort abbots ought to be*

Since the madness of wolves must be deterred by the barking of dogs and the staff of the shepherds toward their subjects, we command all abbots of our province subject to the chapter that, after the example of the good shepherd Jesus Christ, whose place they appear to hold in monasteries, they should kindly cherish and prudently defend their monks and subjects, not lording it over the clergy, but being made an example to the flock.
