*Explanation of the Fourth Book of Our Polygraphy.*

In the fourth book of the Polygraphy, by a similar method, we have placed the words contrived by us in alphabetical order, but in one respect alone we deliberately differ from the preceding book. For in the preceding book the words contain in themselves nothing else of property except that they represent the letters set before them at the head. But in the fourth book, which we now have in hand, each word contains its own [unclear], and nevertheless designates the letters placed before it.
