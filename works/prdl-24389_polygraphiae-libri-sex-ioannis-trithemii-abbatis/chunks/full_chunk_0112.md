We have set forth up to this point, by way of example, the ancient manner of the Germans and Franks of writing numbers with Latin letters, so that we might also furnish knowledge of it to those who do not know it. But as for the fact that, in the placement of the figures of algorism, we have departed from the common rule of computation, this was done not from error but for a reasonable cause, namely so that we should not be overburdened in an excessive progression. There were also some who wrote larger numbers in this manner.
