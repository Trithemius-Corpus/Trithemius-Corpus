And if it was raised, it should not have been left without a good answer; nor thereafter is it to be raised in this way, since it cannot be so raised without suspicion of injury and blasphemy against the Virgin. Therefore it is to be publicly repaired and expounded to the glory of the most pure conception of the most worthy Virgin. ¶ The third proposition: as to the first part, it is false, impious, and offensive to pious ears, drawing back the devotion of the faithful from the immaculate conception of the most blessed Virgin, mother of our Lord and Redeemer Jesus Christ; discordant with ecclesiastical worship, right reason, sacred Scripture, and the faith; to be publicly revoked and repaired. The second part was preached rashly and scandalously. As to the third part, it is not plausible to believe that our most holy lord Pope Sixtus excommunicated those speaking in such a way, and especially in the sense that I was alleging. I therefore, Brother Johannes Verij, religious of the Order of Friars Preachers, priest, professor of sacred theology, wishing, so far as lies in me, to remove every scandal and to be a son of obedience, peace, and an asserter of truth, to preach true and sound doctrine, to honor God and the Virgin his mother, most worthy of all praise, and to obey my University of Paris and the Faculty of Theology, retract the aforesaid three propositions, firmly adhering to and consenting to their qualifications as they have been declared by our sacred Faculty of Theology, and never coming against the aforesaid determination. And to the same University and Faculty I promise and swear by my holy orders; and so may God help me and his most glorious mother, perpetually immaculate. Done at Paris, in the general congregation at Saint-Mathurin, solemnly celebrated by oath, in the year of the Lord 1497, on the sixteenth day of the month of September.

¶ *Statute and determination of the sacred Faculty of Theology of the University of Paris, on the matter of the most pure and immaculate conception of the most glorious Virgin, mother of the Lord Redeemer Jesus Christ.*
