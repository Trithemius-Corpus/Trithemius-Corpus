A foolish and wicked presumption: that he should take confidence from the patronage of the daughter who scorns to honor her venerable mother. For he who has the mother hostile to him will not be able to find the daughter appeased. For the daughter loves her mother, and the whole host of the heavenly court venerates Anne. *There follows the statute and determination of the sacred faculty of theology of the University of Paris, concerning the immaculate conception of the glorious Virgin. And first, the retraction made at Paris in the year of the Lord 1496 by brother John [Verijs?] of the Order of Preachers concerning the aforesaid matter.*

I, brother John [Verijs?], religious of the Order of Friars Preachers, priest, master in theology, was accused and denounced before my mother, the University of Paris, and the faculty of theology, for having preached in the town of Dieppe, in the diocese of Rouen, on the feast of the Conception of the most pure and most glorious Virgin Mary, mother of our Lord Jesus Christ, most recently past, in the year [unclear], the three following propositions.

The first: that the blessed Virgin Mary was cleansed from original guilt. The second, by way of a question: how Mary could say the Lord’s Prayer, and especially as to that part, “forgive us our [unclear],” without resolving the question. The third: those asserting that the Virgin Mary was conceived in original sin do not sin mortally, nor are they heretics; indeed, all who say that they, in saying this, sin mortally or are heretics are excommunicated by Sixtus through a certain bull. From this preaching of mine, as I understood, both from my aforesaid mother the university and faculty of theology, and also from other prelates, bishops, and persons of middle rank, there arose and still remains grave scandal and indignation, as though the hearers had understood that in that preaching of mine I wished to detract from the purity of the Conception of the most blessed and most glorious Virgin Mother, the begetter of our Lord and redeemer Jesus Christ. These three propositions I submitted, and do submit, to proposition, disputation, and every manner of determination; and it qualified them in the following manner.
