For you do not seek the honor of God, you who attack the purity of the Son's mother. But hear my counsel and change your intention. Return at last to yourselves, for it is time, and do not presumptuously defend an uncertain opinion; leave it undiscussed, and pass it by without sin. If you love God, if you love Jesus Christ, the savior of all, do not do so great an injury to his most holy mother, about whom no one is permitted to think anything except what is most holy. To believe that her conception was always most pure is not a matter of necessity, but of piety. Not to believe it belongs to human dullness; but, in not believing, to attack it and wish to defend the contrary opinion is not only not free from fault, but is also a matter of great rashness and folly. For rashness is rightly reproved where piety is rewarded. Nor should it seem to anyone a small sin that is committed against God and his mother. But he who loves the Son of God does not [open?] his mouth against the honor of his mother, does not detract from her purity, does not stubbornly defend an uncertain opinion; for where humble devotion receives a reward, the presumption of the proud will find torment.

For the Mother of God is believed to have been worthy to be conceived without original stain, she who as a virgin was to bear him who would destroy the sins of the whole world. Blessed mother Anna also proved worthy of such a conception, whose renowned way of life was most pleasing to God. Do not marvel that the Mother of God, contrary to the custom of nature, was preserved from original sin, since God, the infuser of grace, had prepared her for himself as a dwelling. Consider the child born, the Mother of God, and you will not marvel at a conception without stain. Great are the things that are said, but true, but holy, but fitting for the Mother of God, whose conception grace anticipated; and at this nature marvels. We too marvel, who faithfully believe. But the adversaries, not believing, do not marvel, but are confounded. If you can receive these things, believe; if you do not grasp them, do not investigate them, if you do not wish to sin. For it is better to keep silence with reverence about the purity of the most blessed Mother of God than to say unworthy things. Therefore take care always to honor with humble silence the things that you cannot understand.

Therefore holy Anna, a holy woman, conceived by a holy husband; she conceived without original stain, she gave birth without fault. O holy and formerly unheard-of conception. She conceived by a man, but this conception was never liable to any fault. She conceived according to the order of nature, but contrary to the custom of nature. O holy conception, to be celebrated with continual praises: it prepares a mother without stain for the Son of God; over it the choir of angels rejoices, and the whole world throughout the earth exults. Truly a conception always to be honored, which existed first in grace before almighty God before it was fulfilled.
