*De Purissima et Immaculata Conceptione Virginis* — "On the Most Pure and Immaculate Conception of the Virgin" — is Trithemius's defence of the Immaculate Conception of the Virgin Mary, a doctrine fiercely debated between Dominicans and Franciscans in the fifteenth century and not yet defined dogma. Trithemius weighs in for the Franciscan, pro-Immaculist position, in the scholastic manner — marshalling authorities and distinctions rather than preaching. It is continuous with his St Anne and Marian writings, the devotional-hagiographic strand of his early Sponheim output, but its register is polemical-theological.

This is the **1506 Strasbourg edition** (BSB scan). It is one of two witnesses of the *De Purissima* in this corpus, alongside the Nuremberg edition (`prdl-24370`).
