# Cluster register notes — bibliographic / catalog

This work is a catalog of ecclesiastical writers (the *De Scriptoribus Ecclesiasticis* and adjacent compilations). Apply these conventions:

- **Terse, entry-style register.** Each author entry follows a fixed pattern: name, role (often *abbas*, *episcopus*, *presbyter*, *monachus*), location, era, and a list of works with incipits. Keep the prose tight; do not pad.
- **Preserve incipits verbatim.** A list like "*scripsit librum De fide unum, cuius initium est: Magnus es Domine; et alium De caritate, cuius initium est: Quaerite Deum*" should render the *initium* phrases as Latin in quotation marks, not translate them.
- **Author names follow conventions.** Patristic and medieval Latin names retain their Latin form when they are the conventional English (Augustinus → Augustine, Hieronymus → Jerome, Gregorius → Gregory). When in doubt, keep the Latin.
- **Place names**: *Spanhemensis* = Sponheim; *Trittenhemensis* = Trittenheim; *Wirceburgensis* = Würzburg; *Hirsaugiensis* = Hirsau; *Fuldensis* = Fulda.
- **Floruit conventions**: "*claruit anno Domini 1100*" = "flourished in the year of Our Lord 1100" (or simply "active around 1100"). Don't over-translate "*claruit*" — "flourished" is the conventional English.
- **Cross-references** (*ut supra*, *ut diximus*, *de quo iam scripsimus*) preserve the bibliographic-internal structure; render naturally without expanding.
