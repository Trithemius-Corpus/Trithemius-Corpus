# Names and proper nouns — preserve as indicated

Render names according to the conventional English form (right column) when they appear in the source. When the Latin form is itself the conventional English (e.g. *Trithemius*, *Hieronymus* in older usage), keep the Latin form. Italicize Latin titles of works.

## Cryptographic / occult corpus

For spirit-prince names in the *Steganographia* tradition (Pamersiel, Padiel, Camuel, Aseliel, Caspiel, Aciel, Gediel, and the dozens of subordinate angels), **preserve the Latin form verbatim** — these are technical terms of the cipher scheme, not figures with established English names.

## Books and titles

Titles of Latin works render with the Latin title in italics (e.g. *De Scriptoribus Ecclesiasticis*, *Steganographia*, *Polygraphia*), not translated into English. The translation of the title may follow in parentheses on first mention (e.g. *De Laude Scriptorum Manualium* — "In Praise of the Scribes").
