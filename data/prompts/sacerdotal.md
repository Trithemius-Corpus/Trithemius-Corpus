# Cluster register notes — sacerdotal / pastoral

This work is a formation manual for priests (*De Cura Pastorali*, *Institutio Vitae Sacerdotalis*, *De Sacerdotum Vita Instituenda*). Apply these conventions:

- **Priestly technical vocabulary**:
  - *sacerdos* = priest (generic); *presbyter* = elder / priest (with ordained-ministry sense); *episcopus* = bishop
  - *cura pastoralis* / *cura animarum* = pastoral care / care of souls (idiomatic; don't literalize)
  - *ministerium* = ministry; *officium* = office
  - *ordo* = order (clerical orders) or order (sequence) depending on context
  - *plebs* (in priestly context) = the laity / the people; not "plebs" in the modern dismissive sense
- **Practical-doctrinal register.** Less hortatory than the monastic-reform works, more practical: what a priest does, how he prepares, the sins he must guard against. Render in plain instructive prose.
- **Gregorian heritage.** Trithemius's vocabulary on pastoral care derives from Gregory the Great's *Regulae Pastoralis*. Where Gregory's English (NPNF Barmby 1895) has a settled rendering, prefer it.
- **Sacramental terminology**: *eucharistia* = Eucharist; *missa* = Mass; *poenitentia* = penance (the sacrament and the virtue, context-dependent); *baptismum* = baptism; *confessio* = confession (when sacramental; "acknowledgment" or "profession" in other contexts).
- **Avoid Protestant-inflected English** for sacramental terms. Trithemius is writing pre-Reformation Catholic; *Eucharist*, *Mass*, *priest* are correct; *Lord's Supper*, *minister*, *elder* are not.
